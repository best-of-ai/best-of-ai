Best of AI has been English-only since day one. That always felt like a strange limit for a directory of tools that people use all over the world, so we've started fixing it. As of today, the site is available in Spanish. Open [bestofai.io/es/](https://bestofai.io/es/) or pick a language from the switcher in the header and footer.

This is experimental. It works, but it isn't finished, and we'd rather say so up front.

## How the translations are made

It's a mix of machine translation and human review. A model does the first pass, working from a style guide and a glossary we wrote so that terms stay consistent: "prompt" stays "prompt," "AI tool" becomes "herramienta de IA," product names never get translated. Then a person reads the result and fixes what sounds off.

We don't have every page reviewed yet. Pages that haven't been translated show the English text, and we tell search engines to treat them as English pages instead of passing them off as Spanish. As translations land, those pages switch over on their own.

## Where you come in

Machine translation gets you most of the way, and a native speaker closes the rest. If you read something in Spanish that sounds stiff, wrong, or just not how anyone would say it, we want to hear about it. The same goes for button labels and menu text, which are short enough that a bad word choice sticks out.

You can open an issue or send a pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/best-of-ai/best-of-ai). Translations live in plain files next to the English ones, so a fix can be a one-line change. You don't need to know Hugo to help.

## Which language should come next?

Spanish is the first, not the last. The setup supports more languages, and adding one mostly comes down to translating the interface strings, writing a glossary, and finding people who can review the result.

If you'd like to see Best of AI in your language, tell us which one on [GitHub](https://github.com/best-of-ai/best-of-ai/issues). If you can also help review it, say that too. A language with a reviewer behind it will get here faster than one without.

Thanks for reading, and for helping us get this right.
