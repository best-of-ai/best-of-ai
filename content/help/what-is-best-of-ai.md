## The short version

Best of AI is a directory of AI tools worth using. Every entry gets checked by a person before it goes live. Nothing pays to get in, and nothing pays to rank higher.

## Why it exists

Most "AI tool lists" online are copy-pasted from each other, full of dead links, with half the entries being thin wrappers around someone else's API. Best of AI is built and maintained by [Dariush Abbasi](https://github.com/dariubs), who wanted a list he could actually trust rather than one built to sell placement. The project runs on open source values: the data is public, the code is public, and anyone can check a listing or send a fix.

## What you'll find here

- Tools grouped into categories, from writing assistants to image generators
- Pages built around specific jobs, so a photographer or a lawyer can see only what applies to them
- A [leaderboard](/leaderboard/) ranking tools by real usefulness, not marketing budget
- Alternative pages for comparing a tool against others that do the same job
- Directories for AI companies, hardware, and models, for anyone who wants to go past "here's an app"

## Who it's for

Anyone trying to find a tool that solves a real problem, without wading through affiliate spam to find it.

## It's open source

The whole project, data, site code, and the pipeline that builds it, is public on GitHub at [best-of-ai/best-of-ai](https://github.com/best-of-ai/best-of-ai). Read more in [How the Site Works](/help/how-the-site-works/) and [Fair, Open Source, and Hand-Picked](/help/curation-policy/).
