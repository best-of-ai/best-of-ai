## What the profession pages are

Instead of browsing by category, you can browse by role: photographer, lawyer, recruiter, and dozens more. Each profession page pulls in every tool from the categories relevant to that job.

## How a tool lands there

A tool doesn't get manually placed on a profession page. It's a byproduct of its category. Every profession lists the categories it cares about, and any tool in one of those categories shows up automatically. Add a tool to the "video editing" category, and it appears on every profession page that includes video editing, no extra step needed.

## The "top picks" tab

At the top of most profession pages sits a short "top picks" tab: a smaller, stronger set of tools for that specific job, mixing hand-picked selections with the highest-ranked tools already pulled from the profession's categories. The rest of the categories sit below it, showing the full, unfiltered grid.

## Don't see your job listed?

Open an issue or a pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/best-of-ai/best-of-ai) with the profession and the categories it should include. That's how new profession pages get added.
