## What's on a company page

Each company page profiles one AI lab or company: founding year, headquarters, founders, and a grid of every tool on the site that it built. A tool links to its company through a single field in its own file, the same way it links to a category.

## Missing a company, or something's wrong?

If a company isn't listed yet, or its details are out of date, open an issue or a pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/best-of-ai/best-of-ai). Add the company's file, then set the `company:` field on the tool's own file to match.
