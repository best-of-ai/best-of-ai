## Submit your tool

There's a dedicated page for this: [Submit Your AI Tool](/submit/). It covers what to include, how long review takes, what happens after approval, and why a submission might get turned down.

## The short version

Fill in the form with your tool's name, website, and a description. A person reviews it, usually within a few days. Once it's approved, it shows up on your category page, any profession pages it fits, alternative comparison pages, and the homepage's recently-added list. There's no fee, and no limit on resubmitting if it's rejected the first time.

Head to [Submit Your AI Tool](/submit/) to get started.
