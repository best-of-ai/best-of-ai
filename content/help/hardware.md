## What's covered

The hardware section lists GPUs and AI accelerators: the chips people actually buy or rent to train and run models. Each entry carries the specs that matter for that decision, including VRAM, memory bandwidth, clock speeds, TFLOPs, and price.

## Why it's here

Best of AI isn't only about apps you click into. Plenty of visitors are trying to decide what hardware to buy before they touch a model at all, so this section covers that decision directly.

## How it connects

Hardware entries aren't tied to individual tools, but they sit next to [Models](/help/models/) for a reason: choosing hardware and choosing a model are usually the same decision. Browse both together if you're trying to work out what you can actually run.
