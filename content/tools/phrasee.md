Phrasee focuses on the words in marketing messages. A team sets up a brand voice, and the platform generates many variations of subject lines, push text, or ads, predicts which will perform best, and tests them against audiences. Results feed back to improve future suggestions.

The company has become part of Jacquard Group, which combines it with other brand messaging tools. Large retailers, financial institutions, and travel brands used it to squeeze more from large campaigns, where small improvements in open rate add up.

Phrasee is sold to enterprise marketing teams on custom pricing. General-purpose chat assistants can now write many variants cheaply, so its value lies in testing and brand control. Alternatives include Persado, Copy.ai, Jasper, Seventh Sense, and Klaviyo's built-in AI. Phrasee suits large marketing teams optimizing messaging.
