UserWay is a web accessibility service. Its widget gives visitors controls to adjust text size, contrast, spacing, and animations, and it applies automated fixes such as adding missing labels. A scanner checks pages against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines and reports issues, so site owners can see problems and prioritize them.

For deeper work, UserWay offers managed services with human experts who audit and fix content, plus tools for documents and PDFs. Reports and accessibility statements help show good faith efforts toward compliance. The product works with common platforms such as WordPress, Shopify, and Wix.

UserWay has a free tier with limited features and paid plans by traffic and services. Accessibility experts and disability advocates warn that overlay widgets cannot fix all barriers and do not by themselves guarantee compliance, so the strongest approach is to repair the underlying site. Alternatives include AudioEye, accessiBe, Deque, Level Access, and Siteimprove. UserWay suits small sites that want a quick start on accessibility.
