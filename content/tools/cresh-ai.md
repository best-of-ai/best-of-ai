Cresh is built for early-stage business research. A user describes an idea or market, and several AI agents search sources, gather information, and assemble a report covering competitors, market size, customer needs, and risks. The aim is a fast first pass that would otherwise require manual searching.

Founders use it to test ideas before investing time, and analysts use it to build starting briefs. Reports from AI research tools can contain errors and unsupported claims, so figures and sources should be checked before decisions or investor materials rely on them.

Independent information is limited. Cresh has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include ChatGPT and Claude research modes, Perplexity, Gemini Deep Research, CB Insights, and Crunchbase. Cresh suits founders doing early market research.
