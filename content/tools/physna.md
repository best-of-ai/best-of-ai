Physna is a geometric search engine. Instead of searching for a part by name, a user uploads a model and the system finds others with similar shapes, even if they were named differently or came from different CAD programs. This helps find existing parts to reuse and detect duplicates.

Manufacturers use it to cut costs from redundant parts, speed up quoting, and manage large catalogs. It also supports analysis such as comparing a design with a reference and checking for intellectual property overlap.

Physna is sold to enterprises on custom contracts. Results depend on how well models are indexed. Alternatives include Siemens and PTC product data tools, CADENAS PARTcommunity, Axiom Search, and in-house systems. Physna suits manufacturers managing big parts libraries.
