The Influencer AI generates a virtual person who can appear in ads, product demos, and social posts. A brand creates or selects a character and the tool produces images and videos of that character holding products, talking to the camera, or appearing in scenes, with consistent looks across content.

The pitch is cost and speed. Producing varied creative with real models requires scheduling, travel, and fees, while AI can generate many variations quickly for testing ads. Features include scripts, voices, and lip-synced video.

Audiences and regulators increasingly expect disclosure of AI-generated people in advertising, and platforms have rules on synthetic content. Quality varies and can feel inauthentic. Alternatives include HeyGen, Creatify, Arcads, Synthesia, and real creators on platforms like Collabstr. The Influencer AI suits marketing teams testing synthetic creative.
