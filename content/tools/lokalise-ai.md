Lokalise helps teams ship products in multiple languages. Developers and designers connect their code repositories and design files, and Lokalise collects the text that needs translating, lets translators and reviewers work on it in an editor with context, and sends finished translations back. Integrations with GitHub, GitLab, Figma, and content systems keep localization in step with development rather than delaying releases.

AI sits at the center of recent versions. It can translate new strings with models that take glossaries, tone, and context into account, flag likely errors, and score translation quality so humans focus on the content that needs attention. Translation memory reuses approved translations for consistency and cost savings, and workflows route content to in-house teams or translation vendors. Reporting shows progress by language.

Lokalise is paid, with plans based on team size and features, and a trial. Machine translation still benefits from human review for brand voice and nuance. Competitors include Crowdin, Phrase, Smartling, Transifex, and Weblate, which is open source. Lokalise is popular with product and engineering teams that want a smooth developer experience. It suits companies localizing apps and websites regularly.
