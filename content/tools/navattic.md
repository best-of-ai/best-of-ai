Navattic helps companies show software without a live demo. A marketer captures a product's screens or pages, adds callouts and steps, and publishes a tour that visitors can click through. Gated or ungated, these demos let prospects see the product before talking to sales.

Analytics show who viewed a demo and how far they went, giving sales a signal about interest. Personalization lets teams tailor tours by account, and integrations send engagement data to CRMs and marketing tools. AI helps create and edit demos and write copy.

Navattic is sold by subscription with plans by features and demos. Demos can go stale as products change. Alternatives include Storylane, Arcade, Supademo, Walnut, and Reprise. Navattic suits B2B marketing and sales teams.
