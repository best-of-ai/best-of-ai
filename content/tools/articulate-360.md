Articulate 360 is a suite for creating online training. Storyline is a slide-based tool for custom interactions, branching scenarios, and simulations, while Rise builds responsive, web-style courses quickly from blocks. Other tools in the suite handle screen recording, video, and review feedback, and a library of templates and stock media is included.

Courses export to standard formats such as SCORM and xAPI, so they work in most learning management systems. Reviewers can comment on a course through a web link, which streamlines feedback. AI Assistant helps write lesson text, quiz questions, and image ideas, speeding up the first draft.

Articulate 360 is sold by subscription, with team plans and discounts for education. Storyline has a learning curve, and the suite is aimed at professionals more than casual users. Alternatives include Adobe Captivate, iSpring Suite, Elucidat, Lectora, and Gomo. Articulate 360 suits instructional designers and training teams.
