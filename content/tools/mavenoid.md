Mavenoid focuses on products people have trouble setting up or fixing. Instead of a long manual or a phone queue, customers use an interactive guide that asks questions, narrows down the problem, and walks through steps with pictures and videos. If the guide cannot solve it, it hands off to support with the details collected.

Manufacturers build these flows from their manuals and support data, and AI helps generate and maintain content, answer free-text questions, and spot recurring issues. Analytics show where customers get stuck, which feeds back to product teams.

Mavenoid is sold to hardware, appliance, and electronics companies on custom contracts. Alternatives include Zendesk Guide, Intercom, Whirlpool-style in-house tools, Helpshift, and Kustomer. Mavenoid suits product makers reducing support volume.
