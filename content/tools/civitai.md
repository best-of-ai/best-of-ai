Civitai is where much of the open-source image generation community meets. Creators upload checkpoints, LoRAs, embeddings, and other add-ons for Stable Diffusion, FLUX, and related models, together with sample images and the prompts used. Others download them to run locally in tools like ComfyUI or Automatic1111, or try them in the browser. Ratings, comments, and example galleries make it easier to judge a model before downloading.

The site offers more than a library. Users can generate images on Civitai's servers using credits, train their own LoRAs from a set of images, and take part in challenges and bounties that reward creators. Articles and guides share techniques, and a point system pays for the use of the site's services. It became the unofficial catalog for community-trained styles, characters, and concepts.

Civitai is free to browse and has paid memberships for more credits and features. Its history is also marked by controversy over adult content and models that imitate real people, and it has restricted such content in response to payment-processor and legal pressure. Downloaded model files should be treated with care, since formats other than safetensors can carry malicious code. Hugging Face is the main alternative for hosting models, and Tensor.Art and SeaArt are similar community sites.
