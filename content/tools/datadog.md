Datadog is software-as-a-service for watching what your systems are doing. You install an agent on servers, containers, or cloud accounts, or connect it through one of hundreds of integrations, and it begins collecting metrics, logs, and traces. Dashboards and alerts then show how hosts, databases, queues, and services are behaving. The value for most teams is having infrastructure monitoring, application performance monitoring, log management, and real user monitoring in one place instead of stitching together separate tools.

AI shows up in a few ways. Watchdog looks for unusual behavior in your data and surfaces anomalies without needing you to write a threshold for every metric. Bits AI is an assistant that answers questions about your environment in plain language, summarizes incidents, and helps investigate root causes. Datadog also has products for security monitoring, CI visibility, and monitoring LLM applications, so the same platform covers the models and agents teams are now shipping.

Pricing is usage-based and split by product, with different meters for hosts, ingested logs, traced requests, and more. That makes it easy to start and easy to overspend, and a lot of the work of running Datadog well is controlling log volume and custom metrics. There is a free trial and a limited free tier, but the product is aimed at companies running production systems. Common alternatives are Grafana, New Relic, Dynatrace, and the open-source stack of Prometheus and OpenTelemetry. Datadog's draw is that it works well out of the box and covers most needs in one product.
