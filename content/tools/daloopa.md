Daloopa serves the finance professionals who build models from public company reports. Instead of typing numbers from filings into Excel, an analyst gets a database of financial line items pulled from documents like quarterly reports and presentations, with each number linked back to its source in the original file.

Updates arrive soon after companies report, so models stay current. An Excel add-in lets analysts pull data into their own templates, and an API and integrations with AI assistants allow data to flow into other workflows. The company has focused on accuracy and traceability, since errors in financial data are costly.

Daloopa is sold to institutional investors, banks, and research firms on subscription. Coverage depends on the companies and sectors included. Alternatives include FactSet, S&P Capital IQ, Bloomberg, AlphaSense, and Visible Alpha. Daloopa suits analysts who build and maintain financial models.
