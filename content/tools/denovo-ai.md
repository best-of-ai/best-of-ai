Denovo aims to shorten the path from idea to launched business. A user describes what they want to start, and the platform helps produce a business plan, suggests names and branding, generates a website, and walks through setup tasks such as registering the company. Putting these steps in one tool reduces the number of services a beginner must find and learn.

The target is people who have never started a company, or who want to test a side project quickly. AI-generated plans and sites provide a starting point, and users still need to validate demand with real customers and check legal requirements in their location.

Independent information is limited, so confirm what is included and what costs extra, such as formation fees. Alternatives include Stripe Atlas, Tiny, LegalZoom, Durable, and general assistants like ChatGPT and Claude for planning. Denovo suits first-time founders who want guidance on early steps.
