Smartcat brings machine and human translation together. A company uploads documents or connects content sources, receives AI translation immediately, and can request human review from freelance linguists or agencies in its marketplace, all with translation memory and glossaries.

Workflow tools manage projects, deadlines, and payments, and the platform supports many file types and integrations. AI agents handle tasks such as quality checks, and the pricing model aims to keep costs lower than traditional agencies.

Smartcat has a free tier and paid plans. Human review adds cost and time. Alternatives include Phrase, Smartling, Lokalise, Crowdin, and Unbabel. Smartcat suits companies that want a mix of AI and human translation.
