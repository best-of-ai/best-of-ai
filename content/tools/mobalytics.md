Mobalytics is a gaming analytics and coaching site. Players link their accounts, and the tool analyzes their games, scoring performance across areas like farming, vision, and teamfights. A profile shows trends over time and compares the player with others at their rank.

Coaching features set it apart. The platform highlights patterns in mistakes and suggests focus areas, offers guides for champions and builds, and provides an overlay with in-game tips. Premium plans add deeper analysis and AI-generated feedback.

Mobalytics is free with a premium tier. Stat-based advice cannot capture everything about a game, and overlays must follow each game's rules. Alternatives include Blitz, U.GG, OP.GG, Porofessor, and Leetify for Counter-Strike. Mobalytics suits competitive players trying to improve.
