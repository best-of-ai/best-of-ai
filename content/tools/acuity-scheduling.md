Acuity Scheduling lets people book time with you through a branded page that you can link to or embed on a website. It syncs with Google, Outlook, and iCloud calendars so you never get double-booked, and clients pick from available slots in their own time zone. It is used by coaches, therapists, tutors, salons, consultants, and other service providers who sell appointments or classes.

Beyond booking, it handles the admin around an appointment. Clients can fill out custom intake forms, pay deposits or full fees, buy packages and gift certificates, and use coupons. Automatic email and text reminders reduce no-shows, and clients can reschedule themselves. Group classes, multiple staff calendars, and multiple locations are supported on higher plans. Integrations connect to Zoom, Stripe, PayPal, QuickBooks, and Zapier.

There is no AI at the center of the product, though Squarespace is adding smart features around scheduling and communication. Plans are paid and scale with the number of calendars and features, and there is a trial. It is more feature-rich than a plain booking link such as Calendly, particularly for taking payments and collecting forms, though Calendly is stronger for sales meetings. Square Appointments, Setmore, and SimplyBook.me are other options for service businesses.
