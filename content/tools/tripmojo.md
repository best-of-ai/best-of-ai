Tripmojo builds one shared itinerary for the whole group. If you allow read-only Gmail access, it pulls flights and stays out of your booking emails automatically. Mojo, the AI assistant, answers questions in the group chat, and family members who aren't traveling get a view-only link to the plan.

Other features include group flight search, automatic web check-in, flight delay alerts, expense tracking with bill splitting, offline access and boarding passes in your phone wallet. You can also book eSIMs, stays and activities inside the app.

The app is free on iOS, Android and the web, and eSIMs, stays and activities are paid for when you book them. It works for groups, families and solo travelers. Its own blog compares it with TripIt and Wanderlog.
