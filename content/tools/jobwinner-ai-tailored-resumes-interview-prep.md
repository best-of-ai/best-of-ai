JobWinner.ai is an AI-powered platform that helps job seekers apply smarter, faster, and with more confidence. Based on a user’s resume and the job description, the platform generates:

- A tailored resume for each job application
- A personalized cover letter
- Job match score, highlighting strengths and gaps
- Practice interview questions and answers
- Job tracker to manage every application

JobWinner is ideal for active job seekers, who need to apply to multiple roles. By automating repetitive tasks, it reduces the time and effort involved in the job application process.
