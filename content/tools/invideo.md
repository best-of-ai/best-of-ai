InVideo AI takes a script or blog post and produces a complete video: it selects stock footage from its library, adds AI-generated voiceover, applies text overlays and transitions, and outputs a finished video ready for YouTube, Instagram, or advertising platforms. The input can be as simple as a topic — "5 tips for remote work productivity" — or as specific as a full written script. The tool assembles the video assets without requiring you to source footage, record narration, or edit in a timeline.

The template library covers the most common video content types in business and marketing: product explainer videos, YouTube listicles, news-format clips, social media ads in various aspect ratios, and educational videos. For content marketing teams producing high volumes of video content, InVideo provides a path from blog content to video without adding video production staff. The AI voiceover covers 50+ voices in multiple languages, and you can edit the script to fix pronunciations or change the pacing.

InVideo offers a free tier with limited exports and an InVideo watermark. Business at $30/month and Unlimited at $60/month remove watermarks, increase export volumes, and provide more premium asset access. The platform targets non-technical content creators and marketing teams rather than professional video producers — someone who needs to produce 20 YouTube videos per month from existing blog content is the core user, not a filmmaker looking for editing tools. The output quality reflects this positioning: competent, watchable, and efficient to produce, but not cinematic.
