InVideo targets creators and marketers who need many videos fast. In InVideo AI, you describe the video you want, such as a product explainer, a faceless YouTube video, or a social ad, and it writes a script, selects stock clips, adds a voiceover and music, and assembles it. You can then give plain-language instructions to change scenes, tone, or length, which makes iteration quick for people without editing skills.

The traditional editor remains for more control. It provides templates, a timeline, text and effects, and a large stock library, and it can turn blog posts and scripts into videos. Voice features include text-to-speech in many languages and voice cloning, and recent versions have added AI-generated video clips and avatars alongside stock footage. Exports are available in formats for different social platforms.

InVideo has a free plan with watermarks and limits and paid plans with higher quality, more stock access, and more generation. Videos built mostly from stock footage and generic narration can look similar to others, so adding original material makes them stand out. Check the licensing terms for commercial use. Competitors include Pictory, Fliki, Canva, CapCut, and Veed, and Runway, Kling, and Veo specialize in generating original video clips. InVideo is strongest for fast, repeatable content.
