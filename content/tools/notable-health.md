Notable automates administrative work in healthcare. Its agents reach out to patients by text, phone, or app to schedule visits, collect forms, confirm insurance, and manage referrals, and they write the results into the health record. The goal is to free staff from repetitive tasks and improve patient access.

Integration is the key difference: agents work inside systems like Epic and others, performing steps in the same workflows staff use. Health systems deploy them for areas such as pre-visit intake, care gap outreach, and prior authorization support.

Notable is sold to health systems on enterprise contracts. Patient communication by AI requires clear disclosure and privacy safeguards. Alternatives include Hippocratic AI, Qventus, Luma Health, Assort Health, and Phreesia. Notable suits health systems automating front-office work.
