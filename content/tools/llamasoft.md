Llamasoft helped companies answer strategic supply chain questions. Where should factories and warehouses be placed? How would costs change if a port closes or demand shifts? Its software builds a digital model of a supply network, then uses optimization and simulation to test alternatives and recommend the best configuration, balancing cost, service levels, and risk. Large manufacturers, retailers, and consumer goods companies used it for network design and planning.

Coupa acquired Llamasoft and folded it into its business spend management platform, so customers can connect supply chain decisions with procurement and spend data. The technology continues under Coupa branding, with capabilities such as network optimization, inventory and sourcing analysis, and demand sensing, supported by machine learning and a digital twin of the supply chain.

The products are sold to large enterprises on custom subscriptions with consulting support. Modeling quality depends on data and assumptions, and implementation takes expertise. Competitors include Kinaxis, o9 Solutions, Blue Yonder, AIMMS, Anaplan, and Optilogic. Llamasoft is referenced today mainly through Coupa's offerings, so check the current names. It suits organizations making big structural supply chain decisions.
