Meltwater is a media monitoring and social listening platform. Users set up searches for brand names, competitors, and topics, and the system collects mentions from news sites, blogs, social networks, forums, and broadcast sources, with alerts and dashboards.

Analytics show volume, sentiment, and reach, and AI highlights trends and unusual spikes, such as the start of a negative story. Tools for PR teams include media databases for finding journalists, and distribution of press releases. Reports demonstrate the impact of campaigns.

Meltwater is sold on custom subscription pricing, which can be high. Coverage varies by source and region, and sentiment analysis is imperfect. Alternatives include Cision, Brandwatch, Talkwalker, Sprout Social listening, and Mention. Meltwater suits communications teams monitoring brand coverage.
