Athletes log activities from phones and wearables, compare segments, and follow training. Paid features include route planning and training analysis, and AI-based summaries of activities.

Data privacy settings are important, because routes can reveal where someone lives.

Garmin Connect and TrainingPeaks are alternatives. Strava suits athletes who want a community.
