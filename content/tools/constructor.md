Constructor serves online retailers that want search and discovery to drive revenue. Its engine powers site search, category pages, and recommendations, ranking products using both shopper behavior and business goals such as margin or inventory. Merchandisers can steer results with rules while the system learns from clicks and purchases.

Additional tools include product quizzes that guide shoppers to the right item, personalization that adapts to each visitor, and analytics that show how discovery affects sales. Generative AI features allow conversational shopping assistants that answer questions and suggest products.

Constructor is sold on enterprise pricing and works with mid-sized and large retailers. It competes in a crowded field and requires integration with a store's catalog and site. Alternatives include Algolia, Bloomreach, Coveo, Klevu, and Searchspring. Constructor suits retailers focused on conversion from search and browse.
