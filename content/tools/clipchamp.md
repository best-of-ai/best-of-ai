Clipchamp is designed for people who need a video without learning professional software. The editor has a drag and drop timeline, trimming and filters, text, transitions, and a library of templates for social posts, ads, and presentations. It records your screen and webcam, so it handles tutorials and quick explainers.

AI features speed up common tasks. Auto Compose builds a draft video from clips and photos, captions are generated automatically, text to speech adds narration, and silence removal tightens recordings. Microsoft folded the tool into its ecosystem, so it integrates with OneDrive and Microsoft 365, and a version of the editor ships with Windows.

Clipchamp has a free tier with watermark-free exports at standard resolution and paid plans for higher quality and extra stock content. It does not match Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve for precision. Alternatives include Canva video, CapCut, iMovie, Descript, and VEED. Clipchamp suits casual creators, students, and small businesses.
