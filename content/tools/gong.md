Gong captures sales conversations and turns them into insight. It records calls and meetings, transcribes them, and pulls in emails and CRM data, then analyzes what is happening across the pipeline: which topics come up in deals that close, how often competitors are mentioned, how much reps talk compared with customers, and where deals are stalling. Managers can search a library of calls instead of relying on reps' summaries.

The AI features save time at several levels. Calls are summarized with key points and next steps, which are written back to the CRM. Deal boards highlight risks, such as no recent contact with a decision-maker, and forecasting tools estimate which deals will close. Coaching tools find moments to review and compare behavior across reps, and newer AI agents help with tasks such as preparing for meetings, researching accounts, and drafting follow-ups.

Gong is sold to revenue teams on annual contracts priced per user plus platform fees, which makes it one of the more expensive tools in the category. It works best in companies with enough sales activity to generate patterns. Recording conversations raises consent requirements, and some customers and reps dislike being recorded. Competitors include Clari, Chorus, Avoma, Fireflies for lighter use, Salesloft, and Outreach. Gong is the market leader in conversation intelligence.
