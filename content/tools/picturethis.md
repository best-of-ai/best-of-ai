Users take a picture, and the app names the plant and offers information on watering, light, and pests. A diagnosis feature spots disease. Subscriptions unlock unlimited use.

Identification can be wrong for similar species.

PlantNet and iNaturalist are free alternatives. PictureThis suits gardeners.
