SalesMagic is an AI-powered sales simulation platform that revolutionizes how sales professionals train and develop their skills. By providing realistic buyer personas and automated analysis of sales interactions, we enable sales teams to practice and improve their skills in a risk-free environment, leading to faster ramp-up times and better performance.

Key Features
- Virtual Role-Play: Make voice calls to any prospect using a familiar video conferencing interface.
- Call Analysis: AI generated analysis and scoring of calls using industry-standard sales methodologies.
- ICP Creation: Generate custom prospects that represent your ideal customer profile (ICP) using generative AI.
- Audio Recordings: Access audio playback of role-play calls in addition to call transcripts.
- Live Call Analysis: Import your existing call data to leverage SalesMagic call analysis.
- Training Modules: Assign practice sessions to sales reps with required proficiency levels required to gain certification.
- Manager Coaching: Perform deep analyses of your team's overall activity and dive into the performance of individual team members.
- Email Analyses: Subscribe to regular email notifications of your team's activity and performance.
- Dial-in Phone Numbers: Support for calling an assigned mobile phone number to perform sales calls training over the phone.
- Webhook Integration: Export your data in bulk or send real-time updates to connected systems like a Learning Management System (LMS).
- Leaderboard: View leaderboards of the highest-performing sales reps in your organization.
- Roles and Permissions: Support for different user roles with a permission model for what each role can do.

Benefits
- Improved Sales Performance: Decrease your sales ramp-up time through targeted and realistic practice with ideal customer profiles.
- Sales Training and Certification: Support on-going certification efforts across your sales organization to ensure consistent and targeted training.
- Sales Manager Coaching: Provide sales managers and enablement teams with deep insights into organizational knowledge and skills.
