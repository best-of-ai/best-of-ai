Suki reduces the paperwork burden on doctors. With patient consent, its ambient system listens to the visit, converts the conversation into a structured clinical note in the specialty's format, and places it in the electronic health record for review. Clinicians can also dictate or use voice commands to look up information, queue orders, and complete tasks hands-free.

The assistant extends beyond notes. It suggests billing codes based on the documentation, helps with information retrieval from the chart, and supports different specialties with tailored templates. Integration with major record systems is a priority, since adoption depends on fitting into existing workflows. Healthcare organizations use it to reduce time spent on documentation and clinician burnout.

Suki is sold to health systems and practices on a subscription basis, with pricing per clinician. Ambient AI can make mistakes or add content that was not said, so clinicians must review every note, and patient consent and privacy rules apply. Competitors include Abridge, Nuance DAX in Microsoft Dragon Copilot, Ambience Healthcare, Nabla, Heidi Health, and built-in tools from record vendors. Suki suits clinicians wanting a flexible voice assistant.
