Lindy lets people create agents without code. A user describes a task or chooses a template, connects apps like Gmail, Slack, and a CRM, and defines triggers and steps. The Lindy then runs on its own: sorting an inbox, drafting replies, booking meetings, or updating records.

Workflows can include several steps with conditions, and Lindies can hand tasks to each other or ask a human for approval. A phone agent product handles calls, and a large catalog of integrations connects to business tools.

Lindy has a free tier and paid plans based on task credits. Agents that act on email and calendars need careful permissions and review of outputs. This page covers the core platform, and a second listing for Lindy AI covers the same product. Alternatives include Zapier Agents, Relay.app, Cassidy, Make, and n8n. Lindy suits teams automating routine knowledge work.
