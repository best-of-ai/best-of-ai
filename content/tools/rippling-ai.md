Rippling combines employee data with the systems that depend on it. When a person joins, Rippling creates their payroll record, sets up benefits, provisions laptops and software accounts, and orders equipment, using one source of truth. Changes in the employee record cascade across the systems.

Modules cover HR, payroll in many countries, time and attendance, benefits, device and app management, and corporate cards and expenses. A workflow engine lets admins automate approvals and tasks, and AI assistants answer questions and draft content.

Rippling is sold per employee per module. It spans a wide scope, which suits growing companies but can cost more than single-purpose tools. Alternatives include Gusto, Deel, Workday, HiBob, and BambooHR. Rippling suits mid-sized companies wanting HR and IT together.
