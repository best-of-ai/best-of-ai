Qure.ai makes AI tools for radiology. Its software reads medical images and highlights suspected abnormalities, such as signs of tuberculosis or pneumonia on a chest X-ray, or a hemorrhage on a head CT. The software returns an annotated image and a short summary, so radiologists and clinicians can prioritize urgent cases.

The company is known for working in settings with few radiologists. Screening programs for tuberculosis use its tools to triage chest X-rays quickly, and hospitals use it to speed up emergency reads. It has regulatory clearances in several markets and integrates with PACS systems, the software that stores and displays medical images.

Qure.ai is sold to hospitals, imaging providers, and public health programs. AI findings are decision support, and clinicians remain responsible for diagnosis. Performance can vary across populations and equipment, so validation matters. Competitors include Aidoc, Lunit, Annalise.ai, and Viz.ai. Qure.ai suits health systems and screening programs that need imaging support.
