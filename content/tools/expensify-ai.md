Expensify is known for making expense reports less painful. Employees photograph receipts or forward them by email, SmartScan extracts the merchant, date, and amount, and the app builds a report for submission. Managers approve in the app, and approved expenses sync to accounting software.

Other features cover company spending. The Expensify Card gives employees corporate cards with real-time receipts matching, policies flag out-of-bounds expenses automatically, and mileage tracking and per diems handle travel. AI helps categorize spending and spot policy issues.

Expensify has free options for small use and paid plans per user. It is aimed at small and mid-sized businesses. Alternatives include Ramp, Brex, Emburse Certify, Zoho Expense, and SAP Concur for large enterprises. Expensify suits small and mid-sized teams wanting simple expense management.
