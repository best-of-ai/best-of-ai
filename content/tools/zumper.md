Zumper lists rentals with photos, prices, and neighborhood details. Renters filter by price, bedrooms, pets, and commute, save searches, and message landlords. A universal application and tools for screening speed up the process.

Recommendations use preferences and behavior, and data on rents helps renters understand local prices. Landlords and property managers pay to list and get leads.

Zumper is free for renters. Listings can be outdated, and renters should watch for scams. Alternatives include Zillow Rentals, Apartments.com, Rent.com, Trulia, and Craigslist. Zumper suits renters in major US cities.
