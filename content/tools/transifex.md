Transifex helps product teams translate content into many languages. Developers connect repositories such as GitHub and GitLab, or use a command-line tool, and new text is sent to Transifex for translation. Translators and reviewers work in an online editor with translation memory, glossaries, and context, and approved translations flow back to the codebase.

A distinctive piece is Transifex Native, which lets apps fetch translations through an SDK, so updating a translation doesn't require a new release. AI and machine translation pre-translate content, and quality checks flag issues, while workflows route text through reviewers or professional translation vendors. Integrations cover design tools, content management systems, and help centers.

Transifex has a free trial and paid plans scaled by usage and features, with enterprise options. As with all machine translation, human review matters for brand voice and technical accuracy. Alternatives include Crowdin, Lokalise, Phrase, Smartling, and open-source Weblate. Transifex suits software companies that want developer-friendly localization with flexible delivery.
