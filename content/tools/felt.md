Users import GIS files and spreadsheets, draw on the map, and add comments. Maps share by link and embed in websites. AI features can help style layers and transform data from a plain-language request.

It is lighter than desktop GIS and lacks some advanced analysis. A free plan has limits.

QGIS and ArcGIS offer deeper analysis. Felt suits planners and cartographers who share maps with non-specialists.
