DeepL built its reputation on translations that read more naturally than many competitors, especially for European languages. You paste text or upload a document, such as a Word file, PDF, or PowerPoint, and get a translation that keeps the formatting. Options let you choose formal or informal tone, add glossaries for specific terms, and see alternative wordings for phrases.

The product has expanded into a broader language platform. DeepL Write improves and rephrases text in the same language, DeepL Voice translates live conversations and meetings, and browser extensions and desktop apps translate as you work. An API serves developers and enterprises, and newer agent features automate tasks. Business plans add data security, which promises that texts are not used for training, plus team glossaries.

DeepL has a free tier with limits on text and documents and paid plans for higher usage and features. It supports fewer languages than Google Translate, though the list has grown. For critical text such as legal or medical content, human translators should check the output. Alternatives include Google Translate, Microsoft Translator, Amazon Translate, and large language models such as ChatGPT and Claude, which can translate with more context. DeepL suits people who value fluent translations.
