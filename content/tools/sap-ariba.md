SAP Ariba helps large organizations buy what they need in an organized way. Procurement teams use it to find and evaluate suppliers, run sourcing events, manage contracts, process purchase orders and invoices, and monitor supplier risk. The Ariba Network connects buyers and suppliers electronically, so orders and invoices flow between systems without manual entry.

AI is being added to reduce manual work. SAP's assistant, Joule, can help users search for information, draft sourcing documents, and answer questions about spend, and features analyze contracts, recommend suppliers, and flag unusual transactions. Integration with SAP's ERP and other systems provides a connected view of purchasing, and spend analytics reveal savings opportunities.

SAP Ariba is sold to enterprises on custom contracts, often with long implementations, and suppliers may pay fees to participate on the network. It's most natural for organizations already using SAP. Competitors include Coupa, Jaggaer, GEP SMART, Oracle Procurement, and Zip for modern procurement orchestration. Ariba suits large organizations with complex purchasing and many suppliers.
