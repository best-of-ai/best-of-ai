Navina addresses chart review. Before a patient visit, a clinician or care team would normally dig through records from different sources. Navina collects data from the electronic health record, outside providers, and claims, summarizes it, and highlights what matters, such as conditions that may be undocumented or screenings that are overdue.

That helps with both quality of care and risk adjustment, which affects payment under value-based contracts. Insights appear in the clinician's workflow so they can act during the visit. Health systems and value-based care groups are the main customers.

Navina is sold to healthcare organizations on custom pricing. AI-suggested diagnoses must be verified by clinicians, and documentation practices need compliance oversight. Alternatives include Epic's tools, Innovaccer, Health Gorilla, Abridge, and Notable. Navina suits primary care organizations working with complex records.
