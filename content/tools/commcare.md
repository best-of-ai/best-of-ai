Program teams design forms and workflows without coding. Workers collect data offline on a phone and sync when connected. Supervisors see dashboards, and case management supports reminders for visits.

A free plan is available for small projects, and paid plans add more users and features.

KoBoToolbox and ODK are open-source options for data collection. CommCare suits community health workers and the programs that manage them.
