Veo 2 generated short high-resolution clips from text or image prompts. It stood out for understanding cinematic language, such as lens choices and camera moves, and for physics that looked plausible, with fewer odd distortions.

It was offered through Google's creative tools and API before being succeeded by Veo 3, which added native audio and further improvements. Many users moved to the newer model for quality and sound.

Check Google's documentation for the currently supported models. Alternatives include Veo 3, Runway, Kling, Luma, and Pika. Veo 2 is of interest mainly to people comparing generations of video models.
