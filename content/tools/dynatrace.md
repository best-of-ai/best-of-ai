Dynatrace is one of the established names in monitoring large, complex IT environments. A single agent, OneAgent, installs on servers, containers, and cloud services and automatically discovers applications and their dependencies, then collects metrics, traces, logs, and user experience data without much manual setup. A model of how components relate, kept up to date automatically, lets the platform see how a slowdown in one service affects others.

Its distinguishing feature is Davis AI. Instead of only alerting when thresholds are crossed, Davis uses causal analysis to identify the likely root cause of a problem and the business impact, reducing the pile of alerts into a smaller number of actionable issues. Newer generative AI features let users ask questions in natural language, and the platform adds automation that can trigger remediation. Dynatrace also covers security, application vulnerabilities, and monitoring of AI workloads.

Dynatrace is sold by subscription based on consumption, and is priced for medium and large enterprises, which makes it more expensive than open-source stacks or lighter tools. It performs best in complex environments with many interdependent services. Datadog, New Relic, and Splunk are the main competitors, Grafana and OpenTelemetry-based setups are the open-source route, and Elastic covers logs and search. Buyers often compare automation depth and total cost over time.
