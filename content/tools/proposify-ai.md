Proposify helps agencies and service companies create and send proposals. Teams build templates with sections, pricing tables, and brand styling, assemble a proposal quickly, and send it to clients who can review, comment, and sign electronically. Notifications show when a proposal is opened.

AI features help with writing content and suggest improvements based on what has worked in past proposals. Analytics reveal how long clients spend on each section, informing follow-ups. Integrations connect with CRMs and billing.

Proposify is paid, with plans by users and features. Alternatives include PandaDoc, Qwilr, Better Proposals, HoneyBook, and Bonsai. Proposify suits agencies and consultants who send many proposals.
