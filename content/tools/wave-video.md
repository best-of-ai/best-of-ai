Wave.video bundles several tools that marketers often use separately. The editor handles videos from templates, with stock footage, music, and text animations for social posts, ads, and promos. A recorder captures your screen or webcam, a live streaming studio broadcasts to multiple platforms, and a hosting service stores and embeds videos with customizable players and calls to action.

AI features help produce videos faster. Tools convert text or a blog post into a video draft, generate captions and subtitles, resize videos for different social formats, and create voiceovers. Brand kits keep colors and logos consistent, and integrations link with email platforms and social networks. It's targeted at small businesses, agencies, and content teams.

Wave.video has a free plan with watermarks and limits, and paid plans that add resolution, hosting, and branding features. It's less powerful than a desktop editor, and the stock library is smaller than those of dedicated stock sites. Alternatives include Canva, InVideo, VEED, FlexClip, and Vimeo for hosting and editing together. Wave.video suits small marketing teams that want an all-in-one video toolkit.
