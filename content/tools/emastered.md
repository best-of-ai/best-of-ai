eMastered applies automatic mastering to a mixed track. A musician uploads the song, the service analyzes it, and an algorithm processes it for balance, loudness, and clarity so it sounds polished on streaming platforms. Users can adjust settings such as intensity, EQ, and reference tracks before downloading.

It is designed for independent artists and producers who cannot afford a mastering engineer for every release. The process takes minutes and costs less than hiring a professional, and a subscription can cover unlimited masters on some plans.

Automated mastering works best on well-mixed tracks and cannot fix serious mix problems. For important releases, many artists still hire a human engineer. eMastered has a free preview and paid plans. Alternatives include LANDR, CloudBounce, iZotope Ozone, BandLab Mastering, and human mastering studios. eMastered suits independent musicians who want quick mastering.
