RapidAPI made discovering APIs easier. Developers browse a catalog of thousands of APIs across categories such as weather, finance, sports, language, and AI, test them in the browser, and subscribe with a single key and billing account. This saves the effort of signing up with every provider separately, and API publishers get a distribution channel and billing.

The company also offered products for businesses, including an enterprise hub for managing internal and partner APIs, and testing tools. Nokia acquired the technology and marketplace to support its efforts to expose telecom network capabilities to developers through APIs, and the public marketplace is expected to continue much as before with some rebranding.

The marketplace is free to browse, and each API has its own pricing, often with free tiers. Quality varies among listings, and reliance on third-party APIs adds risk if a provider changes or disappears. Alternatives include Postman's API Network, APILayer, direct provider sites, Apify for scraping, and OpenRouter for AI models. Check the current brand and terms under Nokia before building on it. RapidAPI suits developers who want to experiment with many APIs quickly.
