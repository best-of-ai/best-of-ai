SmartRecruiters is an applicant tracking system built for large companies. Recruiters post jobs to many boards, receive and manage applications, collaborate with hiring managers on evaluations, schedule interviews, and extend offers, all in one workflow. A marketplace of integrations connects background checks, assessments, video interviewing, and HR systems, which suits global companies with complex hiring needs.

AI features support sourcing and screening. The platform recommends candidates from existing applicants and external pools, scores fit against job requirements, and helps with scheduling and communication. Its AI assistant can draft job descriptions and summarize candidate profiles, and analytics show the hiring funnel, time to fill, and diversity metrics. The company states it emphasizes transparency in how AI recommendations are made.

SmartRecruiters is sold on subscription to mid-sized and large enterprises with custom pricing. SAP announced plans to acquire it to combine with SuccessFactors, so packaging may change over time. AI in hiring is subject to rules on bias and automated decision-making in many places. Competitors include Greenhouse, Lever, Workday Recruiting, iCIMS, and Ashby. SmartRecruiters suits large employers seeking a flexible recruiting platform.
