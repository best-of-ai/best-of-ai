Avoma joins sales, customer success, and recruiting meetings on Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams, then records and transcribes them and produces notes organized by meeting type. Instead of a generic summary, it uses templates suited to the call, such as a discovery call or a customer check-in, and pulls out action items, objections, and next steps. Notes and key fields can be pushed into CRMs like Salesforce and HubSpot, which saves reps from doing data entry after every call.

The conversation intelligence tools analyze talk time, questions asked, and topics covered, and managers can score calls against a framework and leave feedback for coaching. Meeting scheduling and lead routing make it easier to book prospects, and pipeline and forecasting views use meeting data to show deal health. Search across all recordings makes it simple to find what a customer said about pricing or a competitor.

Avoma has a free tier with limited use and paid plans priced per user, with conversation intelligence as an add-on, plus a free trial. Recording calls carries consent and privacy responsibilities, which depend on where participants are located. Gong and Chorus lead the enterprise end of conversation intelligence at higher prices, Fireflies and Otter are general-purpose note takers, and Fathom has a generous free plan. Avoma suits small and mid-sized revenue teams.
