Auditors use CaseWare Working Papers to plan engagements, document procedures, and tie financial statement balances to supporting files. CaseWare IDEA imports full data sets and tests them for gaps, duplicates, and unusual entries, which supports analysis beyond sampling.

Cloud versions let teams review work remotely. Firms of all sizes use the products, and staff generally need training before they are productive.

AuditBoard and Workiva cover related ground for internal audit and reporting. CaseWare suits external auditors and accountants.
