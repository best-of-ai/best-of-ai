AppFolio runs the daily operations of a property management business. Staff list vacancies on rental sites, screen applicants, sign leases, collect rent online, and track maintenance requests and vendors. Owners receive statements and reports, and residents use a portal and app to pay and submit requests.

AI features take over routine communication. An assistant answers common tenant questions by text at any hour, qualifies leads, schedules showings, and flags maintenance requests that need attention. Analytics help managers see trends in occupancy, delinquency, and costs, and accounting tools are designed for property trust accounting.

AppFolio is sold by subscription with pricing based on the number of units and modules, typically to mid-sized and larger property managers. Smaller landlords may find it more than they need. Alternatives include Buildium, Yardi, RealPage, DoorLoop, and Rent Manager. AppFolio suits property managers who want a modern, all-in-one platform.
