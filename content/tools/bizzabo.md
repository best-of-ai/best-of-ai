Bizzabo is a software platform for corporate and large-scale event teams. Planners build event websites, sell tickets and handle registration, manage speakers and agendas, and track who attends. On site, it supports badge printing and check-in, and a mobile app lets attendees see the schedule, message each other, and use polls or Q&A during sessions. For virtual and hybrid events, it can stream sessions and bring remote attendees into the same experience.

Analytics are a key selling point. Data from registration, app use, and session attendance flows into dashboards, so organizers can measure engagement and tie the event to leads and revenue in tools like Salesforce and HubSpot. AI features suggest attendee matches for networking, help draft event content, and summarize engagement. The platform positions an event as a marketing channel, not just a logistics task.

Bizzabo is sold by custom quote to marketing and events teams, with pricing based on scale and modules, and it is not suited to small one-off gatherings. Competitors include Cvent, the largest enterprise event suite, as well as Hopin-style virtual tools, Eventbrite for ticketing, and Swapcard for networking-focused events. A buyer should check support for the specific types of events they run, since requirements differ between conferences, trade shows, and internal meetings.
