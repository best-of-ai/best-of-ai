Zapier's core model is trigger-and-action: when something happens in one app, do something in another. A new form submission creates a CRM contact. A Stripe payment emails a receipt and adds a row to a spreadsheet. A Slack message tagged with a keyword creates a Trello card. With 6,000+ app integrations, the combinations are essentially unlimited for business software. The no-code builder lets non-technical users set these up without any developer involvement.

The AI layer added in 2023 made Zapier significantly more capable. Natural language workflow creation means you describe what you want to automate and Zapier builds the Zap — including choosing the right trigger, action, and field mappings. The AI Action step lets you include ChatGPT or Claude in the middle of a workflow — summarise an email before forwarding it, classify a support ticket before routing it, or generate a personalised reply based on CRM data. This turns Zapier from a data plumbing tool into a genuine AI automation platform.

Zapier's free plan allows five active Zaps with single-step actions. Starter at $19.99/month enables multi-step Zaps and filter logic; Professional and Team plans scale based on task volume and user count. Heavy automation users run into monthly task limits, so teams often estimate their usage carefully before choosing a plan tier. The platform's longevity and reliability are major selling points — Zapier has been running since 2011 and processes billions of tasks monthly, with uptime that businesses depend on.
