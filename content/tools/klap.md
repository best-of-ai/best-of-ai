Klap helps creators and marketers turn long videos into shorts. A user uploads a video or pastes a link, and the tool picks promising segments, crops them to vertical format while tracking the speaker, and adds captions. A score estimates how likely each clip is to perform.

The clips can be edited to adjust timing, captions, and layout, and published or exported. Podcasters, coaches, webinar hosts, and YouTubers use it to get more mileage from content they already have. Language support lets clips be captioned or dubbed for other audiences.

Klap has a free trial and paid plans by minutes of video. Automatic selection does not always match what a creator would choose, so review matters. Alternatives include OpusClip, Vizard, Descript, Munch, and ClipFinder. Klap suits creators repurposing long content.
