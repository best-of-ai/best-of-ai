Heuritech applies computer vision to fashion trends. Its models scan images from social platforms and recognize garments, colors, prints, and shapes, then measure how frequently each appears and how quickly that is changing. The output is a forecast of which items will grow or fade in a market.

Brands use the forecasts to decide what to design, produce, and stock, which can reduce unsold inventory. Reports are segmented by region, age group, and category, and the platform includes tools for tracking competitors and monitoring product performance against trends.

Heuritech is sold to fashion and luxury brands on enterprise contracts. Social media visibility is only one signal of demand, so forecasts work best combined with sales data and merchant judgment. Alternatives include WGSN, Edited, Trendalytics, Stylumia, and Lyst. Heuritech suits fashion planners and buyers.
