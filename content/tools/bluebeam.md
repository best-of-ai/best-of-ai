Bluebeam Revu is a PDF tool built for construction. Teams review plans on screen, add markups such as clouds, callouts, and stamps, and measure lengths, areas, and counts directly on drawings to prepare quantity takeoffs for estimates. Custom tool sets store commonly used markups.

Collaboration is a big part. Studio Sessions let many people mark up the same document at the same time, and Studio Projects store drawing sets in the cloud. Overlay and comparison features show what changed between revisions, with AI helping to line up sheets and highlight differences, which reduces missed updates.

Bluebeam is sold by subscription and widely used by architects, engineers, and contractors. It has a learning curve and mainly works with 2D drawings, with limits on 3D models. Alternatives include Adobe Acrobat, PlanGrid (Autodesk Build), Procore, Kofax Power PDF, and Trimble Connect. Bluebeam suits construction professionals who work from drawings.
