Azure AI Speech brings Microsoft's speech technology into applications. Speech-to-text transcribes audio in real time or from recordings in many languages, with options for custom vocabulary and speaker separation. Text-to-speech offers a large library of neural voices in many languages and styles, with control over tone, speed, and emotion using SSML. Real-time speech translation converts spoken language into another language for captions or voice.

Additional features serve specialized needs. Custom Neural Voice lets approved customers build a branded voice from recorded samples, subject to a restricted-access process to prevent misuse. Voice live and conversational features support building voice agents, and avatar capabilities create talking presenters from text. Container deployments allow running models on premises or at the edge, and the service integrates with Azure OpenAI and other Azure AI tools.

Azure AI Speech is billed by usage, such as per hour of audio transcribed or per characters synthesized, with a free tier. Accuracy varies by language and audio quality, and recording audio brings consent and privacy obligations. Alternatives include Google Cloud Speech-to-Text, Amazon Polly and Transcribe, Deepgram, AssemblyAI, and ElevenLabs. Azure AI Speech suits enterprises standardizing on Microsoft's cloud.
