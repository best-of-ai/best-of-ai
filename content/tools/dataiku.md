Dataiku aims to let analysts, data scientists, and engineers work in the same environment. Teams connect to data sources, clean and prepare data with visual recipes or code, build models, and deploy them as applications or services. The visual interface lowers the barrier for business users, while coders can use notebooks and libraries.

The platform has added tools for generative AI, including connecting to language models, building retrieval applications, and creating agents. Governance features track models and projects, assess risk, and document decisions, which large organizations need to meet internal and regulatory requirements.

Dataiku is sold to enterprises on subscription, with a free edition for learning. Adoption requires organizational change, as the platform works best when many roles use it. Alternatives include Databricks, DataRobot, Alteryx, H2O.ai, and cloud services such as Vertex AI and SageMaker. Dataiku suits large organizations bringing data teams together.
