Runway builds tools for creative professionals. Its generative video models create clips from text or images, and a suite of editing tools handles tasks like removing backgrounds, changing motion, extending shots, and applying styles. Everything runs in the browser.

The company began with accessible video editing and became a leader in generative video, working with artists and studios. Newer models offer longer clips, better consistency, and controls for camera and motion, and the platform includes workflows for combining generation with traditional editing.

Runway has a free tier with limited credits and paid plans. Rights and plan terms matter for commercial use. Alternatives include Pika, Luma, Kling, Google Veo, and Adobe Firefly. Runway suits filmmakers, designers, and creators.
