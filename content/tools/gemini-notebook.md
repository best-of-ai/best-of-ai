Gemini Notebook is the product Google launched as NotebookLM. Google renamed it on July 16, 2026. It stays a standalone app and also works inside the Gemini app, where notebooks sync between the two. Google has said notebooks are coming to AI Mode in Search.

You create a notebook, add sources such as PDFs, Google Docs, web pages, and YouTube videos, and the AI answers using only that material, with citations that point to the passages it used. It can also turn sources into audio and video summaries, study guides, and similar formats. Each notebook now gets a secure cloud computer that can write and run code to analyze data from your sources. At the July launch Google offered it to AI Ultra subscribers first, with AI Pro users to follow.

Gemini Notebook is free to start with a Google account. Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra plans raise the usage limits, and there are versions for work and school accounts. Answers depend on the sources you add, and generated audio can sound sure of itself while missing nuance, so check important details against the originals. Alternatives include ChatGPT, Claude, Humata, and Notion AI. Gemini Notebook suits students and researchers who want answers grounded in their own material.
