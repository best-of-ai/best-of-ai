Revenue.io, formerly RingDNA, combines calling with conversation intelligence. During a call, it listens and surfaces prompts, such as a competitor battlecard or the right answer to a pricing objection. After the call, it transcribes, summarizes, and scores conversations against a playbook.

Managers get coaching insights and forecasting signals. The platform includes a dialer and integrates with Salesforce, so call data flows into the CRM. AI assistants draft follow-ups and update records.

Revenue.io is sold on custom pricing to sales teams. Real-time prompts help most when well tuned. Alternatives include Gong, Balto, Cresta, Salesloft, and Outreach. Revenue.io suits sales teams wanting live call coaching.
