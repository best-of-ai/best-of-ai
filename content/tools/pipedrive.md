Pipedrive is designed by and for salespeople, with the pipeline at the center. Deals appear as cards that move across stages, from first contact to closed, so reps can see at a glance what needs attention. Each deal has activities, emails, notes, and files attached, and the system reminds you of follow-ups so nothing slips. Compared with big CRM suites, the interface is clean and quick to learn, which suits small and growing sales teams.

Around the pipeline are tools for the rest of the sales process. Email sync and templates, scheduling links, a lead inbox, web forms, live chat, and a campaign tool handle outreach, while workflow automations assign leads and create tasks. Reporting and forecasting dashboards track conversion and revenue. An AI assistant analyzes deal history and activity to suggest what to do next, drafts and summarizes emails, and helps with data entry, and a marketplace of integrations connects it to other tools.

Pipedrive has no free plan but offers a free trial, and its tiers add automation, reporting, and customization as you move up. Costs are per user per month, and some add-ons are billed separately. It is easier and cheaper than Salesforce, with fewer options for complicated processes. HubSpot CRM has a more generous free tier and broader marketing tools, and Zoho CRM and Close are also common choices for small teams.
