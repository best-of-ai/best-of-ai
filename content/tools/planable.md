Planable is built around approval workflows. Marketers create posts in a view that looks like the final social post, share it with teammates or clients, collect comments and approvals, and schedule it once signed off. This replaces long email chains and screenshots.

Features include a content calendar, multiple approval stages, and support for many networks. AI tools help draft captions and ideas. Agencies find it useful for client work where sign-off is required.

Planable has a free plan with limits and paid tiers by users and workspaces. It focuses on planning and collaboration more than deep analytics or social listening. Alternatives include Loomly, Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Buffer, and Later. Planable suits agencies and teams that need approvals.
