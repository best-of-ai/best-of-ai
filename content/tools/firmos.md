FirmOS is an AI-powered automation platform designed for accounting firms. It eliminates repetitive tasks, streamlines operations, and helps firms scale with intelligent systems for client acquisition, talent management, and operational efficiency.

Features
Business Development
- Make client acquisition predictable with AI-driven marketing and sales automation.
- Client Segmentation Analysis – Identify and target high-value clients.
- Automated Lead Scraping – Use AI to gather and enrich lead data.
- Niche Market Branding – Attract premium clients with strategic positioning.
- End-to-End Sales Automations – Automate follow-ups and outreach.
- Proof of Concept System – Collect and showcase success stories automatically.

Talent
- AI-driven hiring to attract, assess, and retain top talent effortlessly.
- Role Alignment Framework – Place the right people in the right roles.
- Proactive Hiring Roadmap – Plan critical hires in advance.
- Purpose Setting Guide – Define clear goals and values for team alignment.
- AI Careers Agent – Automate job descriptions and postings.
- Talent Screening System – Use AI-driven interviews and assessments.

Operations
- Turn scattered workflows into efficient, automated systems with AI-powered insights.
- Process Discovery System – Map workflows for efficiency.
- Internal Communication System – Keep teams aligned with structured meetings.
- Bi-Weekly Strategy Sessions – Solve roadblocks in focused 30-minute sessions.
- SOP Generator – Create clear, step-by-step SOPs.
- Master KPI Dashboard – Track 70+ real-time financial and operational metrics.

How It Works
1. Simple 5-Step Automation Cycle
2. Data Collection – Gather key data from existing systems.
3. Identify Roadblocks – Uncover inefficiencies and optimize workflows.
4. Goal Setting & Custom Roadmap – Set 90-day objectives with a tailored plan.
5. System Implementation & Management – Deploy AI-driven solutions with bi-weekly updates.
6. Ongoing Optimization – Monitor KPIs and refine processes for continuous improvement.

Benefits
1. Done-for-You Automation for a More Scalable Firm
2. Save Time – Automate repetitive tasks and focus on high-value work.
3. Take Control – Reduce workload and free up time for growth.
4. Build a Stronger Team – Streamline hiring and optimize roles.
5. Avoid Burnout – Step away from daily tasks without losing efficiency.
6. Future-Proof Your Firm – Protect processes and brands for long-term success.
