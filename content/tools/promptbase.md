PromptBase turned prompts into products. Creators craft prompts that reliably produce a type of result, such as a particular art style, a logo format, or a structured business document, and list them with example outputs. Buyers pay a small amount to get the text, then use it with the relevant AI tool, saving time on trial and error.

The marketplace organizes prompts by model and category, shows sample images, and uses ratings and reviews to help buyers choose. It also supports custom prompt requests and tools for prompt creators to test and sell their work. The business sits on a belief that skilled prompting has value, particularly for image generators where small wording changes make a big difference.

Browsing is free, and each prompt has its own price, with the site taking a cut. The value of paid prompts has fallen as AI tools have become better at following plain instructions and as free prompt libraries have grown. Check example outputs and reviews before buying. Alternatives include FlowGPT, PromptHero, free community collections on Reddit and GitHub, and built-in prompt features in ChatGPT and Claude. PromptBase suits people who want ready-made prompts for image generators.
