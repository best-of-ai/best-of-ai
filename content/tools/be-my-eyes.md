Be My Eyes connects people who cannot see well with help. In the app, a blind or low-vision user can start a video call, and a sighted volunteer sees through the camera and helps with a task such as reading an expiry date, matching colors, finding a dropped item, or navigating an unfamiliar place. Millions of volunteers around the world have signed up, which means that help is usually available quickly, at any hour.

AI has expanded the options. Be My AI lets users take a photo and receive a detailed description, ask follow-up questions, and read text from labels, menus, and screens, without needing to wait for a volunteer. It uses a vision language model and has been welcomed for independence and privacy. The app also provides a way to reach customer support from partner companies, with trained agents who can see through the camera.

Be My Eyes is free for users. AI descriptions can contain errors, so important details such as medication instructions should be double-checked, and the app should not be relied on for safety-critical navigation. Alternatives include Microsoft's Seeing AI, Google's Lookout, Aira, which offers paid professional assistants, and built-in iPhone accessibility features. Be My Eyes is widely used and highly regarded in the blind community.
