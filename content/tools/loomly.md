Loomly supports the planning side of social media. Teams build a content calendar, get post ideas from trends and prompts, draft posts with images and links, and route them through an approval workflow before publishing. A brand asset library keeps logos and images handy.

The platform previews how a post will look on each network and schedules publishing. Analytics show performance, and ad management features support boosted posts. AI assists with captions and ideas.

Loomly is paid, with plans by users and social accounts, and a trial. It is simpler than enterprise suites. Alternatives include Buffer, Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Later, and Metricool. Loomly suits agencies and marketing teams that want structured planning.
