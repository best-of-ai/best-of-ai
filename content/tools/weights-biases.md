Weights & Biases, often shortened to W&B, grew out of a simple need. Machine learning engineers run many experiments with different settings and want to compare them. By adding a few lines of code to a training script, you log metrics, hyperparameters, system usage, and sample outputs to a dashboard, and see runs side by side with charts and tables. That replaces scattered notebooks and spreadsheets.

The platform has grown beyond tracking. Artifacts version datasets and models so you can trace what produced a result, Sweeps automate hyperparameter searches, Reports let teams share findings, and a model registry supports handoff to deployment. Weave covers LLM apps with tracing and evaluation, and W&B Inference offers hosted model access.

CoreWeave, a GPU cloud company, completed its acquisition of Weights & Biases, and the product continues to work with other clouds. W&B has a free tier for personal use and paid plans for teams, with self-hosted options for enterprises. Alternatives include MLflow, Neptune, Comet, TensorBoard, and ClearML. W&B suits researchers and ML teams who run many experiments.
