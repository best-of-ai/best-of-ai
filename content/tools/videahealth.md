VideaHealth is an AI system for dental radiographs. It reviews X-rays as they are taken and highlights suspected decay, bone loss, and other issues on the image.

It is aimed at practices and dental service organizations that want a second read and a shared standard of diagnosis, and the marked images help when explaining treatment to patients.

VideaHealth is sold on subscription. Dentists must confirm every finding. Alternatives include Overjet, Pearl, and Diagnocat. VideaHealth suits practices that want radiograph review built into daily workflow.
