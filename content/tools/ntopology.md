nTopology, now often shortened to nTop, is engineering design software. Traditional CAD builds shapes from sketches and features, while nTop uses fields and rules to generate complex internal structures, such as lattices whose density changes with stress. This produces lighter and stronger parts that suit 3D printing.

Workflows can be saved and reused, so a design process is captured as a repeatable recipe that updates when inputs change. It connects to simulation and manufacturing tools, and supports optimization where the software generates shapes that meet loads and constraints.

nTop is sold on subscription to aerospace, medical, automotive, and consumer product companies. It requires engineering expertise and works alongside CAD, not in place of it. Alternatives include Autodesk Fusion generative design, Altair Inspire, Siemens NX, Carbon design tools, and Hyperganic. nTopology suits engineers designing for additive manufacturing.
