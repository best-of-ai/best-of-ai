Insilico Medicine applies generative AI across the drug discovery process. PandaOmics analyzes biological data and literature to suggest disease targets, Chemistry42 designs novel molecules that bind to those targets, and other tools predict clinical trial outcomes and automate parts of the process. The idea is to cut the time and cost needed to move from an idea to a candidate drug.

The company has taken its approach into the clinic, with its own pipeline of drug candidates in areas such as fibrosis, cancer, and age-related diseases. One of its candidates, designed with the platform for a lung disease, advanced into human trials, which is regarded as an important test of whether AI-designed drugs can succeed. It also partners with pharmaceutical firms and sells software and services.

Insilico is a private company with commercial software offerings and collaborations, with pricing by custom agreement. As with all AI drug discovery, the main questions are whether the molecules prove safe and effective in trials, which takes years and often fails regardless of design method. Peers include Recursion, Isomorphic Labs, Schrödinger, BenevolentAI, and Exscientia, now part of Recursion. Insilico suits pharma and biotech partners.
