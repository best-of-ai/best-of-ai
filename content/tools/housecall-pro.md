Housecall Pro helps small service companies run their operations. Office staff schedule jobs and dispatch technicians on a calendar and map, customers receive confirmations and arrival notifications, and technicians use a mobile app to see their jobs, send estimates, collect signatures, and take payments on site. Invoices and payments flow into accounting.

The platform includes marketing and sales tools. Review requests, email campaigns, and a customer database help a business grow repeat work, and a booking widget lets homeowners schedule online. Some AI features answer calls and messages, help with scheduling and follow-ups, and summarize job details so owners can stay on top of the business.

Housecall Pro is paid, with plans by features and number of users, plus payment processing fees. It targets small and mid-sized companies, and larger operations may want more advanced tools. Alternatives include Jobber, ServiceTitan for larger firms, FieldEdge, Workiz, and Service Fusion. Housecall Pro suits independent trades businesses.
