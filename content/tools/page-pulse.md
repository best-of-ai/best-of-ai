Page Pulse aims to make analytics approachable. A site owner adds a snippet and sees visitors, popular pages, sources, and conversions in a clear dashboard, without needing to learn a complex reporting interface. The focus is on answers to basic questions about what is working.

Typical users are small businesses, bloggers, and agencies that report to clients and want simple summaries. Some AI features explain trends in plain language and suggest improvements.

Independent information is limited, so check whether it uses cookies, how it handles privacy, and how its data compares with other tools. It has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Plausible, Fathom, Simple Analytics, Umami, and Google Analytics. Page Pulse suits owners who want a simple view of site traffic.
