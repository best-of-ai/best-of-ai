Ben's Bites began as a daily email in which Ben Tossell shared what he found interesting in AI. It became popular because he wrote like a person rather than a press release, picking tools worth trying and giving short reactions. Readers range from founders and developers to curious non-technical people who want to keep up.

Over time, it expanded beyond a single email. A community of subscribers shares tips and projects, and there are resources such as prompt collections and a list of tools. Some content is for paying members, while the main newsletter remains free. Sponsorships support the free edition and are labeled.

Ben's Bites is best for finding new tools and following the product side of AI. It is less about deep research or policy than some competitors. Alternatives include TLDR AI, The Rundown AI, The Batch, Import AI, and Superhuman AI. Ben's Bites suits readers who like an informal guide to new AI products.
