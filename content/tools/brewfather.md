It calculates color, bitterness, and alcohol, scales recipes to equipment, and logs fermentation readings. Integrations with smart hydrometers record gravity automatically. Paid tiers add features like more batches and sharing.

It is built for small-scale brewing rather than large breweries.

BeerSmith and Brewer's Friend are alternatives. Brewfather suits homebrewers and nano breweries.
