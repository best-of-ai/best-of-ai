Intercom's Fin AI agent is the product's most significant differentiator. Unlike a chatbot that answers based on a decision tree, Fin uses a large language model grounded in your help centre, product documentation, and connected knowledge base to answer support questions conversationally. Resolution rates for Fin-handled queries are high because it answers from actual knowledge rather than predefined scripts — customers can ask follow-up questions, get clarifications, and describe problems in their own words. Only questions Fin can't confidently answer are escalated to a human agent.

Beyond reactive support, Intercom's proactive messaging features let product teams send targeted in-app messages based on user behaviour. If a user visits a pricing page three times without upgrading, you trigger a message with an offer. If someone gets stuck in an onboarding flow, the system notices and sends help automatically. This combination of reactive support and proactive engagement is why product-led growth companies adopt Intercom — it converts, retains, and supports from a single platform.

Intercom's pricing is higher than most support platforms, reflecting its positioning as a revenue-generating customer engagement tool rather than just a cost-center support system. Plans start at $39/seat/month and increase significantly with AI features and usage volume. The cost is often justified by measuring deflection rate (the percentage of support contacts resolved by Fin without agent involvement) and the impact of proactive messaging on conversion and retention. For B2B SaaS companies in particular, Intercom is embedded deeply in customer success and support operations.
