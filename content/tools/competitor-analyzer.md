Competitor Analyzer helps small teams keep an eye on the market without a full-time analyst. You enter your competitors, and the tool watches their websites, social accounts, content, and in some cases pricing, then summarizes what changed in a weekly digest. The aim is to save the time spent checking sites by hand.

Typical uses include spotting a rival's new feature or campaign, tracking pricing changes, and gathering material for positioning. AI summaries make the digests quick to read and highlight notable changes. The tool is positioned as simpler and cheaper than enterprise competitive intelligence platforms.

There is a free tier and paid plans. Coverage depends on what is publicly visible, and summaries may miss context, so important findings deserve a closer look. Alternatives include Crayon, Klue, Kompyte, Similarweb, and Visualping. Competitor Analyzer suits startups and small marketing teams.
