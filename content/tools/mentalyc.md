Mentalyc is an AI note-taking tool for mental health professionals. A therapist records a session, uploads audio, or enters brief notes, and the tool generates a structured progress note that matches the format their practice uses. The clinician edits and approves it.

The product emphasizes privacy, since therapy content is sensitive. It states that it follows health privacy rules and offers controls such as removing identifying details. It also supports treatment plans and can learn a therapist's style over time.

Mentalyc has a limited free tier and paid plans. Clients should be told about recording and consent should be documented. AI notes can contain errors, so review is essential. Alternatives include Upheal, Blueprint, SimplePractice's AI, Freed, and Heidi Health. Mentalyc suits therapists and counselors who want faster notes.
