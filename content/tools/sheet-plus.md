Sheet+ is for spreadsheet users who know what they want but not the syntax. You describe the calculation, such as the total of sales for a region in a given month, and Sheet+ produces the formula with an explanation. You can paste a formula you don't understand and get a plain-language breakdown, or paste an error and receive suggestions for fixing it.

It extends to related tasks. It can write Google Apps Script automation from a description, generate regular expressions, and create SQL queries, and integrates with Sheets through an add-on so you can work without leaving the spreadsheet. It's popular with small business owners, analysts, and anyone who finds formulas intimidating.

Sheet+ is paid, with a limited free option. AI-generated formulas should be tested on sample data, because mistakes in a spreadsheet can quietly spread through reports. Google's own Gemini in Sheets and the Excel Copilot features provide help inside the apps, and general assistants like ChatGPT and Claude can write formulas for free. Alternatives include Formula Bot, Ajelix, and Numerous. Sheet+ suits Google Sheets users who want quick formula assistance.
