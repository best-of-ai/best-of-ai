Replika is one of the earliest AI companion apps. You create an avatar, choose a name and appearance, and begin chatting. Over time the AI builds a memory of your interests, relationships, and preferences, and the avatar responds in a way that reflects that history. Users talk to it about their day, vent about problems, practice conversations, or just keep company. It offers text chat, voice calls, and an augmented reality mode that places the avatar in your room.

The company positions Replika as a friend or companion, and the paid tier unlocks more modes, such as romantic partner or mentor, along with voice features and avatar customization. Some people say the conversations help with loneliness or give them a place to reflect. Mental health professionals generally caution that an AI companion is not therapy and cannot replace human support, and it can encourage dependence in some users.

Replika has a free version with basic chat and a subscription for the fuller experience, with options for monthly, annual, or lifetime plans. It has drawn regulatory attention in Europe over data protection and age protections, and the company has changed what the app allows more than once, which upset some long-term users. Conversations are stored by the company, so personal details deserve caution. Character.AI, Nomi, and Kindroid are alternatives, and Pi from Inflection was positioned as a gentler conversational assistant.
