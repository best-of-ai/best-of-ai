Plurai Simulation maps an agent as it was built, mocks its tools, sub-agents and knowledge, and runs it through scenarios to find failures. Fixes are verified before you ship, and each failure comes with a trace. A plugin works with Claude Code, GitHub Copilot CLI and OpenAI Codex, and Cursor is listed as coming soon.

The company's second product is Post-training, which turns open models into smaller, cheaper ones for a specific task. There is also SCOPE policy compilation and an open-source repo called intellagent.

Simulation credits are free once, and then the Base and Business plans and pay-as-you-go credits take over. Post-training has a no-card starter with free tokens and per-token pricing, and enterprise is on-premises and custom. Teams building agents and enterprises that run them are the audience.
