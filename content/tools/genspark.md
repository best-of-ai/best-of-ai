Genspark started as an AI search engine and expanded into an agent workspace. A user states a goal, such as researching a market and building a presentation, and the Super Agent plans the steps, searches the web, uses tools, and produces outputs like slide decks, documents, spreadsheets, and media.

It combines several models and tools behind the scenes, and offers specialized agents for tasks like calling businesses on a user's behalf, creating images and video, and building simple websites. Results appear in a workspace where users can review and edit them.

Genspark has a free tier and paid plans with credits. Agents can make mistakes or take actions the user did not intend, so check the work, especially anything that sends information or spends money. Alternatives include Manus, ChatGPT agent mode, Claude, Perplexity, and Gamma for decks. Genspark suits people who want one agent for research and content creation.
