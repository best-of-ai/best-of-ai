Paste a product link, upload images or write a brief, and Creatify generates video ads and static ads from it. A large library of AI avatars can present the product, and a brand kit keeps the output in your colors and logo. Both Creatify's own models and third-party video models sit behind the generator.

Max is the ad agent. It researches the market, creates ads, launches them and optimizes them, connecting to Meta and Google Ads and proposing changes for you to approve. A competitor ad tracker shows what rivals are running, and API and MCP access let you drive it from your own tools or a coding agent.

There is a free plan with a small monthly credit allowance and watermarked exports. Paid subscriptions are credit based, with business and enterprise tiers and separate API plans. It is aimed at ecommerce and direct-to-consumer brands, app and game marketers, and agencies.
