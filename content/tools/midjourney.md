Midjourney is one of the most popular image generators, valued for its distinctive aesthetic. You describe an image, and it returns variations that tend to look polished and painterly, with strong composition and lighting. It began on Discord, where users type commands in a shared channel, and now offers a web interface with an image editor, personal galleries, and organization tools. It's widely used by illustrators, designers, concept artists, and marketers.

Control features have expanded over time. Style references and moodboards let you steer the look using sample images, character and object references help maintain consistency across images, and editing tools allow inpainting and expansion. Parameters adjust aspect ratio, stylization, and randomness. Midjourney has also added video generation that animates images into short clips, and models keep improving at details like hands and text.

There is no free plan; users subscribe to tiers that differ in fast generation time and features, and a license allows commercial use under its terms. Images made on lower tiers can be visible to others, and the company has faced copyright lawsuits from creators and film studios over its training data. Alternatives include FLUX, Ideogram for text rendering, Adobe Firefly for commercially safe images, Leonardo.ai, and OpenAI's image models. Midjourney remains the pick for stylized, artistic output.
