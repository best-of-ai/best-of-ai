Paper Digest helps researchers keep up with a flood of new papers. Instead of scanning listings of new submissions every day, a user sets up topics or keywords and gets a personalized digest with short AI-generated summaries of matching papers. This helps researchers spot relevant work quickly and decide which papers merit a full read.

The service also publishes curated summaries of papers from major conferences and journals, and offers a platform to search, summarize, and organize papers. Paper Digest builds on text summarization models to produce abstracts and key points, and it provides tools such as a reader and a way to generate a quick literature overview. Some features are open to everyone, while others sit behind a login or subscription.

Paper Digest is free to start. Machine summaries lose detail and occasionally misstate findings, so they are a filter for deciding what to read, not a replacement for reading. Semantic Scholar offers feeds and alerts, arXiv has its own mailing lists, Google Scholar alerts track keywords, and Elicit and Consensus provide AI answers. For individuals who want a short daily scan of new papers in a field, it is a useful lightweight option.
