Ghost is a publishing tool focused on independent media. It provides a clean editor, a website with themes, email newsletters, and a membership system that lets readers subscribe for free or pay. Because it is open source, publishers can host it themselves or use Ghost's managed service.

Business features are built in. Payments run through Stripe, so publishers keep most of the revenue, and analytics show how posts convert readers into members. The platform supports multiple tiers, comments, and integrations with other tools. A nonprofit organization develops it, which differs from venture-backed rivals.

Ghost's hosted plans are priced by audience size, and self-hosting is free but requires maintenance. Customization needs some technical skill. Alternatives include Substack, Beehiiv, WordPress, Medium, and Kit. Ghost suits publishers who want ownership and control.
