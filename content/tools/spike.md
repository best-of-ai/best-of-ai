Spike presents email like a chat. Threads become conversations, which can feel faster, and a unified view combines email with notes, tasks, and calendar. Teams can discuss messages privately without forwarding.

AI helps with summaries of long threads, drafting replies, and organizing the inbox, and shared inboxes support small teams. It works with many email providers.

Spike has a free tier and paid plans. Chat-style email is a taste, and some prefer traditional layouts. Alternatives include Superhuman, Shortwave, Hey, Gmail with Gemini, and Front. Spike suits people who want a conversational inbox.
