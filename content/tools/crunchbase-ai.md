Crunchbase is a widely used database about startups and private companies. Users look up a company to see its founders, funding history, investors, and news, or search for companies by industry, location, and stage. Investors, salespeople, and researchers use it to find targets and track activity.

Predictive features add forward-looking signals. AI models estimate which companies are likely to raise money, grow, or be acquired, and alerts notify users when relevant events happen. Lists and integrations send data into CRMs for prospecting.

Crunchbase has a free tier with limited data and paid plans for full access. Coverage of private companies is never complete, and some data is user submitted. Alternatives include PitchBook, CB Insights, Dealroom, Tracxn, and Apollo. Crunchbase suits sales, investment, and research teams looking at startups.
