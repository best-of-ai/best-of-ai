Suna, developed by Kortix, is an open-source generalist AI agent designed to autonomously handle complex tasks.  It excels at diverse applications, including competitor analysis, trip planning, recruitment, and data scraping.  Suna's ease of use, powered by simple prompts, allows individuals and companies to automate research, operations, and strategic planning.  Its capabilities range from generating investor lists and SEO reports to summarizing scientific papers, transforming manual workflows into efficient, autonomous processes.
