Tactiq takes a lightweight approach to meeting notes. Instead of sending a recording bot into the call, it runs as a browser extension that captures the meeting's captions and audio on your device. A live transcript appears in a sidebar as people speak, and you can highlight key moments as the meeting happens. That makes it less intrusive in calls where an unfamiliar bot would raise eyebrows.

After the meeting, AI features produce summaries, action items, and custom outputs from prompts, such as a follow-up email or a status update. The transcript is saved in your account and can be exported to tools like Notion, Google Docs, Slack, and HubSpot. Templates adapt the output for different meeting types, and a search function finds past conversations.

Tactiq has a free plan with limited transcripts and AI credits and paid plans for more. It only works in the Chrome browser, so desktop app calls need workarounds, and the quality of transcripts depends on caption accuracy. Participants should still be told that the meeting is being transcribed. Competitors include Fireflies, Otter, Fathom, Granola, and tl;dv. Tactiq suits people who prefer a no-bot approach in browser-based calls.
