Altern is a browsable directory of AI products. Visitors can search by task or browse categories, such as writing, design, coding, and video, and see short descriptions, pricing, links, and user ratings. Rankings and filters aim to surface popular and well-regarded tools so people do not have to sift through thousands of options alone.

Tool makers can submit their products to be listed, and users can leave ratings and reviews. The directory also offers comparisons and alternatives for specific products, which is useful when you already know one tool and want to see similar ones. Like other discovery sites, it covers a wide range from established products to new launches.

Altern is free to use. Directory listings can be incomplete or outdated, and some include sponsored placements, so check official sites for up-to-date details. Larger directories include There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, and Product Hunt, while AlternativeTo covers software more broadly and Best of AI curates tools with detailed descriptions. Altern suits people looking for a quick way to browse AI tools.
