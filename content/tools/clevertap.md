CleverTap serves brands with mobile apps and large user bases. It collects behavioral data such as app opens, purchases, and feature use, builds segments, and lets marketers send push notifications, email, SMS, WhatsApp, and in-app messages. Journeys can be designed visually so that actions trigger the next message.

AI is used for prediction and optimization. Models estimate which users are likely to churn or convert, choose the best time and channel for each message, and recommend products. A proprietary database designed for large volumes of event data keeps analysis fast, and analytics show retention, funnels, and campaign results.

CleverTap is sold on custom contracts scaled by users and messages, and has a strong presence in India, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East. Setup requires integrating its SDK into apps. Alternatives include MoEngage, Braze, WebEngage, Iterable, and OneSignal. CleverTap suits consumer brands focused on app retention.
