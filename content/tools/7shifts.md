7shifts is built for the quirks of restaurant staffing. Managers create schedules based on forecast sales, availability, and labor targets, and publish them to a staff app where employees can see shifts, request swaps, and pick up open shifts. The goal is to keep enough people on the floor without overspending on wages.

The platform adds operations tools. A time clock tracks hours, compliance features flag overtime and break rules by location, tip pooling and distribution help with fair payouts, and team chat replaces scattered text threads. Integrations with point of sale systems provide sales data that feeds labor forecasts, and payroll exports reduce manual work.

7shifts has a free plan for a single location with basic features and paid tiers for more tools. It focuses on restaurants and hospitality and is less suited to other industries. Alternatives include Deputy, HotSchedules (Fourth), Homebase, When I Work, and Toast's native tools. 7shifts suits independent restaurants and small groups.
