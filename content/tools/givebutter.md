Givebutter is a fundraising platform for nonprofits. Organizations create donation forms, run crowdfunding and peer-to-peer campaigns, sell event tickets, and accept recurring gifts. Its donor CRM keeps a record of each supporter's giving and communications.

The platform tries to be affordable for small groups. It has been known for a model where donors can choose to tip the platform rather than the nonprofit paying fees. Auctions, text-to-give, and email tools are included. AI helps segment lists and draft appeals and thank-yous.

Givebutter has a free tier and paid plans that add features. Check current fee structures, since processing fees apply. Alternatives include Zeffy, Classy, Bloomerang, Donorbox, and Virtuous. Givebutter suits small and mid-sized nonprofits.
