NaturalReader turns reading into listening. You paste text, upload a document or PDF, or use the Chrome extension to read a web page, and it reads aloud in a voice you choose, with adjustable speed. Highlighting of each word as it is spoken helps learners follow along, and the tool supports many languages and accents. OCR features can read text from images and scanned pages.

It serves education and accessibility needs. Students with dyslexia, ADHD, or visual impairments use it to access materials, professionals use it to review documents on the go, and teachers use it with classroom content. Premium plans offer higher-quality AI voices, more file formats, and commercial use for creating audio, while a free version covers personal use with basic voices.

NaturalReader has free and paid tiers, plus plans for schools and businesses. The most natural voices require a subscription, and pronunciation of technical terms can be imperfect. Alternatives include Speechify, which has a larger feature set, ElevenLabs Reader, the built-in readers on iOS, Android, and Edge, Microsoft Immersive Reader, and Google Docs read-aloud features. NaturalReader suits people who want an easy, affordable reader for documents.
