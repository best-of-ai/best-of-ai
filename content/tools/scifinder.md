Users search by structure, reaction, or keyword and see papers, patents, and suppliers. Retrosynthesis tools suggest routes to a target molecule. Results link to the CAS registry.

Access is by subscription, usually through institutions.

Reaxys is the main alternative. SciFinder suits chemists doing literature and synthesis research.
