Users define geometry, add physics, and solve. Modules cover chemical reactions, batteries, fluid flow, and acoustics. Surrogate models can speed up repeated studies.

Licenses are costly, and the learning curve is steep for new users.

Ansys is the main alternative. COMSOL suits chemical and process engineers who need coupled models.
