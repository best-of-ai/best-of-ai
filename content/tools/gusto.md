Gusto is built for employers without a dedicated HR department. A business adds its team, and Gusto calculates pay, withholds and files payroll taxes, and pays employees and contractors. Employees get pay stubs and benefits information in an app, and onboarding checklists collect forms and agreements from new hires.

The platform extends beyond payroll. It offers health insurance and retirement plan administration through partners, time tracking, hiring tools, and compliance support such as reminders and labor law guidance. AI features help flag likely filing errors, draft job descriptions and policies, and answer HR questions in plain language.

Gusto is paid, priced per month plus per employee, with tiers by features. It serves the United States, and international contractor payments are offered through add-ons. Alternatives include ADP, Rippling, Paychex, Justworks, and OnPay. Gusto suits small businesses that want simple payroll and HR in one place.
