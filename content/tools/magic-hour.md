Magic Hour brings several AI video tools together. A user can animate a still image, generate a clip from a prompt, convert a video into an anime or painted style, sync a character's lips to audio, or swap faces. The tools run in the browser and export short videos for social media.

It is aimed at creators and marketers who want a variety of effects without learning several apps. A credit system covers the different features, and templates help with common formats such as memes and talking-head videos.

Magic Hour has a free tier with limits and paid plans. Face swap and talking avatars raise consent and deepfake concerns, so use only likenesses you have permission to use and label synthetic content. Alternatives include Runway, Pika, Hedra, HeyGen, and CapCut. Magic Hour suits creators making quick, stylized videos.
