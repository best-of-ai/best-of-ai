Linear is a project management tool aimed at product and engineering teams. Its pitch is that the tool should get out of your way: pages load instantly, nearly everything can be done from the keyboard, and the data model is opinionated about how software gets built. Work is tracked as issues, grouped into cycles (time-boxed sprints), projects, and initiatives, with roadmaps on top for the longer view. Many teams switch to it from Jira because there is far less configuration to maintain.

The AI features focus on the busywork around issues. Linear can suggest labels, assignees, and duplicates when a new issue comes in, summarize long threads and project updates, and answer questions about your workspace. It also works with coding agents: you can assign an issue to an agent from a connected tool, and the agent picks up the context and reports back in the same thread. Integrations with GitHub, GitLab, Slack, and Figma keep issues in sync with pull requests, messages, and designs.

Linear has a free plan for small teams and paid per-seat plans that add unlimited issues, more advanced planning features, and admin controls. It is deliberately focused, so it is less suited to non-engineering teams that need heavy customization, custom workflows across many departments, or detailed resource planning. Teams in that position often look at Jira, Asana, or ClickUp instead. For small and mid-sized product teams that value speed and a clean interface, it has become one of the most popular choices.
