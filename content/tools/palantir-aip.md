AIP, the Artificial Intelligence Platform, is Palantir's layer for putting language models to work on an organization's data. It builds on Palantir's ontology, a structured model of a company's objects, such as orders, machines, and customers, and the actions that can be taken on them. Models can then read and act within that governed model instead of working on raw text.

Customers build agents and applications that automate tasks, answer questions, and support decisions in areas like supply chain, healthcare, defense, and manufacturing. Controls around access, logging, and human approval aim to make AI usable in sensitive settings. Palantir runs engagement programs that help customers reach production quickly.

AIP is sold to large enterprises and governments on custom contracts, and Palantir has been controversial for its government and defense work. Alternatives include Databricks, Snowflake Cortex, C3.ai, Microsoft Copilot Studio, and ServiceNow. Palantir AIP suits large organizations with complex operational data.
