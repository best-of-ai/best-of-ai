Hailuo AI is the consumer face of video models from MiniMax, a Chinese AI company. You type a prompt or upload an image, and it generates a short video clip, with motion that many users find natural, including complex movement like dancing, sports, and camera moves. It gained a following among creators for the quality of its image-to-video results and its willingness to follow detailed prompts.

The platform offers tools for steering results. A subject reference feature keeps a character consistent across clips, camera controls guide movement, and different model versions trade speed for quality. It also generates images and offers voice and music features through MiniMax's other models. An API lets developers use the video models in their own products.

Hailuo has a free tier with daily credits and paid plans for more generations and higher resolution. Clips are short, results vary from run to run, and as a service from a Chinese company, it follows Chinese content rules, which some users consider when evaluating data handling. Competitors include Kling, Runway, Luma, Google's Veo, Pika, and Sora's former audience. Hailuo suits creators who want strong motion quality at low cost.
