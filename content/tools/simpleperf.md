SimplePerf is a simple, guided 360° performance review tool made for small teams. It gives AI guidance to get better employee feedback and help managers. Better reviews, less hassle.

Key Features
- 360 performance reviews.
- AI guidance to get better questions and better responses.
- Design and customize performance review questions.
- Smart reminders to make sure reviews are submitted on time.
- Clear dashboard for HRs to monitor the cycles.

Benefits
- Simple software to launch a review: No bloated dashboards. Get started in minutes and stay in control with a lightweight process.
- Always on time: Smart reminders ensure each review step is completed (without you chasing people).
- Relevant feedback, faster: AI-generated follow-up questions help employees and managers write meaningful feedback.
