Tana combines outlining with databases. Everything is a node in an outline, and tagging a node with a Supertag gives it fields and behavior, so a note about a person, a book, or a project becomes structured and can be queried and viewed in many ways. Backlinks connect related ideas.

AI is integrated into the workflow. It can transcribe meetings and turn them into tagged notes, fill in fields, and answer questions across a workspace, and it can act on commands users define. Voice capture makes it easy to record ideas on the go.

Tana has a free tier and paid plans. It has a steep learning curve and fans of its power. Alternatives include Notion, Obsidian, Capacities, Reflect, and Logseq. Tana suits people who want a flexible, structured thinking tool.
