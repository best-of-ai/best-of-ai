NAYA is an intelligent infrastructure platform for fintechs and financial services teams looking to replace spreadsheets, fragmented systems, and manual workflows with automated accuracy. It combines AI-powered reconciliation, compliance monitoring, and real-time ledger automation into a single, programmable financial operations layer.

With NAYA, finance and ops teams can instantly match transactions across banks, ERPs, and payment gateways; detect anomalies; and maintain audit-ready books—without writing a single rule or manually checking files. The platform’s AI agents handle edge cases, learn from context, and continuously adapt to new transaction patterns and compliance rules.

Designed for startups and scale-ups alike, NAYA integrates seamlessly into your existing data environment to serve as your system of record for financial truth. Whether you’re a fintech startup managing cash flow at scale or a product team embedding payments and need to stay compliant, NAYA gives you the tools to run finance on autopilot.
