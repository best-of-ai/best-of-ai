Users drag materials onto a model, set up lighting, and see the render update live. It reads files from CAD tools such as SolidWorks and Rhino. KeyShot also includes AI-based features for texture and background creation in newer versions.

It is a paid product with subscription and perpetual options.

Blender Cycles and V-Ray are alternatives. KeyShot suits industrial designers and jewelry designers who need product renders fast.
