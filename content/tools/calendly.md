Calendly removes the back-and-forth of finding a meeting time. You connect your calendar, set the hours you are available and the types of meetings you offer, and share a link. Invitees choose a slot in their own time zone, and the event appears on both calendars with a video link added automatically. Buffers, meeting limits, and minimum notice settings keep the schedule manageable.

Teams use more advanced features. Round-robin scheduling spreads meetings across several people, collective events find times when everyone is free, and routing forms send prospects to the right person based on their answers. Workflows send reminders and follow-ups, payments can be collected through Stripe or PayPal, and integrations connect with Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Salesforce, HubSpot, and Slack. Newer AI features help with note-taking and scheduling assistance.

Calendly has a free plan for one event type per user, with paid plans for more features and team use. It is simple and reliable, which explains its popularity, but many of its best features need a paid tier. Acuity Scheduling is stronger for appointments with payment and intake forms, Cal.com is an open-source alternative, and Google Calendar's appointment pages offer basic booking. Calendly is the standard choice for sales and recruiting meetings.
