Tray.io builds on the idea of integration-platform-as-a-service. Teams use a visual builder to create workflows that connect SaaS applications, databases, and internal systems, with powerful logic, error handling, and the ability to write code where necessary. Hundreds of connectors and a universal connector for APIs cover common enterprise tools such as Salesforce, Workday, and Slack.

The company has moved toward AI agents. Its Merlin AI tools let teams build agents that use the connected systems as tools, so an agent can look up a customer, update a record, and send a message, with governance over what it can access. Embedded integration products let software companies offer customer-facing integrations in their own products.

Tray is sold to mid-sized and large organizations on custom contracts, and is more complex and expensive than simple tools like Zapier. Competitors include Workato, MuleSoft, Boomi, Celigo, and n8n for self-hosted needs. Governance and monitoring are important when connecting many systems. Tray suits organizations with demanding integration and automation needs.
