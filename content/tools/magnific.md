Magnific is known as a creative upscaler. Where a standard upscaler sharpens existing pixels, Magnific uses generative AI to invent plausible detail, such as skin texture, fabric, foliage, and architectural elements, guided by a prompt and sliders for how much the image should change. The results can be striking, especially for AI-generated art.

The tool also supports style transfer, relighting, and other transformations, and its capabilities have been added to Freepik's broader creative platform. Freepik acquired Magnific and kept it as a distinct brand, offering API access to its upscaling features.

Magnific is paid, with plans by credits. Because it adds detail that was not in the original, it changes the image, which may be undesirable for documentary photography or precise product images. Alternatives include Topaz Gigapixel, Let's Enhance, Upscayl, Krea, and Clarity Upscaler. A duplicate listing for Magnific AI covers the same product. Magnific suits creators who want dramatic detail enhancement.
