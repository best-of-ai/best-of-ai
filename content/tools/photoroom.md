Photoroom grew from a background remover into a full product photography tool. Take a picture of a product on a table, and the app cuts it out cleanly, then places it on a white background, a color, or an AI-generated scene with realistic shadows and lighting. For small sellers on marketplaces like Etsy, eBay, and Shopify, that means professional-looking listings without a studio.

Other features expand the range. An AI backgrounds tool creates settings from a text description, an instant shadow and relight tool adds depth, and a magic retouch removes objects and blemishes. Templates in standard sizes speed up posts for social media and marketplaces. Batch editing handles whole catalogs, and an API lets platforms add the same processing to their own apps. Teams can share brand templates.

Photoroom has a free tier with watermarks and limits and paid plans for higher resolution, batch features, and commercial use. It is faster and simpler than Photoshop for this task but gives less control for complex edits. Alternatives include Clipdrop, Canva, Adobe Express, Pixelcut, and Remove.bg for plain background removal. Photoroom suits online sellers, photographers, and marketers who need many product images.
