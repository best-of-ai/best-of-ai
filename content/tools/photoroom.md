Photoroom is built for e-commerce product photography, where the same types of edits get repeated across hundreds or thousands of images: remove the background, replace it with a clean white or a lifestyle setting, adjust the lighting, and resize for multiple platforms. These tasks are automated in Photoroom — background removal works on the first try in most cases, and lifestyle backgrounds can be generated from a text description rather than requiring a photo shoot in a branded environment.

The batch processing via API is what makes Photoroom practical at scale. E-commerce businesses with large product catalogues use the API to process product photos automatically when they're uploaded, applying consistent background treatment, resizing, and formatting without manual effort per image. The same workflow that takes a few seconds per image in the app can be automated across thousands of new products per day programmatically.

Photoroom's mobile app (iOS and Android) is free for basic background removal. Photoroom Pro at $10/month (billed annually) adds HD exports, unlimited background removal, and access to AI background generation. Business and API plans are available for high-volume commercial use. The tool is used primarily by Shopify sellers, Amazon merchants, Instagram brands, and small e-commerce businesses that need consistent, professional product imagery without a photography studio or an image editor on staff. Portrait retouching is also supported, making it useful for headshots and profile photos as a secondary use case.
