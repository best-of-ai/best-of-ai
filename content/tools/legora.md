Legora is a legal AI company from Sweden that has expanded globally. Lawyers use its assistant for tasks such as reviewing contracts and extracting key terms across many documents, researching legal questions, summarizing materials, and drafting and revising documents. A Word add-in brings these abilities into the editor where lawyers already work, so suggestions appear as tracked changes.

The platform emphasizes collaboration and structure. Features such as tabular review let teams compare many documents side by side in a grid, workflows capture a firm's preferred processes, and shared workspaces allow lawyers and clients to work together. Firms can customize the system with their own playbooks and precedents, and security features aim to meet the confidentiality needs of legal practice.

Legora is sold to law firms and legal departments through enterprise agreements, with pricing by custom quote. Like other legal AI tools, its outputs must be reviewed by a lawyer to catch mistakes and fabricated citations. Competitors include Harvey, Spellbook, Thomson Reuters CoCounsel, Lexis+ AI, and Luminance. Legora is considered one of the leading legal AI platforms and suits firms seeking a collaborative AI workspace.
