🚀 Meet Kiddomato — AI Picture Books, Made Personal

Kiddomato is an AI-powered picture book creation platform designed for early childhood learning, built around one core idea:
every child deserves a story made just for them.

✨ What makes Kiddomato different?

1️⃣ Learn by example — not guesswork
We provide ready-made picture book examples and age-specific prompt suggestions, helping parents and educators create better stories even if they’ve never written one before.

2️⃣ Deep personalization beyond text
Choose from multiple illustration styles—or upload your own custom style, characters, or reference images.
You can also input child-specific details (age, interests, preferences) to create truly personalized books.

3️⃣ A conversational creation flow (Chat → Book)
Instead of complex settings, Kiddomato uses a simple chat-based workflow:

Analyze: AI suggests story ideas and structure

Script: Generate and edit the full story script

Book: Confirm and generate illustrated pages

Each page can be edited individually—change images, adjust text, and fine-tune styles at any time.

4️⃣ From screen to hands-on reading
Export your book as a high-quality PDF , ready for printing or sharing with family.

5️⃣ Privacy-first by design
All user data is stored locally in your browser.
Your stories and children’s information stay on your device—no cloud storage, no tracking.

6️⃣ Accessible pricing for families and educators
New users receive 1,000 free credits to get started.
After that, pricing is simple and affordable: $0.08 per page.
(See subscription details on our website.)

We built Kiddomato to make early learning creative, joyful, and personal—without requiring writing, illustration, or design skills.

We’re launching today and would love your feedback ❤️
