Yellow.ai builds AI agents for customer and employee service. A company connects it to systems such as CRMs and ticketing, trains it on content, and deploys across websites, messaging apps, and phone lines. The agents answer questions and complete tasks in many languages.

The platform emphasizes multilingual support, strong in markets such as India, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia. Tools for building, testing, and monitoring agents, along with analytics, support operations teams. Handoff to human agents is built in.

Yellow.ai is sold on enterprise contracts. Alternatives include Kore.ai, Sierra, Decagon, Ada, and Cognigy. Yellow.ai suits large organizations automating support.
