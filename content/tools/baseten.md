Baseten runs models for companies that build AI products. A team packages a model, often with the Truss open-source tool, and deploys it as an endpoint that scales up and down with demand. The platform handles GPU provisioning, scaling, and monitoring, which are chores that distract from building the product.

It focuses on performance. Optimized inference engines, quick cold starts, and support for the latest GPUs help serve models with low latency and cost. Teams can deploy open models from a library, bring their own, or fine-tune, and use dedicated deployments for steady traffic. Options for running in a customer's own cloud address security needs.

Baseten is pay as you go with enterprise plans. It targets teams with engineering skill rather than casual users. Alternatives include Together AI, Fireworks AI, Modal, Replicate, and Amazon SageMaker. Baseten suits companies running models in production.
