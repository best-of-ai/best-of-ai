Fellow helps teams run better meetings. Before a meeting, it provides shared agendas and templates for one-on-ones, team meetings, and client calls. During the meeting, an AI notetaker records and transcribes, capturing decisions and action items automatically. Afterward, summaries and tasks are sent to attendees.

The platform keeps a record of conversations over time, which suits managers who run recurring one-on-ones. Integrations connect with calendars, Slack, Zoom, Teams, and project tools. AI features answer questions across past meetings and draft follow-up messages. Feedback tools support regular check-ins and performance conversations.

Fellow has a free plan with limits and paid plans per user. Recording meetings requires consent from participants. Alternatives include Otter.ai, Fireflies, Granola, Read AI, and Notion's AI meeting notes. Fellow suits managers and teams that want structured meetings.
