You upload a script or a novel, or have the AI write one, and SceneMixer breaks it into characters, sets, props and episodes. Before it generates any video it makes reference art for each character and location. Every shot is generated against those sheets, so faces, outfits and places should stay consistent from episode to episode. A World Model builds each set first, which keeps the geography the same when the camera cuts.

There are three project types: short drama with episodes up to 240 seconds, TV series up to 300 seconds, and film with no episode cap. You pick one visual style per project, from live action to anime, 3D animation, claymation or watercolor, and one of 15 dialogue languages. Output is vertical 9:16 or landscape 16:9, at 480P to 2K depending on the video model. You can edit and regenerate a single segment without redoing the whole episode.

Pricing is credit based, and video is billed per second. New accounts get 50 free credits, 15 free reference sheets and a free storyboard for the first episode. Memberships start at $7.99 a month, and videos from free accounts carry an AI badge. SceneMixer is a production tool, not a place to watch dramas, and you publish the exported files yourself. Alternatives include Runway, Higgsfield, Hedra and Kling, though most of them don't handle continuity across episodes. It suits creators making vertical short series.
