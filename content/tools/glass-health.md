Glass Health helps with clinical reasoning and documentation. A clinician enters or dictates a patient summary, and the assistant proposes a list of possible diagnoses, along with reasoning and suggested next steps. A plan builder drafts assessment and plan sections, and reference citations let the user check where claims come from.

It also supports note writing, and a library of clinical guidelines and content supports decisions. Physicians, residents, and students use it to think through cases and save time on write-ups. The product emphasizes that the clinician stays responsible for decisions.

Glass Health has a free tier and paid plans. AI outputs can be wrong or incomplete, and the tool is not a substitute for clinical judgment or established decision support. Patient data handling must follow privacy rules. Alternatives include OpenEvidence, UpToDate with AI, Doximity GPT, Abridge for notes, and Microsoft Dragon Copilot. Glass Health suits clinicians wanting a reasoning aid.
