Instabase focuses on complex documents that older template-based tools could not handle. Mortgage packages, insurance claims, and financial statements arrive in many formats, and Instabase uses AI models to classify them, extract data, and validate it against rules. Results flow into systems such as loan origination and claims platforms.

Its platform provides tools for building document workflows with a mix of models and human review, and newer products use language models for understanding and answering questions about documents. Customers can deploy it in the cloud or in their own environment, which matters for regulated data.

Instabase is sold to large enterprises on custom contracts. Setup requires effort and tuning. Alternatives include Hyperscience, ABBYY, UiPath Document Understanding, Rossum, and Amazon Textract. Instabase suits organizations with complex document workflows.
