Pattern89 set out to bring data to creative decisions. Advertisers produce many images and videos but often cannot tell which elements drive results. The platform analyzed an advertiser's past ads and performance data, tagging the visual and text elements in each, such as colors, faces, products, and calls to action, and learned which combinations worked for a given audience and channel.

With that knowledge, it could score new creative before launch and suggest changes, and recommend which assets to prioritize. Marketers used it to reduce wasted spend on ads that were unlikely to work, and to give designers concrete guidance. Integrations with social ad platforms pulled in performance data.

Shutterstock acquired Pattern89 as part of an effort to add AI to its creative offerings. Pricing was by custom quote for marketing teams and agencies. Predictions are statistical and depend on having enough historical data, and creative performance also depends on offers, timing, and audience. Competitors and alternatives include AdCreative.ai, Pencil, CreativeX, VidMob, and the built-in creative tools of Meta and Google.
