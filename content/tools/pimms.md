PIMMS is a smart link-sharing and conversion tracking platform built to maximize engagement and drive measurable results across channels. It allows creators, marketers, and teams to share links that automatically open in the correct mobile apps— like YouTube, Amazon, or Vinted —ensuring a seamless user experience and higher conversion rates. With real-time analytics, PIMMS tracks clicks, leads, conversions, and sales, giving users full visibility into what drives performance. The tool supports features like branded short links, QR codes, shared dashboards, and advanced filtering by UTM parameters, source, device, location, and campaign. PIMMS also integrates with tools like Stripe, Shopify, Zapier, and Cal.com to connect marketing activity directly to revenue.
