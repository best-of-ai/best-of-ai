Speechify turns reading into listening. You import text from the web, a PDF, an email, or a photo of a page, and the app reads it aloud at the speed you choose, highlighting the words as it goes. That makes it useful for catching up on articles while commuting, for studying, and for people with dyslexia or other reading differences, who often find audio easier to process. Apps for iOS, Android, Chrome, and the web sync your library.

The company has built voices that sound natural, including options modeled on well-known personalities with their permission, and supports many languages. It also offers a studio product for producing voiceovers from scripts, AI dubbing for video, and a voice cloning feature. Features such as skimming mode, summaries, and AI assistants help you work through long documents.

Speechify has a limited free version and a paid subscription for premium voices and full features. Text-to-speech still struggles with some formatting, tables, and unusual names. Competing options include the built-in readers in iOS and Android, ElevenLabs Reader, NaturalReader, Murf for voiceovers, and Pocket-style apps that include audio. Speechify is a strong choice for people who want to listen to a lot of text.
