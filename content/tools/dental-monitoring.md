Dental Monitoring lets orthodontists check on patients remotely. A patient attaches a small cheek retractor to a phone, scans their teeth on a schedule, and uploads the images. The software compares scans to the treatment plan and measures how teeth are moving.

AI flags problems such as aligners that do not fit, lost attachments, hygiene concerns, or progress that is behind schedule. The orthodontist reviews alerts and decides whether to adjust the plan, send the next aligners earlier, or schedule a visit. This can reduce in-person appointments and catch issues sooner.

The service is sold to dental practices, which pass costs to patients in different ways. It depends on patient compliance with scanning. Alternatives include Align's own monitoring tools, SmileMate, Grin, and Overjet for diagnostic imaging. Dental Monitoring suits orthodontic practices that offer remote check-ins.
