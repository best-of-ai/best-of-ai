Peec AI covers a gap that traditional SEO tools leave: what AI assistants say about your brand. It runs prompts against AI engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini, then reports how often your brand is mentioned, in what position, and with what sentiment.

Source tracking shows which websites the AI cites when it answers, which helps you see where to earn mentions. Reports can be exported, and API and MCP connections let you pull data into other tools. Marketing teams, brands, and agencies use it, and it has separate plans for brands and agencies.

Prices are on the pricing page, and a free trial is advertised. AI answers vary from one run to the next, so treat the numbers as trends and not exact rankings. Semrush and Ahrefs have added AI visibility features, but Peec is built around the topic from the start.
