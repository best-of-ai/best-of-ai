Sellers use it to list antiques, collectibles, parts, and general goods. Seller tools include AI-assisted listing creation from photos and promoted listings. Completed sales are searchable, which helps with pricing.

Fees apply to sales, and rules differ by category. Resellers often use its sold listings as a quick price guide before buying stock.

Etsy, Shopify stores, and Facebook Marketplace are other ways to sell. eBay suits dealers, resellers, and anyone selling second-hand goods.
