Ajelix is built for people who struggle with spreadsheet formulas. You describe what you want, such as the sum of sales in a region for a given month, and it produces the formula along with an explanation, so you can learn as you go. In reverse, you can paste a complicated formula and get a step-by-step explanation in plain language, which is useful when inheriting someone else's workbook.

It also covers related tasks. Generators can write Google Apps Script and VBA macros from descriptions, convert formulas between Excel and Google Sheets, and assist with SQL queries. An analysis feature lets you upload a file and ask questions to create charts and summaries. The tools are available on the web and as add-ons for spreadsheet apps.

Ajelix has a free tier with limited use and paid plans. The AI-generated formulas should be tested, especially for edge cases, because errors can silently spread through a model. General assistants like ChatGPT and Claude can do much of this for free, and Excel's Copilot and Google Sheets' Gemini provide help inside the spreadsheet itself. Formula Bot and Sheet+ are similar dedicated tools. Ajelix suits occasional spreadsheet users who want quick formula help.
