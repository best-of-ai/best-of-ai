Causal was created for finance teams that outgrew spreadsheets. Users build models from blocks that represent revenue, headcount, and costs, link them to data from accounting and CRM systems, and see results as charts and dashboards. The structure makes models easier to follow and share than a sprawling workbook.

Scenario planning is a core strength. Teams can change assumptions, such as hiring plans or pricing, and compare outcomes side by side, then present the results to executives and investors. AI features help create models from descriptions and explain changes in numbers.

Lucanet, a provider of finance software, acquired Causal, which continues to serve customers. Causal has had a free tier and paid plans by usage. It suits startups and mid-sized companies, and very large enterprises may need more complex tools. Alternatives include Pigment, Mosaic, Fathom, Runway, and Anaplan. Causal suits finance teams that want modern planning.
