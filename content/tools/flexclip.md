FlexClip is a browser-based video maker for people without editing experience. You start from a template or a blank timeline, drop in clips, images, text, and music from its stock library, and trim and arrange them with drag and drop. It exports in common formats and sizes for social platforms, and includes screen and webcam recording for tutorials. It is used by small businesses, teachers, and social media managers.

AI features speed up production. Text-to-video builds a draft from a script or idea, an AI script writer drafts the narration, and auto subtitles transcribe speech and add captions in many languages. Background removal for video, text-to-speech voiceovers, and AI image generation fill other gaps. Some features turn a blog post or product page into a video, which marketers use for repurposing content.

FlexClip has a free plan with watermarks and limited export quality, and paid plans that remove them and add longer videos, more stock content, and higher resolution. It is easier and lighter than Premiere or DaVinci Resolve, but it offers less control for professional work. CapCut is a popular free competitor with more advanced AI tools, Canva has a broader design suite, and InVideo and Veed offer similar online editing. FlexClip suits quick marketing videos.
