DeepInfra hosts open models and sells access by the token or by usage. A developer picks a model from its catalog, which includes chat models, embeddings, image generators, and speech tools, and calls it with an API compatible with OpenAI's format. The company handles GPUs, scaling, and optimization.

The appeal is price and simplicity. Because open models are widely available, hosting providers compete on cost and speed, and DeepInfra positions itself at the low end. Developers can also deploy custom models or fine-tuned versions in some cases, and use dedicated GPUs for steady workloads.

DeepInfra has no monthly fee for basic use and bills per usage. Model availability follows what the open community releases, so it lacks closed models from the major labs, and reliability varies with demand. Alternatives include Together AI, Fireworks AI, Groq, OpenRouter, and Replicate. DeepInfra suits developers who want cheap access to open models.
