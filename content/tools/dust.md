Dust helps companies deploy AI assistants on their own information. Administrators connect sources such as Notion, Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, and Confluence, and create assistants for functions like support, sales, or engineering. Each assistant has instructions and access to selected data, so answers are grounded in the company's content.

Employees use assistants in a chat interface or inside Slack, and assistants can take actions through connected tools. Teams share assistants they build, so a good one becomes common property. The platform works with models from several providers, and focuses on security and permissions.

Dust is paid, priced per user, with a trial. Results depend on the connected data and prompt design. Alternatives include Glean, Microsoft Copilot Studio, ChatGPT Enterprise, Cassidy, and Notion AI. Dust suits teams that want assistants tailored to internal knowledge.
