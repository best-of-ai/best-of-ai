Bloomerang is built around donor retention, which is one of the hardest problems in fundraising: many donors give once and never again. The CRM stores each supporter's contact details, gifts, communications, and notes, and it shows a timeline of every interaction. A generosity score and engagement level help staff see who is likely to give again and who may be slipping away, so they can focus outreach where it counts.

Around the CRM are tools for the rest of the nonprofit's work. Online giving pages and forms collect donations, including recurring gifts, and email tools send appeals and thank-you messages. A volunteer module tracks sign-ups and hours, and reports show campaign performance and giving trends. Integrations connect with accounting tools such as QuickBooks and with email platforms. AI features help draft communications and surface insights about donors.

Bloomerang is paid, with pricing that depends on the number of records and modules, and it is aimed at small to mid-sized organizations, not large institutions. It is generally easier to set up than Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, which is more customizable but needs expertise. Competitors include Donorperfect, Little Green Light, Neon One, Virtuous, and Bonterra. The system works best for teams that want guidance on engaging donors without a dedicated administrator.
