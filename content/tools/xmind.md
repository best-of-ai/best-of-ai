XMind is a long-standing mind mapping tool known for its range of layouts. Start with a central idea and branch outward, or switch to another structure such as a logic chart, fishbone diagram, matrix, tree chart, or timeline to fit the problem. It has a polished interface, attractive themes, and features such as markers, stickers, notes, and attachments, and it works offline.

Special modes help with presentation and organization. Brainstorming mode captures ideas quickly, outline view turns a map into a list, and Zen mode removes distractions. A presentation feature plays the map as a slideshow, and exports cover PDF, images, Word, and Markdown. AI features can generate a map from a topic, expand a branch with suggestions, and summarize content into a structure.

XMind has a free version with limited features and a paid plan that unlocks full export, themes, and AI. Collaboration is more limited than in cloud-first competitors, since the app emphasizes local files, though sharing options exist. MindMeister is better for real-time teamwork, Miro and Whimsical offer broader whiteboarding, Coggle is a simple web tool, and MindManager serves enterprise needs. XMind suits individuals who want a capable, good-looking mind mapping app.
