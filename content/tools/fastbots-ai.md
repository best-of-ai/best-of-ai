FastBots lets small businesses add a chatbot without developers. A user points the platform at their website, uploads files, or adds frequently asked questions, and it builds a bot that answers customers using that content. The bot is embedded with a snippet of code or connected to channels such as messaging apps.

Additional features include lead capture, handoff to a human, conversation history, and analytics on what visitors ask. Customization options control the look, tone, and behavior of the bot, and integrations send data to other tools. It is positioned as a quick setup for support and sales questions.

FastBots has a trial and paid plans by messages and bots. Answers depend on the quality of the source content, and bots can still produce wrong replies, so test before launching. Alternatives include Chatbase, CustomGPT, Botpress, Intercom Fin, and Tidio. FastBots suits small businesses wanting a simple website assistant.
