Lattice helps companies run performance and development programs. Managers and employees complete reviews and give continuous feedback, set goals and OKRs, hold one-on-ones with shared agendas, and track growth plans. Engagement surveys measure how employees feel, and compensation tools support pay reviews and planning, while an HR information system keeps employee records.

AI is added to reduce busywork. It can help managers draft feedback and review summaries, analyze survey comments for themes, and suggest talking points for one-on-ones. Insights flag possible retention risks or teams with low engagement, and an AI assistant answers questions about company data. The company has also launched products for managing AI agents as part of the workforce, a controversial idea.

Lattice is sold per employee on subscription, with modules at different tiers, and targets mid-sized companies. Success depends on how managers use the tools, and sensitive employee data demands careful handling of AI features. Competitors include 15Five, Culture Amp, Leapsome, Workday, and Rippling. Lattice suits companies that want a modern, integrated performance and engagement platform.
