Rocket belongs to the prompt-to-app builders. A user describes the product, and the platform generates the interface, back end logic, and database, previews it, and deploys it. Further changes are made through conversation, with the option to edit code.

It targets founders and operators who want to test an idea or build an internal tool without engineers. As with similar tools, generated apps need testing, and security and data handling deserve review before real users arrive.

Rocket has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Base44, and v0. Rocket suits non-technical builders starting products.
