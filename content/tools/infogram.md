Infogram helps turn numbers into visual stories. You choose a template for an infographic, report, chart, or dashboard, import data from a spreadsheet or connect to a live source, and customize the design with your brand colors and fonts. The tool produces polished charts and maps, many of them interactive, that can be embedded on websites or shared as links.

Several features support data communication. Over thirty chart types, including maps and tables, handle different kinds of data, and live data connections update visuals when the source changes. Teams can collaborate on projects, keep brand templates, and publish with analytics. Downloads include images and PDFs, and AI features help generate visuals and suggest ways to present data.

Infogram has a free plan with limited projects and paid plans with more features, branding, and collaboration. It is simpler than business intelligence tools like Tableau or Power BI and more focused on presentation than analysis. Alternatives include Canva, Venngage, Piktochart, Visme, Datawrapper for journalists, and Flourish for interactive stories. Infogram suits communicators who need clean data visuals without design skills.
