Pitch aims to make presentations that look good without wrestling with layouts. It provides modern templates, smart slide types, and a brand-aware design system that keeps a team's decks consistent. Collaboration works like a shared document: several people edit at once, leave comments, and track versions, which suits startups, agencies, and sales teams that produce decks together.

Distinctive features include presentation analytics and sharing. You can send a deck as a link and see who viewed it and for how long, which is helpful for investor and sales decks, and recordings let you narrate a deck for asynchronous sharing. AI tools generate slide drafts from a prompt, rewrite text, and create visuals, and the platform integrates with tools like Slack, HubSpot, and Google Drive.

Pitch has a free plan with limits and paid Pro and Business plans that add branding, analytics, and admin controls. It is less flexible in design than PowerPoint or Figma for fully custom layouts and has fewer enterprise features. Gamma and Beautiful.ai are AI-first alternatives, Canva is the broad design tool, and Google Slides and PowerPoint remain the default for most teams. Pitch suits teams who care about design and collaboration.
