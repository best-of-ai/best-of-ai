INK combines an editor with optimization guidance. As you write an article, the tool analyzes the search results for your target keyword and gives a score that reflects how well you cover the topic, which terms to include, and how readable the piece is. Suggestions appear in the sidebar, so you can improve the draft while writing instead of editing afterward.

AI features generate text and help overcome writer's block. You can ask for outlines, introductions, and rewritten paragraphs, and use a plagiarism checker and content shield tools to monitor originality. The product works in a web editor and has extensions for common platforms, and teams can collaborate on content.

INK has a free tier with limited features and paid plans for more words and tools. As with other content optimization tools, chasing a score can encourage keyword stuffing, so keeping readers in mind remains important. Alternatives include Surfer, Frase, Clearscope, MarketMuse, Semrush's writing assistant, and Grammarly for general writing help. INK suits SEO writers who want guidance and AI support in one editor.
