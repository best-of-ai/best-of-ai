Vast.ai connects people who need GPU compute with those who have spare capacity. Hosts, from individuals with gaming rigs to data centers, list machines with details and prices, and users search by GPU type, memory, location, and reliability, then launch containers on them. The competition among hosts keeps prices lower than on-demand rates from large cloud providers.

The platform provides tools for launching Docker-based environments, Jupyter notebooks, and templates for popular AI frameworks, and offers interruptible instances at deeper discounts as well as on-demand options. A range of GPUs is available, from consumer cards to data center models, which suits experimentation, rendering, fine-tuning, and inference for budget-conscious teams.

Vast.ai is pay-as-you-go. Quality and reliability depend on the host, so machines can be slower, interrupted, or less secure than a dedicated cloud, and sensitive data should be handled carefully, using verified data center hosts. Alternatives include Lambda, RunPod, CoreWeave, Together AI, and the major clouds. Vast.ai suits researchers and developers who value low cost.
