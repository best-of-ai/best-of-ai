InVision was one of the leading prototyping and design collaboration tools of the 2010s. Designers uploaded static screens, linked them into clickable prototypes, and shared them with stakeholders for feedback. It also offered a design system manager and, later, Freehand, a collaborative whiteboard.

As Figma and other tools took over design work, InVision's usage declined, and the company announced it would close its products. Teams moved their work to other platforms, and exports were offered for a period before shutdown.

Anyone with old InVision files should look for archived exports. Alternatives include Figma, Adobe XD (no longer developed), Sketch, Framer, and Miro for whiteboarding. This entry is kept for reference, and InVision is no longer available.
