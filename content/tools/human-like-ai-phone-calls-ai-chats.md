Triviat is an AI-powered voice and chat automation platform designed for businesses that want to enhance customer communication at scale. The tool enables companies to deploy conversational AI agents that work 24/7 across phone, email, web chat, WhatsApp, SMS, and social media — in over 50 languages.

Triviat’s AI agents handle tasks like appointment scheduling, order tracking, customer support, and lead qualification, all with human-like fluency. The platform also allows seamless handover to human agents when needed, ensuring a smooth customer experience. With industry-specific solutions and integration with over 5,000 apps, Triviat helps automate operations while reducing support costs and response times.
