NEXT Insurance focuses on small businesses such as contractors, consultants, cleaners, and shops. Instead of working with a broker, an owner answers a few questions online and gets a quote within minutes, buys the policy, and receives proof of insurance immediately. Digital certificates of insurance can be downloaded or sent to clients whenever needed, which saves time for tradespeople who must prove coverage to customers.

The company offers coverage lines such as general liability, professional liability, workers' compensation, commercial auto, and tools and equipment. Its software uses data and automation to underwrite quickly and to handle policy changes and claims, with many tasks done in an app or on the website. The insurer is rated by industry agencies for financial strength.

NEXT was acquired in full by ERGO, part of Munich Re, and rebranded to include the ERGO name. Policies are sold in the United States, and availability and pricing differ by state and by business type. Online quotes are convenient, but complex businesses may benefit from an agent who can compare options. Competitors include Hiscox, Thimble, Pie Insurance, Insureon, and traditional carriers like The Hartford and Travelers. It suits small businesses with straightforward needs.
