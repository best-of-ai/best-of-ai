Qianfan is Baidu's cloud platform for building with large models. Companies can call Baidu's ERNIE models and a selection of third-party and open models through a common API, then build applications such as chatbots, search over internal documents, and automated workflows. The platform covers the steps in between, including prompt engineering, fine-tuning, evaluation, and deployment.

It includes tools for agents and retrieval, with knowledge-base features that let applications answer from a company's own data, plus plugins and workflows for connecting to business systems. Qianfan is used heavily within China, where Baidu offers local hosting and compliance with domestic regulations.

Qianfan is sold on usage-based pricing with enterprise contracts. Outside China, availability and support are more limited, and organizations must consider data residency and regulatory requirements. Alternatives include Alibaba Cloud's Bailian, Tencent Hunyuan, Volcano Engine from ByteDance, and Western platforms like AWS Bedrock, Azure AI Foundry, and Google Vertex AI. Baidu Qianfan suits companies building AI applications on Baidu's ecosystem.
