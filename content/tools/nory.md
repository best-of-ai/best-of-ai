Nory connects the parts of restaurant operations that usually live in separate tools. Its forecasting engine predicts demand, and the other modules use that forecast: scheduling sets staffing levels, ordering suggests how much stock to buy, and payroll and invoicing handle the paperwork that follows.

The platform describes its AI as a set of assistants for forecasting, scheduling, ordering, compliance, payroll, invoicing, and customer reviews. The idea is that managers review and approve recommendations instead of building schedules and orders by hand each week. It serves independent brands, franchise groups, multi-location chains, and enterprise operators.

Nory does not publish pricing. You book a chat or a demo. Competing products tend to cover one area, such as 7shifts for scheduling or MarketMan for inventory, while Nory aims to cover the whole back office. It makes the most sense for operators with several locations where small forecasting errors add up.
