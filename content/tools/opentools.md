OpenTools is a catalog for finding AI products. Visitors search or filter by what they want to do, see short descriptions and pricing information, and follow links to the tools. Beyond products, the site lists models and Model Context Protocol servers, which are useful to developers building agents.

Guides and news help readers understand what is new and how to use tools well. Builders can submit their products for listing, which is how most directories grow.

OpenTools is free to use. As with any directory, check each tool's own site for current prices and privacy terms. Similar resources include Futurepedia, There's An AI For That, Future Tools, Aixploria, and Best of AI. OpenTools suits people who want to discover AI tools and MCP servers.
