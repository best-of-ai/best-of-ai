Kixie speeds up phone outreach. A power dialer calls through a list automatically, local presence shows numbers with the prospect's area code to improve answer rates, and voicemail drop leaves a prerecorded message with one click. Calls and texts sync with CRMs such as HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive.

Automation sends text messages and follow-ups triggered by call outcomes, and a coaching feature lets managers listen in. AI transcribes calls, summarizes them, and gives feedback on conversations. Analytics track activity, connect rates, and results by rep.

Kixie is paid, with plans per user, and outbound calling and texting are subject to consent and do-not-call rules. Alternatives include Aircall, JustCall, Orum, Dialpad, and Salesloft. Kixie suits small and mid-sized sales teams that live on the phone.
