Litera is known for products lawyers use daily. Its comparison software shows exactly what changed between document versions, a core need in contract negotiation, and drafting tools check formatting, cross-references, and defined terms. Other products handle transaction management and closing checklists.

Newer AI features help review documents, summarize them, and spot inconsistencies or risk. The company has combined many acquired products into a suite for law firms and corporate legal departments, and it integrates with Microsoft Word and document management systems.

Litera is sold on subscription to firms and legal teams. Lawyers remain responsible for checking AI output. Alternatives include Workshare (Litera), Thomson Reuters Drafting Assistant, Luminance, Harvey, and Ivo. Litera suits law firms looking for drafting and review tools.
