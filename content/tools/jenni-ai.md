Jenni AI is designed around the way academics write. In its editor, you start a document and Jenni suggests how to continue sentences, offers to expand or rephrase text, and helps structure arguments. It can generate an outline from a topic and help draft sections, with the writer keeping control and deciding what to accept.

Research features distinguish it from general writing tools. You can search for academic sources, insert citations in styles such as APA and MLA, and manage a bibliography. A feature lets you upload PDFs and ask questions about them, so you can use sources more efficiently. The tool also supports different citation formats and exports to common document formats.

Jenni AI has a free tier with limited AI use and paid plans for unlimited use. Academic integrity is the central issue: many institutions restrict AI-written work, and AI can produce wrong or fabricated citations, so every reference must be verified and policies followed. Alternatives include Grammarly, Paperpal, SciSpace, Elicit for research, and general assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude. Jenni suits students and researchers who want help drafting and citing, within their institution's rules.
