Wobbio is a website builder that works the way you think: describe the site you want, and it creates a polished, on-brand design in minutes—no code, no dragging blocks around. Start from a template or generate a site from scratch, then click any section and tell the AI what to change (new layout, rewritten copy, different tone, extra sections). Everything is responsive by default.

It's built for speed: live previews while you edit and one-click global publishing. You can run multiple sites and domains from the same account. Business basics are included: free SSL, SEO features like sitemaps/meta tags, and professional email on your domain. Need to sell? Wobbio includes e-commerce for physical or digital products. Built-in forms and a media hub keep assets organized and fast.

Teams and agencies get sharing capabilities with permissions and easy client handoff, plus a safe editing mode with checkpoints so you can roll back changes. Privacy-first, built and hosted in Europe.
