ComplyAdvantage provides financial crime tools for banks, fintechs, and other regulated firms. At onboarding, it checks whether a customer or company appears on sanctions lists, politically exposed person lists, or negative news, and continues to monitor them over time. Transaction monitoring flags unusual activity that may signal money laundering.

Its data comes from a large collection of sources that is structured and updated continuously, and machine learning ranks matches to cut down on false alarms that compliance staff would otherwise review by hand. Case management tools help analysts document decisions for regulators, and risk assessment features support a risk-based approach.

ComplyAdvantage is sold on enterprise subscriptions priced by usage. Screening always involves a tradeoff between catching risk and creating noise, and firms remain legally responsible for compliance. Alternatives include LexisNexis Risk Solutions, Dow Jones, Refinitiv World-Check, Sumsub, and Sardine. ComplyAdvantage suits regulated firms managing financial crime risk.
