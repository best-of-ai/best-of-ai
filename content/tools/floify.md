Floify handles the document stage of mortgage lending. Loan officers invite borrowers to a secure portal where they upload pay stubs, bank statements, and other items, sign disclosures electronically, and watch the status of their loan. The portal carries the lender's branding.

Automation reduces manual follow-up. The system sends reminders for missing documents, sorts and labels uploads, and updates borrowers and real estate agents as milestones are reached. It connects to loan origination systems so data moves between them. Analytics show where loans get stuck.

Floify is sold by subscription to mortgage originators. Borrowers still need clear communication from a human loan officer. Alternatives include Blend, Beanstalk, Encompass from ICE Mortgage Technology, LendingPad, and Maxwell. Floify suits mortgage professionals who want a simple borrower portal.
