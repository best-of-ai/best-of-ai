Fiix is maintenance management software used in manufacturing, oil and gas, food and beverage, and heavy equipment. Teams use it to schedule preventive maintenance, create and track work orders, manage assets and spare parts, and see where downtime comes from. It is a product of Rockwell Automation.

The AI features include Fiix MAX, an assistant for maintenance questions and tasks, plus work order insights, asset alerts, and a parts forecaster. These aim to help technicians find problems earlier and keep the right parts on hand. Mobile access lets technicians update work orders in the field.

A free plan is available for getting started, with paid tiers for more features. IBM Maximo targets larger enterprises with heavier needs, and UpKeep is a similar mid-market option. Fiix suits maintenance and facilities teams moving off spreadsheets or paper.
