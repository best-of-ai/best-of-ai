Moralis provides ready-made APIs for blockchain data. Instead of reading raw blocks, developers can ask for a wallet's token balances, NFT holdings, transaction history, or prices and receive clean results across multiple chains, such as Ethereum, Polygon, Solana, and others. This cuts down the time to build wallets, portfolio trackers, NFT marketplaces, and analytics tools.

Other products support app development. Authentication lets users sign in with their wallets, Streams delivers real-time notifications about on-chain events, and node access and data APIs support decentralized finance and gaming projects. Documentation and SDKs help developers integrate quickly, and the platform handles indexing and scaling.

Moralis has a free tier with request limits and paid plans for more usage, with enterprise options. Blockchain markets are volatile, and dependence on a hosted data provider is a trade-off for decentralized apps. Alternatives include Alchemy, Infura, QuickNode, Covalent, The Graph, and Dune for analytics. Moralis suits developers who want fast access to multi-chain data.
