HyperWrite started as a writing tool that predicted and completed text as you typed. It grew into a suite that includes a document editor, a browser extension that works across websites, and tools for rewriting, summarizing, and researching. Users can generate text from prompts and refine it in place.

The company has focused on agents. An assistant can navigate web pages, fill in forms, and complete multi-step tasks in the browser on a user's behalf, which fits its idea of a personal AI assistant. It supports multiple underlying models.

HyperWrite has a free tier and paid plans. Agent features need supervision since they act on real sites. Alternatives include Grammarly, Jasper, Wordtune, ChatGPT, and Claude. HyperWrite suits people who want writing help and light browser automation.
