Hour One produces videos with AI avatars. A user chooses a virtual presenter, pastes a script, and the platform generates a video of the presenter speaking, with options for language, voice, and background. Custom avatars can be created from real people with their consent.

It is aimed at companies that need to make many videos, such as training modules and product updates, without arranging film shoots each time. Changing a script and regenerating the video is faster than reshooting, and translations extend the same video to new languages.

Hour One is paid, with plans by usage. Avatars can feel less natural than real presenters, and disclosure that content is AI-generated builds trust. Alternatives include Synthesia, HeyGen, Elai.io, Colossyan, and D-ID. Hour One suits teams producing scripted video at scale.
