Nabla works as a scribe in the exam room. During a visit, with consent, it captures the conversation and creates a structured note in the clinician's preferred format, which they review and copy into the health record. It also drafts letters and patient instructions from the same encounter.

The company began in France and has expanded to the United States and elsewhere, with support for multiple languages and specialties. It emphasizes privacy, including not retaining audio after processing, and integrates with electronic health record systems.

Nabla has individual and enterprise plans. Notes can contain errors, so clinicians must review every output, and patient consent is required. Alternatives include Abridge, Heidi Health, Suki, DeepScribe, and Microsoft Dragon Copilot. Nabla suits clinicians and health systems seeking an ambient scribe.
