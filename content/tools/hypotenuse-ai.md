Hypotenuse AI is built for online stores with large catalogs. You upload a spreadsheet of products with attributes, and it writes descriptions for each one, in a consistent tone and optimized for search, saving the effort of writing hundreds or thousands of descriptions by hand. It can also produce category pages, meta descriptions, blog posts, and ad copy.

Features support brand and workflow. A brand voice setting keeps text aligned with a company's style, a plagiarism check helps ensure uniqueness, and integrations connect with Shopify and other platforms to publish content directly. The product also offers AI image tools for creating and editing product images and backgrounds, which makes it a toolkit for content across a catalog.

Hypotenuse is paid, with a trial, and plans scale by the amount of content. As with all bulk AI copy, descriptions can be generic or inaccurate if the product data is thin, so review a sample before publishing everything. Alternatives include Jasper, Copy.ai, Writesonic, Shopify Magic for Shopify stores, and general assistants like ChatGPT with spreadsheet uploads. Hypotenuse suits e-commerce teams that must produce descriptions at scale.
