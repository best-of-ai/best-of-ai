Infermedica builds medical decision support for patient-facing use. A person answers questions about symptoms in an adaptive interview, and the system estimates likely conditions and recommends a level of care, such as self-care, a doctor visit, or emergency services. Organizations embed this in their apps and websites.

The engine rests on a medical knowledge base maintained by physicians, combined with probabilistic reasoning and language models for understanding free text. Use cases include directing patients to the right service, preparing clinicians with an intake summary, and reducing unnecessary visits.

Infermedica is sold to healthcare organizations through API access and licensing. Symptom checkers give guidance, not diagnoses, and users with serious symptoms should seek care promptly. Alternatives include Ada Health, Babylon's tools (now closed), K Health, Isabel, and Buoy. Infermedica suits digital health providers adding triage.
