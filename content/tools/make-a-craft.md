Make A Craft helps parents create personalised, age-appropriate craft projects for their kids using everyday items found at home. Just enter your child’s age, interests, and the materials you already have, and the app generates fun, easy-to-follow craft ideas with step-by-step instructions and visuals — no extra supplies or planning required.

Perfect for rainy days, weekends, or spontaneous creative sessions, Make A Craft is built for families who want screen-free, meaningful time together. It uses a simple credit-based system (with 1 free credit to start), so you only pay for the guides you use — no subscriptions, no fuss.
