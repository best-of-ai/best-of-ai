The Operations Center connects Deere equipment to farm management. Tractors, planters, sprayers, and combines send data about where they worked, what they applied, and how crops yielded, and farmers see this on field maps. Records of planting, spraying, and harvest are kept in one place and can be shared with advisors.

From that data, the platform helps plan the next season. Prescriptions vary seed or fertilizer rates across a field, and analytics compare performance between fields and years. Integrations with other agronomy software and equipment from other makers extend its reach, and AI is used for tasks such as image-based crop and weed detection on machines.

The Operations Center is free to use at a basic level, with paid connectivity and add-ons, and it is most useful for farms running Deere equipment. Data ownership and sharing terms deserve a careful read. Alternatives include Climate FieldView, Trimble Ag, Granular, Farmers Edge, and CNH's AFS Connect. John Deere Operations Center suits farmers using Deere machines.
