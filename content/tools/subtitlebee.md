SubtitleBee automates subtitles. You upload a video, choose the spoken language, and the tool transcribes it and creates timed captions, which you can edit in a simple editor. It supports many languages and can translate subtitles into others, helping creators reach international audiences. Typical users are YouTubers, social media managers, educators, and marketers, who need captions because many viewers watch with the sound off.

Style options let captions fit a brand: you can change fonts, colors, positions, and highlight words as they are spoken, which is a popular look on short-form video. Subtitles can be exported as files for use in other editors or burned into the video itself. Plans are tied to the amount of video processed, and the product is in a crowded category.

SubtitleBee is paid, with plans by video minutes, and may offer a limited trial. Accuracy depends on audio quality, accents, and background noise, so always review captions before publishing. Other tools offer similar features along with broader editing, including CapCut, Kapwing, VEED, Descript, and Submagic. YouTube also generates automatic captions at no cost. SubtitleBee suits creators who want a focused captioning tool with strong language coverage.
