Mutiny changes what a website says depending on the visitor. If a visitor comes from a target account, an industry, or a campaign, Mutiny swaps in headlines, proof points, and calls to action that fit, using firmographic data and ad information. Marketers set this up in a visual editor without developers.

AI recommends segments and generates alternative copy, then tests variations and reports on conversions. Integrations connect with CRMs, ad platforms, and customer data tools so personalization can follow account-based marketing programs.

Mutiny is sold to B2B companies on custom pricing. It needs enough traffic to test and a clear ideal customer profile. Alternatives include Optimizely, Dynamic Yield, Intellimize (Webflow), HubSpot personalization, and Demandbase. Mutiny suits B2B marketing teams with account-based strategies.
