Headway offers book summaries you can read or listen to in minutes. Each summary covers the main ideas of a nonfiction title, such as business, psychology, or self-improvement, so users can sample many books quickly. Audio versions suit commutes and workouts.

The app leans on habit building. Users set daily goals, track streaks, and get reminders, and collections organize summaries by theme and goal. Quizzes and flashcards help retain what was read, and AI features personalize recommendations.

Headway has a limited free tier and a subscription, often discounted at signup. Summaries are no substitute for reading a book in depth, and they reflect the editors' choices. Alternatives include Blinkist, Shortform, getAbstract, Readwise, and Audible. Headway suits busy readers who want ideas in short form.
