Relay.app takes the Zapier-style approach and adds two things: AI steps and humans in the loop. A workflow can trigger on an event, call an AI to summarize or draft, then pause for a teammate to approve before the next action runs. This suits tasks where full automation is risky.

Integrations connect common business tools, and a visual builder makes flows easy to design. Examples include drafting replies for review, routing leads, and preparing reports. Approvals can be done in email or Slack.

Relay.app has a free plan and paid tiers by usage. It is younger and smaller than the largest automation platforms. Alternatives include Zapier, Make, n8n, Lindy, and Gumloop. Relay.app suits teams that want automation with oversight.
