Stockcake offers a searchable library of images made with AI. Users browse or search by topic, preview photos, and download them for use in websites, presentations, and social posts without payment or attribution in many cases. The library is aimed at covering common stock needs.

The advantage is speed and no cost. The drawbacks are those of AI imagery: oddities in hands or text, a generic look, and uncertain legal protection around AI-generated content and likenesses. People depicted are not real, which avoids model release issues.

Stockcake is free. Check its terms for commercial use. Alternatives include Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Lummi, and Freepik. Stockcake suits creators needing free stock images.
