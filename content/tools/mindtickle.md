Mindtickle is a sales readiness platform. New hires follow structured learning paths with lessons and quizzes, and practice pitches by recording videos or role-playing with AI, receiving feedback. Managers review the practice and see how reps compare on skills.

The platform links training to results. It tracks which skills correlate with success, identifies gaps across a team, and connects with CRM data to see how readiness affects performance. AI features generate content, score practice sessions, and recommend coaching.

Mindtickle is sold to mid-sized and large sales organizations on custom pricing. Its effect depends on managers using it consistently. Alternatives include Highspot, Seismic, Gong, Showpad, and Salesloft. Mindtickle suits sales enablement teams.
