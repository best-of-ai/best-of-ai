Raw Shorts aims to make animated explainers accessible to non-designers. You paste a script, an article, or an idea, and the AI breaks it into scenes, chooses animated templates, characters, and icons, and adds a voiceover and music. You can then edit scenes, change visuals, adjust timing, and brand the video with your colors and logo before exporting.

It's used for marketing explainers, training videos, social clips, and educational content. A library of animated styles, text-to-speech voices in many languages, and options for adding your own media let teams produce a polished video in a short time. Newer features include generating videos from blog posts or presentations.

Raw Shorts has a free plan with limits and a watermark, and paid plans for HD downloads, more minutes, and removal of branding. The output follows a recognizable template style, which suits explainers but not high-end brand films. Alternatives include Powtoon, Animaker, Vyond, Canva, Renderforest, and VEED. Raw Shorts suits small teams that want quick animated explainer videos.
