Bardeen automates repetitive work that happens on web pages. A user installs the extension, then runs playbooks that click through sites, extract data, and send it elsewhere. Typical uses include scraping lists of people from LinkedIn into Google Sheets, saving a company's details to a CRM, or sending a meeting summary to Slack.

A natural language builder lets users describe what they want, and the AI assembles the steps, which can be edited. Triggers run automations on a schedule or when something happens, and integrations cover hundreds of apps. Sales and recruiting teams, who spend time moving data between tabs, are heavy users.

Bardeen has a free plan and paid tiers by credits and features. Browser automations can break when websites change their layout, and scraping data from sites must respect their terms and privacy rules. Alternatives include Zapier, Make, Browse AI, Clay, and browser agents. Bardeen suits professionals who want to automate web tasks without code.
