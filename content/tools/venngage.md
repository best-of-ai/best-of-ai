Venngage targets people who need to communicate information visually. Users start from thousands of templates for infographics, reports, flyers, presentations, and social graphics, replace the content, and customize colors, icons, and charts. Data can be imported to generate charts, and the tool includes maps and diagrams for explaining processes and comparisons.

It differentiates itself with business features. Brand kits apply logos, fonts, and colors automatically, team workspaces allow collaboration and approvals, and an accessibility checker flags color contrast and other issues. AI tools generate infographic designs from text, write copy, and suggest improvements, and designs can be exported or shared with analytics.

Venngage has a free plan with limited downloads and paid plans for teams and businesses. Template designs can look familiar, and complex data visualization is better served by specialist tools. Alternatives include Canva, Piktochart, Visme, Infogram, and Adobe Express. Venngage suits marketers, educators, and small businesses creating infographics and reports.
