Wordsmith turns data into text. Users define templates and logic, connect a data source, and the platform writes a narrative for each record, such as a financial summary, a sports recap, or a personalized report. The output is consistent and can be produced in volume.

It predates today's chat models. Rule-based natural language generation gives predictable text, which suits regulated reporting where exact control matters, while large language models offer flexibility and fluency but less predictability.

Wordsmith is sold on enterprise pricing. Check the company's site for current status, since newer language model tools now cover many of the same tasks. Alternatives include Arria NLG, Narrative Science (acquired by Salesforce), ChatGPT and Claude with structured prompts, and Yseop. Wordsmith suits teams automating data-driven reports.
