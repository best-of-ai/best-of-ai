SAM TTS is a free, browser-based text-to-speech tool that recreates the classic Microsoft SAM voice from Windows XP. Without needing to download or sign up, users can input text, adjust vocal parameters like pitch, speed, mouth, and throat, and instantly hear the iconic robotic voice that defined early 2000s computing. SAM TTS also offers fun preset voice styles such as Elf, Robot, and Old Lady, making it ideal for creative expression, retro game development, and educational demonstrations. With one-click WAV export, it's easy to integrate into videos, games, or personal projects. Whether you're a nostalgic user or a content creator, SAM Text to Speech provides a unique blend of old-school charm and modern convenience.
