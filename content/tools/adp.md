ADP is one of the best-known payroll companies in the world. It pays employees, files payroll taxes, and manages benefits administration, time and attendance, hiring, and HR records. Products range from simple small-business payroll to large, multi-country platforms for enterprises, and many companies outsource payroll entirely to ADP to ensure accuracy and compliance with changing tax rules.

Technology is increasingly part of the offering. ADP uses AI for assistants that answer HR and payroll questions, flag errors before payroll runs, and give managers insight into workforce trends, using its large dataset of payroll information to benchmark pay and turnover. The platform integrates with accounting and HR systems, and mobile apps let employees see pay stubs and request time off.

ADP is sold by quote with pricing based on employee count and services, and a PEO option where ADP co-employs workers and handles benefits and compliance. It can be expensive and complex compared with newer systems. Competitors include Paychex, Gusto and Rippling for small and mid-sized companies, Workday and UKG for larger employers, and Paycom. ADP suits companies that value scale, compliance support, and a long track record.
