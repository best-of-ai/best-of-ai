Thinkific is an online course platform. Creators build courses from video, audio, text, and quizzes, organize them into chapters, and host them on a site with their own branding and domain. Students log in to learn, and the platform tracks completion and issues certificates.

Features extend beyond courses. Memberships and communities keep learners engaged, bundles and subscriptions provide pricing options, and integrations connect with email tools, payment processors, and Zapier. Businesses also use Thinkific to train customers, partners, and employees, and AI tools help generate course outlines and quiz questions.

Thinkific has a free plan with limited features and paid plans with more customization and lower fees. Marketing remains the creator's responsibility, since the platform does not bring students by itself. Alternatives include Teachable, Kajabi, Podia, LearnWorlds, and Udemy. Thinkific suits creators and companies who want to own their course business.
