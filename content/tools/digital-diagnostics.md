Digital Diagnostics offers autonomous AI for eye screening. A clinic staff member takes photos of a patient's retina with a compatible camera, the software analyzes the images, and within minutes the clinic gets a result: either the screening is negative, or the patient should see an eye specialist. No ophthalmologist needs to read the images.

That matters because many people with diabetes skip yearly eye exams, and early detection of retinopathy prevents vision loss. Putting screening in a primary care office makes it easier to catch disease. The company also develops AI for other conditions and works with health systems on implementation and billing.

The system is sold to clinics and health systems. It screens for one condition and does not replace a full eye exam, and image quality problems can require retakes. Alternatives include Eyenuk's EyeArt, Optomed, AEYE Health, and telemedicine review by specialists. Digital Diagnostics suits primary care providers who screen diabetic patients.
