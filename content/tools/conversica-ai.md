Conversica automates the follow-up that sales teams often drop. When a lead comes in, the AI assistant sends personalized messages, reads replies, answers questions, and tries to book a meeting. If a prospect does not respond, it continues with a sequence of nudges, which keeps leads from going cold.

The assistants are used in industries such as automotive, education, software, and financial services, where large volumes of inquiries arrive. Conversation data flows into the CRM, so salespeople see which leads are qualified and what was said. The company has added generative AI to make conversations more natural.

Conversica is sold on custom contracts. Automated persistence can annoy prospects if it is too aggressive, and messages must follow anti-spam and consent rules. Alternatives include Drift (part of Salesloft), Qualified, 11x, Regie.ai, and Outreach. Conversica suits teams with many inbound leads and limited follow-up capacity.
