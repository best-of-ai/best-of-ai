Outreach is used by sales teams to run structured prospecting and follow-up at scale. Reps enroll leads in sequences that combine emails, calls, LinkedIn steps, and tasks, and the platform schedules each step, tracks replies, and logs activity to the CRM. Managers can see which messages and cadences perform best, and automated rules route leads and keep work organized.

Over time, Outreach has added revenue intelligence and AI. Conversation intelligence records and analyzes calls, deal health scoring flags risk, and forecasting tools combine activity and pipeline data to estimate outcomes. AI assistants summarize meetings, draft emails, suggest next steps, and increasingly act as agents that research accounts or manage parts of the pipeline.

Outreach is sold to sales organizations under annual per-seat contracts, with higher tiers for more features, and is priced for mid-sized and large teams. It takes time to implement well, and small teams may find lighter tools sufficient. Competitors include Salesloft, Apollo, Gong for revenue intelligence, HubSpot Sales Hub, and Clari. As with all outbound tools, deliverability and compliance with laws on unsolicited email are the user's responsibility.
