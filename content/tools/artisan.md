Artisan describes its products as autonomous teammates rather than assistants. Ava, the AI BDR, works through the outbound sales process: building a list of prospects from a database, researching each, writing personalized messages, sending them, and handling follow-up. The company aims mainly at startups and small teams that cannot hire a full sales development group.

Ava connects to a company's email, CRM, and calendar, and can book meetings that appear for human salespeople. The company also talks about expanding to other roles. Automation can increase outreach volume, but results depend on how well the target list and message match the market.

Reviews and the company's own materials differ on how much Ava can handle autonomously, such as responding to objections, so expect to supervise it. Cold outreach must follow anti-spam and privacy rules. Artisan sells by subscription, with a trial. Alternatives include 11x, Clay, Apollo, Outreach, and human SDRs. Artisan suits small sales teams testing AI outbound.
