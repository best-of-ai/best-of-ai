ChurnZero is built for customer success teams at software-as-a-service companies. It pulls in product usage data, support tickets, and CRM information to give each account a health score, so a team can see which customers are thriving and which are at risk of leaving before a renewal conversation turns bad.

Automation handles routine outreach. Playbooks trigger tasks and emails when a customer hits a milestone or goes quiet, in-app messages guide users inside the product, and dashboards track renewals, adoption, and revenue. AI features summarize accounts, draft messages, and suggest next actions, and a customer-facing portal can share progress with clients.

ChurnZero is sold on custom pricing by company size and modules. It works best when product usage data is connected, which takes integration work. Alternatives include Gainsight, Totango, Vitally, Planhat, and the customer success features of HubSpot and Salesforce. ChurnZero suits mid-sized subscription companies with a dedicated customer success team.
