Kaspr is a contact data tool for outbound sales. While browsing a LinkedIn profile, a user clicks the Kaspr extension to reveal a professional email address and phone number, then saves the contact to a CRM or a list. It also supports bulk searches and enrichment of existing records.

The company emphasizes European data and compliance with privacy rules like GDPR, and offers ways for individuals to opt out of the database. Credits limit how many contact details a user can reveal each month, and integrations connect with CRMs and sales engagement tools.

Kaspr has a free tier and paid plans by credits. Data accuracy varies, and outreach must follow privacy and anti-spam laws. Alternatives include Lusha, Cognism, Apollo, ZoomInfo, and Seamless.AI. Kaspr suits sales teams prospecting on LinkedIn, especially in Europe.
