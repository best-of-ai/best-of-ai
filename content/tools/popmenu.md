Popmenu started as a restaurant website and interactive menu product and now covers much of a restaurant's online presence. The platform includes website design and hosting, online ordering, digital remarketing, and Google Business Profile management, with dashboards that track marketing, ordering, and website performance.

The AI features are the reason many operators look at it. Popmenu's AI answering works as a virtual host that picks up calls around the clock, answers routine questions about hours and menu items, and texts guests links for orders or reservations. It reads details from the restaurant's website and Google Business Profile, so answers match what the restaurant has published. Another feature scans the website and recommends social posts each week.

Pricing is not listed publicly and depends on which modules you use. Phone answering is also sold separately. Toast covers ordering and point of sale more deeply, and Synthflow is a general voice agent builder. Popmenu is a fit for restaurants that want marketing, website, and phone coverage from one vendor.
