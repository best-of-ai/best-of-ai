Boundless helps people navigate US immigration paperwork. Users answer questions in a guided flow, and the software builds the forms needed for a case, such as a marriage-based green card or naturalization. Checks flag missing information and inconsistencies that often cause delays.

Human experts remain part of the process. Plans include review by immigration attorneys or specialists, and customers can get help with responding to requests from authorities. Tracking tools show deadlines and the status of the case, and educational material explains steps and timelines.

Boundless is paid, typically as a package or subscription, and costs less than a full-service law firm for straightforward cases. Complex situations, such as past violations or criminal history, call for an attorney's advice. Immigration rules change, so check current requirements. Alternatives include Docketwise, Lawfully, Fragomen for corporate cases, and independent attorneys. Boundless suits individuals with typical family-based filings.
