Lifesum helps people understand what they eat. Users set a goal such as weight loss, maintenance, or muscle gain, then log food and drink by searching, scanning barcodes, or snapping photos. The app shows calories, macros, and nutrient balance, and gives feedback through scores and summaries.

Meal plans and recipes support particular diets, such as low carb, Mediterranean, or intermittent fasting. Integrations with fitness trackers and health apps bring in activity data. AI features help with logging foods from photos and offer personalized suggestions.

Lifesum has a free tier and a premium subscription. Calorie tracking works for some people and can be unhealthy for others, especially those with a history of disordered eating. Food databases contain errors. Alternatives include MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, Yazio, Noom, and MacroFactor. Lifesum suits people who want a friendly approach to tracking.
