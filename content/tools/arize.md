Arize helps teams see what their models are doing after deployment. For classic machine learning, it tracks performance, detects when input data drifts away from training data, and helps identify which segments of users a model is failing. Alerts point teams to problems before they affect the business.

For LLM applications, it traces each request through the chain of prompts, retrieval, and tool calls, and runs evaluations that score responses for relevance, hallucination, and safety. This helps developers find why an answer was wrong and test fixes. Arize Phoenix, the company's open-source tool, offers tracing and evaluation that can run locally.

Arize has free and open-source options and paid enterprise plans. Setting up evaluations well takes thought, since automated judges can be inconsistent. Alternatives include Langfuse, LangSmith, Weights & Biases Weave, Braintrust, Datadog LLM Observability, and Fiddler. Arize suits ML and AI teams running models in production.
