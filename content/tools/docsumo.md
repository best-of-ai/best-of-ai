Docsumo turns documents into structured data. A company uploads or emails PDFs and scans such as invoices, receipts, bank statements, and forms, and the platform reads fields and tables, checks them against rules, and sends clean data to accounting, lending, or ERP systems. This replaces manual entry.

A review interface lets staff correct uncertain fields, and corrections improve accuracy over time. Pre-trained models cover common documents, while custom models handle company-specific layouts. Workflows include validation rules, approvals, and integrations through APIs. Lenders and finance teams are major users.

Docsumo has a free trial and paid plans by document volume. Accuracy depends on document quality, and sensitive financial documents call for attention to security. Alternatives include Rossum, Nanonets, Hypatos, ABBYY, and Amazon Textract. Docsumo suits finance and operations teams processing many documents.
