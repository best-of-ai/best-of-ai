MyHeritage is a genealogy service. Users create a tree, and the site suggests matches in historical records and in other members' trees, helping them find ancestors and living relatives. A DNA test adds ethnicity estimates and connects users with genetic relatives.

The company is known for photo tools. It can colorize black-and-white pictures, enhance old photos, and animate faces in family photographs, which went viral and made genealogy more emotional for many users. Other AI features transcribe and search documents.

MyHeritage has a free tier and paid subscriptions, with DNA kits sold separately. Record hints are not always right, and DNA data raises privacy considerations. Alternatives include Ancestry, FamilySearch, 23andMe, and Findmypast. MyHeritage suits people exploring family history.
