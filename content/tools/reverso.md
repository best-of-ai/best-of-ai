Reverso is known for context. Beyond a plain translation, it shows examples of the word or phrase used in real texts, such as books, subtitles, and documents, with the original and translation side by side. That helps learners and translators choose the right expression, understand nuance, and see how phrases are typically used in each language.

The product includes several tools. A translator handles text and documents, Reverso Context is the example search, and a grammar and spell checker works across languages. Verb conjugators, vocabulary lists, and flashcards support learning, and AI features paraphrase and improve text. Browser extensions and mobile apps make it available while reading or writing.

Reverso has a free version with ads and limits and a Premium subscription that removes ads and adds more features, such as unlimited translation and offline use. Examples come from automatically aligned texts, so quality varies. Alternatives include DeepL, Google Translate, Linguee, Collins and Larousse dictionaries, and LanguageTool for grammar. Reverso suits language learners, writers, and translators who care about how phrases are really used.
