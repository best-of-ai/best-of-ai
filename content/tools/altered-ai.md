Altered focuses on voice conversion, sometimes called speech-to-speech. You record a performance, and the system re-voices it in another voice from its library while keeping the timing, emotion, and delivery of the original. That differs from text-to-speech, where the machine invents the performance. For productions, it means one actor can voice several characters, or a performance can be changed to match a different character without a second recording session.

The tools are designed for professionals. Altered Studio provides a way to process recordings and fine-tune results, and the platform includes text-to-speech and voice cloning options for productions that need them. Typical customers are game studios, audiobook makers, animation and film teams, and agencies that localize content. Licensed voices matter in this field, since using someone's voice without permission raises legal and ethical issues.

Altered is paid, with plans that scale by usage, and the company works with studios on custom arrangements. Quality depends on the input recording and on how different the target voice is from the original. There is limited public information on recent updates, so check current availability before relying on it. ElevenLabs offers a voice changer along with a broad audio toolkit, Respeecher is a well-known professional voice conversion provider, and Resemble AI covers cloning and conversion for developers.
