Bland provides voice agents that talk on real phone calls. Companies use them to answer inbound calls, call leads, confirm appointments, and run surveys, replacing or supplementing call center staff. The agents speak naturally, handle interruptions, and can work around the clock.

Developers control behavior through a system of Conversational Pathways, which define the steps and branches of a call, along with tools to call APIs, look up records, and update systems. The platform supports many languages and voices, call analytics, and handoff to human agents. It offers APIs for scaling to large volumes of calls.

Bland is sold on usage-based and enterprise pricing. Voice AI raises regulatory points, including consent rules for automated calls and disclosure that the caller is an AI. Alternatives include Vapi, Retell AI, Synthflow, Twilio ConversationRelay, and PolyAI. Bland suits companies automating phone conversations at scale.
