Showpad helps sellers find and use approved content. Marketing publishes decks, videos, and guides, and reps get recommendations based on the customer and deal stage. Buyers receive shared links that track engagement, which tells sellers what resonated.

Coaching and training are the other half. Reps practice pitches and receive AI feedback, learning paths build skills, and managers see progress. The platform integrates with CRMs and works well in industries such as manufacturing, healthcare, and technology.

Showpad is sold on enterprise subscriptions. It overlaps with several rivals. Alternatives include Highspot, Seismic, Mindtickle, Salesloft, and HubSpot. Showpad suits mid-sized and large sales teams.
