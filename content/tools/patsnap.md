PatSnap serves teams that need to understand the patent landscape. Its search covers a very large collection of patents and scientific literature worldwide, and semantic search finds relevant documents from a description of an invention instead of exact keywords. Users can analyze who is filing in a technology area, map clusters of related patents, track competitors, and check whether an idea has been patented before.

Several products fit different jobs. Tools help with prior art searches before filing, freedom-to-operate checks before launching a product, and landscape analysis to guide R&D strategy. AI assistants summarize patents, extract key claims, and answer questions about a technology, and workflows let teams share results and collaborate. The platform also covers non-patent literature and market information for innovation scouting.

PatSnap is sold to companies and institutions on subscription with custom pricing, and it's aimed at corporate IP departments, law firms, and universities. Legal decisions, such as filing strategy and infringement assessments, still require qualified patent professionals. Competitors include Derwent Innovation, Orbit from Questel, Lens.org and Google Patents for free searches, and Anaqua and Clarivate for broader IP management. It suits organizations that make regular IP decisions.
