Clearscope helps writers produce content that is complete enough to rank. You enter a keyword, and it analyzes the top results to show the terms, topics, and questions that strong pages tend to include. As you draft in its editor, a grade updates, so you can see whether the page covers what searchers expect. Plugins for Google Docs, Word, and WordPress let writers work in familiar tools instead of switching to a separate app.

The platform covers more than drafting. A discovery tool finds the questions buyers ask, with search volume and intent, and an AI visibility feature shows which engines, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude, mention your brand or competitors. After publication, integration with Google Search Console tracks clicks and content decay, which flags older pages that are losing traffic and need a refresh. Newer options can produce AI-assisted drafts to lift a page's grade.

Clearscope sells monthly plans in tiers, with no long contract and a free trial. It is aimed at agencies and in-house teams that publish often, and it is more expensive than rivals like Surfer SEO, MarketMuse, and Frase. The grade is a guide, not a guarantee: stuffing in suggested terms will not rescue a weak article. Used with judgment, it keeps writers on topic and helps editors compare drafts against the competition.
