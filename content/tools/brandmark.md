Brandmark makes logo design accessible to people without a designer. You type a name and tagline, choose keywords, colors, and styles you like, and its algorithms generate many logo concepts that combine icons, fonts, and layouts. You can edit the colors, fonts, and arrangement, and see how the logo looks on mockups such as business cards and websites.

Rather than a subscription, Brandmark sells one-time packages. The basic package includes a logo in standard file formats, and higher tiers add vector files, social media graphics, a brand style guide, business card templates, and the right to use the design commercially. The vector files can be edited in design software, and the packages focus on getting a usable identity quickly.

AI-generated logos can look generic, and many are similar to others, so they may not be distinctive or trademarkable, which matters for brand protection. For a significant brand, working with a designer is wise. Check ownership terms before relying on the logo legally. Alternatives include Looka, Canva's logo maker, Hatchful, Wix Logo Maker, and Fiverr or 99designs for human designers. Brandmark suits startups and side projects needing an affordable logo.
