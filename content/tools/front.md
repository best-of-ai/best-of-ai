Front treats shared inboxes like collaborative workspaces. Support, sales, and account teams manage messages from several channels in one place, assign conversations, leave internal comments, and see who is handling what. The interface resembles email, so adoption is easy.

Automation and AI reduce manual work. Rules route messages, tag them, and set priorities, while AI summarizes long threads, suggests replies, and answers common questions. Analytics show response times and workloads, and integrations with CRMs and helpdesk tools provide context.

Front is paid, priced per user, with plans by features. It sits between a plain shared inbox and a full ticketing system, which suits teams that value email-style communication. Alternatives include Help Scout, Zendesk, Intercom, Missive, and Gmail shared inboxes. Front suits customer-facing teams that collaborate by message.
