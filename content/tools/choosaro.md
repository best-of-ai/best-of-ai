Choosaro structures the way you decide. You list the options, such as three laptops or two job offers, then define the criteria that matter, like price, quality, or location, and assign each a weight. Scoring the options against the criteria produces a ranked result that shows which choice best fits your priorities.

The method is a weighted decision matrix, a familiar technique that makes tradeoffs explicit. Writing criteria down helps people notice what they really care about and gives teams a shared basis for discussion. AI features can suggest criteria and help fill in details.

A matrix only reflects the numbers you put in, so biased weights lead to biased results. For major decisions, treat the output as input to judgment, not a replacement for it. Choosaro is free. Alternatives include spreadsheets, Notion templates, Airtable, and general assistants like ChatGPT or Claude. Choosaro suits people who like to decide systematically.
