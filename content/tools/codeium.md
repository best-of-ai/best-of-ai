Codeium started as a free alternative to GitHub Copilot, offering code completion and chat in more than 40 editors, among them VS Code, JetBrains IDEs, Vim, and Neovim. Its free individual tier and wide editor support earned it a large user base among developers who did not want to pay for a coding assistant or switch editors. It added features such as natural-language code search, in-editor chat, and support for dozens of programming languages.

The company then moved toward a full AI-first editor and renamed itself Windsurf. Its Cascade agent can plan work, edit several files, and run commands, and the plugin line continues for people who stay in their own editor. Windsurf has since been acquired by Cognition, the company behind the Devin software agent, and has been renamed Devin Desktop, so its direction now ties into that of Devin. Details of the products and plans keep changing as the pieces are integrated.

If you are looking for Codeium today, you will land on Windsurf. The free tier and wide plugin support were the original draw, though the exact limits have shifted, and paid plans offer more usage and stronger models. Enterprise customers have used it with options for self-hosting and strict data controls. Alternatives include GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Cline, and Amazon Q Developer. Older articles and reviews that mention Codeium describe a product that now goes by another name.
