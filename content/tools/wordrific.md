Wordrific aims to simplify content production for marketing teams. A user starts with a topic or keyword, the tool researches what ranks, builds a brief, and drafts an article that follows it. The pieces of the workflow sit in one product instead of several.

B2B content has to be accurate and credible, and AI drafts need expert review and original insight to be useful. Scale without quality does not help rankings.

Independent information is limited. Wordrific has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Surfer, Frase, NeuronWriter, Jasper, and Writesonic. Wordrific suits B2B content teams producing articles regularly.
