Wanderlog combines a travel notebook with a map. You create a trip, add places such as hotels, restaurants, and attractions, and arrange them by day. The app shows everything on a map with travel times between stops, and can optimize the order to reduce backtracking. It imports reservations from your email, tracks expenses, and works offline on mobile apps, which is handy while traveling.

Collaboration is a main feature. Friends and family can edit the same itinerary at once, vote on places, and share notes and budgets. AI tools suggest destinations and activities, generate draft itineraries from a few preferences, and recommend nearby places based on reviews and guides. A catalog of lists and guides provides inspiration for popular destinations.

Wanderlog has a free tier with ads and limits and a Pro plan that unlocks offline use, route optimization, and unlimited attachments. AI itineraries are a starting point and should be checked for opening hours, travel times, and practicality. Tripadvisor, Google Maps lists, TripIt, and Roadtrippers are alternatives, and ChatGPT or Gemini can brainstorm plans. Wanderlog suits travelers who like to organize trips in detail with companions.
