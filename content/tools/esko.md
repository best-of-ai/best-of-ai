Esko serves the packaging and label industries. Designers use ArtiosCAD to create the structural design of boxes and cartons, with dielines that show cuts and folds. ArtPro+ and related tools prepare artwork for printing, handling color, trapping, and layout across the many steps between a design and the press.

Workflow tools automate preparation and approval. Software checks files for errors, compares versions, and routes proofs, which reduces mistakes in printed packaging where a typo can mean a costly reprint. Cloud platforms support collaboration between brands, designers, and printers, and AI helps with checking and automating routine tasks.

Esko is part of Veralto and is sold by license and subscription to brand owners, converters, and printers. Its tools are specialized and take training. Alternatives include Adobe Illustrator with plug-ins, Kongsberg tools, Dalim, and Hybrid Software. Esko suits packaging professionals.
