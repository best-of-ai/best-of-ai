Sketch helped define modern interface design. Designers create screens with vectors and reusable symbols, build design systems, and prototype interactions. Its Mac-native app is fast and has a large plugin ecosystem.

Collaboration moved to the web, where teammates view, comment, and hand off designs, and a Windows-compatible web editor extends access. AI features help generate placeholder content, adjust layouts, and speed up repetitive work.

Sketch is sold by subscription. Figma has taken much of its market because of browser-based collaboration. Alternatives include Figma, Penpot, Adobe XD (no longer developed), Framer, and Affinity Designer. Sketch suits Mac-based designers who prefer a native tool.
