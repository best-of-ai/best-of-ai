Relevance AI lets teams create agents without code. A user defines what an agent does, gives it instructions and tools such as web search, email, or a CRM, and connects knowledge sources. Several agents can be assembled into a team, with one handing work to another.

Common uses are sales development, research, support, and operations. Templates give a starting point, and a monitoring layer shows what agents did and where they need help. Integrations connect with business tools, and agents can run on triggers or schedules.

Relevance AI has a free tier and paid plans with credits. Agents that act on external systems need testing and guardrails. Alternatives include Lindy, Zapier Agents, Cassidy, Dust, and Microsoft Copilot Studio. Relevance AI suits business teams building agent workforces.
