Jason AI acts as an automated sales development rep within Reply.io. It finds prospects, writes personalized emails and LinkedIn messages, sends them, and replies to responses to move conversations toward a meeting. It works within Reply.io's sequencing system, which also handles calls and tasks.

Users give it information about their product and target customers, and Jason builds campaigns. A human can review messages and take over when a lead is interested. The goal is to let small teams reach more prospects without hiring more reps.

Jason AI is part of Reply.io's paid offering. Automated outreach must follow email and privacy laws, and poor targeting can damage sender reputation. Alternatives include Artisan, 11x, Apollo, Outreach, and Instantly. Jason AI suits teams already using Reply.io.
