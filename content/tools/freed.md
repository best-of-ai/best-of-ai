Freed helps clinicians finish notes faster. During a visit, with patient consent, it records the conversation and produces a structured note in a familiar format, which the clinician reviews, edits, and copies into the record. It also drafts instructions for the patient.

It is popular with independent practitioners, such as physicians, nurse practitioners, and therapists, because it is easy to set up and priced for individuals. Features include templates by specialty, custom note styles, and a learning feature that adapts to how a clinician writes.

Freed has a free trial and a subscription. Clinicians must review every note because errors can appear, and they need patient consent and compliance with privacy rules. Alternatives include Heidi Health, Abridge, Suki, DeepScribe, and Nabla. Freed suits small practices and solo clinicians.
