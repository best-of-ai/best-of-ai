Writesonic began as a template-driven AI writer for marketing copy. You choose a type of content, such as a blog post, ad, product description, or social caption, fill in some details, and get a draft. A longer-form article writer builds full posts with an outline, headings, and optional SEO guidance, and a chat assistant answers questions and can search the web. A browser extension and integrations with WordPress and other tools help get content published.

The product has shifted toward search visibility. Alongside traditional SEO features such as keyword research, content scoring, and optimization suggestions, it offers tools that monitor whether a brand is mentioned in answers from AI assistants and search engines, and suggest content to improve those results. Teams can set brand voice, keep a library of approved information, and use AI agents to automate recurring content tasks.

A free trial or limited free plan lets you test it, with paid plans scaling by the number of articles, words, and seats. Like other AI writing tools, drafts need human review for factual accuracy, originality, and tone, and publishing large volumes of unedited AI text can hurt search performance. Jasper, Copy.ai, and Surfer cover similar ground, and general assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude can produce comparable drafts. Writesonic's value is the marketing-specific workflow and AI-visibility tracking.
