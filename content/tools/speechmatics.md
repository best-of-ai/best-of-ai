Speechmatics provides speech-to-text as a service. Developers send audio and get transcripts with timestamps, speaker labels, and language detection. The company emphasizes accuracy across accents and dialects, which is an area where many systems show bias.

Products include real-time transcription for live captioning and voice agents, batch processing, translation, and deployment options on premises for sensitive data. Customers include media, contact center, and healthcare companies.

Speechmatics is priced by usage. Alternatives include Deepgram, AssemblyAI, OpenAI Whisper, Gladia, and Google Cloud Speech-to-Text. Speechmatics suits organizations needing accurate multilingual transcription.
