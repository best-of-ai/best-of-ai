Buildxact is built for builders who work from plans and spreadsheets and need a more organized way to price jobs. Users can create estimates from templates and cost catalogs, perform takeoffs from digital plans, and turn the estimate into a professional proposal. After a job is won, the same data feeds purchase orders, schedules, and cost tracking, so you can compare what you estimated with what you spent.

The software covers the flow of a residential project: estimating, quoting, selections, job scheduling, subcontractor coordination, and invoicing, with integrations to accounting software such as QuickBooks and Xero. AI features help read plans and speed up takeoffs, suggest items, and draft proposals. It targets small to mid-sized companies, such as custom home builders, remodelers, and specialty trades.

Buildxact is paid, priced by subscription tiers. It is simpler than enterprise construction systems such as Procore, and fits smaller businesses better. Competitors include Buildertrend, CoConstruct, which merged into Buildertrend, JobTread, Houzz Pro, and Jobber for service trades. Cost accuracy depends on keeping catalogs up to date. Buildxact suits residential builders who want to improve estimating and job costing.
