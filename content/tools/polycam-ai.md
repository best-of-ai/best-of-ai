Polycam makes 3D scanning accessible. A user walks around an object or room with their phone, the app captures images or LiDAR depth data, and processing creates a textured 3D model. Models can be measured, shared, and exported for use in design, games, or construction.

Modes include photo-based capture for detail, LiDAR for room scans and floor plans, and tools to build models from photos taken with any camera. Newer features include Gaussian splatting for realistic views and AI tools for generating models from images.

Polycam has a free tier and paid plans for exports and higher quality. Results vary with lighting and technique. Alternatives include Luma AI, Scaniverse, Matterport, Reality Capture, and Kiri Engine. Polycam suits creators, builders, and designers capturing real objects.
