Reka is a research lab and company founded by researchers from DeepMind, Google, and Meta. It trains models from scratch that take mixed inputs, so a user can ask questions about a video or combine audio and text. The Flash and Edge models are smaller and faster, and Core is the largest.

Products built on the models include tools for research tasks that search and reason over the web, and for analyzing video. Developers use the API, and the company has released some models openly. Efficiency is a recurring theme in its work.

Reka offers a free tier and usage-based pricing. It competes with much larger labs, and model details change quickly, so check its documentation. Alternatives include Google Gemini, OpenAI models, Anthropic Claude, Twelve Labs for video, and Qwen. Reka AI suits developers who need multimodal models.
