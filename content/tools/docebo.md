Docebo is a cloud learning platform for businesses. Administrators host courses, assign training, track completion, and report on results for employees, customers, and partners. Enterprises use it for onboarding, compliance training, and external education that supports product adoption.

AI is built throughout. It automatically tags content, recommends courses to learners by role and interests, and can generate lessons, quizzes, and summaries from documents and videos. A skills layer maps what learners know to what roles need. A content marketplace provides courses from outside providers.

Docebo is sold on subscription, scaled by users, and priced for mid-sized and large organizations. Setup requires planning, and learning programs need good content to succeed. Alternatives include Cornerstone, 360Learning, Absorb LMS, TalentLMS, and LearnUpon. Docebo suits companies with extensive training programs.
