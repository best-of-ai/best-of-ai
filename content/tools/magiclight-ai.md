MagicLight is designed for narrative video. A user writes or pastes a story or script, chooses a style, and the platform produces scenes with characters, backgrounds, narration, and music. The aim is to let people who cannot animate create short animated stories and explainers.

It is used for children's stories, educational content, social media series, and marketing videos. Features such as consistent characters across scenes help with storytelling, and editing tools allow changes to scenes and text before export.

MagicLight has a free tier and paid plans with credits. AI-generated animation can be uneven in quality, and long stories need checking for consistency. Alternatives include Steve AI, Pictory, InVideo, Animaker, and Runway. MagicLight suits creators making animated stories.
