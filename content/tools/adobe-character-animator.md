Character Animator brings puppets to life. You prepare a character in Photoshop or Illustrator with layers for eyes, mouth, and body parts, import it, and the software maps your face and voice to the character. As you speak and move, the character blinks, talks, and reacts in real time, with lip sync generated from your audio.

It was designed for speed. Rigged characters can be recorded live, and the performance can be edited afterward on a timeline. Triggers set up with keyboard keys can switch poses, props, or expressions. The tool has been used for animated shows, live broadcasts, and explainer videos, and it integrates with Premiere Pro and After Effects.

Character Animator comes with Adobe's Creative Cloud subscription. Results depend on the quality of the character artwork and rigging, and it is best suited to flat, cartoon styles. Alternatives include Animaze, Cartoon Animator, Moho, VTube Studio, and Live2D. Adobe Character Animator suits creators who want performance-driven 2D animation.
