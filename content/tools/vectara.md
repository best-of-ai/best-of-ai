Vectara offers RAG as a service. Developers upload documents or connect sources, and the platform handles chunking, embedding, retrieval, and generation, returning answers with citations. Teams avoid assembling these components themselves.

The company emphasizes factual consistency, publishing a hallucination evaluation model and leaderboard, and offers agent features. Security options include private deployments for sensitive data.

Vectara has a free tier and paid plans by usage. Retrieval quality still depends on document quality. Alternatives include Pinecone with custom pipelines, Cohere, Azure AI Search, Glean, and LlamaIndex. Vectara suits teams that want RAG without building infrastructure.
