Enhance Speech is a simple tool with a dramatic effect. You upload a recording of a voice, such as an interview, podcast, voiceover, or video narration, and the AI processes it to remove noise, reverb, and room echo, and rebalances the voice. In moments, a recording made on a phone in a kitchen can sound much closer to something recorded in a studio. It has become popular among creators who record in imperfect spaces.

The tool works in the browser with no installation, and results can be downloaded or used within Adobe Podcast's editor. Controls let you adjust the strength of the enhancement, and a companion check can help you assess your microphone setup. Because it targets speech, it isn't intended for music. It's used by podcasters, journalists, teachers, video creators, and remote workers.

Enhance Speech has been free within usage limits, with higher limits and integrations available through Adobe plans. Heavy enhancement can make voices sound processed or slightly robotic, and it cannot repair severely clipped or overlapping speech. Alternatives include Auphonic, Krisp, Descript's Studio Sound, Cleanvoice, and the built-in enhancers in Premiere Pro. See the Adobe Podcast entry for the broader recording and editing studio.
