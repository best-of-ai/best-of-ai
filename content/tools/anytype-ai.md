Anytype is a workspace for notes and structured information built around privacy. Data is stored locally and encrypted, and syncs between devices peer to peer or through backup nodes, so you keep ownership of your content rather than leaving it on a company server. The app is open source.

Its model uses objects and types. Everything is an object, such as a page, a person, a book, or a task, and each type has fields. Objects link to each other and appear in sets and collections, which function like databases. This lets users create a personal wiki or a custom organizer without code. Optional AI features can summarize and help write.

Anytype is free for personal use with paid options for extra storage and features. It is younger than Notion and Obsidian, so integrations and polish are still developing, and the object model takes time to learn. Alternatives include Notion, Obsidian, Logseq, AppFlowy, and Capacities. Anytype suits people who want privacy and a flexible structure.
