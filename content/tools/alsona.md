Alsona starts from signals instead of a static lead list. It tracks events such as hiring, funding, reviews and podcast or comment activity, with more than 30 signal types, then scores the people and companies that show them and queues outreach for the best fits.

AI agents write the messages and the replies, and you can hold them for approval before anything is sent. LinkedIn and email conversations land in one inbox, and several sender accounts can rotate to spread out volume. It connects to CRMs including HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive, and the higher plans add an API and an MCP server.

Pricing is self-serve and monthly, with Starter, Pro and Ultra plans that raise the limits on prospects, agents and senders. Every plan begins with a free trial. It fits solo founders, sales operators and agencies running outreach for several clients.
