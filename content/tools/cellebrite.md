Cellebrite is a digital forensics company. Its tools pull data from mobile devices, cloud accounts, and computers, including deleted items where possible, and organize it so investigators can review messages, photos, locations, and app activity. The company serves police, intelligence agencies, and corporate security teams.

Analysis products help investigators manage large amounts of data. AI can classify images, search through text, find connections between people and devices, and build timelines. Cloud platforms support collaboration across a case and sharing evidence with prosecutors.

The tools are sold to government and professional customers under licensing. Use raises serious legal, ethical, and privacy questions, and the company has been criticized when its tools were used against activists and journalists in some countries. Competitors include Magnet Forensics, Oxygen Forensics, and MSAB. Cellebrite suits forensic professionals operating under legal authority.
