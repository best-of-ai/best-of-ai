FiscalNote is a policy intelligence platform. Government relations and compliance teams set up alerts for topics they care about, and the system monitors legislation, regulatory dockets, and court cases in many jurisdictions, notifying them when something changes. This replaces manual tracking of dozens of websites.

AI summarizes long documents, estimates which bills are likely to move, and helps teams assess the impact on their organization. The platform also supports collaboration, so teams can share notes, assign tasks, and report to executives. Related products cover policy research and the news in a field.

FiscalNote is sold to corporations, trade associations, and nonprofits on subscription. Forecasts of legislative outcomes are uncertain. Alternatives include Quorum, Bloomberg Government, Plural Policy, vLex Fastcase, and LexisNexis State Net. FiscalNote suits government affairs teams that need to track policy.
