Plimo puts image and video generation behind one account and one pool of credits. The template gallery is the main way in: each template comes with its prompt and settings filled in, so you mostly just add a photo. Categories include dance, outfit try-on, hairstyles, portraits, anime and cartoon styles, and effects such as a cinematic push-in.

Beyond templates it has the usual tools: text to image, image to image, photo editing, text to video, and image to video where you describe the motion. It runs models from several labs, including Nano Banana, GPT Image, FLUX and Seedream for images, and Kling, Veo, Seedance, Hailuo, Wan and Vidu for video. You can send one prompt to several models and compare the results. It also has its own model, Plimo Image 1.0.

New accounts get 100 one-time credits that don't expire, and you don't need a credit card. Output has no watermark. Without a paid membership, results are for personal, non-commercial use and are kept for 7 days. Alternatives include Higgsfield, Kling, Pika and Runway. Plimo suits people making social clips and stylized photos who don't want a separate subscription for each model.
