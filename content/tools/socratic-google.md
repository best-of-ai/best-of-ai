Socratic began as a startup and was acquired by Google, which relaunched it as a free app for students. A learner took a photo of a homework problem, and the app identified the concept and pointed to videos, explanations, and step-by-step guidance from across the web instead of just giving an answer.

It covered subjects such as math, science, and literature, and used machine learning to match questions to educational content. The goal was to help students understand a topic, not only to produce a solution.

The standalone app has been retired and its functions folded into Google's other products, so students now use Google Lens and the Gemini and Google apps for similar help. Alternatives include Photomath, Brainly, Khan Academy's Khanmigo, Quizlet, and Gauth. This entry is kept for reference.
