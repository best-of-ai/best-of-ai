Wisebits – Tales Untold transforms classic literature into immersive, narrated stories, offering a modern way to enjoy and learn from books. The app delivers stories in both readable and audio formats — with rich narration, visuals, and subtitles — so users can listen or read at their convenience. Wisebits surfaces “life lessons” and personal‑growth insights through fictional tales, making literature more meaningful and relevant. With a large and diverse library (hundreds of stories across genres) and tools for tracking reading/listening goals, Wisebits aims to help readers, students and lifelong learners deepen their understanding, grow mentally, and stay inspired.
