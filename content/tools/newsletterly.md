Newsletterly is an end-to-end workspace for building and sending email newsletters fast. It combines AI content generation with real-time news curation so a full draft can be produced from a simple prompt, enriched with timely stories related to your niche. Sources can also come from uploads—PDFs and YouTube links are summarized into clean sections ready to edit.
A rich, block-based editor supports headlines, text, images, buttons, and true multi-column layouts, with drag-and-drop control. An integrated AI image generator helps create on-brand visuals, and reusable templates keep formatting consistent from issue to issue.
Audience tools cover list importing and growth: bring in contacts via CSV, embed opt-in forms on a site, and organize subscribers for targeted sends. Built-in email delivery means campaigns can be sent directly, while performance insights (opens, clicks) feed back into the next edition for continuous improvement.
Newsletterly suits creators, founders, agencies, and marketing teams who want reliable, repeatable output without heavy writing or design lift—going from idea to inbox in minutes.
