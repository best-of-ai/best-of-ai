Preply matches students with tutors. Learners browse profiles, filter by language, price, and availability, book a trial lesson, and then continue with a tutor of their choice. Lessons take place on the platform's video classroom with tools for sharing materials.

The company has added AI to support learning between lessons. Speaking practice partners simulate conversation, summaries and homework are generated from lessons, and vocabulary tools help with review. Tutors can use AI to prepare materials.

Preply charges per lesson, set by each tutor, plus platform fees, and learners can change tutors easily. Quality varies between tutors. Alternatives include italki, Cambly, Verbling, Lingoda, and Duolingo for self-study. Preply suits learners who want live tutoring.
