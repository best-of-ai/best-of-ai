Zapier handles the plumbing between business apps. A workflow listens for an event in one tool and does something in another, with filters, paths, and delays to model real processes. Templates cover common needs in sales, marketing, and operations.

Newer capabilities include AI actions that read and write text, agents that carry out multi-step tasks, and an MCP offering that exposes Zapier actions to AI assistants. Teams can monitor runs, handle errors, and manage access centrally.

Pricing is task based with a free tier. A duplicate listing covers the no-code view of the same product. Alternatives include Make, n8n, Workato, Power Automate, and Pipedream. Zapier suits teams that automate across many apps.
