Bing Image Creator made AI image generation easy to try. You type a description, and it returns several images within a few seconds, with no installation and no cost beyond a Microsoft account. Its popularity came from being free and being available inside Bing and Edge, long before many alternatives had a free tier. Microsoft has since renamed the tool Image Creator from Designer and tied it into Copilot and the Designer app.

The models behind it have changed. It started with OpenAI's DALL-E 3, and Microsoft has added its own image model, MAI-Image-1, along with OpenAI's newer image generation, so the exact model can depend on the app and options you choose. Generated images can be saved, edited in Designer, and used in presentations and documents. A system of daily boosts or credits has limited how quickly free users can generate.

Content filters block many prompts, such as those involving real people or violent and sexual material, and they sometimes block harmless prompts too. Images may include invisible markers indicating they were AI-generated. The results are good for illustrations and quick concepts, though Midjourney and FLUX often produce more artistic detail. Adobe Firefly, Google's Gemini image tools, and ChatGPT's image generation are the other well-known free or low-cost options.
