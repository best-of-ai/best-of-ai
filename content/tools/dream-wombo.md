Dream by WOMBO is aimed at casual creators who want to see their ideas as art without learning prompt techniques. You type a phrase, pick a style such as oil painting, anime, fantasy, or neon, and the app returns an image in a few seconds. The style menu does much of the work that detailed prompts would do in other tools, which makes it approachable on a phone. It is popular for profile pictures, wallpapers, and quick social posts.

The app lets you create your own images, browse a gallery of what others have made, and share results. Additional options include generating in different aspect ratios, using an image as a starting point, and higher-quality models for subscribers. WOMBO is also the company behind other playful AI apps, such as one that makes photos sing, and Dream sits within that consumer entertainment focus.

Dream has a free tier with daily limits and ads, plus paid plans for more generations and features. The images tend to be stylized instead of photorealistic, and fine control is limited compared with Midjourney, FLUX, or Stable Diffusion tools. Check the terms before using results commercially. Alternatives include Leonardo.ai and Bing Image Creator, which are free to start, as well as NightCafe and Canva's image generator. It is best for fun and quick results.
