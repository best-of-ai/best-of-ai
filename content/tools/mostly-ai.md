MOSTLY AI helps organizations use data that would otherwise be locked away by privacy rules. It trains a generative model on a real dataset, such as customer transactions or patient records, and then produces a new dataset that reflects the same patterns, relationships, and distributions but contains no real individuals. Banks, insurers, and healthcare companies use it to share data across teams and borders, test software, and develop models.

The platform handles multi-table relational data, preserving links between tables such as customers and their orders. Quality and privacy reports compare the synthetic data with the original and check for leakage, and features can rebalance datasets to reduce bias or fill in rare cases. An open-source SDK allows developers to run generation in their own environment, while the platform adds a user interface, scheduling, and enterprise controls.

MOSTLY AI offers a free tier for limited use and paid enterprise plans. Synthetic data is not automatically anonymous, so privacy checks and expert review remain important, and rare patterns can be lost. It's most valuable when real data is hard to access, not when it's already freely available. Competitors include Gretel, now part of NVIDIA, Tonic.ai, Hazy, Synthesized, and open-source libraries like SDV. Regulated industries are its main audience.
