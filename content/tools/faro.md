FARO's scanners measure a space by sweeping a laser and recording millions of points, which form a detailed 3D copy of a room, a crash site, or a factory. Software then turns the point cloud into measurements, floor plans, and models. The captured scene can be revisited long after a site has been cleaned up.

Forensic and public safety teams use it to document crime and crash scenes and to reconstruct events. Architecture, engineering, and construction firms scan buildings to check progress against designs and to capture existing conditions. Manufacturing uses related tools for inspection and measurement of parts.

FARO sells hardware and subscription software, which makes it a significant investment. AI-assisted processing helps register scans and clean up data. AMETEK acquired the company. Alternatives include Leica Geosystems, Trimble, Matterport for simpler capture, NavVis, and Artec. FARO suits professionals who need precise 3D documentation.
