CoachHub is a digital coaching platform sold to employers. Employees are matched with certified coaches for one-on-one sessions on topics such as leadership, communication, and career growth. Sessions take place by video, and the company offers coaches across many countries and languages.

Between sessions, an AI coach named AIMY supports employees. It is available for quick conversations, helps rehearse difficult discussions, and supports goal setting, extending coaching beyond what a limited number of human sessions can cover. The platform reports on usage and progress to the sponsoring company.

CoachHub is sold to organizations on custom pricing. AI coaching cannot replace the judgment and empathy of a human coach and is not a substitute for therapy. Alternatives include BetterUp, Torch, Ezra, Valence, and Lattice for performance. CoachHub suits companies that offer coaching as a benefit.
