Personio centralizes HR work. Companies keep employee data, manage leave and attendance, handle recruiting from job posting to offer, prepare payroll, and run reviews, in one system designed around European employment rules. Self-service features let employees update their own data and request time off.

AI features help with common tasks. They draft job descriptions, assist with screening candidates, answer HR questions from company policy, and automate workflows. Reports show headcount, turnover, and other metrics.

Personio is sold on subscription to companies from about ten employees upward, with pricing by modules. Its strength is Europe, and it is less common in the United States. Alternatives include HiBob, BambooHR, Factorial, Workday, and SAP SuccessFactors. Personio suits growing European employers.
