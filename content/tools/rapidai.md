RapidAI makes software for time-critical imaging. For suspected stroke, it processes CT or MRI scans, identifies large vessel occlusions and measures tissue at risk, then alerts the team.

Its platform also covers aneurysm and other vascular conditions and helps coordinate transfers between hospitals.

It is sold to hospitals and stroke networks. Results guide care but do not replace clinical judgment. Alternatives include Viz.ai, Aidoc, and Brainomix. RapidAI suits stroke centers and their referring hospitals.
