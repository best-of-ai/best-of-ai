Lex is a document editor with AI woven in. The interface is clean and focused on writing, and the AI helps in small ways: continuing a thought when you are stuck, offering alternative phrasings, and giving feedback on drafts. Users keep control, and the tool tries not to replace their voice.

Features include a way to ask for critique, suggestions for headlines and next paragraphs, and the ability to have the AI respond to your own text in a side conversation. Writers use it for essays, newsletters, and notes where they want a thinking partner rather than automatic content.

Lex has a free tier and paid plans. It is a smaller product than major word processors and has fewer collaboration and formatting features. Alternatives include Notion AI, Google Docs with Gemini, Sudowrite, Wordtune, and Claude. Lex suits writers who want light AI support.
