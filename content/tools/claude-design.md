Claude Design lets you build visual work by talking to Claude. You can ask for a slide deck, a landing page mockup, or a clickable prototype, then adjust it with follow-up requests instead of dragging elements around by hand.

It came out of Anthropic Labs in April 2026. It makes the most sense for people who need a decent first draft quickly and already work in Claude. Designers who want pixel-level control will likely still prefer a dedicated editor.
