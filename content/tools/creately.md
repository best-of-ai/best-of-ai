Creately combines diagramming and whiteboarding in one workspace. You can draw flowcharts, org charts, mind maps, UML diagrams, process maps, wireframes, and more using shape libraries and templates, then connect them with smart connectors that stay attached when you move things. An infinite canvas supports brainstorming and planning, and teams can edit together in real time with comments and cursors.

Some features go beyond plain drawing. Diagrams can be linked to data, so a shape can show information from a spreadsheet or tool, and cards and tables let you build lightweight planning boards. AI features generate a diagram from a text description, summarize or expand notes, and suggest ideas for brainstorming. Presentations and exports in common formats help you share work, and integrations connect with Slack, Confluence, Google Workspace, and Microsoft Teams.

Creately has a free plan with limited features and paid plans for individuals and teams. It covers much of what Lucidchart and Miro offer at a lower price, with fewer integrations and a smaller user base. Miro is stronger as a whiteboard, Lucidchart for formal diagrams, and diagrams.net (draw.io) is free and capable for simple needs. FigJam suits design teams, and Mermaid-based tools generate diagrams from code. It is a sensible middle option.
