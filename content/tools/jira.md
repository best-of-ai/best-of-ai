Jira tracks work through issues in boards, backlogs, and timelines. Software teams use it for sprints, bug tracking, and roadmaps, and many other teams use it for requests and projects. Workflows are customizable, and it integrates with code hosts, chat tools, and the rest of Atlassian's products.

Atlassian has added AI across Jira. It summarizes long ticket discussions, drafts descriptions and subtasks, and turns natural language into search queries. Rovo agents can take on work such as triaging issues or answering questions across connected tools, and the AI draws on a team's own data.

Jira has a free plan for small teams and paid tiers, plus enterprise options. It is powerful but can feel complicated, especially without clear conventions. Alternatives include Linear, Asana, ClickUp, Monday.com, and GitHub Issues. Jira suits software and IT teams that need configurable tracking.
