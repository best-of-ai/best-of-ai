CC acts like a morning assistant. Each day it sends an email, called Your Day Ahead, that pulls together calendar events, important messages, bills, and documents relevant to the day. You reply to the email to ask for changes, such as adding an event or digging into a topic, and CC acts on it.

It connects to a user's Gmail, Calendar, and Drive and can also use the web for context. Because it works through email, there is no new app to learn. Google has expanded the experiment to support shared use by members of a household, with each person connecting their own accounts.

CC is an experiment offered to select paid subscribers and by waitlist, so availability and features change. Giving an assistant access to email and calendars needs trust and care with settings. Alternatives include ChatGPT Pulse, Gemini in Gmail, Superhuman AI, Shortwave, and Fyxer. CC suits people who want a daily summary of their day.
