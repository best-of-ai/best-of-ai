Mistral is Europe's best-known frontier AI lab. It first drew attention by releasing compact open-weight models that matched or beat larger ones, and it has continued to release models in several sizes for chat, coding, reasoning, and vision. Some are free to download under permissive licenses, while its strongest models are offered through its API and Le Chat. Developers like that it gives them the choice to run models on their own hardware.

Le Chat is the consumer and workplace assistant. It handles ordinary questions and writing, searches the web, analyzes files and images, generates images, and runs code, with features such as agents and a canvas for working on documents. For developers, the API includes models for general use, code completion, embeddings, document OCR, and moderation. Enterprises can deploy Mistral models in their own cloud or on-premises with support and customization.

Le Chat has a free tier, with paid individual and team plans raising limits and adding features. API usage is priced per token. Mistral emphasizes data sovereignty and European hosting, which appeals to governments and companies concerned about where data goes. Its models are generally less capable than the very top systems from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google on the hardest tasks, but they are strong for the size and cost. Meta's Llama, Cohere, and DeepSeek are comparable options for open or enterprise use.
