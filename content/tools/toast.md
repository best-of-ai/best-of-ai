Toast serves the restaurant industry. Servers take orders on handheld devices, kitchen displays route tickets, guests pay at the table or online, and managers track sales, labor, and inventory. Online ordering, delivery integrations, and loyalty programs are part of the ecosystem.

Data and AI support operations. Sales forecasts help schedule staff, menu analytics show what sells, and assistants help write menu descriptions and answer business questions. Payroll and team management tools extend the suite.

Toast sells hardware and software subscriptions plus payment processing fees. Contract terms and fees deserve a careful look. Alternatives include Square for Restaurants, Clover, Lightspeed, TouchBistro, and SpotOn. Toast suits restaurants wanting an integrated system.
