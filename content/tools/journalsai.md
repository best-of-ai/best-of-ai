JournalsAI creates custom, 30-days journals tailored to your unique goals, challenges and preferences. Each day you get a prompt to write on in a clean, distraction-free writing space.

Other unique features:

1. Analyze: after writing at least 3 entries, you can analyze your journal and get insights about your progress, trends and more.
2. Spark: If you are stuck on a prompt and don't know what to write, using the Spark button will give you a custom suggestion to start writing.
3. Next phase: When you complete a journal, you will be able to create a 'Next phase' one. JournalsAI will analyze your previous journal and create a new one that builds upon its progress.
4. No fluff: Journaling as it's meant to be. No habit trackers, mood trackers, chatbots etc. Simple, tailored-to-you journaling with minimalistic and powerful features.
