Users work in notebooks that combine code, results, and text. The Wolfram Language has built-in functions for a huge range of math and data tasks. It connects with Wolfram Alpha knowledge and, in newer versions, with language model tools.

Licenses are paid, and student editions are cheaper.

MATLAB and SageMath are alternatives. Mathematica suits mathematicians who need symbolic power.
