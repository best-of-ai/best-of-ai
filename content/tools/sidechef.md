SideChef is built for people who want help in the kitchen. Each recipe is broken into short steps with photos or video, so you can follow along while cooking, and hands-free voice control and timers keep you moving without touching your phone with messy hands. Recipes cover a wide range of cuisines and diets, and you can scale servings, swap ingredients, and save favorites.

Planning features reduce the work around cooking. Meal plans suggest weekly menus based on tastes and dietary needs, and shopping lists are built automatically from chosen recipes, with options to order groceries through delivery partners. The app also works with smart kitchen devices, and AI helps personalize recommendations and adjust plans over time.

SideChef has a free tier with ads and limited features and a premium subscription for full meal planning and ad-free use. Recipe quality varies by source, and nutritional estimates are approximations. Alternatives include Yummly, Mealime, Paprika for recipe management, NYT Cooking, and Whisk, now Samsung Food. SideChef suits home cooks who like guided steps and automated shopping lists.
