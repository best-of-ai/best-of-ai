Nous Research started as a group of researchers and hobbyists working in the open. Its Hermes models fine-tune open base models to follow instructions, use tools, and handle roleplay and reasoning, and have been popular among people running models locally. Data, methods, and weights are shared publicly.

The group has also worked on decentralized training, exploring ways to train large models across many machines run by different parties, and on tooling for agents. It operates as a company and offers access to its models through an API and an agent product.

Nous models are free to download, with some commercial services. Open fine-tunes depend on the quality of the base models they build on. Alternatives include Mistral, Meta's Llama releases, Qwen, Allen AI's OLMo, and community fine-tuners. Nous Research suits developers who want open models with a strong instruction-following focus.
