Gleamer is a French radiology AI company known for tools that find fractures on X-rays and for a chest X-ray product. Findings appear as marked overlays inside the radiologist's viewer.

Its products are grouped in a suite for emergency and routine imaging, and the company has expanded toward other modalities such as MRI.

Gleamer is sold to hospitals and imaging centers. Overlays are an aid, not a diagnosis. Alternatives include Annalise.ai, Qure.ai, Lunit, and Aidoc. Gleamer suits emergency and musculoskeletal imaging workflows.
