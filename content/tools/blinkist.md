Blinkist is designed for people who want the main ideas of a book without reading all of it. Editors and writers distill nonfiction titles in business, psychology, science, health, and self-improvement into short summaries, with key takeaways and often a quick audio version. Users listen on a commute or skim on a break, and it's used as a way to preview a book before deciding to read it fully.

The app has broadened with audiobooks, original content, and tools such as highlighting and collections of titles by topic. AI features include personalized recommendations and generated summaries or answers based on its library, and an AI assistant that lets users ask questions about topics. Audio can be downloaded for offline listening.

Blinkist is sold by subscription with a free trial. Summaries inevitably lose nuance and are no substitute for reading books that matter to you, and critics note that they can make people feel informed without deep understanding. Alternatives include Shortform, which offers longer summaries and analysis, getAbstract for business, Audible for full audiobooks, and library apps such as Libby, which are free. Blinkist suits busy people who want an overview of many books.
