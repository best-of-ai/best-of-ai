Thmenu combines QR digital menus across 50 translated languages, table ordering, point-of-sale billing, kitchen display ticket routing, and reservation management in one web dashboard for restaurants and cafes.

Through its public remote Model Context Protocol endpoint (`https://mcp.thmenu.com/`) and Agent-Readable Menu Protocol (ARMP), AI assistants can inspect restaurant hours, search structured menu items and allergen metadata, build carts, and request table reservations via OAuth 2.1.

The core restaurant plan carries no monthly subscription fee, applying a 1% platform fee only on online payments processed through the platform.
