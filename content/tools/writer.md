Writer targets large organizations that need AI output to stay on brand and compliant. Teams set style guides, terminology, and rules, and the system applies them as people draft, with checks for claims and tone. A knowledge graph lets AI answer from company data.

The company builds its own Palmyra language models and a platform for building agents that handle multi-step work across tools. Governance features such as permissions, logging, and fact checks support regulated industries.

Writer is sold on enterprise subscriptions. Do not confuse it with Writerly, Grammarly, or Wordtune. Alternatives include Jasper, Grammarly Business, Microsoft Copilot, Anyword, and ChatGPT Enterprise. Writer suits enterprise content and communications teams.
