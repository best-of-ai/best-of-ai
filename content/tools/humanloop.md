Humanloop helped teams build reliable applications on language models. Developers managed and versioned prompts, ran automated and human evaluations, and monitored behavior in production. It was used by enterprises that needed to test changes before shipping and keep a record of how prompts evolved.

Anthropic hired the company's co-founders and most of its team, without acquiring the product or its intellectual property. The standalone service was wound down shortly afterward, and customers were given guidance for migrating to other tools.

Anyone who used Humanloop should move prompts and evaluation data to another platform. Alternatives include Braintrust, LangSmith, Langfuse, Weights & Biases Weave, and PromptLayer. This entry is kept for reference, and Humanloop is no longer available.
