Drift popularized the idea of replacing a website contact form with a live conversation. A chat widget greets visitors, asks a few questions, and either answers them, routes them to the right salesperson, or books a meeting on the spot. For B2B companies with long sales cycles, the goal was to catch interested visitors while they were still on the site instead of waiting for them to fill out a form and wait for a reply.

Its automation grew more advanced over time. Chatbots could qualify leads against criteria such as company size and role, recognize visitors from target accounts, and show them tailored messages. Meeting scheduling, lead routing, and CRM sync tied the conversations to sales workflows in tools like Salesforce and HubSpot. AI replies drew on a company's content to answer common questions without waiting for a person.

Salesloft acquired Drift, and it has since become one part of Salesloft's wider sales engagement and revenue platform, so the product and its packaging may be different from what older reviews describe. Pricing was aimed at mid-sized and enterprise teams and was not cheap. Smaller companies often choose Intercom, HubSpot's chat tools, or Qualified, which is built for Salesforce users. Check Salesloft's current materials before choosing Drift for a new project.
