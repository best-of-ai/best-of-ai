Vanta helps companies prove they run secure operations. It integrates with tools such as AWS, Google Workspace, GitHub, Okta, and HR systems, continuously checks whether controls like encryption, access reviews, and employee training are in place, and collects the evidence auditors need. This replaces manual screenshots and spreadsheets and keeps a company audit-ready year-round.

The platform has grown into a broader trust management suite. It tracks vendor risk, manages security policies and employee onboarding tasks, runs risk assessments, and provides a trust center where customers can see a company's security posture. AI features answer security questionnaires, map controls across frameworks, and help prioritize remediation, saving time for small security teams.

Vanta is paid, with pricing based on frameworks and company size, and a demo-based sales process. Automation does not replace the work of operating a secure environment, and an independent auditor still issues the report. Drata is the closest competitor, along with Secureframe, Sprinto, and Scrut. Vanta suits startups and mid-sized companies preparing for their first certifications.
