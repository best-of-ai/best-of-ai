Syte brings visual search to e-commerce. A shopper uploads a photo or taps on an image, and the system finds similar items in the store's catalog. Computer vision also tags products with detailed attributes, which improves search, filters, and recommendations.

Retailers use it for shop-the-look features, similar item suggestions, and personalization. Integrations work with common e-commerce platforms, and generative AI supports conversational shopping assistants.

Syte is sold to retailers on subscription. Alternatives include Algolia, Bloomreach, Vue.ai, Klevu, and Google Lens integrations. Syte suits fashion and home retailers with large catalogs.
