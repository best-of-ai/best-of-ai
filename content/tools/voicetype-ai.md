Voicetype AI lets people speak instead of type. A user holds a hotkey, speaks, and the text appears in the active app, with punctuation and formatting handled automatically. It works across email, documents, chat, and code editors.

AI cleans up filler words and false starts, and supports many languages. Custom dictionaries help with names and jargon. Dictation can speed up writing for people who think out loud.

Voicetype AI has a free tier and paid plans. Independent information is limited, so check privacy and where audio is processed. Alternatives include Wispr Flow, Superwhisper, Dragon, Apple dictation, and Talon. Voicetype AI suits people who want voice typing everywhere.
