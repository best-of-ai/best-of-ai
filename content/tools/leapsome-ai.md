Leapsome gathers people processes in one product. HR teams run review cycles, set goals and OKRs, send engagement surveys, deliver learning content, and manage compensation planning, with the data connected so decisions draw on the full picture. It is used by mid-sized, often distributed companies.

AI helps with the heaviest reading and writing. It summarizes feedback, analyzes comments from surveys for sentiment and themes, helps managers draft reviews, and suggests development actions. Dashboards show trends by team.

Leapsome is sold per employee by module. It is a broad tool and implementation requires process design. Alternatives include Lattice, Culture Amp, 15Five, Betterworks, and HiBob. Leapsome suits HR teams running performance and engagement programs.
