remio collects what you read and write in one place and lets you ask questions about it. It indexes notes, files, and web pages visited, then answers queries such as where an idea came from or what a document said, with links to the source.

The idea is to address the problem of forgetting where information lives. Capture happens automatically through a browser extension and app, with less manual filing than a traditional note tool.

Capturing browsing history raises privacy questions, so check what is stored and where. Independent information is limited. remio has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Recall, Mem, Rewind-style tools, Notion AI, and Obsidian. remio suits professionals who want a searchable memory of their work.
