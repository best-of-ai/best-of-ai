StarryAI brought AI art generation to phones. In the app, you enter a text prompt, pick an art style or model, and receive images within a minute or so. Styles range from fantasy and anime to realism and abstract, and you can use an image as a starting point for variations. It's aimed at hobbyists and creators who want art for wallpapers, profile pictures, book covers, or game concepts.

Features support experimentation. You can control aspect ratio, choose among models, and upscale results, and a gallery shows creations from other users for inspiration. Different pricing tiers offer more credits and faster generation, and the terms for commercial use vary by plan, with ownership of outputs depending on the subscription.

StarryAI has a free daily allowance and paid subscriptions. The output quality is below that of leading generators like Midjourney, FLUX, and OpenAI's image models, and results can vary widely. Content filters limit what you can generate. Alternatives with free tiers include Leonardo.ai, NightCafe, Bing Image Creator, and Craiyon, and Dream by WOMBO is another mobile-first option. StarryAI suits casual creators who want an easy phone-based art generator.
