Waldo is an agent for spatial data science and engineering inside Wherobots Cloud, and it is in public preview. You ask a question in plain English and it finds datasets in your catalogs (Havasu, Iceberg, Databricks Unity, AWS Glue and S3), writes the spatial SQL or RasterFlow code, shows you what it runs, and maps the results.

Before each job it asks for approval and shows the runtime size and the hourly cost range. Finished work can be scheduled as a pipeline, and an MCP server lets other agents connect. The audience is GIS teams, data engineers and teams working with Earth observation and machine learning.

A free trial needs no payment method. The Professional plan starts at zero with a trial, then has a monthly usage minimum, and Waldo tokens are included. Higher tiers and enterprise are priced separately, and usage is billed by compute. Wherobots compares itself with Apache Sedona, Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery and Google Earth Engine.
