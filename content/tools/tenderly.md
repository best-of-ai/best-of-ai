Its simulator replays a transaction with changed parameters or code without spending gas. The debugger traces execution step by step. Virtual TestNets fork mainnet state for testing, and alerts watch contracts for events.

It covers major EVM chains, though support depth varies by network. Plans range from free to enterprise.

Hardhat and Foundry tools cover local testing. Tenderly suits blockchain developers who need hosted debugging.
