Photographers upload past edits to build a personal profile. Imagen then edits new shoots and returns adjustments to Lightroom or Photoshop. It also includes culling and cropping tools.

It is billed per photo or by plan.

Aftershoot and Lightroom presets are alternatives. Imagen suits photographers who edit thousands of images.
