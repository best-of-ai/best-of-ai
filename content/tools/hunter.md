Hunter is a tool for finding contact details for business outreach. A Domain Search shows the email addresses associated with a company along with the sources where they were found, and the email pattern the company uses. An Email Finder takes a person's name and company and returns the most likely address with a confidence score. Email Verifier checks whether an address is valid and likely to deliver, which reduces bounces.

The product extends into outreach. Campaigns let you send personalized emails in sequences from your own account, track replies, and follow up automatically. A Chrome extension finds addresses while you browse a company website or LinkedIn profile, and integrations connect with CRMs and tools like HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zapier. AI helps write outreach messages and clean lead lists. Users include sales teams, recruiters, PR professionals, and link builders.

Hunter has a free plan with a small monthly allowance of searches and paid plans with larger credit limits. Results are based on publicly available information and pattern inference, so some addresses will be wrong, and verification matters. Cold outreach is regulated by laws such as GDPR and CAN-SPAM, so use contact data responsibly. Competitors include Apollo, Snov.io, Lusha, RocketReach, and Clearbit, and the larger sales platforms bundle similar data.
