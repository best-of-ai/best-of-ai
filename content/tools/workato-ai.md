Workato is an integration and automation platform. Teams build recipes that link applications such as Salesforce, Workday, and Slack, trigger actions, and move data. Business users can create simple automations while IT governs the platform.

The company has added AI across the product. Users describe a workflow in plain language and Workato builds a draft, document understanding extracts data, and agents handle tasks with enterprise controls. Governance, security, and monitoring suit regulated companies.

Workato is sold on custom pricing, usually to mid-sized and large organizations. Alternatives include Zapier, Make, Boomi, MuleSoft, and Tray.ai. Workato suits enterprises automating across many systems.
