In-hive sensors track temperature, humidity, vibration, and sound. In-field sensors count bee activity in crops. Data flows through a gateway to an analytics platform that flags weak colonies and helps schedule pollination.

BeeHero works with commercial beekeepers and growers, mainly in crops like almonds and blueberries. Reports of results come from the company, so growers should ask for trial data.

Hobbyists will find it too large in scale. BeeHero suits commercial beekeepers and pollination managers.
