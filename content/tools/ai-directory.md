AI Directory is one of many catalogs that try to keep up with the flood of AI products. Visitors can browse tools by category or search for a use case, such as writing, image generation, coding, or video, and see short descriptions with links to each tool's own site.

Directories help with discovery. New tools appear constantly, and a list organized by task is easier to scan than a search engine. Makers can submit their products for listing, which is how most such sites grow, so coverage depends on who submits and how closely entries are reviewed.

The site is free to use. Since directories differ in how carefully they check entries, it is wise to verify a tool's claims, pricing, and privacy practices on its own website. Similar resources include Aixploria, There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, AllThingsAI, and Best of AI. AI Directory suits people who want another place to scan for new tools.
