Booksellers can open a storefront on the site, curate lists, and earn a share of sales. Customers shop from the full catalog and the store receives a share. The platform handles fulfillment.

It is an alternative to selling through large online retailers. Store owners still need their own marketing.

Shopify stores and Ingram services are other options. Bookshop.org suits independent bookstore owners.
