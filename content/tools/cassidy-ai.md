Cassidy lets non-technical teams build AI workflows. A user connects sources such as documents, wikis, and apps, then designs assistants and multi-step workflows in a visual builder: take an input, retrieve company knowledge, draft an output, and send it onward.

Typical uses include drafting proposals and emails in the company's voice, summarizing meetings, researching accounts, answering internal questions, and processing forms. Different model providers can be used, and permissions control who can see which sources. Teams share assistants so a good workflow becomes a reusable tool.

Cassidy has a free tier and paid plans. The value depends on how well processes are defined and how complete the connected data is. Alternatives include Zapier Agents, Relay.app, Lindy, Dust, and Microsoft Copilot Studio. Cassidy suits business teams building AI workflows without engineers.
