StyleSeat serves independent beauty professionals. Clients search for stylists, browse portfolios and reviews, and book appointments, while professionals manage their calendar, take deposits, and process payments through the platform.

The marketplace helps new clients find providers, and the business tools handle reminders, no-show protection, and client records. Recommendations use location and preference data to match clients.

StyleSeat has a subscription for professionals and fees on some transactions. Fees for marketplace clients can be a concern for stylists with an existing following. Alternatives include Vagaro, Fresha, Booksy, Square Appointments, and Glossgenius. StyleSeat suits independent stylists and barbers.
