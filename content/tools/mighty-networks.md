Mighty Networks is a platform for building a membership community. A creator sets up a space with feeds, groups, live events, and online courses, and members join by paying a subscription or one-time fee. The pitch is an experience that feels like your own app, with mobile apps that can carry your brand.

Monetization is built in. Creators can charge for memberships, courses, and events, offer tiered access, and run bundles. The AI assistant helps with tasks such as naming a community, drafting posts and course outlines, and suggesting engagement ideas. Analytics show member activity, which helps community managers improve retention.

Mighty Networks has paid plans, with higher tiers for the branded app and more features, and charges transaction fees on some plans. Newer rivals have grown, and choosing a platform often comes down to where members already spend time. Alternatives include Circle, Skool, Kajabi, Discord, and Facebook Groups. Mighty Networks suits coaches and creators building a paid community.
