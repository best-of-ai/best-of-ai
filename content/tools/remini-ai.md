Remini gained a following for photo restoration. A user uploads an old family photo or a blurry picture, and the AI sharpens it, repairs damage, and enhances faces. It can also upscale images and colorize black-and-white ones.

The app expanded into AI-generated portraits and avatars from selfies, which went viral at times. Video enhancement is also offered. It runs mainly on phones, with processing in the cloud.

Remini has a free tier with ads and limits, and paid plans, and has faced criticism over subscription practices. Enhancement invents plausible detail, so faces may change from the original. Photos are uploaded, so check the privacy policy. Alternatives include Topaz Photo AI, Let's Enhance, Photoshop's neural filters, VanceAI, and Palette.fm. Remini suits people restoring family photos.
