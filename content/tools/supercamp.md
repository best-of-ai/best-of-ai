Supercamp transforms how companies work by creating a unified AI workspace where multiple LLMs, tools, and custom assistants collaborate seamlessly. Instead of juggling different AI platforms and losing context between tools, you get one intelligent hub where your AI assistants understand your business, remember your preferences, and connect with your existing workflow. Save thousands of hours by having AI that actually knows what you need.

Key Features
- Multi-LLM Integration: Access ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other leading AI models from one interface, choosing the best AI for each specific task.
- Custom AI Assistants: Create specialized AI assistants tailored to your business needs with company knowledge and skill sets.
- Team Management: Organize team members, share AI assistants across departments, manage permissions, and maintain consistent AI performance.
- Automate Tasks: Set up intelligent automation for repetitive work, from data processing and report generation to customer communications and workflow management.

Benefits
- Company Knowledge: Centralize your institutional knowledge so AI assistants access the same company information, maintaining consistency and accuracy across all teams.
- Thousands of Apps: Integrate with your existing tech stack through thousands of available connections, ensuring your AI assistants work seamlessly.
- Security and Control: Maintain enterprise-grade security with granular access controls, data encryption, and compliance features.
- Enhanced Team Productivity: Eliminate context-switching between AI platforms while ensuring every team member has access to powerful, contextually-aware AI assistants.
