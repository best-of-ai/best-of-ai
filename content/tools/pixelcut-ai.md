Pixelcut focuses on product images. A seller photographs an item, removes the background in one tap, and places the product on a clean or styled backdrop. AI can generate scenes that suit a product, add shadows, and upscale low-resolution pictures.

Batch tools process many images, and templates help create ads, social posts, and listing graphics. The mobile app is popular with small sellers who shoot products with a phone.

Pixelcut has a free tier and paid plans. AI-generated scenes should still show the product accurately to avoid returns. Alternatives include Photoroom, Canva, Pebblely, Remove.bg, and Adobe Express. Pixelcut suits small online sellers.
