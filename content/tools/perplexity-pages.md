Pages is a publishing layer on top of Perplexity's research. After you explore a topic with the assistant, you can convert the thread into a structured page with headings, summaries, and citations to the sources it used. It organizes the material in the way a short article or study guide would be written, instead of leaving you with a conversation log.

You control the result. Sections can be rewritten, reordered, or removed, images can be added, and the page can be published with a shareable link or kept private. Educators use Pages to create study guides, students for report drafts, and professionals for quick explainers on topics they research often. Because the citations travel with the content, readers can check where claims came from.

Pages is part of Perplexity's product line, with availability and limits that depend on plan, and the feature set may change as the company shifts its focus. AI-written pages can contain mistakes or lean on weak sources, so review them before publishing, and consider disclosure when they are shared publicly. Alternatives include Notion AI and Google Docs with Gemini for drafting, NotebookLM for source-grounded study guides. It suits fast, cited write-ups.
