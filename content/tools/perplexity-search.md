The core difference between Perplexity and a standard search engine is what you get back. Instead of ten blue links, you get a synthesised answer written in plain language, with numbered citations you can click to verify each claim. The AI reads the most relevant web pages and distills them for you — which means you skip the part where you open six tabs, skim for the relevant paragraph, and try to reconcile contradictory information across sources.

Perplexity handles follow-up questions naturally, treating a research session as a conversation rather than isolated queries. Ask it to compare two programming frameworks, then ask it to explain one specific difference in more depth, and it maintains the context. Its Copilot mode uses GPT-4 for more complex reasoning tasks. The Focus setting lets you restrict searches to Reddit, academic papers, YouTube, or news depending on what kind of source you want.

The free tier covers unlimited basic searches; Pro at $20/month unlocks GPT-4, Claude, and other advanced models for more nuanced answers, plus file upload for document analysis and 600+ daily searches with no throttling. Perplexity Pro also ships as an iOS and Android app, which has made it a genuinely useful replacement for search on mobile — particularly for anything that would require reading multiple articles to answer properly.
