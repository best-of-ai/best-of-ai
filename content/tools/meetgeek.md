MeetGeek joins your calendar's online meetings, records them, and produces a transcript and summary after each one. The summary lists decisions, action items, and key points, and highlights flag important moments. Notes are sent automatically to attendees or to tools like Slack, Notion, HubSpot, and Google Docs, which cuts down on manual write-ups.

Beyond a single meeting, it organizes knowledge across your calls. Hubs group meetings by team or topic, and you can search past conversations or ask questions about them. Meeting templates tailor summaries for sales calls, interviews, or team check-ins, and analytics show participation and talk time. Teams can get insights into patterns across customer calls.

MeetGeek has a free plan with limited hours and paid plans with more transcription, AI features, and integrations, plus a business tier. As with any recording tool, participants should know they are being recorded, and sensitive meetings may need extra care. Accuracy declines with poor audio and strong accents. Fireflies, Otter, Fathom, and Avoma are the closest competitors, with Fathom offering free unlimited recording. MeetGeek works for teams that want automated, structured notes without much setup.
