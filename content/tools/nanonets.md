Nanonets turns documents into data. A company sends invoices, purchase orders, receipts, or forms by email or upload, and the platform reads them, extracts fields and line items, and validates them against rules. Approved data flows into accounting or ERP systems.

Pre-trained models cover common documents, and teams can train models for their own layouts with a few samples. Workflows add approval steps, matching against purchase orders, and exception handling. Language models help with messy or varied documents.

Nanonets has a free tier and paid plans by volume. Extraction accuracy depends on document quality, and sensitive financial data needs security review. Alternatives include Docsumo, Rossum, Hypatos, ABBYY, and Amazon Textract. Nanonets suits finance and operations teams processing documents.
