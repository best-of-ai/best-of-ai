Autodesk Construction Cloud brings together the tools that general contractors, owners, and subcontractors use to run building projects. A shared data environment keeps drawings, models, and documents in one place with version control, so everyone works from the current set. Teams use it for design review, model coordination across disciplines, quantity takeoff, cost estimating, issue tracking, and quality and safety checks in the field.

Autodesk has been folding the product line into Forma, its industry cloud, and the construction products now carry the Forma name, such as Forma Build, Forma Data Management, Forma Takeoff, and Forma Estimate. They connect to Autodesk's design tools like Revit, which keeps information flowing from the design model to the construction site. AI features help automate routine work and surface insights, for example by flagging risks, extracting information from documents, or speeding up takeoff.

The tools are sold by subscription, usually per project or per user depending on the module, with enterprise agreements for large contractors. The platform works best when the whole project team uses it, which can be a hurdle with many subcontractors. Procore is the main competitor in construction management, and Trimble and Bentley offer tools that overlap on the design and engineering side. Older references to Autodesk Construction Cloud describe the same set of products under the former name.
