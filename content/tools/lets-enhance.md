Let's Enhance takes a small or low-quality image and makes it larger. The AI adds plausible detail as it increases resolution, so a photo can be printed bigger or used on a high-resolution display with less blur. It also reduces noise and compression artifacts and improves color and lighting.

E-commerce sellers use it to improve product photos, designers to rescue old assets, and photographers to prepare prints. Batch processing and an API handle large volumes, and extra tools cover background removal and image generation.

Let's Enhance has free credits and paid plans. Upscaling invents detail rather than recovering it, so results on faces and text need checking. Alternatives include Topaz Gigapixel, Adobe Photoshop Super Resolution, Upscayl, Magnific, and Remini. Let's Enhance suits people who need larger, cleaner images.
