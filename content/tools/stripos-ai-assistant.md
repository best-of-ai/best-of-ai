Stripo AI Assistant - an email production tool designed to automate repetitive steps and free you up to focus on what really matters.

With it, you have full control over every aspect of your email campaigns. You can choose the number of emails to generate, set the subject lines, and customize the content to suit your needs. Stripo AI Assistant streamlines routine tasks so you can focus on strategy and creativity. 

Stripo’s AI Assistant enhances the email creation process by:
- Generating emails instantly – AI-powered automation reduces time spent on production.
- Optimizing email design and structure – AI ensures well-structured layouts while allowing users to fine-tune branding.
- Improving content clarity – AI-generated emails maintain strong readability, minimizing the need for manual editing.

The AI Assistant can generate different types of emails to fit your needs:
- series of webinar emails — from invitations and reminders to post-event follow-ups — ensuring no step in your funnel goes unnoticed;
- email digest, bundling your latest news or product updates into a concise, engaging format;
- surprise campaign, specifying a topic so the AI Assistant can craft a randomly generated promo email to spark fresh ideas or test out a novel approach.

Stripo AI Assistant is an invaluable asset for marketers looking to speed up their workflow and reduce manual effort.
