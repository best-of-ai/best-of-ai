Slab gives teams a clean place to write and organize internal documentation. Content is arranged in topics, and the editor emphasizes readability. Engineering, support, and operations teams use it for runbooks, handbooks, and policies.

Search is the core feature. It queries Slab content and connected tools such as Google Drive, GitHub, and Slack in one place, and the AI answers questions in natural language with links to sources. Verification features prompt owners to keep pages up to date.

Slab has a free tier and paid plans per user. Alternatives include Notion, Confluence, Guru, Glean, and Bloomfire. Slab suits small and mid-sized teams that want simple knowledge sharing.
