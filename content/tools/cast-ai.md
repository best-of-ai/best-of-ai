CAST AI is an automation layer for Kubernetes. After connecting to a cluster on AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure, it analyzes workloads, chooses the best-priced instance types, scales nodes up and down, and replaces expensive capacity with spot instances when workloads can tolerate interruptions.

The platform also covers related needs. Savings reports show the effect, workload autoscaling adjusts resource requests, and security scanning finds misconfigurations. More recently it added tools for running GPU workloads and AI inference more efficiently. Customers keep their own cloud accounts.

CAST AI offers a free analysis and pricing tied to the compute it manages, often a share of the savings. Automation needs trust and careful rollout, since aggressive changes can affect reliability. Alternatives include Spot by Flexera, Karpenter, Kubecost, PerfectScale, and native cloud autoscalers. CAST AI suits teams with large Kubernetes bills.
