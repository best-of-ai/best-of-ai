Yodlee lets financial apps pull in customers' account data. A budgeting tool, lender, or wealth platform can use Yodlee's APIs to link to thousands of financial institutions, retrieve balances and transactions, and keep them updated. That saves each company from building separate connections to every bank.

Beyond raw data, Yodlee enriches transactions. Machine learning cleans merchant names, assigns categories, and flags patterns, and analytics products use aggregated data to describe consumer spending trends. Account verification and income checks help lenders confirm information, and the company supports open banking rules in regions where they exist.

Envestnet owned Yodlee for years before selling it to STG, and Yodlee continues to supply Envestnet and other customers. Financial data access raises privacy and security duties, and the industry has been moving from screen scraping to bank-approved APIs. Alternatives include Plaid, MX, Finicity from Mastercard, Akoya, and TrueLayer in Europe. Yodlee suits fintechs and financial institutions that need account data.
