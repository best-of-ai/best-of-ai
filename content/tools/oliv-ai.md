Oliv AI assists sellers before, during, and after calls. Before a meeting, it gathers context on the account and contacts and drafts a briefing. During the call, it records and transcribes. Afterward, it writes notes, updates the CRM, and drafts follow-up emails.

For managers, it scores deals against frameworks such as MEDDIC and highlights risks and gaps, which supports coaching and forecasting. The product presents these functions as agents that take over routine sales admin.

Oliv AI is sold on subscription to sales teams. Recording calls requires consent, and CRM updates by AI should be checked. Alternatives include Gong, Clari, Avoma, Fireflies, and Salesforce Einstein. Oliv AI suits sales teams that want less admin.
