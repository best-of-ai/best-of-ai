Watermelon is aimed at support teams that want automation without engineering. They design conversation flows in a visual builder, train the bot on help content, and connect it to chat channels such as websites and WhatsApp. Complex cases transfer to humans with the conversation history.

AI features answer free-form questions from a knowledge base, and analytics show resolution rates and topics. The platform includes a shared inbox for agents, so the bot and humans work in one system.

Watermelon is sold on subscription. Alternatives include Intercom Fin, Tidio, Zendesk AI agents, Landbot, and Kommunicate. Watermelon suits small and mid-sized support teams.
