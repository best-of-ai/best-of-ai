Salesloft helps sales teams organize outbound and follow-up work. Reps follow cadences, which are structured sequences of emails, calls, and social touches, and the platform tells them who to contact next and records each activity in the CRM. Managers can see which messages and sequences generate replies and meetings, and coach reps with call recordings and analytics.

The platform has expanded into revenue orchestration. Conversation intelligence records and summarizes calls, deal management tracks stage progress and risk, and forecasting tools combine pipeline data with engagement signals. Salesloft acquired Drift for conversational marketing and has combined with Clari, a revenue forecasting company, to build a broader revenue platform. AI assistants prepare meeting briefs, draft messages, and suggest next steps.

Salesloft is sold on annual contracts per seat with tiers, aimed at mid-sized and enterprise sales teams, and implementation takes time. It's heavier than lightweight tools like Apollo or Lemlist. Outreach is the closest rival, Gong dominates conversation intelligence, and HubSpot Sales Hub is an integrated alternative. Because of the company's mergers, product packaging has been changing, so check the current offering with the vendor.
