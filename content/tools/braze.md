Braze helps brands talk to customers on the channels where they spend time. Marketers segment users by behavior, design journeys in a visual canvas, and send messages triggered by actions such as opening an app, abandoning a cart, or reaching a milestone. Real-time data means messages respond to what is happening now.

AI features improve targeting and creation. Predictive models estimate who might churn or buy, optimization chooses the best send time and channel for each person, and generative tools help write subject lines and copy. Analytics measure the effect of campaigns on retention and revenue.

Braze is sold on custom contracts, scaled by users and messages, and is popular with consumer apps, retail, and media. It takes technical work to integrate and skilled teams to run well. Competitors include Iterable, MoEngage, CleverTap, Klaviyo, and Salesforce Marketing Cloud. Braze suits large consumer brands with sophisticated lifecycle marketing.
