Users search for a wine and vintage and see listings from retailers and auction houses, with average prices. Ratings from critics appear next to prices. A paid Pro tier adds price history and bulk tools.

Data depends on merchant listings, so rare bottles may have little data.

Vivino and CellarTracker are alternatives. Wine-Searcher suits sommeliers who source and price wine.
