Stats Perform collects detailed data about professional sports. Trained collectors and tracking technology record events in matches, such as passes, shots, and player positions, and the company supplies that information to broadcasters, media companies, sportsbooks, leagues, and clubs. Its Opta brand is widely seen in football statistics on television and websites. The data feeds power live scores, fantasy games, and graphics.

AI is used both to create data and to analyze it. Computer vision extracts player and ball tracking from video, which reduces the manual work of collecting information, and models predict outcomes, evaluate player performance, and estimate the value of actions on the field. Coaches and analysts use the tools for scouting and tactical analysis, while media and betting clients use predictions and content such as automated match summaries.

Stats Perform sells to organizations under custom agreements, with prices depending on sports, data depth, and use. It isn't a consumer product, though fans see its data in many apps. The quality of predictions depends on the sport and the data available. Competitors include Sportradar, Genius Sports, Hudl's Wyscout, and Second Spectrum, with teams also building their own analytics. It's most relevant to professional organizations that rely on structured sports data.
