AspenTech sells engineering software to process industries. Chemical engineers use Aspen Plus and Aspen HYSYS to model how reactions, separations, and heat flows behave across a plant, which lets them design and test processes on screen before building them, and tune existing plants for efficiency, safety, and emissions.

The company also sells operations software. Optimization tools help plants decide how to run units to hit targets for cost and throughput, and asset performance products use machine learning on sensor data to predict equipment failures before they cause downtime. Digital twins of plant units connect design models to real operating data.

AspenTech is sold to refiners, chemical makers, utilities, and engineering firms through licenses and subscriptions, with significant training and implementation. Emerson, a large industrial automation company, owns it, and the two product lines are being combined. Alternatives include AVEVA, Honeywell, Siemens, ChemCAD, and Schneider Electric software. AspenTech suits process engineers and plant operators.
