Bria builds visual generative AI around licensing. Its models are trained on licensed data from content partners rather than scraped web images, and contributors share in revenue, which gives companies a clearer position on copyright than many alternatives. The company also offers indemnification to enterprise customers.

The product is delivered as APIs and tools for businesses. Capabilities include generating images from prompts, editing and background removal, creating product shots, and controls for brand consistency. Enterprises in advertising, e-commerce, and media can embed these features in their apps, with options for fine-tuning on their own assets.

Bria is sold by usage and enterprise contracts. Models trained on a narrower licensed dataset may offer less variety than the largest general models. Alternatives include Adobe Firefly, Getty Images generative tools, Shutterstock's generator, Stability AI, and Midjourney. Bria suits businesses that need images with clear licensing.
