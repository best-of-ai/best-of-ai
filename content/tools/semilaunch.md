SemiLaunch gives small makers a place to announce a product. Makers submit their project, which appears in a weekly cohort, and visitors browse, vote, and comment. Top products are highlighted, helping early-stage founders gain attention and feedback.

The weekly format differs from daily launch sites, giving products more time to collect votes. Listings also serve as backlinks and discovery for new tools in directories.

Visibility depends on community size. SemiLaunch has a free tier and paid promotion options. Alternatives include Product Hunt, Peerlist, BetaList, Indie Hackers, and Dang.ai. SemiLaunch suits indie makers looking for launch exposure.
