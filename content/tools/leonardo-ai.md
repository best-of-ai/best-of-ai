Leonardo AI was built specifically for game developers and digital artists who need consistent, high-volume asset production. Where general image generators prioritise variety and artistic range, Leonardo's community-developed fine-tuned models are optimised for specific needs: stylised game characters, photorealistic concept environments, fantasy creature design, product visualisation. You choose the model that fits your art style rather than fighting a general-purpose model to stay in one direction.

The Model Training feature lets studios upload their own reference images and create a private model fine-tuned on their proprietary art style. This solves the consistency problem that makes generative AI difficult in professional game pipelines — instead of output that wanders in style between generations, a trained Leonardo model produces assets that look like they belong to the same game. The Canvas editor layers additional control, letting you paint masks over specific regions, inpaint replacements, and make targeted edits to generated images without starting over.

Leonardo uses a token-based credit system. The free tier provides 150 tokens per day — enough for around 30 images at standard quality. Apprentice ($12/month), Artisan ($30/month), and Maestro ($60/month) tiers scale up token counts, API access, and training capacity. A mobile app is available for generating assets on the go. Game development teams, concept art studios, indie developers, and NFT artists are the primary users — all sharing the need for consistent, high-volume visual output in a specific style.
