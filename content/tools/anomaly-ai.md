Anomaly AI is for people who work with data that is too big or messy for a normal spreadsheet. A user uploads Excel or CSV files or connects sources such as databases, warehouses, and Google Sheets, then asks questions in natural language. The tool answers with tables, charts, and written explanations.

The vendor emphasizes traceable answers, meaning the analysis is run as code against the data so results can be inspected and not just generated by a language model. It can merge files with database data in one analysis, find trends, and flag unusual values. A connector allows assistants like Claude to hand questions about your data to the service.

Anomaly AI has a free tier and paid plans. It is a young product, upload limits depend on the plan, and many of the comparison articles online are published by the vendor itself. Always spot check results against the raw data. Alternatives include Julius AI, ChatGPT's data analysis, Hex, Power BI Copilot, and Excel's Copilot. Anomaly AI suits analysts handling large spreadsheets.
