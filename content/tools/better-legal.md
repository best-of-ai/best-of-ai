BetterLegal Assistant is an AI-powered Chrome extension designed to simplify complex legal language into plain English, making it easier for users to understand legal documents such as contracts, terms of service, and privacy policies. It features instant legal text simplification, where users can select legal text and have it translated into everyday language. The extension is user-friendly and includes a unique Pirate Mode for a fun twist on legal translations. It operates on a credit-based system, starting with 50 free credits for new users. While it helps demystify legal jargon, it is not a substitute for professional legal advice and may not guarantee 100% accuracy in translations.
