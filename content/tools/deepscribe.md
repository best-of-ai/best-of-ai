DeepScribe is an ambient scribe for doctors. With the patient's consent, the system listens during the visit, separates the conversation from small talk, and produces a structured note in the format a specialty expects. The clinician edits and signs it in the electronic health record.

The company started with oncology, where notes are long and complex, and has expanded to other outpatient fields. Beyond notes, it helps capture details needed for coding and quality reporting, and works with major record systems so notes appear where doctors expect them. The goal is to give clinicians their evenings back.

DeepScribe is sold to practices and health systems, with pricing by agreement. As with all ambient tools, notes can contain errors, so clinicians must review them, and patients should know recording is happening. Competitors include Abridge, Ambience, Suki, Nuance DAX in Microsoft's Dragon Copilot, and Heidi Health. DeepScribe suits outpatient practices wanting less documentation time.
