Leo Books is a personalized childrenʼs book platform that turns your child into the hero of a one-of-a-kind story. By uploading a few photos, choosing an illustration style, and selecting or writing a story prompt, parents can generate an original story and custom illustrations featuring their child, plus pets, favorite toys, and meaningful details. Users can preview their personalized storybook in minutes,then order a high-quality printed hardcover or softcover.
