Users build recipes from ingredient databases and see estimates of color, bitterness, and strength. It handles water chemistry, fermentation schedules, and shopping lists. Desktop and mobile versions sync.

It is a paid license.

Brewfather and Brewer's Friend are alternatives. BeerSmith suits homebrewers and small commercial brewers.
