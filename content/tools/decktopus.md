Decktopus is built for people who need a presentation quickly. You enter a topic or a short brief, answer a few questions about the audience and goal, and the AI produces an outline and a deck of designed slides with suggested images and speaker notes. You then edit the text and layout in a drag-and-drop editor. It also offers templates for pitches, reports, and sales decks, and a brand kit for colors and logos.

Some features go beyond slides. Presentations can include interactive elements such as forms and quizzes, and shared decks have analytics so you can see who opened them and how long they viewed each slide. Tools help with rehearsal, such as timing and talking points, and there is a feature to turn a deck into a shareable web link. Export to PowerPoint and PDF is supported.

Decktopus has a free option with limits and paid plans for more decks, branding, and analytics. AI-generated slides tend to be generic until you add specifics, so the deck usually needs real editing for accuracy and tone. Gamma is the best-known AI deck generator and produces a more modern look, Beautiful.ai focuses on auto-formatting, Canva and Pitch are strong general options, and Microsoft's Copilot works inside PowerPoint. Decktopus is a sensible pick for fast drafts and trackable sales decks.
