Rayyan streamlines a laborious step in evidence synthesis. Researchers import search results from databases, and multiple reviewers screen titles and abstracts, marking papers as included or excluded, often blind to each other's decisions. The tool tracks conflicts and helps resolve them.

Machine learning ranks the unscreened papers by likelihood of relevance, so the likely includes surface earlier and screening can end sooner. Highlighting of keywords and filtering by terms speeds reading. Newer features use AI to assist with extraction and summaries.

Rayyan has a free tier and paid plans, and is widely used in health sciences. AI ranking supports but does not replace careful screening required by review standards. Alternatives include Covidence, DistillerSR, EPPI-Reviewer, ASReview, and Elicit. Rayyan suits researchers conducting systematic reviews.
