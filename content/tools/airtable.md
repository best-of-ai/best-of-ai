Airtable looks like a spreadsheet but behaves like a database. Records live in tables with typed fields such as dates, attachments, checkboxes, and links to records in other tables, which allows relationships that spreadsheets cannot handle. The same data can be shown as a grid, kanban board, calendar, gallery, or timeline, and filters and groups tailor each view. It's used for content calendars, product roadmaps, CRM, event planning, and operations.

Beyond data, it provides tools for building workflows. Automations trigger actions when records change, interfaces let you design custom dashboards and apps on top of the data, and integrations connect with Slack, Google Workspace, and other tools. AI features can summarize, categorize, extract, and generate content in fields, and the platform has added AI agents and app-building features for creating workflows from a description.

Airtable has a free plan with limits and paid plans per user with more records, automations, and features. Costs rise for large teams, and databases at scale can become hard to manage. Notion databases are simpler, Coda blends documents and tables, Smartsheet suits project management, and Baserow and NocoDB are open-source alternatives. Airtable suits teams that need structured, customizable data without engineering.
