Feedzai provides real-time fraud detection for banks, card issuers, and payment companies. Its models look at each transaction along with customer and device behavior and decide in milliseconds whether to approve, block, or send it for review. Flagging account takeover, card fraud, and scams quickly limits losses.

The platform also covers anti-money laundering and customer risk, combining signals from different parts of a bank in a single view. Case management tools help investigators work alerts, and explainability features show why a decision was made, which matters to regulators. Newer work addresses authorized scams where customers are tricked into sending money.

Feedzai is sold to financial institutions on enterprise contracts. Fraud tools involve a constant tradeoff between stopping criminals and annoying legitimate customers, and criminals adapt. Competitors include Featurespace (acquired by Visa), BioCatch, Sardine, Actimize, and Socure. Feedzai suits banks and payment firms fighting fraud at scale.
