Mew.Design is a multi-agent AI design platform that allows users to generate and refine graphic content using natural language. Instead of templates or fixed styles, users interact with AI-powered Meow Designers—specialized agents trained with real designers' experience and aesthetic logic. Each Meow Designer has a distinct visual personality and domain expertise, simulating how real creative teams work.

Just describe your idea in plain text, and Mew.Design will generate editable visuals—such as posters, flyers, social posts, business cards, invitations, or brochures—ready for real-world use. Users can fine-tune the output through conversational feedback or visual references. No design tools or technical knowledge needed.


Key Features:
- Natural language design creation
- Text-based precision editing
- AI agents with unique styles and specialties
- Multi-agent collaboration system
- Visual input understanding (upload reference images)

Use Cases:
- Social media marketing
- Event and business collateral
- Personal projects and branding
- Rapid prototyping for startups and freelancers
