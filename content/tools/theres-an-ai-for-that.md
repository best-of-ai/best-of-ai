There's An AI For That, often shortened to TAAFT, started as a simple search for AI tools by task. A user types what they want to do, such as write a cover letter or remove a background, and gets a list of tools for that job, with ratings and descriptions.

Its size is the draw. It adds new tools constantly, offers an alert for launches, and includes pricing and user reviews. Some listings are promoted by makers.

The site is free to browse. With so many entries, quality varies, and details need verification on each product site. Similar resources include Futurepedia, Future Tools, Aixploria, OpenTools, and Toolify. There's An AI For That suits people who start with a task and want to find a tool.
