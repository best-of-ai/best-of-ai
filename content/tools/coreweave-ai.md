CoreWeave began as a company mining cryptocurrency and turned its GPU capacity into a cloud for AI. Customers rent clusters of NVIDIA GPUs for training and running models, with networking and storage built for large workloads. Because it specializes, it can often deploy new GPU generations quickly.

The platform runs on Kubernetes and offers managed services for training, inference, and storage. Major AI labs and big technology companies have signed large contracts, and the company has built or leased data centers around the world. It acquired Weights & Biases to add developer tooling to its infrastructure.

CoreWeave is a public company and sells mostly through contracts, plus on-demand options. Its business depends on demand for AI compute, financing for data centers, and supplier relationships. Alternatives include Lambda, Crusoe, Nebius, Together AI, and the major clouds. CoreWeave suits organizations that need large GPU capacity.
