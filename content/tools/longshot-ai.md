LongShot AI is aimed at publishers and marketers who want blog posts with fewer unsupported claims. You provide a topic or keyword, and the tool researches the web and writes a structured article, with options to cite sources and run fact checks against search results. It can generate outlines, intros, and full drafts, and has a workspace for editing.

Additional features help with the content workflow. An SEO assistant suggests keywords and optimization steps, a rewrite tool paraphrases and adjusts tone, and a calendar helps plan publishing. Teams can collaborate in the editor, and templates cover common formats such as product reviews, how-to articles, and listicles. Users range from solo bloggers to small agencies.

LongShot AI has a free tier with limited use and paid plans for more words and features. Fact-checking tools reduce but do not eliminate errors, and AI-generated drafts still need human editing to add original insight. Competitors include Koala, Frase, Surfer, Writesonic, Jasper, and general assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude. LongShot AI suits small content teams that want quick, researched drafts.
