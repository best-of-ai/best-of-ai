Shortly AI was one of the early GPT-based writing tools built for fiction and long-form content. A writer types, and with a keystroke the assistant continues the text in the same voice, or offers rewrites and alternatives. It aimed to keep the writer in control of the direction.

It was used for blog posts, stories, and essays, particularly to push through slow stretches. The product emerged before general chat assistants became widely used, and those assistants now cover much of the same ground.

Information about its current status is limited, so check the site for availability and pricing. Alternatives include Sudowrite, Lex, Jasper, Claude, and ChatGPT. Shortly AI suits writers who want inline continuation help.
