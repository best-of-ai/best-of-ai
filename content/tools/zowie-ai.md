Zowie focuses on online retail support. Its AI agent connects to a store's order system, answers questions about delivery and returns, processes simple actions, and hands off tricky cases to staff. It works across chat, email, and messaging channels.

The platform emphasizes decision-making that follows a brand's policies, and gives support managers tools to review and adjust behavior. Analytics show resolution rates and topics.

Zowie is sold on subscription. Alternatives include Gorgias, Intercom Fin, Ada, Netomi, and Zendesk AI agents. Zowie suits e-commerce support teams.
