Ludo.ai aims to speed up the early stages of game development. Designers can brainstorm game concepts and mechanics, get suggestions based on trends in app stores, and generate descriptions and feature lists. A market research tool analyzes top-performing games to show what genres and themes are popular, which helps indie studios and publishers decide what to build.

The platform includes asset generation. It can produce concept art and character designs, sprites and sprite sheets, short animations, UI elements, and sound effects and music from prompts, and convert images into basic 3D models in some plans. Outputs are intended for prototypes and mockups to test ideas quickly, and can be exported for use in engines like Unity.

Ludo.ai is paid, with plans by credits and a trial. Generated art often needs refinement by artists for production use, and the ownership and licensing terms for AI-generated assets should be reviewed. Alternatives include Scenario for game asset generation, Layer AI, Leonardo.ai, Midjourney, Inworld for characters, and traditional tools like Aseprite. Ludo suits small teams and solo developers who want quick prototypes and market insight.
