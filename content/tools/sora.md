Sora's original demo videos in February 2024 were the moment AI video went from a curiosity to something that genuinely alarmed professional filmmakers. The clips showed coherent camera motion, realistic lighting that tracked moving objects, and physically plausible interactions between scene elements — qualities that previous video generators couldn't maintain for more than a second or two. The general release in December 2024, while more limited in clip length than the demos suggested, confirmed the underlying capability was real.

The generation process works through a text description of the scene, camera movement, and mood. You can specify the aspect ratio, duration (up to 20 seconds on most plans), and style. A storyboard mode lets you chain multiple prompts together into a longer sequential video. Remix mode takes an existing video and transforms it according to a new description while preserving the general composition and timing. The quality is high enough for b-roll and concept video but not yet consistent enough for hero content without human post-production.

Sora is included with ChatGPT Plus ($20/month) at 50 priority and 50 relaxed generations per month, and with ChatGPT Pro ($200/month) at higher limits. No standalone subscription exists — it's bundled with the ChatGPT account. Output quality and generation options scale with plan tier. The primary use cases at this stage are concept development, storyboarding, social media content, and situations where a director wants to visualise an idea before committing to a production budget.
