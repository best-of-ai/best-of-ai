Find My Script is a concierge-style service that helps patients locate prescription medications that are difficult to find due to shortages or inconsistent pharmacy inventory.

Instead of calling dozens of pharmacies, users submit a request and our team checks local and regional pharmacies on their behalf. When availability is found, users are notified so they can fill their prescription faster.

Find My Script is especially helpful for medications affected by ongoing shortages, including ADHD treatments and GLP-1 medications. Our goal is to save patients time, reduce stress, and make the prescription process easier during periods of limited supply.
