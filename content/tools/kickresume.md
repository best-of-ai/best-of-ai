Kickresume helps people create resumes quickly. You choose a template, enter your experience, and the builder arranges everything in a clean layout. A library of professionally designed templates covers different industries and styles, and the exports are designed to be readable by applicant tracking systems, which scan resumes before a human sees them. It also builds cover letters, personal websites, and portfolios.

AI assists with the writing. The AI resume writer can generate summaries and bullet points from your role and achievements, tailor a resume for a specific job description, and write a cover letter. A resume checker evaluates structure, keywords, and clarity and suggests improvements, and extra tools help with interview practice and job tracking. The AI features speed up drafting but can produce generic language.

Kickresume has a free plan with limited templates and downloads and paid plans with full access. Generated text should be edited so that it accurately reflects what you did, since exaggerated or invented claims can backfire in interviews. Recruiters increasingly recognize generic AI phrasing. Alternatives include Canva, Zety, Resume.io, Rezi, Teal, and Enhancv, and ChatGPT or Claude can help refine content for free. Kickresume is a convenient option for formatting plus AI drafting.
