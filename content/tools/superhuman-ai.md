Superhuman AI is a free daily email aimed at professionals who want to apply AI at work. Issues summarize news in brief, then spend time on practical advice: tools worth trying, prompts that work, and ways to save time.

The audience skews toward business users and non-technical readers rather than researchers. The newsletter is supported by sponsors, which are labeled, and has grown to one of the larger AI newsletters.

It is free. Do not confuse it with Superhuman, the email app, or with Grammarly's rebranded company name. Alternatives include The Rundown AI, Ben's Bites, TLDR AI, The Batch, and AI Breakfast. Superhuman AI suits busy professionals who want practical AI tips.
