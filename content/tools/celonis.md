Celonis is the best-known process mining platform. Every action in business systems, such as creating an order or approving an invoice, leaves a record with a time stamp. Celonis reads these logs and rebuilds the real process, showing the paths that cases take, where they wait, and how often they deviate from the intended flow.

With that picture, teams find delays, rework, and costly exceptions. The platform quantifies the impact, suggests improvements, and can trigger automated actions or alerts. AI features answer questions about process data and recommend where to focus. Common uses include order-to-cash, procure-to-pay, and supply chain work.

Celonis is sold to large enterprises on subscription. Setting up data extraction from complex systems takes effort, and benefits require follow-through by process owners. Alternatives include SAP Signavio, UiPath Process Mining, Microsoft Process Mining, Software AG ARIS, and Apromore. Celonis suits large organizations improving operational processes.
