AI For Developer is a niche directory focused on the developer audience. Instead of listing every AI product, it gathers tools that matter to people who write software: coding assistants, AI-powered editors, model APIs, testing and documentation tools, and infrastructure for building AI features. Listings give a short description, links, and categories, which makes it easy to browse by use case.

Directories like this serve as a starting point for discovery. They help developers learn what exists, compare similar tools, and keep up with the rapid pace of new launches. A curated approach aims to reduce the noise of general AI directories that include thousands of products across every field, by focusing on relevance to engineering.

The site is free to use. Like all directories, it can lag behind fast-moving tools, and listing does not indicate endorsement or quality, so check the official sites for current features and pricing. Other places to find developer AI tools include Product Hunt, GitHub's trending lists, Hugging Face, and community forums such as Hacker News and Reddit. Best of AI offers a broader directory with categories including code assistants and developer tools. AI For Developer suits engineers who want a focused list.
