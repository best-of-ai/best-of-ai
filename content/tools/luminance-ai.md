Luminance is built for lawyers who deal with large volumes of agreements. Teams upload contracts, and the platform identifies key terms, highlights unusual or risky clauses, and compares documents against company standards. Its origins lie in machine learning developed at the University of Cambridge.

Beyond review, Luminance supports negotiation and drafting. An AI assistant can propose edits in Word, summarize positions, and handle routine agreements with limited human involvement, escalating exceptions to lawyers. In due diligence, it speeds through thousands of documents to find issues across a deal's data room.

Luminance is sold to law firms and corporate legal departments on enterprise contracts. Lawyers remain responsible for the final work. Alternatives include Harvey, Ivo, LegalOn, Spellbook, and Kira, now part of Litera. Luminance suits legal teams reviewing contracts at volume.
