CodeRabbit adds itself as a reviewer to every pull request automatically, posting a structured summary of what the PR changes before any human has looked at it. The summary covers the scope of changes, areas of potential risk, and a quick read on the approach taken — which helps human reviewers orient quickly rather than spending the first several minutes figuring out what they're looking at. Line-by-line comments flag specific issues with explanations of why they matter.

The tool learns from feedback over time. When developers dismiss a comment with an explanation ("this pattern is intentional because..."), CodeRabbit adjusts and stops flagging similar patterns on future PRs. This reduces false positives as it acclimates to your team's conventions and style. It also integrates with Linear and Jira, linking PR feedback to relevant tickets and keeping context connected across your development workflow.

The free plan covers open-source projects with unlimited public repositories. Pro at $15/month per seat covers private repositories with the full feature set. Enterprise plans add SSO, data retention controls, and self-hosted deployment options. For teams where PR review is a bottleneck — either because reviewers are scarce, reviews are slow, or recurring issues keep slipping through — CodeRabbit provides consistent, immediate first-pass feedback that frees human reviewers to focus on architectural concerns rather than mechanical bugs.
