AlphaSense is a search engine for business information. Analysts, investors, and strategy teams use it to find what companies and experts are saying across a large body of documents: earnings call transcripts, SEC filings, broker and industry research, press releases, news, and expert call transcripts. Its search understands financial language, so a query for supply chain risk or pricing pressure returns relevant passages instead of just keyword matches, with highlights and context.

The AI layer saves reading time. Summaries pull out key points from calls and filings, and a deep research feature builds a structured report on a company or topic with citations back to the sources. Alerts track mentions of topics, competitors, or executives and push them to you as they appear. Expert transcript libraries from its acquisition of Tegus add primary-source commentary that is otherwise expensive to obtain.

AlphaSense is sold by enterprise subscription with custom pricing, used by investment banks, hedge funds, private equity firms, asset managers, and large corporations. It is expensive, so it suits professional users. Because summaries are generated by AI, important facts should be checked against the underlying documents. Bloomberg Terminal and FactSet offer broader data, and Daloopa and Sentieo-style tools overlap on filings analysis. For searching text-heavy sources quickly, AlphaSense is among the leaders.
