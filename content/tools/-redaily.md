红薯养号器是一款专为小红书（中国领先的生活方式社交平台）创作者和运营者设计的Chrome浏览器扩展。

核心功能：
• 智能浏览 - 模拟真实用户行为
• 互动自动化 - 自动点赞、收藏、评论
• 数据分析 - 追踪账号养号数据
• 定时任务 - 设置自动化养号计划
• AI评论 - 智能生成互动内容

很多小红书创作者每天要花费大量时间来维持账号活跃度。红薯养号器就是为了解决这个痛点而生，它可以帮助创作者每天节省30分钟以上的重复性工作。

确保所有自动化操作都符合平台规则，同时保持互动的真实性。通过简单的浏览器扩展，创作者就能轻松实现账号日常运营的自动化。

Redaily is a Chrome browser extension designed specifically for creators and operators on Xiaohongshu (China's leading lifestyle social platform).
Core Features:
Smart Browsing - Simulates real user behavior
Interaction Automation - Automatic likes, saves, and comments
Data Analytics - Tracks account growth metrics
Scheduled Tasks - Sets automated account growth plans
AI Comments - Intelligently generates interactive content
Many Xiaohongshu creators spend significant time maintaining account activity daily. Redaily was created to solve this pain point, helping creators save over 30 minutes of repetitive work each day.
Ensures all automated operations comply with platform rules while maintaining authentic interactions. Through a simple browser extension, creators can easily achieve automation of daily account operations.
