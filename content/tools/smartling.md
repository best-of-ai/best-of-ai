Smartling connects content systems to translators. Websites, apps, and documents flow in through integrations, and Smartling applies translation memory and machine translation, then routes text to human linguists where quality matters most. Translations return to the source system.

Features include visual context for translators, glossaries, quality scoring, and AI that translates and edits at scale. The company has pushed AI-first workflows to reduce cost and time, and reports on translation quality and spend.

Smartling is sold on enterprise subscriptions. Alternatives include Phrase, Lokalise, Transifex, Smartcat, and RWS Trados. Smartling suits large companies localizing content continuously.
