Google Cloud Vision is a set of pretrained image analysis features available through an API. Send an image, and it can return labels describing what it contains, detect objects and their locations, read printed and handwritten text, identify landmarks and logos, detect faces and emotions without identifying individuals, and flag explicit content with SafeSearch. It processes single images and batches, including scanned documents.

Typical uses include indexing photo libraries, extracting text from receipts and signs, moderating uploaded images, and powering search by image. Google offers related products for more specialized needs, such as Document AI for forms and Vertex AI for custom models and newer multimodal Gemini models that can describe and reason about images in more detail. Product search features help retailers match photos to catalogs.

Pricing is per image per feature, with a free monthly allowance. The classic API is simple but less flexible than multimodal models, and developers increasingly choose Gemini for tasks that require understanding. Face detection raises privacy considerations. Alternatives include Amazon Rekognition, Azure AI Vision, Clarifai, and open-source models. Google Cloud Vision suits developers who want a straightforward, scalable image analysis service.
