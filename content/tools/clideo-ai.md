Clideo offers simple video tools that run in the browser. A user uploads a clip, picks a tool such as trimming, merging, compressing, resizing, or converting formats, and downloads the result. The pitch is convenience for quick jobs where installing editing software is not worth it.

AI features add some automation. Auto subtitles transcribe speech and add styled captions in many languages, and background removal and other effects help prepare clips for social media. The editor handles basic timeline work, and tools for audio, GIFs, and screen recording are included in the suite.

Clideo has a free tier that adds a watermark and limits file size, and paid plans remove them. Because files are uploaded, privacy-minded users should check the terms. Alternatives include Kapwing, Clipchamp, VEED, CapCut, and Canva video. Clideo suits people who need quick, simple video edits.
