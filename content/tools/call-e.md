CALL-E works in three steps: plan, confirm, dial. Your assistant drafts the call, you approve it, and CALL-E places it, handling phone menus, hold, voicemail and transfers. You get back a summary, a transcript, a confidence label and a typed JSON result.

It installs as a skill in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and OpenClaw, and you can also reach it through WhatsApp, a REST API, an MCP server, or TypeScript and Python SDKs. It makes outbound calls only, to real numbers in the US and Canada. The company states SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 and GDPR compliance.

New users get free credits to try it, and billing details are in the documentation. Individuals who want their assistant to make routine calls are one audience, and developers adding calling to an agent are the other.
