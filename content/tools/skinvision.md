Users take a photo, and the AI classifies it as lower or higher risk, with a prompt to see a doctor when needed. Some health plans and clinics use it for triage. Clinical studies of accuracy have been published.

It does not replace a dermatologist, and false negatives are possible.

Miiskin and DermEngine are related tools. SkinVision suits dermatologists who want to understand patient-facing skin check apps.
