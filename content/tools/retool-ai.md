Retool is designed for engineers who need to build internal software quickly. Developers connect databases, APIs, and services, drag components such as tables, forms, and charts onto a canvas, and write queries and logic in SQL and JavaScript where needed. The result is an application for operations, support, or finance teams to manage data, approve requests, or run workflows, built in hours instead of weeks.

AI is integrated across the platform. A generative assistant can create an app from a description, write queries, and generate components, and Retool offers AI actions to classify, summarize, and extract information as parts of apps and workflows. Retool Agents let teams build agents that use their data and tools, and workflows automate scheduled and event-driven tasks. Enterprise controls cover permissions, audit logs, and self-hosting.

Retool has a free plan and paid plans per user, with enterprise options. It's powerful but requires technical skill, so it's different from consumer no-code tools. Alternatives include Appsmith and ToolJet as open-source options, Superblocks, Airplane's successors, Microsoft Power Apps, and AI app builders like Lovable and Bolt for customer-facing apps. Retool suits engineering teams building internal tools.
