Hex combines notebooks with business intelligence. Teams connect their data warehouse, write SQL and Python in a notebook, and turn the results into interactive apps that non-technical colleagues can use without code. Everything lives in one place with version history and comments.

AI is built into the workflow. Magic helps write and fix queries and code, and agents let business users ask questions of governed data in plain language. A semantic layer keeps definitions of metrics consistent, so answers match across reports. Scheduled runs keep outputs fresh.

Hex has a free tier and paid plans for teams. It suits organizations with analysts and a warehouse, and less so those without data infrastructure. Alternatives include Deepnote, Mode, Sigma, Observable, and Jupyter. Hex suits data teams that collaborate with the rest of the business.
