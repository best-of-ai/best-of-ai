Qureos is an AI-powered recruitment and talent intelligence platform built for modern hiring teams. You can simply describe the role and get matched with top-fit candidates instantly. It feels less like filling forms and more like having a conversation with someone who just gets it.


Already have candidates? Upload your list and let Iris enrich, screen, and match them to your open roles. You can also post jobs with one click to over 100 job boards, including LinkedIn and Glassdoor. When promising candidates are found, Iris sends personalised messages that reflect your brand’s voice. 


You can assess applicants through AI video interviews and receive in-depth reports on skills and culture fit. It supports over 20 languages, making it easy to hire across borders. Qureos also integrates smoothly with your ATS, CRM, and ERP tools with no extra effort needed. From sourcing to shortlisting, everything moves faster, so you can fill roles in record time.

Features
Upload and screen your own candidates
Candidate profile enrichment beyond resumes
One-click posting to 100+ job boards
Personalised automated outreach
AI-driven candidate video assessments
Multilingual support (20+ languages)
ATS, CRM, and ERP integrations
