Juro puts the contract workflow in a browser editor. Sales and business teams generate contracts from approved templates, negotiate with counterparties in the same document, collect signatures, and store the result in a searchable repository. Legal sets the rules and approvals, so routine deals move without a lawyer.

AI is part of the product. It can draft clauses from a plain-English description, review third-party paper against a company's positions, summarize agreements, and answer questions about the contract portfolio. Integrations with CRMs and messaging tools keep the process connected to sales.

Juro is sold on subscription to mid-sized companies, especially in technology. The design favors speed for high-volume contracting. Lawyers should review AI suggestions. Alternatives include Ironclad, PandaDoc, DocuSign CLM, Ivo, and Spellbook. Juro suits legal and commercial teams that handle many standard deals.
