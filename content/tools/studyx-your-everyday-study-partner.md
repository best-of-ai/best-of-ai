StudyX is an all-in-one AI learning assistant that helps students get more out of their study time by providing intelligent support before, during, and after class. Students can use StudyX to receive real-time assistance, convert lectures, PDFs, and videos into structured AI notes, and automatically generate flashcards and quizzes for active learning and long-term retention. StudyX also offers a personal study space where students can organize notes, flashcards, quizzes, and past study material in one place, making review and exam preparation more efficient. With multi-format input and an emphasis on understanding concepts rather than just finding answers, StudyX supports effective learning habits across subjects and study scenarios.
