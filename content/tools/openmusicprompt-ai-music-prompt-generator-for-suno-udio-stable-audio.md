OpenMusicPrompt is a professional-grade prompt engineering platform designed specifically for the next generation of AI music models, including Suno (v3.5/v4), Udio, and Stable Audio 2.5.

Unlike generic prompt generators, OpenMusicPrompt focuses on the structural and technical nuances of music production. It allows creators to transform simple ideas or reference tracks into highly structured, model-ready prompts. The platform features a specialized "Music to Prompt" tool that analyzes audio characteristics—such as mood, instrumentation, and BPM—to reverse-engineer effective text prompts.

Key Features: - Multi-Model Support: Tailored outputs for Suno, Udio, and Stable Audio. - Structured Templates: Deep-dive guides for complex genres like Drill, including specialized metatags and arrangement cues. - Music to Prompt: Convert existing audio references into descriptive text prompts. - Community Library: Access a curated collection of high-quality, tested prompt templates for various musical styles.

Whether you are a professional producer looking to speed up your workflow or an enthusiast exploring the limits of generative audio, OpenMusicPrompt provides the precision tools needed to achieve consistent, high-quality musical results. The tool is free to use and requires no complex setup.
