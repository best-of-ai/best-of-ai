Sprinklr serves very large organizations. It manages publishing across social channels, listens for brand mentions, handles customer care across messaging and voice, and analyzes market trends, using a shared data layer so teams see the whole customer picture.

AI runs through the platform: models classify conversations, detect sentiment, route cases, suggest replies, and generate content, and agents handle some customer interactions. Governance tools suit regulated industries and large teams.

Sprinklr is sold on enterprise subscriptions, with high cost and complexity. Alternatives include Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Khoros, Emplifi, and Salesforce. Sprinklr suits large brands managing many channels.
