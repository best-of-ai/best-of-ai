Stable Video Diffusion extends the Stable Diffusion approach to motion. A user supplies a still image, and the model generates a few seconds of video that animates the scene. It was released with open weights, so researchers and hobbyists could run and adapt it.

Many later tools and workflows built on it, adding controls, longer clips, and fine-tunes. It helped prove that open video models were feasible, though newer models from other labs have surpassed it in length and quality.

The model is free to download with licensing terms for commercial use. Running it requires a capable GPU. Alternatives include Runway, Pika, Kling, Luma, and open models such as Wan and HunyuanVideo. Stable Video Diffusion suits researchers and tinkerers.
