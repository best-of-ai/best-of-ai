PrepGo is an AP exam preparation platform built for high school students who want a structured and effective way to study for AP exams. It combines AP-aligned study guides with active learning tools to help students understand key concepts and prepare with confidence.

The platform offers smart flashcards using spaced repetition, AP-style practice questions, unit tests, and full-length mock exams that mirror real exam formats. By guiding students through a clear learning flow—from review and practice to testing—PrepGo helps identify weak areas, improve retention, and reduce exam stress.

PrepGo is well suited for self-study, ongoing review during the school year, and final AP exam preparation.
