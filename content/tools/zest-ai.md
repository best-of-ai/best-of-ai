Zest AI helps lenders approve more borrowers safely. Its models use many variables to estimate credit risk, aiming to include people thin credit files would exclude while keeping losses in check. Lenders deploy the models in their own decisioning workflows.

Explainability is central, since regulators require lenders to give reasons for adverse decisions. Zest provides tools to explain scores and to test models for disparate impact across groups. Credit unions and community banks are among its customers.

Zest AI is sold to lenders on enterprise contracts. Algorithmic lending requires ongoing monitoring for fairness. Alternatives include Upstart's platform, Pagaya, FICO, Experian, and in-house data science. Zest AI suits lenders modernizing underwriting.
