ELSA Speak is focused on spoken English. You speak into the app, and it analyzes your pronunciation and tells you which sounds need work. A personalized learning path sets lessons based on where you struggle, and progress tracking shows improvement over time.

Newer features add conversation practice. A bilingual AI tutor can explain mistakes in your own language, and role-play scenarios let you rehearse situations such as interviews or meetings. Learners include professionals, job seekers, students, and travelers, and there are versions for companies and schools.

The individual plan has a free tier, with paid upgrades for the full set of lessons and tutor features. ELSA is stronger on pronunciation than on general vocabulary or grammar, so many learners pair it with another app. Speak and Duolingo cover broader language learning if you need more than accent work.
