Mixpanel helps product teams understand behavior. You send events from your product, such as sign-ups, searches, and purchases, along with properties about users, and Mixpanel turns them into reports. Funnels show where people drop off, retention charts show who comes back, flows map the paths people take, and cohorts group users by behavior. Self-serve reports let product managers and marketers answer questions without writing SQL.

The platform includes more than charts. Session replay shows what users actually did, feature flags and experiments test changes, and lexicon tools manage the data schema. AI features let people ask questions in plain language and generate reports, summarize findings, and surface anomalies. Data can be connected from warehouses, and Mixpanel integrates with tools for marketing, support, and customer data pipelines.

Mixpanel has a free plan for smaller volumes and paid plans based on events or monthly tracked users. Good analytics depends on clean instrumentation, and poorly named events produce confusing reports. Amplitude is its closest competitor, PostHog is open source and bundles analytics with replays and flags, Heap captures events automatically, and Google Analytics serves general web measurement. Mixpanel suits teams that want fast, event-based analysis of how people use their product.
