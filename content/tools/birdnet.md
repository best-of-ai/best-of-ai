Users record a bird, and the model suggests species with confidence levels. Researchers analyze long recordings from field recorders. The project comes from the Cornell Lab of Ornithology and Chemnitz University of Technology.

Accuracy depends on recording quality and local species lists.

Merlin Bird ID also identifies birds by sound and photo. BirdNET suits wildlife photographers and biologists.
