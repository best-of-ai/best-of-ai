PandaDoc helps sales teams close deals faster. Users build proposals and quotes from templates in a drag-and-drop editor, add pricing tables with live calculations, embed videos and payment links, and send them to clients, who can review and sign electronically. Tracking shows when a document is opened and viewed, so reps know when to follow up.

Automation and integration make it practical for teams. It connects with CRMs like Salesforce and HubSpot to populate documents with deal data and update records when documents are signed, offers approval workflows, content libraries, and a payment step. AI features help generate document content from a prompt, summarize contracts, suggest edits, and extract information, speeding up proposal creation.

PandaDoc has a free e-signature plan with limits and paid plans for templates, workflows, and integrations, priced per user. It competes with DocuSign, which is stronger in enterprise signature, and with Proposify, Qwilr, and Better Proposals for proposals. AI-generated contract text should be reviewed by a legal professional. PandaDoc suits small and mid-sized sales teams that send many proposals and contracts.
