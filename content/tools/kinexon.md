Kinexon is a real-time location system. Small sensors worn by athletes or attached to a ball or a piece of equipment communicate with fixed anchors around a venue, and the system calculates positions many times per second. Teams use the data to measure distance, speed, accelerations, and tactical positioning.

In sports, coaches and sports scientists monitor workload to manage fatigue and reduce injury risk, and analysts study movement patterns. The company has been used in leagues for handball, basketball, hockey, football, and others. Broadcasters use the tracking data for graphics.

The same technology serves industry. Kinexon offers tracking in factories and warehouses for locating assets, improving processes, and supporting safety. It is sold to teams, leagues, and enterprises with installation. Alternatives include Catapult, STATSports, Zebra (formerly used by the NFL), Second Spectrum, and Hawk-Eye. Kinexon suits organizations that need precise indoor tracking.
