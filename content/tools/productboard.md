Productboard is a product management platform. Feedback from many sources flows into a central place, where it is linked to features and customers. Product managers see which problems come up most and who is asking, which grounds roadmap decisions in evidence.

AI helps process the volume of input. It groups similar requests, summarizes calls and tickets, and drafts specs and release notes. Prioritization frameworks score ideas against objectives, and roadmaps can be shared with stakeholders at different levels of detail.

Productboard has a free tier for small use and paid plans by makers. It overlaps with tools such as Jira Product Discovery and Aha!. Alternatives include Aha!, Airfocus, Canny, Linear, and Notion. Productboard suits product teams managing customer input.
