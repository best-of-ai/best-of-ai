Lexion helped legal teams make sense of their contracts. It read agreements, pulled out terms like parties, dates, renewal windows, and liability caps, and stored them in a searchable repository. Alerts warned teams about upcoming renewals and obligations, and workflows routed new requests.

AI answered questions across the portfolio, such as which contracts have auto-renewal or a particular clause. The company was built for fast-growing technology companies that wanted contract management without a heavy implementation.

DocuSign acquired Lexion and integrated its AI and repository into its Intelligent Agreement Management offerings, so new customers typically encounter it under the DocuSign name. Alternatives include Ironclad, Evisort (Workday), LinkSquares, Juro, and Icertis. Lexion suits legal teams that want AI to organize contract data, now through DocuSign.
