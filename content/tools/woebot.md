Woebot was one of the best-known mental health chatbots. It talked with users about mood and thoughts, using techniques from cognitive behavioral therapy to help them notice patterns, challenge unhelpful beliefs, and practice coping skills. Conversations were short and structured, with a friendly tone, so people could use it when they felt stressed or anxious, at any time of day.

It differed from general chatbots by using carefully written, clinically reviewed content instead of free-form generation, and the company ran studies to evaluate it. It aimed to make support more accessible and affordable than traditional therapy, and was used by individuals, employers, and health systems. It was always described as a supplement to, not a replacement for, professional care.

Woebot Health shut down its consumer app, saying that meeting the regulatory expectations for such a product, including from the FDA, was too costly. The retirement highlighted the challenges of offering mental health tools in a regulated environment, especially as general AI chatbots grew popular. People looking for support can consider licensed therapists, services like BetterHelp or Talkspace, or apps with structured exercises such as Wysa, Youper, and Headspace. In crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line. This entry stays for reference.
