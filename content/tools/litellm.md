LiteLLM solves the problem of integrating many model providers. Developers write code once against the OpenAI format, and LiteLLM translates the call to the provider they choose. Switching models then means changing a name, not rewriting code.

The proxy server adds features for teams: a gateway with virtual keys, budgets and rate limits per team, logging, load balancing across deployments, and automatic fallback when a provider fails. Platform teams use it to give internal users a controlled path to models and to track costs.

LiteLLM is free and open source, with a paid enterprise version that adds features such as single sign-on and audit logs. Running a gateway means taking on its operation and security. Alternatives include OpenRouter, Portkey, Cloudflare AI Gateway, Helicone, and Kong AI Gateway. LiteLLM suits developers and platform teams managing multiple model providers.
