Rev started as a human transcription marketplace and added automatic transcription as speech recognition improved. Users upload audio or video, and receive a transcript within minutes from the AI service, or within hours from human transcribers who deliver higher accuracy. The choice lets you trade cost and speed for precision, which matters for legal proceedings, journalism, and broadcast captions.

Beyond transcripts, Rev offers captions and subtitles in many languages, translation, and a web editor for correcting and exporting text. Meeting tools record calls and produce notes, and an API supports developers who want to add transcription to products. Accessibility compliance is a big use, because captions help meet legal requirements for education and media.

Rev charges per minute for human services and by subscription for AI features, with a free trial. Automatic transcription struggles with heavy accents, crosstalk, and poor audio, so critical work still benefits from human review. Competitors include Otter, Happy Scribe, Sonix, Descript, Trint, and Verbit, and Whisper is a free option for technical users. Rev suits people who need reliable transcripts with the option of human accuracy.
