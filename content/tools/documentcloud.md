DocumentCloud is a platform built for newsrooms. Reporters upload documents such as court filings, government reports, and leaked files, and the system makes them searchable through optical character recognition. They can annotate passages, add notes, and publish documents so readers can see the original source behind a story.

For large collections, the tool helps with analysis. Entity extraction finds people, places, and organizations mentioned, and search across projects lets teams collaborate on investigations. Add-ons extend the system with automated tasks, including redaction, translation, and data extraction.

DocumentCloud is operated by MuckRock, with the support of journalism organizations, and is free for eligible users. Sensitive documents require care with sharing settings. Alternatives include Pinpoint from Google, Aleph by OCCRP, Relativity for legal work, and general OCR tools. DocumentCloud suits journalists who work with large document sets.
