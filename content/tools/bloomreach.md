Bloomreach serves online retailers. Its discovery product powers site search, category pages, and recommendations, using machine learning to understand what shoppers mean, rank products, and personalize results. Merchandisers can set rules and see how changes affect sales.

The platform also includes engagement tools. A customer data platform unifies behavior across channels, and marketing automation sends email, SMS, and push messages triggered by actions. Content management supports headless sites. AI assistants help marketers build campaigns and analyze performance.

Bloomreach is sold on enterprise contracts. It is a broad suite and takes work to implement, and some retailers prefer assembling best-of-breed tools. Competitors include Algolia, Coveo, Klaviyo, Braze, and Dynamic Yield. Bloomreach suits mid-sized and large e-commerce brands.
