Slashspace rethinks chatting with AI as a workspace instead of a single scrolling thread. Conversations live on a canvas where users branch ideas, keep multiple threads open, and combine sources, so research does not get buried in a long chat history.

The app supports several models, lets users bring in documents and web pages, and keeps context organized. Writers and analysts use it to explore a topic from different angles and compare lines of reasoning.

Independent information is limited. Slashspace has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Heptabase, Obsidian with AI plugins, Claude Projects, NotebookLM, and Kosmik. Slashspace suits people who do exploratory research with AI.
