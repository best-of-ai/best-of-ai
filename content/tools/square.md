Square is known for its card readers and registers. A small business accepts payments in person, online, or by invoice, tracks inventory, and manages staff and payroll from a single account. Hardware ranges from a simple reader to full countertop systems.

Additional services include appointments, loyalty programs, marketing, and small business loans based on sales. AI assistance writes item descriptions, generates insights from sales data, and answers business questions.

Square charges processing fees per transaction and sells hardware, with paid software tiers for advanced features. It has a free plan for basic use. Alternatives include Toast for restaurants, Clover, Shopify POS, Stripe Terminal, and SumUp. Square suits small retail and food businesses.
