Watershed focuses on carbon accounting for large companies. Organizations connect data from finance systems, energy bills, travel, and suppliers, and the platform converts that activity data into greenhouse gas emissions across all scopes, including the harder-to-measure supply chain. The methods follow recognized standards and are designed to stand up to audits, which matters as more companies face disclosure rules.

Beyond measurement, the platform supports action. Companies set targets, model reduction scenarios, and track progress, and tools for engaging suppliers help gather better data and cut emissions in the value chain. Reporting features produce disclosures for frameworks and regulations, and AI helps classify purchases, fill gaps in data, and prepare reports, which reduces the manual effort that carbon accounting requires.

Watershed is sold to enterprises on custom contracts, so it's not aimed at small businesses, and implementation involves data work across the organization. Results depend on the quality of the underlying data and the emission factors used. Competitors include Persefoni, Sweep, Normative, Salesforce Net Zero Cloud, and Microsoft Sustainability Manager, with consultancies like the Carbon Trust offering advisory services. Watershed suits large organizations preparing credible climate disclosures.
