Qualtrics is an experience management platform. Organizations use it to run customer satisfaction surveys, product research, brand studies, and employee engagement programs. A powerful survey builder handles complex logic, and results flow into dashboards segmented by customer, team, or time.

AI features turn feedback into action. They analyze comments for sentiment and themes, spot issues as they emerge, predict which customers may leave, and suggest next steps. The platform also pulls in data from support calls, reviews, and social media.

Qualtrics is sold on enterprise subscriptions with modules, and universities use it widely for research. It is more costly and complex than simple survey tools. Alternatives include SurveyMonkey, Medallia, Typeform, Alchemer, and Momentive. Qualtrics suits large organizations that run formal feedback programs.
