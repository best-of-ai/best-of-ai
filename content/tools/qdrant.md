Qdrant stores vectors, the numerical representations of text, images, or other data, and finds the closest matches quickly. Applications use it for semantic search, recommendations, and retrieval-augmented generation. Metadata filters narrow results, such as by date or category.

It is built in Rust for speed and efficiency, supports quantization to reduce memory, and scales across nodes. Hybrid search combines vector similarity with keyword-style sparse vectors. It can be self-hosted or used in the managed Qdrant Cloud.

Qdrant is free and open source, with paid cloud plans. Alternatives include Pinecone, Milvus, Weaviate, Chroma, and pgvector. Qdrant suits developers who want a fast, flexible vector database.
