Talkwalker monitors the public conversation about brands and topics. Users set up searches, and the platform collects posts, articles, forums, and broadcast mentions from around the world, then analyzes volume, sentiment, and who is talking. Alerts flag spikes that may signal a crisis or opportunity.

Image and video recognition detect logos in pictures even when the brand name is not written, and AI summarizes themes and trends. Marketing and communications teams use the findings to measure campaigns, track competitors, and shape messaging, and a generative assistant helps analysts query the data.

Hootsuite acquired Talkwalker to combine social listening with publishing and analytics. It is sold on enterprise subscriptions. Alternatives include Brandwatch, Meltwater, Sprinklr, Sprout Social listening, and Mention. Talkwalker suits communications and insights teams monitoring brand conversations.
