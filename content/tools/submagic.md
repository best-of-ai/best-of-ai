Submagic speeds up editing of talking-head clips. A creator uploads a video, the tool transcribes speech, adds styled captions that highlight words as they are spoken, and suggests emojis, sound effects, and B-roll to keep viewers engaged.

Templates provide the caption looks seen on popular social videos, and users can edit text, timing, and style. Tools for trimming silence and magic clips from longer videos add to the toolkit. Multiple languages are supported.

Submagic has a free trial and paid plans. Videos edited with popular templates may look alike. Alternatives include CapCut, Opus Clip, Captions, VEED, and Descript. Submagic suits short-form creators.
