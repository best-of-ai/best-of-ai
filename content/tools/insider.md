Insider helps brands treat each visitor differently. It collects behavior data across channels, builds unified profiles, and uses them to personalize websites, app experiences, and messages. A marketer can design a journey that reaches a customer by email, then push, then SMS depending on what they do.

The AI layer predicts what people are likely to buy or whether they may churn, recommends products, and optimizes timing and content. The platform also includes tools for search, recommendations, and conversational commerce. It is used heavily by retailers and consumer brands in many regions.

Insider is sold on enterprise pricing. Implementation takes work to integrate data sources. Alternatives include Braze, Klaviyo, Bloomreach, MoEngage, and Salesforce Marketing Cloud. Insider suits retail and consumer brands that want a unified personalization platform.
