Takeorder AI is a specialized voice-first technology company that transforms restaurant operations through intelligent conversational AI.

Built exclusively for the food service industry, Takeorder AI functions as a 24/7 voice assistant that handles phone calls, takes orders, manages reservations, and responds to customer inquiries with human-like conversation skills.

Core Mission

To empower restaurants with always-on, intelligent voice AI that delivers fast, reliable, and human-like service without the overhead of additional staff.

Main Solutions

1. Phone AI
2. Drive-thru AI
3. Kiosk AI
4. Pizza AI

Key Features & Capabilities:

- 24/7 Automated Operations- Works continuously, including after-hours, weekends, and holidays, to capture every potential order and booking
- Advanced Speech Recognition - Accurately processes orders even in noisy restaurant environments with 99% accuracy rates
- Seamless POS Integration - Connects directly with major systems like Toast, Clover, and Revel for real-time order processing
- Multi-Solution Platform - Supports Phone AI, Drive-Thru AI, Kiosk AI, and specialized Pizza AI for comprehensive coverage
- Intelligent Call Routing- Handles complex inquiries, transfers to human staff when needed, and maintains zero hold times
- Customizable Voice & Branding - AI voice adapts to match your restaurant's tone and brand personality

Business Impact

- Eliminates missed calls and lost orders during busy periods
- Reduces staff burnout from constant phone management
- Increases revenue by up to 30% through consistent availability
- Delivers consistent, high-quality customer service
- Enables effortless scaling without proportional staff increases

Target Industries: Quick Service Restaurants (QSR), drive-thrus, pizzerias, cafés, coffee shops, ghost kitchens, full-service restaurants, and franchise chains.

Transform your restaurant's phone operations with AI that learns, adapts, and delivers exceptional customer service 24/7.
