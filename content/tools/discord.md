Community managers set roles, channels, and rules, and use bots for moderation and welcome messages. Voice channels and events support live sessions. AutoMod features filter spam and harmful content.

Free to use, with optional paid subscriptions. It works best for real-time chat, and information can get buried.

Slack and Circle are alternatives. Discord suits community managers running active chat communities.
