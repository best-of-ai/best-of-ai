Voicemod changes how you sound as you speak. You choose a voice effect, such as a robot, a deep voice, or a character, and the app processes your microphone audio in real time. It works as a virtual microphone, so apps like Discord, Zoom, OBS, and many games treat it like a normal mic. Gamers and streamers use it for fun and for roleplay, and some people use it for privacy.

The product has grown with AI. Newer AI voices and a voice cloning feature create more natural and varied options, and a soundboard plays effects and memes with hotkeys. A voice lab lets you customize effects, and a text-to-speech option generates spoken lines. Integrations and a mobile app extend its reach, and a library of community voices offers more choices.

Voicemod has a free tier with a limited selection of voices and a paid plan with the full library and features, sold by subscription or a lifetime option. Voice changers can be misused for impersonation and harassment, so use them responsibly, and platforms may have rules. Quality and latency depend on your hardware. Competitors include Clownfish, MorphVOX, ElevenLabs voice changer, and Krisp for noise removal rather than effects. Voicemod suits gamers and streamers.
