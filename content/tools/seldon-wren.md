Wren is the software QA product from Seldon, an applied research lab that builds models of how people reason and decide. You define tests in plain English, and agents explore the paths through your app, including ones a scripted test would never take. Each finding comes with a recorded journey so you can see what happened.

It catches broken layouts, failed API calls and incorrect totals. Seldon's other products are Finch, for usability and UX research, and Rook, which is for government and defense work only. The footer lists SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA and ISO 27001.

Wren charges per completed test run after a 14-day free trial. There are no seat fees, and model usage and browser execution are included. Volume pricing is available for enterprises. Engineering and QA teams that want exploratory coverage on top of scripted tests are the fit.
