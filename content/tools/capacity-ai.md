Capacity aims to be a single assistant across several support functions. It connects to a company's knowledge sources and systems, then answers questions from employees about IT, HR, and policies, and from customers about products and orders. Automated workflows can complete actions such as resetting a password or opening a ticket.

The pitch is reducing the volume of tickets that require a person. By resolving common requests automatically and escalating the rest with context, it can free staff for harder issues. Analytics show which questions come up most and where documentation is lacking.

Capacity is sold to businesses on subscription with custom pricing. Quality depends on the content connected and on setup of workflows. Alternatives include Moveworks (part of ServiceNow), Aisera, Zendesk AI, Intercom Fin, and Atlassian Rovo. Capacity suits organizations wanting a unified support assistant.
