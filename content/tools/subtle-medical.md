Subtle Medical builds AI that sits on top of existing scanners. Its tools denoise and enhance images, which lets sites shorten MRI scans or reduce the dose in PET and contrast-enhanced studies.

Imaging centers use it to raise throughput and make exams easier on patients, without replacing their hardware.

It is sold to hospitals and imaging centers. Results depend on scanner and protocol, so sites validate before changing clinical practice. Alternatives include vendor-built reconstruction tools from scanner makers. Subtle suits imaging departments focused on speed and dose.
