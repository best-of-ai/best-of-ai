AnythingLLM gives you a private place to talk to your files. You add documents, web pages, or other content to a workspace, and the app indexes them in a vector database. When you ask a question, it retrieves relevant passages and sends them to a language model, which answers with references.

Flexibility is the main draw. You can choose a local model through Ollama or LM Studio, or a cloud model from providers such as OpenAI and Anthropic, and swap them without rebuilding your workspace. It has agents that can browse the web or run tools, supports multiple users and permissions in the server version, and offers an API and embeddable chat widget.

The desktop version is free and open source, and a hosted and enterprise option is available. Local models are less capable than the biggest cloud models, and performance depends on your hardware. Alternatives include Open WebUI, GPT4All, Msty, LibreChat, and Khoj. AnythingLLM suits individuals and teams who want private document chat.
