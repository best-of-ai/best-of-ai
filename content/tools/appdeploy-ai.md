AppDeploy fills in a gap between writing code and running it. AI coding tools can produce a working app quickly, but putting it on the internet still requires choosing a host, setting up databases, configuring domains, and managing environments. AppDeploy aims to automate those steps so a developer or non-developer can publish from their AI workflow.

The idea is similar to a hosting platform designed for the AI era. A user connects their project, and the service builds and deploys it, handling infrastructure details behind the scenes. That suits prototypes, internal tools, and side projects made with coding assistants.

Little independent information is available, so check what it supports, where your data lives, and what happens if you want to move. Established alternatives include Vercel, Netlify, Railway, Render, Fly.io, and Replit's built-in deployment. AppDeploy suits builders who want an easy publishing path for AI-made apps.
