edX was founded by Harvard and MIT as a nonprofit to put university courses online. Learners can audit many courses for free or pay for verified certificates, and the catalog includes professional certificates, bootcamps, and online degree programs from partner universities and companies.

The platform later became part of 2U, a company that builds online programs with universities. edX.org remains a major destination for learners seeking credentials, and offers courses in computer science, data, business, and many other fields. Business offerings train employees. The open-source Open edX software that powers many other learning sites is separate from edX.org.

Course pricing varies, with free auditing in some cases and paid certificates and degrees. Free access to course materials has narrowed over time, so check each course. Alternatives include Coursera, Udacity, Udemy, FutureLearn, and Khan Academy for free basics. edX suits learners seeking university-backed courses and credentials.
