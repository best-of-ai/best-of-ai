D-ID's defining feature is animating portrait photographs into speaking, expressive video. Upload any headshot — a LinkedIn profile photo, a historical photograph, an illustration — provide a script or audio file, and D-ID generates a video of that face speaking the content with natural head movement and lip sync. This works for real people's photos, generating AI presenter avatars, or even bringing historical figures to life for educational content.

The Creative Reality Studio provides a full web interface for creating talking avatar videos with background customisation, slide overlays, and branding elements. You can build a complete corporate training video or product demo entirely within the studio interface. The API allows developers to integrate avatar video generation into external applications — chatbots that respond with video, personalised video messaging platforms, and interactive digital humans in kiosks or websites.

D-ID's pricing is credit-based with plans starting at $5.9/month for 10 minutes of video and going up to $299/month for 150 minutes. API access is available for developers building video generation into products. D-ID has particular adoption in e-learning, real estate (AI agents for property videos), customer service (video avatars for FAQ responses), and personalised video marketing. Compared to Synthesia, D-ID has a lower minimum price point and more flexibility for developer use cases, while Synthesia has higher production quality and more enterprise-grade tooling.
