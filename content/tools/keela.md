Keela helps small and mid-sized nonprofits manage supporters. It keeps donor records, gifts, and communication history, processes online donations, runs email campaigns, and supports events and volunteer tracking. The platform aims to be affordable and approachable for teams without a dedicated data staff.

AI features guide outreach. Engagement scoring highlights supporters likely to give again or increase gifts, timing suggestions prompt contact when it is most effective, and a writing assistant drafts appeals and thank-you notes. Reports show campaign results and giving trends.

Keela is sold by subscription. It is lighter than enterprise donor systems. Alternatives include Bloomerang, Neon One, Little Green Light, Virtuous, and Givebutter. Keela suits small nonprofit development teams.
