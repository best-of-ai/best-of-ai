Ivo is an AI contract review tool built for in-house legal teams. A lawyer opens an incoming contract, and Ivo compares it with the team's preferred positions, flags issues, and suggests redlines in the document, using the editor the lawyer already works in.

Beyond review, Ivo has added tools to manage the whole flow. Business users submit requests, contracts are routed to the right reviewers, negotiations are tracked, and approvals are collected. A repository holds executed agreements for analysis.

Ivo is sold to legal departments on subscription. As with any legal AI, lawyers must check suggestions, and playbooks need to be well defined. Alternatives include Ironclad, Spellbook, Luminance, Harvey, and DocuSign CLM. Ivo suits in-house teams that review many contracts.
