Marqo handles vector search end to end. A developer sends documents or images to Marqo, and it generates embeddings with a chosen model, stores them, and serves similarity queries, so there is no separate pipeline for embedding and indexing. Queries can combine text and images, which suits product search.

The company has focused on e-commerce relevance, offering models trained for product search and tools to improve ranking with feedback from user behavior. It runs as a self-hosted open-source service or as a managed cloud.

Marqo has a free open-source version and paid cloud plans. Teams still need to evaluate relevance on their own data. Alternatives include Algolia, Typesense, Meilisearch, Weaviate, and Elasticsearch. Marqo suits developers building semantic and multimodal search.
