Neural.love combines creation and repair tools in one web app. The generator creates images from text prompts with a choice of models and styles, while the enhancement tools improve existing pictures. Upscaling increases resolution, denoising cleans grainy photos, and restoration repairs scratches and faded color on old family pictures. Face enhancement can sharpen portraits, and colorization adds color to black-and-white images.

The platform is practical for photographers, genealogists, designers, and anyone with old or low-quality images. Users upload a file, choose the effect, and download the result, with credits consumed by each operation. An API lets developers add enhancement features to their own apps, and batch options handle many images. Generated images can be used in marketing and design, subject to the terms for each plan.

Neural.love offers a free allowance of credits and paid credit packs or subscriptions. AI restoration invents plausible detail, which can look natural but may differ from reality, so results of historic photos should be treated as reconstructions. Alternatives include Topaz Photo AI, a desktop product, Remini and VanceAI for mobile and web enhancement, Adobe Photoshop's Neural Filters, and open-source tools like Real-ESRGAN and GFPGAN. It suits people who want quick fixes without installing software.
