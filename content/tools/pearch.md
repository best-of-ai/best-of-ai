Pearch is a free AI-powered shopping assistant that simplifies your online experience. It scans your email receipts (with permission) and builds a private shopping profile based on your actual purchases. Using that data, it surfaces smarter recommendations, showing better deals, similar items, and personalized picks as you browse your favorite retailers.

What sets Pearch apart is its privacy-first approach and intelligent matching. Your data is never sold, and you stay in control. Whether you’re deal hunting or trying to avoid endless scrolling, Pearch helps you find what fits your budget, style, and needs, without the noise.
