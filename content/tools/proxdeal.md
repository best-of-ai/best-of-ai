ProxDeal is an AI-agent-based high-performance tool designed for M&A professionals, simplifying research and buyer/target identification. ProxDeal generates comprehensive longlists of potential buyers, investors and targets and moreover supports personalized outreach efforts, saving time and ensuring precision. Tailored for sell-side and buy-side processes, ProxDeal enhances decision-making with data-driven insights.

Key Features
Longlist Generation in 2 Minutes: Leverage AI agents to perform live analysis of websites, corporate registries, and transaction data, including recent M&A activity, to generate accurate and comprehensive longlists instantly.

Transaction Insights: Our AI-powered search identifies the most relevant buyers from past deals based on content similarity to a short description of an industry or target company—no need for keywords or industry codes

Personalized Outreach: Create high-conversion emails and letters tailored to your target audience, maximizing response rates and transaction success.

Benefits
Intelligent M&A Search, Not Just Static Lists: ProxDeal delivers fully automated search intelligence that goes beyond traditional solutions. Every longlist is dynamically created from scratch within the specific search context—no pre-generated lists, no generic descriptions. ProxDeal provides tailored results that precisely match your requirements.

You Receive All the Data You Need for Outreach: With ProxDeal, you gain access to all the data required for effective and personalized outreach. The information is always up-to-date, as our AI agents individually search relevant websites and data sources in real time. Once complete, you can easily download the results as an Excel file for seamless integration into your workflow.

Use Case
Sell-Side Transactions:
ProxDeal simplifies the process of identifying potential buyers for sell-side mandates. By leveraging AI agents, the platform generates a dynamic longlist of strategic and financial buyers tailored to the target company's industry, size, and geography. With up-to-date contact and transaction data, advisors can prioritize outreach and increase the chances of finding the right buyer quickly and efficiently.

Buy-Side Transactions:
For buy-side mandates, ProxDeal helps identify acquisition targets that align with the buyer’s strategic goals. The AI-powered platform scans industries, company profiles, and past transaction activity to pinpoint relevant targets. This allows buy-side teams to save time, focus on high-quality leads, and maintain a competitive edge in sourcing attractive opportunities.

Finding New Mandates:
ProxDeal aids advisors and intermediaries in expanding their client base by identifying companies likely to need M&A support. The platform analyzes large sets of variables to identify businesses in growth, succession, or restructuring phases. This proactive approach helps advisors target and win new mandates, ensuring a steady pipeline of opportunities.
