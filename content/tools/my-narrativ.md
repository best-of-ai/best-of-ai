Narrativ is a career storytelling tool that helps professionals store and organize their experiences, and turn them into compelling stories for resumes, interviews, job transitions, and personal branding. Whether you're switching careers, applying for your first job, or going after a promotion, Narrativ helps you position yourself with clarity and confidence.

Key Features
- Career Wins Tracker: Capture and store your accomplishments all in one place so you're never scrambling when opportunities come up.
- Story Builder: Leverage AI to analyze your past experiences to identify key themes, strengths and transferable skills - then shape them into narratives for resumes, cover letters, and interviews.
- STAR Response Assistant: Craft strong, structured answers for interviews and applications. Applies to all STAR like frameworks. 
- Story Recap Rituals: Weekly, monthly or quarterly prompts to help reflect on achievements and write them down.

Benefits
- Everything in One Place: Centralize your career wins, stories, and STAR responses so you're never scrambling across docs, Dontes, or memory when opportunities arise. 
- Stronger, Clearer Positioning: Craft clearer, more compelling narratives that showcase your strengths and transferable skills.
- Accelerate your job search: Cut down prep time for resumes, cover letters, and interviews by having read-made stories tailored to different roles, so you can apply faster and with more confidence.

Use Case
- Use case 1: Applying for jobs - Use Narrativ to tailor your resume and interview stories for each role, helping you lead more interviews, faster. 
- Use case 2: Switching roles/careers - Reframe your past experiences to highlight transferable skills and confidently position yourself for a new role/industry. 
- Use case 3: Prepping for a promotion - Track accomplishments and impact over time so you're ready to showcase your value during performance reviews. 
- Use case 4: Entering the job market for the first time - Organize internship and project work, create strong STAR stories, and build confidence for your first round of applications.
