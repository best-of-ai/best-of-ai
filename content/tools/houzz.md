Houzz started as a place to browse home photos and save ideas. Users look through millions of images of kitchens, bathrooms, gardens, and living rooms, save them to idea books, and find the products and professionals behind them. A directory lists architects, designers, and contractors with reviews and portfolios, so homeowners can contact people who match their taste and budget.

The platform includes a marketplace for furniture, lighting, and home goods, with augmented reality features that show how an item might look in your room. For professionals, Houzz Pro is software for managing leads, estimates, invoices, projects, and client communication, with tools to showcase their work. AI features help with design suggestions, image search, and automating parts of the business workflow.

Houzz is free for homeowners, earning revenue from advertising, product sales, and professional subscriptions. Reviews and rankings of professionals can be influenced by paid placement, so do your own checks. Alternatives include Pinterest for inspiration, Angi and Thumbtack for finding contractors, Modsy-style design services, and Buildertrend or Jobber for contractor software. Houzz suits homeowners planning renovations and professionals looking for customers.
