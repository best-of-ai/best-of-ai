PostHog bundles the tools product teams use to understand and improve their software. After adding its SDK, you can track events, build funnels and retention charts, watch session recordings, run experiments, release features gradually with flags, collect feedback through surveys, and analyze data with SQL. Having these in one product, with a shared data set, avoids the cost and complexity of stitching several tools together.

AI features include an assistant that can answer questions about your data, write queries, create charts, and summarize insights in plain language, and tools for monitoring AI applications such as tracing model calls and costs. Because PostHog is open source, it can be self-hosted for data control, or used through PostHog Cloud, and a pipeline can send data to other destinations.

PostHog has a free tier with generous monthly allowances for each product and usage-based pricing beyond that. Its breadth means some individual features are less deep than specialized tools. Alternatives include Amplitude and Mixpanel for analytics, Hotjar and FullStory for replays, LaunchDarkly and Statsig for flags and experiments, and Google Analytics for web traffic. PostHog suits startups and engineering-led teams who want an all-in-one product toolkit.
