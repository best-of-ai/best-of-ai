MiroFish is an open-source swarm intelligence engine that builds a high-fidelity parallel digital world from real-world data — news, policy signals, financial inputs — then runs thousands of AI agents through it to simulate and predict how events will unfold. Each agent has an independent personality, long-term memory, and behavioral logic, enabling realistic modeling of social dynamics, public opinion, crisis spread, market sentiment, and more. Built for analysts, researchers, strategists, and creatives who need to rehearse decisions before making them, MiroFish turns "what if" scenarios into data-backed predictions in five automated steps.
