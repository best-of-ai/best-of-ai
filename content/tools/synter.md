Synter plugs ad accounts into AI clients such as Claude, Cursor and ChatGPT through a universal MCP server. The agent can then plan campaigns, launch them and tune them across more than 40 platforms, covering search, social, commerce, programmatic and mobile.

A human approval step and spend caps sit between the agent and the budget. Around that are an agent console, budget pacing and anomaly checks, visitor and account identification, and CRM sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio and Pipedrive. Marketing mix modeling and incrementality testing come with the Scale plan, and there is a Slack and Teams assistant.

The MCP server is open source, and the free tier covers reading data. Launching live changes needs a paid plan: Solo for a single developer, Scale for a team of five seats, and Custom for sales-led deals. Agent actions and model use are metered on top, and the ad platforms bill media spend directly. It fits B2B growth teams and agencies.
