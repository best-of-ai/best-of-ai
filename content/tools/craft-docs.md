Craft aims to make notes and documents that look good. It works as a block-based editor where text, images, tables, and cards live in nested pages that you can reorganize easily. Native apps for Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch are fast and polished, with offline support, and there is also a web and Windows version. Daily notes and tasks cover personal organization, and Spaces and collections keep larger projects tidy.

Sharing is a strong point. Any document can be published as a web page with a link, shared with editing permissions, or turned into a presentation-style layout. Craft's AI assistant is built into the editor: it can draft text, improve writing, summarize a page, translate, and answer questions based on the contents of your notes. A developer API and integrations connect it with other tools.

Craft has a free plan with limits and paid plans with more storage, AI use, and collaboration features. It is stronger on design than on deep structure, so people who want databases or heavy customization lean toward Notion or Obsidian. Apple Notes is free and simpler, Bear focuses on Markdown writing, and Notion covers team workspaces. Craft suits people who live in the Apple ecosystem and care about how their documents look.
