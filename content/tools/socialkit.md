SocialKit is a simple, unified API to extract video summaries, transcripts, and engagement metrics from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and more.

YouTube Summarizer API: Get concise summaries of YouTube and YouTube Shorts videos with key insights and main points in seconds.

YouTube Transcript API: Retrieve precise, timestamped transcripts for content analysis, accessibility, and data processing.

YouTube Stats API: Access detailed video stats including views, likes, comments, subscriber data, and engagement metrics.

Instant, Reliable Data: Get transcripts, summaries, and stats in seconds, no manual scraping or maintenance.

Developer & No-Code Friendly: Works easily with code, and with no-code tools like: Zapier, Make, and n8n for easy automation.
