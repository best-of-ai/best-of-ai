Undermind is designed for the situation where a simple search leaves you wondering what you missed. You describe your research question in plain language, including constraints and nuance, and the agent runs a deep search: it queries multiple sources, reads abstracts and text, follows citations, and refines its search based on what it finds. After some minutes, it delivers a ranked list of relevant papers with explanations of why each matches.

The tool also estimates how complete the search is, giving a sense of whether the major relevant papers have been found, which helps researchers gauge their coverage. It supports follow-up questions and refinements, and is used by academics, graduate students, and R&D teams doing literature reviews, patent-adjacent research, and technical scouting. Coverage focuses on scientific and technical fields.

Undermind has a free tier with a limited number of searches and paid plans for more. The agent's results depend on the databases it can access and the quality of the question you write, and it may miss papers behind paywalls. Elicit, Consensus, SciSpace, and Semantic Scholar offer other AI-assisted search, Google Scholar is the broad baseline, and Perplexity or ChatGPT deep research can handle general questions. Undermind suits thorough literature searches.
