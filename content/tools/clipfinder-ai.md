ClipFinder helps creators repurpose long content. A streamer or podcaster uploads or links a long recording, and the tool analyzes it for moments that stand out, such as reactions, jokes, or key points, then cuts them into short clips. This saves hours that would otherwise go to scrubbing through footage.

The clips can be formatted for vertical platforms such as TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels, often with captions. Creators review the suggestions, trim as needed, and publish. The tool is aimed at those who produce hours of material and lack an editor.

Independent information on ClipFinder is limited, so test it with a recent stream to see if it picks moments you would choose. Alternatives include OpusClip, Vizard, Descript, Munch, and Klap. ClipFinder suits creators who want help turning long streams into short clips.
