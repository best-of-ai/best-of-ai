Trados is one of the best-known tools among professional translators. When translators work on a document, Trados breaks it into segments and checks a translation memory for matches from previous projects, offering exact and fuzzy matches so work is reused and terminology stays consistent. That improves speed and quality, especially on repetitive technical content, and lowers costs for clients who pay by new words.

The product has evolved with the technology. It integrates machine translation engines, including adaptive and neural options, and supports AI-assisted features for suggestions and quality checks. Trados Enterprise is a cloud version for teams and organizations, supporting collaboration, project management, and workflows with vendors, while the desktop Studio remains popular among freelancers and agencies.

Trados is sold as a subscription or license, at a significant price, and its interface has a steep learning curve. Competitors include memoQ, Memsource now called Phrase, Smartling, Wordfast, and open-source OmegaT. The rise of large language models is changing translation workflows, with human translators increasingly reviewing machine output. Trados suits professional translators and language service companies.
