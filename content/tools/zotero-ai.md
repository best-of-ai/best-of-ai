Zotero saves references with a click. A browser connector captures a paper or web page along with its citation details and PDF, and the library organizes items in collections with tags and notes. Word and Google Docs plugins insert citations in thousands of styles.

Group libraries support collaboration, and the open ecosystem has plugins for annotation, syncing, and AI summaries and search. Because it is community driven and non-profit, users keep control over data.

Zotero is free, with paid storage plans. Alternatives include Mendeley, EndNote, Paperpile, and Papers. Zotero suits students and researchers.
