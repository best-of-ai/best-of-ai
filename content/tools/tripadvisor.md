Tripadvisor built its name on traveler reviews. Visitors rate and describe hotels, restaurants, tours, and sights, and the site ranks places based on those opinions, which people use to decide where to stay and eat. Photos, forums, and Q&A add detail. It covers millions of places worldwide and is free to browse.

The site has developed into a booking and planning platform. It compares hotel prices and links to booking partners, sells tours and activities through Viator, and supports restaurant reservations through TheFork. AI trip-planning features create itineraries from a short description, suggest places based on reviews, and organize saved items into trips. Businesses use Tripadvisor to manage their listings, respond to reviews, and advertise.

Tripadvisor is free for travelers and earns money from advertising, commissions, and business products. Reviews can be skewed by selective or fake feedback, and the company works to detect fraud, but it remains wise to read many reviews and look at patterns. Google Maps has become a major rival for restaurant and attraction reviews, Booking.com and Expedia dominate lodging, and Yelp is stronger for local restaurants in the U.S. Tripadvisor suits travelers planning trips abroad.
