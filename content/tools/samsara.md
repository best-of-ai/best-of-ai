Samsara collects data from the physical world and puts it to work. Gateways and cameras installed on vehicles, trailers, and equipment report location, engine data, driver behavior, and conditions, and the cloud platform presents it through dashboards and apps. Fleet managers use it for tracking, routing, maintenance, compliance with driver hours rules, and safety coaching.

AI dashcams detect risky events like distracted driving, tailgating, and rolling stops, and alert drivers and managers, and video evidence helps with accidents and insurance claims. The platform also covers equipment monitoring, site safety, temperature tracking for cold chains, and workflow tools such as digital forms and inspections. An AI assistant helps users analyze data and set up actions in plain language.

Samsara is sold by subscription per device, with hardware included in contracts, and it is a public company that targets mid-sized and large operations. Cost and contract terms are considerations, and driver monitoring requires clear communication and policy. Competitors include Motive, Geotab, Verizon Connect, Lytx, and Netradyne. Samsara suits companies running vehicles and equipment who want an all-in-one platform.
