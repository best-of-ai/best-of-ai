Huntr helps people stay organized during a job hunt. Users save job postings with a browser extension, move them across stages as they apply and interview, and attach notes, contacts, and documents to each one. Reminders prompt follow-ups so opportunities do not slip.

AI features assist with the writing. They can tailor a resume and cover letter to a posting, suggest keywords, and help prepare for interviews. A resume builder and job board aggregator add to the toolset, and career coaches use Huntr to track clients.

Huntr has a free plan and paid tiers. AI-tailored documents should be reviewed so they stay truthful. Alternatives include Teal, Simplify, Notion templates, Trello, and spreadsheets. Huntr suits job seekers who want a structured process.
