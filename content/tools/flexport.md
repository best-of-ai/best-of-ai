Flexport is a freight forwarder with a software platform. Companies that import and export goods book shipments, track containers, manage documents, and handle customs through a single interface, instead of coordinating by email with many parties. It also offers financing, warehousing, and trade services.

Technology handles repetitive work. AI reads shipping documents, checks customs data for errors, and predicts delays based on port, carrier, and weather information. An assistant answers questions about shipment status and helps with classification of goods. Data from many shipments feeds analytics on cost and performance.

Flexport is sold to businesses, with costs based on freight services and software features. It operates within a volatile industry affected by trade policy and carrier capacity. Alternatives include Freightos, Kuehne+Nagel, DHL Global Forwarding, Expeditors, and Forto. Flexport suits importers and exporters wanting visibility and control.
