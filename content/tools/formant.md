Formant solves operational problems that arise once robots leave the lab. Companies running fleets of mobile robots, drones, or arms need to know what each machine is doing, diagnose problems, and sometimes take control. Formant collects telemetry, video, and sensor data from robots, shows it in dashboards, and raises alerts when something goes wrong, working with robots running ROS or other software.

Teleoperation is a key feature: an operator can view a robot's cameras and drive or guide it remotely, which helps with situations where autonomy fails. Teams can capture and tag data to improve machine learning models, schedule and analyze missions, and manage software updates across a fleet. Integrations connect with cloud services and business systems, and AI tools help find anomalies.

Formant is sold to robotics companies and operators on custom contracts. It's a specialized platform for organizations with robots in the field, not an individual tool. Competitors include Foxglove for visualization and data, InOrbit for fleet management, Freedom Robotics, and in-house tools built on open-source software. Formant suits robotics teams scaling from prototypes to deployments.
