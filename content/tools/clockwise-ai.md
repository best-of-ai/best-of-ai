Clockwise worked inside Google Calendar and Slack to protect time for deep work. It looked at a team's calendars, moved flexible meetings, and combined scattered gaps into longer blocks, aiming to cut the constant switching between tasks that fragments a workday. It could also find meeting times that worked for groups.

The product grew a following among engineering and product teams, and added AI scheduling features and a way to plan work around priorities. The company ended the service and the team joined Salesforce, where they work on AI agents. The product itself did not move over.

People who relied on Clockwise should look to other tools for similar goals. Alternatives include Reclaim, Motion, Calendly and Cal.com for scheduling, Google Calendar's own focus time features, and Sunsama for daily planning. Clockwise is listed here for reference and is no longer available.
