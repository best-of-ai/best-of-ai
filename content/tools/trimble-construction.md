Trimble is a major technology supplier to the construction industry. Its portfolio includes software for estimating costs, scheduling, managing projects and finances, coordinating building models, and running equipment. SketchUp and Tekla, used for design and structural detailing, are part of the group, and Trimble Connect provides a collaboration space for project data.

Its hardware is a distinctive strength. Survey instruments, GPS and GNSS receivers, and machine control systems guide excavators and other equipment based on a 3D model, linking digital plans to work on the ground. Integrating these systems with software lets data flow from the model to the field and back as built information. AI features assist with planning, quantity takeoff, and analyzing project data.

Trimble sells through subscriptions and equipment purchases, typically to contractors, engineers, and owners, and its tools suit mid-sized and large projects. Integration across its products offers value but requires investment. Competitors include Autodesk Construction Cloud (Forma), Procore, Bentley, Hexagon, and Topcon. Trimble suits construction firms wanting a connected set of software and field technology.
