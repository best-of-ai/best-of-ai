You record yourself doing a recurring accounting task, such as a bank reconciliation, while you narrate it. Latch turns the recording into a sidekick that repeats the task in the tools you already use. Month-end close and reconciliations are the stated focus.

Each step can be reviewed and tested. When the task needs a judgment call, the sidekick marks it "Leave for review" instead of guessing, and multi-factor prompts pause the run. Formulas are preserved in workbooks. Team plans add audit log export, and role-based access arrives on higher tiers. Latch says it is SOC 2 compliant.

Teaching a sidekick is free, and you pay when one runs, using monthly credits. The Open plan is free for one user, there are Solo, Power and Team plans above it, and enterprise terms are custom. It is built for accountants, bookkeepers and finance teams.
