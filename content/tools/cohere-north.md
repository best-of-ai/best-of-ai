North is Cohere's platform for putting agents to work inside companies. Employees use a chat interface and can build agents without code for tasks in HR, finance, IT, and other areas, connecting them to the apps and documents they already use. Search across company data is powered by Cohere's own retrieval technology.

Security and control are the main pitch. Organizations can deploy North in a private cloud, on premises, or in air-gapped settings, which appeals to banks, governments, and defense contractors with strict data rules. Cohere designs the models for enterprise use and works with customers on custom solutions.

North is sold on enterprise contracts. Compared with consumer assistants, it is meant for organizations that need governance and private deployment. Competitors include Microsoft Copilot Studio, Google Agentspace and Gemini Enterprise, Salesforce Agentforce, Glean, and ChatGPT Enterprise. Cohere North suits large organizations with strict data requirements.
