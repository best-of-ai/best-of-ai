Aquant is a service intelligence platform. Equipment makers and service organizations hold years of repair history, manuals, and technician notes, which are hard to search in the moment. Aquant uses AI to learn from that data and recommend probable causes and fixes for a given symptom.

For a technician on a job, this works like an expert on call. They describe the problem or enter an error code, and the system suggests what to check, which parts are likely needed, and what has worked on similar cases. Service managers use the same data to see recurring failures and to reduce repeat visits and unnecessary parts.

Aquant is sold to businesses on custom contracts. Results depend on the volume and quality of service data, and recommendations still need the judgment of an experienced technician. Alternatives include ServiceMax, Salesforce Field Service, Oracle Field Service, and Augury for machine health. Aquant suits manufacturers and service teams with large amounts of repair history.
