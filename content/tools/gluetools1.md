Glue.tools is the AI-powered platform that eliminates the chaos of product documentation and replaces it with clarity. Teams often waste most of their time writing, revising, and debating requirements instead of focusing on strategy. Glue.tools fixes this by assigning nine specialized AI agents to the task, each handling a different role from strategist to prototyper. These agents collaborate in real time to produce comprehensive PRDs that engineers can immediately act on. Exporting requirements to Jira, Linear, or Figma keeps the entire product lifecycle connected. The system reduces revisions and catches edge cases that even senior architects might miss. With Glue.tools, teams ship smarter, faster, and with confidence.
