DataRobot pioneered automated machine learning. A data team uploads a dataset, and the platform tries many algorithms and feature combinations, ranks them, and explains the best models, which lowers the barrier to building predictive models for tasks such as churn, demand, and risk. It also handles deployment and monitoring, tracking model accuracy and drift once models are live.

The company has pivoted toward generative AI and agents. Its platform now helps companies build and run AI agents and applications, with governance features such as documentation, bias checks, approvals, and audit trails that matter in industries such as banking, insurance, and healthcare. It can run in the cloud or on a company's own infrastructure and integrates with major cloud providers and data platforms.

DataRobot is sold to enterprises on custom contracts, with pricing that is not published and can be substantial. As open-source tools and cloud-native services have improved, some teams build equivalent pipelines themselves. Competitors include Databricks, Dataiku, H2O.ai, Amazon SageMaker, Google Vertex AI, and Azure Machine Learning. DataRobot suits large organizations that want governed, repeatable AI deployment.
