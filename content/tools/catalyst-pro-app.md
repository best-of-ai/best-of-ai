Catalyst is your AI-powered co-founder, built to help you think, test, and refine like a seasoned entrepreneur. Simply describe your business idea in plain language, and Catalyst will guide you through a structured stress test with an expert AI panel representing real-world investors, strategists, and industry specialists.
You’ll receive investor-style feedback highlighting strengths, risks, and blind spots, along with a viability score to benchmark your concept. Catalyst then helps you refine your idea step by step, from positioning and audience clarity to revenue models and differentiation, until it’s pitch-ready.
When you’re done, export a full report that includes insights, recommendations, and action steps to turn your concept into a tangible venture. Whether you’re brainstorming your next startup or validating a new product, Catalyst gives you the clarity, confidence, and direction you need to build smarter and faster.
