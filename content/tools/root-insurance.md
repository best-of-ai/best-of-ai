Root built its pitch on the idea that safe drivers should pay less. New customers take a test drive period with the app, which uses the phone's sensors to measure things such as braking, speed, turning, and time of day driving. Combined with other information, those readings help set the price, so a careful driver can get a lower rate than under traditional pricing based mainly on demographics.

The process is online. People get a quote, buy a policy, and manage their coverage in the app, including proof of insurance and claims. Root also sells through partners, embedding insurance into other companies' purchasing flows, and has added products such as renters insurance. Machine learning supports pricing, fraud detection, and claims handling.

Root is a public company and a licensed insurer, with availability limited to certain states. Telematics-based pricing is a trade-off: it can reward good driving, but it involves sharing detailed location and movement data, and the test drive may result in a quote that is higher than expected. Competitors include Progressive's Snapshot, State Farm's Drive Safe & Save, Lemonade, and Metromile, which became part of Lemonade. Compare quotes and read the privacy terms before enrolling.
