Steve AI is a text-to-video tool aimed at people who need videos without filming. You paste a script, an article, or a prompt, and the platform creates a video with scenes, characters or stock footage, text, voiceover, and music, which you can edit. It offers animated styles and live-action style videos built from stock clips.

It supports many typical use cases. Marketers create explainers and promos, educators make lesson videos, and social media managers turn blog posts into short clips. Options include different aspect ratios, branding, and a library of characters and backgrounds. Voiceover and avatar features add narration in various languages.

Steve AI has a free tier with a limited number of videos and watermarks, and paid plans with more credits and higher resolution. Videos built from templates and stock assets can look generic, so editing is needed to match a brand. Alternatives include InVideo, Pictory, Fliki, Raw Shorts, Animaker, Powtoon, and Canva. Steve AI suits small teams that want fast videos from existing text.
