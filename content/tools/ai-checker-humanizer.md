You paste a draft and the tool marks the sentences that look machine-written, then rewrites those instead of the whole text. You pick a tone: Standard, Academic, Casual or Concise. A detection score shows before and after the rewrite, so you can see what changed. It works in English, Spanish, Portuguese and French.

Detection scores are estimates, and different detectors disagree about the same text. A lower score does not mean a text will pass a particular checker, and a rewrite can shift the meaning, so read the output before you use it. Passing AI-written work off as your own breaks the rules at many schools and workplaces.

There is a free trial, and paid plans after it. Alternatives include HumanizerFree, QuillBot, GPTZero, and Originality.ai. AI Checker Humanizer suits writers who want to check a draft and fix flagged sentences in one place.
