Veo 3 produces short video clips from text or image prompts with sound included. Earlier video models were silent and required separate audio work; Veo 3 creates speech, effects, and music that match the action, which makes clips feel more complete.

It is available in Google's Gemini app, in Flow, a filmmaking tool, and through APIs for developers. Filmmakers and marketers use it for concept videos, ads, and social content, and it is known for realistic physics and prompt following.

Access is tied to paid Google AI plans or API usage, with clip length limits. Generated video carries a watermark identifying it as AI made. Alternatives include Runway, Kling, Luma, OpenAI's Sora (now shut down), and Pika. Veo 3 suits creators wanting video with built-in audio.
