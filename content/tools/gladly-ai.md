Gladly organizes support around the customer, not the ticket. Instead of separate cases for each channel, all interactions with a person appear in a single timeline, so an agent sees the entire history when a customer reaches out. That makes support feel more personal and reduces repeating information.

Sidekick is the AI part. It answers common questions, completes tasks like order lookups, and hands off to a human with context when needed. Assistive features summarize history and suggest replies. Gladly is used by consumer brands that care about the quality of service.

Gladly is sold on subscription with custom pricing. It targets mid-sized and larger consumer companies. Alternatives include Zendesk, Intercom, Kustomer, Gorgias for e-commerce, and Salesforce Service Cloud. Gladly suits brands that want a relationship-based support model.
