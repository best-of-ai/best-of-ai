Moonvalley builds generative video for creative professionals. Its Marey model is trained on licensed and rights-cleared footage, which the company positions as a safer choice for studios concerned about copyright. Users generate clips and have controls over camera movement, motion, and style.

Filmmakers use it to visualize scenes, build storyboards, and test ideas before expensive shoots, and agencies use it for concept work. The company works with filmmakers and studios, aiming at professional workflows more than casual social content.

Moonvalley has a free tier and paid plans, and access has varied as the product developed. Generated video has limits in length and consistency. Alternatives include Runway, Google Veo, Luma, Kling, and Adobe Firefly Video. Moonvalley suits filmmakers who want video generation with clear data provenance.
