Pauta runs on your own computer. It detects meetings or starts when you tell it to, captures microphone and system audio, and transcribes with speaker recognition that learns voices over time. No bot joins the call, and you don't need an account.

Summaries of decisions and tasks come from a small local Qwen model by default, or from your own Claude or ChatGPT API key. Projects group recordings so you can ask questions across them, and notes can sync to Obsidian. A menu-bar calendar is built in. It runs on macOS and Windows, and iOS and Android are on a waitlist.

There is a seven-day free trial with no card, then a one-time lifetime license instead of a subscription, good for two activations. The site opens in Portuguese by default, with an English version available. Professionals, students and anyone who wants private transcription are the audience.
