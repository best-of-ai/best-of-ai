Lusha helps sales and recruiting teams get in touch with prospects. While viewing a profile on LinkedIn or a company website, a user clicks the Lusha extension to reveal contact information, then saves it to a CRM. A database search finds contacts by role, company size, and industry.

Additional features include intent signals, technology data, and enrichment of existing lists, as well as integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, and sales engagement tools. The company emphasizes compliance with privacy laws and offers ways for people to opt out.

Lusha has a free plan with a small credit allowance and paid plans by credits and features. Data accuracy varies, and outreach must follow anti-spam and privacy rules. Alternatives include Cognism, Apollo, ZoomInfo, Kaspr, and Seamless.AI. Lusha suits sales teams and recruiters building prospect lists.
