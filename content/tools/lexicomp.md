Users search a drug and see dosing by age and condition, interaction severity, and patient counseling points. Content is reviewed by pharmacists and updated often. It is available as an app and inside electronic health records.

Subscriptions go to individuals and institutions.

Micromedex and Medscape are alternatives. Lexicomp suits pharmacists and pharmacy technicians.
