Researchers build forms, schedule events for longitudinal studies, and capture data with audit trails. It supports surveys, branching logic, and data exports to statistical software. Institutions host it themselves.

It is free to member institutions of the REDCap consortium.

Castor and Medidata Rave are alternatives. REDCap suits clinical researchers and data managers.
