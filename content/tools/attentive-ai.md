Attentive helps brands market over text message. Shoppers opt in through signup forms and popups, and the brand sends campaigns, product alerts, and automated messages such as abandoned cart reminders and back-in-stock notices. Messages can include images and links, and customers can reply to start a conversation.

AI plays a part in targeting and content. The platform chooses send times, predicts which offers each subscriber might respond to, and helps write messages in a brand's voice. Integrations with Shopify, Klaviyo, and other tools bring in purchase data, and analytics tie messages to revenue. A service team often helps brands set up programs.

Attentive is sold on custom pricing based on subscriber count and message volume. SMS marketing is tightly regulated, so brands must collect consent and follow carrier rules. Alternatives include Klaviyo, Postscript, Omnisend, Iterable, and Braze. Attentive suits mid-sized and large consumer brands that rely on text messaging.
