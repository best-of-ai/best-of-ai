Technicians and service advisors build estimates from labor guides and parts catalogs, send them to customers for approval, and track the job through completion. Digital vehicle inspections with photos help explain recommended work. Text messaging keeps customers informed.

Shops use it to replace paper tickets and spreadsheets. Parts ordering and accounting integrations vary by plan.

Tekmetric and Shop-Ware are close alternatives. Shopmonkey suits independent repair shops.
