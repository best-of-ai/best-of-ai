AutoGrid builds software for the changing electricity grid. As more homes and businesses add solar panels, batteries, and electric vehicles, utilities need ways to coordinate them. AutoGrid's platform forecasts supply and demand, controls devices, and combines many small resources into virtual power plants that can respond when the grid needs help. Machine learning predicts energy use and optimizes when to charge, discharge, or reduce load.

Utilities and energy retailers use it to run demand response programs, manage commercial and residential batteries, and monetize flexibility in energy markets. It integrates with device makers and energy management systems, so a single platform can orchestrate thermostats, batteries, EV chargers, and industrial loads. The goal is to lower costs and reduce the need for new power plants during peak demand.

AutoGrid was acquired by Uplight, which combines its flexibility management with Uplight's customer engagement software for utilities. Sales are enterprise-level, with custom contracts and long deployments. Competitors in distributed energy management include Voltus, Enel X, Generac Grid Services, and Camus Energy, and large vendors such as Schneider Electric and Siemens provide related grid software. It is not a consumer product and is only relevant for utilities and energy companies.
