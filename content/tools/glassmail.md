Glassmail connects straight from your Mac to your mail provider, with no Glassmail server in between. It works with Gmail, iCloud, Outlook, Yahoo, Fastmail and any IMAP account. The inbox splits into People and Updates, and there is a one-key triage mode, send later, undo send, snooze and one-click unsubscribe.

Tracking pixels are stripped, suspicious messages get scam warnings, and mail works offline. The AI features, which cover summaries, search and drafts, run on the Mac itself, using Apple Intelligence on recent macOS or a downloadable Gemma or Qwen model. Claude, Codex and Cursor can search and read your mail, and each change needs your approval.

A 14-day free trial includes every feature with no card. After that you buy a one-time license that covers up to three Macs, with no subscription, and the launch price rises as more people buy. An iPhone app is on the way. It is a personal mail client, not one built for shared inboxes.
