Mercury provides online banking designed for startups. A founder can open a business account in minutes, get checking and savings, send and receive wires and ACH transfers, issue virtual and physical debit and credit cards, and pay bills, all in a clean web and mobile interface. Mercury is a financial technology company, not a bank itself, and works with partner banks to hold deposits and provide FDIC insurance through sweep arrangements.

Features cater to growing companies. Teams can set up permissions, approvals, and multiple users, connect with accounting software such as QuickBooks and Xero, and manage treasury, venture debt, and international payments. AI features read invoices and bills, match receipts to transactions, categorize expenses, and help with bookkeeping questions, reducing manual finance work.

Mercury's core banking has no monthly fees for most accounts, and the company earns money from interchange and balances, with paid tiers for advanced features. Because it relies on partner banks, businesses should understand how deposit insurance and holds work, and some businesses have been declined or lost accounts after compliance reviews. Alternatives include Brex, Ramp, Relay, Novo, Chase and other traditional banks, and Wise for international payments. Mercury suits startups and online businesses.
