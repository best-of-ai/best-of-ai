Securiti addresses the problem that companies often do not know where their sensitive data lives. It scans cloud storage, databases, SaaS apps, and data pipelines, classifies personal and confidential information, and builds a map of where it sits and who can access it. This approach is known as data security posture management.

On top of that, it automates privacy and governance tasks. Teams respond to data subject requests, manage consent, run privacy assessments, and enforce policies that block risky access. For AI, Securiti helps discover models and data feeding them, apply guardrails, and meet emerging regulations, which has become a priority as companies adopt generative AI.

Veeam, a data resilience company, completed the acquisition of Securiti to combine backup and recovery with data security and governance. The product is sold on enterprise contracts. Competitors include BigID, OneTrust, Varonis, Cyera, and Transcend. Securiti suits large organizations managing data risk and AI governance.
