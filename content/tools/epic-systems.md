Clinicians chart, order tests, and review results in one system. Patients use MyChart to see records and book visits. Epic has added generative AI features for drafting messages and notes.

Implementations are large projects run by health systems, and individuals cannot buy it directly.

Oracle Health and MEDITECH are alternatives. Epic suits health informaticists and hospital administrators.
