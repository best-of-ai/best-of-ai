ServiceTitan is built for home service trades. Call center staff book jobs, dispatchers assign technicians, technicians use a mobile app to quote and invoice with good-better-best pricing, and the office tracks job costs, marketing, and payroll. Everything ties to one customer record.

Data and AI help companies grow. Tools score inbound calls, flag missed opportunities, recommend pricing, and predict technician performance, and agents assist with booking and follow-ups. Reporting shows revenue per technician and marketing return.

ServiceTitan is sold on custom pricing, usually to mid-sized and larger contractors, and implementation takes time. Alternatives include Housecall Pro, Jobber, FieldEdge, Workiz, and Service Fusion. ServiceTitan suits established trades companies.
