TurboTenant is designed for landlords who manage their own properties. The software covers the main jobs: posting rental listings, screening applicants, creating and signing leases, collecting rent, and handling maintenance requests. Landlords who do not want to be hands-on can hire full-service management for a flat fee.

AI shows up in small, practical places. An AI description generator writes listing copy, and a free Lease Agreement Audit reviews a lease for problems. The company earns money mainly from fees that tenants pay for screening and rent payments, which is how the core landlord tools stay free.

That model means costs shift to tenants, which some landlords like and others do not. TurboTenant suits small landlords and first-time owners. Hemlane adds human support, and DoorLoop and Buildium serve larger portfolios with fuller accounting.
