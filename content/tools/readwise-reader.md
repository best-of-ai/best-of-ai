Reader is a single inbox for things you want to read. You can save web pages, subscribe to newsletters at a dedicated email address, follow RSS feeds, upload PDFs and ebooks, and import videos with transcripts. Everything appears in a clean reading view, with tools for highlighting, tagging, and taking notes, and it works across web, mobile, and browser extensions.

The connection with Readwise makes it more than a reading list. Highlights flow into Readwise, which resurfaces them through spaced repetition so you remember what you read, and exports them to Notion, Obsidian, Roam, and other note apps. Ghostreader is the AI assistant that can summarize a document, define terms, answer questions about it, and generate custom prompts, and it also supports text-to-speech and features for catching up on a backlog.

Readwise Reader is part of a paid Readwise subscription, with a free trial, which puts it at a higher cost than free read-later apps. It's best for heavy readers who want to process and retain information. Alternatives include Pocket, which has shut down in favor of other apps, Instapaper, Matter, and Omnivore, which closed, plus browser reading lists. Notion and Obsidian can store notes but do not offer the same reading experience. Reader suits knowledge workers and researchers.
