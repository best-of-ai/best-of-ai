Stem began by installing battery storage systems for businesses, and its software, Athena, became the heart of the company. Athena uses forecasts of energy prices, demand, and generation to decide when a battery should charge or discharge, balancing cost savings for the owner with revenue from grid services. This kind of optimization can make storage economically viable.

The business has shifted from selling hardware toward software and services. Athena manages solar plus storage, standalone batteries, and other distributed assets for developers and owners, and also offers analytics and asset performance tools. Machine learning helps predict conditions and handle the complexity of electricity markets, which differ by region.

Stem is a public company selling to commercial, industrial, and utility-scale customers through contracts. The energy storage market is competitive and depends on policy and prices, and the company has faced financial challenges. Competitors include Fluence, Tesla's Autobidder, Wärtsilä, and software firms like Ascend Analytics and Customized Energy Solutions. Stem is relevant to energy developers and businesses seeking battery optimization.
