ACE Studio lets musicians create vocals without a singer. You enter a melody in a piano-roll editor along with lyrics, choose a voice model, and the software sings it, with controls for vibrato, breath, dynamics, and expression that make the performance sound human. It works as a standalone app or as a plugin inside digital audio workstations such as Ableton, Logic, and FL Studio. Songwriters use it for demos and finished tracks.

Additional features expand its use. A voice changer converts a recorded vocal into a different singer's voice, tools can transform instrumental performances like a hummed melody into instrument sounds, and a library of licensed voice models offers a range of styles and languages. The company states that its voices come from consenting singers who are compensated, which is a key issue in the debate over AI voices in music.

ACE Studio has a free trial or limited free tier and paid subscriptions or perpetual options, with commercial use allowed under license terms. Results depend on the skill of the user in programming expression, and they can still sound synthetic in exposed passages. Competitors include Synthesizer V, Vocaloid, Kits.ai, Dreamtonics, and text-to-song tools like Suno and Udio, which generate whole songs instead of controlled vocals. ACE Studio suits producers who want detailed control over synthetic singing.
