Krisp installs as a virtual microphone and speaker, so it sits between your hardware and whatever calling app you use. It removes noise before it reaches the other person: barking dogs, keyboard clatter, traffic, and other voices in the room. It can also clean incoming audio, so a participant on a bad connection or in a loud space sounds clearer to you. Because it works with Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack, and nearly any other app, one setup covers all of them.

Over time the product has added a meeting assistant. Krisp can transcribe and summarize meetings without a bot joining the call, because it captures audio from your device. It produces notes, action items, and a searchable record. Other features include accent conversion for call centers, which makes speech easier for listeners to understand, and AI-powered tools for contact centers that clean audio and give agents live help.

A free plan covers a limited amount of noise cancellation and meeting minutes each week, with paid plans removing the limits and adding more features. Noise removal runs on the device, so audio does not need to be sent anywhere for that part. Results are strong but not perfect, and heavily processed audio can sometimes sound slightly artificial. NVIDIA Broadcast and the built-in noise suppression in Zoom and Teams are alternatives, and Otter and Fireflies compete on meeting notes.
