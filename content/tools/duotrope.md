Writers search by genre, length, pay rate, and reading period. They log each submission and see response times from other users. Listings include submission guidelines and acceptance statistics.

It is a paid subscription with a trial. Statistics come from user reports, so they are estimates.

Submittable and Chills are related tools. Duotrope suits poets who submit work widely.
