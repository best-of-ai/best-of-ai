Speak puts spoken practice first. Instead of tapping through vocabulary drills, you speak aloud in lessons that simulate real situations such as ordering food, introducing yourself, or having a job interview. The app listens, corrects pronunciation and grammar, and prompts you to try again, so you gain confidence speaking, which many apps neglect.

An AI tutor handles open-ended conversation. You can discuss topics you choose, practice roleplays, and ask the tutor to explain mistakes, with the system adapting to your level. The app began with a focus on English for Korean speakers and has expanded to teach other languages, with courses designed for particular native languages. It was developed with backing from OpenAI's startup fund.

Speak has a limited free tier and subscription plans, often at lower annual rates. AI tutors can make occasional errors and are not a substitute for talking with real people, but they offer practice at any hour. Alternatives include Duolingo Max, Babbel, Praktika, ELSA Speak for pronunciation, and live tutors on italki or Preply. Speak suits learners who want lots of speaking practice.
