Rocket Lawyer is aimed at people who need legal help without a full law firm engagement. Members answer questions in an interview format and the service generates documents such as lease agreements, NDAs, wills, and operating agreements. Documents can be signed electronically and stored, and templates are tailored to the user's state.

Beyond documents, membership gives access to attorneys through ask-a-lawyer services and, for some matters, discounted consultations. Small businesses use it for formation, contracts, and compliance support. AI tools help explain legal terms and draft documents, and are positioned as a way to get started before consulting a professional.

Rocket Lawyer is paid through subscriptions, with a free trial and extra costs for some services. Templates cannot account for every situation, and complex matters need an attorney. It is not a substitute for individualized legal advice. Alternatives include LegalZoom, Nolo, Clerky for startups, and local attorneys. Rocket Lawyer suits individuals and small businesses with routine legal needs.
