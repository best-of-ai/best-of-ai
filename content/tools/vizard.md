Vizard helps creators repurpose long content. A user uploads a podcast, webinar, or interview, and the tool analyzes it to pick highlight moments, cuts them into clips, crops them for vertical formats, and adds captions. A text-based editor lets users trim by editing the transcript.

Extra features include B-roll suggestions, brand templates, and scheduling to social platforms. It supports many languages and serves podcasters, educators, and marketers.

Vizard has a free tier and paid plans by minutes. Clip selection is automated and may not match a creator's taste, so review matters. Alternatives include OpusClip, Klap, Submagic, Descript, and Munch. Vizard suits creators making short clips from long recordings.
