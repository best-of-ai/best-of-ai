Tidio targets small online businesses that want to answer customers quickly without a big support team. A chat widget on the website lets visitors ask questions, and the tool collects messages from chat, email, and social channels in one inbox. Visual chatbot flows handle common tasks, such as collecting contact details, recommending products, and recovering abandoned carts.

Lyro, its AI agent, answers customer questions using the store's help content and product data, and hands off to a human when needed. Automations trigger messages based on visitor behavior, and integrations with Shopify, WordPress, WooCommerce, and others bring in order and product information. Tidio is aimed at ease of setup, and most stores can install it in minutes.

Tidio has a free plan with limited conversations and paid plans scaled by features and the number of AI conversations. It's simpler and cheaper than enterprise platforms like Zendesk or Intercom, with fewer advanced options. AI answers depend on the content you supply, and sensitive requests should route to people. Alternatives include Intercom, Crisp, Gorgias for e-commerce, LiveChat, and Chatbase. Tidio suits small shops that want chat and AI support in one tool.
