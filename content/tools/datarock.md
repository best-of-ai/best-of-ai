Datarock works in mineral exploration. Geologists take photos of drill core samples and log what they see, which is slow and subjective. Datarock's models analyze the images to identify rock types, measure fractures, and capture structural features, producing consistent digital logs.

The faster, more objective data helps exploration teams make decisions about where to drill next and supports resource modeling. Results integrate with geological software, and the company has expanded to other imagery such as hyperspectral scans and borehole images.

Datarock is sold to mining and exploration companies on subscription. Models need calibration for each deposit, and geologists remain responsible for interpretation. Alternatives include Imago, Minalyze, Orica's digital solutions, and in-house computer vision. Datarock suits mining teams that log large amounts of core.
