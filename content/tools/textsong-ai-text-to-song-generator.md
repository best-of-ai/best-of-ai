TextSong.net is an innovative free AI text-to-song generator that revolutionizes music creation by transforming user-provided text into captivating melodies and full songs. Designed for budding songwriters, content creators, and music enthusiasts, it leverages advanced AI algorithms to produce original tracks without requiring any musical expertise.
The platform operates in two modes: Instrumental Mode, ideal for background music with up to 200-character text inputs, generating pure melodies; and Song with Lyrics Mode, supporting up to 3,000 characters to create complete songs incorporating the provided text as lyrics. Users input their text prompt, then customize via intuitive dropdowns: genres (pop, rock, jazz, electronic, etc.), moods (uplifting, somber, energetic), voices (male, female, neutral), instruments (piano, guitar, drums, synths), and tempos (slow, medium, fast). The AI processes these to output high-quality audio in seconds, reflecting the user's vision.
Fully online and accessible, TextSong.net ensures easy downloads for personal use. While privacy details aren't explicitly stated, outputs are original and user-controlled. As of September 2025, enhancements include smoother vocal synthesis and broader genre support, making it a go-to tool for effortless, professional-grade music generation. TextSong.net democratizes songwriting, turning words into harmonious art.
