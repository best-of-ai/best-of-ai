Brevo started as an email marketing service called Sendinblue and has expanded into a broader customer engagement suite. It covers email and SMS campaigns, WhatsApp messaging, transactional email for apps, landing pages, forms, live chat, and a CRM for sales pipelines. Automation workflows send messages based on behavior, such as a signup or abandoned cart, and segmentation lets marketers target the right audience.

AI features support content and timing. A writing assistant drafts subject lines and email copy, generates images, and adjusts tone, while send-time optimization picks the best time to reach each contact. Predictive tools estimate engagement, and AI chat features help with customer conversations. The product is positioned for small and mid-sized businesses that want many channels in one tool at a reasonable price.

Brevo has a free plan with a daily email sending limit, and paid plans priced by email volume instead of by contact count, which can be cheaper for businesses with large lists that send infrequently. Higher plans add automation, A/B testing, and removal of branding. Its design tools are less polished than some competitors, but the pricing model is a strong draw. Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, and Klaviyo are the main alternatives, and Mailerlite is a simpler low-cost option.
