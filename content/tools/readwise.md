Readwise addresses a common frustration: highlighting passages and never seeing them again. It imports highlights from Kindle, Apple Books, Instapaper, Pocket-style services, podcasts, and PDFs into one library, and sends a daily review of a few of them by email or in the app, using spaced repetition so important ideas return at intervals.

The service integrates with the note-taking ecosystem, exporting highlights to Notion, Obsidian, Roam, Logseq, and others, so notes stay linked to sources. Search lets you find a highlight across everything you have read, and a mobile and browser extension make capture easy. Readwise also makes Reader, a read-it-later app, and AI features can summarize and let you chat with your highlights.

Readwise is a paid subscription with a free trial. The benefit depends on forming a reading and review habit, and the cost is higher than free alternatives. Alternatives include Matter, Instapaper for reading, Notion and Obsidian plugins, Anki for spaced repetition, and Kindle's own notebook. See the Readwise Reader entry for its reading app. Readwise suits avid readers who want to retain more.
