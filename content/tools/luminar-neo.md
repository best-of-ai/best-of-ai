Luminar Neo is built for photographers who want strong results without a lot of manual work. Instead of slider-heavy workflows, it offers AI tools that do the hard parts in a click. Sky AI replaces or enhances skies, Relight AI changes the lighting in a scene using depth information, Portrait Bokeh AI creates background blur, and tools for skin, face, and body adjustments handle portraits. Generative tools can erase objects and expand the canvas.

Other features include noise reduction, sharpening and upscaling, layers and masks, and presets for fast styles. It works as a standalone app and as a plugin for Lightroom and Photoshop, so it can fit into an existing workflow. The AI tools run locally for many functions, and extensions add features such as HDR merge or focus stacking.

Skylum sells Luminar Neo as a subscription or a one-time purchase, with a trial. It is quicker for landscapes and portraits than a traditional editor, but it is less suited to managing a large library and lacks the cataloging of Lightroom. AI edits can look overprocessed if overused. Alternatives include Adobe Lightroom and Photoshop, ON1 Photo RAW, Capture One, DxO PhotoLab, and Topaz Photo AI. Luminar Neo suits enthusiasts who like fast, creative edits.
