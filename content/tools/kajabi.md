Kajabi is an all-in-one platform for knowledge businesses. A creator builds a site, sells courses, memberships, coaching, and digital downloads, collects payments, runs email campaigns, and hosts a community, all from one dashboard instead of stitching together several tools.

Marketing and automation are built in. Landing pages, funnels, email sequences, and a pipeline for leads let creators sell without extra software. The AI assistant helps write copy, plan courses, and generate emails, and analytics show revenue and engagement. Mobile apps for members are available.

Kajabi is paid and priced above many alternatives, with plans by features and number of products. Creators who only need a course page may find it more than needed. Alternatives include Teachable, Thinkific, Podia, Kit, and Circle. Kajabi suits coaches and creators building a full online business.
