HoneyBook is designed for people who sell services, such as photographers, designers, coaches, and consultants. A client inquiry arrives through a form, the owner sends a proposal with a contract and an invoice, and the client signs and pays online, all within a single flow. This replaces a patchwork of documents, email threads, and payment apps.

Other features support day-to-day work. Automations send reminders and follow-ups, a scheduler lets clients book meetings, and a client portal keeps messages, files, and payments in one timeline. Templates for proposals and questionnaires make repeat work faster, and AI helps draft emails and project descriptions. Reports show income and pipeline.

HoneyBook is paid, with plans by features, and charges payment processing fees. It is built for small service businesses and is less suited to complex projects with large teams. Alternatives include Dubsado, 17hats, Bonsai, Moxie, and general tools like Notion and QuickBooks. HoneyBook suits solo creatives and small studios.
