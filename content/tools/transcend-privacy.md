Transcend takes a developer-oriented approach to privacy compliance. It connects to a company's databases, SaaS tools, and warehouses to discover where personal data is stored, then automates responses to requests from individuals to access or delete their data. Instead of manually searching dozens of systems, the platform executes the request across them and keeps a record.

Other products cover related needs. Consent management controls cookies and tracking according to each visitor's choices and location, a gateway can enforce those choices in what data flows to third parties, and data mapping documents processing activities. Newer tools address AI governance, helping teams track what data feeds models and meet emerging regulations.

Transcend is sold to mid-sized and large companies on subscription, with custom pricing. Implementation requires technical integration, which suits engineering-led organizations. Competitors include OneTrust, Osano, DataGrail, Ketch, BigID, and Securiti. Compliance depends on correct configuration and legal interpretation, and a privacy professional should oversee it. Transcend suits tech-forward companies wanting automation of privacy operations.
