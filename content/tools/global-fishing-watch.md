Users see vessel tracks, fishing effort, and protected areas. Fishery managers and enforcement agencies use it to check activity near marine reserves and to spot suspicious behavior. Data downloads and an API support research.

Coverage depends on vessels broadcasting their position, so some boats are invisible.

Windward and Spire provide commercial maritime data. Global Fishing Watch suits fishery managers and conservation scientists.
