Spectora is software for home inspection businesses. Inspectors document a property on a phone or tablet, adding photos, notes, and ratings, and Spectora compiles a professional report that can be delivered to clients and agents. Scheduling, agreements, and payment tools run the business side.

AI speeds up the slow part. It helps summarize defects, produce clear language for findings, and organize repair requests, and voice dictation turns spoken notes into text. Reports include summaries that buyers and agents can use during negotiations.

Spectora is paid, by plan. Inspectors remain responsible for accuracy and standards of practice. Alternatives include ISN, HomeGauge, Horizon, Palmetto, and ITB. Spectora suits home inspectors.
