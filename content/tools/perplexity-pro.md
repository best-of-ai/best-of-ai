Perplexity Pro changes what model answers your questions. The free tier uses lighter models with search access; Pro gives you GPT-4o, Claude Sonnet, and Gemini Advanced as selectable answer engines, with higher daily limits on advanced model queries. The quality difference is noticeable for complex multi-part questions where reasoning depth affects the accuracy and nuance of the answer. You select the model per query depending on whether you need speed (default model) or depth (GPT-4o or Claude).

Deep Research is the feature most frequently cited by Pro users as the reason to upgrade. Rather than a single search pass, Deep Research autonomously runs 20–30 targeted searches, reads the relevant pages, evaluates the information across sources, and synthesises a structured report with inline citations — typically taking two to five minutes. The output is closer to a junior analyst's research brief than a search engine result, which is useful for competitive analysis, due diligence, technical comparisons, and any research task that would otherwise require reading a dozen articles.

File upload lets you attach PDFs, documents, and spreadsheets to your query so Perplexity can answer questions about your specific documents rather than just the web. This makes it usable for analysing financial reports, research papers, legal documents, or internal knowledge. The Pro plan at $20/month also gives API access to Perplexity's models for developers building search-grounded applications. For knowledge workers who do serious research regularly — consultants, journalists, analysts, executives — the upgrade pays for itself in time saved on tasks that would otherwise require extended web browsing.
