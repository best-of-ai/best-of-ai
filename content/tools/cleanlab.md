Cleanlab starts from the idea that model quality depends on data quality. Real datasets contain mistakes: images with wrong labels, duplicated examples, and outliers. Cleanlab's open-source library uses statistical methods and model predictions to find the examples most likely to be wrong, so a team can correct them instead of reviewing everything.

The commercial platform, Cleanlab Studio, extends this with a no-code interface for cleaning and improving datasets and training models on the result. The company has also moved into reliability for generative AI, offering tools to score the trustworthiness of language model outputs and catch hallucinations in production systems.

The open-source library is free, and the platform has free and paid tiers. Cleaning data requires human judgment about what the correct label is. Alternatives include Snorkel AI, Label Studio, Argilla, Encord, and Labelbox. Cleanlab suits machine learning teams with messy datasets.
