Clipdrop packages common image edits into simple web tools. Remove Background cuts out a subject in a click, Cleanup erases unwanted objects, Image Upscaler enlarges small pictures with added detail, and Uncrop extends an image beyond its borders. Relight changes the lighting on a subject, and Replace Background swaps in a new scene from a text prompt. A text-to-image generator rounds out the set, using Stability AI models.

Product photographers, e-commerce sellers, marketers, and casual users reach for it when they need a quick edit without opening a heavy editor. The tools work in the browser and on mobile, with an API for developers who want to add the same features to their own apps. The project had a short stint under Stability AI before moving to Jasper, which acquired it to expand its marketing tools.

Clipdrop has a free tier with limited use and watermark or resolution limits, plus paid plans for higher volumes and quality. API access has changed under the new ownership, so developers should check the current terms. Quality is good for common cases but variable for complex scenes. Photoroom is a strong competitor for product photos, Remove.bg is the classic background remover, and Adobe Express and Canva offer similar tools inside broader design apps.
