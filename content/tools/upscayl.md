Upscayl makes AI upscaling available to anyone. You drag in a low-resolution image, choose a model, and the app enlarges it by two, three, or four times with added detail, saving the result to your computer. It includes models tuned for general photos, digital art, and sharpening, and supports batch processing for many files. Because it runs on your own hardware, there is no per-image fee, and your images stay private.

The software is based on open models such as Real-ESRGAN, and is built to be simple: a clean interface, a few settings, and no accounts. Users include photographers, designers, game modders, and people restoring old or low-resolution pictures. Community members can add custom models, and the project is developed openly with contributions from volunteers.

Upscayl is free, with the project supported by donations. It needs a reasonably capable graphics card, and processing large images can be slow or run out of memory on weak hardware. As with other AI upscalers, it invents detail, which can look artificial on faces or text. Topaz Gigapixel and Topaz Photo AI are paid options with more polish, Real-ESRGAN offers command-line use, and cloud tools like Let's Enhance work in the browser. Upscayl suits people who want a free local upscaler.
