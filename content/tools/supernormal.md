Supernormal aims to make meeting notes require no effort. A bot joins your scheduled meetings, records and transcribes them, and creates notes organized by templates for different types of meetings, such as sales calls, interviews, one-on-ones, and standups. The notes list decisions, action items, and key points, and are shared with attendees or saved to the tools your team uses.

The product works across platforms, including Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, and also supports in-person meetings. Integrations push notes to Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, and Google Docs. Search and chat features let you look back at past meetings and ask questions, and customization options allow you to adjust the format of the notes.

Supernormal has a free plan with limited use and paid plans for unlimited notes and features, plus team options. As with all meeting bots, tell participants when recording, and check the data handling terms. Fathom, Fireflies, Otter, Granola, and Read AI are alternatives, with Granola taking a no-bot approach that captures audio from your device. Supernormal suits individuals and small teams that want reliable, well-structured notes.
