Sisense helps organizations analyze data and share insights. Analysts connect data sources, build models and dashboards, and users explore results in a browser. Its strength is embedding: software companies place Sisense charts and analytics inside their products for customers.

AI features include asking questions in plain language, forecasting, and automatic detection of unusual patterns, plus a generative assistant that builds dashboards. The platform works with cloud data warehouses and offers an API-first approach.

Sisense is sold on enterprise pricing. It competes in a mature field. Alternatives include Tableau, Power BI, Looker, ThoughtSpot, and Qlik. Sisense suits companies that need embedded analytics.
