Amplemarket helps sales teams find and contact potential customers. It combines a database of B2B contacts and companies with search filters and intent signals, so reps can build lists of people who match their ideal customer and show signs of interest, such as hiring, funding, or website activity. Contact data is verified and enriched to reduce bounced emails and wrong numbers.

Outreach happens through multichannel sequences that mix email, phone calls, and social steps such as LinkedIn messages. A parallel dialer lets reps call through lists quickly. The AI layer researches each account and prospect, drafts personalized emails and call notes, and surfaces the best people to contact first. Duo Intelligence is Amplemarket's approach of AI preparing the work while the rep reviews and sends, instead of fully automatic sending.

Amplemarket is paid, with pricing based on seats and credits and typically on a custom quote, and it is aimed at startups and mid-sized sales teams. Outbound tools raise compliance and deliverability questions: sending large volumes of cold email can harm your domain reputation and may be restricted by privacy laws in some regions. Apollo and ZoomInfo are the largest competitors on data, Outreach and Salesloft focus on engagement, and Clay is popular for building custom enrichment workflows.
