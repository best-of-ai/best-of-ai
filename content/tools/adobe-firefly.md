Adobe Firefly's commercial safety story is its defining advantage over other AI image generators. It was trained exclusively on licensed Adobe Stock images, openly licensed content, and public domain work — not scraped from the internet. This makes Firefly-generated images safe to use commercially without IP risk, which matters enormously for ad agencies, brands, and studios where legal clearance is non-negotiable. Adobe provides a content authenticity credential with each generated image.

The integration inside Photoshop is where Firefly becomes genuinely transformative rather than just another generator. Generative Fill lets you select any region of an existing photo and describe what you want there — extend a sky, replace a background, add a person, remove an object — and Photoshop generates multiple options that blend seamlessly with the surrounding image. This is photo editing at a level of quality that previously required hours of masking and compositing work. Generative Expand extends images beyond their original borders, which solves the constant problem of photos not being the right aspect ratio.

Firefly is available standalone at firefly.adobe.com with monthly credit limits, and is included in Creative Cloud subscriptions. All-Apps CC subscribers get the largest monthly credit allocation. Illustrator's Vector Recolour and Generative Shape Fill use Firefly under the hood for vector work. Premiere Pro's Generative Extend fills short gaps in footage. Adobe's roadmap shows Firefly being embedded across the entire Creative Cloud suite, making it the AI layer for professional creative software rather than a standalone product.
