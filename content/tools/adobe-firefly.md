Firefly is Adobe's generative AI platform. The Firefly web app generates images from a text description, extends or edits existing ones, creates short video clips, and produces audio and vector artwork. Adobe trained its own models on licensed content such as Adobe Stock and public-domain material, and it markets them as safer for commercial use than models trained on scraped images. Outputs carry content credentials that note they were made with AI.

Much of the value comes from integration. The same models power Generative Fill and Generative Expand in Photoshop, Text to Vector in Illustrator, Generative Extend in Premiere Pro, and image generation in Express. Firefly also lets you work with some models from other companies in the same interface, so you can compare results without leaving Adobe's tools. Boards and other collaboration features help teams explore ideas together, and an API serves developers.

Usage is measured in generative credits that come with Creative Cloud plans or with standalone Firefly subscriptions, and heavier video and audio features use more credits. There is a limited free tier. Firefly images are polished but sometimes less striking than Midjourney's, and the focus on commercial safety makes it popular with agencies and brands. Midjourney, FLUX, OpenAI's image models, and Google's Imagen and Gemini image models are the main alternatives.
