VidFlux takes a still image and brings it to life. A user uploads a photo, optionally describes the motion, and the tool generates a short video with movement such as camera pans, flowing hair, or moving water.

Marketers use it to give product photos life in ads and social feeds, and creators animate portraits and artwork. The clips are short and quality varies with the image.

Independent information is limited. VidFlux has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Runway, Pika, Luma, Kling, and PixVerse. VidFlux suits creators animating still images.
