SongGen.net is a cutting-edge free AI song generator that empowers users to create original songs, melodies, and lyrics effortlessly online. Harnessing the latest AI technology, it transforms simple text inputs into complete musical compositions in seconds, making music creation accessible to everyone without needing skills or equipment.

The tool features an intuitive interface with key modes: a toggle for Instrumental Mode to produce lyric-free tracks based on up to 200-character descriptions, ideal for beats or backgrounds; and the default mode for full songs incorporating up to 3000 characters of text as lyrics. Users customize via dropdowns: genres (pop, rock, hip-hop, electronic, etc.), moods (energetic, serene, dramatic), voices (male, female, synthetic), instruments (guitar, piano, drums, synths), and tempos (with an additional 80-character input for refinements).
