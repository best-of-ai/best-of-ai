Typefully started as a better way to write threads on X. The editor is clean and focused, with automatic thread splitting, a preview of how the post will look, and collaboration so someone else can review drafts. Posts can be scheduled, queued, and cross-posted to several networks, so one piece of writing can reach audiences on different platforms.

Analytics and growth features help creators learn what works. Dashboards show impressions, engagement, and follower trends, and tools help plan content, track ideas, and reuse past posts. AI features suggest rewrites, hooks, and alternatives, and can adjust tone, though the tool emphasizes the writer's voice.

Typefully has a free plan with limits and paid plans for more accounts, AI, and analytics. Reliance on social platform APIs means features can change when networks change rules. Alternatives include Buffer, Hypefury, Taplio for LinkedIn, Postwise, and Hootsuite. Typefully suits creators, founders, and writers who share ideas on social networks.
