Analysts write code in RStudio or Positron, build reports with Quarto, and publish apps and dashboards with Shiny. Posit Connect hosts them for a team. The open-source tools are free, and servers for teams cost money.

Biostatisticians and clinical teams use it widely, often with validated environments for regulated work.

JupyterLab and Hex are alternatives. Posit suits statisticians and R users.
