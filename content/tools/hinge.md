Hinge takes a different approach from swipe-first dating apps. Profiles are built from photos plus written or voice answers to prompts, which give people something specific to respond to. Users like or comment on a particular part of a profile, which starts a conversation with context.

The app uses algorithms to suggest people likely to be compatible, based on preferences and behavior, and has added AI tools such as prompt feedback to help people write better profiles. It limits how many likes a user can send, which encourages more selective choices, and offers premium tiers.

Hinge is free with paid options, and belongs to Match Group. Like other dating apps, it raises safety and privacy considerations, so meet in public places and protect personal information. Alternatives include Bumble, Tinder, Feeld, Raya, and Coffee Meets Bagel. Hinge suits people looking for relationships.
