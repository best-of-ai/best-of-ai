Hootsuite is one of the oldest social media management tools. Teams connect accounts for networks such as Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, and YouTube, plan content on a calendar, schedule posts, and respond to comments and messages in a shared inbox. Approval workflows suit larger organizations.

Listening and analytics tools track brand mentions and compare performance across channels. OwlyWriter, the AI assistant, helps write captions, suggest ideas, and repurpose content. Advertising management and employee advocacy products extend the suite for enterprises.

Hootsuite is paid, with plans by users and accounts, and a trial. It is often considered pricier than newer tools. Alternatives include Buffer, Sprout Social, Later, Metricool, and Sprinklr for enterprises. Hootsuite suits social teams that manage many accounts.
