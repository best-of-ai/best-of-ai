Vectorizer.ai converts bitmap images into vectors, which are scalable files used for logos, print, and cutting machines. Upload a low-resolution logo or illustration, and the service traces the shapes into paths and fills, returning an SVG, EPS, PDF, or DXF that can be resized without losing sharpness. The AI approach produces cleaner curves and fewer stray points than older tracing tools, with controls to adjust the level of detail.

Designers use it to recover logos from low-quality files, sign makers and crafters use it to prepare artwork for vinyl cutters and laser engravers, and developers add it to workflows through an API. It handles flat-color artwork best, such as logos and icons, and it works less well on photographs, which turn into complex tracings with many shapes. A preview lets you see the result before paying.

Vectorizer.ai offers free previews and charges for downloads through a subscription or credit packs. For complex or very fine artwork, manual cleanup in Illustrator or Inkscape may be needed. Adobe Illustrator's Image Trace is built into a tool many designers already own, Vector Magic is a long-standing competitor, and Inkscape includes a free trace function. Vectorizer.ai suits quick, high-quality vector conversions.
