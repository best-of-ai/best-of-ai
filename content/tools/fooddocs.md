FoodDocs is a digital food safety system. A restaurant, producer, or caterer answers questions about what it makes and how, and the software generates a food safety plan with hazard analysis and control points, along with the daily tasks to carry it out. Staff complete checklists on a phone or tablet.

Monitoring features include temperature logging, cleaning schedules, and alerts when tasks are missed or readings are out of range. Records are stored and ready for inspectors, and AI helps adapt the plan when menus or processes change. Sensors can send temperature readings automatically.

FoodDocs is sold by subscription to food businesses of varied sizes. Requirements differ by country and the plan still has to match what actually happens in the kitchen. Alternatives include Safefood 360, SafetyCulture (iAuditor), Zenput, Squadle, and FoodLogiQ. FoodDocs suits food businesses that need compliant, digital records.
