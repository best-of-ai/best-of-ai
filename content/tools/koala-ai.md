Koala focuses on creating articles that are designed to rank. You enter a keyword, and the tool analyzes current search results, collects information from the web, and writes a structured article with headings, optional images, and links. It supports one-click generation of long pieces, and special modes for product reviews, listicles, and how-to guides. Real-time data is meant to keep information fresh compared with models that only use training data.

Additional tools include a chat assistant that can browse the web, bulk generation for producing many articles, and integrations to publish directly to WordPress, Shopify, and other platforms. A feature can add affiliate links to products for monetized content sites. Users are mainly bloggers, affiliate marketers, and small agencies.

Koala is a paid service with plans by the number of words or articles, and often offers trials. Content created at scale still needs human review for facts and quality, and search engines favor useful, original content over mass-produced text. Alternatives include Surfer, Frase, Jasper, Writesonic, Article Forge, and general assistants such as ChatGPT. Koala suits content publishers who want quick, researched drafts.
