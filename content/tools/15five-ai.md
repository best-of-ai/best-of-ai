15Five helps organizations manage how people work and develop. Employees submit short weekly check-ins about priorities, progress, and morale, and managers review them and respond, which keeps a regular rhythm of communication. The platform includes performance reviews, goal and OKR tracking, one-on-one meeting agendas, recognition features, and engagement surveys to measure how employees feel.

AI is used to support managers. The system can summarize check-in responses and survey results, suggest coaching tips, help write review feedback, and flag potential issues like declining engagement or flight risk. Its Best-Self Management framework offers training for managers on developing teams. Reports give HR leaders a view of trends across teams.

15Five is sold on subscription per employee, with modules at different tiers, aimed at mid-sized companies. Success depends on managers actually using the tools, and engagement scores should be treated as signals, not verdicts. Competitors include Lattice, Culture Amp, Leapsome, Workday, and Betterworks. Employee data is sensitive, so organizations must handle AI analysis of feedback carefully. 15Five suits companies that want to build regular feedback habits.
