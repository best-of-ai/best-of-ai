Guru stores verified knowledge in cards that teams can find where they work. A browser extension and Slack integration surface relevant answers without leaving the page, and experts verify cards on a schedule so that the information stays trustworthy. The idea is a single source of truth for sales, support, and internal teams.

AI search connects to the company's other sources, such as documents, chat, and ticketing systems, and answers questions in natural language with citations. Analytics show what people search for and which content is stale. Admins control who can see what, and the AI respects those permissions.

Guru is sold per user with a trial. Value depends on the team keeping content current. Alternatives include Glean, Notion AI, Confluence, Bloomfire, and Slab. Guru suits teams that need verified, easily accessible answers.
