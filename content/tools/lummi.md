Lummi collects AI-generated pictures in a searchable library, organized by category and style. Users browse or search for backgrounds, textures, scenes, and illustrations, then download them for use in websites, presentations, and campaigns. The pitch is variety and a modern look compared with familiar stock photography.

The platform includes generation tools that let users create images from prompts and adjust them, along with options for editing. It is popular with designers who want unusual or abstract visuals and do not want to prompt from scratch.

Lummi has a free tier and paid plans. Licensing terms and the copyright status of AI-generated images vary, so check the terms before commercial use, especially for images of people or recognizable brands. Alternatives include Unsplash, Pexels, Adobe Stock, Freepik, and generating images directly with Midjourney or GPT Image. Lummi suits designers who want fresh, AI-made stock visuals.
