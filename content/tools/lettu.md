Lettu is a platform for brands, agencies, and creators who work together. Influencer and creator campaigns involve briefs, contracts, approvals, payments, and usage rights, which are often handled in scattered emails. Lettu puts these into one workflow with legal templates drafted by lawyers.

The platform helps brands and agencies draft briefs, agree terms, track deliverables, and manage rights, such as how long content can be used. It draws on the law firm's experience in advertising and media law, and AI features speed up drafting and review.

Lettu has a free tier and paid plans, and is aimed mainly at the UK and Europe. Legal templates do not replace advice for unusual deals. Alternatives include Aspire, Grin, CreatorIQ, Upfluence, and general contract tools like PandaDoc. Lettu suits marketing teams and agencies running creator campaigns.
