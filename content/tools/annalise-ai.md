Annalise.ai builds radiology AI that looks for many findings at once rather than one condition per algorithm. Its chest X-ray product covers a long list of abnormalities, and its brain CT product targets urgent findings such as bleeds.

Results are shown to the radiologist with the suspicious area marked, and the tool can help prioritize urgent studies in a worklist.

It is sold to hospitals and imaging groups. Output needs radiologist review, and local validation matters. Alternatives include Aidoc, Lunit, Qure.ai, and Gleamer. Annalise.ai suits radiology departments that want broad coverage from one vendor.
