Brex built a corporate card and spend platform for startups and growing companies. Businesses issue cards to employees with set limits, and expenses are captured and matched automatically, with receipts collected through the app and policies enforced in real time. Finance teams get visibility into spending and can close their books faster than with manual expense reports.

The platform grew into a broader finance system. It includes bill payments, reimbursements, travel booking, banking features, and budgeting, with integrations to accounting software such as NetSuite and QuickBooks. AI agents handle tasks such as filling out expense reports, checking receipts for policy violations, and answering finance questions, aiming to reduce time spent on back-office work.

Capital One acquired Brex, and it continues to operate under its brand with its existing leadership. Pricing combines software plans with card interchange revenue, and eligibility depends on a company's finances. Competitors include Ramp, which is a close rival with a similar product, Amex and Chase business cards, Divvy from Bill, and Expensify for expense reports. Brex suits startups and mid-sized companies that want modern spend management.
