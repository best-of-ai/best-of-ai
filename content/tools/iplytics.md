IPlytics focuses on the overlap between patents and technical standards. Many technologies, from 5G to Wi-Fi to video codecs, depend on standards, and companies hold patents they declare essential to implementing them. IPlytics links patent databases with standards documents, declarations, and standardization meeting contributions, helping users understand who owns what and how influential each player is.

Typical users are IP and licensing professionals, R&D strategists, and legal teams. They use it to search for standard-essential patents, benchmark portfolios against competitors, find licensing opportunities, and see which companies are shaping emerging standards. The platform includes analytics, dashboards, and data feeds, and AI features help classify patents and find relevant documents.

IPlytics is sold by subscription to corporations, law firms, and research organizations, with custom pricing. It is a specialist tool, and its value depends on working in standards-heavy fields such as telecommunications, automotive, and the Internet of Things. Claims of essentiality are self-declared and legal conclusions need expert analysis. Alternatives and complements include PatSnap, Derwent Innovation, Questel Orbit, Cipher, and LexisNexis PatentSight. IPlytics suits teams managing licensing and standards strategy.
