Looka makes logo design approachable. You answer a few questions about your business, choose styles and colors you like, and Looka generates many logo concepts using different icons, fonts, and layouts. You can customize any option in an editor, then see how it looks on mockups such as business cards, signs, and social media profiles.

It offers two ways to pay. A one-time purchase of a logo package provides high-resolution and vector files, and a subscription Brand Kit adds a library of templates, a style guide, and tools for marketing materials, business cards, and social posts in your brand colors. It also includes a simple website builder, so a new business can establish a basic presence in one place.

AI-generated logos resemble each other more than custom designs do, and trademark clearance is the buyer's responsibility, so important brands should consider a human designer. Pricing is higher than some free tools. Alternatives include Brandmark, Canva's logo maker, Shopify's Logo Maker, Wix Logo Maker, and 99designs or Fiverr for custom work. Looka suits startups and small businesses wanting a polished identity quickly.
