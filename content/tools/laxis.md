Laxis joins online meetings, records and transcribes them, and produces summaries with action items and key points. Sales teams use it to log call notes in the CRM automatically, and researchers and recruiters use it for interviews. Search across past meetings helps find specific topics.

Extra features include templates for different meeting types, follow-up email drafts, and analytics on conversations. Integrations connect with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and CRMs such as Salesforce and HubSpot.

Laxis has a free tier and paid plans. Recording meetings requires consent from participants. Alternatives include Fireflies, Otter, Fathom, Avoma, and Gong. Laxis suits sales and client-facing teams that want automatic notes.
