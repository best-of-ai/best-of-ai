Freshsales is a sales CRM that keeps lead and deal information in one view. Teams track contacts, run pipelines, and log calls and emails, with a built-in phone and chat so reps can contact prospects without switching tools. Automation moves leads through stages and assigns tasks.

Freddy AI assists with prioritization. It scores leads by likelihood to convert, predicts which deals will close, and suggests next best actions, while generative features draft emails and summarize conversations. Analytics show pipeline health, and integrations connect with Freshdesk, marketing tools, and common business apps.

Freshsales has a free plan for small teams and paid tiers. It is more affordable than some rivals but less deep than Salesforce for complex needs. Alternatives include HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, Close, and Salesforce. Freshsales suits small and mid-sized teams wanting an easy CRM.
