Scholé rethinks workplace learning. Instead of asking employees to finish courses on the side, it delivers short, relevant learning moments tied to real tasks, adapting to what a person already knows and needs to do. Principles from learning science, such as practice and spaced repetition, shape the design.

Teams can use it to build skills in areas like communication, sales, or technical topics, with AI creating content and feedback. The idea is learning that fits into work and sticks.

Independent information is limited, so evaluate it with a pilot. It has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Sana, 360Learning, Docebo, Degreed, and Coursera for Business. Scholé suits teams seeking in-workflow learning.
