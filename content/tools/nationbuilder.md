NationBuilder is designed for people building a movement. A campaign or organization keeps records of supporters, volunteers, and donors in one database, runs a website, sends email and texts, organizes events, and takes donations. Having everything together helps small teams manage a large network.

Tools support organizing, such as lists, tags, and tasks for volunteers, and reporting on progress toward goals. AI features help draft messages and summarize supporter information. The platform is used by campaigns, community groups, and associations around the world.

NationBuilder is paid, with plans by supporter count. Political communications are subject to election and data protection laws that vary by country. Alternatives include NGP VAN for US political campaigns, Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, Action Network, Mobilize, and Bonterra. NationBuilder suits campaigns and advocacy groups.
