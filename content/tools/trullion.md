Trullion tackles accounting standards that require reading contracts. For leases and revenue contracts, finance teams must pull terms such as payments, dates, and options into calculations. Trullion's AI reads documents, extracts the data, and generates the schedules and journal entries.

Each figure links back to its source in the document, which helps auditors verify. The platform integrates with accounting systems and handles changes such as lease modifications. It is used by finance teams and audit firms.

Trullion is sold on subscription. Accounting judgments still belong to qualified people. Alternatives include LeaseQuery, Visual Lease, CoStar's Lease Administration, NetSuite modules, and Zuora RevPro. Trullion suits finance teams handling ASC 842 and IFRS 16.
