Screenpipe captures screen text and audio transcripts so people can retrieve earlier work and provide context to AI assistants. Capture must be enabled beforehand; it does not reconstruct unrecorded history.

Raw history is stored locally by default. Configured cloud AI, transcription, sync, integrations and model providers can process context off-device.

A limited free plan is available, with paid subscriptions for expanded access. See [current pricing](https://screenpipe.com/pricing) for limits and features.

The source is available under the [Screenpipe Commercial License](https://github.com/screenpipe/screenpipe/blob/main/LICENSE.md), not an OSI open-source license. See the [repository](https://github.com/screenpipe/screenpipe) and [MCP setup documentation](https://github.com/screenpipe/screenpipe/tree/main/packages/screenpipe-mcp#installation).
