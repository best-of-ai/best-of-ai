Remove.bg made background removal fast and easy. You upload a photo, or drag it onto the page, and an AI model detects the foreground and returns the image with a transparent background in a few seconds. It handles people, products, animals, and cars, and it copes reasonably well with difficult edges like hair and fur. It is used for e-commerce product photos, profile pictures, ID photos, and design mockups.

The service extends beyond the website. Plugins connect it to Photoshop, Figma, and other tools, apps let you process images on a phone or desktop, and an API allows platforms to add automatic background removal at scale. Once the background is removed, you can add a solid color, a template, or another image. Bulk tools handle large catalogs. Remove.bg is owned by Canva.

Remove.bg gives free low-resolution previews and charges for full-resolution downloads through credits or subscriptions. The market has changed because background removal is now built into Canva, Adobe Express, Photoshop, phone gallery apps, and tools like Photoroom, so a separate service matters less for casual use. Quality can fail on low-contrast edges or complex scenes. Clipdrop, Photoroom, Erase.bg, and PhotoScissors are alternatives. It remains handy for quick, one-off cutouts and API integrations.
