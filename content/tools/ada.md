Ada helps companies handle customer support questions without adding staff. Its AI agent answers inquiries on websites, in apps, and over messaging channels by drawing on a company's knowledge base and systems, and it can take actions such as checking an order, changing an address, or processing a return by connecting to back-end tools. When a request is too complex or sensitive, it hands the conversation to a human agent with context.

The platform emphasizes measurable outcomes. Ada reports automated resolution rates, shows where answers failed, and suggests improvements, and it allows teams to define guardrails and tone. Support managers use a no-code interface to shape the agent without engineering help, and integrations with helpdesks like Zendesk and Salesforce keep conversations in existing workflows. It supports many languages.

Ada is sold to mid-sized and large companies on custom contracts, often priced by the number of resolutions. Quality depends on the quality of the underlying knowledge and processes. Competitors include Intercom's Fin, Zendesk's AI agents, Forethought, Decagon, Sierra, and Salesforce Agentforce. Companies should test the agent on real conversations and track customer satisfaction. Ada suits businesses with high volumes of repetitive questions.
