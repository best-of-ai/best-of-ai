CharlieHR is designed for companies without a dedicated HR team. It keeps employee records, tracks time off, runs onboarding checklists, stores contracts, and supports regular reviews and one-on-ones. The interface is simple, so small teams can adopt it without lengthy setup.

An AI assistant helps with everyday HR questions about policies and employment topics, and supports tasks such as drafting job descriptions or other documents. Integrations connect with payroll providers, so data does not need to be entered twice. Reports give founders a view of headcount and absence.

CharlieHR is paid and priced per employee, and it is aimed at UK-based and similar small businesses. Advice from an AI assistant is not legal advice, and employment rules differ by country. Alternatives include BambooHR, Personio, HiBob, Gusto, and Factorial. CharlieHR suits small teams that need straightforward HR tools.
