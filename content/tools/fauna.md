Fauna set out to give developers a database that scaled globally without managing servers. It combined a flexible document model with relational features, offered strong consistency across regions, and was accessed through an API with its own query language, FQL, and earlier through GraphQL. Developers built web and mobile apps on it without running infrastructure.

The company announced that it would wind down its hosted service, and the service closed, with accounts deleted afterward. It also said it would release parts of its technology as open source, so some of the ideas may live on in community projects.

People who relied on Fauna had to migrate data to other databases. Alternatives include PlanetScale, Neon, Supabase, CockroachDB, MongoDB Atlas, and DynamoDB. This entry is kept for reference and Fauna is no longer available.
