Shortwave is a Gmail client that rethinks the inbox. Related messages are grouped into threads, newsletters and receipts can be bundled so they do not clutter the main view, and snooze, scheduling, and split inboxes help you process email faster. It works on Mac, Windows, iOS, Android, and the web, and connects to your Gmail account.

AI is central. You can ask the assistant questions about your email, such as what a client asked for last month or when a trip was booked, and it searches and answers using your messages. It summarizes long threads, drafts replies in your tone, and can take actions like organizing, labeling, and scheduling. The goal is to cut the time spent finding and writing.

Shortwave has a free plan with limited history and AI use and paid plans with more. It only supports Gmail and Google Workspace accounts, which excludes users on other providers, and AI features require handing it access to your mail, so review the privacy terms. Superhuman is a polished, more expensive competitor, Spark and Hey are other clients, and Gmail's own Gemini features add AI inside the standard interface. Shortwave suits heavy Gmail users who want an AI-first experience.
