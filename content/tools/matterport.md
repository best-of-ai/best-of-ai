Matterport captures spaces in 3D. A camera or a phone scans a property, and the software stitches the data into a navigable model, a virtual tour where viewers can walk through rooms, take measurements, and view floor plans. Real estate agents use it to show listings, and others use it to document sites.

Beyond tours, the platform serves professional needs. Contractors document existing conditions, insurers record property states, and facility managers keep a digital record of buildings. AI helps generate floor plans, label rooms, and create other outputs from scans.

CoStar, a real estate information company, acquired Matterport. It is sold through subscriptions with hardware options, and cheaper phone capture lowers the barrier. Alternatives include Zillow 3D Home, CubiCasa, Polycam, Ricoh 360 tours, and NavVis. Matterport suits real estate and property professionals.
