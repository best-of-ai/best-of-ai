RunPod rents GPU computing in several forms. Users launch pods with a chosen GPU for development and training, or deploy serverless endpoints that scale with traffic for inference. Templates for common tools like Stable Diffusion and popular language model setups speed up starting.

Storage volumes persist data across sessions, and a community cloud offers lower prices on capacity from independent hosts alongside a secure cloud with data center hardware. Per-second billing suits bursty work.

RunPod is pay as you go. Community capacity varies in reliability and sensitive data should go on secure options. Alternatives include Vast.ai, Lambda, Modal, Together AI, and the major clouds. RunPod suits developers and researchers who need affordable GPUs.
