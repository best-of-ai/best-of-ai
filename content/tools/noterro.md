Noterro is built for small bodywork and rehab practices. Clients book online and fill in intake forms before arriving, and therapists record sessions with notes. An AI feature turns a few prompts into a structured SOAP note, saving time at the end of each appointment.

Other tools cover everyday needs: scheduling, reminders, payments, packages and memberships, and insurance claims where relevant. Reports track revenue and client retention.

Noterro is paid, with plans per practitioner. It is aimed at solo practitioners and small clinics. Alternatives include Jane, Cliniko, MassageBook, Vagaro, and SimplePractice. Noterro suits manual therapists who want simpler admin.
