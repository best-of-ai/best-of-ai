Persado focuses on the specific words in marketing messages. Its premise is that the choice of language, including emotions, calls to action, and phrasing, affects whether people respond, and that machine learning can learn what works. Marketers give it a campaign goal and brand guidelines, and it generates message variations and predicts which will perform best, based on its library of language and results from past campaigns.

Large brands in finance, retail, and telecommunications use Persado to optimize email subject lines, ads, and push notifications at scale. It integrates with marketing platforms, tests variants, and learns from the results to improve future messages. More recently, the company has added generative AI tools for drafting and personalizing content within brand guardrails.

Persado is sold to enterprises on custom contracts, and is priced beyond most small businesses. Results depend on the volume of messaging, and improvements from word choice are usually incremental and measured in percentage lifts. Alternatives include Phrasee, now part of Jacobs, Anyword, Jasper, Copy.ai, and the optimization features in platforms like Braze and Salesforce Marketing Cloud. Persado suits large marketing teams that send many messages and test them rigorously.
