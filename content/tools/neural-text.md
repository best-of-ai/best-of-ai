NeuralText is an SEO content suite. Users find keywords, group them into clusters by intent, study what ranks for a term, and create content briefs that list topics and questions to cover. The AI writer then drafts articles or sections using those guidelines.

The tool aims to let one person handle research, planning, and drafting. A content editor scores text against the brief, and additional templates cover ads, product descriptions, and social posts.

NeuralText is paid with a trial. AI drafts need editing and expert input to be useful and rank well, and search engines favor original, helpful content. Alternatives include Surfer, Frase, NeuronWriter, Keyword Insights, and Clearscope. NeuralText suits small teams and freelancers producing SEO content.
