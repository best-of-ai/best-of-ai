ClassX is an AI-powered education platform that transforms how students and teachers learn, teach, and grow. It offers personalised learning experiences through intelligent tutors, conversation tools, and creative content-generation assistants. Designed for every level of learner, ClassX helps people study smarter—not harder.

Key Features
- AI Tutor & Conversation Practice: Learn languages and academic subjects through natural, human-like conversations powered by AI.
- AI Homework Helper: Get step-by-step assistance with essays, questions, and study tasks—instantly.
- Teacher Tools: Create rubrics, lesson plans, and graded feedback in seconds with educator-specific AI tools.
- Interactive Learning Modules: Access bite-sized video lessons, phonics games, and storytelling adventures for younger learners.

Benefits
- Saves Time for Students & Teachers: Speeds up study, lesson planning, and grading with intelligent automation.
- Boosts Confidence & Autonomy: Learners can practise at their own pace with friendly AI companions, reducing pressure and increasing retention.
- Supports Diverse Learning Needs: Tailors content to individual strengths, whether visual, auditory, or conversational.
- Encourages Playful Engagement: Turns learning into a joyful, curiosity-led journey for all ages.

Use Case
A high school student uses ClassX’s AI Tutor to prepare for a literature exam by practising essay questions, receiving feedback, and exploring key themes. Meanwhile, their teacher uses the AI Rubric Generator to quickly create a grading framework aligned with curriculum standards.
