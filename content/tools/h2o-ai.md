H2O.ai built its reputation on open-source machine learning. H2O-3 is a widely used library for training models on large datasets, with an AutoML feature that tries many algorithms and picks the best. The company's commercial Driverless AI automated feature engineering and model building for business users, and its tools have been used in finance, insurance, and healthcare.

More recently, H2O.ai has focused on generative AI. H2O LLM Studio lets teams fine-tune language models without much code, h2oGPT is an open-source project for chatting with documents, and its enterprise platform lets companies deploy private assistants and agents, including on-premises or in air-gapped environments. It has also released small language models designed to run efficiently.

H2O's open-source tools are free, and enterprise products are sold on custom contracts. The company competes with Databricks, DataRobot, Dataiku, and the major cloud AI platforms, and the broad open-source ecosystem has overtaken some of its early advantages. It remains attractive to organizations that want control over deployment and data. H2O.ai suits data science teams that want open-source flexibility with an enterprise option.
