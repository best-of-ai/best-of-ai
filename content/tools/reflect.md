Reflect aims to be a quick place to capture thoughts and connect them. You write notes, link them to each other with simple syntax, and see backlinks that show where an idea is referenced. Daily notes serve as an inbox, a calendar view ties notes to meetings, and a graph helps visualize connections. The app is built for speed and a clean interface, with sync across devices.

The AI assistant works on your notes. It can summarize a page, rewrite text, brainstorm, and answer questions based on the content you have saved, and voice transcription turns spoken notes into text. Reflect also emphasizes privacy: notes are end-to-end encrypted, which limits what the company can see. Browser extensions and integrations let you save web content and connect with services like Kindle highlights and Readwise.

Reflect is a paid subscription with a free trial, aimed at individuals who want a premium note experience. It does not have the team collaboration, database features, or plugin ecosystem of larger tools, and its focus on Apple platforms may leave other users with a lesser experience. Obsidian and Logseq are local-first alternatives, Notion is broader, Roam Research is the original networked notes tool, and Craft focuses on documents. Reflect suits people who value speed and simplicity.
