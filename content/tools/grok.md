Grok is the chatbot from xAI, Elon Musk's AI company. It is built into X, so you can ask it about a post, a trending topic, or a thread without leaving the feed, and it is also available as a separate app and at grok.com. Its main distinguishing feature is live access to posts on X, which makes it unusually quick at summarizing what people are saying about a breaking story. The tone is a little looser than other assistants, and it will answer some questions that more cautious tools decline.

The product has several modes. A reasoning mode works through math, coding, and logic problems step by step. DeepSearch looks across the web and X and writes up a sourced answer, and image and video generation are available through Grok Imagine. There is a voice mode and a set of companion characters in the mobile app. xAI also sells API access to its models for developers, and the Grok models have scored near the top of several public benchmarks for reasoning and coding.

Grok has a free tier with limited use, and paid plans, either through an X subscription or a dedicated Grok plan, raise limits and unlock the strongest models. The exact split between tiers changes, so check the current pricing. Grok has also drawn criticism for answers that were inaccurate, biased, or offensive in some cases, and for weaker moderation than rival assistants, so treat its answers about news and politics the way you would any social media summary: check the sources. It suits people who already spend time on X and want live context.
