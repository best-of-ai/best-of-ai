ManyChat lets businesses and creators respond to people in direct messages at scale. A visual flow builder defines how a conversation proceeds: someone comments a keyword on an Instagram post, ManyChat sends a DM with a link or a guide, asks questions, collects an email address, and tags the contact. It is widely used by influencers and online businesses to deliver lead magnets, run giveaways, and sell products through chat.

The platform supports several channels from one account, plus a growth toolkit including website widgets, QR codes, and ads that start conversations. Contacts and tags go into a CRM-like list that can be segmented for broadcasts, and integrations send leads to email platforms, spreadsheets, and tools like Shopify and Zapier. AI features help write messages, understand customer questions, and run AI-assisted conversations within the flows.

ManyChat has a free plan for a small number of contacts and paid plans that scale with contacts and features. Costs rise as an audience grows. Platforms such as Meta set the rules for messaging, including windows during which you can respond and restrictions on promotional content, so compliance matters or accounts can be limited. Competitors include Chatfuel, MobileMonkey, Landbot, and Botpress, with Respond.io and Intercom for broader support use.
