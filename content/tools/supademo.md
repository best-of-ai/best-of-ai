Supademo makes product demos quickly. A user records clicks through an app with a browser extension, and Supademo converts them into a step-by-step interactive demo with callouts, which can be edited, branded, and embedded on a site or emailed.

AI adds voiceover narration and translations, and helps write step text. Analytics show who viewed a demo and where they stopped. The tool is also used for onboarding guides and help documentation.

Supademo has a free plan and paid tiers. Demos can go out of date as products change. Alternatives include Arcade, Storylane, Navattic, Scribe, and Guidde. Supademo suits teams that need fast interactive demos.
