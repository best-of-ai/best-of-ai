Perfect365 started as a makeover app. A user takes or uploads a selfie, and face detection places virtual makeup, such as lipstick, eyeshadow, and foundation, or changes hair color. Retouching tools smooth skin and adjust features.

Salons and beauty professionals use virtual try-on to help clients picture a result, which supports decisions and sales. It is also popular for social media photos.

Heavy retouching can distort self-image, so use with care, and check the privacy policy since the app processes face photos. It is free with ads and paid options. Alternatives include YouCam Makeup, Facetune, FaceApp, and Modiface. Perfect365 suits casual users and beauty professionals.
