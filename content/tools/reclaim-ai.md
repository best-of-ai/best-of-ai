Reclaim is a smart scheduling assistant. You tell it your priorities, tasks with deadlines, recurring habits such as lunch or exercise, and preferred meeting hours, and it places them in your calendar. When a new meeting appears, Reclaim moves flexible items around automatically, so your calendar stays workable without constant manual rescheduling.

Several features support teams. Smart one-on-ones find times that work for both participants and adjust over time, scheduling links let others book you at times that fit your priorities, and focus time blocks protect deep work. Integrations pull tasks from tools like Asana, ClickUp, Todoist, Linear, and Jira and schedule them, and analytics show how you spend time.

Reclaim has a free plan with core features and paid plans for teams with more habits, integrations, and analytics. It works with Google Calendar and Outlook, and results depend on keeping tasks and estimates up to date. Alternatives include Motion, which is more automatic and costlier, Clockwise for team calendars, Sunsama and Akiflow for manual planning, and Calendly for booking. Reclaim suits busy professionals and teams who want their calendars managed automatically.
