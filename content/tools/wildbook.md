Wildbook turns photos into population data. Scientists and citizen scientists upload pictures of animals such as whale sharks, giraffes, and sea turtles, and the software recognizes individuals by distinctive markings, such as spot patterns or fin shapes, and links sightings over time.

That reveals movement, survival, and population size without physically tagging animals. The platform supports many species, with separate databases for each, and photos from tourists and social media can contribute.

Wildbook is free for research use and run by a nonprofit. Matching requires good photos, and expert verification remains important. Alternatives include iNaturalist for species identification, MegaDetector for camera traps, Zooniverse projects, and species-specific catalogs. Wildbook suits wildlife researchers and conservationists.
