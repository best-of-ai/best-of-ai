Power Automate connects apps and automates repetitive work. Users build flows that trigger on events, such as an email arriving or a form submission, and perform actions in hundreds of services. It works smoothly with Teams, SharePoint, Outlook, and Dynamics.

AI features extend it. Copilot lets people describe a flow in plain language, AI Builder processes documents and forms, and agent capabilities handle more open-ended tasks. Desktop flows use robotic process automation to control applications that have no API.

Power Automate has plans per user or per flow, with some use included in Microsoft 365 licenses. Governance matters in large organizations. Alternatives include Zapier, Make, UiPath, n8n, and Workato. Power Automate suits organizations built on Microsoft.
