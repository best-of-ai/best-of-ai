Content at Scale launched as a tool for generating complete blog posts. You enter a keyword, a URL, or a video, and the system researches the topic and writes a long article designed to read naturally, with sections, headings, and optimization for search. It was marketed on producing content that would pass AI detection and be useful to readers, which made it popular with SEO agencies and publishers.

The company has rebranded to BrandWell and widened its offering. It now describes a platform for brand growth that includes keyword research, content planning, optimization, and generation, with the long-form writer continuing as one of its core tools. Existing customers kept their accounts, and the older name may still appear on some pages or in billing.

The service is paid, with plans scaled by the number of articles, and pricing has changed over time, so check the current site. AI-written articles still need human review for accuracy, original insight, and brand voice, and relying on detection evasion is a poor strategy because quality and helpfulness matter more to readers and search engines. Alternatives include Frase, Surfer, Jasper, Writesonic, and general assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude. It suits content teams that produce many articles.
