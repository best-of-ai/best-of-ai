Sloyd takes a different approach from mesh generators. Its models are parametric, so a user can generate a chair or a sword and then adjust sliders for size, shape, and style, and get clean, game-ready geometry with proper topology. Real-time generation fits into workflows and game engines.

A library of templates and an API let developers generate assets inside their own tools and games, including at runtime. The aim is to reduce the time artists spend on common props and set dressing.

Sloyd has a free tier and paid plans. Its assets suit stylized and low-poly games more than photoreal ones. Alternatives include Meshy, Tripo, Rodin, Luma AI, and Kaedim. Sloyd suits game developers needing quick, editable props.
