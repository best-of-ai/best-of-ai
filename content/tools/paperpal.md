Paperpal is designed for researchers. It works in a web editor, a Word add-in, and a browser extension, checking grammar, clarity, and tone against academic conventions. Suggestions are tuned to scholarly writing, where general tools may flag correct technical phrasing.

Beyond language editing, it offers help with paraphrasing, summarizing, and reference management, plus a literature search feature that finds papers and answers questions with citations. A journal submission check reviews manuscripts against common requirements before submission.

Paperpal has a free tier and paid plans. Many journals have policies on AI use in writing, so authors should check and disclose where required. Alternatives include Grammarly, Writefull, Trinka, Wordvice AI, and QuillBot. Paperpal suits students and researchers preparing manuscripts.
