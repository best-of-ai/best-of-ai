Labelbox helps AI teams turn raw data into labeled datasets. Its tools let annotators draw boxes and masks on images and video, tag text, transcribe audio, and rate model responses. Project management features assign work, review quality, and measure agreement between labelers, so teams can catch mistakes early. The platform supports both in-house labelers and external workforces.

The company has focused on generative AI. Tools support reinforcement learning from human feedback and model evaluation, where experts compare and rank responses, and a labeling service provides specialist annotators for domains such as coding, math, and medicine. Model-assisted labeling uses a model to pre-label data so humans only correct it, and data curation tools help find the most valuable examples. Integrations connect with cloud storage and ML pipelines.

Labelbox has a free tier for small projects and paid enterprise plans based on usage, plus managed labeling services priced per task. It competes in a crowded field with Scale AI, Surge AI, Appen, V7, Encord, and open-source options like CVAT and Label Studio. Choosing a platform depends on data types, privacy needs, and whether you need software, a workforce, or both. Label quality, not volume, usually drives model quality.
