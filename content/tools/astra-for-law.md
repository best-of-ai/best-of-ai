Astra for Law packages OpenAI's model for legal work. Rather than a separate model, it pairs a general model with a dedicated search index of U.S. case law, statutes, regulations, and court rules, and instructions shaped for legal analysis and drafting. The goal is research answers that are grounded in sources a lawyer can check.

Access has been limited. OpenAI offered it first to selected firms through a trusted access program, with plans to make it available to legal technology companies through the API so they can build it into their own products. Partner plugins connect it to tools used in legal practice, such as document review, practice management, and document management systems.

OpenAI reported its own benchmark results showing better performance than the base model with web search alone, but those are company figures. Legal AI has a record of invented citations, so every authority must be verified by a lawyer. Alternatives include Harvey, Legora, Thomson Reuters CoCounsel, Lexis+ AI, and Claude for legal work. Astra for Law suits law firms that want a general AI lab's model tuned for legal research.
