SwiftAlerts gathers market data into a structure that agents can query. Instead of hunting through separate sources, an agent can ask about relationships between trades, holdings, filings, and sentiment, and get connected answers.

The idea fits the rise of AI agents that research and monitor markets. Reliable data is essential, since trading decisions on wrong information lose money, and nothing from such a tool is financial advice.

Independent information is limited, so check data sources, update speed, and terms. SwiftAlerts is free to start. Alternatives include Unusual Whales, Quiver Quantitative, Benzinga, Polygon.io, and Financial Datasets. SwiftAlerts suits developers building market agents, with caution.
