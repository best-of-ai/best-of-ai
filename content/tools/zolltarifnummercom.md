Zolltarifnummer.com is an AI-powered platform that makes customs classification simple, fast, and reliable. The tool finds, verifies, and explains the correct 11-digit German customs tariff number (Zolltarifnummer), including HS, KN, TARIC, and national codes, in just seconds.

With flexible input options, you can type a product name, upload an image, provide a shop link, or submit invoices, PDFs, and even bulk CSV/Excel files. Our AI analyzes the data, asks clarifying questions if needed, and delivers an accurate tariff code with a legally sound explanation based on customs regulations (AV 1–6).

The system is directly connected to EU TARIC, German EZT, and vZTA databases, ensuring that all results are always up to date and compliant. Each classification comes with a full product description, duty rates, taxes, and official measures, ready for export as PDF, CSV, or Excel.

For importers, exporters, and e-commerce businesses, Zolltarifnummer.com saves hours of manual research, prevents costly misclassifications, and scales effortlessly to thousands of products. Trusted by hundreds of companies, it provides over 99% accuracy, maximum transparency, and compliance peace of mind — all at a fraction of the cost of traditional customs agents or consultants.
