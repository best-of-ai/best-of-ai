It includes a large library of instruments and loops, plus tools like Session Players, which generate drummer, bass, and keyboard parts. Stem splitter separates tracks from mixed audio. Dolby Atmos mixing is supported.

It is a one-time purchase on Mac and a subscription on iPad.

Ableton Live and FL Studio are alternatives. Logic Pro suits musicians in the Apple ecosystem.
