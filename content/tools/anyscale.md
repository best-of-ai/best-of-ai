Anyscale was founded by the creators of Ray, a framework that makes it easier to spread Python code across many machines. Ray is widely used in AI for tasks such as distributed model training, large-scale data processing, reinforcement learning, and serving models, and it is the engine behind a number of big AI systems. Anyscale offers a managed platform that handles cluster setup, scaling, and monitoring, so teams do not have to operate the infrastructure themselves.

The platform provides optimized runtimes, job scheduling, and tools for development and production. It supports training and fine-tuning of large models, running batch inference over large datasets, and online serving with autoscaling, with a focus on cost efficiency and utilization of GPUs. It runs on major clouds and in a customer's own cloud account, and works with libraries such as PyTorch and vLLM.

Anyscale is sold on usage-based and enterprise terms, and Ray itself is free and open source under the Apache 2.0 license, so teams can run it without Anyscale. Alternatives include Databricks, AWS SageMaker, Google Vertex AI, Modal for serverless GPU compute, and Kubernetes-based stacks with Kubeflow. Anyscale suits ML teams that want Ray without the operational burden.
