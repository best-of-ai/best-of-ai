Unit21 is a risk and compliance platform. It ingests transaction and customer data, flags suspicious activity with rules and machine learning, and routes alerts to analysts through case management. Teams build and adjust rules in a no-code interface.

The platform covers fraud prevention, anti-money laundering monitoring, and onboarding checks, and generates reports such as suspicious activity filings. AI agents help summarize cases and speed investigations.

Unit21 is sold to fintechs, banks, and marketplaces on custom pricing. Alerts need tuning to avoid overload. Alternatives include Sardine, Feedzai, Alloy, ComplyAdvantage, and Actimize. Unit21 suits risk teams at financial companies.
