BeFunky is a browser-based editor for people who want quick results without learning Photoshop. The photo editor has sliders for exposure and color, crop and resize tools, and a large set of filters and effects. A collage maker arranges photos into layouts, and the graphic designer tool offers templates for social media posts, flyers, and cards. Everything runs in a web page, with mobile access too.

AI tools handle common edits. An enhancer sharpens and improves photos, a background remover cuts out subjects, and retouching tools smooth skin and remove blemishes. Artistic effects turn photos into paintings or cartoons, and an upscaler increases resolution. Newer features add AI image generation and a way to remove objects. Many of these are one-click operations, which is the main appeal.

BeFunky has a free tier with a limited set of tools, and a paid plan that removes limits, adds premium effects, and increases storage. It is a good fit for hobbyists, small businesses, and bloggers who need simple edits. For serious photography, Lightroom and Photoshop go much deeper, and Canva has a wider design template library. Photopea is a free browser alternative that mimics Photoshop's interface, and Pixlr offers a similar quick-edit approach.
