Medisafe helps people take medicines as prescribed. Users enter their medications, get reminders at the right time, and log doses. If a dose is missed, the app follows up, and caregivers can be notified. Interaction checks warn about conflicts between drugs when a new one is added.

Programs with pharmacies, health plans, and drugmakers build on the app, offering support for particular conditions and tracking adherence. Features include refill reminders, health measurement tracking, and educational content. AI helps personalize reminders and detect patterns.

Medisafe is free for consumers, with sponsored programs. Reminders are no substitute for medical advice, and users should talk to their pharmacist or doctor about interactions. Alternatives include MyTherapy, Round Health, CareClinic, and Apple Health medication tracking. Medisafe suits people managing several medications.
