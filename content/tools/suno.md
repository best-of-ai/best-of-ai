Suno produces complete songs — not background loops or stems, but full productions with sung vocals, layered instruments, and mastered audio — from a text prompt that takes about thirty seconds to write. Describe the genre, mood, energy, and a theme or lyrical concept, and Suno handles arrangement, production, and vocal performance automatically. The speed and completeness of the output makes it unlike any music tool that came before it.

What Suno does particularly well is genre flexibility. Prompt it for a 1970s soul ballad, a hyper-pop track with distorted 808s, a lo-fi hip hop instrumental, or a jazz trio playing a minor key standard — and it delivers something that genuinely fits each description, with appropriate instrumentation and production choices. You can provide custom lyrics in the prompt to control what the AI sings, or let it write lyrics based on your thematic direction. The interface is simple enough that people with no music training use it daily.

The free tier provides 50 song credits per day for personal, non-commercial use. Pro at $8/month and Premier at $24/month increase limits and unlock commercial rights to the music you generate. Suno is available as a web app and integrates with Microsoft Copilot. The commercial licensing question is the biggest consideration for professional users — what you can legally publish depends on your plan tier, so reading the terms before using generated tracks commercially is essential.
