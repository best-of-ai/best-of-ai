MoEngage is built for brands with mobile apps and large user bases. It collects behavior data such as app opens, purchases, and browsing, then lets marketers segment users and design campaigns across channels. Journeys can be built visually, so a user who abandons a cart gets a push notification, then an email if they don't respond.

Analytics show how campaigns perform and how users move through funnels. AI features predict who is likely to churn or buy, choose the best time to send each message, and suggest content, and a generative assistant helps write copy. The platform integrates with data warehouses and other tools, and operates worldwide, with a strong presence in Asia.

MoEngage is sold on custom contracts, scaled by users and messages, and is used by retail, fintech, media, and travel companies. It competes in a crowded category and takes setup work to integrate. Alternatives include CleverTap, Braze, Iterable, Klaviyo for e-commerce, and Customer.io. MoEngage suits consumer brands running cross-channel campaigns.
