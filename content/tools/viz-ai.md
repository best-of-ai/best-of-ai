Viz.ai is known for its stroke software. When a patient has a CT scan that may show a large vessel occlusion, a blockage in a major brain artery, the AI analyzes the images within minutes and alerts the stroke team on their phones, with images they can view immediately. Speeding up that notification can cut the time to treatment, which matters in stroke, where each minute affects outcomes.

The platform has expanded beyond stroke. It supports other conditions, including pulmonary embolism, aortic disease, and heart conditions, and uses AI to identify patients who may be eligible for treatment or follow-up. A care coordination layer links hospitals, so specialists at a distance can review cases, communicate, and arrange transfers. Several of its algorithms have received regulatory clearance in the United States.

Viz.ai is sold to hospitals and health systems under enterprise contracts, and its tools support rather than replace clinicians. Performance depends on image quality and workflow adoption, and false alerts or missed cases can occur. Competitors and peers include RapidAI, Aidoc, Brainomix, and Rad AI, along with imaging vendors' own tools. Viz.ai is one of the most widely deployed clinical AI platforms for emergency conditions.
