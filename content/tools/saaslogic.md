Saaslogic is a subscription management and revenue billing platform that offers extended development support for all SaaS products. Saaslogic integrates seamlessly with software products to automate nearly 90% of its subscription and user management functions, enabling it to respond and adapt effectively to market needs. The platform takes care of integrating all the non-core SaaS functions of a product, allowing businesses to focus on developing their core features. Apart from subscription management, saaslogic offers an intuitive pricing package composer, user profile management, SaaS reports and analytics, license management, role-based security, tax calculation, Single Sign-Ons for products, and much more.
