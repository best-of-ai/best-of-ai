It handles multitrack recording, comping, and large session management. Surround and Dolby Atmos mixing are supported. It works with Avid and third-party interfaces and plug-ins, and has added assisted features such as stem separation.

It is sold by subscription or perpetual license, with a free entry version.

Logic Pro and Reaper are alternatives. Pro Tools suits sound engineers in professional studios.
