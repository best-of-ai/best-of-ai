SMMRY is an advanced AI text summarization tool designed to combat information overload for busy professionals and students. Born from a collective desire to give people their time back, SMMRY transforms lengthy content into clear, actionable summaries so users can focus on what matters. Our powerful AI engine analyzes complex text from various sources—articles, documents, books, websites, videos, and podcasts—to extract key insights while maintaining critical context.

With customizable summary lengths, multi-format support, and personal libraries, SMMRY adapts to your specific needs. Whether you're a student tackling research papers, a knowledge worker managing industry news, or a professional handling project documentation, our tool helps you learn faster and work smarter. Users can create up to 100 summaries per day with the Advanced plan, customize summary length, and organize everything in their personal library.

SMMRY empowers users to break free from information overload, allowing them to spend more time innovating, solving problems, or simply taking a well-deserved break. Our mission is to simplify complex content and sharpen understanding, ultimately helping you learn, teach, and innovate without drowning in information.
