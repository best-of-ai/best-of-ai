Transistor is designed for people who run more than one podcast. A single account can host unlimited shows, which suits agencies, companies, and networks, and plans are based on downloads and team size instead of per-show fees. Episodes distribute to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and other apps through an RSS feed Transistor manages.

Other features address professional needs. Private podcasts let companies share internal or members-only audio, embeddable players and a branded website help promote shows, and detailed analytics show downloads and audience. Collaboration features give team members access to specific shows, and integrations connect with Zapier and other tools. AI tools support transcripts and episode copy.

Transistor is paid with a free trial, and plans depend on monthly downloads. It has fewer built-in monetization options than some platforms, and no free tier. Alternatives include Buzzsprout, Captivate, Libsyn, Podbean, Spotify for Creators, and Simplecast. Transistor suits agencies, companies, and creators with several shows.
