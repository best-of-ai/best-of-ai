Holistiplan helps financial advisors use tax returns in planning. An advisor uploads a client's return, and the software extracts the figures and generates a summary showing income, deductions, brackets, and observations. It highlights ideas worth discussing, such as Roth conversions, capital gains harvesting, or the timing of donations.

Scenarios let advisors model the effect of a strategy on taxes. Reports are designed to be shared with clients, helping advisors show value beyond investments. AI assists with reading documents and drafting talking points.

Holistiplan is sold by subscription to advisory firms. It provides planning insights, not tax filing or legal advice, and advisors must verify results. Alternatives include Corvee, Income Lab, Mind My Money, and tax software for preparers. Holistiplan suits advisors who integrate tax planning into their service.
