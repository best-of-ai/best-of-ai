Lumen5 is built for people who have words but no video. You paste in a link or text, and the tool breaks it into scenes, picks suggested footage and images from its library, and adds captions and music. You then edit the storyboard, swap clips, and adjust colors, fonts, and timing in a simple editor.

The service suits content marketing teams, publishers, and small businesses that want to turn articles into social clips quickly. Brand settings keep logos and colors consistent, and templates provide formats for different platforms and aspect ratios. AI can summarize text into short scene copy and suggest visuals.

Lumen5 has a free plan with watermarks and limits and paid plans for higher resolution and more media. Videos can look similar to each other because they rely on stock footage, so adding your own clips helps. Alternatives include Pictory, InVideo, Canva, VEED, Descript, and Synthesia for avatar video. Lumen5 suits marketers repurposing written content.
