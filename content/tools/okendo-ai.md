Okendo helps online stores build trust with reviews. After a purchase, it asks customers for reviews with photos and video, displays them on product pages, and syndicates them to Google. Surveys and attributes gather more detail about the customer and product fit.

Additional tools include a loyalty and referral program and a customer data layer. AI summarizes reviews into themes, analyzes sentiment, and suggests replies, helping merchants learn what customers like and dislike. Integrations connect with email, helpdesk, and ads tools.

Okendo has a free tier and paid plans by orders. It focuses on Shopify. Alternatives include Yotpo, Judge.me, Stamped.io, Loox, and Junip. Okendo suits growing Shopify brands.
