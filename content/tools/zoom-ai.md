AI Companion is the assistant built into Zoom's meeting, chat, phone, and document tools. Its most used feature is the meeting summary: it listens to the call, produces a transcript, and writes up the main points and action items, which it can share with attendees right after the meeting ends. If you join late, it can catch you up on what was said. It can also answer questions about a meeting in progress, such as what was decided on a topic a few minutes ago.

Beyond meetings, it helps with writing and organizing. It drafts chat replies and emails, summarizes long chat threads, and works in Zoom Docs, where it can create and edit documents. A web-based version can search across your meetings, chats, and connected apps to answer questions. Admins can switch features on or off, and Zoom says it does not use customer audio, video, chat, or documents to train its models. Some customization options and agent features are sold as paid add-ons.

For paid Zoom Workplace accounts, the core AI Companion features come at no additional cost, which is a real contrast with competitors that charge per seat for an AI add-on. Free users get limited access, and a paid standalone option exists for people who do not otherwise subscribe. Its main limit is that it works best on content that lives in Zoom, so teams that run meetings in Google Meet or Microsoft Teams get less from it. Otter, Fireflies, and Fathom are common third-party alternatives that join any meeting platform.
