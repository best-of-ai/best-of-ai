Qventus works on hospital operations. It connects to scheduling and records systems, forecasts demand in the emergency room, operating rooms, and inpatient units, and flags bottlenecks like discharges that are running late or beds that will not be ready. It recommends actions for staff.

Automation is part of the pitch. The platform can complete tasks like reaching out to patients for scheduling or following up on referrals, and AI agents handle routine work in areas such as surgical scheduling and call centers. The goal is to improve access while easing staff burden.

Qventus is sold to hospitals and health systems on enterprise contracts. Results depend on integration with local workflows and clinician buy-in. Alternatives include LeanTaaS, Hospital IQ, Epic's tools, GE HealthCare Command Center, and Notable. Qventus suits health systems working on capacity.
