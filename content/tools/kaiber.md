Kaiber is known for stylized, artistic video. A user supplies a prompt, an image, or a video clip, chooses a style, and the platform generates moving footage in that look. Audio reactivity lets visuals respond to a music track, which made it a favorite for music videos and live visuals.

The platform has grown into a broader creative studio with a canvas for combining generated images and video and with access to several underlying models. Artists use it to experiment with motion and aesthetics rather than realistic footage, and it has been featured in music video projects.

Kaiber has a free trial and paid plans with credits. The AI video field has become crowded, and clip lengths and consistency still have limits. Alternatives include Runway, Pika, Luma, Higgsfield, and freebeat. Kaiber suits musicians and artists making stylized motion.
