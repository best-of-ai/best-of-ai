Derwent Innovation serves IP professionals who need thorough patent research. It provides search across patent documents from many countries, with tools to construct complex queries, filter results, and analyze trends. Its distinguishing asset is the Derwent World Patents Index, in which Clarivate's editors rewrite patent abstracts in clear language and standardize names and classifications, making it easier to find relevant documents despite varying terminology.

Analytics and workflow tools support decisions about filing, licensing, and litigation. Users can visualize technology landscapes, compare competitors' portfolios, and track legal status, and AI features help with semantic search, summaries, and finding similar patents. Teams collaborate on search results and export reports. Clarivate also offers related products for trademark and scientific research.

Derwent Innovation is sold by subscription to corporations, law firms, and research institutions, and pricing is high and not public. For quick or informal searches, free tools such as Google Patents, Espacenet, and Lens.org may be enough. Competitors include PatSnap, Questel Orbit, LexisNexis PatentSight, and Cipher. As with any patent search, critical decisions should involve a qualified patent attorney. Derwent suits professional IP teams.
