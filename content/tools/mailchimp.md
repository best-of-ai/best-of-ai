Mailchimp is one of the best-known email marketing services. You build an audience list, design emails with a drag-and-drop editor or templates, and send campaigns with reporting on opens and clicks. Automations send welcome series, abandoned cart reminders, and birthday messages, and signup forms and landing pages help grow the list. It also supports basic CRM features, social posting, and simple websites.

AI is built into content and targeting. Generative tools help write subject lines and email text, and build designs from a brand's assets. Predictive segmentation estimates customer behavior, such as likelihood to buy, and send-time optimization chooses delivery times. Intuit's AI assistant brings help with questions about campaigns. Integrations connect with Shopify, WooCommerce, and hundreds of other apps.

Mailchimp has a free plan with limits and paid plans priced by contact count, and costs rise as lists grow. Over time, free-tier limits have tightened, which pushed some users to alternatives. It's easy to start but can feel expensive and limited for complex automation. Brevo, MailerLite, Klaviyo for e-commerce, ActiveCampaign for automation, and Constant Contact are common alternatives. Mailchimp suits small businesses and creators who want a familiar, easy-to-use tool.
