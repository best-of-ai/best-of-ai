Phrase helps companies translate software and content into many languages. Developers connect repositories and design tools, and new text is sent to translators or machine translation, then returned to the product when approved. Translation memory reuses earlier work and glossaries keep terminology consistent.

The platform brings together several products that grew from a merger: a translation management system, a string management tool for software teams, and a computer-assisted translation editor. AI translation engines and quality estimation reduce the amount of human review needed for lower-risk content.

Phrase is sold on subscription to enterprises and mid-sized companies. Machine translation still needs human review for brand voice and technical accuracy. Alternatives include Lokalise, Crowdin, Transifex, Smartling, and memoQ. Phrase suits companies localizing software and content at scale.
