Cal.com is an alternative to Calendly built around openness. Users connect their calendars, define when they are free, and share a link that lets others book a slot. Event types handle one-on-one meetings, group sessions, and team round-robin assignments, and bookings sync to video tools and calendars.

Because the code is open source, companies can self-host it and customize it, or embed scheduling inside their own products with APIs and components. Workflows send reminders and follow-ups, payments can be collected at booking, and routing forms direct prospects to the right person. AI assistants can handle scheduling conversations.

Cal.com has a free plan for individuals and paid tiers for teams and enterprise features. Self-hosting needs technical skill. Alternatives include Calendly, SavvyCal, Acuity Scheduling, Reclaim, and Microsoft Bookings. Cal.com suits teams that want control and flexibility in scheduling.
