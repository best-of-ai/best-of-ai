Ashby brings together tools that recruiting teams often buy separately. The core is an applicant tracking system for jobs, candidates, and interview plans. Around it sit scheduling that coordinates interviewers automatically, a CRM for sourcing and nurturing talent, and an analytics layer that shows pipeline health, time to hire, and source effectiveness.

AI features handle repetitive work, such as summarizing candidates, drafting outreach, and taking notes in interviews. Structured scorecards and interview plans promote consistent evaluation, and reporting is built into the product rather than added on, which analytics-minded recruiting teams like.

Ashby is sold by subscription to growth-stage and larger companies, with pricing by quote. Its depth can be more than very small companies need. AI in hiring is regulated in some jurisdictions, so teams should understand rules on bias and automated decisions. Alternatives include Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Workable, and Gem. Ashby suits startups and scale-ups that want data-driven hiring.
