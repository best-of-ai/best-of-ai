Genesis R&D is a long-standing tool for food product development. A formulator enters ingredients and amounts, and the software calculates nutrients using a large food database, then generates a nutrition facts panel and an ingredient list formatted for regulatory requirements in different countries.

It also helps with design choices. Users can compare formulas, adjust ingredients to hit targets for calories, sodium, or protein, check claims such as low fat or high fiber, and flag allergens. These features reduce the risk of labeling errors, which can lead to recalls.

ESHA, now part of Trustwell, sells Genesis by license to food manufacturers, labs, and consultants. Labels still require review by regulatory experts, and lab testing can verify values. Alternatives include Nutritionix for recipes, FoodWorks, Formulator, and ingredient database services. Genesis R&D suits food companies preparing compliant labels.
