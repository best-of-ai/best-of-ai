FairPact AI finds contract "gotchas". Contracts are full of terms that could cost you money, trap you in bad deals, or create unexpected obligations. FairPact AI shows you exactly what scenarios could go wrong and gives you the right questions to ask before you sign.

Key Features
- Customized Scenarios: Upload any contract, and FairPact will find plausible scenarios that you might face, and tell you how this contract might affect them. That way you can understand what you're signing and what it means for you.
- Conversation Questions: FairPact generates questions you can ask the other party, or your lawyer, to start conversations and put you in a better negotiating position.

Benefits
- Cure Contract Anxiety: Sign with confidence, not anxiety. No legal jargon or confusing explanations, so you know exactly what you're agreeing to.
- Save Money Now: Turn expensive lawyer meetings into focused strategy sessions. Know the problem areas upfront so you're paying for expert advice on solutions, not paying premium rates for someone to simply read the contract to you.
- Save Money Later: Catch problems before they catch you. Spot the clauses that could cost you money, limit your options, or create unexpected obligations down the road.
- Negotiate Better: Turn expensive lawyer meetings into focused strategy sessions. Know the problem areas upfront so you're paying for expert advice on solutions, not paying premium rates for someone to simply read the contract to you.
