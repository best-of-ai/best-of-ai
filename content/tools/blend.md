Blend builds software for lenders. Instead of paper forms and email, borrowers apply on a digital platform that collects information, connects to bank accounts and payroll sources to verify income and assets, and tracks the status of the application. Loan officers and processors see the file in a shared workflow.

Automation cuts the manual work in underwriting preparation. The platform pulls data from verified sources, checks documents, and flags missing or inconsistent items so staff can fix problems early. It covers mortgages, home equity, and consumer lending products, and integrates with loan origination systems and credit providers.

Blend is sold to banks, credit unions, and mortgage companies on enterprise contracts. Mortgage lending is cyclical, and the platform's business depends on lenders' volume. Competitors include nCino, Encompass from ICE Mortgage Technology, Finastra, and Roostify (now part of Sagent). Blend suits financial institutions modernizing loan applications.
