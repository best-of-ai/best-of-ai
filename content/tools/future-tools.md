Future Tools is a directory of AI products maintained by Matt Wolfe, who covers AI on YouTube. Instead of accepting every submission automatically, the site reviews tools before listing them, which keeps the collection more selective than open directories.

Visitors browse by category, such as writing, video, productivity, and coding, or sort by what has been popular, and the site highlights new additions. Each listing has a short description and a link to the tool. A newsletter shares updates.

The site is free to use. Curation reflects editorial choices, and listings may include sponsored placements, so check details on each tool's site. Similar resources include Futurepedia, There's An AI For That, Aixploria, Dang.ai, and Best of AI. Future Tools suits people who want a curated starting point for AI tools.
