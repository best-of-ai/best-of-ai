Recharge enables subscribe-and-save models for online stores. Merchants on Shopify and other platforms set up subscription products, let customers manage deliveries and payments in a portal, and bill on schedule. It is common in consumables such as food, supplements, and beauty products.

Analytics show retention, churn, and revenue, and tools help reduce cancellations. Smart payment retries recover failed charges, and AI models predict which subscribers may leave so merchants can intervene with offers or pauses.

Recharge is paid, with plans by volume and a fee on transactions. Costs add up for growing stores. Alternatives include Skio, Loop Subscriptions, Smartrr, Bold Subscriptions, and Shopify's own subscription tools. Recharge suits e-commerce brands selling by subscription.
