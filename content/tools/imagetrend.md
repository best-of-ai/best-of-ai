ImageTrend makes software for first responders. Paramedics and EMTs record patient information, treatments, and outcomes on a tablet or computer, producing the report used for handoff to hospitals, billing, and quality review. The data also feeds state and national registries.

The company also provides tools for state EMS offices, trauma and stroke registries, and hospital data collection, as well as analytics for system performance. Newer tools use AI to help reduce documentation time and check data quality.

ImageTrend is sold to agencies and governments on subscription or contract. Data standards and state requirements shape how it is used. Alternatives include ESO, Zoll, Traumasoft, and Emergency Reporting. ImageTrend suits EMS agencies and public health bodies.
