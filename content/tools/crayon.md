Crayon helps companies keep an eye on their rivals. It continuously monitors thousands of data points, such as changes to competitors' web pages, pricing, messaging, reviews, hiring patterns, and press coverage, and alerts the team when something meaningful changes. Instead of manually checking competitor sites, product marketers receive a feed of updates they can review and share.

The platform turns the data into usable materials. Teams build battlecards that give sales reps talking points against each competitor, and distribute them in the CRM or in chat tools such as Slack. Analytics track which competitive content is used in deals, and AI summarizes changes, drafts updates, and filters noise. Win-loss insights connect competitive information to outcomes.

Crayon is sold to B2B companies on annual contracts with custom pricing, and it is typically used by product marketing and sales enablement teams. The value depends on how well the team curates and acts on the information, and tracking is limited to public data. Competitors include Klue, Kompyte, Similarweb for traffic data, and AlphaSense for broader market intelligence. Crayon suits companies selling in competitive markets with active sales teams.
