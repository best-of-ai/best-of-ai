Soundraw makes custom background music for creators. You select parameters such as mood, genre, theme, tempo, and length, and the AI generates a number of options. You can then adjust the track in an editor, changing the intensity of different sections and instruments to match the pacing of a video, and download the result. This is more flexible than browsing a fixed stock library.

It's aimed at content creators, marketers, filmmakers, and developers who need affordable music. The subscription offers unlimited downloads and a license for use on social platforms and in commercial projects, with protections against copyright claims on the creator's own content. The tracks are made from music produced by in-house artists, and Soundraw positions the training data as licensed.

Soundraw is a paid subscription with limited free previewing. It's meant for functional background music and does not produce songs with vocals, so it differs from Suno and Udio. Check the license terms if you plan to distribute the music itself. Competitors include AIVA, Beatoven.ai, Mubert, Loudly, and stock libraries like Epidemic Sound and Artlist. Soundraw suits video creators who need many tracks that fit their edit.
