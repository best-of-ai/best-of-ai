Meme Generator, at memegenerator.net, lets users pick a template, add top and bottom text, and share the result. Its library includes classic image macros and newer formats, and users can upload their own images. A gallery shows popular memes made with the tool.

The AI features help with captions: a user chooses a template and the tool proposes text, which is useful for getting past the blank caption box. Sharing links and downloads are straightforward.

The site is free with ads and optional upgrades. Quality and moderation vary with user-made content, and memes using copyrighted images carry usual caveats for commercial use. Alternatives include Imgflip, Kapwing, Canva, and Mematic. Meme Generator suits people making quick, classic-style memes.
