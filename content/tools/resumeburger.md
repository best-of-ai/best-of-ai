ResumeBurger is an AI-powered résumé and cover letter enhancement platform designed to help job seekers create standout applications with minimal effort. Whether you're applying for your first job or aiming for a career upgrade, our intelligent system ensures your documents meet industry standards and hiring expectations.

With our platform, users can:

- Upload a résumé in PDF format, and our AI refines its structure, clarity, and impact to better align with job descriptions and industry expectations.
- Generate tailored cover letters in seconds, ensuring each application is customized for the specific role.
- Edit AI-generated résumés and cover letters directly in a powerful, user-friendly text editor (Tiptap).
- Download the final versions in a clean, recruiter-approved layout as high-quality PDFs.
- Save and manage multiple résumé versions for different job applications, making it easier to tailor documents for various roles.

By automating time-consuming edits and formatting tasks, ResumeBurger helps job seekers submit standout applications effortlessly. Whether you're entering the workforce or advancing your career, our AI-driven platform enables you to craft professional, job-ready documents in minutes.
