Intuo helps salespeople prepare. It tracks news and events about accounts, markets, and competitors, filters what matters to the user's deals, and delivers a short brief with suggested conversation starters. The goal is to replace time spent searching the web before each call.

Account executives and sales leaders use it to stay informed about prospects and to find reasons to reach out. The briefs adapt to the seller's focus areas, and some features suggest actions or talking points tied to the signals.

Intuo has a free tier and paid plans. Independent information is limited, and AI-selected news may miss details only a rep would know. Alternatives include Gong, ZoomInfo, 6sense, Clay, and Google Alerts. Intuo suits sales reps who want daily account intelligence.
