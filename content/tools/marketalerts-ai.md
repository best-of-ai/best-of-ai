MarketAlerts.ai watches the market on behalf of its users. Investors list their holdings, and the service monitors news, filings, price moves, and other events, sending alerts when something relevant happens and summarizing why it might matter. Some alerts include AI-generated signals about possible actions.

Such tools save time for people who cannot follow the market all day, but they carry risks. AI-generated trade signals are not reliable predictions, markets are unpredictable, and acting on automated signals can lose money. Nothing here is financial advice.

MarketAlerts.ai has a free tier and paid plans. Independent information is limited, so verify claims about performance and data sources. Alternatives include Seeking Alpha alerts, TipRanks, Benzinga Pro, Finviz, and brokerage alert features. MarketAlerts.ai suits retail investors who want automated monitoring, with caution about signals.
