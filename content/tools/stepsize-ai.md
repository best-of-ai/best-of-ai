Stepsize addressed the problem of technical debt that never gets recorded. Developers flag issues directly in code while they work, and Stepsize collects them in a backlog that can be prioritized and linked to issue trackers. Managers see where debt concentrates and plan time to address it.

The company has since introduced Stepsize AI, a product that generates reports on development progress from engineering tools, and its materials describe AI collaboration for software projects. Teams should check which products are currently offered.

Stepsize had a free tier and paid plans. Some teams moved to other methods, such as tracking debt in their existing issue trackers. Alternatives include Jira, Linear, SonarQube, CodeScene, and Swimm. Stepsize suits engineering teams managing technical debt.
