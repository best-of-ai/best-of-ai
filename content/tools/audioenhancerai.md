AudioEnhancer.ai is a browser-based tool that improves the clarity of your audio and video recordings using AI. You upload a file (or record live), and the system removes background noise, reduces echo, balances volume levels, and isolates vocals. It also handles issues like plosives, sibilance, and hum so voices (or instrumental parts) come through clean and intelligible.

It’s useful for creators, podcasters, teachers, video producers, or anyone who wants better sound without complicated software. Because everything runs in the cloud, there’s no need for powerful hardware — just upload, let the AI do its work, and download the improved version.
