Kinaxis helps large manufacturers and retailers plan and respond. Its platform brings demand, supply, inventory, and capacity into one model that updates together, rather than in separate steps, so planners can test the effect of a disruption or a decision in minutes.

Planners run what-if scenarios, see the impact on service and cost, and collaborate across functions. Machine learning improves demand sensing and supply risk detection, and newer AI agents help summarize issues and recommend actions. Customers include companies in high tech, life sciences, automotive, and consumer goods.

Kinaxis is sold on enterprise subscriptions and implementations take time. Competitors include o9 Solutions, Blue Yonder, SAP Integrated Business Planning, Oracle, and Coupa's supply chain tools. Kinaxis suits large companies with complex supply networks.
