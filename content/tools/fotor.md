Fotor covers everyday editing for people who do not want Photoshop. In the photo editor, you crop, adjust color and light, apply filters and effects, and retouch portraits by smoothing skin and removing blemishes. A collage maker and a design studio offer templates for social posts, posters, and cards. Everything works in a browser, with matching apps for phones and computers.

AI tools handle the harder steps. One-click enhancement improves exposure and sharpness, background removal makes cutouts, an object remover erases distractions, and an upscaler enlarges small pictures. Newer features include text-to-image generation, avatars, and generative fill for extending or changing parts of an image. Fotor also has an AI video editor for simple clips.

Fotor offers a free tier with watermarks on some features and limited downloads, and a Pro plan that removes limits. It is more approachable than Lightroom or Photoshop but less powerful, and results from AI tools vary. BeFunky, Canva, Pixlr, and Photoroom are close alternatives, with Canva stronger for design and Photoroom for product photos. Fotor suits casual users, small businesses, and content creators who need quick edits and templates.
