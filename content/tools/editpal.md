Editpal is an AI‑powered image editor that lets you transform photos with just a few words. You can easily replace backgrounds, change styles, adjust poses, add or remove objects, and merge multiple images — while keeping characters and objects consistent across edits. No design skills required; simply type what you want, and Editpal makes it happen.

One of Editpal’s standout features is consistent character and object rendering — even after multiple edits, people and items keep their look, expression, and details. The tool also supports multi‑image fusion, blending objects from different pictures into one realistic scene while keeping visual logic intact.

Editpal understands more than just photos — it can read sketches or follow detailed instructions to produce teaching diagrams, concept visuals, marketing assets, or creative artwork. From product photos to storyboards, Editpal helps anyone turn ideas into polished visuals, fast.
