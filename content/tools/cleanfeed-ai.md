Cleanfeed lets people record or broadcast audio over the internet without installing software. A host creates a session, guests join by link in a browser, and the platform streams audio with low delay and records each participant on a separate track. It is popular with radio and podcast teams who need dependable quality.

The audio chain is a focus. It offers a noise gate, level controls, and processing options that tidy up voices, plus connections for studio equipment and phone callers. Producers can monitor and route audio, record backups, and download multitrack files. A newer version improves collaboration and integration with video.

Cleanfeed has a free tier and paid plans with more features and recording capacity. It concentrates on audio, so video-heavy shows may want other tools. Alternatives include Riverside, Zencastr, SquadCast, Source-Connect, and Ringr. Cleanfeed suits audio professionals and podcasters.
