Apollo gives sales teams a place to find and contact prospects. Users search a database of people and companies by title, industry, size, and technology used, then add leads to email sequences, calling lists, and LinkedIn tasks. A browser extension finds contact details as you browse the web.

The platform includes tools for the whole outbound workflow: email verification, scheduling, a dialer, meeting booking, analytics, and integrations with CRMs like Salesforce and HubSpot. AI features help write messages, score leads, and automate steps, and workflows can enrich existing records to keep a CRM current.

Apollo has a free tier with limited credits and paid plans by user and usage. Contact data in any database can be outdated, and bulk outreach must follow privacy and anti-spam laws. Alternatives include ZoomInfo, Lusha, Cognism, Outreach, and Clay. Apollo suits startups and sales teams that want prospecting and outreach in one tool.
