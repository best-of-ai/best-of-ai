Powtoon helps people make animated videos without an animation background. You start from a template for an explainer, training video, or presentation, edit the text and scenes, and choose characters, props, and backgrounds from a library. A timeline lets you control timing, and you can add voiceovers, music, and screen recordings. It is used for onboarding, corporate communication, education, and marketing.

AI tools reduce the work of getting started. A text-to-video feature creates a draft from a script or topic, AI can write scripts, and text-to-speech generates narration in many languages. Avatars can present, and translation features help localize videos. Branding tools keep colors, fonts, and logos consistent, and collaboration features let teams review and approve content.

Powtoon has a free plan with watermarks and limited exports and paid plans for HD, more media, and team use. The cartoon style is recognizable and suits explainers, but is less fitting for premium brand films. Animaker is a close alternative with more characters, Vyond is the corporate animation leader, Canva and Adobe Express offer simpler video, and Synthesia and Colossyan use realistic avatars. Powtoon suits internal communications and training teams.
