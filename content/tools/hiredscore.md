HiredScore applies AI to the early stages of hiring. When applications arrive, it grades candidates against the requirements of the role, surfaces people who might be good fits, including past applicants and current employees, and helps recruiters focus on promising profiles first.

It integrates with applicant tracking systems and HR platforms, and Workday acquired the company to build AI into its talent products. Features include internal mobility recommendations, which help companies fill roles from within, and reporting to monitor process fairness and compliance.

HiredScore is sold to large enterprises. AI in hiring is subject to regulation on bias and transparency in several jurisdictions, and employers must audit tools and keep humans in decisions. Alternatives include Eightfold, Beamery, Phenom, Paradox, and SeekOut. HiredScore suits large employers that want AI-assisted screening with governance.
