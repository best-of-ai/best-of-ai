Homestyler makes home design accessible. You draw a floor plan or start from a template, add walls, doors, and windows, and then furnish rooms from a catalog of real products and styles. A 3D view lets you walk through the space, and realistic renderings show how finishes and lighting would look. Homeowners use it to plan renovations and arrange furniture, and designers use it to present ideas to clients.

AI features speed up design. Tools can generate interior styles from a photo of a room, suggest layouts, and produce renderings from a simple description, and some features let you scan or upload a room as a starting point. Community galleries share designs for inspiration, and links to retailers make it easier to buy items you have selected.

Homestyler offers a free tier supported by paid options for higher-resolution renders and extra features. It is aimed at consumers and small professionals, and its precision is lower than professional architecture software. Alternatives include Planner 5D, RoomSketcher, SketchUp, Floorplanner, and IKEA's planning tools, along with AI redesign apps like RoomGPT. Homestyler suits people planning a room makeover or renovation.
