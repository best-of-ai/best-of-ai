Trust & Will makes estate planning approachable. A user answers questions about family, assets, and wishes, and the platform produces documents tailored to their state's law. Plans can include wills, revocable trusts, powers of attorney, and healthcare directives.

Legal review options and notarization help make documents valid, and the service stores them securely. Price is usually lower than a full attorney engagement, which draws people who have put off planning.

Trust & Will is paid per document package or plan. Complex situations, such as large estates or business interests, call for an attorney. Alternatives include Quicken WillMaker, LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer, Ethos for insurance and wills, and local estate lawyers. Trust & Will suits people with straightforward estate planning needs.
