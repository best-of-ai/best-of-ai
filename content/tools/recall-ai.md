Recall acts as a personal archive that organizes itself. A user saves a web page, YouTube video, podcast, or document with a browser extension or app, and Recall produces a summary, extracts key points, and links the item to related content already in the library through a knowledge graph.

An assistant lets users ask questions across everything they have saved, and spaced repetition quizzes help them remember material. It suits people who consume a lot of information and lose track of it.

Recall has a free tier and paid plans. Summaries can miss nuance, so original sources remain important. Alternatives include Readwise Reader, Mem, Notion AI, Obsidian with plugins, and NotebookLM. Recall suits researchers and lifelong learners.
