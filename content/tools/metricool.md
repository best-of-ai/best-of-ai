Metricool brings several marketing tasks into one dashboard. Users can plan and schedule posts across networks on a calendar, see analytics on posts, followers, and competitors, and manage Facebook, Instagram, Google, and TikTok ads reports. A link-in-bio tool, inbox for comments and messages, and web analytics help connect social activity to site traffic.

Reporting is a strong point. Customizable reports can be exported or scheduled for clients, and integrations connect with Looker Studio and other business intelligence tools, which suits agencies. AI features help write captions, suggest posting times, and generate content ideas, and a feature can plan a calendar from a brief. Plans cover multiple brands, so one account can manage several clients.

Metricool has a free plan for a small number of posts and one brand, and paid plans priced by number of brands and features, which is often cheaper than larger platforms. It has fewer enterprise features, such as advanced social listening, than Sprout Social or Hootsuite. Alternatives include Buffer, Later, Planoly, Hootsuite, and Sprout Social. Metricool suits freelancers, small businesses, and agencies who want analytics and scheduling together.
