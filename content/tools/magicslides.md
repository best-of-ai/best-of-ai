MagicSlides speeds up deck creation. A user enters a topic, pastes text, or uploads a document, and the tool builds slides with titles, bullets, and images. Because it works as an add-on inside Google Slides and PowerPoint, the results land in a familiar editor for refinement.

It supports many languages and offers themes and layouts. Students use it for assignments, and professionals use it to produce a first draft of a presentation, which they then edit. Images can be sourced automatically.

MagicSlides has a free tier and paid plans. Generated slides tend to be text heavy and generic, and content should be checked for accuracy. Alternatives include Gamma, Beautiful.ai, Canva, Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint, and Claude Slides. MagicSlides suits people who work in Slides or PowerPoint and want a quick draft.
