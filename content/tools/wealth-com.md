Wealth.com brings estate planning into the advisory relationship. Advisors can generate estate documents for clients through guided workflows, with attorney review options, and offer a client portal that shows the plan visually. A vault stores documents.

AI reads existing estate documents and summarizes provisions, beneficiaries, and issues, which helps advisors understand a client's situation quickly. The platform integrates with financial planning tools so estate information sits alongside the rest of the plan.

Wealth.com is sold to advisory firms on subscription. Estate planning is state specific, and complex cases need attorneys. Alternatives include Trust & Will for consumers, Vanilla, Savvy, Holistiplan for tax planning, and traditional estate attorneys. Wealth.com suits financial advisors.
