Glasshand puts real, dedicated iPhones and Macs in a data center and hands them to your agent over an API. They are not virtual machines, emulators or simulators. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenClaw, Hermes or any other agent can see the screen, tap and type.

Typical uses are running social accounts (posting to Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn and replying to comments and DMs), building and testing native apps in Xcode on a hosted Mac, and signing in to iCloud and apps. You can add an eSIM plan to receive verification texts, and the device is erased and reset when you release it.

There is no free tier or trial. Devices are rented month to month at a flat monthly rate, and you can cancel at any time. It is for people who want an agent to operate real iOS and macOS accounts and apps. Teams that only need to run tests may find a cloud device farm or a Mac hosting service enough.
