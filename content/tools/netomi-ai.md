Netomi builds AI agents for customer support. Large brands connect it to their help desks and back-end systems, and the agents answer questions, track orders, process returns, and handle other requests across channels. Where an agent cannot resolve something, it hands off to a person with context.

The platform learns from past tickets and company content, and is built with controls such as guardrails and testing for regulated and high-volume environments. Customers include airlines, retailers, and travel companies that field heavy, repetitive inquiries.

Netomi is sold on enterprise contracts. Success depends on integrations and data quality. Competitors include Sierra, Decagon, Ada, Intercom Fin, and Forethought. Netomi suits large consumer brands automating support.
