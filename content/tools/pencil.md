Pencil helps marketing teams produce a steady stream of ads. You connect your brand assets and product information, and Pencil generates video and image ads with copy and voiceovers in different formats for platforms like Meta, TikTok, and YouTube. Teams can create many variations, which makes testing easier than designing each ad by hand.

The prediction layer is the distinctive part. Pencil scores creative on how likely it is to perform, using models trained on a large amount of ad performance data and refined with the account's own results. It also helps track which messages and visuals win and feeds that back into the next round of creative. Integrations with ad platforms and Shopify bring in performance data and product catalogs.

Pencil is paid, sold by subscription and enterprise arrangements, and part of The Brandtech Group, which also launched a higher-end offering for large brands. Generated ads can look generic without strong brand inputs, and predictions are probabilities, not guarantees. Competitors include AdCreative.ai, Pattern89, Predis.ai, Canva's tools, and the generative ad features inside Meta and Google. It suits e-commerce teams that need volume and testing.
