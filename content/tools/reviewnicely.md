Reviewnicely is an all-in-one AI review management platform that helps businesses easily request, manage, and showcase customer feedback. It supports review requests via WhatsApp, SMS, email, and QR codes, with automated reminders and customizable templates.

Consolidate reviews from over twenty platforms, filter and respond from one dashboard, and share positive feedback on social media or your website through versatile widgets. Reviewnicely simplifies reputation management, empowering businesses to turn customer reviews into growth opportunities. video https://youtu.be/ux8InNG36vg?si=Lj1nuCDkY1OPkdHi 
 
Reviewnicely Features

Request reviews via WhatsApp, SMS, Email, QR Code
Send an automatic SMS, Email reminders after three and/or seven days
Customize review request templates
Edit the review link: change the review link URL, link preview title; Edit logo, copy, image, evaluation filter threshold; Enable / disable review platforms, private feedback, unlimited video testimonials
Optionally internalize negative feedback
Consolidate reviews from more than twenty review platforms
Get notified by email when you get new reviews
Find reviews with the keywords
Reviews filtering by Assessment, Type, Answer, Date
Monitor and reply to reviews from one centralized place
Reply to Google and Facebook reviews with AI
Share reviews on Social Media
Customize social media post template
Showcase positive reviews on the website via different types of widgets: Carousel, Feed, Video, Pop-up, Badge
Dashboard, consisting of reviews count, growth chart, funnel (invites sent, total unique visits, QR code unique visits, new public reviews, negative feedback) and average rating growth
The platform is translated into up to twenty languages
