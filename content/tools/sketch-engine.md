Users search for words and phrases, see collocations, and compare usage across corpora. A word sketch summarizes grammatical patterns for a word. Users can also build corpora from their own texts.

It is a subscription service, with free access for some academic users.

AntConc is a free desktop alternative. Sketch Engine suits linguists and terminologists.
