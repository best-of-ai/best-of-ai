C3.ai builds packaged AI applications and a platform for developing them. Instead of starting from scratch, a company can adopt an application for a defined problem, such as predicting when equipment will fail, detecting fraud, or optimizing inventory, and connect it to their data. The platform provides tools for data integration, modeling, and deployment.

The company has moved to add generative AI. Its offerings include tools that let users ask questions about enterprise data in natural language and agent features for business workflows. Customers include utilities, manufacturers, banks, and defense and government agencies, where large-scale deployments are common.

C3.ai is a public company selling enterprise subscriptions with long sales cycles. Results depend on data readiness and organizational adoption, and the company has faced competition from cloud providers and from companies building their own tools. Alternatives include Palantir, Databricks, DataRobot, Microsoft Azure AI, and Google Cloud's Vertex AI. C3.ai suits large organizations seeking packaged industrial AI.
