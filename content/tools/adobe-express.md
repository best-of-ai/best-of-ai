Adobe Express is Adobe's answer to Canva: a simple editor where you start from a template, change the text and images, and export. It covers social media posts, banners, flyers, simple videos, and quick edits to PDFs. It runs in the browser and on phones, and it needs no design background. Brand kits, shared templates, and a content scheduler help small teams keep their posts consistent.

The AI tools come from Adobe's Firefly models. You can generate images from a text prompt, extend or fill parts of a photo, apply text effects from a description, remove backgrounds, and resize a design for several platforms in one step. Because it shares libraries with Creative Cloud, assets from Photoshop or Illustrator can be used directly in Express projects, and designers can hand off a template that colleagues then adjust.

There is a free plan with a limited library of templates and fonts, plus a paid tier that adds premium content, brand controls, and more generative credits. Express is also included with many Creative Cloud subscriptions. It is not meant for heavy professional layout, where Photoshop, Illustrator, or InDesign are better suited. For marketers and small businesses already inside Adobe's ecosystem it is a sensible choice, while people with no Adobe ties often default to Canva.
