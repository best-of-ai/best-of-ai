Adobe Express occupies the middle ground between Canva (template-driven, highly accessible) and the full Creative Cloud apps (powerful but requiring expertise). It's designed for people who need branded content produced quickly — a social post, a flyer, a short video clip — and who want Adobe's quality standards without needing to open Photoshop. The template library covers every common social media format and marketing material type, and templates update automatically when you change a brand colour or font.

The AI features are powered by Adobe Firefly, which means they're commercially safe to use — no IP risk from training data. Text-to-image generates backgrounds and accent elements from prompts. Generative Fill lets you replace or extend parts of a photo. Remove Background works on photos with one click. Text Effects applies AI-generated styles to any headline or callout. These features are integrated into the editing workflow, so you don't switch between the AI tool and the design tool — you're just designing, and the AI helps where needed.

Adobe Express Free covers all core features with access to Adobe's template library and the core AI tools with monthly credit limits. Express Premium at $9.99/month unlocks the full template library, removes the AI credit cap, adds brand kit management, and enables premium content assets. Teams can use Express with shared brand kits, which ensures that everyone's output uses the same approved colours, logos, and fonts regardless of design ability. Communications, marketing, and social media teams are the primary user base.
