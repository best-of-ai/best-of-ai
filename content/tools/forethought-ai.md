Forethought applies AI to the work that fills support queues. It reads incoming tickets and classifies them by topic and urgency, routes them to the right team, and drafts answers for human agents using the company's knowledge base and past tickets. For common questions, its AI agents answer directly in chat and email and take simple actions such as checking an order status, handing off to a person when they cannot resolve the issue.

The platform learns from outcomes. It identifies gaps in help content where customers keep asking about something the knowledge base does not cover, and it measures which answers resolve issues. Integrations connect with major helpdesk systems, such as Zendesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, and Freshdesk, so it can sit on top of tools a company already uses.

Zendesk acquired Forethought to strengthen its own AI agents, and said Forethought would remain available to new customers without requiring them to use Zendesk's platform. Pricing is by quote and often tied to the volume of resolved conversations. Because the product is now part of a larger vendor, roadmap details may shift. Competitors include Intercom's Fin, Ada, Decagon, Sierra, and Zendesk's own AI agents. It suits support teams with a lot of repetitive tickets and a decent knowledge base.
