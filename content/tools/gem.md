Gem helps recruiting teams build pipelines. Recruiters search for candidates across LinkedIn and other sources, add them to talent pools, and send sequences of personalized outreach. A candidate CRM tracks every interaction, so when a role opens, the team can reach people they have already engaged.

Analytics show how sourcing channels perform, how long hiring takes, and where candidates drop out. AI features help with search, writing outreach, and identifying candidates who may be open to new roles. An applicant tracking system component extends the suite for teams that want everything in one place.

Gem is sold to growth-stage and larger companies on subscription. AI in hiring is subject to rules on bias and automated decisions in some places. Alternatives include Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, Beamery, and SeekOut. Gem suits recruiting teams focused on sourcing.
