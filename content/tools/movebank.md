Researchers upload tracks, annotate them with environmental data, and analyze or share them. A public map displays selected studies. It is run by the Max Planck Institute of Animal Behavior with partners.

Data access depends on each study's sharing settings.

Wildlife Insights covers camera traps. Movebank suits wildlife biologists and zoologists who track animals.
