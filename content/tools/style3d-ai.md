Style3D AI is a powerful AI-powered fashion design and marketing platform for designers, patternmakers, and small fashion brands. It combines AI virtual try-on technology, 3D garment modeling, image-to-video generation, AI clothing enhancement, and pattern creation to help you design, visualize, and market fashion products faster and smarter.
As the first AI agent in the fashion industry, it helps you easily complete your entire workflow. From graphic design, style and fabric creation, to styling and e-commerce marketing, just use simple conversations to bring your ideas to life and speed up your projects.
Key Features:
● AI Virtual Try-On: Preview realistic garment fit and movement on digital models to reduce sample costs.
● Image-to-Video Generation: Create engaging videos from your images without expensive photoshoots.
● AI Agent: Use conversational AI to complete design, production, and marketing tasks quickly.
● Accessory Try-On: Virtually try on bags, hats, and other accessories for full outfit visualization.
● Model & Pose Switching: Easily change models and poses for different markets and campaigns.
Style3D AI helps brands speed up design cycles, reduce production costs, and improve marketing impact—empowering you to succeed in AI-driven fashion innovation and fashion e-commerce.
