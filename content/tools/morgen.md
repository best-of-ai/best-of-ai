Morgen is a calendar and planner. Users connect Google, Outlook, iCloud, and other calendars to see everything in one view, and bring in tasks from tools like Notion, Todoist, and Linear. Tasks can be dragged onto the calendar to block time for them.

The planner feature suggests where tasks fit in the day, taking events, deadlines, and durations into account, and rearranges when plans change. Scheduling links let others book time, with rules for availability and buffers. Frames let users reserve time for certain types of work.

Morgen has a free plan and paid tiers. Automatic planning needs tasks with realistic estimates. Alternatives include Motion, Reclaim, Fantastical, Sunsama, and Notion Calendar. Morgen suits people who want to time-block work.
