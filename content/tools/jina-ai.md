Jina AI focuses on the retrieval side of AI applications. Its embedding models turn text and images into vectors for search in many languages, rerankers sort results by relevance, and a reader API converts a web page into clean text that language models can use. Search and deep research endpoints build on these.

Many developers used Jina's open models from Hugging Face and its free-to-start APIs when building retrieval-augmented generation. The company also open-sourced framework projects for building multimodal systems in its early years.

Elastic acquired Jina AI to strengthen its search and AI offerings, and said it would continue releasing the models and offering them through Elastic's inference service. Alternatives include Cohere Embed and Rerank, Voyage AI, OpenAI embeddings, Firecrawl for web content, and open models like BGE. Jina AI suits developers building retrieval systems.
