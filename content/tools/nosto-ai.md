Nosto helps online stores treat every visitor differently. It tracks browsing and purchase behavior and uses it to recommend products, order category pages, personalize search, and show targeted messages. Merchants configure these experiences with a visual tool and rules, and the system learns what works.

Features include product recommendations across the site and in emails, a search engine that understands intent, and tools to personalize content by audience. Shopify, Magento, and other platforms are supported. Generative AI helps write product copy and campaign content.

Nosto is paid, with pricing by traffic and features. Results depend on enough traffic and a clean catalog. Alternatives include Klevu, Bloomreach, Dynamic Yield, Algolia, and Klaviyo for email. Nosto suits mid-sized online retailers.
