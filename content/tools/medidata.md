Medidata is a major provider of clinical trial technology. Rave Electronic Data Capture collects patient data from trial sites, and other products handle randomization, imaging, patient-reported outcomes, and trial planning. Pharmaceutical companies and research organizations use the platform to run studies from start to finish.

AI and analytics help trial teams. Models flag sites with unusual data, predict enrollment problems, and help design studies using historical trial data, and the company has built synthetic control arm methods that use past patients to supplement studies. These aim to make trials faster and more efficient.

Medidata is owned by Dassault Systèmes and sold to life sciences organizations on enterprise contracts. Clinical trial software is heavily regulated and implemented with validation. Alternatives include Veeva Clinical, Oracle Clinical One, Castor, and REDCap for academic studies. Medidata suits sponsors and research organizations running clinical trials.
