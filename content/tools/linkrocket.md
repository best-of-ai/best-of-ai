LinkRocket is the all-in-one SEO platform built to streamline link building, improve search rankings, and save you time.

At its core, LinkRocket offers a Backlink Exchange and a Backlink Marketplace, giving you two powerful ways to acquire high-quality backlinks. With the exchange, you earn credits by placing backlinks for other users. You can then spend those credits to request backlinks pointing to your own website—no outreach or awkward emails required. And with our intelligent matching system, we automatically prevent reciprocal backlinks, ensuring your SEO efforts stay clean and effective.

Prefer to buy backlinks outright? Our Backlink Marketplace connects you to vetted, high-authority websites, allowing you to purchase links with full transparency. Each listing includes key metrics and pricing, so you know exactly what you're getting.

But LinkRocket doesn’t stop at links. We’ve built in an AI Content Writer that helps you draft SEO-optimized blog posts, articles, and landing pages—perfect for content-driven SEO campaigns. We also offer a Rank Tracker that keeps tabs on your keyword performance across projects, so you can see what’s working and where to double down.

With paid subscriptions, users get even more: monthly free credits, full access to the AI tools, keyword tracking, backlink indexing, and team collaboration features with role-based access (Owner, Admin, Member).
