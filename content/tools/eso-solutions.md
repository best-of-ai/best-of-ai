ESO makes software for emergency medical services and fire departments. Paramedics record patient information, vitals, treatments, and outcomes on a tablet or laptop, producing the patient care report that is shared with hospitals and used for billing and quality review.

AI helps reduce documentation time. The software can draft narrative sections from the structured data a medic entered, and flag missing or inconsistent items so reports are complete. The company also sells products for hospitals, health data analytics, and fire records management.

ESO is sold to agencies on subscription, and reports must meet national data standards. AI-drafted narratives still need review by the clinician responsible. Alternatives include ImageTrend, Zoll, Traumasoft, and Emergency Reporting. ESO suits EMS and fire agencies.
