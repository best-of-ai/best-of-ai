Finastra supplies the systems that sit behind a bank's services. Its portfolio includes core banking platforms, commercial and retail lending, payments, trade finance, and treasury and capital markets tools. Banks of different sizes use it, from community institutions to global groups, and often run Finastra systems for decades.

The company has been moving customers toward the cloud and open banking. Its FusionFabric.cloud platform lets banks and third-party developers build apps and connect to Finastra's services through APIs, and it has been adding AI features for tasks such as credit analysis, document processing, and customer service. Partnerships with large cloud providers support hosting.

Finastra is a large private company, and its products are sold through enterprise contracts. Replacing or upgrading core banking systems is slow and risky, so changes happen gradually. Competitors include FIS, Fiserv, Jack Henry, Temenos, nCino, and Mambu. Finastra suits banks and financial institutions that need established, broad banking software.
