UnleashX provides voice and workflow automation. Teams configure AI agents that handle phone conversations and carry out actions across systems, such as calling leads, qualifying them, updating records, and sending follow-ups. Rules define what the worker may do.

The platform presents these as digital employees for roles like sales development and support. Outbound calling needs consent and compliance with telemarketing rules, and AI voice agents should disclose themselves.

UnleashX is paid, and independent information is limited, so run a pilot first. Alternatives include Bland AI, Retell AI, Vapi, Synthflow, and 11x. UnleashX suits small teams automating calls and follow-ups.
