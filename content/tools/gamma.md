Gamma reimagined the presentation. Instead of slides built from a blank template, you describe what you want, such as a pitch for a new product or a lesson on photosynthesis, and Gamma generates a complete, designed deck with text, layouts, and images. The format uses flexible cards that expand and collapse, so presentations can feel more like interactive documents, and it also produces documents and one-page websites.

Editing is conversational and visual. You can ask the AI to rewrite a section, change the style, add images, or restructure, or edit manually with simple controls. Themes keep designs consistent, brand settings apply company fonts and colors, and sharing by link includes analytics on who viewed it. Decks can be exported to PowerPoint or PDF, and it integrates with other tools.

Gamma has a free plan with limited AI credits and paid plans for more credits, branding, and export features. AI-generated content often needs fact-checking and tailoring, and some people find the card layout less suitable for traditional formal slide decks. Alternatives include Beautiful.ai, Pitch, Canva, Plus AI, Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint, and Google Slides with Gemini. Gamma suits people who need a polished draft fast.
