Gamma generates a complete, designed presentation from a text outline or topic in under a minute — not a template with placeholder text, but actual content with relevant copy, appropriate images, and a coherent visual theme. The first pass isn't always perfect, but it's good enough to work from rather than starting with a blank slide. For someone who needs a 15-slide deck in two hours, eliminating the setup time makes a real difference.

The output format is a shareable web link, not a PowerPoint file, which changes how people experience the presentation. Viewers scroll through on any device with no software required, and embedding video, data from Google Sheets, or interactive elements is built into the editor. Presenters can switch between a scroll view (for async sharing) and a slideshow mode for live presentations. The design stays consistently polished without you needing to manage alignment or spacing manually.

Gamma's free tier lets you generate unlimited AI decks with Gamma watermarks and 400 initial AI credits. Pro at $8/month removes watermarks, gives more AI credits, and adds custom domains for published decks. Teams use it for internal updates, sales presentations, product briefings, and investor materials — anything that needs to look polished and shareable quickly. Gamma has become particularly popular with startup founders and consultants who pitch frequently and can't afford to spend hours in PowerPoint each time.
