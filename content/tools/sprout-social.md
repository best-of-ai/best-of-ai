Sprout Social is built for teams that manage social media as a function, not a side task. A calendar plans and schedules content across networks such as Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, and TikTok, and approval flows keep drafts under review before they go out. A smart inbox gathers messages, comments, and mentions from every channel into one stream that can be assigned, tagged, and replied to by a team.

Analytics are a major selling point. Reports show how posts perform, how audiences grow, and how competitors compare, and they can be exported or shared with clients and executives. Listening tools track brand mentions, topics, and sentiment across social and the web, which helps with crisis monitoring and market research. Influencer marketing and employee advocacy tools extend into related work. AI Assist helps write posts, change tone, and draft replies, and it can summarize sentiment from conversations.

Plans are billed per seat in tiers, from Standard through Professional and Advanced, plus custom Enterprise pricing, and there is a free trial. It costs noticeably more than Buffer or Later, so it is aimed at mid-sized and large organizations, agencies, and brands that need reporting depth and collaboration. Small businesses and solo creators may find simpler tools sufficient. Hootsuite is its main enterprise competitor, and Sprinklr targets the largest companies.
