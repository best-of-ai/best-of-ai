Klaviyo is built on the idea that a store's own customer data is the best basis for marketing. It connects to platforms like Shopify, BigCommerce, and WooCommerce, pulling in orders, browsing behavior, and product information. Marketers build segments, such as customers who bought twice in the last 60 days, and send targeted email and SMS campaigns and automated flows, like abandoned cart reminders, welcome series, and post-purchase follow-ups.

AI adds prediction and automation. Klaviyo estimates a customer's likelihood to buy again, expected lifetime value, and probable next order date, and uses those signals for segmentation. Product recommendations personalize emails, send-time optimization chooses delivery times, and generative tools write subject lines and campaign copy and build flows from a description. A customer hub, reviews, and a CDP add other parts of the retention workflow.

Klaviyo has a free tier for small lists and paid plans priced by the number of profiles and messages, which can get expensive as the list grows. It is deeply focused on e-commerce, so businesses outside retail may find it less natural. Mailchimp is more general, Omnisend and Brevo are lower-cost alternatives, and Attentive specializes in SMS. For online stores with decent order data, Klaviyo is the standard choice for retention marketing.
