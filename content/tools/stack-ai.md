Stack AI offers a visual builder for AI workflows. Users drag together components such as inputs, language models, document search, and outputs, then connect them to enterprise tools. The result can be deployed as a chatbot, form, or API.

Common cases include document processing, internal knowledge assistants, and support triage. The platform emphasizes security and governance, with options for private deployment. Templates give a starting point.

Stack AI has a free tier and paid plans. Workflows need testing and monitoring. Alternatives include Relevance AI, Dify, Flowise, n8n, and Microsoft Copilot Studio. Stack AI suits business teams building AI workflows without code.
