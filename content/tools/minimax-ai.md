MiniMax builds models across modalities. Its Hailuo video generator produces short clips from text or images and became popular with creators for motion quality. The company also offers language models, speech synthesis, and music generation, available through apps and an API.

Developers use the API to add generation to products, and the consumer apps let users try the models directly. MiniMax has also released open-weight language models that have been used in agents and coding, adding to its profile among Chinese AI labs.

MiniMax has a free tier with credits and paid plans. As a Chinese company, it is subject to local regulations, and some organizations have data policies that limit its use. Alternatives include Kling, Runway, Luma, Google Veo, and Pika. MiniMax suits creators and developers exploring Chinese generative models.
