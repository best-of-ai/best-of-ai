Reaxys is a chemistry research database from Elsevier. A chemist can search by structure, reaction, or property and find experimental data on compounds, along with the reactions used to make them and the sources. This helps plan syntheses and avoid repeating known work.

Predictive synthesis tools propose routes to a target molecule by combining literature data with machine learning, ranking options by feasibility. It also covers properties, bioactivity, and patent chemistry, serving drug discovery and materials work.

Reaxys is sold on institutional subscriptions, and priced for universities and companies. Predicted routes need expert evaluation and lab validation. Alternatives include SciFinder from CAS, Pistachio, Synthia, PubChem for free data, and ChemSpider. Reaxys suits chemists and pharmaceutical researchers.
