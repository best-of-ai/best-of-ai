Demandbase focuses on selling to companies rather than individuals. It builds a view of target accounts from first-party and third-party data, including website visits, content use, and intent signals showing which accounts are researching a topic. Marketing and sales teams see which accounts are heating up and who within them is involved.

The platform acts on those signals. Advertising features serve targeted ads to the buying committee at chosen accounts, sales tools alert reps when accounts show interest, and orchestration coordinates campaigns across channels. AI helps score accounts, predict fit, and recommend actions, and an assistant answers questions about account data.

Demandbase is sold to mid-sized and large B2B companies on custom pricing. Results depend on data quality and alignment between marketing and sales. Alternatives include 6sense, ZoomInfo, HubSpot, Terminus, and Bombora for intent data. Demandbase suits B2B teams running account-based programs.
