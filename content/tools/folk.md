Folk is a relationship manager for people who find traditional CRMs heavy. Users import contacts from email, LinkedIn, and spreadsheets, organize them into groups, and track conversations and deals in a clean interface. It suits agencies, recruiters, investors, and founders who manage many relationships.

AI and automation reduce the manual work. Enrichment fills in details like company and role, a browser extension saves profiles from LinkedIn, and sequences send personalized emails at scale. AI helps write messages and summarize history, and reminders prompt follow-ups so nobody falls through the cracks.

Folk has paid plans per user, with a free trial. It is intentionally simpler than Salesforce or HubSpot, so it may not fit complex sales processes. Alternatives include Attio, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Notion CRM setups, and Airtable. Folk suits small teams wanting a flexible contact manager.
