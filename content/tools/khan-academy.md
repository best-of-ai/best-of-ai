Khan Academy provides free education to anyone with an internet connection. Its library of short videos, articles, and interactive exercises covers school subjects from basic arithmetic through calculus, biology, chemistry, grammar, history, and test preparation such as the SAT. Learners can follow structured courses, and the mastery system encourages students to practice until they have a skill before moving ahead.

For teachers and schools, it offers tools to assign work, track progress, and see which students need help. District partnerships connect it with curricula, and Khan Academy Kids offers content for young children in a separate app. The nonprofit is funded by donations and philanthropic support rather than by charging learners, which sets it apart from commercial education companies.

AI is part of the newer offerings through Khanmigo, a tutoring and teaching assistant. The core library is free, and any paid features are limited to specific AI tools. Content quality is high but can lean on videos and exercises that may not fit every curriculum or teaching style, and self-directed learning requires motivation. Alternatives include Coursera and edX for college-level courses, IXL for practice, Brilliant for interactive STEM, and school-specific curricula. Khan Academy is a free starting point for many subjects.
