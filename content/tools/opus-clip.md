Opus Clip automates a task that eats hours for podcasters, streamers, and marketers: cutting a long recording into shorts. You paste a link or upload a video, and the AI analyzes the transcript and visuals to find segments with a strong hook and a complete idea. It generates several clips, each with a title, captions, and a vertical layout that keeps the speaker in frame.

A virality score estimates how well each clip is likely to perform, based on patterns from social video, which helps you decide what to post first. You can edit the transcript, trim, change caption styles, apply brand templates, and add B-roll. Features such as ClipAnything let you search for a moment by describing it, and a scheduler publishes to connected social accounts.

Opus Clip has a free plan with watermarks and limited processing, and paid plans with more minutes, longer videos, and branding. Clip selection is a good first pass, but human judgment still matters for context and tone, and the virality score is a heuristic. Alternatives include Munch, Vizard, Klap, Descript, and CapCut, and tools such as Riverside include clip generation. Opus Clip is among the most popular for turning interviews and talks into a stream of short posts.
