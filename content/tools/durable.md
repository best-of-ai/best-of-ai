Durable promises a website with minimal effort. You tell it the kind of business you run and where you are, and it builds a site with pages, copy, images, and a layout in under a minute, which you can then edit with simple controls. It's aimed at freelancers and local service businesses, such as plumbers, photographers, and consultants, who need an online presence quickly.

Beyond the site, Durable includes small-business tools. A built-in CRM tracks leads and customers, an invoicing feature handles billing, and AI assistants help write blog posts, social content, and marketing copy. It offers hosting and a custom domain, and basic analytics. Everything lives in one account, so owners avoid piecing together several services.

Durable is paid, with a trial, and plans scale with features. AI-generated sites tend to look similar and generic, and they need personalization with real photos and details to stand out. Design flexibility is limited compared with Squarespace or Webflow. Alternatives include Wix with its AI builder, Hostinger, Squarespace, Framer for designers, and 10Web for WordPress. Durable suits small business owners who want a site fast and don't plan to customize heavily.
