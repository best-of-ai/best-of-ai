Busuu offers courses in several popular languages, organized by the levels of the Common European Framework, from beginner to upper-intermediate. Lessons teach vocabulary, grammar, listening, reading, and speaking in short sessions, and a review system revisits words to help you remember them. Learners can set a goal, such as being ready for travel or a certain level, and the study plan adjusts to fit.

The feature that sets it apart is community feedback. You can write or record a short answer and have it corrected by native speakers who use the app, which gives real human feedback on your output. AI tools add conversation practice, personalized review, and explanations of mistakes. Some plans offer certificates and exam preparation for recognized language tests.

Busuu has a free version with limited content and a premium subscription that unlocks the full courses, offline use, and personalized features. It is more structured than Duolingo and cheaper than live tutoring, though its content is not as deep as a dedicated course for advanced learners. Babbel is a close competitor with a similar approach, Duolingo is the game-style leader, Pimsleur focuses on audio, and iTalki or Preply connect you with human tutors.
