SlidesAI works inside Google Slides. You paste in text, such as an essay, notes, or an article, or enter a topic, and the add-on creates a set of slides with titles, bullet points, and layout. It lets you pick a presentation style, color theme, and the number of slides, and can add relevant images. The result is a draft that you edit like any other Google Slides deck.

Additional features support content work. It can summarize long text into key points, paraphrase for clarity, and generate text for slides from a prompt. Because the output is a native Slides file, it can be shared, collaborated on, and presented as usual. It's popular with students, teachers, and professionals who need a quick starting point.

SlidesAI has a free plan with a limited number of presentations per month and paid plans with more. The generated designs are fairly basic and the content needs checking and refinement. Gamma, Beautiful.ai, and Plus AI offer more polished AI presentation generation, and Google's own Gemini in Slides adds similar features inside Google Workspace. SlidesAI suits people who want a simple, inexpensive add-on within a familiar tool.
