Patch simplifies buying carbon removal. Companies browse projects, compare prices and methods, and purchase credits through a platform or API instead of negotiating deals one by one. Developers can embed purchases into other software, such as financial or e-commerce products.

The company has shifted toward helping enterprises plan and procure carbon removal at scale, with tools for sourcing, due diligence, and reporting. AI supports tasks like drafting requests for proposals and evaluating projects.

Carbon removal is a young market with differences in quality, permanence, and verification, so buyers should scrutinize what they are purchasing. Credits do not replace cutting emissions. Alternatives include Frontier, Watershed, Sylvera for ratings, Pachama, and direct deals with suppliers. Patch suits companies buying carbon removal.
