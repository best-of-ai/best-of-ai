Nudge AI is the most advanced documentation platform built specifically for healthcare professionals. Our proprietary Ambient Scribe technology captures clinical sessions and instantly transforms conversations into structured, insurance-proof clinical notes—whether you need psychiatric evaluations, therapy progress notes, or comprehensive treatment plans.

Our platform features Direct EHR Integration with 50+ EHRs, allowing you to transfer completed notes with a single click. The sophisticated AI Billing Assistant identifies appropriate CPT codes based on session complexity and automatically generates add-on justifications, helping clinicians maximize reimbursement without compliance concerns.

Clinicians love our Infinite Customizations that adapt to their workflow preferences rather than forcing standardized templates. Simply paste any ideal note and our AI converts it into a reusable template in minutes, giving you 100% control over format, language, and structure. The intelligent Note Continuity Engine references prior sessions to ensure treatment progression is clearly documented, maintaining narrative consistency that both improves clinical quality and satisfies insurance requirements.

For enterprises, Nudge offers robust administrative tools through the Member Management Portal and delivers actionable practice insights via the Enterprise Analytics Dashboard, helping leadership optimize operations and improve outcomes.

The result? Clinicians save 8+ hours weekly on documentation, reduce burnout, improve billing accuracy, and maintain audit-proof records—all while focusing on what matters most: patient care.
