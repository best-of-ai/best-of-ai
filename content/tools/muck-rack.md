Muck Rack is a public relations platform. PR professionals search its database to find journalists who cover a topic, see what they have written recently, and build media lists. They send pitches and track responses, and the platform monitors news to show where their company or clients are mentioned.

Reporting tools measure coverage by reach and sentiment, and dashboards show how campaigns perform. AI features summarize articles, suggest journalists, and help draft pitches, saving time on research and writing. The company also publishes research about the relationship between journalists and PR.

Muck Rack is sold on subscription at professional pricing. Journalist contact data requires constant updating, and spray-and-pray pitching annoys reporters, so personalization matters. Alternatives include Cision, Meltwater, Prowly, Propel, and Onclusive. Muck Rack suits PR and communications teams.
