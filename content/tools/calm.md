Calm is one of the most downloaded wellness apps. Its content library includes guided meditations of different lengths, sleep stories read by narrators in a soothing voice, calming music, breathing exercises, and masterclasses on topics such as stress and focus. A daily session and reminders encourage a habit. Many people use it mainly to fall asleep, which is where the app is strongest.

The company has extended into other areas. Calm Health offers programs for employers and health plans, with tools for sleep, stress, and mental wellbeing, and content for kids and families. Personalization suggests content based on your goals and mood, and newer features use AI to tailor recommendations. The app is not a substitute for therapy, but it can support relaxation and routines.

Calm offers a limited free version and a paid subscription for the complete library, including annual and lifetime options. Research on meditation apps shows modest benefits for stress and sleep for many users, but people with significant anxiety or depression need professional care. Headspace is the closest rival, with a more structured approach to meditation, Insight Timer has a huge free library, and apps like Sleep Cycle focus on sleep tracking. Calm suits people who want soothing audio and easy sessions.
