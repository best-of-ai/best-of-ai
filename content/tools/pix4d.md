Pix4D is photogrammetry software. Photos taken by a drone or a camera are processed into orthomosaics, digital surface models, and 3D meshes with accurate coordinates, so users can measure distances, areas, and volumes from the results.

Products target different jobs. One handles mapping from drones, another focuses on surveying with scanners and phones, and others serve fields like agriculture and inspection. AI helps classify features and clean up point clouds, and cloud processing handles big projects.

Pix4D is sold by subscription and license, with pricing by product. Accuracy depends on flight planning and ground control. Alternatives include DroneDeploy, Agisoft Metashape, Propeller Aero, Bentley ContextCapture, and OpenDroneMap. Pix4D suits surveyors and engineers.
