Stamped helps online stores build trust. After a purchase, it emails customers asking for reviews, collects star ratings with photos or videos, and displays them on product pages and in search results with structured data. Social proof improves conversion rates, and review requests can be automated and timed, with options to send coupons for leaving a review.

The platform also offers loyalty and referral programs. Customers earn points for purchases and actions, redeem rewards, and refer friends, which encourages repeat business. AI features summarize reviews into key themes, surface what customers love or dislike, and help merchants respond. Reports show review volume, ratings, and the effect on sales, and integrations connect with email, helpdesk, and ads tools.

Stamped.io has a free plan for small stores and paid plans scaled by order volume, with Shopify as its main home. Review authenticity matters, and stores should follow the rules around incentivized reviews. Alternatives include Yotpo, Judge.me, Okendo, Loox, and Trustpilot. Stamped suits growing e-commerce merchants wanting reviews and loyalty in one tool.
