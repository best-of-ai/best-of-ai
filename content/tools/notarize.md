Notarize brought notarization online. A signer uploads a document, verifies identity by scanning an ID and answering questions, joins a video call with a commissioned notary, and signs digitally. The notary applies a digital seal and the document is stored securely, making a process that required an in-person visit possible from home.

The company, now operating as Proof, expanded into broader identity and signature products for businesses, including title and escrow, banks, and real estate. AI-based checks examine ID documents and confirm that a live person is present, which helps resist fraud.

Online notarization is legal in many but not all jurisdictions, and some document types still require other steps. Pricing is per notarization or by business plan. Alternatives include DocuSign Notary, OneNotary, Notarize competitors like NotaryCam, and traditional notaries. Notarize suits people and businesses who need remote notarization.
