Copy.ai began as one of the first popular AI copywriting tools. Users choose a template or write a prompt, and the tool generates headlines, product descriptions, email sequences, ads, and social captions. A brand voice feature helps keep the tone consistent.

The product later shifted toward workflows for go-to-market teams. Marketers and sales teams can chain steps that research accounts, draft outreach, and produce content at scale, using multiple language models. Chat and knowledge features let the AI draw on company information.

Fullcast, a revenue operations company, acquired Copy.ai, so its direction may change as it combines with Fullcast's products. The tool has had a free tier and paid plans. AI drafts need editing to be accurate and distinctive. Alternatives include Jasper, Writesonic, ChatGPT, Claude, and Copysmith. Copy.ai suits marketing and sales teams wanting draft content and workflows.
