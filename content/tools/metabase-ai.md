Metabase makes data approachable. Connected to a database or warehouse, it lets non-technical users build questions by clicking through filters and summaries, view results as charts, and assemble dashboards. Analysts can write SQL when they need it, and everything is shareable.

The AI assistant lets users describe what they want, and generates the query or chart, which helps people who do not know the data model. Embedded analytics lets companies place dashboards in their own products. Permissions and models keep data governed.

Metabase is open source and can be self-hosted for free, with paid cloud and enterprise plans. It is simpler than Looker or Tableau and suits small and mid-sized teams. Alternatives include Superset, Redash, Tableau, Power BI, and Looker Studio. Metabase suits teams that want accessible, low-cost analytics.
