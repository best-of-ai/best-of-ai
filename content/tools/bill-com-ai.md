BILL helps businesses handle bills and payments without paper and manual entry. Vendors' invoices arrive by email or upload, the system extracts the details, routes them for approval, and schedules payment by ACH, check, or card. On the receivables side, it sends invoices to customers and collects payments.

AI takes on the data entry. It reads invoice fields, suggests accounting categories, matches invoices to purchase orders and receipts, and flags duplicates or unusual items. Integrations with QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, and other accounting systems keep the books in sync, and spend management tools add corporate cards and expense tracking.

BILL charges per user and for payment services, with plans by features. It serves small and mid-sized businesses and accounting firms. Alternatives include Ramp, Brex, Tipalti, Melio, and Stampli. BILL suits finance teams that want to automate payables and approvals.
