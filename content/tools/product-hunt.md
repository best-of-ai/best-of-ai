Product Hunt started as a simple list of cool new products and became a standard place for startups to debut. Makers submit a product with a tagline, description, images, and a link, and the community upvotes and comments throughout the day. Top-ranked products earn visibility, and a launch can bring early users, feedback, and press attention. The site covers apps, developer tools, hardware, and books.

For readers, it's a way to see what is new. You can browse by day, week, or month, follow topics such as artificial intelligence, productivity, or design, and read discussions that often include makers answering questions. Collections and newsletters curate products, and a leaderboard highlights trending launches. Makers can also post updates and use the site's tools to share a launch checklist and gather reviews.

Product Hunt is free for both visitors and makers, and the company earns money through promotions and other products. Rankings reflect voting, which can be influenced by launch-day mobilization and does not equal quality, so a high rank is not a recommendation. Many listings are early-stage and may not last. Alternatives and complements include Hacker News Show HN, BetaList, Indie Hackers, Futurepedia, and There's An AI For That for AI tools.
