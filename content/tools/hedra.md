Hedra animates faces. A user supplies a still image, which can be a photo, illustration, or generated character, and an audio track or script, and Hedra produces a video of the character speaking with matching lip movement and expression. The results are known for emotive performances.

Uses include short films, explainer videos, social content with characters, and marketing. Text to speech and voice options make it possible to start from only a script, and longer and higher resolution outputs are available on paid plans. The platform has expanded into broader video generation features.

Hedra has a free tier with limits and paid plans. Animating a real person's face raises consent and deepfake concerns, so use likenesses only with permission and label synthetic content. Alternatives include HeyGen, D-ID, Synthesia, Captions, and Runway. Hedra suits creators who need expressive character video.
