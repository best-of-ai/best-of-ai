Influencer Hero is an all-in-one influencer marketing SaaS platform designed for DTC e-commerce brands and agencies to streamline influencer discovery, outreach, campaign management, and ROI tracking. Covering eight major platforms including Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube, it combines advanced search filters, AI-powered creator recommendations, automated outreach flows, and a purpose-built CRM to help teams scale from 10 to 10,000 influencers without losing efficiency.

Beyond discovery and outreach, Influencer Hero offers gifting and seeding integrations with Shopify and WooCommerce, automated affiliate tracking and payments, customizable influencer storefronts, and real-time reporting dashboards. With a 4.8/5.0 rating on G2, intuitive UX, and white-glove support, the platform equips brands to run performance-driven campaigns at scale while saving time and maximizing ROI.
