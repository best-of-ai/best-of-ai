MOVA Guide gives recommendations through conversation. Instead of scrolling through lists of top-rated places, a user tells it something like I want a quiet evening with good food, and it suggests spots matching that mood in their area. It aims to feel more personal than review sites.

The app is aimed at both tourists and residents bored with their usual routine. Suggestions can include restaurants, cafes, activities, and events, and the guide adjusts as the user reacts to ideas.

MOVA Guide is free. As with any recommendation engine, check opening hours, prices, and safety before going, because AI suggestions can be outdated or wrong. Alternatives include Google Maps, TripAdvisor, Yelp, Wanderlog, and ChatGPT or Gemini for travel ideas. MOVA Guide suits people looking for spontaneous local ideas.
