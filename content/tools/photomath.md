Photomath makes it easy to get unstuck on math. You point your phone's camera at a printed or handwritten problem, and the app recognizes it and shows the answer along with steps for solving it. It covers arithmetic, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, and calculus, and explains the reasoning for each step, so students can learn the method instead of copying an answer.

Further features support learning. Animated tutorials illustrate how steps work, multiple solution methods are available for some problems, and a graphing tool shows functions. Recent versions add AI-powered explanations that answer follow-up questions, and the app works in many languages. Teachers and parents use it to check work and see how a problem can be approached.

Photomath has a free tier with basic solutions and a paid Plus subscription for detailed explanations and extra content. Using it to complete graded work without understanding can hurt learning, and OCR may misread messy handwriting. Alternatives include Google Lens and Gemini, Microsoft Math Solver, Symbolab, Wolfram Alpha, Khan Academy, and Mathway. Photomath is a popular choice for students who want to see how to solve problems.
