Copy.ai is built around the idea that marketing copy follows repeatable patterns — if you define the product, the audience, and the goal, the AI can handle the structural work of first drafts. The 90+ template library covers practically every copy format: cold email sequences, LinkedIn connection requests, product page bullets, Instagram captions, YouTube descriptions, customer case study outlines, and sales call scripts. You pick the template, fill in a few context fields, and get several variations to choose from.

The workflow engine, added to the platform in 2023, turns Copy.ai from a single-output tool into a production system. A workflow takes a single input — a product URL, a customer interview transcript, a keyword list — and automatically produces a series of outputs: blog post, meta description, social captions, and email newsletter, all in sequence without manual input at each step. For content teams that need to produce these assets repeatedly across many products or campaigns, this automation saves significant time.

The free plan provides 2,000 words per month and access to core templates — useful for evaluation but limited for production use. Pro at $49/month gives unlimited words and workflow access for one user; Team plans scale for multiple users. Copy.ai has leaned into GTM (go-to-market) workflows more than other writing tools, making it a natural fit for sales and marketing operations teams who need structured, repeatable content production processes rather than open-ended AI chat.
