Copy.ai started in the first wave of AI writing tools. You picked a template, such as a product description, email, or social caption, answered a few prompts, and got several drafts to edit. It was popular with marketers and small businesses who needed a lot of copy quickly. Over time it moved away from one-off text generation and toward automating sales and marketing processes.

Its later product centered on workflows. A team could chain steps together, for example researching an account, pulling in data, drafting outreach, and writing a CRM update, and let AI agents run the sequence on a schedule or trigger. A data layer called Tables kept the inputs organized, and the aim was to replace manual go-to-market busywork with repeatable automations rather than just generating paragraphs.

Copy.ai was acquired by Fullcast, and the product is being rebranded and integrated into Fullcast's revenue operations platform. That means the independent product and its plans may change, so check the current site before committing. Anyone who just wants an AI to draft copy can do that in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini at no or low cost. Jasper is the other well-known dedicated marketing writing platform, and Writer serves enterprises that need brand controls.
