Munch focuses on video repurposing. You upload a webinar, podcast, interview, or live stream, or connect a link, and it identifies segments likely to perform well as short clips. Rather than simply cutting at fixed intervals, it analyzes the transcript and content and compares it with trending topics and keywords, then proposes clips with captions, titles, and formatting for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn.

Features that support the workflow include automatic captions, reframing to vertical or square, branded templates, and the ability to edit clips before exporting. Social copy and hashtags can be suggested, and a scheduler publishes clips to connected accounts. Marketing teams, agencies, podcasters, and course creators use it to get more from long content without manual editing.

Munch is paid, with plans based on the amount of video processed, and a trial. Clip selection is a guide; people often adjust the choices to fit their own audience and voice. Quality varies with content style, and chat-heavy videos work better than visual ones. Opus Clip is the best-known competitor, with Vizard, Klap, Descript, and Kapwing offering similar features. Munch suits teams that publish a lot of long-form video and want a steady stream of clips.
