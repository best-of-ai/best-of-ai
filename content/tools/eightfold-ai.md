Eightfold AI treats talent as a graph of skills. Its models read resumes, job descriptions, and employee profiles, infer skills, and match people to roles based on what they can do and are likely to learn, not just on keywords or job titles. Recruiters use it to find candidates in a company's applicant database and external sources, and employees can see roles and learning paths that fit their skills.

The platform extends across the employee lifecycle. Modules cover talent acquisition, talent management, internal mobility, and workforce planning, with analytics that show skill gaps and diversity trends. Agent-style features automate steps such as screening and scheduling. It integrates with applicant tracking and HR systems such as Workday and SAP SuccessFactors.

Eightfold is sold to large enterprises on custom contracts. AI in hiring is regulated in a growing number of places and has faced scrutiny over bias and transparency, so employers need audits and clear processes, and candidates have rights regarding automated decisions. Competitors include Beamery, Phenom, HireVue, SeekOut, and the AI features of Workday and SAP. Eightfold suits large organizations with sizable hiring and internal mobility needs.
