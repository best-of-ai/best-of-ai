tl;dv, short for "too long; didn't view," saves people from rewatching meetings. A bot joins your calls to record and transcribe them in many languages, and you can mark highlights during the call. Afterward, the recording is available with an AI-generated summary and a list of action items, plus timestamps so you can jump to the part that matters. Clips can be shared with people who missed the meeting.

The tool is popular with sales and product teams. It can fill in CRM fields in tools like HubSpot and Salesforce, produce reports across many meetings to spot patterns such as customer objections, and send notes to Slack and Notion. Templates tailor summaries to sales calls, user interviews, and team meetings, and search lets you find topics across your library.

tl;dv has a free plan with unlimited recordings and limited AI use, and paid plans for more AI features, integrations, and team administration. Anyone being recorded should be informed, and the transcript accuracy depends on audio quality. Fathom, Fireflies, Otter, Grain, and Gong overlap with it, with Gong aimed at enterprise sales analytics. tl;dv suits teams who want a free, generous option for meeting recordings and highlights.
