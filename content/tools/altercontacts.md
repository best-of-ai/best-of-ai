AlterContacts is built for people whose work depends on client relationships, such as financial advisors, insurance and property agents and consultants. You dictate a quick voice note or record a meeting, and the assistant, Alter, files it to the right client profile and tracks promises and tasks.

It connects to Gmail and Google Calendar, and Microsoft Workspace is also shown. Follow-ups are drafted for you and nothing is sent until you approve, and it checks your inbox for proof that a task was done. Profiles can be enriched from LinkedIn and the web. For meeting recordings you start the recording yourself, and the client must consent.

The app is free to download with a monthly credit allowance. The paid Starter, Pro, Power and Power Plus tiers give more credits, and team and enterprise pricing is by contact. Subscriptions can be bought on the website or through the App Store. It compares itself with transcription apps such as Granola and Otter more than with a CRM.
