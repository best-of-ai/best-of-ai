Spacefast is a hosting home for what AI makes. Ask Claude, ChatGPT, Grok or another assistant to publish a page or an app and you get a live link, with no account needed to start. The site is clear that it isn't an AI builder: it hosts, and it doesn't generate.

Spaces are private by default, with invite, share-link and public options, plus version history with restore and comments on pages. A built-in database, file uploads and stats come with it. You can publish by drag and drop, through Git (main is production and branches are previews) or with a CLI. It supports Next.js, TanStack Start, Astro, PHP and static HTML.

A free personal plan has a small monthly usage allowance and limits on spaces and custom domains. The paid Go and Pro plans raise them, and enterprise adds SSO and an SLA. Unclaimed spaces expire after roughly a day and a half, and claiming one keeps it. Vercel and Netlify are the usual alternatives for developers.
