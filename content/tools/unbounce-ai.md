Unbounce helps marketers create landing pages that convert. A drag-and-drop builder and templates make it possible to publish pages without developers, with popups and sticky bars to capture leads on websites. Marketers connect pages to ad campaigns and test variants to find what performs best.

AI is a core part. Smart Copy generates headlines and body text from a brief, and Smart Traffic uses machine learning to send each visitor to the variant they are most likely to convert on, based on their attributes, which can improve results faster than a standard A/B test. Newer tools analyze pages and suggest improvements, and AI builder features create a page from a prompt.

Unbounce is paid, with plans based on traffic and conversions and a trial. Smart Traffic needs enough traffic to learn, and the generated copy needs human review for brand and accuracy. Alternatives include Instapage, Leadpages, Webflow, Framer, HubSpot landing pages, and Carrd for simple pages. Unbounce suits marketers running paid campaigns.
