Resemble AI builds voices for products and productions. You provide recordings of a speaker, with consent, and the platform trains a voice model that can read any text in that voice, or convert one person's speech into another voice in real time. Companies use it for branded voices, localization, games, call centers, and media. An API makes it possible to generate speech in applications, and the system supports multiple languages and emotional control.

The company also works on the risks of synthetic voices. Its detection tools aim to identify AI-generated audio and deepfakes, which has become important for banks, media organizations, and security teams. Watermarking features mark generated audio so it can be traced. It also released an open-source text-to-speech model, which lets developers experiment with voice generation locally.

Resemble AI is paid, with pricing based on usage and enterprise plans, and a way for developers to begin. Voice cloning raises serious consent and fraud concerns, so responsible providers verify permission from the speaker. Quality depends on the recording quality and the amount of data. ElevenLabs is the best-known competitor in cloning, with WellSaid, Murf, Respeecher, and Cartesia offering other approaches. It's well suited to companies that need both generation and tools to detect misuse.
