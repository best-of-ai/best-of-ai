Together AI focuses on open models. Developers call popular open-weight models for text, code, images, and more through an OpenAI-compatible API, and the platform's optimized inference stack delivers speed and competitive prices. It includes serverless endpoints for quick use, dedicated endpoints for steady workloads, and batch processing to reduce cost.

The company also supports customization and training. Teams can fine-tune models on their data, including with reinforcement methods, and rent GPU clusters for large training runs, with an emphasis on cutting-edge hardware. Together's researchers have contributed techniques such as FlashAttention that improve model efficiency, which feeds into its products.

Together AI has a pay-as-you-go model priced per token or GPU time, with credits for new users and enterprise agreements. Its catalog follows the open-source ecosystem, so it lacks closed models from major labs. Competitors include Fireworks AI, Groq, Cerebras, Baseten, Replicate, and OpenRouter as an aggregator. Together AI suits developers who want to build on open models.
