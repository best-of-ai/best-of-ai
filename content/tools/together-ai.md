Together AI's core value is inference cost. It consistently offers some of the lowest prices per million tokens for popular open-source models like Llama 3 70B, Mixtral 8x7B, and Qwen — often 50–80% cheaper than running equivalent models on general-purpose cloud GPU instances. For applications that make millions of API calls per month, this cost difference is significant. The API is OpenAI-compatible, so switching from OpenAI to Together AI (or using Together AI as a cheaper alternative for less critical tasks) is typically a base URL and model name change.

The fine-tuning service lets you train a customised version of a supported open model on your own data, which is deployed to a Together-hosted endpoint when complete. This gives you the quality benefits of domain-specific training without managing GPU clusters or ML infrastructure. The playground environment lets you experiment with prompts and models interactively, compare outputs across different model options, and test system prompts before integrating them into an application.

Together AI is a pay-per-token service with no monthly minimum — you pay only for what you use, with a small credit provided on signup for evaluation. The model catalogue includes LLMs, embedding models, and FLUX image generation. Enterprise customers can negotiate dedicated capacity, SLAs, and private model deployment. Startups building on open models — particularly those with high token volumes where OpenAI pricing becomes a meaningful business expense — frequently migrate to Together AI as they scale and optimise their AI infrastructure costs.
