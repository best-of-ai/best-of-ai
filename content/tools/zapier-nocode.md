Zapier lets people automate work between apps without programming. A Zap starts with a trigger, such as a new form entry, and performs actions in other apps, like adding a row to a spreadsheet and sending a Slack message. It connects thousands of products.

AI has become part of the platform. Users describe an automation in plain language, add AI steps that summarize or classify data, and build agents that act across apps. Tables, forms, and interfaces let teams build small apps around their automations.

Zapier has a free plan and paid tiers by tasks. Costs grow with volume. A second listing covers Zapier's workflow-automation side. Alternatives include Make, n8n, Power Automate, Workato, and Pipedream. Zapier suits non-technical teams automating tasks.
