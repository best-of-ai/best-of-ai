Users add items with photos, tag them with barcodes or QR codes, and scan to update counts. Low-stock alerts prompt reordering. Reports show stock by folder and value.

A free plan covers basic use, and paid plans add more items and users.

inFlow and Zoho Inventory are alternatives. Sortly suits inventory managers at small operations.
