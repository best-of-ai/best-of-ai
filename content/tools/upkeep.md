UpKeep helps maintenance teams plan and track their work. The core of the product is work orders, preventive maintenance schedules, asset history, and parts inventory, with a mobile app for technicians. Teams in manufacturing, facilities, food service, fleets, and healthcare use it.

Nova is the AI layer. It can generate work orders, build custom apps, flag problems in your data, and answer questions from technicians in the field by voice. The goal is to cut data entry and make it easier to get answers while standing at a machine or in a mechanical room.

There is no permanent free plan, but there is a free trial that does not need a credit card, and paid tiers are priced per user. Fiix has a free tier and IBM Maximo serves larger enterprises. UpKeep is a good fit for facility and maintenance managers who want a quick setup and a mobile-first tool.
