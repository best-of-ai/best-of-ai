Zeni provides outsourced finance for startups. Companies connect their bank accounts, and Zeni handles bookkeeping, accounts payable, tax, and CFO services, with a dashboard showing cash, burn, and runway in real time. Humans check the AI's work.

The service replaces a part-time accountant or finance hire for early-stage companies. Monthly reports help founders and investors understand the business.

Zeni is a subscription service. Alternatives include Pilot, Bench, Puzzle, Digits, and a traditional accountant. Zeni suits startups wanting outsourced finance.
