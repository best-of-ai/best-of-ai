Pory turns spreadsheets and databases into websites. A user connects an Airtable base or Google Sheet, designs pages and layouts with a visual editor, and publishes a directory, portal, or dashboard. Logins and permissions control who sees what.

It is popular for member directories, job boards, simple CRMs, and client portals. Because the data stays in the source, updating the spreadsheet updates the site.

Pory has a free tier and paid plans. Complex logic is limited compared with full app builders. Alternatives include Softr, Glide, Stacker, Noloco, and Bubble. Pory suits non-developers building front ends on Airtable or Sheets.
