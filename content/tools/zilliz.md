Zilliz Cloud runs Milvus as a service. Teams avoid operating clusters themselves and get a managed database for embeddings with features such as automatic scaling, backups, and security controls. It supports search for recommendations, retrieval for AI applications, and image or audio similarity.

Zilliz created Milvus and contributes to the open-source project, which means customers can move between self-hosting and the managed service. Hybrid search, filtering, and multi-tenant setups serve production workloads.

Zilliz has a free tier and usage-based plans. Alternatives include Pinecone, Qdrant, Weaviate, Chroma, and turbopuffer. Zilliz suits teams wanting managed Milvus.
