LangSmith helps teams understand and improve AI applications. Developers send traces from their applications, and LangSmith shows each step: prompts, retrieved documents, tool calls, and model outputs, with timing and cost. This makes it possible to find where an agent went wrong.

Evaluation is the second pillar. Teams build datasets from real traffic, run experiments across prompt or model versions, and score results with automated evaluators or human review. Monitoring dashboards and alerts track quality in production, and a deployment product hosts agents built with LangGraph.

LangSmith has a free tier and paid plans, with self-hosting for enterprises. It integrates most smoothly with LangChain tools but works with other frameworks. Alternatives include Langfuse, Braintrust, Arize Phoenix, Helicone, and Weights & Biases Weave. LangSmith suits teams building agents with LangChain and LangGraph.
