Slack AI addresses the problem of catching up. When you return from time off or join a busy channel, it can summarize what happened, list decisions, and highlight what needs your attention. Thread summaries cut long discussions into a few lines, and daily recaps cover channels you choose, so you can stay informed without reading every message.

AI search turns Slack into a place to ask questions. Instead of hunting for keywords, you can ask something like what the timeline is for a project, and receive an answer drawn from messages, files, and connected apps, with links to the sources. Slackbot is being reshaped as an assistant that can help draft messages, find information, and take actions, and Salesforce's Agentforce agents can work in Slack. Huddle notes summarize audio calls.

Slack AI is included with higher paid plans or sold as an add-on depending on the plan, and it only uses data from your workspace, with Slack stating it does not use customer data to train its models. Summaries can miss nuance or context from outside Slack. Microsoft Teams with Copilot, Google Chat with Gemini, and Notion AI are alternatives. Slack AI makes the most sense for teams who live in Slack and want to reduce message overload.
