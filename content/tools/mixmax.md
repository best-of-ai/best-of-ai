Mixmax turns Gmail into a sales workspace. It tracks opens, clicks, and replies, lets you schedule messages, and adds a calendar tool so recipients can book meetings from your email. Templates and snippets speed up repetitive writing, and polls, surveys, and documents can be embedded. It is used by sales, recruiting, and customer success teams that live in their inbox.

Sequences are a main feature: automated multi-step email campaigns that send follow-ups until someone replies, with rules for pausing and routing. Integrations connect with Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach alternatives, Slack, and Zapier, so activity is logged in the CRM. AI helps draft emails, personalize at scale, and suggest next steps, and call recording and meeting notes have been added in some plans.

Mixmax is paid, with plans per user and a free tier that has limited usage. It works best for teams that want to stay inside Gmail instead of adopting a separate platform. Because it tracks recipients, privacy rules and etiquette on tracking apply. Competitors include Outreach and Salesloft for larger sales teams, Apollo and Lemlist for outbound, Yesware and Mailtrack for simpler tracking, and HubSpot Sales Hub for an integrated CRM. It fits small and mid-sized sales teams.
