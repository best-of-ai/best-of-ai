Superhuman is built around a single, opinionated premise: that most professionals waste significant time and mental energy in email, and the right product design can dramatically improve both speed and focus. Every action in Superhuman has a keyboard shortcut. Triage flows take one or two keystrokes. The interface strips away everything that isn't essential to reading and responding quickly. The result is an email experience that feels like it was designed for efficiency rather than maximum feature surface area.

AI Reply drafts a complete email response from a few words you type — "decline politely, suggest next week" becomes a three-sentence reply in your writing style. The AI reads your previous emails to match your tone rather than sounding generic. Superhuman Instant Reply suggests short replies you can send in one click. Priority markers surface emails that need attention now, and reminders prompt you to follow up on emails where you're waiting for a response. The split inbox separates important correspondence from notifications and newsletters, keeping the primary view focused.

Superhuman is $30/month per person with no free tier — one of the most expensive email clients available. The company's positioning is that it's aimed at executives, founders, and knowledge workers whose time has high enough value that saving an hour per day on email is worth the price. The onboarding includes a 30-minute one-on-one setup call. The typical profile is someone spending two or more hours a day in email who has already tried conventional productivity systems and found them insufficient. For people who spend significant time in email professionally, the speed improvement is often immediately apparent.
