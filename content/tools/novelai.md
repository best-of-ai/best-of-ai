NovelAI started as a tool for writers who wanted an AI collaborator for fiction. In its editor, you write a passage and the AI continues it, with controls for style, memory, and the characters and world in your story. Features such as lorebooks store details about characters and places so the AI keeps them consistent, and you can edit, retry, and steer the output freely. It's used by hobbyist novelists, roleplayers, and fan fiction writers.

The service also includes image generation, focused on anime and illustration styles, with tools for pose and style control, inpainting, and variations. It trains and hosts its own text models instead of relying on those of the large AI labs. Stories are encrypted so that, according to the company, even it cannot read them, which appeals to users who write sensitive or adult material.

NovelAI is paid, with tiers that differ in available models, context length, and image generation credits, and no free tier beyond a trial. The less restrictive content policy is both a draw and a reason for caution about what you create. Alternatives include AI Dungeon, Sudowrite, Claude and ChatGPT with their content rules, and locally run models through tools like SillyTavern. NovelAI suits writers who want a dedicated, private creative writing tool.
