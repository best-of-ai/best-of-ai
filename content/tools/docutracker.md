Docutracker is a powerful document analytics platform that lets you know exactly when and how recipients interact with your proposals and sales materials. The tool notifies you instantly when someone opens a document, tracks repeated views, and shows the last activity timestamp so you can follow up at the perfect time. It provides page-level insights, revealing which sections are read, skipped, or revisited, and tracks completion rates to distinguish serious prospects from casual browsers. With secure, trackable sharing links, password protection, and expiration dates, Docutracker ensures your documents are seen only by the right people. A unified dashboard aggregates engagement across all your documents to identify your most interested leads and highest-performing materials. By measuring real engagement rather than just opens, Docutracker helps you prioritize follow-ups and make smarter sales decisions.
