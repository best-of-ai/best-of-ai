Uizard is a design tool built for speed and accessibility. You can upload a photo of a sketch or a screenshot of an existing app and have it converted into an editable design, or describe a screen in text and let the AI generate a layout. A library of templates, components, and themes helps non-designers build presentable prototypes for products, websites, and apps.

Collaboration features let teams comment and iterate, and prototypes can be linked into clickable flows. An Autodesigner feature creates multi-screen designs from a prompt, and the AI can generate color themes from an image. It's popular with founders, product managers, and consultants who need quick mockups to communicate ideas.

Uizard has a free plan with limits and paid plans for more projects and features. It produces less refined output than professional design software, and designers often move to Figma for production work. Alternatives include Figma with its AI features, Google Stitch, Visily, Claude Design, Framer, and v0 for code-based UI. Uizard suits people who need prototypes quickly and have limited design experience.
