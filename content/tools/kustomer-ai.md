Kustomer takes a CRM-style approach to support. Instead of treating each ticket as an isolated item, it builds a timeline for each customer that includes every conversation, order, and interaction across channels. Agents see the full history in one view, which reduces the need to ask customers to repeat themselves. Channels include email, chat, SMS, social messaging, and voice, and workflow rules automate routing and tagging.

AI features have grown in importance. AI agents can answer common questions, check order status, and complete simple actions, escalating complex cases to humans with context. Tools for human agents summarize long histories, suggest replies, and classify issues, and insights analyze conversation patterns to find problems in products or processes. The system connects with e-commerce and business platforms to bring in order and account data.

Kustomer is sold on subscription plans, with pricing by agent and add-ons for AI features, and it is typically used by mid-sized and larger consumer brands. It has gone through ownership changes in recent years, so confirm the roadmap with the vendor. Competitors include Zendesk, Gorgias, Intercom, Salesforce Service Cloud, and Freshdesk. Kustomer suits businesses that value a customer-centered view of every interaction.
