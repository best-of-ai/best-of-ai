Magnific AI takes a low-resolution image and produces a larger version with added, invented detail. Users control how much creative freedom the AI takes with settings such as creativity and resemblance, and can add a prompt describing what the detail should look like. This makes it different from conventional upscalers.

Artists use it to polish AI-generated images and to turn rough concept art into detailed illustrations. Designers use it to prepare images for large formats. The company was acquired by Freepik, and the technology is available through Magnific's own site and Freepik's tools.

Pricing is subscription based with no long free tier. Heavily creative settings can change faces and objects, so results need review. Alternatives include Topaz Gigapixel, Krea, Upscayl, Clarity Upscaler, and Let's Enhance. Magnific AI suits artists who want imaginative upscaling.
