Cramd is an AI-driven study platform designed to help students learn more efficiently by transforming raw content—like notes, documents, or videos—into interactive study tools such as flashcards and quizzes. Built for high schoolers, college students, and lifelong learners, Cramd offers features like spaced repetition, gamified progress tracking, and collaborative study modes. Whether you’re preparing for an exam or reviewing coursework, Cramd personalizes your learning experience to make studying smarter, faster, and more engaging.
