Portkey sits between applications and model providers. Developers send requests to Portkey, which forwards them to the chosen provider and handles retries, fallbacks to other models, caching, and load balancing. This increases reliability and makes it easy to switch models.

The platform adds visibility and governance: logs and analytics on cost and latency, prompt management, guardrails that check inputs and outputs, and budgets and access controls for teams. It supports the Model Context Protocol and agent frameworks.

Portkey has a free tier and paid plans, and the gateway is partly open source. An extra layer adds a dependency. Alternatives include LiteLLM, OpenRouter, Cloudflare AI Gateway, Helicone, and Kong AI Gateway. Portkey suits teams running AI applications in production.
