Arcadia has two sides. For consumers, it helps people use renewable energy even if they cannot install solar panels. Customers subscribe to a share of a local community solar project and receive credits on their utility bill, or choose plans that match their usage with renewable generation. The platform tracks bills and savings in one account.

For businesses, Arcadia offers access to utility data. It connects to thousands of utilities to pull bill and usage information through APIs, which energy software companies, solar developers, and sustainability teams use to analyze consumption. This data layer is a major part of its business and helps other companies build energy products.

Community solar availability depends on state rules and utility territories, and savings vary. Customers should read the terms of subscriptions. Alternatives include Nexamp, Solar Landscape, Palmetto, Octopus Energy in some markets, and Genability for utility data. Arcadia suits households seeking renewable options and developers needing utility data.
