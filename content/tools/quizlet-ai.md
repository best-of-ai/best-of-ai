Quizlet is a long-running tool for learning through flashcards. Students create or find sets of terms and definitions, then study them with modes such as flashcards, learn sessions that adapt to what you miss, matching games, and practice tests. A huge library of user-created sets covers school subjects, languages, and professional exams, and teachers use it to build class activities.

AI has added help with creating and understanding material. Tools can generate flashcards or quizzes from uploaded notes, textbook pages, or slides, saving the effort of typing each card. An AI tutor can explain a concept, quiz you, and give step-by-step help, and features track progress and suggest what to review. Study guides and summaries can be produced from your own materials.

Quizlet has a free tier with ads and limits and a paid plan that unlocks more AI features, offline use, and removal of ads. AI-generated cards can contain errors, and user-made sets of unknown quality still circulate widely, so check important facts. Using AI tools for graded assignments may violate school rules. Anki is a powerful free spaced repetition app, Brainscape and Knowt are similar, and Chegg and Khanmigo offer help with homework. Quizlet is popular for its simplicity and large library.
