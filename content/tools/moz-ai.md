Moz was one of the earliest SEO software companies, and Moz Pro remains a staple for agencies and in-house marketers. Users research keywords, audit sites for technical problems, track rankings over time, and analyze links, with Domain Authority, Moz's well-known score, used as a quick measure of a site's strength.

The MozBar browser extension overlays metrics on search results and web pages, making competitor checks quick. Reporting tools produce client-ready summaries, and newer AI features suggest priorities and help with content. A separate local product manages business listings.

Moz Pro is paid with a trial. Its link index is smaller than Ahrefs and its data refresh slower than some rivals, so many professionals use it alongside other tools. Alternatives include Semrush, Ahrefs, SE Ranking, Mangools, and Ubersuggest. Moz Pro suits small teams and agencies that value its metrics and interface.
