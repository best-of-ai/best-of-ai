Publer provides the essentials of social media planning at a modest price. Users connect accounts, build a calendar, schedule posts and recurring content, and see analytics on performance. Bulk scheduling and a library of media help manage volume.

Features include collaboration and approvals, link in bio pages, and a social inbox. The AI assistant writes captions, suggests hashtags, and repurposes content, and tools recycle evergreen posts.

Publer has a free plan with limits and paid tiers by social accounts. Alternatives include Buffer, Later, Metricool, Hootsuite, and Pallyy. Publer suits creators and small teams who want an affordable scheduler.
