IXL provides practice at scale. Students work through skills organized by grade level, and the questions get easier or harder based on their answers. Instant feedback explains mistakes, and a real-time diagnostic estimates the level at which a student is working across subjects.

Teachers and parents get reports showing progress, strengths, and gaps, and suggested next skills. The platform aligns with state standards, which helps classroom use. AI features offer personalized recommendations and help with tutoring, and the company offers add-ons such as a writing coach.

IXL is sold by subscription to families and to schools and districts. Heavy reliance on drill can feel repetitive for some students. Alternatives include Khan Academy, Prodigy, DreamBox, Zearn, and Duolingo for languages. IXL suits schools and families wanting structured practice.
