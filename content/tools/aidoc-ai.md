Aidoc builds AI for radiology workflows. When a scan such as a head CT or chest CT is taken, Aidoc's algorithms analyze it in the background and, if they detect a suspected critical finding, move the study to the top of the radiologist's worklist and notify the relevant team. This shortens the time to review urgent cases like intracranial hemorrhage, pulmonary embolism, and aortic dissection, where minutes matter.

The company has grown from single-finding triage into a broader platform. It offers an operating system for deploying many algorithms, including those from other developers, and coordinates care for conditions that need multidisciplinary response. Recent work includes foundation models for imaging and tools that combine imaging with clinical data, and a growing number of FDA clearances.

Aidoc is sold to hospitals and imaging groups on enterprise contracts, and is widely deployed in the United States and elsewhere. AI triage tools assist rather than replace radiologists, and false positives and negatives can occur. Competitors include Viz.ai, RapidAI, Annalise.ai, Lunit, and Nanox AI, which includes the former Zebra Medical Vision. Aidoc suits hospitals aiming to speed up diagnosis of urgent conditions.
