Balto sits beside an agent during a call. The software listens to the conversation, recognizes where it is in a script or process, and shows prompts on the agent's screen: a question to ask, a compliance statement to read, a way to answer an objection. The aim is consistent performance across a team and less reliance on memory.

Supervisors benefit as well. Balto scores calls automatically, shows which behaviors lead to better outcomes, and reduces the number of calls reviewers must listen to by hand. New agents can ramp faster because the software coaches them in real time. Industries with strict rules, such as insurance and financial services, use it for compliance.

Balto is sold to contact centers on custom contracts. Agents sometimes find real-time prompts distracting if they are not well tuned. Competitors include Cresta, Observe.AI, Uniphore, NICE, and Gong for sales calls. Balto suits call centers that want live coaching.
