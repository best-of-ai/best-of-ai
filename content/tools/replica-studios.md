Replica Studios was an Australian startup that made AI voices aimed at creative productions. Its library offered voices of professional actors who had agreed to license their performances, and its tools let game developers and filmmakers generate dialogue with emotion and style. Integrations for game engines such as Unreal and Unity made it easy to put placeholder or final voices into a project, and it also supported voice conversion.

The company drew attention for working with actors instead of against them. It signed an agreement with the actors' union SAG-AFTRA on terms for licensing digital voice replicas in games, which some actors welcomed and others criticized as inadequate. That debate was part of a wider fight over how AI voices should be used in entertainment, which continues.

Replica stopped providing its service and told users to download their data, offering refunds for recent purchases. It has no continuing product. Teams that used it now turn to ElevenLabs, Respeecher, Resemble AI, WellSaid, and Cartesia for voice generation, or to human voice actors under clear contracts. Anyone building game dialogue pipelines should check that their provider's licensing with performers is sound. This entry stays for reference, since older articles still describe Replica.
