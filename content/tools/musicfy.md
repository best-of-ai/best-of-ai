Musicfy offers several music tools in one place. Users can generate a track from a text prompt, convert one instrument or vocal to another voice or sound, separate a song into stems, and create voice covers with AI voices. It is aimed at creators who want to experiment with songs without a studio.

The same tools raise rights questions. Copying a real artist's voice without permission can violate publicity and copyright rules, and platforms and labels remove such content. Generated music has varying licensing terms, so read them before releasing or monetizing songs.

Musicfy has a free tier and paid plans. Quality varies and long, coherent songs are difficult. Alternatives include Suno, Udio, Kits AI, Jammable, and ElevenLabs music. Musicfy suits hobbyists exploring AI music tools.
