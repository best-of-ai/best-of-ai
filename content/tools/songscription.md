Songscription converts music you can hear into notation you can read. You give it an audio file, a video link, or a photo of sheet music, and it returns sheet music, MIDI, guitar tabs, or MusicXML. It handles several instruments and can produce piano arrangements and lead sheets.

Transcription is never perfect, so the built-in editor matters. You can fix notes in a piano roll or the score view, and an AI assistant called Annie helps with edits. Musicians use it to learn songs by ear, teachers use it to make arrangements for students, and composers use it to capture ideas quickly.

A free tier handles short transcriptions of about half a minute, and longer ones need a free trial or a paid plan. Check the accuracy on complex or dense music, since errors increase with polyphony and noisy recordings.
