ZeroTwo is a multi-model chat workspace. A user picks a model for each conversation or compares several, and keeps history, files, and prompts in one place. It supports web search, image generation, and document analysis.

For people who use several AI products, a combined interface can simplify life and cost. Pricing is typically a single subscription instead of many.

Access to the newest features of each provider may lag the official apps. ZeroTwo has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Poe, TypingMind, ChatHub, OpenRouter's chat, and Open WebUI. ZeroTwo suits power users who work across models.
