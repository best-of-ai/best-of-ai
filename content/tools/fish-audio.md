Fish Audio generates synthetic speech with an emphasis on voice variety. Users type text, choose a voice, and get audio. A cloning feature creates a voice from a short sample, and a public library offers voices shared by other users, including character and celebrity-style options.

Creators use it for narration, videos, games, and podcasts, and developers integrate it through an API for apps and agents. The company has released open models for speech synthesis, which has helped it build a community of users and researchers.

Fish Audio has a free tier and paid plans by usage. Voice cloning raises consent and misuse concerns, so clone only voices you have permission to use, and be careful with community voices that imitate real people. Alternatives include ElevenLabs, Cartesia, OpenAI voice models, Murf, and Play.ht (now shut down). Fish Audio suits creators and developers seeking flexible voices.
