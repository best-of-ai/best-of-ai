Wan generates video from descriptions. Users provide a prompt or an image, and the model produces a clip with motion and detail. Alibaba has released several versions, including open-weight models that run on local hardware and have attracted a large community of tools and fine-tunes.

Creators and filmmakers use Wan for text-to-video, image-to-video, and editing tasks, through hosted sites and through open-source workflows such as ComfyUI. Open availability makes it a base for research and custom pipelines.

Wan can be tried free on hosted services with credits, and running it locally needs a strong GPU. As a Chinese company's model, data and licensing terms vary by version. Alternatives include HunyuanVideo, Kling, Runway, Google Veo, and Pika. Wan suits creators and developers who want capable open video models.
