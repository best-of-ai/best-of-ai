ElliQ is a physical device, not an app. It sits on a table and talks with the person using it. Older adults can chat with it, ask for music and news, play games, do light exercise, and get reminders for medications and appointments. Family members can send photos that ElliQ displays.

The caregiver side matters for families. Caregivers receive alerts and can see how the person is doing, which gives some reassurance when they live far away. The product is meant to reduce loneliness and encourage routines, not to replace human contact or medical care.

ElliQ is sold as a subscription with the device included, and the terms vary by program, so check the current offer. A companion robot is a personal choice, and some people prefer a smart speaker or regular phone calls. For caregivers looking for something that talks back and keeps a daily routine, it is one of the few products designed specifically for this.
