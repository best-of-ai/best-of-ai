A host runs a quiz, and players join with a code and answer on phones or laptops. Scores appear after each question. AI features can generate questions from a topic or a document.

A free version covers basic games, and paid plans add reports and larger groups.

Quizizz and Mentimeter are alternatives. Kahoot! suits educators who want energetic review sessions.
