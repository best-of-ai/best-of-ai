Lovo makes synthetic speech for creators and businesses. Users type or paste a script, choose a voice, and adjust tone, speed, and emphasis to generate narration. Genny, the editor, lets them place speech alongside video, images, and music in one timeline.

Voice cloning and a marketplace of voices expand the options, and the platform supports many languages. Typical uses are explainer videos, e-learning, ads, and audiobooks. An API provides access for developers.

Lovo has a free tier and paid plans by usage. The company has faced a lawsuit from voice actors over its use of their voices, a reminder to check licensing for any voice you use commercially. Alternatives include ElevenLabs, Murf, WellSaid Labs, Speechify, and Fish Audio. Lovo suits creators needing flexible narration.
