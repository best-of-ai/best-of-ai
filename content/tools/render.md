Render simplifies hosting. A developer connects a repository, and Render builds and runs the app, handles TLS certificates, and scales as needed. It supports web services, static sites, cron jobs, private services, and managed Postgres and Redis.

Preview environments build each pull request automatically, and infrastructure can be defined in a file. Teams like its predictable pricing and straightforward interface, and it works for AI apps that need background workers and databases.

Render has a free tier with limits and paid plans by compute. Free services spin down when idle. Alternatives include Railway, Fly.io, Heroku, Vercel, and DigitalOcean App Platform. Render suits developers wanting simple deployment.
