Yotpo helps online stores build trust and repeat business. It collects reviews with photos, shows them on product pages and in search, and runs loyalty programs and referral offers. SMS and email tools extend marketing.

AI summarizes reviews into themes, flags issues, and drafts responses, and analytics tie content to conversion. Integrations cover Shopify, BigCommerce, and other platforms.

Yotpo has a free tier and paid plans that scale with orders. Alternatives include Okendo, Judge.me, Stamped.io, Klaviyo, and Trustpilot. Yotpo suits growing online stores.
