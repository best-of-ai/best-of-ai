Attio starts from the idea that every company sells differently. Instead of fixed objects like leads and opportunities, teams build their own structures, such as customers, partners, or investors, and link them. The interface feels more like a spreadsheet and a database than a traditional CRM, so it is easier to reshape.

Data fills in with little typing. Attio connects to email and calendar to log interactions, enriches company and contact details, and shows the strength of relationships. AI features summarize accounts, answer questions about the pipeline, and fill in fields from conversations. Workflow automation moves records, sends notifications, and updates data.

Attio has a free plan for small teams and paid tiers by seats and features. It suits startups and growth-stage companies, and it has fewer ready-made enterprise features than Salesforce. Alternatives include HubSpot, Pipedrive, Folk, Close, and Salesforce. Attio suits teams who want to shape their own CRM.
