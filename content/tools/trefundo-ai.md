Trefundo is an AI-powered platform that takes the stress out of dealing with non-refundable hotel bookings. Travelers often face unexpected changes—plans get canceled, schedules shift, or emergencies arise—only to discover that their hotel reservations can’t be refunded. Trefundo is designed to solve exactly this problem.

Using advanced automation and AI negotiation strategies, Trefundo works on your behalf to recover money from bookings that are typically considered “lost.” Whether through refund requests, policy loopholes, resale opportunities, or consumer protection laws, the platform explores every possible option to maximize your chances of saving money.
