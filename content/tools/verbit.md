Verbit blends machine and human work. AI produces a first draft of the transcript, and professional editors review and correct it, which yields higher accuracy than automatic transcription alone. Services include captioning for live events, court reporting, and transcripts of recorded media.

Education is a big market: universities use Verbit to caption lectures and meet accessibility requirements. Its platform integrates with video tools and learning systems, and the company has acquired several transcription and captioning firms.

Verbit is sold on custom and per-minute pricing. Human review adds cost and time. Alternatives include Rev, 3Play Media, Otter.ai, Trint, and Amara. Verbit suits organizations needing accurate captions and transcripts.
