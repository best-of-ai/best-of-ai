Satellogic builds and operates its own satellites with the goal of making high-resolution imagery affordable and frequent. Its NewSat satellites capture sharp images and multispectral and hyperspectral data, and the company has emphasized low production costs, which allows it to launch many satellites and offer tasking and archive imagery. Customers use the data for mapping, agriculture, infrastructure monitoring, defense, and environmental analysis.

The company layers analytics on top of imagery. Machine learning detects objects and changes, such as new construction or vehicle movement, and produces insights at scale, and cloud tools and APIs let customers search the archive and request new captures. It also provides satellites and data to government customers for sovereign needs.

Satellogic is a public company that sells through contracts and subscriptions, with pricing varying by area and use. Like other imagery providers, results depend on weather and revisit rates. Competitors include Planet Labs for daily imagery, Vantor, formerly part of Maxar, Airbus, BlackSky, and Capella Space for radar. Satellogic suits organizations that need frequent high-resolution views of the Earth.
