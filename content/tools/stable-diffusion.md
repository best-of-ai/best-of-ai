Stable Diffusion changed image generation by being openly available. Rather than a service you rent, the models can be downloaded and run on a consumer GPU, which let anyone generate images privately and without per-image fees. Starting from prompts, the models create pictures in many styles, and later versions improved quality, resolution, and text rendering. Stability AI released several generations, including larger models for higher quality.

A huge ecosystem grew around it. Interfaces such as ComfyUI and Automatic1111 give control over the process, ControlNet guides composition from poses or sketches, and LoRAs let people fine-tune styles or characters with a small number of images. Communities share thousands of models, and hosted services like NightCafe, Leonardo, and Mage.space offer it in the browser. Developers embed the models in applications and pipelines.

The models are released under licenses that allow broad use, with community terms that limit commercial use above certain revenue thresholds, so read the license for each version. Newer models from Black Forest Labs, with FLUX, and others often surpass it in quality, and Stability AI has faced business difficulties. Running locally requires a capable GPU and some technical skill. Midjourney, FLUX, and OpenAI's image models are leading alternatives. Stable Diffusion remains important for customization.
