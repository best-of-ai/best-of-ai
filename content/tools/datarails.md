Datarails is aimed at finance teams that live in Excel and do not want to leave it. The platform consolidates data from hundreds of sources into a single governed layer, then lets analysts keep working in the spreadsheets they already know while the numbers update underneath.

Its modules cover FP&A, month-end close, cash management, and spend control, with audit trails and permissions so changes can be traced. A ticketing feature handles internal requests to the finance team. The AI Connector links the consolidated data to AI tools, so a CFO or controller can ask questions in plain language and get answers drawn from the company's own figures.

Datarails does not list prices or a free plan. You request a demo and get a quote. It fits CFOs, controllers, and FP&A staff at growing companies who want better reporting without a full ERP overhaul. Teams that want a spreadsheet-free planning suite will find other products closer to what they need.
