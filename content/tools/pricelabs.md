PriceLabs automates the pricing decisions short-term rental hosts used to make by hand. Its algorithm looks at local demand, seasonality, events, and comparable listings, then updates nightly rates on your calendar. You can set minimum and maximum prices and rules for stay lengths, and bulk calendar tools let you edit many listings at once.

Other features support the pricing work. Market dashboards show how your area is performing, portfolio analytics compare properties, Revenue Estimator Pro helps assess a purchase, and Listing Optimizer reviews your listing content. It connects with a large number of property management systems and channels, so prices flow to Airbnb, Vrbo, and others without manual copying.

A free trial is available without a credit card, and paid plans are listed on the pricing page. PriceLabs serves individual hosts, property managers, and hotels. Hospitable and Hostaway include their own pricing tools, but dedicated pricing software like PriceLabs usually gives more control over the rules.
