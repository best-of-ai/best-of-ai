Maxma works as a growth team with six roles: media buyer, analyst, creative, marketing ops, data scientist and engineer. It builds ads and runs campaigns on Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn and Microsoft Ads, and it also covers ChatGPT Ads.

Proposed changes wait for your approval by default. It reads from Shopify, GA4, Tag Manager, Google Drive and GitHub, and you can work with it from Slack. Incrementality tests and marketing mix modeling are available as add-ons, and they measure whether the ad spend actually caused the growth.

New accounts get a 30-day trial with Pro access. The Starter, Standard, Pro and Enterprise tiers scale with monthly ad spend and seats, with credit allowances and top-ups, and agencies get custom pricing. Shopify merchants, SaaS companies and in-house marketing teams are the main audience.
