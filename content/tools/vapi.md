Vapi handles the hard parts of voice agents. Developers define an assistant with a prompt and tools, connect a phone number, and Vapi orchestrates speech-to-text, the language model, and text-to-speech, with handling for interruptions and turn-taking. Bring-your-own provider choices allow swapping components.

Use cases include customer support lines, appointment scheduling, outbound calls, and voice assistants in apps. The platform includes testing, analytics, and call transfer features.

Vapi is billed by minutes plus provider costs. Voice calls are regulated, and callers should know they are speaking with an AI. Alternatives include Retell AI, Bland AI, Synthflow, Twilio ConversationRelay, and LiveKit. Vapi suits developers building voice agents.
