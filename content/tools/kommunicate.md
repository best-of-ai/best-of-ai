Kommunicate combines a chatbot builder with a shared inbox for agents. Businesses add a chat widget to their site or app, train a bot on help articles and FAQs, and let it answer routine questions, handing the conversation to a person when the bot cannot help or the customer asks.

The platform supports generative AI models and connects to knowledge bases, so answers draw on company content. Multi-channel support extends to messaging apps, and analytics show deflection rates and satisfaction. Integrations with CRMs and helpdesks keep data in sync.

Kommunicate has a free trial and paid plans by chats and features. Bots need monitoring so they do not give wrong answers. Alternatives include Intercom Fin, Tidio, Freshchat, Chatbase, and Zendesk. Kommunicate suits small and mid-sized support teams.
