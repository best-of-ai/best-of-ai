Softr turns data into software. A user connects Airtable, Google Sheets, or its own database, and builds pages with blocks such as lists, forms, dashboards, and charts. Logins and permissions control which users see which records.

Typical products include client portals, directories, simple CRMs, and internal tools. The AI builder creates an app from a description, and workflows automate actions. Hosting and a custom domain are included.

Softr has a free plan and paid tiers by users and features. Complex logic is limited compared with full code. Alternatives include Glide, Pory, Stacker, Bubble, and Retool. Softr suits non-developers building apps on spreadsheets and databases.
