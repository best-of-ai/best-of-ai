CloudHealth was one of the first tools built for managing cloud costs across several providers. Companies connect their AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud accounts and get a consolidated view of spending, broken down by team, project, or application using tags and rules. Reports show where money goes, budgets raise alerts when spending drifts, and recommendations point to unused resources and oversized instances. That helps finance and engineering teams share a view of cloud costs.

The platform also includes governance and policy features, such as rules that flag noncompliant resources, and tools for committing to discounts like reserved instances and savings plans. A relaunch added a redesigned interface and a generative AI assistant that answers questions about spending and summarizes insights in plain language, aimed at making FinOps accessible to non-technical staff as well as engineers.

CloudHealth was originally independent, was bought by VMware, and now belongs to Broadcom. Broadcom has made Arrow Electronics the sales channel while keeping development in-house. Pricing is based on cloud spend under management and is sold by quote. Since the ownership changes, some customers have looked at alternatives, including Apptio Cloudability from IBM, Flexera, Vantage, and CloudZero, along with the cost tools built into AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. It suits enterprises with large multicloud footprints.
