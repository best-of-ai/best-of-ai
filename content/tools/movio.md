Movio is an avatar video generator. A user selects a digital presenter, pastes or writes a script, chooses a voice and language, and the platform renders a video of the avatar speaking. Backgrounds, text, and images can be added in an editor that works like a slide deck.

The pitch is speed and cost. Filming a presenter needs scheduling, equipment, and editing, while an avatar video can be revised by changing the script and regenerating. Translation into other languages makes the same video usable in several markets, and templates cover common formats.

Movio has a free tier with limits and paid plans. Avatars can look stiff compared with a human presenter, and viewers may notice they are synthetic, so disclosure helps. Alternatives include Synthesia, HeyGen, Elai.io, Hour One, and D-ID. Movio suits small teams producing scripted video.
