Trails makes it simple for anyone to build a step-by-step guide. No copying and pasting screenshots - just record your process and out comes a beautiful guide in just 3 simple steps:

1️⃣ Record any process - Click "capture" in the chrome extension and walk through the workflow. When you're finished, Trails generates a beautiful step-by-step guide.

2️⃣ Share anywhere - Add more detail by adding tips and annotating screenshots. Blur sensitive information to keep names, emails, and other data private.

3️⃣ Easily edit - Share with your team or customers via link, embed in your knowledge base, or export to PDF.

Empower your team with guides that are easy to follow as they are to create.
