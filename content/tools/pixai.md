PixAI is built around anime-style generation. Users type prompts, pick models trained for particular looks, and generate images. A feed of community creations offers inspiration, and sharing prompts lets others learn techniques.

Features include image-to-image transformation, model and LoRA libraries, and tools for creating characters consistently. A mobile app and credit system support casual use.

PixAI has a free tier with daily credits and paid plans. Community models can be trained on artwork without artists' consent, which is controversial, and content rules apply to the platform. Alternatives include Civitai, Leonardo AI, NovelAI, Midjourney with niji, and Tensor.Art. PixAI suits anime fans and illustrators exploring AI art.
