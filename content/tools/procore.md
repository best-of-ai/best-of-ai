Procore is one of the most widely used software platforms in construction. It gives everyone on a building project a shared place to manage drawings and documents, RFIs and submittals, daily logs, schedules, budgets, change orders, and inspections. Instead of email chains and spreadsheets, project information lives in one system with a record of who did what, which reduces disputes and rework.

The platform spans the project lifecycle, from preconstruction bidding and estimating through construction and closeout. Financial tools handle cost tracking, invoicing, and payments, while quality and safety modules support checklists, observations, and incident reports. A marketplace of integrations connects accounting, scheduling, and design tools. AI features help search across documents, summarize information, and automate routine tasks, and agents are being added to take on project management work.

Procore's pricing is unusual: it is typically based on annual construction volume instead of per user, which allows unlimited users but can mean substantial costs. Implementation requires change management across many parties. Autodesk Construction Cloud, now Forma, is the main competitor, with Trimble, Oracle Aconex, and Buildertrend serving different segments. Procore suits mid-sized and large contractors and owners who want one system across projects.
