Cardtree is a tool that helps you create branded, lead-generating profiles in minutes. It solves the problem that most online profiles are just resumes built for recruiters, not stories for customers. Our platform gives you a focused, distraction-free page designed to go beyond a basic digital business card and build instant trust. 

You can add features that drive action, like an embedded calendar for direct bookings or a built-in form to capture new leads. You can also showcase videos and client logos to build credibility with potential customers. For companies, profiles can be centrally managed to keep the brand story consistent for the whole team, which helps shorten sales cycles.
