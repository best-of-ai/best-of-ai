Lever pairs two things that recruiting teams often keep separate: an applicant tracking system for people who apply, and a relationship manager for people you want to attract. In one place, recruiters post jobs, move candidates through stages, schedule interviews, collect feedback, and build talent pools for roles that open later. The pipeline view is visual and quick to scan, which suits teams that hire continuously.

Sourcing and nurture are a focus. Lever can run email sequences to passive candidates, track responses, and bring prospects into the same pipeline as applicants. Interview scheduling is automated so recruiters spend less time on calendars, and scorecards keep feedback structured. Reporting covers the funnel, diversity data, and time to hire. Integrations link it to job boards, HR systems, and assessment tools.

AI features cover areas like drafting job descriptions and outreach, recommending candidates from your database, summarizing interview feedback, and automating repetitive steps. Lever is owned by Employ, which also owns Jobvite and JazzHR, and it continues to be sold under its own name. Pricing is quote-based by company size. Greenhouse is the usual comparison, with Ashby as a newer, analytics-heavy rival. Hiring software that screens people with AI needs careful use, since bias and legal rules vary by location.
