Dynamic Yield helps businesses show each customer the content most likely to interest them. It collects behavior data from web, mobile, and email channels, builds profiles and segments, and uses machine learning to choose which products, banners, or messages to show. Retailers use it for recommendations like customers also bought, personalized homepages, and tailored promotions.

The platform also covers testing and optimization. Teams run experiments to compare versions of pages, with statistics to determine winners, and use triggers such as exit intent to present timely offers. Integrations connect with e-commerce platforms, customer data platforms, and marketing tools, and AI features suggest audiences and automate decisions. It supports use in industries including retail, travel, banking, and media.

Dynamic Yield is sold to mid-sized and large companies on custom contracts, which is costly. Success depends on traffic volume, data quality, and an organization's ability to act on insights. Competitors include Adobe Target, Optimizely, Salesforce Personalization, Bloomreach, and Algolia for search and recommendations. Because it uses behavioral data, privacy rules such as GDPR and CCPA apply. Dynamic Yield suits large e-commerce and digital teams focused on conversion.
