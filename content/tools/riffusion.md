Riffusion started as a research demo that generated music clips by running Stable Diffusion on spectrogram images, an early sign that diffusion models could make audio. It grew into a platform where users type a prompt and get a song with vocals, and into a chat-like agent that helps produce tracks.

The brand changed to Producer.ai, and reports say it has since moved under Google's music efforts, so product names and features may keep changing. Users should check the current site and how their saved songs and rights are handled through any transition.

The tool offers free credits and paid plans. AI music raises rights and royalty questions, and terms for commercial use matter. Alternatives include Suno, Udio, Stable Audio, ElevenLabs music, and Google's Lyria. Riffusion suits people exploring AI song generation.
