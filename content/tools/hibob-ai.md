HiBob is built for companies that want a modern HR system for distributed teams. It stores employee records, handles onboarding and time off, supports performance reviews, and manages compensation and benefits. The interface is designed for employees as well as HR, with a social feel and company-wide features.

Analytics and AI help leaders. Compensation benchmarking compares pay with market data, workforce planning tools model headcount, and an assistant answers HR questions and drafts documents. Payroll integrations and a marketplace of apps connect Bob with finance and IT systems.

HiBob is sold on subscription by quote, priced per employee. It targets mid-sized companies and is less suited to very small firms or global enterprises with complex needs. Alternatives include Personio, BambooHR, Rippling, Workday, and HiBob's rival Factorial. HiBob suits growing companies with distributed teams.
