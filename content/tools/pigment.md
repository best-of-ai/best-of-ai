Pigment is a modern take on planning software. Finance teams build models of their company, including headcount, revenue, and spend, in an environment that feels familiar to spreadsheet users but works with large data volumes and multiple dimensions. Plans link across departments, so a hiring plan updates the budget automatically.

It supports scenario planning, which lets teams test the impact of different assumptions side by side, and produces dashboards and reports for executives. AI agents help analyze variances, generate commentary, and build parts of models from prompts. Integrations bring in data from ERP and CRM systems and data warehouses.

Pigment is sold to mid-sized and large organizations by quote. It competes in a space with established players and requires a modeling effort to set up. Alternatives include Anaplan, Workday Adaptive Planning, Planful, Oracle EPM, and Vena. Pigment suits finance teams that want flexible planning beyond spreadsheets.
