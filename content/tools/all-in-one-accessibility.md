All in One Accessibility® is an AI-based accessibility tool to enable websites to be accessible among people with hearing or vision impairments, motor impaired, colour blind, dyslexia, cognitive & learning impairments, seizure and epileptic, and ADHD problems. It manages website UI and design-related alterations as an accessibility interface.  

All in One Accessibility® installs in just 2 minutes. It reduces the risk of time-consuming accessibility lawsuits.
