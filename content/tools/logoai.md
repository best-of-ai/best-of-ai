LogoAI creates logo options from a short brief. A user enters a name, picks an industry and styles they like, and the tool generates a set of designs. They can customize colors, fonts, and layouts, and download vector files and brand assets.

Small businesses and startups use it for a fast start on branding. Packages include business cards, social media graphics, and brand guidelines. The vector output can be used in print and on the web.

LogoAI is paid, with plans by asset sets. AI logos can resemble each other and may lack the thought of a custom design, and trademark checks are the buyer's responsibility. Alternatives include Looka, Canva, Namecheap Logo Maker, Brandmark, and hiring a designer. LogoAI suits small businesses needing a quick logo.
