Greenlight Guru serves a heavily regulated industry. Medical device makers must document how a product was designed, how risks were assessed, and how problems are corrected. The platform organizes these records, links requirements to risks and tests, and keeps them audit ready, replacing documents scattered in shared drives.

Modules cover design controls, risk management, document control, training, supplier management, and corrective and preventive actions. AI features help draft documents and risk analyses and answer questions about regulations, saving time on repetitive writing. A clinical module supports post-market work.

Greenlight Guru is sold on subscription to device companies from startups to large manufacturers. AI drafts need review by qualified quality staff, and regulatory responsibility stays with the company. Alternatives include MasterControl, Qualio, Veeva Vault QMS, Arena, and Orcanos. Greenlight Guru suits medical device quality and regulatory teams.
