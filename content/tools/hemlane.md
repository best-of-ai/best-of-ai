Hemlane mixes software with people. Owners and property managers use the platform to list rentals, screen tenants, collect rent, and track maintenance, and they can get help from the Hemlane team when they want it. It covers single-family homes, multi-family buildings, and student housing.

The AI features are limited. The main one is AI-generated listing descriptions. The bigger draw is the setup: rent is paid directly to the owner, and the company highlights that it charges no fees for ACH payments. Real estate agents and tenants have their own sections of the platform.

Pricing is not detailed on the main page, so check Hemlane's site for plans. It makes sense for owners who want some help without handing over their properties to a traditional manager. TurboTenant is the closer pure-software option, and DoorLoop and Buildium offer deeper accounting.
