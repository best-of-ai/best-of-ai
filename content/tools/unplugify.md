Reclaim your time and attention with Unplugify — an AI-powered app that helps you build better screen habits, stay focused, and live intentionally.

On average, we check our phones 46 times a day — not because we need to, but because we’re conditioned to. Every scroll, every notification steals a bit of our attention, our energy, our future. Social media may be free, but your time isn’t.

Unplugify is your personal guide to digital mindfulness. Using powerful AI, it helps you break free from distractions, develop healthier screen habits, and focus on what truly matters.

- Personalized Insights – Every session is tailored to your goals, helping you build momentum day by day.
- Custom Sessions & Limits – Set app limits and focus sessions to regain control over your screen time.
- Daily Rewards – Stay motivated and celebrate every win.
- Supportive Community – Connect with others on the same journey to reclaim their time.
Whether you want to be more present, creative, productive, or peaceful — Unplugify helps you become who you’re meant to be.

Available now for iOS.
