NotebookLM Plus gives heavier users more room in NotebookLM, Google's tool for working with your own documents. The free version lets you add sources, ask questions with citations, and generate study aids, but it limits the number of notebooks, sources, and daily queries. The Plus tier lifts those limits, so people with large projects, such as researchers, students writing theses, or analysts working across many documents, can keep more material in one place.

Additional features include more control over generated outputs, such as customizing the style and length of Audio Overview podcasts and responses, and sharing notebooks with analytics on how others use them. Higher tiers include more advanced capabilities, and the Plus tier is often bundled into Google's AI subscription plans, which also include other Gemini features and storage. The same grounded behavior applies: answers draw on your sources and cite them.

Plus is paid through a Google subscription, and details of the plans change, so check Google's current offerings. For occasional use, the free tier may be enough. Because answers are only as reliable as the sources and the model, important claims should be verified in the original documents. Alternatives include ChatGPT and Claude with file uploads and Projects, Perplexity Spaces, and Microsoft Copilot Notebooks. See the NotebookLM entry for the core product.
