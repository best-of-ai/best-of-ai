Gorgias is a helpdesk made for online stores. Messages from email, live chat, social media, SMS, and phone arrive in one inbox, with the customer's order history, shipping status, and account details shown next to the conversation. Agents can refund an order, edit an address, or cancel a shipment without leaving the ticket, which speeds up the most common support tasks in retail.

Automation takes care of a lot of the repetitive work. Rules route and tag tickets, macros fill in standard replies with order data, and self-service flows let shoppers track orders or request returns. An AI agent answers questions about shipping, returns, and products using the store's policies and catalog, and can complete actions in the store before passing complex issues to people. Gorgias can also support sales conversations and product recommendations.

Pricing is based on the number of tickets per month, with separate charges for AI-resolved conversations, and a trial. This suits growing stores but can get costly during peak seasons. It is less flexible than Zendesk for non-retail use cases. Competitors include Zendesk, Re:amaze, Help Scout, and Intercom, and Shopify offers basic support tools in its own inbox. Gorgias is a common choice for Shopify merchants because of its deep integration.
