Libsyn, short for Liberated Syndication, has hosted podcasts since the early days of the medium. Creators upload audio, and Libsyn produces the RSS feed that podcast apps use, so a show appears across Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and many other directories. It also offers an embeddable player and a basic website.

Pricing has historically been based on the amount of storage you upload each month, which differs from plans tied to downloads. Statistics show downloads by episode and location, and the platform has monetization options, including dynamic ad insertion and sponsorship marketplaces, plus tools for video podcasts and premium subscriptions.

Libsyn's interface looks dated compared with newer hosts, though it is reliable and has a long track record. Newer services have simpler onboarding and more modern features. Alternatives include Buzzsprout, Transistor, Captivate, Podbean, Spotify for Creators, and Acast. Libsyn suits podcasters who value stability and an established host.
