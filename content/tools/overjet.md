Overjet is dental AI that reads radiographs. When a dentist takes X-rays, the software outlines suspected cavities, measures bone levels, and identifies calculus and other issues, giving a second look that supports diagnosis and helps explain problems to patients by showing them the marked images.

The technology also serves payers. Insurers use it to review claims and check whether treatment matches findings, and large dental groups use it to standardize care across offices. It has regulatory clearance for several functions in the US.

Overjet is sold to dental practices, group practices, and insurers on subscription. AI findings need dentist confirmation, and patients may worry when insurers use AI to evaluate claims. Alternatives include Pearl, Denti.AI, Dentrix Detect, VideaHealth, and Align's tools. Overjet suits dental practices and organizations.
