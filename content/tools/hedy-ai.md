Hedy AI takes a different role from notetakers that summarize afterward. It listens during a call or conversation and shows prompts in the moment: a suggested answer, a question to ask, or a reminder of context. The goal is to help the user perform better while the conversation is happening.

After the meeting, it produces transcripts and summaries and can store notes for future sessions. People use it for sales calls, interviews, negotiations, and learning situations. It can run alongside video calls or in person on a phone or computer.

Hedy has a free tier and paid plans. Recording and live AI assistance raise consent questions, so check the rules where you work and tell participants when required. Real-time prompts can distract, and some employers prohibit tools like this in interviews. Alternatives include Otter, Fireflies, Granola, Cluely, and Gong. Hedy AI suits people who want live support in conversations.
