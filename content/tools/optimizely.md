Optimizely began as an A/B testing tool and grew into a suite for digital experiences. Teams use web experimentation to compare versions of pages, feature experimentation to test changes in apps behind feature flags, and personalization to show different content to different audiences. Statistical engines determine which variations win, and rollouts can be controlled to reduce risk.

The platform also includes content management and commerce. A CMS lets marketers create and manage web content, and commerce products support B2B and B2C stores, so a company can build, publish, test, and optimize in a single environment. AI features, under the Opal brand, help generate content, suggest experiment ideas, and run agents that automate steps in marketing workflows.

Optimizely is sold to mid-sized and large organizations on custom contracts, which can be expensive. Success requires enough traffic to test meaningfully and processes for acting on results. Competitors include Adobe Experience Manager and Target, VWO, AB Tasty, LaunchDarkly for feature flags, Statsig, and Contentful or Sitecore for content. Optimizely suits teams that want to experiment systematically.
