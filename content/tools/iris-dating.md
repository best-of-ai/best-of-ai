Iris Dating tries to reduce the mismatch of attraction. Users swipe on photos, and the system learns their visual preferences, then predicts which other users they would find attractive and shows only those who are likely to find them attractive as well. The aim is fewer, better matches.

The approach uses facial recognition and machine learning on profile photos, which raises privacy questions about how biometric data is processed and stored. Users should read the policy before uploading photos. Dating apps also carry safety concerns, so meet in public places and verify identities.

Iris has a free tier with paid options. Independent information is limited. Alternatives include Hinge, Bumble, Tinder, Coffee Meets Bagel, and Feeld. Iris Dating suits people curious about attraction-based matching, with attention to privacy.
