Canva's value proposition is that professional-looking design shouldn't require professional software training. The drag-and-drop editor, combined with tens of thousands of templates for every format imaginable — Instagram posts, pitch decks, resumes, email headers, YouTube thumbnails — means most people can produce something polished in 20 minutes without ever touching Photoshop or Figma. The template quality is high enough that many small businesses run their entire visual identity through Canva.

The AI features added to Canva Pro extend this considerably. Magic Design generates a complete multi-slide presentation or social kit from a topic or uploaded image. Magic Edit lets you select a region of an image and describe what you want there instead — replace a background, add an object, change a product colour. Magic Write handles copywriting inside your design: captions, headline variations, or a full email body. These tools sit inside the existing editor, so there's no context switch between designing and using AI.

Canva Free covers the core editor and most templates with limitations on premium assets. Canva Pro at $15/month per person unlocks the full AI suite, brand kit (custom fonts, colours, logos), background remover, and 1TB of cloud storage. Canva for Teams adds collaboration features and shared brand controls. Canva is used heavily by social media managers, small business owners, teachers, non-profits, and anyone who needs visual output at speed without a dedicated designer on staff.
