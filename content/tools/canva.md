Canva is a design tool for people who are not designers. You start from a template, swap in your own text and photos, and export the result, which cuts out most of the layout decisions that make design software hard to learn. It covers social media posts, presentations, flyers, resumes, posters, short videos, whiteboards, and simple websites in one editor. A built-in library of photos, graphics, fonts, and music means most projects can be finished without leaving the app.

Magic Studio is the name for its AI tools. Magic Write drafts copy, Magic Design builds layouts from a prompt or an uploaded image, and Magic Edit and Magic Eraser change or remove parts of a photo. Background Remover, Magic Expand, and an AI image generator round out the set, and Magic Switch converts a design into another format or language. These tools work inside the same editor as everything else, so the output is an ordinary editable design rather than a flat picture.

Canva has a free plan with a large set of templates and a paid plan that adds the Brand Kit, a bigger content library, and more AI usage. Team plans add shared brand assets, approval flows, and admin controls, which is where most companies use it. It runs in a browser and as mobile and desktop apps, and Canva also owns the Affinity design software, which targets professionals. Canva is not a replacement for Photoshop or Illustrator on complex work. Its strength is making good-enough branded material quickly and letting a whole team do it.
