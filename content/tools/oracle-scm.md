Oracle SCM Cloud brings the functions of a supply chain together in one cloud application suite. It covers planning demand and supply, buying from suppliers, managing inventory and warehouses, running manufacturing, handling orders and fulfillment, and moving goods through transportation and global trade, with product lifecycle management for design data. Because it shares data with Oracle's finance and HR applications, companies can connect supply chain decisions to financial outcomes.

AI and analytics support the suite. Machine learning improves demand forecasts and supply plans, predictive models flag delays and quality risks, and generative AI assistants help users find information and complete tasks. Oracle has been adding AI agents that can handle routine actions across processes. The suite is delivered in the cloud and updated regularly.

Oracle SCM is sold to large enterprises on subscription with implementation by partners, which makes it a major investment. It's more comprehensive than specialized tools but may be more than smaller companies need. Competitors include SAP, Blue Yonder, Kinaxis, o9 Solutions, Manhattan Associates, and Microsoft Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management. Oracle SCM suits organizations that want an integrated enterprise suite across finance and operations.
