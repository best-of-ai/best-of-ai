JobGPT covers much of the job application workflow. It searches for postings that match a profile, adjusts the resume's wording to fit each description, drafts a cover letter, and fills in application forms. Users review results and track their applications.

The appeal is time saved on repetitive forms. The risk is quality: automatically generated materials can be generic or inaccurate, and recruiters can spot copy-paste applications. Tailoring should stay truthful, and every application deserves a quick review before it goes out.

JobGPT has a free tier and paid plans, and independent information is limited. Alternatives include Jobscan, Simplify, Teal, Huntr, and LazyApply. JobGPT suits job seekers who want to automate routine applications while staying involved.
