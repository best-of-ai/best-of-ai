Wisecut speeds up editing of simple videos. A creator uploads a recording, and the tool detects pauses and filler, cuts them, adds smooth transitions, and generates subtitles from the transcript. Background music automatically ducks when someone speaks.

It helps podcasters, educators, and social creators who want clean videos without learning a full editor. Autoreframe adapts videos to different aspect ratios, and the transcript lets users edit by text.

Wisecut has a free tier and paid plans. It handles basic edits, so complex projects need a fuller editor. Alternatives include Descript, CapCut, Submagic, Kapwing, and VEED. Wisecut suits creators editing talking-head content.
