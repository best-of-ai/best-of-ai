Paragraph AI brings AI writing to the smartphone. A user types a few words or dictates an idea, and the app produces a full email, message, or post, which can be adjusted for tone, length, or formality. Rewrite and proofreading tools tidy existing text.

The appeal is convenience for writing on the go, when opening a full desktop assistant is awkward. Typical uses include replying to emails, drafting social captions, and polishing short messages.

Independent information is limited. It has a free tier and paid plans, and AI-written text should be reviewed for accuracy and personal voice. Alternatives include Grammarly, Wordtune, ChatGPT and Claude mobile apps, Apple Intelligence writing tools, and Gboard assistants. Paragraph AI suits people who write mostly on their phone.
