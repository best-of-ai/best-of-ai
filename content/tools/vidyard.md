Vidyard is a video platform for business teams. Salespeople record quick webcam or screen videos for prospects, send them by email or link, and see who watched and for how long. Marketers host videos on their websites with branded players and gated forms.

AI features speed up production. Script suggestions help with what to say, avatars let a rep send a personalized video without recording each one, and transcripts, summaries, and chapters make longer videos easier to use. Integrations with Salesforce and HubSpot put engagement data in the CRM.

Vidyard has a free plan for basic recording and paid tiers for teams. Alternatives include Loom, Wistia, Bonjoro, Sendspark, and Tavus. Vidyard suits sales and marketing teams that use video.
