Leetify is an analytics and coaching tool for Counter-Strike players. After a match, it reads the demo data and produces a report with ratings across aim, positioning, and use of grenades, plus round-by-round breakdowns that show why a round was won or lost. Players compare themselves with others at their level.

Team features support scrims and coaching. Coaches review stats across players, see where the team struggles, and track improvement over time. A public profile shows a player's progress, and a leaderboard ranks users, with an optional paid tier for deeper analysis.

Leetify is free with a premium subscription. It focuses on one game family, and numbers tell only part of the story, so reviewing demos still matters. Alternatives include Scope.gg, CS Demo Manager, FACEIT stats, and Skybox for training. Leetify suits competitive CS players who want to improve.
