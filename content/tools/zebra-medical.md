Zebra Medical Vision developed AI that reads medical scans and flags findings. Its algorithms examined chest X-rays, CT scans, and mammograms for conditions such as lung nodules, bone density loss, fatty liver, coronary calcium, and signs of fractures, often picking up incidental findings that a radiologist focused on another question might not report. The company had several algorithms cleared by regulators in the United States and Europe.

A distinctive aspect was population health analysis: by screening scans that were already taken for other purposes, hospitals could identify patients at risk of conditions such as osteoporosis or cardiovascular disease and follow up. Zebra Medical Vision offered its tools through a cloud platform that integrated with radiology workflows.

Nanox acquired Zebra Medical Vision, and the company now operates as Nanox AI, so the original brand is not the one customers see today. Check Nanox's materials for the current products. Competitors and peers include Aidoc, Viz.ai, Annalise.ai, Rad AI, Lunit, and Qure.ai. As with all clinical AI, the tools assist radiologists and are not replacements, and performance depends on the population and scanners used.
