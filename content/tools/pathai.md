PathAI works on digital pathology. Pathologists traditionally examine tissue slides under a microscope. With scanned slides, algorithms can highlight regions of interest, count cells, and measure biomarkers, which helps make reads more consistent. PathAI builds these tools and a platform for viewing and managing slides.

The company also supports pharmaceutical research. Its models analyze tissue from clinical trials to measure disease features and treatment responses, and it operates a laboratory service. The goal is to reduce variability and speed drug development.

PathAI is sold to labs, health systems, and drug developers. AI outputs support pathologists, who make the diagnosis, and tools require regulatory clearance for clinical use. Alternatives include Paige (Tempus), Proscia, Ibex, Aiforia, and Leica's digital pathology. PathAI suits labs and life sciences companies.
