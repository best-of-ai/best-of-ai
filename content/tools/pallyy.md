Pallyy keeps social management simple and affordable. Users connect accounts, plan posts on a visual calendar, and publish to several networks. A first-comment feature, link-in-bio pages, and a media library support common creator workflows, and a unified inbox collects comments and messages.

Analytics show growth and engagement, and client-friendly reports help agencies prove results. Collaboration tools let clients approve content before it goes live. AI features suggest captions, hashtags, and ideas to speed up planning.

Pallyy has a free plan for a small number of posts and paid tiers by social sets. It is lighter than enterprise tools like Sprout Social. Alternatives include Buffer, Later, Metricool, Planable, and Hootsuite. Pallyy suits small agencies and solo creators.
