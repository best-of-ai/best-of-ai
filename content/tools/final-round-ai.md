Final Round AI covers interview preparation from practice to the real thing. You can run AI practice interviews, set goals, and get automatic debriefs that point out where your answers were weak. The prep side is useful on its own, even if you never use the live features.

The headline product is Interview Copilot, a desktop app for macOS and Windows. During a live interview it listens and suggests answers privately, and Screen Help reads questions from your screen. A stealth mode hides its windows from screen sharing. Some employers treat live AI help as cheating, so read the rules of the interview before using it.

A free plan covers goals and prep materials, and the live Copilot needs a paid plan. Google's Interview Warmup is a free option for basic practice. Final Round AI suits people who want deeper mock interviews, and the live features are a personal decision about what is acceptable.
