Workable is an applicant tracking system aimed at small and mid-sized companies. Recruiters create a job, post it to many job boards with one click, and manage candidates through a pipeline with scorecards, notes, and team collaboration. A careers page and templates for emails, offers, and interview kits cover the basics of hiring.

AI features speed up repetitive work. The platform can write job descriptions, suggest candidates from a large database and from your own applicants, screen resumes against criteria, and summarize interview recordings and notes. An AI assistant helps recruiters draft messages and prepare interview questions. Integrations connect to HR systems, calendars, background check providers, and assessment tools.

Workable is paid, with pricing by plan and the number of open jobs, and a free trial. Companies using AI to screen candidates should be careful about bias and local laws, which increasingly regulate automated hiring decisions. Competitors include Greenhouse and Lever for structured hiring, Ashby for analytics, BambooHR and Breezy for smaller teams, and large systems like Workday for enterprises. Workable suits growing companies that want an easy, capable hiring tool.
