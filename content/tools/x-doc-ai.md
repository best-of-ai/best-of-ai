X-doc.ai is an advanced online AI translation platform specializing in technical translation, medical translation, academic translation, and regulatory translation for over 100 languages. Trusted by 1,000+ global companies, including leaders in life sciences and academia, it offers unparalleled precision (99% accuracy) for high-stakes document types such as clinical trial protocols, FDA submissions, regulatory dossiers, scientific publications, patent filings, and product manuals. Designed for industries that demand accuracy and compliance, X-doc.ai combines batch document processing, OCR translation, context memory, and terminology management to ensure consistency and efficiency across ultra-long, complex files.

Built for enterprises navigating global markets, X-doc.ai dramatically improves turnaround times and reduces translation costs—ideal for organizations handling SOPs, IRB submissions, CTDs, academic theses, multilingual technical manuals, and more. The platform supports various file formats (.docx, .xlsx, .pdf, .pptx) and enables seamless workflows through AI automation and optional manual typesetting. With robust data security (SOC2, ISO27001) and proven performance in life sciences, legal, and academic sectors, X-doc.ai stands out as the go-to solution for high-accuracy, large-scale document translation.
