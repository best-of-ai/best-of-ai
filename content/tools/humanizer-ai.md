Humanizer AI is a widely used platform for identifying AI-generated content and transforming it into natural, human-like writing. It detects whether a text was produced by large language models like ChatGPT by analyzing it at the sentence, paragraph, and full-document levels. Once detected, users can instantly improve the text’s authenticity using the Humanize AI mode. Trained on extensive datasets of both human and AI-written material, Humanizer AI has helped over 110,000 users worldwide and partners with more than 100 organizations in education, publishing, blogging, and other sectors.
