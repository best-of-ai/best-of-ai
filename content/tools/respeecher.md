Respeecher works through speech-to-speech conversion. A performer records a line, and the system transforms it into the voice of a target speaker, keeping the performer's emotion and timing. This differs from text-to-speech, which generates speech from scratch and can lack nuance.

Productions have used it to recreate the voices of actors who are unavailable, age or de-age a voice, and dub into other languages while preserving a character's sound. The company emphasizes licensing and consent from voice owners.

Respeecher is sold to studios and creators with custom projects. Voice cloning raises concerns about consent and misuse, and labor groups track its use. Alternatives include ElevenLabs, Replica Studios (now closed), Resemble AI, WellSaid Labs, and Voice-Swap. Respeecher suits professional productions needing high-quality voice conversion.
