Producers build a schedule from a script breakdown and send call sheets to the crew. Shot lists and storyboards help directors plan coverage. A free plan covers basics, and paid plans add team features.

It suits small and mid-size productions more than large studios.

Movie Magic and Celtx are alternatives. StudioBinder suits filmmakers who run their own productions.
