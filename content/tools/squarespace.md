Squarespace appeals to people who want an attractive site without a developer. You choose a template, change the content with a visual editor, and publish with hosting, security, and a domain included. Its designs are known for clean layouts and good typography, which makes it popular with creatives, small businesses, restaurants, and portfolio sites.

The platform goes beyond static pages. Built-in commerce handles products, subscriptions, and checkout, email campaigns support marketing, and Acuity Scheduling covers appointments. AI features include a site builder that generates a starting design and content from answers to a few questions, text-writing assistance, and tools for improving search visibility. Analytics show traffic and sales.

Squarespace is paid, with plans by feature level, and no permanent free tier beyond a trial. It offers less flexibility than open platforms like WordPress for custom functionality, and moving a site elsewhere can be difficult. Wix has more apps and AI site-building features, Webflow gives designers more control, Shopify is stronger for large stores, and WordPress offers the most flexibility. Squarespace suits people who value design and simplicity.
