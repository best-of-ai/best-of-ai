Murf is aimed at people who need a professional voiceover without hiring a voice actor or booking a studio. In its browser-based studio, you paste a script, choose a voice, and adjust pitch, speed, and emphasis. Voices come in many accents and languages and suit narration, e-learning, product videos, ads, and presentations. You can add background music, line the audio up with video or slides, and export the result.

The platform has expanded to related audio jobs. Dubbing translates a video or audio file into other languages and keeps it timed to the original. An API gives developers text-to-speech for their own apps, including fast, streaming voices for conversational use. Murf Agents power voice calls and chat for customer service and similar needs, and a voice changer converts one voice into another. Team workspaces help companies share projects and keep audio consistent.

A free plan allows limited voice generation but no commercial use, and paid plans add voices, downloads, commercial rights, and team features, with custom enterprise plans. Murf positions itself as a business-friendly alternative to ElevenLabs, with strong tools for presentations and training content. Voices sound natural but can still miss emotional nuance in long, dramatic reads. Murf also offered a transition path for customers of PlayHT after that service closed. WellSaid, Speechify, and ElevenLabs are the other main options.
