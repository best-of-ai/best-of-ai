Murf is a voiceover production studio in a browser — no recording equipment, no voice talent booking, no studio time. You type or paste your script, pick a voice from a library of 120+ across 20 languages, adjust the pace and pitch, add emphasis on specific words, and sync the audio to a video timeline if needed. The voices include different ages, accents, and styles — casual presenters, authoritative narrators, warm instructors — that cover most professional voiceover requirements.

The studio interface is built for production at scale. Multiple voice characters can speak in the same project for interview-style content, each assigned to different script sections. Background music can be added from Murf's library and automatically ducked under the voiceover. The audio export includes WAV, MP3, and direct sync to a video file. For eLearning content that requires updating a few lines of narration, you edit the text and regenerate just those clips rather than re-recording the entire module.

Murf's free tier provides 10 minutes of voice generation per month with Murf watermark and access to 120+ voices for previewing. Creator at $19/month provides 24 hours of voice generation annually and commercial usage rights. Business plans at $26/month include 96 hours and collaboration features. Enterprise adds API access, custom pronunciation libraries, and higher limits. eLearning developers are the largest user segment by volume — producing Articulate or Lectora modules with professional narration without hiring voice actors for every language version is the core value. Marketing, product, and HR teams use it for explainer videos and internal training content.
