The SEO Agent automates the content workflow. A user enters a site or topic, and the tool finds keywords, plans articles, and writes drafts optimized for search, with options to publish to a content management system.

It targets small sites and marketers who need regular content. The risk with automated SEO articles is quality: generic or inaccurate content rarely ranks well and can hurt a site's credibility, so editing and original insight matter.

Independent information is limited. It has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Surfer, Frase, Writesonic, BlogSEO, and Averi. The SEO Agent suits site owners wanting automated drafts, with human review.
