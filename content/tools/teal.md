Teal helps people manage a job hunt. A Chrome extension saves postings from job boards to a tracker with stages and notes, and tools remind users to follow up. A resume builder creates versions tailored to each posting, with suggestions for keywords and phrasing.

AI features help write bullet points, summaries, and cover letters, and a match score shows how well a resume fits a job. Teal also offers career resources and tools for networking and LinkedIn.

Teal has a free plan and a paid tier with more AI. Generated text should be edited to stay accurate and personal. Alternatives include Huntr, Jobscan, Simplify, Enhancv, and spreadsheets. Teal suits job seekers who want structure.
