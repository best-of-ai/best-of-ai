TheLibrarian.io puts an assistant in WhatsApp. Users message it to check their email, schedule events, create tasks, and get reminders, in natural language or voice notes. It connects to services like Gmail and Google Calendar.

The appeal is simplicity: no new interface, and the chat is available anywhere. Connecting an assistant to email and calendars requires trust, so review permissions and privacy terms.

Independent information is limited. It has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Notis, Poke, Lindy, Motion, and ChatGPT with connectors. TheLibrarian.io suits people who want to manage tasks through WhatsApp.
