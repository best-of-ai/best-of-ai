Semrush bundles many marketing tools in one subscription. Users research keywords, audit websites, track rankings, analyze backlinks, spy on competitors' traffic and ads, and plan content, all from a shared dashboard. It is widely used by agencies and in-house teams.

AI features help with writing and analysis. A writing assistant drafts and optimizes content, AI tools summarize data and suggest actions, and newer products track how brands appear in AI-generated answers from chat assistants, a growing concern for search marketers. Reports and integrations suit client work.

Semrush is paid, with plans by features and limits, and Adobe announced plans to acquire the company, so packaging may change. It can overwhelm beginners with options. Alternatives include Ahrefs, Moz Pro, SE Ranking, Mangools, and SpyFu. Semrush suits marketers who want one broad toolkit.
