Deel is a global hiring and payments platform. A company that wants to employ someone in another country can use Deel as the employer of record, which means Deel holds the legal employment relationship locally and handles contracts, payroll, benefits, and taxes. It also manages contractors, with agreements and payments in many currencies.

Beyond employment, Deel has built an HR suite covering global payroll for companies with their own entities, benefits, equipment, time tracking, and immigration support. AI features review and generate contracts against local rules, answer compliance questions, and automate workflows, which reduces the research that cross-border hiring requires.

Deel charges per employee or contractor, with plans by service. It has been involved in public legal disputes with a competitor, which readers evaluating vendors may want to read about. Alternatives include Remote, Oyster, Rippling, Papaya Global, and Velocity Global. Deel suits companies hiring across borders.
