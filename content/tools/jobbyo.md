Jobbyo automates the repetitive parts of looking for work. A user uploads a resume and states the roles they want, and the agent searches for matching openings, fills in applications, and tracks responses. The idea is to apply to many more jobs than a person could by hand.

Volume is not the same as success. Hiring teams complain about floods of automated applications, and many applicant tracking systems filter them, so tailored applications to well-chosen roles usually work better. Some employers also restrict automated submissions in their terms.

Independent information about Jobbyo is limited, so read what data it uses and what it submits in your name. Alternatives include Simplify, Teal, LazyApply, Huntr, and JobGPT. Jobbyo suits job seekers who want help managing a high-volume search, with a review of what goes out.
