Artbreeder began with an unusual idea: instead of typing a prompt, you start with existing images and breed them together, like mixing genes. Each image has sliders for traits, such as age, expression, or lighting for a portrait, so you can nudge it in a direction and save the results. Children of images can be crossed again, which lets a visual idea evolve step by step. The community shares images that others can remix.

The platform has grown to include modern generative tools. Prompt-based generation creates images from text, and composition tools let you sketch or arrange elements and have the AI fill in the details. Collage and character tools help creators produce consistent figures for stories, games, and tabletop role-playing. Writers and game designers use it to find faces and scenes for characters, and artists use it for concept exploration.

Artbreeder has a free tier with limited credits and paid plans for more generation, higher resolution, and commercial use. The slider-based approach offers more control over specific traits than most prompt-only tools, though newer models from Midjourney, FLUX, and OpenAI produce stronger raw quality. It is also worth reading the licensing terms for images you plan to sell. Leonardo.ai and Midjourney are the common alternatives for concept art.
