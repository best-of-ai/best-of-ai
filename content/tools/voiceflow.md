Voiceflow lets teams design conversational agents together. On a visual canvas, designers and developers map out how an agent should respond, add steps that call language models, search knowledge bases, run logic, and connect to APIs and tools. Non-engineers can shape conversations while developers handle integrations, which makes it practical for cross-functional teams in customer support, sales, and operations.

The platform handles the whole lifecycle. You can add a knowledge base of company content so the agent answers from it, test the agent in a built-in environment, and deploy it to a website, messaging channels, phone lines, or via API. Analytics and transcripts show how conversations go, and versioning and collaboration features support teamwork. Voiceflow works with a choice of language models.

Voiceflow offers a free plan and paid plans that scale with agents, usage, and team features, and enterprise options. A visual builder is easier for prototyping than writing everything in code, but complex logic can still get complicated, and reliability depends on testing and guardrails. Competitors include Botpress, Chatbase, Dify, Intercom's Fin, and Microsoft Copilot Studio, and frameworks like LangGraph for developers. Voiceflow suits teams that want to design agents collaboratively.
