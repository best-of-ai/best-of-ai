SeekOut builds a searchable database of professionals from public profiles and sources. Recruiters filter by skills, experience, and background, find candidates for roles like engineers and scientists, and reach out with outreach sequences.

AI helps match candidates to jobs and understand skill sets, and analytics show talent pools in a region or company. Diversity filters support recruiting programs, subject to legal rules on how they are used. Products extend to internal talent management for employers.

SeekOut is sold on subscription to recruiting teams and agencies. Candidate data must be handled under privacy rules, and AI matching should be audited for bias. Alternatives include Gem, LinkedIn Recruiter, hireEZ, Findem, and Eightfold. SeekOut suits recruiters sourcing specialized talent.
