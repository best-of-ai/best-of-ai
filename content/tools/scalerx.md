Use cases include customer support, community moderation and FAQs, and onboarding for projects, especially in crypto and creator groups where Telegram is central. No-code setup lowers the barrier.

Independent information is limited, and bots must be tested to avoid wrong answers in public groups. ScalerX has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Botpress, Chatbase, ManyChat, Voiceflow, and Telegram bot frameworks. ScalerX suits community managers deploying Telegram bots.
