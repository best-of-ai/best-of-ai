Claude Slides makes decks from a conversation. You describe the audience and the point of the presentation, share notes or source files, and Claude produces a set of slides with an outline, text, and layout. You can then ask for changes, such as a different order, a tighter message, or new visuals, and edit individual slides directly.

The finished deck can be presented from within Claude or exported to PowerPoint or PDF for use elsewhere. Because it lives in the same place as the conversation, you can pull information from uploaded documents and revise based on feedback without switching tools. It sits alongside Claude's other creation features, including Docs.

Claude Slides is a paid feature that has been rolling out in beta, so availability and controls vary by plan and may change. A separate Claude add-in for PowerPoint works inside Microsoft's app. AI-built decks still need a review for accuracy and tone. Alternatives include Gamma, Beautiful.ai, Canva, Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint, and Google Slides with Gemini. Claude Slides suits people who want to go from notes to a deck in one chat.
