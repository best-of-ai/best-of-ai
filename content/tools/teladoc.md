Teladoc Health delivers healthcare through phones and video. Members can see a clinician for common conditions, get prescriptions where appropriate, and access specialists, therapists, and dermatologists without traveling. Employers and health plans buy the service for their members, which makes access fast and convenient for non-emergency issues, and it includes expert medical opinion services and virtual nursing.

The company also runs programs for chronic conditions, such as diabetes and hypertension, combining connected devices, coaching, and digital tools. Mental health care is a major area, including its consumer therapy brand BetterHelp. AI is used for matching patients with providers, supporting clinicians with documentation, and personalizing care programs.

Teladoc is a public company that earns money through subscription fees from clients and per-visit fees. Access depends on your employer or health plan, though some services are available to individuals. Telehealth is not suitable for emergencies or conditions that need physical examination. Competitors and alternatives include Amwell, MDLIVE from Evernorth, Doctor on Demand, which is now part of Included Health, and virtual services from health systems and Hims & Hers. Teladoc is one of the largest names in virtual care.
