HeadsUp is competitor monitoring that tells you exactly what competitors are up to and what to do next. Get actionable intelligence on price changes, product launches, and positioning shifts with specific recommendations for how to respond. Skip the anxiety and guesswork. Know precisely when competitors make moves and what actions to take.

Key Features
- Competitor monitoring on autopilot: Tracks competitor websites 24/7 and alerts you when they change pricing, features, or messaging
- Actionable intelligence: Tells you what changed, why it matters, and exactly what to do about it — not just that something changed
- 60-second setup: Add competitors and get your first actionable alert in under a minute
- 90-day competitive history: See what competitors changed over the past 3 months so you understand their strategy patterns

Benefits
- Stay ahead of moves: Know about competitor changes before they impact your business and respond faster
- Skip manual checking: Stop wasting time monitoring competitor sites — get only changes that matter delivered to you
- Get specific recommendations: Receive concrete advice on how to respond to competitor moves instead of guessing
- Protect market position: Quickly counter competitor price drops or new features before losing customers
