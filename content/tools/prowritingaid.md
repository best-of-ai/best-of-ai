ProWritingAid is built for writers who want to improve their craft. As well as catching spelling and grammar errors, it generates reports on overused words, sentence length variation, clichés, passive voice, pacing, dialogue, and readability, which editors and novelists use to revise drafts. The detailed feedback is aimed at long-form writing more than quick messages.

It offers integrations with Microsoft Word, Google Docs, Scrivener, and browsers, and includes a plagiarism checker in higher plans. Newer features use AI to rephrase sentences, suggest rewrites, and offer feedback on structure, and writers can set a house style or custom rules. A lifetime purchase option has been a distinguishing choice compared with subscription-only competitors.

ProWritingAid has a free plan with limits on word count and a paid subscription or lifetime license. Its volume of suggestions can feel overwhelming, and style advice is not right for all writing, so authors must use judgment. Alternatives include Grammarly, which is more polished for everyday writing, LanguageTool, Hemingway Editor, Wordtune, and AutoCrit for fiction. ProWritingAid suits novelists, bloggers, and editors who want in-depth analysis.
