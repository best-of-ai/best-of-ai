Topaz Photo AI aims to fix the technical flaws in photos with little effort. When you open an image, it analyzes the content and suggests corrections, using models for noise reduction, sharpening, and increasing resolution. It handles problems like grainy high-ISO shots, soft focus, and motion blur, and can enlarge photos while adding believable detail. Many wildlife, sports, and event photographers use it to rescue difficult images.

The tool also offers face recovery, light adjustment, color balance, and a way to apply different adjustments to selected subjects. It can be run as a standalone application, as an external editor from Lightroom, or as a plugin for Photoshop, and processes batches of images. Models run locally on your computer and benefit from a good graphics card.

Topaz sells the software as a license that includes a period of updates, and offers trial versions. Heavy AI processing can create over-smoothed or invented details, so results should be inspected at full size. Alternatives include DxO PureRAW, ON1 Photo AI, Adobe's Denoise in Lightroom, and Luminar Neo, and free options such as Upscayl and Real-ESRGAN for upscaling. Topaz Photo AI suits photographers who want strong enhancement with minimal tweaking.
