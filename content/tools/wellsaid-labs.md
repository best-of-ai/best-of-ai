WellSaid focuses on voiceovers for businesses. In its web studio, you type or paste a script, pick a voice, and generate narration with control over pacing, pronunciation, and emphasis. The voices are built from recordings by professional voice actors who agreed to the use, which gives companies a clear license position. Typical projects include training courses, explainer videos, product tours, corporate announcements, and audio for apps.

For larger teams, collaboration and control matter. Projects can be shared, commented on, and reviewed, and custom pronunciation settings keep brand names and technical terms consistent across a library of content. An API lets developers add speech to their own products, and integrations connect WellSaid to e-learning and creative tools. Enterprise plans emphasize security, privacy, and compliance, and custom voices can be created for a brand.

WellSaid has a free trial, a Creator plan for individuals, and an Enterprise plan for companies. It costs more than consumer options like ElevenLabs or Murf, and it offers fewer voices and less emphasis on cloning. Its argument is dependable quality, licensed voices, and corporate governance. That makes it a fit for training and communications teams more than for hobby creators or game developers. Murf, Speechify, and Amazon Polly cover similar voiceover needs at different price points.
