HeyGen removes the production requirements from video content entirely. You type or paste a script, choose an avatar from a library of over 100 pre-built options or upload a two-minute video of yourself to create a personal avatar, and HeyGen produces a presenter video with natural-looking lip sync in minutes. The output is a polished MP4 with the avatar speaking your script, complete with background options and branding elements.

The multilingual capability is where HeyGen becomes especially useful at scale. The same avatar can speak the same script in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and dozens of other languages — with the voice and lip movement adapting to each language automatically. Companies producing training materials, product explainers, or onboarding videos for international markets use HeyGen to localise content without hiring voice actors or re-shooting.

HeyGen's video translation feature can also take existing footage — a recorded presentation, a product demo, a CEO address — and re-dub it in another language, matching the speaker's lip movements to the translated audio. Plans start at $24/month for five minutes of video per month, with higher tiers for heavier production schedules. Enterprise plans support custom avatar training, team workspaces, and API access for integrating avatar video generation into broader content pipelines.
