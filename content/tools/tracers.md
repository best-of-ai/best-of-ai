Tracers is an investigative data service. A user enters a name, address, or other identifier, and the platform returns linked records about people and businesses, such as addresses, relatives, liens, and court filings. Matching algorithms connect pieces of information.

Customers include private investigators, attorneys, collections agencies, and law enforcement. Access is restricted by law in many cases and requires a permissible purpose, and results have limits in accuracy.

Tracers is sold on subscription and per search. Using such data raises privacy and legal issues, and background checks used for employment or housing are subject to consumer reporting laws. Alternatives include LexisNexis Accurint, TLOxp, Thomson Reuters CLEAR, and IRB Search. Tracers suits licensed investigative professionals.
