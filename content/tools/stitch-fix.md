Stitch Fix popularized personal styling by mail. A new client fills out a style profile, and a stylist assisted by algorithms selects pieces from many brands, ships them in a box, and the client keeps what they like and returns the rest. Feedback on each item improves later selections, and clients can also shop items directly through a browsing experience.

Data science has been central to the company's identity. Algorithms analyze client preferences, item attributes, and inventory to predict what each customer will like, and stylists apply judgment and personal touches. Generative AI tools help create outfit suggestions and visual try-on features, and the company has also experimented with ways to let customers shop without a stylist.

Stitch Fix charges a styling fee that is credited toward purchases, and prices vary by item. The business has had to adapt as growth slowed and customers moved to other ways of shopping. Alternatives include Trunk Club, which has closed, Amazon Prime Wardrobe, Nordstrom's styling services, Rent the Runway, and AI shopping assistants from retailers. Stitch Fix suits people who want clothing selected for them.
