Collate focuses on private document work. A user opens a PDF, such as a paper, contract, or textbook, and asks questions about it or requests a summary. Because the processing runs locally on the device, the document does not need to leave the computer, which matters for sensitive files.

Typical uses include studying, reviewing research papers, and pulling key terms from long reports. Local models are smaller than cloud models, so results are good for summaries and questions about the text but may be less strong on complex reasoning, and speed depends on the hardware.

Collate is free. Independent information is limited, so check what platforms it supports and how it handles models and updates. Alternatives include AnythingLLM, GPT4All, NotebookLM, ChatPDF, and Claude or ChatGPT for cloud-based analysis. Collate suits people who want PDF assistance with strong privacy.
