Users upload artwork and choose components such as cards, boards, tokens, and boxes. Orders ship as single copies, so there is no minimum run. It also lets designers sell games through its shop.

Unit costs are higher than offset printing at volume, so it suits prototypes and small runs.

Print-on-demand services like Printerstudio are alternatives. The Game Crafter suits independent board game designers.
