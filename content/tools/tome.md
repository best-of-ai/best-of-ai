Tome generates complete presentations with a different philosophy than PowerPoint or Google Slides. Instead of discrete slides you fill and move around, Tome creates a flowing narrative — pages that scroll like a document but can be presented like slides. This makes it particularly suited for content that tells a story rather than presenting a list: investor memos, product proposals, research reports, case studies. The structure follows the argument rather than forcing the argument to fit a slide grid.

The AI generation is its most used feature. Describe what you're building — "a pitch deck for an AI-powered logistics startup targeting mid-market e-commerce" — and Tome produces a complete, designed narrative with relevant sections, plausible content, and generated imagery. The result isn't ready to present without editing, but it's a substantial first draft that takes the structural thinking work off you. Tiles (Tome's page units) support embedded video, live data from Figma or Airtable, and interactive elements alongside standard text and images.

Tome's free plan covers unlimited pages with a watermark. Pro at $8/month removes the watermark and adds more AI generation credits, custom branding, and analytics showing who viewed your presentation and for how long. Team plans add shared workspaces and role management. Tome has found a strong user base among startup founders, product managers, and consultants — people who regularly need to build persuasive documents that communicate a point of view rather than just present information, and who want the AI to do more than auto-format what they've already written.
