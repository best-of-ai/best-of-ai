vidIQ helps YouTubers grow. A browser extension adds data to YouTube pages and Studio, showing keyword scores, competitor stats, and tags used by successful videos. Research tools find topics with demand and low competition.

AI features suggest video ideas, titles, and thumbnails, write descriptions, and give feedback on videos. Analytics track subscriber growth and performance, and a coach feature gives guidance.

vidIQ has a free plan and paid tiers. Optimization scores are guides, and content quality drives results. Alternatives include TubeBuddy, Morningfame, Social Blade, and YouTube's own research tools. vidIQ suits creators optimizing a channel.
