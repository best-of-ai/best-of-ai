It has hundreds of brushes, a custom brush engine, blend modes, and fast canvas handling. Procreate Dreams adds frame-based animation. Files export to PSD and common image formats.

It is a one-time purchase with no subscription. Procreate has said it does not use generative AI, which many artists value.

Adobe Fresco and Clip Studio Paint are alternatives. Procreate suits illustrators and art therapists who want a simple, tactile tool.
