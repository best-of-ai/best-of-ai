Smartly, formerly Smartly.io, serves brands and agencies that run large ad programs. Its platform connects to major social networks and lets teams build and launch many campaigns from one interface, apply rules, and update thousands of ads at once. For retailers with big catalogs, dynamic ads are generated automatically from product feeds, adjusting to inventory and price.

The product emphasizes creative and optimization. Creative automation produces many ad variations in different sizes and languages from templates, which helps with testing and localization. Predictive budget allocation shifts spend among campaigns based on performance, and reporting brings results across platforms together. Newer generative features help create and adapt creative assets, with brand controls.

Smartly is sold to enterprises on custom contracts, typically with a fee linked to ad spend. It is built for large advertisers and agencies, so small businesses will find it too costly and complex. Competitors include Skai, Adobe Advertising, Madgicx, and in-platform tools from Meta and Google. Value depends on the volume of campaigns and the skills of the team using it. It suits brands that manage substantial social ad budgets.
