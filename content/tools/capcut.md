CapCut became popular for short-form video because it is easy to use and well connected to TikTok. You can assemble clips on a timeline, trim and cut, add music and transitions, and apply effects, filters, and animated text. Templates let you drop your footage into a ready-made edit, which is a quick route to a polished social video. It runs on iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, and in the browser.

The AI features are a major draw. Auto captions transcribe speech and style subtitles, background removal works without a green screen, and smart tools stabilize footage, retouch faces, and clean up audio. Newer capabilities include AI avatars, voice changing and text-to-speech, translation, image and video generation, and tools that cut long videos into short clips. Together they speed up work for creators who post often.

CapCut is free with a watermark-free export on many features, and a Pro subscription unlocks premium effects, higher-end AI features, and cloud storage. Some capabilities have moved behind the paywall over time. The app is owned by ByteDance, which has raised data and regulatory questions in some countries, so check the terms for your region. Adobe Premiere Rush and Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, and Descript are alternatives, and InShot and VN also serve mobile editors.
