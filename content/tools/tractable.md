Tractable applies computer vision to insurance claims. After an accident, a policyholder or repairer uploads photos, and the AI identifies damaged parts and estimates repair costs, speeding up decisions that used to wait for an inspection.

Insurers use it to settle simple claims fast, to triage complex ones, and to detect inconsistencies. The company has expanded to property damage after disasters and to the used-parts and repair industries.

Tractable is sold to insurers and repair networks on enterprise contracts. Automated estimates affect people's payments, so fairness, accuracy, and appeal routes matter. Alternatives include CCC Intelligent Solutions, Solera, Mitchell, Shift Technology, and Cambridge Mobile Telematics. Tractable suits insurers speeding up claims.
