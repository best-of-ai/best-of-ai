StudySmarter helps students prepare for exams. You upload lecture notes, a PDF, or a textbook chapter, and the app generates flashcards and summaries from the content. Quizzes test what you have learned, spaced repetition schedules reviews, and a study planner breaks your exam preparation into manageable sessions. A large library of shared flashcards and notes from other students covers school and university subjects.

AI features act as a study partner. An assistant can explain concepts, answer questions, and generate practice problems, and the app can create mind maps and summaries from uploaded materials. Progress tracking shows what you know and what needs more review. The app was founded in Germany and is used in many countries, with content aligned to various curricula.

StudySmarter has a free tier with limits and a premium plan that unlocks unlimited AI use, more storage, and extra features. User-created content varies in accuracy, and AI-generated cards can contain errors, so students should check them against reliable sources. Schools have different rules about AI assistance, so know your institution's policy. Quizlet, Anki, Knowt, and Brainscape are alternatives, and Khanmigo and ChatGPT provide tutoring. StudySmarter suits students who want an all-in-one exam prep tool.
