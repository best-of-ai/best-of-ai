Flair AI replaces much of a product photo shoot. You upload a product image, place it on a 3D canvas, and describe or choose the scene around it. The tool produces styled images that look like they were shot in a studio or on location, and it can place products on AI-generated human models for on-model shots.

Beyond stills, it makes product videos and ad creative. Templates let you reuse a scene across a whole catalog, a brand asset library keeps colors and logos consistent, and team collaboration features let several people work in one workspace. An API is available for stores that want to generate images programmatically.

You can start for free, with paid plans for higher volume and more features. The output needs a quick review, since AI product images can alter fine details such as labels or textures. Flair AI works well for small brands, marketing teams, and sellers who need many images quickly and do not have a photography budget.
