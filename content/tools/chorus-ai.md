Chorus records sales calls and video meetings and turns them into searchable transcripts. Managers can look through conversations to see what customers say about price, competitors, or objections, instead of relying on reps' summaries. The tool tags topics, tracks talk time and question patterns, and highlights key moments, which makes it faster to review a call than to listen to the whole recording.

The AI layer analyzes patterns across many conversations. It can surface deals that appear to be at risk, detect competitor mentions, summarize each call, and suggest next steps. Coaching features let managers share clips, give feedback, and compare reps against top performers. Integrations with Salesforce and other CRMs send call data and notes into the customer record so reps spend less time on updates.

Chorus belongs to ZoomInfo, which bundles it with its sales intelligence data, and it is sold by quote to sales organizations, not individuals. It is typically used by mid-sized and larger revenue teams. Recording customer calls requires disclosure and consent according to local law. Gong is the market leader and the closest competitor, Avoma and Fireflies serve smaller teams, and Salesloft and Outreach include their own conversation features. Buyers should confirm how ZoomInfo is packaging Chorus before starting a long contract.
