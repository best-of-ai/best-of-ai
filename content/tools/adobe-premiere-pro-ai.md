Premiere Pro is a timeline-based video editor used for everything from YouTube videos and corporate content to broadcast and feature work. It handles most camera formats, supports proxy workflows for heavy footage, and connects directly to After Effects, Audition, and other Adobe apps. That connection is a large part of why editing teams keep using it: a project can pass between an editor, a colorist, and a motion designer without leaving the Adobe ecosystem.

The AI tools target the slow parts of editing. Speech to Text creates a transcript and captions, and Text-Based Editing lets you cut footage by deleting words from the transcript. Enhance Speech cleans up noisy dialogue, Auto Reframe follows the subject when you convert a horizontal sequence to vertical or square, and Auto Ducking lowers music under speech. Generative Extend uses Firefly to add a few extra frames to a clip when a shot ends too early. Media Intelligence indexes your footage so you can search for clips with plain-language descriptions.

Premiere Pro is available through a Creative Cloud subscription, either alone or in a bundle, and runs on Windows and macOS. Generative features draw on a monthly credit allowance. Alternatives include DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, and CapCut. Those can be a better fit for color work, Mac-only editors, or fast social edits, but Premiere is the one most commonly requested in shared team workflows.
