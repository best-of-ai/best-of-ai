Applitools solves a problem that ordinary automated tests miss: whether a page looks correct. A functional test can confirm a button exists while the layout is broken, overlapping, or off-screen. Applitools Eyes takes screenshots during tests and compares them to approved baselines using a visual AI that understands layout, text, and color the way a person would. That avoids the flood of false alarms from pixel-by-pixel comparison, where tiny rendering differences trip every check.

The platform runs across many browsers, viewports, and devices through its Ultrafast Grid, so one test can verify a site on dozens of configurations quickly. Applitools Autonomous adds AI that helps create, run, and maintain tests, including writing steps in plain English and adapting when the interface changes. Dashboards group similar differences so reviewers can approve or reject many at once, and results feed back into the team's workflow.

It integrates with more than 60 tools, including Selenium, Cypress, Playwright, and CI/CD systems such as Jenkins and GitHub Actions, along with bug trackers. Pricing is tiered and based on usage, with a free starting plan, and enterprise options. It works best for teams with stable, automated test suites, since visual testing adds value on top of them. Percy from BrowserStack, Chromatic for components, and open-source Playwright snapshots are the alternatives.
