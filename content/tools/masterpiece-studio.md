Masterpiece Studio began with virtual reality tools for sculpting, painting, and animating in 3D, making 3D creation more intuitive for artists. It later moved toward generative AI, with Masterpiece X, which generates 3D models from text or images. Users describe an object, and the system produces a mesh with textures that can be refined or used as a starting point for game assets, virtual worlds, and prototypes.

The aim is to make output useful in production. Generated models can be downloaded in common formats and used in game engines such as Unity and Unreal, or opened in Blender and other tools for editing. Additional features help with rigging and animation, so characters can be posed and moved without a complex setup. A no-code approach targets creators who do not have a modeling background, including developers and designers who need many assets.

The company has worked with NVIDIA on parts of the technology and offers free and paid tiers. The market for text-to-3D has become crowded, and generated models often need cleanup for clean topology, scale, and materials before they are production ready. Meshy, Tripo, Rodin, Luma, and Kaedim are close competitors, and Blender, Maya, and ZBrush remain the standard tools for handcrafted work. Check the company's current status and terms before relying on it for a project.
