LANDR started with automated mastering. An artist uploads a mixed song, the system analyzes it, and an algorithm applies equalization, compression, and loudness adjustments, producing a master in minutes at a price below hiring an engineer. Style and loudness options guide the result.

The company now offers a wider set of services for independent musicians: distribution to streaming platforms, a marketplace of samples, plugins, a collaboration network, and tools for mixing and creating. A subscription bundles many of these.

Automated mastering works best on strong mixes and cannot repair serious problems, and some artists still prefer a human engineer for major releases. LANDR has free trials and paid plans. Alternatives include eMastered, CloudBounce, iZotope Ozone, BandLab, and DistroKid for distribution. LANDR suits independent artists releasing music.
