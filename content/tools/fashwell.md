Fashwell works on a persistent problem in online retail: product data is often incomplete or inconsistent. Its AI looks at product photos and automatically identifies attributes such as category, color, pattern, shape, neckline, and material, creating detailed, standardized tags. Those tags help retailers organize catalogs, improve search, and power filters that match how customers describe items.

The same technology supports discovery features. Visual search lets shoppers find similar items from an image, recommendation systems suggest products that match a style, and outfit and style-matching tools help shoppers complete a look. Retailers use it to save the effort of manual tagging across thousands of items and to improve the consistency of information across channels.

Fashwell sells to fashion and home retailers and marketplaces, typically through custom agreements and integrations. It's a small specialist, and public information about its recent activity is limited, so check the company's current offering before relying on it. Alternatives include Algolia and Bloomreach for search, Vue.ai, Syte, and Lily AI for fashion attribution and visual search, and Google's Vision Product Search. It suits retailers that need better product data.
