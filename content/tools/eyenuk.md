Eyenuk focuses on automated eye screening. A technician captures retinal images with a compatible camera, and EyeArt analyzes them and reports whether signs of more-than-mild disease are present, flagging patients who need referral. The process takes about a minute and does not need a specialist to read the images.

The purpose is to expand screening for people with diabetes who miss annual eye exams. Primary care clinics, endocrinology practices, and optometrists use it to catch disease earlier. The company has expanded the technology to other conditions and offers versions for different clinical settings.

EyeArt is sold to health providers, and reimbursement varies by region. It screens, and does not replace a full eye exam. Alternatives include Digital Diagnostics, AEYE Health, Optomed, and teleophthalmology review. Eyenuk suits clinics screening diabetic patients.
