Redfin helped modernize home search. Its site and app show listings with details, photos, and maps, along with estimated home values, price history, and tools to compare neighborhoods. A key difference from traditional brokerages is that its agents are salaried employees instead of paid mostly on commission, and Redfin has competed on lower listing fees and a customer-focused model.

Technology plays a large role. Its valuation model, the Redfin Estimate, uses machine learning on recent sales and listing data to estimate home values, and tools offer alerts, tours scheduling, and offers online. AI features help with search and recommendations. Rocket Companies, the owner of Rocket Mortgage, acquired Redfin, bringing mortgage and agent services together under a combined offering, including financing incentives when buyers use both.

Redfin's site is free for consumers, and the brokerage earns commissions and fees when it helps buy or sell. Service quality and fees vary by market, and valuations are estimates that can differ from sale prices. Alternatives include Zillow, Realtor.com, Compass, traditional agents, and Opendoor for instant offers. Redfin suits home buyers and sellers who value data-driven search and a salaried agent model.
