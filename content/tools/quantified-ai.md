Quantified AI helps sales reps practice before they talk to real customers. A rep enters a simulated conversation with an AI buyer that responds in real time, raises objections, and changes direction, and the system scores the performance on things like discovery, handling pushback, and messaging.

Managers use the results to see who needs coaching and track progress over time, and companies can create scenarios that mirror their own sales motions and products. Practice is available any time, which helps new hires ramp faster than relying only on role-play with colleagues.

Quantified AI is sold to sales organizations on custom pricing. Simulations cannot capture every real situation, and scores are guides rather than verdicts. Alternatives include Hyperbound, Second Nature, Mindtickle, Yoodli, and Gong. Quantified AI suits sales teams that want scalable practice.
