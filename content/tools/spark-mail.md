Spark is an email client that works with Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo, and other accounts. Its Smart Inbox sorts messages into categories, putting people and important mail first and keeping newsletters and notifications out of the way. Features such as snooze, send later, follow-up reminders, and quick replies make it faster to process mail, and a unified inbox combines accounts.

Collaboration is a differentiator. Teams can share emails, comment privately on messages, and draft replies together before sending, which suits small teams who handle shared mailboxes. AI features in the paid tier help write and rewrite emails, summarize long threads, and suggest replies, and a feature can search by asking questions. The app is available on Apple devices, Android, and Windows.

Spark has a free version and a paid plan for individuals and teams with advanced features and AI. Its AI tools need access to message content, so check the privacy policy. Superhuman is a premium alternative with an emphasis on speed, Shortwave is AI-first for Gmail, Apple Mail and Outlook are the defaults, and Hey takes a different approach to the inbox. Spark suits people who want a polished, cross-platform client with team features.
