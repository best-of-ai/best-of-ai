K Health begins with a conversation. In the app, you describe your symptoms and answer questions, and the AI compares your information with a large database of anonymized patient records to suggest what similar patients turned out to have, along with possible next steps. The symptom checker is free and meant to help you decide what to do.

If you need care, you can move to a visit with a clinician through messaging or video, often within minutes, for common issues such as colds, infections, and prescriptions, and for ongoing care with a primary care provider. The company works with health systems and insurers in some places, and it offers programs for conditions like mental health and weight management. AI helps summarize information for doctors and speeds up triage.

K Health offers free symptom checking and charges for visits and memberships, though pricing and insurance acceptance vary. Virtual care has limits: it cannot handle emergencies or conditions that need a physical exam or tests. Alternatives include Teladoc, Amwell, MDLIVE, Ada Health for symptom checking, One Medical, and Hims & Hers for specific services. K Health suits people who want convenient care for common conditions.
