Gemini in Workspace brings Google's models into the apps people use at work. In Gmail, it drafts replies and summarizes long threads. In Docs, it writes, rewrites, and proofreads, and can pull in information from files and email. In Sheets, it helps create formulas, tables, and analysis. In Slides, it generates slides and images, and in Meet it takes notes and summarizes.

A side panel gives a chat assistant that can search across a user's Drive, Gmail, and Calendar, and answer questions using that content. Organizations can control access, and Google states that business data is not used to train models without permission. Newer features include agents and tools such as NotebookLM for deeper source work.

Availability depends on Workspace plan, as Google has bundled the AI into its business and enterprise tiers. Output needs checking for accuracy.
