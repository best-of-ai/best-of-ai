Moveworks brings an assistant to employee support. Workers ask for help in Slack, Teams, or a portal, and the system understands the request, finds the answer in company knowledge, or completes the action by connecting to IT and HR systems. Common examples are unlocking an account, requesting software, or finding a policy.

The company grew by focusing on large enterprises and on language understanding specific to workplace requests. It added generative AI and agent capabilities, with a platform for building custom agents and connecting to many business systems.

ServiceNow acquired Moveworks and has been combining it with its own AI under the Otto experience. It is sold on enterprise contracts. Alternatives include ServiceNow Now Assist, Aisera, Capacity, Microsoft Copilot, and Glean. Moveworks suits large organizations that want to automate employee support.
