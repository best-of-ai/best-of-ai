Vertex AI is where Google Cloud customers build with AI. At its center is Model Garden, a catalog that includes Google's Gemini, Imagen, and Veo models, open models like Llama and Gemma, and models from partners such as Anthropic. You can call a model through an API, test it in a studio, and then deploy it with Google Cloud's security, networking, and compliance controls. Data stays under the customer's governance, which matters for regulated industries.

The platform also supports the engineering around models. You can tune models on your own data, ground answers in company documents or Google Search, evaluate outputs, and monitor performance. Agent Builder and related tooling help assemble agents that call tools and follow instructions, while pipelines, feature stores, and notebooks serve traditional machine learning teams that train their own models. It connects with BigQuery and other Google Cloud data services.

Pricing is usage-based, charged per token for generative models and by compute for training and hosting, with a free tier and credits for new Google Cloud accounts. The breadth of features can make it complicated to get started, and Google has renamed and reorganized its AI products several times. Amazon Bedrock and Azure AI Foundry are the direct competitors. For individuals who just want to try Gemini, Google AI Studio is a simpler starting point.
