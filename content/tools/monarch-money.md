Monarch Money brings accounts into one dashboard. Users link banks, cards, loans, and investments, and the app tracks spending, net worth, and recurring bills. Budgets can follow a flexible or category-based model, and goals help save toward targets.

Couples and families can share views and manage budgets together while keeping some accounts private. AI categorizes transactions, spots subscriptions, and gives summaries and insights, and an assistant answers questions about finances. Reports show trends over time.

Monarch is a paid subscription with a trial, and it became a popular choice after Mint shut down. Bank connections occasionally break. Alternatives include YNAB, Copilot, Empower, Actual Budget, and Rocket Money. Monarch Money suits people who want a complete view of their finances.
