Remesh scales the focus group. A moderator poses questions to hundreds of participants at once, and people respond in their own words and then rate each other's answers, so the group quickly reveals where opinions agree and differ. Responses are analyzed as they arrive.

AI clusters comments into themes, summarizes sentiment, and highlights segments by demographic. Researchers use it for message testing, employee listening, public policy input, and product research. A single session can produce depth and breadth that would take many interviews.

Remesh is sold to enterprises, agencies, and public sector bodies on subscription. Online sessions reach people who are comfortable with the format. Alternatives include Qualtrics, Discuss.io, Dovetail for analysis, Respondent, and traditional focus groups. Remesh suits researchers seeking qualitative insight at scale.
