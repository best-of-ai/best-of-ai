Resume.io helps job seekers create a polished resume quickly. You choose a template, fill in your experience, education, and skills in a guided form, and see the layout update. The templates are designed to be clean and readable by applicant tracking systems, and you can adjust colors and fonts, reorder sections, and download the result as a PDF. Cover letter and CV builders follow the same approach.

AI features help with the hard part, which is the wording. The assistant suggests bullet points for a job title, drafts a professional summary, and can tailor a resume to a job description. A resume checker highlights weaknesses, and a job tracker helps manage applications. The tool also provides guides and examples for different professions.

Resume.io lets you build for free but charges to download, usually through a trial that converts into a recurring subscription, and users have complained that cancellation is easy to miss, so read the terms. AI-generated phrasing can sound generic, so edit it to reflect your real accomplishments. Alternatives include Canva, which is flexible, Kickresume, Zety, Rezi, Enhancv, and Novoresume, and Google Docs templates cost nothing. Resume.io suits people who want a quick, professional look.
