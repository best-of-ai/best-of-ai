TubeBuddy is a browser extension and mobile app for YouTube creators. It adds tools to the YouTube interface, including a keyword explorer that estimates how competitive a search term is, tag suggestions, and a score that shows how well a video is optimized. These help creators choose topics and fill in titles and descriptions with search in mind.

Other features save time. Bulk tools update many videos at once, thumbnail A/B testing compares designs for click rates, and scheduling and reminders organize publishing. Analytics show how a channel performs and how it compares to others. AI tools suggest titles, descriptions, and ideas based on a video's topic.

TubeBuddy has a free tier and paid plans by number of channels and features. YouTube's algorithm is not fully public, so optimization scores are guides rather than guarantees, and good content matters most. Alternatives include vidIQ, Morningfame, Social Blade for stats, and YouTube's built-in research features. TubeBuddy suits creators who want to optimize their channels.
