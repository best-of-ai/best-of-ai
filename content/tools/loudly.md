Loudly offers music for people who need soundtracks without composing. Users can create tracks by choosing a genre, mood, and tempo, or by describing what they want in a prompt, and then download the result. A library of ready-made AI tracks lets you browse by genre and use case. It is intended for video creators, podcasters, streamers, game developers, and businesses that need background music.

Several features support practical use. A personalization option adjusts elements like instruments and structure, stems can be downloaded for editing, and an API allows developers to embed music generation in their own products, such as apps that need adaptive soundtracks. The company positions its music as safe for commercial use, with licenses that cover content on social platforms.

Loudly has a free tier with limits and paid plans for downloads and commercial rights. Terms vary by plan on whether you can distribute the music as your own song on streaming services, so read the license. Compared with song generators like Suno and Udio, Loudly is aimed at functional background music and usable licensing rather than creative songwriting with vocals. Soundraw, Mubert, Beatoven, and AIVA are close alternatives. Quality varies by genre.
