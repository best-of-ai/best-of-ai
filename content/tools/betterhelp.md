BetterHelp is one of the largest online therapy platforms. You fill out a questionnaire about your needs and preferences, and the service suggests a licensed counselor. You can message your therapist, join live sessions by video, phone, or chat, and schedule at times that suit you, which removes commuting and makes weekly care easier for many people. Therapists are independent licensed professionals such as psychologists, social workers, and counselors.

The pricing model is a recurring subscription based on location and therapist, rather than per-visit billing, and it generally does not accept insurance, which some people find convenient and others find expensive. It is owned by Teladoc Health. Some reviewers praise the convenience and access, while others report difficulty finding a good match or a pushy sales process. Therapy that happens in messages works differently from a weekly hour in an office, and some people prefer live sessions.

The company has faced regulatory scrutiny, including an action over how it handled user data for advertising, so privacy settings and policies deserve attention. Online therapy is not appropriate for emergencies or severe conditions that need in-person or psychiatric care. Talkspace, Cerebral, and Headway are alternatives, some of which work with insurance. In-person therapists and employer assistance programs are other routes, and 988 is the crisis line in the United States.
