Mo takes an exploratory approach to testing. Instead of writing scripts for each test, a team points Mo at an app and supplies login credentials, and AI agents navigate the interface, trying flows, entering unusual inputs, and checking for errors or broken behavior. The agents report what they find.

Momentic's wider platform helps teams write and maintain end-to-end tests in natural language, which reduces upkeep when interfaces change. Mo complements that by finding issues no one thought to test, like a human tester poking around.

Momentic is a paid product for software teams. Exploratory agents can produce noise and may need guidance on what matters, and they run against live environments, so use a staging site with test data. Alternatives include Testim, mabl, Playwright with AI agents, BrowserStack Test Companion, and QA Wolf. Mo suits teams adding AI exploratory testing.
