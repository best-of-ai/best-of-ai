Ghostwall belongs to the group of click fraud prevention tools. Advertisers who pay per click can lose money when bots or competitors click ads. The service watches traffic to paid campaigns, looks for suspicious patterns, and blocks the sources that appear fraudulent, for example by adding them to exclusion lists on ad platforms.

Reports show blocked clicks and estimated savings. The ad platforms filter some invalid traffic themselves, so independent tools add value mainly for accounts with significant spend or visible fraud problems. Results depend on how good the detection is, and aggressive blocking can also exclude real customers.

Because independent coverage is limited, test with a trial and compare reported savings against your own analytics. Alternatives include ClickCease, PPC Protect, Lunio, TrafficGuard, and Fraud Blocker. Ghostwall suits advertisers concerned about invalid clicks.
