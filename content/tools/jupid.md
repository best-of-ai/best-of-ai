Jupid is accounting help for sole proprietors. Users link their bank accounts and cards, and the app sorts each transaction into the categories used on Schedule C, which is the tax form for self-employment income. It flags business expenses, helps separate personal spending, and keeps a running view of profit.

The tool estimates quarterly taxes and highlights deductions that people often miss, which reduces the scramble at tax time. Reports can be exported to share with an accountant or to use when filing. AI handles the sorting and learns from corrections.

Jupid has a free tier and paid plans. Tax rules vary and categories depend on the situation, so a tax professional should confirm anything important. It focuses on U.S. taxes. Alternatives include QuickBooks Self-Employed (now part of QuickBooks Solopreneur), Keeper, Hurdlr, FreshBooks, and Bench. Jupid suits freelancers who want simple bookkeeping.
