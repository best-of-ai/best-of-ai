Palette.fm adds color to old photos. A user uploads a black-and-white image, and the AI predicts plausible colors for skin, clothing, sky, and objects, returning a colorized version in seconds. Different style options change the overall palette, and results can be refined.

Families use it for old portraits, and historians and archivists use it to make collections more engaging. Colorization is an educated guess based on learned patterns, so colors may not match the real scene, and it should not be presented as historically accurate.

Palette.fm has a free tier with limits and paid plans. Alternatives include MyHeritage In Color, Colourise.sg, DeOldify, Photoshop Neural Filters, and Remini. Palette.fm suits people restoring and sharing historical photos.
