Airfinity provides data and forecasts about health threats and the products to counter them. Its analysts and models track outbreaks, monitor vaccine and drug development pipelines, and estimate supply, demand, and production. The data helps decision makers judge where a disease is heading and which products might reach the market.

The company became widely known for its work during the COVID-19 pandemic, when it tracked vaccine production and distribution around the world, and has since expanded to other diseases and to wider life sciences analysis. Its customers include pharmaceutical companies planning strategy, investors, public health bodies, and policy groups. Machine learning helps process large volumes of data from many sources.

Airfinity sells through subscriptions and custom projects, so detailed access is aimed at professionals. Forecasts rely on assumptions and available data, and public figures it publishes should be read as estimates. Comparable data providers include Clarivate, Evaluate, and GlobalData, and Metaculus offers crowd forecasting. Airfinity suits organizations tracking infectious disease and the biopharma market.
