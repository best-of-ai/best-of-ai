Vic.ai reads invoices and processes them with little manual input. It extracts data, suggests coding to the correct accounts, routes approvals, and flags unusual items, learning from how accountants correct it. The aim is touchless processing for routine invoices.

It integrates with ERP systems such as NetSuite, SAP, and Microsoft Dynamics, and offers payment and analytics features. Accounting firms and mid-sized to large finance teams use it to cut manual work.

Vic.ai is sold on subscription with custom pricing. Alternatives include Stampli, BILL, Tipalti, Coupa, and Rossum. Vic.ai suits finance teams automating payables.
