Le Chat is Mistral's answer to ChatGPT and Claude. The French company designed the app for general use: answering questions, writing and editing, summarizing documents, analyzing images, generating images, and running code. Fast response modes make it quick for everyday queries, and a canvas supports working on documents and code beside the chat.

Further features address more advanced needs. Deep research compiles longer reports from web sources, connectors link to services like Gmail, Google Drive, and Notion, and custom agents let users set up specialized assistants. Memory can retain preferences between chats, and mobile apps bring it to phones. The assistant uses Mistral's own models, some of which are open-weight, and emphasizes European hosting and privacy options.

Le Chat has a free tier, a Pro plan with higher limits and features, and Team and Enterprise plans that add administration and the option to deploy within a company's own infrastructure. In capability, it trails the top systems from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google on the hardest tasks, though it is strong for the cost and fast. Alternatives include ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and DeepSeek. Le Chat suits users who prefer a European provider.
