RSM Stone Forest IT partners with forward-looking businesses to deliver technology advisory, implementation support, and managed services, focusing on IT infrastructure, IT security, data protection, and AI-driven business application tools (ERP/CRM/Microsoft 365 & Copilot). As your Technology Consultant, we simplify, digitalise and manage all your technology needs with IT best practices and governance frameworks, so you can accelerate business growth, all while saving costs, staying future-ready, secure, and compliant. 

Recognised for our CISO and technology consulting expertise, our GRC team are the appointed consultants for CSA's CISOaaS for Cyber Essentials Mark (CEM) and IMDA's Data Protection Essentials (DPE). These nationwide government initiatives aim to provide expert guidance on IT governance, cybersecurity, and remediation support to help businesses transform and stay protected in the digital age.
