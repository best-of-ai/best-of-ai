[No Stairs Please](https://nostairsplease.com/) is a directory of small live-music venues across all 50 US states, Washington, DC, and British Columbia ("Every small stage in all 50 states, and who books it.").

Touring bands, solo performers, and AI booking agents can browse the [venue directory](https://nostairsplease.com/venues) or connect directly to the remote [No Stairs Please MCP server](https://nostairsplease.com/mcp) to search venues by location, capacity, and booking contact information.
