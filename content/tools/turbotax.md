TurboTax makes tax filing a guided interview. You answer plain-language questions, import documents like W-2s and 1099s, and the software fills in the forms, checks for errors, and estimates your refund or amount owed. It supports many situations, from simple returns to investments, self-employment, and rental income, and e-files returns with federal and state tax agencies. It has long been the most recognized brand in consumer tax software in the U.S.

Help options range from self-service to full service. Live expert tiers connect you with tax professionals who answer questions or review and file your return, and audit support is available. Intuit Assist, its AI assistant, answers tax questions and helps find deductions and credits. Importing prior-year data and connecting to financial accounts reduces manual entry.

TurboTax is sold in tiers based on complexity, with a free offering for simple returns that has been controversial: Intuit faced regulatory action over how it advertised free filing. Prices rise as your situation becomes more complicated, so compare tiers carefully. Alternatives include H&R Block, FreeTaxUSA, Cash App Taxes, and the IRS's Free File programs, or a professional preparer. TurboTax suits people who want guided filing and optional expert help.
