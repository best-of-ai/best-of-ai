Master Assistant analyzes a track and suggests a starting chain, and users then adjust the modules. It includes a maximizer, imager, and match EQ. Metering shows loudness and spectrum.

Several editions are sold at different prices. Assistant settings are a starting point, not a final master.

LANDR and eMastered offer automated mastering online. Ozone suits sound engineers who master themselves.
