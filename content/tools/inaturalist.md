iNaturalist is a citizen science project and app. Users upload photos or sounds of organisms they find, a computer vision model suggests what species it is, and other observers weigh in. When enough people agree, the observation reaches research grade, and the data is shared with scientific databases.

The result is one of the largest collections of biodiversity observations. Researchers use it to track species ranges, detect invasive species, and study changes over time. Projects and bioblitz events organize community efforts, and schools use it for nature education.

iNaturalist is free and run by a nonprofit partnership with the California Academy of Sciences and the National Geographic Society. AI suggestions can be wrong, especially for similar species, so community confirmation matters. Alternatives include Seek, Merlin Bird ID for birds, PlantNet, and Google Lens. iNaturalist suits nature lovers and researchers.
