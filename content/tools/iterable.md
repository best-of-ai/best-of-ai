Iterable lets marketers build cross-channel journeys. They segment customers by behavior, design workflows in a visual canvas, and send messages triggered by actions, such as signing up, abandoning a cart, or reaching a milestone. Data from websites, apps, and warehouses feeds the profile used for personalization.

AI assists in several areas: optimizing the time a message is sent for each person, predicting engagement, recommending content, and helping generate copy. Experimentation tools test variants, and analytics tie campaigns to outcomes. Integration with data warehouses supports teams that centralize customer data.

Iterable is sold on custom pricing for mid-sized and large brands. It competes with several large engagement platforms. Alternatives include Braze, Klaviyo, MoEngage, Customer.io, and Salesforce Marketing Cloud. Iterable suits consumer brands running multichannel campaigns.
