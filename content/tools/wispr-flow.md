Wispr Flow turns speech into polished text. A user presses a key, talks, and the cleaned-up text appears wherever the cursor is. The AI removes filler words, fixes false starts, handles punctuation, and adjusts tone to the app, so messages and documents read as if typed.

It supports many languages, custom vocabulary for names and terms, and commands for editing by voice. Writers, developers, and professionals use it to speed up emails, notes, and prompts for AI tools.

Wispr Flow has a free tier with limits and paid plans. Audio is processed in the cloud, so privacy settings matter. Alternatives include Superwhisper, Voicetype AI, Dragon, Apple dictation, and Talon. Wispr Flow suits heavy writers who prefer to speak.
