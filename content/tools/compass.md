Compass is a brokerage that positions itself as a technology company. Its agents use a platform that combines a customer relationship manager, marketing tools for listings, market data, and transaction management in one place. Homebuyers and sellers use the site and app to search listings, see market trends, and work with an agent, and the tools are free to consumers because the company earns money through commissions on sales.

Technology supports the agent workflow. The platform helps with pricing recommendations, creates marketing materials such as brochures and social posts, tracks leads and client conversations, and organizes documents and deadlines through a deal. AI features assist with drafting listing descriptions, summarizing client interactions, and surfacing homes or clients to contact. The company has also tested private listings strategies for sellers.

Compass operates in many U.S. metro areas, and it's part of a consolidating industry in which brokerages have been merging. Its model is attractive to agents who want a modern toolset, though brokerage fees and splits vary. Competitors include Redfin, Zillow, which markets rather than brokers, eXp Realty, Keller Williams, and RE/MAX. Consumers should compare agents on experience and fees rather than on technology alone. Compass suits home sellers and buyers working with a technology-oriented agent.
