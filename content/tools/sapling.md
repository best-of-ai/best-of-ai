Sapling helps support and sales teams write faster and more consistently. As an agent types in the help desk or CRM, Sapling suggests completions based on past replies, fixes grammar and tone, and inserts approved snippets. This reduces typing time and variation.

Managers use quality analytics to monitor response times and consistency, and see how suggestions are used. It integrates with Zendesk, Salesforce, Intercom, and other tools, and offers an API and an AI content detector.

Sapling has a free tier and paid plans per user. Suggestions based on past replies carry old mistakes too, so keep content current. Alternatives include Grammarly Business, Zendesk AI, Intercom Copilot, Textio, and Front's AI. Sapling suits support teams wanting faster replies.
