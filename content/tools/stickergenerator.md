StickerGenerator helps people quickly turn photos into sticker-ready PNGs without manual editing. Users upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP, choose one of six sticker styles and an aspect ratio, then generate and download a transparent PNG. Key features include preset styles, ratio selection, side-by-side preview, mobile-friendly flow, and credit-based usage with a limited free guest quota. It is for creators, small businesses, and teams who need stickers for chat apps, social posts, product mockups, or printable sheets. Output is a downloadable PNG suitable for digital use or print.
