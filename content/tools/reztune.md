Reztune helps job seekers customize applications. A user uploads a resume and pastes a job posting, and the AI rewrites sections to match the role, emphasizing relevant skills and adjusting wording to align with terms in the posting.

Tailoring takes time to do by hand, and tools like this make it faster, which appeals to people applying to many jobs. The risk is overstating experience or producing generic phrasing, so users should review every line for accuracy.

Reztune has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Jobscan, Teal, Rezi, Enhancv, and Kickresume. Reztune suits job seekers who tailor resumes often.
