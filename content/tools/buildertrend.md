Buildertrend focuses on residential construction. Builders manage a project from estimate to completion in a single system: schedules, selections, budgets, purchase orders, daily logs, photos, and documents. Clients can log in to approve choices, view progress, and see invoices, which cuts down on phone calls.

Financial tools track costs against budgets, handle change orders, and send bills and payments. Subcontractors get access to their schedules and tasks, and mobile apps let teams update from the job site. AI features assist with tasks like summarizing communication and extracting data from documents.

Buildertrend is sold by subscription, and pricing varies by plan. It is oriented toward residential builders more than large commercial contractors. Alternatives include Procore, CoConstruct (merged into Buildertrend), JobTread, Houzz Pro, and Sage. Buildertrend suits home builders and remodeling firms.
