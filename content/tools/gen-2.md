Runway builds generative video tools for filmmakers, designers, and creators. Gen-3 Alpha was a major step in its line, generating short clips from a text prompt or an image with better detail, motion, and consistency than earlier versions. It supported controls for camera movement and style.

Runway's platform around its models includes editing tools, motion brush features, and ways to extend or modify clips. Its research focuses on world models and video generation for creative work, and the company works with studios and artists. The newer models in the line have largely taken the place of Gen-3.

Runway uses a credit system with a free allowance and paid plans. Generated video has limits in length, physics, and fine control, and rights to use outputs depend on plan terms. Alternatives include Google Veo, OpenAI Sora (now shut down), Luma, Kling, and Pika. This entry covers the Gen-3 Alpha era model, which suits creators exploring Runway's tools.
