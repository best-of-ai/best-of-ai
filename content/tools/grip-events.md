Grip focuses on a problem every large event has: people attend hoping to meet the right contacts, then wander the floor without finding them. Grip collects attendees' goals when they register, such as buying a product, finding partners, or recruiting, and uses matchmaking to recommend whom to meet. People can accept suggestions, message one another, and schedule meetings before and during the event.

Organizers get tools to manage the experience. Exhibitors can see qualified leads and capture them at the booth, sponsors get measurable engagement data, and the event team can track how many meetings happened and how satisfied participants were. A mobile app and integrations with registration systems, CRMs, and event platforms connect the pieces. Typical customers are trade show and conference organizers in industries like technology, healthcare, and manufacturing.

Grip is sold to event organizers under custom contracts, with pricing based on the size of the event or the number of events. Matchmaking quality depends on the information people provide, and the benefit relies on participants actually using the app. Competitors and alternatives include Brella, Swapcard, Bizzabo, and Cvent's networking tools. It suits events where meetings and lead generation are the main reasons people attend.
