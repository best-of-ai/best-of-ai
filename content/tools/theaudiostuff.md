The Audio Stuff covers personal audio gear with an emphasis on independence. Reviews of headphones, in-ear monitors, speakers, digital-to-analog converters, and amplifiers combine listening impressions with objective measurements, and the site states that it does not publish sponsored reviews. Comparisons and buying guides help readers navigate crowded categories.

For people buying audio equipment, the combination of measured data and subjective notes is useful. Frequency response graphs and other measurements show how products behave, which helps predict how they will sound, and the writing explains what that means for different music and preferences. The site also covers news and recommendations for budgets.

The site is free to read. As with any review source, taste differs and measurements do not capture every aspect of sound, so reading several sources and, when possible, trying gear yourself is wise. It has a connection to the AI directory category in this list only as a general resource. Other audio review sites include Rtings, Crinacle, Audio Science Review, Headphones.com, and Soundguys. The Audio Stuff suits hobbyists researching audio purchases.
