Pearl focuses on dental radiograph analysis. Dentists upload X-rays, and the Second Opinion tool highlights suspected problems, such as caries, calculus, and periapical lesions, in real time, which helps catch issues and show patients the findings.

The company has expanded into other uses, including claims review for insurers and tools for dental service organizations that want consistent standards across offices. It holds regulatory clearances for several of its functions.

Pearl is sold on subscription to practices and organizations. AI findings need dentist confirmation, and insurers using AI on claims draw scrutiny. Alternatives include Overjet, VideaHealth, Denti.AI, Dentrix Detect, and Align's tools. Pearl suits dental practices adding AI radiograph review.
