Aerogram is a collaborative AI workspace that redefines interaction with artificial intelligence by acting as a "Figma-meets-GitHub" canvas for prompting. It functions as a comprehensive AI-powered workspace hub, integrating a visual thinking board, multi-model automation tool, and prompt orchestration canvas. The platform directly addresses critical challenges posed by traditional linear AI chat interfaces, such as lost context, difficulty in organizing prompts, and the absence of robust version control, by allowing users to "snap together AI prompts like Lego blocks" on an infinite, multiplayer canvas.

Core features include card-per-idea clarity, where each AI output becomes a distinct, remixable card, enabling users to branch ideas into new threads while preserving original context. It offers one-click fork & compare for seamless side-by-side refinement of both text and image prompts, with an inherent history for every card. For modular reuse, any card or cluster can be templated and saved as a "Snippet" for quick deployment across projects.

Aerogram boasts multi-model integration, providing access to and the ability to switch between leading AI models such as GPT-4o, Claude-Opus, Gemini-2.5, and specialized models like Ideogram V2 and FLUX.1 [dev], as well as local models, all within a unified workspace. This model-agnostic approach ensures flexibility and helps optimize content, accuracy, and cost.

Real-time collaboration is a cornerstone feature, allowing teammates to co-build and edit workflows simultaneously, complete with visible cursors, comments, and version tracking. This transforms AI into a shared organizational capability, moving beyond isolated chat interactions. The Visual Workflow Builder helps users understand complex AI logic flows, making them easy to debug and explain.

Aerogram is designed for a broad audience, encompassing "literally everyone" from individual knowledge workers to large corporates, agencies, creators, and educators. It simplifies prompt building for users without development backgrounds, streamlines creative workflows, and fosters critical thinking skills. The business operates on a bottom-up SaaS model, offering a free canvas option. Its primary goal is to turn "AI transformation into action" by providing unparalleled tools for collaboration, context, and continuous learning in AI.
