The tool helps people to learn AI. Once they provide their profile, the system creates a personalized learning path adapted to their role and industry. They follow then micro lessons of around 10 min and have quizzes with badges rewarding if they succeed. The app is giving practical example and use cases of what to do and how to use AI in their position. They also have access to Sam, a learning agent that keeps all the context and memory about the discussions. Sam helps them about everything related to their job.
