Evisort uses AI to understand contracts. It ingests agreements, identifies clauses and key terms such as renewal dates, payment terms, and liability limits, and stores them as structured data. Teams can search across thousands of contracts, instead of hunting through PDFs.

It supports the contract lifecycle: drafting from templates, negotiating, tracking obligations, and getting alerts before renewals. Reports show risk and spend across the portfolio. Generative AI helps review contracts against playbooks and suggests changes.

Workday acquired Evisort, and its technology is now offered within Workday as contract intelligence and contract lifecycle management. It is sold to enterprises on subscription. Legal teams should review AI findings, since a missed clause can matter. Alternatives include Ironclad, Icertis, DocuSign CLM, LinkSquares, and Luminance. Evisort suits legal and procurement teams that manage many contracts.
