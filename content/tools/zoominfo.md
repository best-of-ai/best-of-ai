ZoomInfo provides one of the largest databases of business contacts and companies. Sales and marketing teams search by title, industry, company size, location, and technology to build lists, and see verified email addresses, direct phone numbers, org charts, and firmographic details. Intent data shows which companies are researching topics related to a product, helping teams reach out when interest is high.

The platform has expanded from data into a workflow product. It includes tools for sales engagement, marketing advertising, conversation intelligence through Chorus, and enrichment that keeps CRM records up to date automatically. AI features include assistants that research accounts, draft outreach, and recommend who to contact, and agents that handle prospecting tasks. It integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, and other systems.

ZoomInfo is sold by annual subscription with credits and usage tiers and a reputation for being expensive and for contracts that are hard to exit, so negotiate carefully. Data accuracy varies, and sending outreach based on purchased data raises compliance considerations such as GDPR and CAN-SPAM. Competitors include Apollo, Lusha, Seamless.AI, Cognism for European data, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator. ZoomInfo suits mid-sized and large sales teams that depend on data coverage.
