Activepieces is an alternative to Zapier built with openness in mind. Users build automations called flows by chaining a trigger, such as a new form entry, to actions across apps like Google Sheets, Slack, Gmail, and CRMs. The editor is friendly to non-developers, and code steps are available for custom logic. The integrations, called pieces, are open-source and community-contributed, and each can also work as a tool for AI agents.

AI is built in. Flows can call language models, use agents that choose their actions, and expose pieces through the Model Context Protocol, so AI assistants can use them to take action in connected apps. Teams use it to automate sales follow-ups, support triage, data syncing, and content workflows. Because the platform is open source, organizations can run it on their own servers for data control and customize it.

Activepieces has a free self-hosted community edition and cloud plans priced by usage, as well as enterprise features. It is newer and has fewer integrations than Zapier or Make, though the catalog is growing, and self-hosting requires technical effort. n8n is the best-known open-source competitor with more advanced capabilities, Make offers a rich visual builder, and Zapier is easiest for non-technical users. Activepieces suits teams that value openness and simple pricing.
