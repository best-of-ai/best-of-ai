Taromi pairs the practice of tarot with AI interpretation. A user poses a question, draws cards from a virtual deck, and receives a written reading that explains the cards in context. Many users treat it as a prompt for reflection more than a prediction.

The format is easy to use daily, with options for different spreads and for saving past readings. Like horoscopes, tarot has no proven ability to foretell events, and readings generated by AI are creative text, not guidance backed by evidence.

It is best used for entertainment and self-reflection, not for decisions about health, money, or relationships. Taromi has a free tier and paid options. Alternatives include Co-Star for astrology, Labyrinthos, Golden Thread Tarot, and The Pattern. Taromi suits people who enjoy tarot as a reflective ritual.
