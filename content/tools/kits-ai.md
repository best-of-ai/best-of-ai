Kits AI targets music creators. A producer can record a vocal and convert it into another singer's voice from a library, create a model of their own voice for consistent takes, or separate a song into vocals and instruments to remix. A text to speech option and tools for vocal cleanup are included.

Its distinguishing approach is licensing. Kits offers voices from artists who have agreed to participate and be paid, which contrasts with unauthorized voice clones that raise copyright and rights-of-publicity problems. Users still need to follow license terms for how converted vocals are released.

Kits AI has a free tier with limits and paid plans. Voice conversion quality depends on input recordings. Alternatives include Jammable, ElevenLabs, Moises, LALAL.AI, and Voice-Swap. Kits AI suits musicians and producers who want legitimate vocal tools.
