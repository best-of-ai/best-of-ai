Coupa gives companies control over what they spend. Employees request purchases through approved channels, procurement manages suppliers and contracts, and accounts payable processes invoices and payments. Having it in one system improves visibility and helps enforce policy.

The company's distinctive asset is aggregated data. With permission, Coupa analyzes anonymized spend across its customer base to benchmark prices, identify supplier risk, and recommend savings. AI features help with spend classification, contract review, and answering questions about spending, and supplier networks connect buyers with vendors.

Coupa is sold to mid-sized and large organizations on subscription, and is owned by private equity after being taken private. Implementations are significant projects. Alternatives include SAP Ariba, Oracle Procurement, Ivalua, Zip, and Jaggaer. Coupa suits companies centralizing procurement and spend control.
