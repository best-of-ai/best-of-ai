Sassbook AI is a small suite of text tools. The AI story writer takes a premise and creates a narrative, with options to continue and steer the plot, aimed at hobby writers and students looking for ideas. Other tools summarize long text into shorter versions, paraphrase passages in different styles, and generate headlines and titles.

The tools work in a browser with a simple interface. Users paste text or enter a prompt, choose settings such as length or style, and get results they can edit. It also includes a plagiarism check and a few utilities for writers. The target audience includes bloggers, students, and casual writers who need quick help.

Sassbook has a free tier and paid plans for more usage and features. Its output quality is comparable to general AI models, which also provide these functions freely, and it's less polished than specialized platforms. Academic and professional use needs care for originality and accuracy. Alternatives include QuillBot for paraphrasing and summarizing, Rytr, Wordtune, NovelAI and Sudowrite for fiction, and ChatGPT or Claude. Sassbook suits casual users who want a few quick text tools.
