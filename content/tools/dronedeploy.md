DroneDeploy is a platform for drone data. Users plan an automated flight from a tablet or phone, the drone captures images, and the software stitches them into orthomosaic maps and 3D models. From these, it can measure distances, areas, and volumes, such as a stockpile or excavation.

Repeat flights show progress. Construction teams compare site conditions to plans, farmers monitor crop health, and mining operations measure material movement. AI detects objects and changes, and the platform has expanded to capture from ground robots and 360 cameras for indoor progress tracking.

DroneDeploy has a free tier with limits and paid plans by features and users. Accuracy depends on flight planning and ground control points, and drone operations are subject to aviation rules. Alternatives include Pix4D, Propeller Aero, Esri Drone2Map, Procore integrations, and OpenDroneMap, which is open source. DroneDeploy suits construction, agriculture, and mining teams that map sites by drone.
