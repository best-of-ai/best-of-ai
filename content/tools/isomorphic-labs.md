Isomorphic Labs aims to use AI to design medicines. It builds on DeepMind's work on predicting protein structures and extends the idea to the interactions between proteins and drug molecules, so models can propose compounds that bind to a target. The goal is to reduce the time and cost of finding candidates.

Rather than selling software, the company runs drug discovery programs, both on its own and in collaboration with pharmaceutical companies. It has described building a platform that can be applied across diseases, with early efforts in areas such as oncology and immunology.

Isomorphic Labs is a private subsidiary of Alphabet. AI-designed drugs still need laboratory testing and clinical trials, which take years, and success is not assured. Peers include Recursion, Insilico Medicine, Schrödinger, Xaira, and Generate Biomedicines. Isomorphic Labs is relevant to those following AI-driven drug discovery.
