Users add bottles to a virtual cellar, track what to drink when, and see aggregated notes from other members. Sommeliers use the community notes to judge how a wine is aging.

The service is free with optional paid features, and notes are user submitted.

Vivino has a larger casual user base. CellarTracker suits sommeliers and serious collectors.
