MotherDuck takes the DuckDB analytical database, which runs on a laptop, and adds a cloud service. A user connects from their local DuckDB, queries data in the cloud, and combines it with local files, in what the company calls hybrid execution, using each machine for what it does best.

It targets teams that do not need a large data warehouse. Features include shared databases, collaboration, integrations with common data tools, and an AI assistant that helps write SQL. The pitch is simple, fast analytics at lower cost for data in the gigabytes to terabytes range.

MotherDuck has a free tier and usage-based plans. Very large or highly concurrent workloads may need bigger warehouses. Alternatives include Snowflake, BigQuery, ClickHouse, Databricks SQL, and plain DuckDB. MotherDuck suits small and mid-sized data teams.
