Agiloft is a configurable system for managing contracts from request to renewal. Teams create agreements from templates and clause libraries, route them through approvals, negotiate with redlining, collect signatures, and store them in a searchable repository. Alerts remind owners of renewals and obligations, and reports show how contracts are performing, so legal, procurement, and sales teams share a single source of truth.

Its design emphasizes flexibility without custom coding. Organizations can adjust fields, workflows, and rules through no-code tools, which suits companies with unusual processes, and it connects with systems such as Salesforce, SAP, and DocuSign. AI features extract data from existing contracts, identify risky or missing clauses, summarize agreements, and let users ask questions in plain language, which speeds up migration from messy archives.

Agiloft is sold to enterprises on custom subscriptions, with implementation partners common, and is considered a strong choice for complex requirements. It can take time to configure well. Competitors include Ironclad, Icertis, DocuSign CLM, ContractPodAi (now Leah), Sirion, and Conga. As with any AI in legal work, extracted terms and risk flags need review by legal professionals. Agiloft suits organizations with high volumes of varied contracts.
