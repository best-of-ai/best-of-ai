Beatoven.ai is aimed at video makers, podcasters, and game developers who need background music without searching a stock library. You describe the track you want, such as a calm acoustic piece for a travel vlog, or choose a genre, mood, and duration, and the AI composes an original piece. You can change the emotion of different sections along the timeline, which is helpful when a video moves from a tense moment to a resolved one.

Tracks can be adjusted after generation by swapping instruments, changing tempo, and exporting stems for editing in other software. Beatoven also generates sound effects from text, and it provides MP3 and WAV downloads. The company trains its models on licensed material from contributing artists, and it positions that as a clean way to avoid copyright disputes.

Plans are priced by track or by monthly download minutes, with a free tier for trying it. Each download comes with a non-exclusive, perpetual license to use the music in your content, while Beatoven keeps ownership of the track, and you cannot distribute it directly on streaming platforms as your own music. Compared with Suno and Udio, it is built for background scoring rather than songs with vocals. Soundraw, AIVA, and Mubert are the main alternatives.
