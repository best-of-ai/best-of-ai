Zycus is a source-to-pay suite. Companies manage supplier onboarding, sourcing events, contracts, purchase orders, and invoice processing in one platform, giving procurement teams visibility and control over spend.

AI agents assist with the work. They read contracts to extract terms, detect anomalies in pricing or invoices, suggest suppliers, and draft communications. Analytics show savings and compliance.

Zycus is sold to large enterprises on subscription. Alternatives include Coupa, SAP Ariba, Jaggaer, Ivalua, and GEP. Zycus suits procurement teams at large organizations.
