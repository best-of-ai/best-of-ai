Trinka is built for scholarly and professional documents. It reviews text for grammar, style, and consistency with academic conventions, and understands field-specific terms in areas like medicine, engineering, and law. Suggestions explain why a change is recommended.

Extra tools check for plagiarism and consistency, offer paraphrasing, and run publication readiness checks. Plugins work with Microsoft Word and browsers, and a web editor is available.

Trinka has a free tier and paid plans. Authors should follow journal policies on AI-assisted editing. Alternatives include Paperpal, Grammarly, Writefull, Wordvice AI, and QuillBot. Trinka suits researchers and students polishing manuscripts.
