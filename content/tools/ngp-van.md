Campaign staff use VAN to cut turf, build call and door lists, and record voter responses from the field. MiniVAN lets canvassers log results on a phone. NGP handles fundraising and reporting for campaigns.

Access depends on party and organization rules, and training matters because data entry quality affects every list.

NationBuilder and Action Network cover parts of the same work. NGP VAN suits campaign managers on progressive campaigns.
