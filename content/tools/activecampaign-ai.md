ActiveCampaign is built around automation. You design customer journeys that react to behavior: someone joins a list, visits a page, abandons a cart, or opens an email, and the system sends the right follow-up at the right time. Segmentation, split testing, site tracking, and a built-in CRM let small and mid-sized businesses personalize campaigns without a large marketing team. It is common in e-commerce, services, and B2B companies.

Its AI tools help build and manage those journeys. Active Intelligence can audit an account, draft campaigns and automations from a short description, flag automations that are broken or underperforming, and answer questions about results in plain language. Predictive features estimate the best time to send, how likely a contact is to buy, and which deals are most likely to close. A content assistant writes subject lines and email copy.

ActiveCampaign is paid, with plans tied to the number of contacts and features, and a free trial. Costs rise as your list grows, and the depth of automation takes time to learn. It is more capable than Mailchimp for complex journeys and cheaper than enterprise tools like Marketo or HubSpot's higher tiers. Klaviyo is the usual pick for online stores, and Brevo and Mailchimp serve simpler needs.
