Transkriptor turns speech into text. Users upload files, record in the app, or connect meeting platforms, and receive a transcript with timestamps and speaker labels that can be edited and exported. Subtitles and translations are supported.

Features include meeting bots that join calls, AI summaries, and a chat assistant to ask questions about a recording. It serves students, journalists, researchers, and businesses.

Transkriptor has a free trial and paid plans by hours. Accuracy depends on audio quality, and sensitive recordings need privacy checks. Alternatives include Otter.ai, Trint, Descript, Fireflies, and Whisper-based tools. Transkriptor suits people transcribing interviews and meetings.
