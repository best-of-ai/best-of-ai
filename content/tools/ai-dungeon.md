AI Dungeon pioneered AI storytelling games. You choose a setting, such as fantasy, mystery, or survival, or create your own, and then type what your character does. The AI responds with what happens next, with no fixed script, so you can attempt anything and the story adapts. The result is a different kind of game from traditional adventures, with open-ended possibilities and sometimes surprising turns.

Creative tools are part of the platform. Players can build custom scenarios with their own characters and rules, share them with the community, and play adventures others have made. Story features let you edit and redo the AI's output to steer the narrative, and memory settings help it keep track of characters and plot. Multiplayer modes let friends play together in one story.

AI Dungeon is free to start with options to subscribe for more advanced models, longer memory, and extra features. The AI can lose track of details, contradict itself, or drift off course, which is part of the experience but can frustrate players, and content moderation has been a recurring topic. Alternatives include NovelAI for writing, Character.AI for chat roleplay, and the narrative features in general chatbots. AI Dungeon suits players who enjoy improvised, open-ended stories.
