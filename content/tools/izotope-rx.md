RX is the tool engineers reach for when audio is flawed. Its spectral display shows sound as an image, making it possible to see and remove specific problems, such as a cough, a phone buzz, or a chair squeak, without affecting the rest. Modules cover denoising, de-clicking, de-hum, de-reverb, de-clipping, and other repair tasks, and are used by post-production teams, podcast producers, forensic analysts, and musicians.

Machine learning powers its newer tools. Dialogue Isolate separates speech from background sounds, Music Rebalance lets you adjust the levels of vocals, bass, and drums in a finished mix, and Spectral Recovery restores high frequencies lost in low-quality files. Dialogue Contour and other features help produce consistent speech. It works as a standalone editor and as plugins in digital audio workstations and video editors.

iZotope RX is sold in tiers, from Elements to Advanced, as perpetual licenses or subscriptions, and is owned by Native Instruments. The learning curve is real, and the advanced version is expensive. Alternatives include Adobe Audition, Acon Digital, Waves, Descript's Studio Sound, and Adobe Podcast for simpler cleanup. RX suits audio professionals who need precise repair tools.
