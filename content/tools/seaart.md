SeaArt gives users a rich set of image generation tools in a friendly interface. You write a prompt, choose among many models and styles, and generate images, with options such as ControlNet to guide pose or composition and LoRA models to apply particular styles or characters. A library of community-shared models, workflows, and characters lets users build on others' work, and a node-based workflow editor offers more advanced control for those who want it.

The platform has grown beyond still images. It supports image-to-image editing, upscaling, video generation, and character-based chat features, and its community shares prompts and creations. Many users are hobbyists making fan art, anime-style characters, and game concept art, and the platform works on browsers and phones.

SeaArt has a free tier with daily credits and paid memberships with more credits and faster generation. The platform has faced criticism for how it handles adult content and for the copyright status of community-shared models, so review its content rules and licensing terms. Results depend on the model chosen. Civitai, Tensor.Art, Leonardo.ai, and NightCafe are comparable, and running ComfyUI locally offers the most control. SeaArt suits creators who want community resources in a polished interface.
