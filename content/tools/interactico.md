Interactico is a tool designed to enhance Google Meet meetings by making them more interactive. It allows users to add polls, quizzes, Q&A sessions, and word clouds to their meetings. Participants can join activities via a shared link, without needing to install any add-ons. The platform offers features like real-time feedback and analytics to help users make informed decisions during and after meetings. Interactico offers different pricing plans, including a free plan and paid options with more features.
