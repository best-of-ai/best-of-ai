Morningstar is known for independent research on funds, stocks, and markets. Its star ratings for mutual funds and ETFs became a standard reference, and analysts publish research and fair value estimates on companies. Individual investors use its website and premium subscriptions to screen investments and build portfolios, while advisors and institutions use products like Morningstar Direct for deeper analysis, portfolio construction, and reporting.

AI has been added to make research easier to navigate. Mo is a conversational assistant that answers investing questions using Morningstar's research and data, summarizes reports, and explains concepts, with an emphasis on drawing from the company's own vetted content to limit errors. Other uses include automated summaries of earnings calls and analyst notes, and data tools that help build screens and analyze holdings.

Morningstar has free content on its website, a paid Investor subscription for individuals, and enterprise products for professionals sold by custom quote. Ratings and fair values are opinions, not guarantees, and past performance does not predict future results. AI answers about investments need checking and are not personalized financial advice. Alternatives include Bloomberg for professionals, Seeking Alpha and Value Line for individuals, FactSet and S&P Capital IQ for institutions, and brokerage research tools.
