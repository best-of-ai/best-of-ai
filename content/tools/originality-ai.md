Originality.ai was built for people who commission or publish written content and want to know whether it was written by AI. You paste text or scan a URL, and it returns a score estimating the likelihood the text was generated by models such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, alongside a plagiarism check against web sources. Content teams, SEO agencies, and website buyers use it to check contractors' work.

Additional tools include a fact-checking aid, a readability analysis, and a feature that shows the writing history of a Google Doc to demonstrate how it was composed. A Chrome extension and API support integration with publishing workflows, and team accounts allow reviewing many pieces. The company publishes accuracy studies, although its results are disputed by others.

Originality.ai is paid, using credits per scan or subscription. No AI detector is fully reliable: false positives can unfairly label human writing as AI, particularly for non-native English writers, and false negatives occur when text is edited. Scores should prompt conversation and not serve as proof. Alternatives include GPTZero, Copyleaks, Turnitin for education, and Winston AI. Originality.ai suits publishers who want a screening step.
