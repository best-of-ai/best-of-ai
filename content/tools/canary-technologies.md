Canary builds software for hotels. Guests can check in online before arrival, sign registration forms, verify identity, and pay deposits, which shortens lines at the front desk. After arrival, messaging lets them ask for towels, late checkout, or recommendations through text instead of calling.

An AI layer answers common questions and handles requests, routing issues to staff when needed. Upsell tools offer room upgrades and add-ons at the right moment, which brings extra revenue. Other products cover contactless payments, digital tipping, and a guest-facing digital tablet in rooms.

Canary is sold to hotels and hospitality groups by subscription. It integrates with property management systems, which affects deployment time. Alternatives include Mews, Cloudbeds, HotelKey, and Intelity. Canary suits hotels wanting to modernize guest communication.
