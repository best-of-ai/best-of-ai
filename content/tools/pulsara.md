Pulsara speeds time-critical care. A paramedic starts a patient channel from the field, adds vitals and images, and alerts the receiving hospital, so teams can prepare before arrival. Stroke, heart attack, and trauma cases benefit when clinicians coordinate early.

Within the hospital, Pulsara links departments such as the emergency room, imaging, and specialists, and keeps a timeline of communications. The data supports review of response times and quality improvement. AI features assist with transcription and information routing.

Pulsara is sold to EMS agencies and hospitals on subscription. It depends on adoption across the care network. Alternatives include Zoll and ESO for documentation, TigerConnect, Vocera, and hospital-specific tools. Pulsara suits emergency systems coordinating patient handoffs.
