Arcade makes interactive demos simple to create. A person records their screen using a Chrome extension, and Arcade turns the clicks into a step-by-step walkthrough with highlights and captions. The result can be edited, branded, and shared as a link or embedded on a website or in an email.

Teams use demos for several purposes. Marketing places them on landing pages to show a product in action, sales sends them to prospects, and customer success uses them for onboarding and help content. AI features add voiceovers, generate captions, and personalize demos by changing names and details. Analytics show who watched and where they stopped.

Arcade has a free plan and paid tiers with more features. Demos can become stale as the product changes, and they show the interface but not live behavior. Alternatives include Storylane, Navattic, Supademo, Loom for video, and Scribe for written guides. Arcade suits teams that want quick, shareable product tours.
