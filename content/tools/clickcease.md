ClickCease deals with click fraud in pay-per-click advertising. When advertisers pay for each click on Google or Facebook ads, bots, competitors, and repeated clicks can drain budgets. The tool watches traffic, flags suspicious patterns such as repeated clicks from the same device or location, and adds the offending sources to block lists automatically.

A dashboard shows blocked clicks, estimated savings, and details about where bad traffic came from. Reports can be used to request refunds from ad platforms in some cases. Extras include tracking of invalid traffic on other channels and tools for monitoring ad campaigns and landing pages.

ClickCease is paid, with plans by ad spend or clicks monitored, and a free trial. CHEQ, an ad verification and cybersecurity company, acquired it and kept the brand for smaller advertisers. Savings vary, and the ad platforms also filter some invalid clicks themselves. Alternatives include PPC Protect, Lunio, Fraud Blocker, and TrafficGuard. ClickCease suits small and mid-sized advertisers worried about wasted spend.
