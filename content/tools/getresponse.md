GetResponse bundles several marketing tools. Users collect subscribers with forms and landing pages, send newsletters, and build automated workflows that respond to behavior such as signing up, clicking, or buying. A webinar feature lets businesses host live events and follow up with attendees.

AI helps create content. An assistant drafts emails, subject lines, and landing pages, and builds campaigns from a short brief, while segmentation and analytics help improve results. E-commerce features include product recommendations and abandoned cart emails, and integrations connect with online stores and CRMs.

GetResponse has a free plan for small lists and paid tiers. Its breadth can feel crowded compared with simpler tools. Alternatives include Mailchimp, Brevo, ActiveCampaign, MailerLite, and Kit. GetResponse suits small businesses wanting email, landing pages, and webinars together.
