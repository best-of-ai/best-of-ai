Virtuous aims to help nonprofits treat donors as individuals. Its CRM unifies donor records, giving history, communications, and activity from the website and email, and uses scoring to show which supporters are likely to give again or give more. Fundraisers use the data to plan outreach and prioritize relationships, rather than sending the same message to everyone.

Additional tools expand the system. Marketing automation builds journeys and email campaigns triggered by donor behavior, online giving forms and text-to-give accept gifts, and volunteer management tracks service. Newer AI features help write appeals, summarize donor histories, and recommend next steps for each supporter, and an analytics layer shows campaign performance.

Virtuous is sold to nonprofits on subscription with custom pricing, and typically suits mid-sized organizations. Setup requires time to clean and import data. Competitors include Bloomerang, Blackbaud, Bonterra, Neon One, Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, and DonorPerfect. Virtuous suits fundraising teams that want modern automation and donor insights.
