ForeFlight is an app for planning and flying. Pilots build routes, check weather and airspace, file flight plans, and view approach plates and charts. In the air, it shows moving maps and traffic and weather from compatible receivers, which replaces paper charts.

Weather is a big part. Layers show forecasts, radar, and hazards, and tools highlight conditions most likely to affect a given route. Performance and weight and balance planning help with safety, and integrations connect to avionics and tablets. Teams in flight departments use it to share data.

ForeFlight is sold by subscription in tiers, and Garmin acquired the company. Pilots remain responsible for their own preparation, and electronic tools supplement but do not replace judgment. Alternatives include Garmin Pilot, FltPlan Go, SkyDemon in Europe, and Avplan. ForeFlight suits pilots who plan and fly with tablets.
