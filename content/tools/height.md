Height set out to make project management less manual. It was a task and project tracker with a clean interface, and its main idea was that AI should do routine upkeep: sorting incoming requests, writing and updating subtasks, assigning work, and keeping descriptions consistent. Teams used it as a lighter alternative to Jira with more automation, and the product drew fans among startups for its design and its early AI features.

The company announced that it would close the product, and gave customers months to export their data and move elsewhere. The service is no longer available to new or existing customers. Height did not have a successor or acquirer that continued the product, so anyone who used it needed to migrate their work.

Teams looking for similar tools have several options. Linear is the closest in spirit, with speed and a focus on software teams, Jira suits larger organizations, Asana and ClickUp cover broader business use, and Notion's databases work for lighter needs. The shutdown illustrates the risk of building a workflow on a young product, so exporting data regularly is a good habit. This entry remains for reference, since older articles still compare Height with other tools.
