Plus AI takes the approach that you shouldn't have to leave your slide editor to use AI. After installing the add-in in Google Slides or PowerPoint, you describe the presentation you want, and it drafts an outline and slides in the editor, which you can then adjust like any other deck. It can also build from a document, a set of notes, or a web page.

Editing features save time on existing decks. The remix tool can restyle or rework slides, and the rewrite function changes tone or length of text on a slide. It can convert content into a different layout, summarize, and create speaker notes. Because the output is native slides, the file remains fully editable and compatible with company templates and brand assets.

Plus AI has a free option with limits and paid plans for more usage and features. The generated decks need editing for accuracy and style, and the design is shaped by the template you provide. Gamma, Beautiful.ai, and Pitch are standalone AI presentation tools with their own editors, while Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint and Gemini in Slides are the built-in alternatives. Plus AI suits people who want AI help but must deliver decks in familiar formats.
