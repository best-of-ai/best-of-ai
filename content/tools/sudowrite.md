Sudowrite is designed for fiction writers. Instead of a generic chatbot, it provides tools shaped around storytelling: a Describe function that expands a moment with sensory detail, Rewrite options that change tone or style, Brainstorm tools for names, plot twists, and settings, and a feature to continue a scene in your voice. Writers can paste their own text and have the AI follow its style.

The platform has bigger story-level tools as well. Story Engine helps build a novel from a premise by generating characters, outlines, and chapters that the writer edits, and it keeps a database of story details so the AI maintains consistency. Sudowrite developed its own model focused on prose, and offers features for feedback and revision. It has appealed to authors who want a collaborator for drafting.

Sudowrite is paid, with plans based on credits and a trial. The use of AI in fiction is contentious, as some readers and publishers object, and writers should check publisher and contest rules and disclose usage where required. Alternatives include NovelAI, Claude and ChatGPT with fiction prompts, Jasper for marketing, and Scrivener for manuscript management. Sudowrite suits authors who want AI help drafting and revising fiction.
