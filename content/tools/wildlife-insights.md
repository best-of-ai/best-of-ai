Users upload images, and a model labels species and empty frames, which saves hours of manual sorting. Project teams review and correct results. Aggregated data supports population and range studies.

It is free for conservation use. Accuracy varies by species and region, so reviews are still needed.

Zooniverse and MegaDetector are other options. Wildlife Insights suits conservation biologists.
