Codecademy teaches coding by doing. Lessons present a short explanation beside an editor where learners write code and see results right away, which makes it approachable for beginners. Courses cover languages such as Python, JavaScript, and SQL, as well as web development, data analysis, and machine learning.

Career paths and skill paths structure the learning, with projects and quizzes to check progress. An AI assistant helps learners who are stuck by giving hints and explaining errors, while avoiding simply handing over answers. Business plans let companies train teams, and Skillsoft acquired Codecademy to add it to its corporate learning catalog.

Codecademy has a free tier with basic courses and paid plans for projects, career paths, and the AI tools. Browser-based exercises are less like real development than building your own projects, so many learners move to larger work afterward. Alternatives include freeCodeCamp, Udemy, Coursera, The Odin Project, and Scrimba. Codecademy suits beginners who learn well interactively.
