Asana helps teams keep track of who is doing what by when. Work is organized into projects and tasks, viewable as lists, boards, timelines, or calendars, with dependencies, owners, due dates, and custom fields. Goals connect daily tasks to company objectives, and portfolios give leaders an overview of many projects.

AI features reduce the overhead of managing work. They can generate project plans from a description, summarize updates and conversations, answer questions about project status, and spot risks to deadlines. Asana has also introduced AI teammates that can be assigned work and operate inside projects, with controls to keep humans in charge. Automations handle routing and reminders.

Asana has a free plan for small teams and paid tiers for more features and AI. Its flexibility can lead to cluttered setups without agreed conventions. Alternatives include Monday.com, ClickUp, Smartsheet, Jira, Trello, and Notion. Asana suits teams that coordinate cross-functional work.
