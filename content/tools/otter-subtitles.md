Otter.ai joins online meetings, transcribes the conversation as it happens, and produces notes people can search and share. Speakers are labeled, key points are summarized, and action items are pulled out, so attendees can focus on the discussion instead of taking notes.

Features include an assistant that can answer questions about a meeting or across past meetings, automatic sharing of notes with attendees, and integrations with calendars and collaboration tools. Mobile and web apps handle in-person conversations, lectures, and interviews.

Otter has a free plan with limits and paid tiers. Recording requires consent from participants, and some organizations restrict third-party bots in meetings. Alternatives include Fireflies, Fathom, Granola, Read AI, and built-in tools in Zoom and Teams. Otter.ai suits professionals and students who want automatic meeting notes.
