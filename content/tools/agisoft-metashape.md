Users load images from cameras or drones, align them, and generate dense geometry and textures. Archaeologists use it to document sites and artifacts, and surveyors use it for mapping. It can classify points and measure volumes.

Standard and professional editions differ in georeferencing and processing features. Good results depend on photo overlap and lighting.

Pix4D and RealityCapture are alternatives. Metashape suits archaeologists, surveyors, and conservators.
