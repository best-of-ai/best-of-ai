Studio Sound targets the common problem of recording in imperfect spaces. Apply it to a clip in Descript, and the AI removes noise, reduces echo and reverb, and evens out the voice, making recordings made on a laptop or phone sound much more professional. It works on a whole project or a selected portion and has an intensity setting, so you can balance cleanup against natural sound.

It is built into Descript's editing workflow, so you can clean audio while editing the transcript, and it works on both audio and video projects. Podcasters, interviewers, educators, and video creators use it to rescue recordings from noisy environments. It can be combined with other features in Descript, such as filler word removal and eye contact correction for video.

Studio Sound is available within Descript's plans, with usage tied to the plan's media hours. It works best on speech and can leave voices sounding slightly processed when pushed hard, and it can't fully repair clipped or overlapping audio. Alternatives include Adobe Podcast Enhance Speech, Auphonic, Krisp, Cleanvoice, and NVIDIA Broadcast for live use. Studio Sound suits people who already use Descript and want a quick fix.
