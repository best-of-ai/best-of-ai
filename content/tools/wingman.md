Wingman positions itself as a coach for dating apps. A user shares their profile or chat, and the AI suggests better photos, rewrites the bio, and proposes replies to messages. The aim is to help people present themselves well and keep conversations going.

AI-written messages raise authenticity questions, since the person on the other end expects to talk to you. Many users prefer feedback and ideas to full ghostwriting. Sharing screenshots of chats involves other people's messages, so consider privacy.

Wingman has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Rizz, Hinge's own prompt tools, Photofeeler, and general assistants such as ChatGPT. Wingman suits people wanting dating profile feedback.
