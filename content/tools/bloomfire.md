Bloomfire is a knowledge management platform. Employees add articles, files, videos, and Q&A to a central library, tag them, and find them later with search. The platform is meant to hold what a company knows so that people do not keep asking colleagues the same questions.

AI features make retrieval easier. Users ask questions in plain language and get answers drawn from the company's own content with links to sources, and the system can suggest related material and flag content that needs updating. Analytics show what people search for and where gaps exist.

Bloomfire is sold to mid-sized and large organizations, often for sales, support, and customer success teams. A knowledge base is only useful if content stays current, which takes ownership. Alternatives include Guru, Confluence, Notion, Glean, and Slab. Bloomfire suits teams that need a curated, searchable knowledge hub.
