Chatbase started as a simple way to create a chatbot from your own data. You upload files, point it to a website, or connect sources such as Notion, and it builds an agent that answers questions using that information. You embed the chat widget on your site with a snippet of code and you have support available around the clock. The simplicity made it popular with small businesses and startups that wanted a support bot without engineering work.

The product has grown into a broader agent platform. Agents can follow procedures, call external services through actions, and take steps such as booking, looking up orders, or creating tickets. They can run on websites and in messaging channels like WhatsApp, Messenger, Slack, and email, and a helpdesk view lets human agents take over conversations. Analytics show common questions and gaps in your content, and integrations connect to CRMs and support tools.

Chatbase offers a free trial and subscription plans based on message credits and features, with enterprise options. Answers depend on the quality of the source content, and agents can still say wrong things, so testing and guardrails are important. Compared with Intercom's Fin or Zendesk AI, Chatbase is lighter and cheaper but offers fewer enterprise features. Botpress, CustomGPT, and Voiceflow are other builders.
