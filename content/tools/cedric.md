Cedric acts as a stand-in for meetings. A user gives it slides, notes, and background documents, and it attends a call, presents, and responds to questions using that material. When it does not know something, it can note the question for follow-up.

The idea appeals to people with a heavy meeting load, such as founders or sales teams running repeated demos. It raises etiquette and trust questions: others in the meeting should know they are talking to an AI agent.

Cedric is a new product, so verify capabilities in a trial. Alternatives include Fireflies and Otter for notes, Tavus for video agents, Lindy, and live presenters. Cedric suits teams experimenting with delegating meetings to agents.
