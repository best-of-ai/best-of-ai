Dimensions connects different kinds of research outputs. Instead of searching only for papers, users can see how a grant led to publications, which patents cite a study, and whether a trial or policy document refers to it. The linked structure helps trace the path from funding to impact.

Institutions, funders, and researchers use it for several purposes. Universities benchmark their output, funders track results of the grants they award, and companies look for experts and emerging fields. AI features summarize results, help with literature searches, and surface connections, and APIs let organizations pull data into their own systems.

Dimensions has a free version with limited access and paid subscriptions for full analytics. Coverage varies by field and region. Alternatives include Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar, OpenAlex, which is free and open, and Semantic Scholar. Dimensions suits research administrators and analysts studying scholarly output.
