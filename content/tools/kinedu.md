Kinedu helps parents support their child's early development. After entering a baby's age, the app creates a plan with short, playful activities for each day, such as tummy time ideas, sensory games, and language exercises, with videos that show how to do them. Activities are grouped by areas like physical, cognitive, social-emotional, and language development.

The app tracks milestones and shows a baby's progress against typical ranges, explaining what is normal and when to speak to a pediatrician. Articles and guidance from experts cover sleep, feeding, and parenting questions, and parents can keep a journal of moments. Some features use personalization to adjust suggestions to a child's progress.

Kinedu has a free version with limited content and a subscription for the full library. Milestone charts are general guides, and each child develops at their own pace, so concerns should be discussed with a healthcare provider. Alternatives include Wonder Weeks for developmental leaps, Huckleberry for sleep, The Bump and BabyCenter for pregnancy and baby information, and Montessori-inspired activity apps. Kinedu suits new parents who want structured play ideas.
