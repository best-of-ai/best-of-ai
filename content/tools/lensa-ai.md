Lensa is a mobile photo editor best known for a single viral feature. Magic Avatars let users upload a set of selfies and receive a collection of stylized portraits in the style of fantasy art, anime, or illustration. The effect spread quickly on social media, driving downloads and making many people aware of AI image generation. The avatars are created with a model fine-tuned on each user's photos.

The core of the app is a selfie and portrait editor. It offers retouching for skin and eyes, background replacement, blur and sharpening, and a range of filters and effects. Subscribers get access to the full tool set, and Magic Avatars can be purchased as packs. Prisma Labs, the developer, also makes other photo and AI apps.

Lensa is free to download with in-app purchases and subscriptions. The avatar feature raised concerns: artists questioned how image models were trained, and some users, especially women, found the output over-sexualized or altered their features in unwanted ways. Privacy is another issue, since the app handles personal photos, so read the terms about how images are stored and used. Alternatives include Photoroom, Facetune, Remini, and the avatar and portrait features in tools like Midjourney.
