Ironclad serves legal and business teams that handle many contracts. Requests start from templates through a workflow, drafts are negotiated with counterparties inside the platform, and approvals and e-signatures finish the process. Executed contracts become structured data in a repository.

AI features are central. They read incoming contracts, flag deviations from company positions, suggest edits, and extract key terms such as renewal dates and liability caps. A repository search lets teams answer questions about their agreements, and reports show where contracts get stuck.

Ironclad is sold to mid-sized and large companies on custom contracts. Implementation requires mapping workflows. Lawyers still review AI suggestions. Alternatives include DocuSign CLM, Icertis, Evisort (Workday), LinkSquares, and Ivo. Ironclad suits legal teams that want contract workflows and intelligence together.
