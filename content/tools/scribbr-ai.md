Scribbr started as a proofreading and thesis editing service and built free tools for students. The citation generator formats references in common styles, the paraphraser rewrites text, and the grammar checker catches errors. A plagiarism checker compares text against sources, and an AI detector estimates machine-written text.

Students use these tools while writing essays and theses. Paid services include human editing by professional editors, which is a differentiator from purely automated tools. Guides explain academic writing topics.

Scribbr has free tools and paid services. Automated citations should be checked, since generators can mis-format sources, and AI detectors are imperfect. Alternatives include Grammarly, QuillBot, Zotero, Mendeley, and Turnitin. Scribbr suits students writing academic papers.
