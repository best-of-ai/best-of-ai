Catchlight is a lead scoring tool for financial advisory firms. It enriches a prospect's profile with data such as estimated income, investable assets, age range, and interests, then applies a model trained on past conversions to produce a score showing how likely they are to become a client.

Advisors use it to prioritize their time. Instead of calling every inquiry, they can focus on those with the best fit, and see context to start a conversation. It integrates with CRMs used by advisors, such as Wealthbox, and appears in platforms that serve wealth management firms.

Using estimated personal financial data raises privacy and fairness concerns, and advisors should follow regulations on data use and outreach. Estimates may be inaccurate, so they are a guide to prioritize and not a verdict. Alternatives include Salesforce Einstein, HubSpot lead scoring, ZoomInfo, and Zoe Financial for lead sourcing. Catchlight suits advisory firms that manage inbound leads.
