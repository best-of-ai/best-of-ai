LanguageTool checks writing for spelling, grammar, punctuation, and style, and is notable for its broad language support, including English, German, French, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese, and many more. It runs as browser extensions, add-ins for Microsoft Word and Google Docs, a desktop app, and an API, so it can check text wherever you write. Many people use it for writing in a second language or switching between languages.

The core engine is open source and can be self-hosted, which appeals to organizations with privacy requirements and to developers who want to integrate a checker. The premium tier adds more advanced style suggestions, tone and clarity feedback, and AI-powered rephrasing, along with higher character limits. Teams can set custom dictionaries and style rules.

LanguageTool has a free version with basic checks and a premium subscription. Its suggestions are less polished than Grammarly's for English style, though the multilingual coverage is a strength. As with any checker, suggestions can be wrong, especially for creative or technical writing. Alternatives include Grammarly, ProWritingAid, Reverso, DeepL Write, and Hemingway Editor. LanguageTool suits multilingual writers and privacy-conscious organizations.
