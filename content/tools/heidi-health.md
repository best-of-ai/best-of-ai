Heidi is an AI scribe for clinicians. During a visit, with the patient's consent, it records and transcribes the conversation, then generates a note in the structure the clinician prefers, such as SOAP or a specialty template. The clinician reviews and edits before saving it to the record.

Beyond notes, it can write letters, patient instructions, and other documents from the same consultation, and supports multiple languages. Heidi began in Australia and has grown internationally, with a free tier that made it popular with individual practitioners alongside plans for clinics and health systems.

Heidi has free and paid plans. Notes can contain errors, so every output needs clinician review, and privacy regulations apply to recordings. Alternatives include Abridge, Freed, Nabla, Suki, and Microsoft Dragon Copilot. Heidi Health suits clinicians who want a flexible, template-driven scribe.
