Tomorrow.io focuses on weather as a business input. Companies in aviation, logistics, energy, retail, and sports use its platform to get forecasts and alerts tailored to their locations and operations, such as a warning when wind will halt crane operations or when snow will affect deliveries. The goal is to turn weather into decisions, not just data.

It builds its own data sources. The company has launched satellites carrying radar to observe precipitation, which supplements ground sensors and models and helps in regions with limited coverage. AI blends these inputs to make short-range forecasts, and an assistant helps users create alerts and ask questions about conditions. APIs deliver the data into other applications.

Tomorrow.io offers a free developer tier and paid plans for businesses, with enterprise contracts for custom solutions. Forecast accuracy varies by location and lead time, and no model is perfect. Alternatives include AccuWeather, The Weather Company, DTN, Meteomatics, and public data from national weather services. Tomorrow.io suits organizations whose operations are sensitive to weather.
