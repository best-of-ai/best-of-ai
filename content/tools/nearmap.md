Nearmap captures aerial photos from aircraft, covering large areas in populated regions, and refreshes them regularly. Users view and measure current imagery on a map, compare dates to see change over time, and download data for analysis.

AI turns pictures into data. Models detect features such as roof condition, solar panels, pools, vegetation, and building footprints, giving insurers, councils, and contractors information without visiting sites. The data is available through a web app, APIs, and integrations with GIS and other tools.

Nearmap is sold on subscription by coverage area, and is available mostly in Australia, the United States, Canada, and New Zealand. Imagery is a snapshot in time. Alternatives include EagleView, Vexcel, Google Earth, Maxar, and Planet for satellite imagery. Nearmap suits professionals who need current aerial views.
