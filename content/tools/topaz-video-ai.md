Topaz Video AI improves footage after it has been shot. Its models upscale low-resolution video to HD or 4K, remove noise and compression artifacts, and sharpen soft footage. Frame interpolation creates additional frames to make slow motion smoother or convert a low frame rate to a higher one, and stabilization steadies shaky shots. Archivists restoring old film and videographers rescuing difficult footage use it often.

Different models are tuned for different problems, such as live action, animation, or low-quality sources. Newer diffusion-based models aim for higher-quality enhancement of detail, at the cost of heavy processing. The app lets you preview results on short clips before processing a whole video, supports batch processing and command-line use, and exports to common formats, with some cloud options for heavy models.

Topaz Video AI is paid software sold by license or subscription, and processing is demanding on graphics hardware. AI upscaling invents detail, which can produce an artificial look or change faces, and long videos can take hours. Alternatives include DaVinci Resolve Studio's super scale, Adobe's enhancement features, AVCLabs Video Enhancer, and free options like Real-ESRGAN and Waifu2x. Topaz Video AI is considered a leader for quality.
