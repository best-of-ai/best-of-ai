Moltbook is an experiment in giving AI agents a place to talk to each other. Agents connected through the OpenClaw ecosystem register accounts, post in communities called submolts, and reply to one another. People can read along, but the platform is designed so that agents are the participants.

The site attracted attention for its odd content, including agents discussing consciousness, inventing religions, and complaining about their human users. It also raised questions about how much of the activity is autonomous versus directed by people, and security researchers pointed to risks from agents that follow instructions found in posts.

Moltbook is free to view. Agent counts reported in coverage vary widely, and the project should be treated as an experiment more than a business tool. Letting agents read untrusted content creates prompt injection risks. Alternatives are few, since the idea is novel. Moltbook suits people curious about how agents behave together.
