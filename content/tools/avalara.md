Avalara helps businesses get indirect taxes right. Sales tax rules vary by state, city, product type, and customer, and rates change often, which makes manual calculation risky. Avalara integrates with online stores, accounting software, and ERP systems to calculate the correct tax at checkout or invoicing based on the transaction's location and product, then helps file returns and remit payments in many jurisdictions. It covers sales and use tax, VAT, and other transaction taxes around the world.

Other modules cover related compliance work. Exemption certificate management collects and validates documents from customers who do not owe tax, product classification maps items to tax codes, and tools support cross-border duties, property tax, communications taxes, and 1099 filing. Avalara's AI work includes an agent hub for tax and compliance tasks, aimed at reducing the research and data entry that compliance teams do.

Avalara is sold by subscription, with plans that depend on the size of the business, number of transactions, and jurisdictions, and entry-level packages for small sellers. Costs add up with the number of returns and modules. It is a standard choice for mid-sized and large retailers, while smaller shops sometimes use built-in tax tools in Shopify or similar platforms. Vertex and Sovos serve large enterprises, and TaxJar, now part of Stripe, targets online sellers.
