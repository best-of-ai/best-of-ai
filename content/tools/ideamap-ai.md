Ideamap lets users start with a topic and watch a map grow. The AI suggests branches, groups related ideas, and links concepts, so brainstorming moves beyond a first list. Users can edit nodes by hand, add notes, and rearrange the canvas.

Typical uses include planning projects, outlining articles, studying a subject, and mapping product ideas. The tool aims to reduce the friction of starting from a blank canvas and help with the organizing step that follows brainstorming.

Ideamap has a free plan and paid tiers. AI suggestions can be generic, so the value comes from pairing them with your own thinking. Alternatives include Miro, Whimsical, Xmind, MindMeister, and Obsidian canvas. Ideamap suits people who think visually.
