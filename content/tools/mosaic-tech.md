Mosaic is aimed at finance teams that want a modern view of the business. It pulls data from accounting systems, sales tools, and HR platforms into a single model, so metrics like revenue, headcount, and cash are current and consistent. Teams build reports and board decks from live data.

Forecasting and scenario tools let teams test assumptions, such as hiring plans or pricing changes, and see the impact. AI allows users to ask questions in plain language and get analysis, and automates routine commentary and variance explanations.

Mosaic is sold by subscription to growth-stage and mid-sized companies. Setup depends on clean source data. Alternatives include Pigment, Causal (Lucanet), Runway, Anaplan, and Cube. Mosaic suits finance teams modernizing planning and reporting.
