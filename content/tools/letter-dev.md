Letter works on your site's repository. On your own site, press ⌘L to change text, rearrange or move elements, or talk through an edit, and Letter ships it as a pull request. Letterpress-1, its agent, also finds problems on the site and opens "Fix" pull requests with live previews.

It suggests and writes SEO, GEO and ideal-customer-profile landing pages, identifies companies that visit the site, and writes goals, posts, llms.txt and AGENTS.md into the repo. API, CLI, MCP and Slack access are included.

Pricing is token based and billed monthly, in tiers that rise with the token allowance up to custom enterprise terms. New accounts get free tokens, and signup asks for a card but nothing is charged until you pick a plan. Marketing, growth and engineering teams that manage a site in git are the audience.
