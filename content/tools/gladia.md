Gladia provides speech-to-text as a service for developers. Companies send audio from calls, meetings, or videos and receive transcripts with timestamps, speaker labels, and optional translation. It handles dozens of languages and can detect which one is being spoken.

Real-time transcription suits live captioning and voice agents, while batch processing handles recordings at scale. Extra features include summaries, sentiment, and named entity detection. The API is aimed at products built on audio, such as meeting assistants, call analytics, and media tools.

Gladia is priced by usage with a free allowance. Accuracy varies with audio quality and accents. Alternatives include Deepgram, AssemblyAI, OpenAI Whisper, ElevenLabs Scribe, and Speechmatics. Gladia suits developers adding transcription to products.
