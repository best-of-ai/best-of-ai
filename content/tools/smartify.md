Smartify is an app for cultural venues. A visitor opens it in a partner museum, scans an artwork with the camera, and the app identifies it and shows background, artist information, and audio commentary. Collections can be saved for later.

Museums use it to enrich visits without installing hardware, and to learn what visitors engage with. The platform includes tools for creating tours and for accessibility. Some partners are major institutions.

Smartify is free for visitors, and museums pay for the service. It depends on a museum having joined. Alternatives include Bloomberg Connects, Google Arts & Culture, Cuseum, and Guide by Cell. Smartify suits art lovers and museums offering digital guides.
