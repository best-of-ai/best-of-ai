Warmbox addresses a deliverability problem. New or little-used email accounts sending outreach often land in spam. Warmbox connects an account to a network of inboxes that exchange emails, open and reply to them, and gradually build a positive sending history.

It is used before and during cold email campaigns, with dashboards that show inbox placement and health. Settings control volume and ramp-up.

Warmbox is paid, with plans by mailboxes. Warm-up does not fix poor targeting or bad content, and spam laws still apply. Alternatives include Instantly, Smartlead, Lemwarm, Mailreach, and Warmup Inbox. Warmbox suits teams sending cold email.
