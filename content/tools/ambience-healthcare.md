Ambience builds AI for the paperwork around medical care. With patient consent, its system captures the conversation between clinician and patient, then generates a structured note in the clinician's preferred format. The clinician reviews and signs it, which cuts the time spent charting after hours.

Its distinct focus is coding and compliance. From the same conversation and the chart, the tool suggests diagnosis and procedure codes with supporting evidence, which can reduce missed codes and billing errors. Because of that, it targets health systems and large practices rather than single clinicians, and it works with different specialties.

Ambience sells to enterprises on contract and integrates tightly with major record systems. Like every ambient scribe, it can produce errors, so clinicians must check the output and patients should be told that recording is happening. Competitors include Abridge, Microsoft Dragon Copilot, Suki, Nabla, and Heidi Health. Ambience suits large organizations that want documentation and coding help in one platform.
