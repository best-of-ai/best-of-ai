Tettra helps teams keep answers in one place. Teams write pages for policies, processes, and FAQs, organize them in categories, and assign owners who verify content periodically so it stays accurate. Integrations with Slack make answers available where people ask questions.

An AI assistant searches the wiki and connected sources to respond to questions in chat. Requests that cannot be answered become tasks for subject experts, which grows the knowledge base over time.

Tettra has a free tier and paid plans per user. It is simpler than larger knowledge tools. Alternatives include Guru, Slab, Notion, Confluence, and Bloomfire. Tettra suits small and mid-sized teams building an internal wiki.
