TrueSource.AI is an AI tool for e-commerce product data management. It helps businesses clean, standardise and enrich their product catalogues with AI and automation.

You can centralise product information, automate enrichment, and publish it across channels.

It offers capabilities like data quality checks (duplicates, missing fields, inconsistent attributes) and AI-powered enrichment (auto-generate copy, fill gaps, normalise categories).

It’s positioned for brands with all sizes of catalogues and covers various roles (e-commerce manager, merchandiser, ops/catalog developer).
