Meta Quest headsets made virtual reality mainstream by removing the need for cables and a powerful computer. The headsets have built-in processors, controllers, and cameras that track your hands and surroundings, and run apps from the Quest store. The latest models support mixed reality, blending virtual objects with the live view of the room, which opens up games and tools that interact with your space.

The ecosystem includes a wide range of experiences. Popular uses include games, fitness apps, social worlds, watching videos on virtual screens, and creating or collaborating in 3D. Meta is adding AI features, including Meta AI as a voice assistant and tools that help creators build worlds, and headsets can connect to a PC for more demanding games. Models vary in price and capability, and the software is called Horizon OS.

Quest headsets are sold as hardware with no subscription required, though some apps charge, and accounts are tied to Meta. Concerns include privacy, since headsets collect sensor data, and comfort during long sessions. Alternatives include Apple Vision Pro, which is far more expensive, Sony PlayStation VR2 for console players, Pico headsets, and Valve's Index for PC enthusiasts. Meta Quest suits people who want an affordable way into VR.
