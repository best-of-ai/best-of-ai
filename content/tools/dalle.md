DALL-E 3 was a text-to-image model known for following detailed prompts closely. Its integration with ChatGPT let users describe an image in conversation and have the assistant refine the prompt, which made image generation accessible to people who had never used a dedicated art tool. Developers could also call it through OpenAI's API.

It was part of a line that began with the original DALL-E and DALL-E 2, which helped popularize text-to-image generation. Over time, OpenAI moved to newer image models built natively into its multimodal systems, which handle text in images and edits better. DALL-E branding faded as a result.

OpenAI ended API access to DALL-E 2 and DALL-E 3, and developers were told to switch to its newer GPT image models. Image generation remains available in ChatGPT under those models. Alternatives include GPT image models, Midjourney, Google's Imagen and Nano Banana, Adobe Firefly, and Stable Diffusion. This entry is kept for reference.
