Roboflow helps teams build computer vision applications without starting from scratch. You upload images or video, label objects with AI-assisted annotation tools, and Roboflow handles formatting, augmentation, and splitting data into training sets. It then trains or fine-tunes models such as YOLO and other popular architectures, and shows performance metrics so you can see where the model fails and what data to add.

Deployment is a main focus. Models can run through a hosted API, on your own servers, or at the edge on devices such as NVIDIA Jetson or a mobile phone. Workflows let you chain models with logic and actions, for example detecting a defect, counting items, and sending an alert to a system. Roboflow Universe is a public collection of datasets and pretrained models shared by the community, which gives a fast start for common tasks.

Typical uses include quality inspection in manufacturing, counting inventory, monitoring safety equipment, sports analytics, and agriculture. There is a free tier for public projects, and paid plans add private projects, more training capacity, and team features, with enterprise options for larger deployments. The platform covers the whole path from data to production, but accuracy still depends on the quality and variety of your images. Labelbox, V7, and Encord compete on data labeling, and cloud services such as AWS Rekognition cover simpler needs.
