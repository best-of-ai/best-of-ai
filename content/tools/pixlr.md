Pixlr offers editing in a browser without installing software. Pixlr E is a more advanced editor with layers, masks, and adjustment tools that resemble Photoshop, while Pixlr X is a simpler editor for quick fixes, collages, and templates. It opens common image formats, including PSD files to an extent, and works on desktop and mobile browsers.

AI features handle the hard parts. Background removal makes cutouts in a click, an object eraser removes distractions, generative fill and expand add or extend content from a prompt, and automatic enhancement and retouching improve photos. An AI image generator and tools for creating designs from templates broaden what it can do. Pixlr is part of the Inmagine group, which also offers stock content.

Pixlr is free with ads and limited AI use, and premium plans remove ads and raise AI limits and downloads. It is a good no-install choice for quick work and for people on low-powered computers, but it lacks the depth and file handling of Photoshop or GIMP. Photopea is a free browser editor that closely resembles Photoshop, Canva handles design, Fotor and BeFunky are comparable simple editors, and Adobe Express is the Adobe equivalent.
