Elai.io is aimed at people who need a presenter video without filming. A user picks from a library of avatars, enters a script or imports a document, selects a voice and language, and the platform produces a video of the avatar speaking. Backgrounds, text, and media can be added in a slide-like editor.

Typical uses are training videos, product explainers, and marketing content, and the ability to translate into many languages helps localize quickly. Custom avatars can be created from a person's footage. Features such as converting articles or links into videos speed up content creation.

Elai.io has a limited free tier and paid plans by video minutes. Avatars can feel stiff compared with real presenters, and consent matters when creating a likeness of a real person. Alternatives include Synthesia, HeyGen, D-ID, Colossyan, and Hour One. Elai.io suits teams producing training and explainer videos at scale.
