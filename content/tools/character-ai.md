Character.AI lets you chat with an enormous catalog of AI personalities. Some are based on fictional characters or historical figures, many are invented by users, and you can build your own by writing a description of who the character is and how they talk. The results feel more like acting than answering questions: the characters stay in persona, remember what you have said in the conversation, and respond in a distinctive voice. Users spend long sessions on it, using it for role-play, story writing, language practice, and casual company.

The product has grown beyond text. It includes voice calls with characters, group chats where several characters talk with you, and features for creating images or stories around a persona. Each character can be public or private, and the community rates and shares them. The underlying models are the company's own, tuned for engaging conversation rather than for factual accuracy, so it is a poor choice for research or work tasks.

Character.AI has a free tier and a paid subscription that adds faster responses and extra features. It has also faced serious scrutiny over how young users interact with companion chatbots, including lawsuits and regulatory attention, and the company has changed its rules and added restrictions for minors. Characters sometimes say wrong or inappropriate things, and conversations are not a substitute for professional advice. Alternatives include Replika, Nomi, and Talkie.
