Help Scout is for teams that want customer support without a heavy ticketing feel. Messages arrive in shared inboxes, where agents can assign conversations, leave private notes, and collaborate, while customers get replies that look like ordinary emails. That personal feel is a selling point for businesses that value relationships.

The product includes a knowledge base called Docs, a chat and messaging widget called Beacon, and reports on response times and satisfaction. Workflows automate routing and tagging. AI features draft replies from past conversations and help articles, summarize long threads, and adjust tone, and an AI answers questions for customers using the knowledge base.

Help Scout has paid plans per user with add-ons, and trials rather than a long-term free tier. It is simpler than Zendesk or Salesforce Service Cloud and has fewer enterprise features. Alternatives include Zendesk, Intercom, Front, Freshdesk, and Gorgias for e-commerce. Help Scout suits small and mid-sized teams that value a human, email-like experience.
