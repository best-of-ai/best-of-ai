DeepRails is an AI reliability and guardrails platform that helps teams ship trustworthy, production-grade AI systems. As large language models are increasingly embedded into real-world products, hallucinations and incorrect outputs have become one of the biggest blockers to adoption.

DeepRails is the only guardrails solution designed to both hyper-accurately identify hallucinations and substantively fix them, rather than merely flagging issues. The platform evaluates AI outputs for factual correctness, grounding, and reasoning consistency, enabling teams to distinguish true errors from acceptable model variance with high precision.

Beyond detection, DeepRails provides automated remediation workflows, custom evaluation metrics aligned with business goals, and human-in-the-loop feedback loops that continuously improve model behavior over time.
