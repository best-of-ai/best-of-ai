Octane AI lets stores add an interactive quiz to their site. Shoppers answer questions about preferences, and the quiz recommends products and captures their answers and contact details. Because customers volunteer the information, it is called zero-party data, and it supports personalized email and text marketing.

The platform integrates with Shopify and marketing tools such as Klaviyo, so quiz results can feed segments and flows. Brands use it for skincare, supplements, food, and other categories where choosing among many products is hard. Analytics show how quizzes affect conversion.

Octane AI is paid, with pricing tied to store size, and is intended for Shopify merchants. Quizzes work best when the questions are well designed. Alternatives include Typeform, Jebbit, Shop Quiz Builder, Rebuy, and Klaviyo's tools. Octane AI suits e-commerce brands that want personalization.
