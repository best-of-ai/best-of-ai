Dolby.io brings Dolby's audio expertise to developers as web services. A developer sends an audio or video file and receives a processed version: cleaner speech, reduced background noise, balanced loudness, or improved dialogue. These APIs fit podcast tools, video platforms, and conferencing products.

Other parts of the platform focus on real-time streaming. Millicast delivers low-latency video to large audiences, which suits live events, interactive broadcasts, and remote production. Analysis and transcoding APIs help prepare content for different devices.

Dolby.io uses pay as you go pricing with a free allowance. Developers should test the quality on their own content, and some features overlap with newer AI tools from other vendors. Alternatives include Adobe Podcast Enhance, Auphonic, Krisp, AssemblyAI for transcription, and Mux for video infrastructure. Dolby.io suits developers adding audio quality to their products.
