Two reviewers screen independently, and conflicts are resolved in the tool. It supports risk-of-bias assessment and data extraction forms. It records PRISMA-style counts as the review proceeds.

It is subscription-based, and many universities hold licenses.

Rayyan is an alternative. Covidence suits clinical researchers running systematic reviews.
