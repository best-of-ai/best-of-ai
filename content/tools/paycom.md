Paycom is an HR and payroll platform built on one database for all functions. Employers manage hiring, onboarding, time and attendance, payroll, benefits, performance, and learning in a unified system, so data entered once appears everywhere. This design is meant to reduce errors from syncing separate tools.

Its best-known idea is Beti, which stands for Better Employee Transaction Interface. Employees review and approve their own timecards, expenses, and paid time off before payroll is run, which pushes corrections to the person who knows the data best and reduces payroll mistakes. Paycom has also added AI assistants for HR questions and tasks.

Paycom is sold to mid-sized and large employers by quote, with no public pricing, and typically includes implementation support. Competitors include ADP, Paylocity, Paycor, Ceridian Dayforce, and UKG. Paycom suits employers who want a single payroll and HR system with employee self-service.
