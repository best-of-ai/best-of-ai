Genmo works on video generation with an open approach. Mochi 1 is a large video model released with open weights, so researchers and developers can study it, run it on their own hardware, and fine-tune it. It was noted for strong motion and for following prompts well among open models at release.

The company also offers a hosted product where users generate clips from text prompts without managing hardware. Creators use it for experimentation, short clips, and concept work. Running the open model locally takes a powerful GPU and some technical skill.

Genmo has a free tier and paid plans for the hosted service. Open video models trail the best closed systems in some respects, and clips are short. Alternatives include Runway, Kling, Luma, Pika, and open models such as Wan and HunyuanVideo. Genmo suits researchers and creators interested in open video generation.
