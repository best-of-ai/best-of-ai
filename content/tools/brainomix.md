Brainomix is a UK imaging AI company. Its stroke products assess non-contrast CT and angiography scans and give clinicians scores and maps that support treatment decisions.

It also offers lung software that analyzes chest CT for fibrosis, helping track disease over time.

Brainomix is sold to hospitals and health systems. Regulatory status varies by region, so check what is cleared where you work. Alternatives include RapidAI, Viz.ai, and Aidoc. Brainomix suits stroke services and respiratory clinics.
