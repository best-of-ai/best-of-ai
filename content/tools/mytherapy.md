MyTherapy is a free medication reminder app. Users enter their drugs and schedule, receive alerts at the right times, and confirm each dose. The app also tracks health values such as blood pressure and weight, and records symptoms and mood, which builds a picture of how treatment is going.

Reports summarize adherence and measurements, giving doctors useful data at appointments. Family members can be set up to follow along, which helps those who care for relatives. The app supports many languages.

MyTherapy is free for users. It is a tracker and does not replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist, and users should check interactions with a professional. Alternatives include Medisafe, Round Health, CareClinic, and phone-native medication tools. MyTherapy suits people managing regular medications.
