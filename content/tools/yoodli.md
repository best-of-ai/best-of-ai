Yoodli is an AI speech coach. A user records themselves or practices with an AI role-play partner, and the tool analyzes delivery: filler words, speaking rate, pauses, and word choice, and gives a report with suggestions and trends over time.

Role-play scenarios cover interviews, sales calls, and presentations, where an AI plays the other party and responds. Organizations use Yoodli for sales and leadership training, with private practice that removes the embarrassment of rehearsing in front of others.

Yoodli has a free tier and paid plans for individuals and enterprises. Feedback focuses on delivery rather than substance. Alternatives include Poised, Orai, Speeko, Interview Warmup from Google, and human coaches. Yoodli suits people practicing speaking.
