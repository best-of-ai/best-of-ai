Soundful helps creators find music without licensing hassles. A user picks a genre, mood, and tempo, and the platform generates a track that can be customized and downloaded. The pitch is that each track is unique and cleared for use in videos, streams, and podcasts.

It serves YouTubers, streamers, marketers, and podcasters who need background music at scale. Different plans set what usage rights apply, including for monetized content and for commercial work.

Soundful has a free tier with limits and paid plans. Generated music is generic compared with composed pieces, and licensing terms should be read, especially for broadcast use. Alternatives include Suno, Udio, Epidemic Sound, Artlist, and Mubert. Soundful suits creators needing background tracks.
