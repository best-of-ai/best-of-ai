LinkActions is a fully-automated internal linking tool for your website. 

It uncovers the internal links that people are missing out on so that they can rank higher on Google and boost their organic traffic. Unlike other tools that only work for WordPress, LinkActions works with any web platform. 

This means users using Webflow, Shopify, Ghost, Squarespace, Wix, Joomla, etc can now organize and automate internal linking for their entire website.
