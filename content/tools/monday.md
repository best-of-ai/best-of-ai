Monday.com organizes work in colorful boards that resemble spreadsheets. Teams track projects, sales pipelines, marketing campaigns, and operations with columns for status, owners, dates, and more, and view the same data as a timeline, calendar, or chart. No-code automations handle routine updates and notifications.

The platform has expanded into products for sales CRM, service desks, and software development, built on the same foundation. AI assistants create boards from a description, summarize items and updates, write content, and extract information, and agents can take on tasks within workflows.

Monday.com has a limited free plan and paid tiers by seats and features, with a minimum seat count on some plans. Flexibility can lead to inconsistent setups without governance. Alternatives include Asana, ClickUp, Smartsheet, Notion, and Jira. Monday.com suits teams that want a configurable, visual tracker.
