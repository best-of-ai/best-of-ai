Rogo focuses on the work of junior bankers and analysts, who spend hours digging through filings, transcripts, and data. A user asks a question in plain language, such as how a company's margins compare with peers, and Rogo retrieves and analyzes the sources, citing where numbers came from.

It connects to financial data providers and a firm's internal documents, produces outputs like comps and summaries, and fits into the workflow with tools such as Excel and PowerPoint. Security and compliance features suit regulated firms.

Rogo is sold to banks, private equity firms, and asset managers on enterprise contracts. Financial outputs must be verified before use in client work. Alternatives include AlphaSense, Hebbia, Daloopa, FactSet, and Bloomberg. Rogo suits finance professionals doing company research.
