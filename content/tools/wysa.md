Wysa is a conversational app for stress, low mood, and anxiety. Users chat with a friendly penguin character that asks about how they feel and offers exercises, such as reframing negative thoughts, breathing techniques, sleep tools, and guided meditations. The responses draw on structured therapeutic content written and reviewed by clinicians instead of unconstrained text generation, which is meant to make it safer and more predictable than a general chatbot.

The product extends beyond self-help. Paid tiers add access to human coaches through messaging, and Wysa works with employers, health systems, and insurers who provide it to their members, in some places combined with therapy services. It publishes research on outcomes, and a clinical version has been developed for supporting people in treatment. The system also detects signs of crisis and points users to emergency resources.

Wysa has a free version with core chat and tools and paid options for coaching and premium content. It is not a replacement for professional treatment, and people with severe symptoms or thoughts of self-harm should contact a clinician or a crisis line. Competitors and alternatives include Youper, Woebot, which has retired its consumer app, Headspace, Calm, and teletherapy services like BetterHelp and Talkspace. Wysa suits people who want accessible, low-cost, private support.
