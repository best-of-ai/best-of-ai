TLDR AI is a quick daily read for people who work with or follow AI. Each issue lists a handful of top stories, such as model releases, research papers, tools, and industry news, with a two- or three-sentence summary and a link to the full source. The format is meant to be read in a few minutes, so subscribers can stay current without sifting through feeds.

It belongs to a family of TLDR newsletters covering tech, startups, and other fields. The AI edition includes sections for headlines, research, engineering and open-source projects, and miscellaneous items, with reading times shown so readers can choose what to open. It is supported by sponsorships, which are labeled.

TLDR AI is free by email and on the web. Brief summaries sacrifice depth, so important items deserve a click-through to the source. Alternatives include The Batch for weekly analysis, Import AI, Ben's Bites, The Rundown AI, and Last Week in AI. TLDR AI suits busy people who want a fast daily briefing.
