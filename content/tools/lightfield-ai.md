Lightfield rethinks the CRM as a system that fills itself. It connects to email, calendar, and call recordings, and extracts contacts, companies, deals, and notes automatically, so records stay current without reps typing. The aim is to remove the data entry that makes CRMs unpopular.

Beyond capturing data, it uses the context to help with work, such as drafting follow-ups, answering questions about accounts, and surfacing deals that need attention. The team behind it previously built Tome, a presentation tool, before turning to this product.

Lightfield is a young product aimed at startups and small sales teams, with paid plans. Automatic capture raises privacy and accuracy questions, and teams should check how data is stored. Alternatives include Attio, HubSpot, Folk, Clarify, and Salesforce with Einstein. Lightfield suits small teams that want a self-updating CRM.
