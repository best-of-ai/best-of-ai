Vagaro runs the front desk for beauty and wellness businesses. Clients book online or through a marketplace app, staff manage calendars, take payments, and track inventory. Reminders and no-show protection reduce lost appointments.

Marketing tools include email and text campaigns, loyalty programs, and a presence on the Vagaro marketplace. AI answers calls and messages, helps book appointments, and suggests promotions.

Vagaro charges a monthly fee per business, with add-ons and payment processing fees. Alternatives include Fresha, Booksy, StyleSeat, Square Appointments, and Mindbody. Vagaro suits salons, spas, and studios.
