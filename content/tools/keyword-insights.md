Keyword Insights helps turn a long keyword list into a content plan. A user uploads thousands of keywords or discovers them in the tool, and the platform clusters them based on how search results overlap, which indicates whether a group of terms should be targeted by one page or several.

It also determines search intent for each cluster, suggests content types, and produces briefs that outline what a page should cover based on top results. Rank tracking and content tools support the workflow after publishing, and AI assists with drafting.

Keyword Insights is paid, with plans by keyword volume. Clustering is a method, not a guarantee of ranking, and results depend on the quality of content. Alternatives include Surfer, Semrush, Ahrefs, Frase, and Cluster AI. Keyword Insights suits SEO teams planning content at scale.
