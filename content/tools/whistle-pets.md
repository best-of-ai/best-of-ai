Whistle sold small GPS devices that attached to a pet's collar and reported location through cellular networks. Owners could set safe zones, get an alert when a pet left them, and view recent routes. The devices also tracked activity, rest, and behaviors such as scratching and licking, and the app compared these against typical patterns to point out possible health changes.

The company was owned by Mars Petcare before Tractive, an Austrian pet tracking company, took over the brand. Rather than run two competing platforms, Tractive shut down the Whistle service, and the trackers no longer work. Tractive offered affected customers a replacement device, so owners are generally pointed to its products.

If you still own a Whistle device, expect that it will not report location or activity. Any health data you want to keep should be exported or noted down, and a new tracker will need its own subscription. Alternatives include Tractive, Fi, Garmin for sporting dogs, and Apple AirTag in a protective case as a basic non-GPS option. Whistle is listed here for reference and no longer sold.
