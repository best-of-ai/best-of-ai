freebeat takes a different approach to video generation. A user uploads a track, and the platform produces visuals that follow the rhythm of the music, cutting and transforming on the beat. The result is a music-driven video that can be shared on social platforms.

Musicians use it to create lyric visuals and promotional clips without a video team. Creators use it for background visuals and short-form content. Styles and prompts guide the look, and users can adjust timing and effects.

freebeat has a free tier with limits and paid plans. AI-generated visuals vary in quality, and rights to the music are the user's responsibility. Alternatives include Neural Frames, Runway, Kaiber, Pika, and Canva's video tools. freebeat suits musicians and creators who want beat-synced AI video.
