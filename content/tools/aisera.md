Aisera provides AI agents for internal and external service desks. Employees can ask for help with IT issues, such as resetting a password or requesting software, or HR questions, such as time off policies, and the agent resolves the request by taking actions in connected systems instead of simply pointing to articles. For customers, similar agents handle support inquiries. The goal is to reduce the number of tickets that reach human staff.

The platform connects with common enterprise systems, including ServiceNow, Jira, Salesforce, Workday, and Microsoft 365, and uses a mix of language models and the company's own domain-specific models. Analytics show resolution rates and gaps in knowledge, and workflow automation handles multi-step processes. Governance features let companies control what the agents can access and do.

Aisera is sold to large enterprises on custom contracts, and implementation involves integrating with several systems. Results depend on the quality of knowledge and the clarity of processes. Competitors include ServiceNow's own AI agents, Moveworks, now part of ServiceNow, Atomicwork, Microsoft Copilot Studio, and Salesforce Agentforce. As with all autonomous agents, testing and oversight are important. Aisera suits organizations with heavy IT and HR support volume.
