Sales Navigator gives salespeople deeper access to LinkedIn's network. It provides advanced filters for finding people and companies, such as by seniority, function, company size, industry, and recent job changes. Users save leads and accounts into lists, and the system sends alerts when something relevant happens, like a prospect changing jobs or a company hiring in a certain department, which can prompt a timely message.

Further features support outreach. InMail messages reach people outside your network, TeamLink shows how colleagues are connected to a prospect for warm introductions, and integrations sync with CRMs such as Salesforce and HubSpot to keep records current. AI tools recommend leads, summarize accounts, and suggest ways to start a conversation, using the profile and activity data on the platform.

Sales Navigator is sold by subscription per seat, in tiers for individuals, teams, and enterprises, and it costs significantly more than a regular LinkedIn Premium plan. Its value depends on how well your target buyers are represented on LinkedIn and how well your team uses the signals. LinkedIn restricts automation and scraping, and accounts that violate the rules can be limited. Alternatives and complements include Apollo, ZoomInfo, Seamless.AI, and Lusha for contact data, and Clay for combining sources.
