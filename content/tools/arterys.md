Arterys was an early company in medical imaging AI. Its cloud platform ran deep-learning tools on scans such as cardiac MRI, chest CT, and liver studies, helping radiologists and cardiologists measure and analyze images faster. It was among the first companies to receive regulatory clearance in the United States for a deep-learning clinical application, and its viewer was designed to be used inside the browser without special workstations.

The platform's main idea was to embed AI directly in the workflow clinicians already use. It connected to hospital systems such as PACS and electronic health records, so results appeared alongside the images instead of in a separate tool. Automating routine measurements, like heart chamber volumes, could save time and reduce variation between readers.

Arterys became part of Tempus, a precision medicine company, and its technology was reintroduced as Tempus Radiology. The standalone Arterys name is no longer in active use, and new buyers should look at Tempus's offerings. Other medical imaging AI platforms include Aidoc, Viz.ai, Rad AI, and Annalise.ai, and cloud vendors such as Nuance have also offered radiology tools. This entry stays for reference because older materials still refer to Arterys.
