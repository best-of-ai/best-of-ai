Mubert generates music in real time. You pick a mood, genre, or activity, such as focus, workout, or sleep, and it streams a continuous track that does not loop in an obvious way. Creators use it for background music in videos, streams, podcasts, and apps, and the royalty-free licensing makes it practical for online content.

Several products serve different needs. Mubert Render lets users create tracks of a chosen length from a text prompt or style, and a mobile app offers streaming for listening. An API allows developers to add generated music to games, fitness apps, and other products, delivering music that adapts to context. Artists can contribute sounds to the system, with revenue sharing for contributors.

Mubert has a free tier with limitations and paid plans for commercial use and higher volumes. Generated music is best for ambient and background use, with less variety and emotional depth than human-composed tracks, and it does not make songs with vocals. Check license terms, particularly regarding distribution on streaming platforms. Alternatives include Soundraw, Beatoven, AIVA, and Loudly for background music, with Suno and Udio focused on full songs.
