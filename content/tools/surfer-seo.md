Surfer helps writers create content that matches what already ranks. You enter a keyword and it analyzes the top search results, then suggests the terms, headings, length, and structure those pages share. As you write in its editor, a content score updates, giving a quick sense of how well your page covers the topic. Writers and SEO teams use it to guide drafts and to update old posts that have dropped in rankings.

AI tools are built into the workflow. Surfer AI can produce a full first draft that follows the analysis, and an auto-optimize feature rewrites sections to raise the score. Topical map and keyword research tools help plan clusters of related articles, and an AI tracker monitors whether your brand and pages show up in answers from tools such as ChatGPT and Google's AI summaries. Integrations with Google Docs and WordPress let writers work in familiar places.

Surfer is sold on monthly or annual plans with limits on the number of articles and tracked items, plus a free trial or demo. It is a guide rather than a guarantee: chasing a content score can lead to bloated, repetitive writing that readers dislike, and rankings depend on many factors outside the page. Clearscope and MarketMuse offer similar analysis, and Semrush and Ahrefs provide broader SEO toolsets. Edit AI-generated drafts carefully for accuracy and originality.
