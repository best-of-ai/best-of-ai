Phenom helps employers attract and manage talent. Its career sites show candidates jobs and content tailored to their interests, a recruiting CRM nurtures talent pools, and tools handle scheduling, chat, and applications. The same platform supports employees through internal job matching and career paths.

AI infers skills from resumes and profiles to match people with roles, recommend jobs, and surface candidates already in the system. Analytics show the hiring funnel and diversity data. The company sells to large enterprises.

Phenom is sold on custom contracts. AI in hiring faces regulation on bias and transparency, and employers should audit outcomes. Alternatives include Eightfold, Beamery, iCIMS, Paradox, and Workday Recruiting. Phenom suits large employers modernizing recruiting.
