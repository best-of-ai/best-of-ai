Vidyo.ai set out to save creators the time of cutting short clips from podcasts, webinars, and long videos. You upload a video or paste a link, and the AI analyzes the content to find segments that could stand alone, then produces clips with captions and a vertical layout. You can edit the transcript, adjust the clip boundaries, and customize caption styles before exporting.

The company has rebranded to quso.ai, expanding from a clipping tool into a broader social content platform. It now includes features for scheduling posts across social networks, analytics on performance, and AI helpers for creating captions and ideas. Accounts and plans carried over from Vidyo.ai, and the core clipping features continue.

Quso has a free plan with limited processing and watermarks and paid plans with more minutes, higher quality, and branding options. As with other clipping tools, the AI picks may not match what you think is best, so reviewing them is part of the process.
