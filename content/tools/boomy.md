Boomy is one of the easiest ways to generate music. You pick a style such as lo-fi, electronic, rap beats, or cinematic, and the system composes a track in moments. You can then adjust it by changing the arrangement, adding vocals or effects, and recording your own voice over the music. It requires no musical knowledge, which is why it appeals to casual creators and people who want custom background tracks.

A distinctive feature is distribution. Boomy lets users submit songs to major streaming platforms, such as Spotify and Apple Music, and earn royalties from streams, with the company taking a share. This turned it into a source of a huge number of released tracks. It also attracted scrutiny: streaming services have removed tracks that were flagged for artificial streaming activity, and the wider debate over AI-generated music on platforms continues.

Boomy has a free plan with limited song saves and releases, and paid tiers that increase them and offer more control over royalties. Music made on the free plan is subject to the company's terms on ownership and use, so read them before building a career on it. The results are generic compared with songs from newer text-to-music tools such as Suno and Udio, which can produce full vocals. Soundraw, AIVA, and Beatoven are alternatives for background music.
