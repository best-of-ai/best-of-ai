Price Tracker is a user-friendly app that uses AI to monitor product prices across major online retailers like Amazon, Walmart, and Best Buy, alerting you instantly when prices drop on items you're interested in. It works by allowing users to simply paste product URLs into the app, which then checks prices daily and sends notifications when there's a sale or price reduction.

I created Price Tracker for my girlfriend, who was always on the lookout for the best deals but found it time-consuming to constantly check multiple websites for price changes. This personal project turned into a powerful tool that now helps thousands of smart shoppers save both time and money, making it easier than ever to snag great deals on the products they love.
