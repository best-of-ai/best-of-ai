Let AI interview your customers and turn their best moments into shareable video reels. No hassle, no scheduling – just results.

Key Features
- AI-Powered Customer Interviews: Collect testimonials effortlessly with AI conducting the interviews.
- Low-Friction Collector link: Share a link to capture testimonials without scheduling or hassle.
- Instant Clips: Get AI-clipped, ready-to-use video reels.
- Professional Video Editing: Human video experts edit clips for high-quality, branded content.
- Dedicated Support: Receive tailored video marketing support.

Benefits
- Dynamic AI-Driven Interviews: Engage customers with dynamic conversations in real time, creating authentic and story-rich testimonials that outperform static, self-recorded videos.
- Global and Scalable Reach: Share a single AI Video Collector link to reach thousands of customers instantly, eliminating the need for time-consuming scheduling.
- Launched in Minutes: Launch your AI Video Collector in 5 minutes, so you can start capturing impactful testimonials today.
- Increase Conversion Rate Through Social Proof: Close deals faster by leveraging customer stories to bridge trust gaps across your go-to-market strategy.
- Testimonials on Autopilot: Automate the process of collecting, managing, and sharing customer stories, saving time and effort.

Use Case
SaaS Product Testimonials:
SaaS founders can use ReelProof to gather authentic user feedback & customer stories that highlight the value and impact of their software. These testimonials can be embedded on landing pages, shared on social media, or included in email campaigns to boost conversions and build trust.

Agency Success Stories:
Agencies can collect video testimonials from their clients, showcasing the results they’ve achieved and the value they provide. These testimonials can help demonstrate expertise, build credibility, and attract new customers in a competitive market.

E-commerce Brand Social Proof:
E-commerce brands can leverage ReelProof to collect product reviews in video format, demonstrating customer satisfaction and product quality. These testimonials can be used in ads, on product pages, or in retargeting campaigns to increase sales and reduce purchase hesitation.
