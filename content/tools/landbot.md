Landbot lets non-developers design conversations visually. A user drags blocks onto a canvas to ask questions, branch based on answers, and connect to other tools, then publishes the flow on a website, a landing page, or WhatsApp. The conversational format often gets more responses than a plain form.

AI features add the ability to answer free-text questions from a knowledge base and to build flows from a description. Integrations send leads to CRMs and spreadsheets, and handoff routes complex chats to human agents. Templates cover lead generation, support, and onboarding.

Landbot has a free plan and paid tiers by chats and features. Complex logic grows hard to manage on a visual canvas. Alternatives include Typeform, Drift, Tidio, ManyChat, and Botpress. Landbot suits marketing teams building conversational lead capture.
