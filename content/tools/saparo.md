Tell Saparo what you want to buy and it builds a checkout plan. It compares stores, filters out expired coupon codes, checks cashback portals, matches card rewards and looks for discounted gift cards. A savings panel lists each line item and the final price with shipping and tax.

It works as a Chrome extension, and you approve the final payment yourself. The savings figures on the site are labeled as examples, not promises.

Saparo is in beta. New accounts get a block of credits plus daily credits, with no subscription, no success fee and no card required during the beta. Everyday online shoppers buying shoes, electronics, clothing or home goods are the target. Coupon extensions such as Honey and cashback sites such as Rakuten do parts of the same job.
