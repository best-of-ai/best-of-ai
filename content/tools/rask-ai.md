Rask AI makes video localization simpler. You upload a video or provide a link, and the tool transcribes the speech, translates it into one of many languages, and produces a new audio track with a synthetic voice. Editing tools let you correct the transcript and translation before generating the final version.

Additional features improve the result. Voice cloning keeps the speaker's voice character in the new language, and lip sync adjusts the mouth movements to match the dubbed audio. It also handles multiple speakers, subtitles, and exports, and offers an API for teams that localize at scale.

Rask AI has a free trial and paid plans by minutes of video. Machine translation and synthetic voices can miss nuance, humor, and technical terms, so review matters for important content. Voice cloning requires consent from the speaker. Alternatives include HeyGen, ElevenLabs Dubbing, Papercup (now shut down), Dubverse, and Synthesia. Rask AI suits creators and marketing teams that want to reach new language audiences.
