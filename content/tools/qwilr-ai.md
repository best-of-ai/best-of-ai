Qwilr replaces static PDF proposals with web pages. A seller builds a proposal from blocks, such as text, images, videos, pricing tables, and acceptance sections, and sends a link. Buyers can adjust options in a pricing table, and sign online, which shortens the path to closing.

Analytics show who viewed the page, how long they spent on each section, and when they came back, which guides follow-up. Templates keep branding consistent, and integrations push data into CRMs. AI drafts content from notes and suggests improvements.

Qwilr is sold on subscription per user. It focuses on proposals and quotes, not full contract lifecycle management. Alternatives include PandaDoc, Proposify, Better Proposals, HoneyBook, and DocuSign. Qwilr suits sales teams that want modern, trackable proposals.
