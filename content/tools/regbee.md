Launching a new beauty brand? Don’t let FDA compliance be the roadblock. Regbee is your regulatory sidekick—guiding you step-by-step through MoCRA rules, FDA filings, and label validation. Whether you’re selling on Shopify or preparing for retail, Regbee ensures your products are legally ready for market. Our intuitive dashboard helps you generate official submissions like SPL and MedWatch XML without technical know-how. Scan your label, click to file, and securely store every document in your built-in Evidence Vault. Now you can focus on building your brand—not deciphering FDA regulations.
