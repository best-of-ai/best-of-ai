Vidu turns prompts into short videos. A user writes a description or supplies reference images, and the platform generates a clip. Features include reference-to-video, which keeps a character or object consistent across shots, and templates for effects that are popular in social content.

The company is one of several Chinese labs competing in video generation, alongside Kling, MiniMax, and others, and its models have gained attention for motion quality and speed. Creators use it for short films, ads, and animation tests.

Vidu has a free tier with credits and paid plans. Output length is limited and complex scenes can show artifacts. Data handling follows the policies of a Chinese company, which some organizations consider. Alternatives include Kling, Runway, Pika, Luma, and Google Veo. Vidu suits creators exploring AI video.
