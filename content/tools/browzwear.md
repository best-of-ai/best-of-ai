Browzwear helps clothing companies design digitally. In VStitcher, designers and pattern makers import 2D patterns, stitch them together virtually, and see the garment on an avatar with accurate fabric behavior. Changes to patterns or materials show immediately, and the 3D model can be checked for fit across sizes and body shapes.

The goal is to reduce physical samples, which are slow and costly. Teams can review a design with buyers using realistic renderings, adjust it, and send tech packs to factories, and the same files can feed marketing images. Browzwear also offers tools for fabric scanning and connections to product lifecycle management systems.

Browzwear is paid, sold to apparel brands and manufacturers, and takes time to learn and to build a fabric library. Digital samples help but do not replace physical checks entirely for some products. Alternatives include CLO 3D, Optitex, Style3D, Marvelous Designer, and Lectra. Browzwear suits fashion and apparel teams adopting 3D design.
