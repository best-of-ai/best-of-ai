GetSolved AI Humanizer takes a draft written by an AI model and rewrites it with different sentence structure, word choice and flow. The facts and meaning stay the same. You paste text into the box or upload a PDF, DOCX or TXT file, pick a style such as professional, academic, casual or conversational, and get a rewritten version back. It works on text from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other tools.

The page says it handles English, Spanish, French and 15 other languages, and that each request works best up to about 10,000 words. Longer text should be split. GetSolved says it does not store your text after processing. The writers, marketers and content teams it targets use it for emails, blog drafts, product descriptions and social captions.

The headline says free, but you need an account and a plan to use it. Downloading, editing and exporting the result requires a paid plan, and the cheap trial renews into a monthly subscription, so check the live pricing and cancellation terms first. The site says the tool is about style and readability, not about beating AI detectors. Detectors are unreliable and some schools and employers ban undisclosed AI text, so read the output and edit it yourself before you publish or submit anything.
