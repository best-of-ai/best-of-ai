Stability AI helped start the open-source image generation boom with Stable Diffusion, a model anyone could download, run, and modify. The release spurred a huge community of tools, fine-tunes, and applications, and changed expectations about access to generative models.

The company has since released a family of models, including newer image generators, Stable Video Diffusion, Stable Audio for music and sound, and 3D tools. It sells API access and commercial licenses, and partners with enterprises, and with media and entertainment companies.

Stability has faced financial and leadership turbulence, and its newer releases come with licensing terms that limit large commercial use without a license. Alternatives include Black Forest Labs' Flux, Midjourney, OpenAI's GPT Image, Adobe Firefly, and Google's Imagen. Stability AI suits developers wanting open and licensable models.
