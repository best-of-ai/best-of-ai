Mem is built on the idea that notes should organize themselves. Users capture thoughts, meeting notes, and links without choosing folders, and the system connects related items and surfaces them when relevant. Search understands meaning, so finding an old idea does not require remembering exact words.

AI features extend this. A chat assistant answers questions using your notes, summarizes meetings, and drafts text from your own material. Integrations bring in content from calendars and other apps, so context accumulates in one place.

Mem has a free tier and paid plans. Self-organizing systems are less predictable than manual structures, and some people prefer tools like Obsidian that give full control. Alternatives include Notion AI, Obsidian, Reflect, Capacities, and Apple Notes. Mem suits people who want effortless capture and retrieval.
