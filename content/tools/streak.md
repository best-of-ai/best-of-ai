Streak adds pipeline management to Gmail. Users create boxes for deals, candidates, or projects, move them through stages, and see all related emails, notes, and files in one place. Because it sits in the inbox, there is little context switching.

Features include mail merge, email tracking, scheduling, shared inboxes, and automations. AI helps summarize threads and draft emails. It suits small teams that already run on Google Workspace.

Streak has a free plan and paid tiers. It is tied to Gmail. Alternatives include Copper, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Close, and Attio. Streak suits small teams working from the inbox.
