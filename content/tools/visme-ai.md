Visme is aimed at people who need to present information visually without design skills. It offers a drag-and-drop editor with thousands of templates for presentations, infographics, reports, charts, social media posts, and documents. Data widgets create charts and graphs from spreadsheets, and interactive elements such as links and animations can be added. Brand kits keep colors, fonts, and logos consistent across a team.

AI features speed up creation. An AI designer can generate a presentation or document from a prompt, write and rewrite text, create images, and suggest layouts. Teams can collaborate in real time with comments and workflows, and content can be exported as PDF, PowerPoint, or HTML, or shared by link with analytics. Visme is often used by marketers, educators, consultants, and communications teams.

Visme has a free plan with limited features and paid plans for individuals, teams, and businesses. It has a wider range of business-oriented content than Canva, particularly for data visualization, but a smaller community and asset library. Canva is the most popular alternative, Piktochart is strong for infographics, Venngage targets infographics and reports, and Gamma and Beautiful.ai focus on AI presentations. Visme suits business users who need data-rich visuals.
