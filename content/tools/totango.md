Totango helps subscription companies keep customers. It gathers usage and account data, scores health, and triggers playbooks when customers need attention. Modules called SuccessBLOCs package best-practice workflows that teams can adopt and adjust.

The company merged with Catalyst, another customer success platform, and the combined product serves teams from startups to enterprises. AI features summarize accounts and draft communications, and analytics show retention and expansion.

Totango has had a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Gainsight, ChurnZero, Planhat, Vitally, and HubSpot. Totango suits customer success teams at software companies.
