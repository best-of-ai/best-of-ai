Product Introduction

Welcome to our cutting-edge AI story generation platform! Whether you are a writing enthusiast or a reader seeking inspiration, this platform caters to all your needs. Our AI not only generates captivating stories but also provides unique illustrations that bring each story to life. With a simple interface, you can effortlessly create, edit, and optimize your stories, enjoying an endless creative experience.

Product Features

- AI-Generated Stories: Leveraging the latest AI technology, effortlessly create stories across various genres, from romantic tales to sci-fi adventures.
- Illustration Generation: Beyond text, the AI can generate stunning illustrations for your stories, making your work even more engaging.
- Custom Editing: Whether it’s the story content or illustrations, you can freely modify everything until you are fully satisfied.
- Optimized AI Prompts: Even with concise prompts, the AI can produce high-quality stories that meet your expectations, offering concise yet inspiring results.
- Multiple Generations: The AI supports repeated generation, allowing you to refine and perfect your story until it reaches its best form.
- Social Interaction: Browse and read a wide array of AI-generated short stories, engage by liking, bookmarking, sharing your stories, and interacting with other creators.

Product Functionality

- **Automatic Story Generation**: With simple prompts or themes, the AI automatically generates complete storylines and illustrations.
- **Story Editing**: Powerful editing tools allow users to make detailed modifications and optimizations to the generated stories.
- **Repeated Generation**: Supports multiple generations, enabling users to continuously refine and improve story content.
- **Prompt Optimization Tool**: Helps users simplify prompts, ensuring the AI understands your needs more accurately, generating stories that better match your expectations.
- **Points System**: Users generate stories using points. Earn free points by daily check-ins or completing tasks.

### **Pricing Model**

Our platform uses a flexible points-based system, offering free trials and recharge options:

- **Free Points**: New users receive 30 free generations upon registration, allowing them to fully experience the joy of AI story creation.
- **Recharge Options**: Once you’ve used up your free points, you can purchase more through a single payment. Recharge 5,000 points for just $10, providing ongoing support for your creative journey.

### **Supported Platforms**

This product is available on the web, allowing users to enjoy the convenience and creativity that AI brings through their browser. Whether you’re using a computer or a mobile device, you can easily access and use our service.
