AI21 Labs was founded by AI researchers in Tel Aviv and has been building language models since before ChatGPT made them mainstream. Its best-known models are the Jamba family, which blend a state-space architecture called Mamba with transformer layers. The aim is a model that handles very long documents with less memory and faster output than a standard transformer of similar size. Several Jamba versions have been released with open weights.

The company's business focus is on enterprise use. Its platform provides models through an API and through cloud marketplaces, along with tools for question answering over company documents, summarization, and writing. AI21 also builds agent-style systems that plan and complete tasks with checks along the way. The company also makes Wordtune, a consumer writing assistant.

Developers can try the models through a free tier or trial and pay by usage for production. Customers can also deploy models in their own cloud environment, which suits companies with strict data rules. AI21 is smaller than OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google, so it competes by specializing in efficient long-context models and enterprise deployment rather than by matching the largest general-purpose chatbots. Cohere and Mistral are the closest peers in that business-focused niche.
