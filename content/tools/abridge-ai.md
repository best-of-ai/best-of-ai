Abridge targets one of healthcare's biggest burdens: the time doctors spend writing notes. With patient consent, the app records the conversation during a visit, and AI turns it into a structured clinical note in the format the clinician needs, ready for review and insertion into the electronic health record. After the visit, a patient-friendly summary can also be produced. The aim is to let doctors focus on the patient instead of the screen.

It integrates deeply with record systems such as Epic, and has been adopted by large health systems. Beyond notes, Abridge has developed features that help with coding and billing, authorization, and orders, based on what was said in the visit. Linking the notes back to the parts of the conversation they came from lets clinicians verify content, which matters for accuracy and trust.

Abridge is sold to health systems on enterprise contracts. Ambient documentation tools can make mistakes, such as inventing details or missing nuance, so clinician review is essential, and privacy rules like HIPAA apply. Competitors include Microsoft's Nuance DAX Copilot, Suki, Ambience Healthcare, Nabla, and Heidi Health. Abridge is one of the leading products in the fast-growing ambient clinical documentation category.
