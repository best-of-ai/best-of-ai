Docketwise is built specifically for immigration law. Attorneys collect information from clients with online questionnaires, and the software places answers on the correct government forms, which saves repeated typing. Because forms change, the platform keeps them updated.

It manages the rest of a case. Deadlines and receipts are tracked, status updates can be pulled from government sources, and client portals let people upload documents and see progress. Billing and email integrations support daily practice, and AI features help with drafting and summarizing.

MyCase acquired Docketwise, and it remains available as a product. It is sold by subscription per user. Immigration rules change often, so attorneys must verify forms and requirements. Alternatives include INSZoom, Clio with integrations, LollyLaw, Boundless, and Lawfully. Docketwise suits immigration lawyers and consultants.
