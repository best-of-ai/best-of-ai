Kerplunk reimagines recruitment with its innovative AI-powered platform designed for asynchronous video interviews. This transformative technology enables organizations to conduct interviews seamlessly, regardless of geographical barriers or time constraints. By automating the screening and evaluation process, Kerplunk accelerates time-to-hire and improves hiring outcomes. Its advanced AI algorithms analyze candidate responses, providing objective assessments that assist in making informed decisions. Supporting interviews in over 95 languages, Kerplunk facilitates global talent acquisition, empowering companies to attract and retain top talent from diverse backgrounds.
