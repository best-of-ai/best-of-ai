Hospitable is software for people who run short-term rentals, from a single host to a property management company. It syncs listings and calendars across booking channels, handles guest messaging, assigns tasks to cleaners and co-hosts, and manages owner payouts.

The AI features focus on messages, where hosts spend much of their time. AI Auto Reply answers guest questions on its own, Inbox AI drafts replies for you to approve, and AI Copilot answers questions about your portfolio. An MCP integration lets AI agents work with your Hospitable data. Dynamic pricing is included in the plan rather than sold as an add-on.

Hospitable has a free Essentials plan and a free trial, with paid tiers for more features. Hostaway targets larger property managers, and PriceLabs is a specialist pricing tool. Hospitable fits self-managing hosts and small teams that want guest communication handled well.
