Nutrium helps nutrition professionals run a practice. They manage clients, schedule appointments, and keep records of measurements and goals, while clients use an app to log meals and see their plan. A food database with nutrient data supports analysis and meal planning.

AI speeds up plan creation by proposing menus that fit a client's calorie and nutrient targets and preferences, which the dietitian then adjusts. Reports and communication tools keep clients engaged between visits.

Nutrium has a free trial and paid plans by number of clients. Generated plans need professional review for medical conditions and allergies. Alternatives include Practice Better, NutriAdmin, Cronometer for Pros, Healthie, and Nutritionist Pro. Nutrium suits dietitians and nutritionists.
