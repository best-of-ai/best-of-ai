Everbridge helps organizations respond when something goes wrong. A hurricane, an active threat, an IT outage, or a supply chain disruption triggers the need to reach employees, residents, or customers quickly. The platform sends alerts by text, voice, email, and app notification, and tracks who has responded.

Its risk intelligence monitors global sources to detect events near offices, facilities, and travelers, and AI helps filter the noise. Organizations can map assets, identify who is affected, and launch response plans. Governments use related tools for public warning systems.

Everbridge is sold to enterprises and public agencies on subscription. It is owned by private equity after being taken private. Effectiveness depends on accurate contact data and well-practiced plans. Alternatives include OnSolve, Rave Mobile Safety, Genasys, Regroup, and Dataminr. Everbridge suits organizations needing reliable emergency communication.
