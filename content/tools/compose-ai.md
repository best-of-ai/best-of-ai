Compose AI works wherever you type in Chrome. As you write an email or a message, it suggests the next words or sentence, which you accept with a keystroke, similar to code completion in a programming editor. It can also draft whole replies, rewrite text in a different tone, and fix grammar, with a menu of shortcuts for common tasks.

The product came from a startup that focused on autocomplete for writing, and later added generative features that call large language models for longer tasks. It is intended to save keystrokes on repetitive writing such as emails, support replies, and social posts, and to reduce the effort of starting a draft. A free tier covers basic use, with paid plans for more generations and features.

Users have reported that autocomplete quality and speed changed as the extension expanded, and the developer appears to update it less often than larger products. Because it reads what you type in web pages, review its permissions and privacy practices. Grammarly, Wordtune, and Gmail's built-in Help me write offer similar help, and general assistants like ChatGPT and Claude can draft text when you paste a request. Compose AI suits people who like inline suggestions.
