Quantilope aims to let research teams run advanced studies without waiting weeks on an agency. Researchers pick from automated methods, such as concept tests, segmentation, pricing studies, and brand tracking, set up the survey, and field it to respondents from a panel. Analysis and charts are generated as data comes in.

The platform's AI helps design studies, suggests suitable methods, writes summaries of findings, and answers questions about the data. Because methodologies are built in, non-specialists can run studies that would normally need a statistician.

Quantilope is sold on subscription to brands and research agencies. Panel costs add to the price, and automated analysis still needs human judgment. Alternatives include Qualtrics, SurveyMonkey, Zappi, Dynata, and Toluna. Quantilope suits insights teams that run frequent research.
