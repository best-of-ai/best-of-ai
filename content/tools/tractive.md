Tractive helps owners keep track of their pets. A small GPS device attached to a collar sends the animal's location to a phone app via cellular networks, so you can see where your dog or cat is in real time, view recent routes, and set virtual fences that trigger alerts if the pet leaves a defined area. A light and sound function helps find a pet at night.

Beyond location, the devices monitor activity, sleep, and behavior, and some models track signs of health changes such as excessive scratching or licking. Data shows trends and can help owners share information with veterinarians. Features such as a lost-pet mode, sharing access with family, and integration with pet services support everyday use.

Tractive hardware is a one-time purchase and requires a subscription for cellular service and app features, with discounts for longer plans. Coverage and battery life vary by model and country. Alternatives include Fi collars, Apple AirTags in a case, which rely on nearby devices instead of GPS, Garmin dog trackers for hunting, and Whistle devices. Tractive suits pet owners who want peace of mind about their pet's whereabouts.
