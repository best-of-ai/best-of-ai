Cognite addresses data silos in industry. A plant produces time series from sensors, maintenance records, engineering diagrams, and 3D models that live in different systems. Cognite Data Fusion links them into a graph in which a pump, its sensor readings, its work orders, and its drawings are connected, so people and applications can find everything about an asset.

That foundation supports applications. Teams build digital twins, predictive maintenance models, and dashboards, and an AI assistant helps users ask questions about assets and operations in natural language. The platform is used in oil and gas, power, and manufacturing, where downtime is costly.

Cognite is sold to large industrial companies on enterprise contracts, and projects take time to connect data sources. Value depends on data quality. Alternatives include AVEVA, AspenTech, Siemens, Palantir Foundry, and PTC. Cognite suits industrial operators building a unified data layer.
