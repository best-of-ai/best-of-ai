Upload your photo, select from five pixelation levels ranging from minimal to extreme, and preview results instantly in your browser. Our intelligent detail-preserving algorithm keeps key shapes sharp while delivering authentic pixelated aesthetics.

Whether you are creating retro game assets, social media visuals, marketing campaigns, or mood boards, Pixelate Image streamlines the entire process. No downloads, no watermarks, no learning curve—just a smooth experience on desktop and mobile. All processing happens locally in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Key Features
1. One-click pixelation: Convert images to pixel art instantly with adjustable intensity levels.
2. Smart detail preservation: Maintain essential outlines and silhouettes for professional-looking pixelated images.
3. Multi-format support: Upload JPEG, PNG, or WebP images up to 5MB and export high-quality files.
4. Real-time preview: See every change as you adjust pixel sizes and quickly iterate toward the perfect look.
5. Full commercial usage: Download pixelated artwork with unrestricted rights for personal or commercial projects.
6. Privacy-first workflow: 100% browser-based processing—no uploads, no tracking, no watermarks.
7. Responsive design: Optimized UI for desktop, tablet, and mobile so you can pixelate on the go.

How It Works
1. Upload a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image (max 5MB) via drag-and-drop or file picker.
2. Select your pixelation level—minimal, light, medium, heavy, or extreme.
3. Review the live preview and fine-tune the effect until it matches your creative vision.
4. Download the final pixelated image in your preferred format and start sharing or editing.
