Perplexity treats search as a conversation rather than a keyword lookup. Ask it a complex question — "what are the main differences between LLCs and S-Corps for a solo freelancer?" — and it reads the relevant web pages, synthesises the key points, and gives you a direct answer with numbered citations you can click to verify. The citations aren't just URLs — they're specific passages highlighted in context, so you can immediately see where each claim came from without reading the full source.

The conversational structure means follow-up questions work naturally. If the first answer mentions something you want to know more about, you ask; Perplexity already has the context from the previous exchange. You can also choose what type of source to search: Academic mode restricts results to scholarly papers, Reddit mode surfaces community discussions, YouTube mode finds relevant videos, and News mode prioritises recent journalism. These focus modes significantly change what kind of answer you get for the same question.

Perplexity is free to use with daily usage limits on advanced models. Pro at $20/month increases the daily limit, gives access to GPT-4o and Claude Sonnet for more nuanced answers, enables file uploads for document analysis, and unlocks deeper search capabilities. It's available on web, iOS, and Android. Many users find it most valuable for professional research tasks — due diligence, competitive analysis, technical learning — where thoroughness matters and you'd otherwise spend 20 minutes clicking through search results yourself.
