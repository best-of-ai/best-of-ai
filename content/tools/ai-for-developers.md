AI for Developers is written for people who build software and want to use AI well. Issues tend to focus on how to do things: setting up an AI coding assistant, writing prompts for common programming tasks, comparing tools, or building small projects with language model APIs. The tone is practical rather than speculative.

A developer-focused newsletter fills a gap between general AI news and academic papers. Readers look for ideas they can apply the same day, such as workflows for code review, testing, or documentation, and for honest comparisons that show what works and what does not. The Substack format also lets readers comment and share tips.

The newsletter is free to read, and Substack authors sometimes offer paid tiers. As with any independent newsletter, content reflects the author's choices and experience, so it pairs well with official documentation. Alternatives include TLDR AI, The Batch, Latent Space, Simon Willison's blog, and Ben's Bites. AI for Developers suits programmers looking for hands-on guidance.
