Textio focuses on how words affect people at work. Its model, trained on large amounts of text paired with outcomes like application rates, flags language in a job posting that may discourage certain candidates, such as gendered phrases or jargon, and suggests alternatives that attract a broader and larger pool. Recruiters get a score and specific edits as they write.

The platform extends to employee communications. Textio helps managers write clearer, fairer performance reviews and feedback by spotting vague, biased, or unhelpful phrases, and supports recruiting emails and other HR writing. Generative AI features help draft job posts and feedback from a few inputs while applying the same guidance. Integrations work within applicant tracking systems and email tools.

Textio is sold to organizations on subscription with custom pricing. Evidence of its effects comes from the company's data, and language is one factor among many in hiring and reviews. AI-generated content should be reviewed, especially for performance evaluations. Alternatives include Datapeople and Ongig for job postings, Grammarly Business for general writing, and the writing features of applicant tracking systems. Textio suits HR and talent teams focused on inclusive language.
