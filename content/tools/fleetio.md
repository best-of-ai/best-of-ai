Fleetio helps organizations that rely on vehicles keep them running. It maintains a record for every vehicle, truck, or piece of equipment, including service history, documents, and costs. Preventive maintenance schedules trigger reminders by mileage, hours, or time, which reduces breakdowns and extends vehicle life. Drivers can complete inspections on a phone app, report issues, and log fuel, and shops can manage work orders and parts.

Data from telematics devices flows into the system, so mileage, location, and diagnostic trouble codes update automatically and can trigger work orders. Reports show total cost of ownership, fuel use, downtime, and compliance items, and integrations connect with accounting, fuel card, and telematics providers. AI features help summarize issues, surface patterns, and suggest actions to reduce cost.

Fleetio is sold by subscription priced per vehicle, with plans for different fleet sizes and a trial, and it is used by delivery companies, utilities, municipalities, contractors, and service businesses. Smaller fleets might find a basic spreadsheet or lighter tool enough. Competitors include Samsara, Geotab, Verizon Connect, Motive, and Whip Around, with Samsara and Motive combining telematics hardware with software. Fleetio's strength is maintenance management, and it works with many different hardware providers.
