Notis works where people already chat. Users send a text or voice note to the assistant in a messaging app, and it can save notes, draft emails, set tasks, search the web, or update tools like Notion and calendars. The aim is to reduce the friction of opening several apps.

Connections to services such as Gmail, Google Calendar, and Notion let the assistant act on requests, and it keeps a memory of preferences. Voice notes are transcribed and organized.

Notis has a free tier and paid plans. Giving an assistant access to email and calendars requires trust and review of what it does. Alternatives include Lindy, Poke, Zapier Agents, Motion, and ChatGPT with connectors. Notis suits professionals who prefer to delegate through chat.
