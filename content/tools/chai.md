Chai lets people talk to a huge variety of AI personalities. Users browse or search for characters, such as a friendly companion, a fantasy hero, or a fictional figure, and chat in a messaging-style interface. Creators can build their own characters by writing a short description and a first message, and they are shared with the community, which has produced a very large catalog.

The experience is aimed at entertainment and companionship. The AI maintains a persona and some memory of the conversation, and the app offers features such as voice and image options and a feed of popular bots. Free users see ads and message limits, while subscribers get faster responses and more features.

Like other companion apps, Chai has raised concerns about the emotional dependence some users develop and about content safety, especially for young or vulnerable people, and it has been involved in public discussions after reports of harm. Users should treat AI characters as fiction, avoid sharing sensitive information, and seek human support when struggling. Alternatives include Character.AI, Replika, Talkie, Nomi, and Kindroid. Chai is best seen as casual entertainment.
