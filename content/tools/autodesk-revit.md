Changes made in one view update plans, sections, schedules, and quantities. Teams coordinate architecture, structure, and building systems in a shared model. Dynamo supports scripted automation, and generative design tools can test layout options.

It runs on Windows and has a steep learning curve. Large firms rely on it for coordination.

Archicad and Vectorworks are alternatives. Revit suits architects and engineers who work in BIM.
