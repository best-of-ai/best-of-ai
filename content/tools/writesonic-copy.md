Writesonic started as a template-based copywriter and has widened into a content and marketing suite. Users pick a tool for blog articles, ad copy, product descriptions, emails, or social posts, give a brief, and get a draft. A long-form article writer can produce SEO-minded posts from a keyword with outlines and sources, and a chat assistant answers questions and drafts on demand.

Recent additions center on search visibility. Writesonic has tools to see how a brand is mentioned in AI answer engines such as ChatGPT and Perplexity, plus recommendations for improving content. It also offers brand voice settings, an editor with SEO scoring, and integrations with WordPress and other platforms.

Writesonic has a limited free plan and paid plans by usage and features. AI drafts need fact-checking and editing, and long articles can come out generic without careful prompts. Alternatives include Jasper, Copy.ai, Surfer, Frase, and general assistants like ChatGPT and Claude. Writesonic suits marketers and small teams that want fast drafts plus some SEO guidance.
