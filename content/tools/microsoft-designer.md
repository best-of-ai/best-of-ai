Microsoft Designer helps people make graphics quickly. You describe what you want, such as an invitation for a birthday party or a banner for a sale, and Designer produces several layout options with text, images, and styling, which you can edit. It offers templates for common formats, background removal, and tools for resizing a design for different platforms.

AI image generation is built in, powered by Microsoft's and OpenAI's image models, so you can create custom illustrations or photos to include in a design. It integrates with Microsoft 365 apps and Copilot, and is available on the web, as a mobile app, and inside tools such as Edge. Brand kits and shared templates are available on paid Microsoft 365 plans.

Designer is free with a Microsoft account, with extra generative credits and features for Microsoft 365 subscribers. It is less capable than Canva for deep template libraries and team workflows, or than Adobe tools for advanced editing, and results can look generic. Alternatives include Canva, Adobe Express, Figma, Visme, and Piktochart. Microsoft Designer suits people who need quick visuals within the Microsoft ecosystem.
