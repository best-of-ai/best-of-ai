Younet helps businesses build agents tailored to their data. Teams connect knowledge sources, define roles and tasks, and deploy agents for areas like support, sales, and operations. The platform aims to let non-engineers assemble working agents.

Training on a company's own information improves relevance and keeps answers consistent with policies. Governance features and integrations connect agents to business tools.

Independent information is limited, so evaluate with a pilot and check data handling. Younet has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Relevance AI, Dust, Cassidy, Stack AI, and Microsoft Copilot Studio. Younet suits teams exploring custom business agents.
