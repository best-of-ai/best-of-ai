Formula Bot is aimed at spreadsheet users who know what they want but not the syntax. You describe the calculation, such as finding the average of a column when another column equals a value, and it produces the formula with an explanation. It can also explain a formula you paste in and fix errors, which helps when you inherit a workbook full of nested functions.

The tool extends beyond formulas. It can write scripts, SQL queries, and regular expressions from descriptions, convert between formula dialects, and answer questions about data you upload by building charts and summaries. A chat-style data analysis feature lets users ask questions of a spreadsheet and receive results, aimed at people who don't write formulas at all.

Formula Bot has a free tier with limited use and paid plans for more. AI-generated formulas should be tested, as errors in a spreadsheet can silently affect results. Excel's Copilot and Google Sheets' Gemini provide help inside the apps, and general assistants like ChatGPT and Claude can produce formulas for free. Ajelix and Sheet+ are similar dedicated tools. Formula Bot suits occasional spreadsheet users who want quick answers.
