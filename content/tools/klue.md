Klue is a competitive enablement platform. Product marketing teams gather information on rivals from news, websites, reviews, and internal sales feedback, and organize it into battlecards with talking points, strengths, weaknesses, and objection handling. Sales reps access these cards in their CRM or Slack during deals.

AI helps keep the content fresh. It scans for changes, summarizes updates, and helps draft battlecard sections. Win/loss analysis tools collect feedback from deals and reveal patterns. Reports show which competitive content is used and how it relates to outcomes.

Klue is sold on subscription to mid-sized and large B2B companies. It needs ongoing curation by a competitive intelligence owner. Alternatives include Crayon, Kompyte, Highspot, Competitor Analyzer, and Contify. Klue suits product marketing and sales enablement teams.
