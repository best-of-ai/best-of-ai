Shift Technology serves insurers. When a claim arrives, its models assess the likelihood of fraud, explain the red flags, and route the case, so investigators spend time on the suspicious ones while clean claims are paid quickly.

The company extends the approach to underwriting, subrogation, and customer service, and has added generative AI agents that help adjusters summarize files and handle routine tasks. Its customers include large carriers around the world.

Shift is sold on enterprise contracts. Automated claim decisions raise fairness and explainability concerns that regulators watch, so keeping people in the loop is important. Competitors include FRISS, Verisk, Guidewire's tools, Hyperscience for documents, and SAS. Shift Technology suits insurance carriers fighting fraud.
