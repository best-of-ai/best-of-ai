Brokers and investors search for properties by criteria, then look up the owner behind an LLC, recent sales, and mortgages. Map search helps find off-market opportunities. Contact data supports outreach.

Subscription pricing is aimed at professionals, and ownership data can lag for recent sales. It is now part of Altus Group.

CoStar and PropStream are alternatives. Reonomy suits real estate developers and investors.
