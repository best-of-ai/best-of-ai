Diagnocat analyzes dental imaging. A clinician uploads a CBCT scan, panoramic radiograph, or intraoral X-rays, and the AI segments the teeth and bone and flags issues in a report organized by tooth.

For orthodontics, the 3D view helps with impacted teeth, root position, and airway or bone assessment, and the reports can be shared with patients or referring dentists.

Diagnocat is a paid service. Findings are decision support, so the dentist has to confirm them. Alternatives include Overjet, Pearl, and VideaHealth. Diagnocat suits practices that use CBCT regularly.
