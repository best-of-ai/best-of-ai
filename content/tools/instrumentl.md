Instrumentl is a grant discovery and management platform. A nonprofit describes its mission and programs, and the system recommends foundation and government grants that fit, with details on the funder's giving history, typical award sizes, and past recipients. Alerts notify the team when new matches appear.

Once a grant is identified, a tracker handles tasks, deadlines, reports, and documents for each application, with collaboration for the team. AI assists with research, summarizing funder priorities and drafting parts of proposals, though grant writing still depends on strong program knowledge.

Instrumentl is sold by subscription to nonprofits and grant consultants. Matches are suggestions that need judgment about fit and effort. Alternatives include Candid's Foundation Directory, GrantStation, Grant Gopher, Fundsforngos, and Grants.gov for federal grants. Instrumentl suits nonprofit development teams seeking funding.
