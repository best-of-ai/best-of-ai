Pimsleur is an audio-first method developed by linguist Paul Pimsleur. Each short lesson has the learner listen to a conversation, repeat phrases aloud, and answer prompts, with vocabulary reintroduced at expanding intervals so it moves into memory. Lessons are designed for use while commuting or doing chores.

The app offers many languages and adds features such as reading lessons, flashcards, and speech practice. Its approach centers on speaking and listening more than grammar rules or writing.

Pimsleur is a subscription or by-course purchase. It builds a strong foundation in pronunciation and basic conversation, but advanced learners need broader material. Alternatives include Duolingo, Babbel, Michel Thomas, Rosetta Stone, and Speak. Pimsleur suits learners who want to speak and listen.
