Tipalti automates accounts payable for growing companies. Suppliers and partners onboard through a portal that collects tax forms and payment details, invoices are captured and approved, and Tipalti pays them in the local currency and method across many countries.

Compliance is a main benefit: the platform handles tax documentation, sanctions screening, and reporting, which is hard for companies paying people worldwide. AI reads invoices, flags anomalies, and helps reconcile payments in accounting systems.

Tipalti is sold on custom subscription pricing with transaction fees. Alternatives include BILL, Stampli, Coupa Pay, Payoneer, and Airwallex. Tipalti suits mid-sized companies paying many global suppliers.
