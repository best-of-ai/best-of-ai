Sunsama is built around the idea that planning your day deliberately reduces overwhelm. Each morning, a guided ritual walks you through gathering tasks from different sources, choosing what you can realistically do, estimating the time each will take, and placing them on your calendar. The app shows your total planned hours against the day's available time, which discourages overcommitting.

It connects to many other tools. Tasks from project managers, issue trackers, email, and Slack can be brought into your daily view without leaving Sunsama, and calendar events appear alongside them. An end-of-day shutdown ritual lets you review what you finished and move unfinished tasks forward. AI features help with suggestions for scheduling, summaries, and organizing tasks.

Sunsama is a paid subscription with a trial, aimed at professionals and teams who want a thoughtful planner more than an automated one. It is more manual than Motion or Reclaim, which schedule tasks for you, and some people find the daily ritual a benefit while others find it demanding. Alternatives include Akiflow, Todoist, TickTick, and Notion for task tracking. Sunsama suits people who want mindful daily planning.
