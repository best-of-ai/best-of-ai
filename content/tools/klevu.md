Klevu improves how online shoppers find products. Instead of matching keywords exactly, its search engine uses natural language processing to interpret queries, handle synonyms and misspellings, and rank results based on relevance and merchandising goals. It works with platforms such as Shopify, Magento, and BigCommerce.

The product also includes category browsing, recommendations, and analytics on what shoppers search for and fail to find. Merchandisers can adjust results with rules, and AI personalization adapts to a shopper's behavior. A chat-style assistant answers product questions.

Klevu is sold by subscription with pricing based on catalog size and traffic. Its value depends on a well-maintained product catalog. Alternatives include Algolia, Searchspring, Bloomreach, Constructor, and Boost Commerce. Klevu suits mid-sized online stores that want better search.
