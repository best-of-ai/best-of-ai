Landing AI is built around data-centric AI, the idea that improving the quality of training data often matters more than tweaking models. Its LandingLens platform lets manufacturing, agriculture, and healthcare teams train computer vision models for tasks like detecting defects, counting objects, and classifying images, with a no-code interface that emphasizes labeling quality and error analysis. Domain experts can build models without being machine learning engineers.

The company has expanded into document intelligence. Its agentic document extraction uses vision and language models to read complex documents such as financial statements, forms, and reports, and to turn them into structured data with visual grounding that shows where each value came from. Developers use APIs to add this to workflows.

Landing AI sells to enterprises with tiered plans and trials, and it's used in industries like electronics manufacturing. Building a reliable vision model still needs well-defined problems and good data. Alternatives include Roboflow, Google Vertex AI and Document AI, AWS Rekognition and Textract, Azure AI Vision, and Cognex or Zebra for industrial inspection. Landing AI suits teams that want to deploy vision AI without a large machine learning staff.
