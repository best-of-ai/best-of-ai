Bonsai bundles the paperwork of independent work. A freelancer sends a proposal, converts it into a contract with e-signature, tracks time or milestones, sends an invoice, and receives payment. A client portal keeps everything in one place, which helps a one-person business look established.

Additional tools cover running the business. Expense tracking, tax estimates, and banking features support finances, and workflow automations send reminders for unpaid invoices. AI helps draft contract language and project descriptions. Templates are available for design, consulting, development, and other fields.

Bonsai has a free trial and paid plans by features. It is aimed at freelancers and small studios, and it does not replace legal advice for large or unusual contracts. Alternatives include HoneyBook, Dubsado, FreshBooks, Moxie, and QuickBooks. Bonsai suits freelancers who want one tool for client work.
