AgentKit from Scalekit is a single MCP endpoint in front of more than 1,100 connectors and tens of thousands of tools. Instead of wiring OAuth for each app yourself, you get managed authentication and a token vault for per-user credentials.

Access is scoped with virtual MCP servers, triggers run agents on events, and every call is logged, with streaming to SIEMs such as Datadog and Splunk. You can add custom connectors or bring your own MCP server, and enterprise adds BYOK, VPC and self-hosting.

A free plan includes monthly tool calls and trigger events, with a hard stop at the limit and no card. The Growth and Scale plans raise the limits and charge per call beyond them, and Enterprise is custom. There is also a separate Auth for SaaS product. The homepage names Composio, Nango and Pipedream among its competitors.
