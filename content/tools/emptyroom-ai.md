EmptyRoom AI serves real estate listings. Agents upload a photo of a vacant or occupied room, and the tool furnishes it in a chosen style while walls, windows, and doors stay fixed. Listing photos can be imported from major portals, and furniture removal clears occupied rooms before the new stage goes in.

Pricing is per room, and restages of a paid room are free, so an agent can test styles until the photo reads. Staged photos should be labeled as virtually staged, since many markets require disclosure of digitally altered listing images. Alternatives include BoxBrownie, Virtual Staging AI, Collov AI, and RoomGPT. EmptyRoom AI suits agents who want listing-ready staged photos without physical staging.
