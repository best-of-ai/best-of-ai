Yardi is one of the largest suppliers of software to the real estate industry. Property managers use it to market units, screen applicants, sign leases, collect rent, track maintenance, and run accounting. Different products fit different segments: Voyager serves larger portfolios, Breeze suits smaller operators, and other tools cover commercial space, senior living, and investment management.

AI features are collected under Virtuoso, which Yardi uses for tasks like answering resident questions, drafting leasing responses, handling invoices and documents, and analyzing performance. The goal is to take routine work off property staff and to provide insights from data that the company already holds in its accounting and leasing systems.

Yardi sells through quotes and customized implementations, which makes it more expensive and complex than simple landlord apps. Its breadth is a draw for big portfolios, but smaller owners may find it heavy. Alternatives include AppFolio, RealPage, Buildium, MRI Software, and Entrata. Yardi suits property management companies and owners with large or varied holdings.
