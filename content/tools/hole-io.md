Hole io is a fun action game where you control a mobile "black hole" with a simple but addictive goal: swallow the whole city!

You start as a small hole, then swallow lamp posts, cars and even buildings to become bigger. The bigger you get, the more you can swallow – even… your opponents! The game is fast-paced, easy to understand and extremely satisfying every time you "eat" the whole neighborhood. Try and see if you can become the hole that swallows everything in the time limit!
