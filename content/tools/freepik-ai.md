Freepik began as a stock site for vectors, photos, and icons, and many designers already knew it. It has added a full AI suite on top. The image generator creates pictures from text prompts, and tools for editing, upscaling, retouching, and expanding images let users refine them. Video generation and animation features turn stills or prompts into short clips, and a workspace organizes projects and lets you mix different tools.

A distinctive part is access to many models in one place. Freepik offers its own models alongside models from other providers, so users can try different generators without separate accounts. Features such as Mystic produce detailed realistic images, and style and character reference tools help keep a consistent look across a project. Because it connects to the stock library, you can combine AI-generated and traditional assets in a design.

Freepik has a free tier with limited generations and paid plans with credits and commercial rights, bundled with downloads from the stock library. The mixture of AI and stock content makes licensing important, so check the terms for each asset and generation. Compared with Midjourney or FLUX directly, you get convenience and variety at the cost of credit limits. Canva, Adobe Firefly, and Leonardo.ai offer similar mixes of design resources and AI.
