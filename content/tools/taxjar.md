TaxJar tackles the complexity of U.S. sales tax for online businesses. Rules differ by state, county, and city, and a seller can owe tax in many places once they pass certain thresholds. TaxJar connects to selling channels such as Shopify, Amazon, WooCommerce, and BigCommerce, pulls in sales data, calculates the tax owed, and shows where the business has obligations. Reports summarize what is due in each jurisdiction.

The service can also file and remit returns in many states for an additional fee, which saves sellers from dealing with each state's portal. An API provides tax calculations at checkout for platforms and developers. It was acquired by Stripe, which has been building its own tax product, Stripe Tax, and TaxJar continues to operate and support filing, while its role is evolving alongside Stripe's tools.

TaxJar is paid, with plans by transaction volume and pricing for filing. Sales tax obligations depend on facts like where you ship and where you have a presence, so businesses with unusual setups benefit from an accountant. Alternatives include Avalara, Anrok for software sellers, Kintsugi, Zamp, and Stripe Tax. Check the current relationship between TaxJar and Stripe Tax when choosing, as packaging may change. TaxJar suits small and mid-sized online sellers.
