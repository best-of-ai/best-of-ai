HiringNow watches hundreds of thousands of employer career sites and more than 65 applicant tracking systems, including Greenhouse, Workday, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters, and it rechecks them about every five minutes. It matches openings to your profile with a score and tailors your resume for each role.

Nothing is sent without your sign-off. You approve, edit or skip each application, and receipts record the posting time, the apply time and the exact content that was sent. Rules let you set roles, locations and companies to avoid. AI mock interviews, interview prep, an ATS resume checker, a resume builder, a cover letter generator and a job tracker are included.

The free plan allows a small one-time batch of auto-applies with no card. The paid Pro, Autopilot and Autopilot Max plans raise the monthly auto-apply limits, and quarterly billing is discounted. Applying in volume doesn't guarantee interviews, so the approval step is worth using to keep applications targeted.
