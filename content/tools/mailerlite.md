MailerLite offers the core tools of email marketing without clutter. Users build a list, design emails with a drag-and-drop editor, set up automations that respond to subscriber behavior, and publish landing pages and sign-up forms. Websites and simple stores can also be built within the platform.

Features include segmentation, A/B testing, an AI writing assistant, and e-commerce integrations with Shopify and WooCommerce. Analytics show opens, clicks, and revenue. Paid subscription newsletters are supported, which has drawn creators.

MailerLite has a free plan for small lists and paid tiers as the list grows. It has fewer advanced features than enterprise tools. Alternatives include Mailchimp, Kit, Brevo, Beehiiv, and ActiveCampaign. MailerLite suits small businesses and creators who want straightforward email marketing.
