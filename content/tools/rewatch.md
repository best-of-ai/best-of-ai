Rewatch is built on the idea that useful knowledge is trapped in recordings no one rewatches. It captures meetings and async videos, transcribes them, and organizes them into channels and playlists, so employees can find the moment they need by searching words said.

Features include summaries, chapters, comments on moments in the video, and integrations with Zoom, Slack, and calendars. The goal is to let people catch up on meetings quickly and reduce the number of live sessions.

Independent information about the product's current status is limited, so check the official site before relying on it. Alternatives include Loom, Fireflies, Otter, Grain, and Notion AI meeting notes. Rewatch suits teams that record many meetings and want them searchable.
