Teachers create classes, share materials from Drive, and grade submissions. Students see due dates and turn in work. Gemini features help teachers draft lesson plans and quiz questions in education plans.

It is free for Google Workspace for Education accounts and works well with Docs and Meet.

Canvas and Schoology are fuller learning management systems. Google Classroom suits educators who already use Google tools.
