MosMos is a dictation and meeting tool for Mac. A user speaks and text appears in the active app, formatted to fit the context: casual in a chat, structured in a document, code-friendly in an editor. A personal glossary learns names and technical terms, reducing repeated mistakes.

In meetings, MosMos can record and transcribe with labels for each speaker and produce notes and action items afterward. Combining dictation and meeting features in one app saves switching between tools.

Independent information about MosMos is limited, so check its privacy approach and whether processing happens locally or in the cloud. It has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Superwhisper, Wispr Flow, MacWhisper, Granola, and Apple dictation. MosMos suits Mac users who dictate and attend many meetings.
