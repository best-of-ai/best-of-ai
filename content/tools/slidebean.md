Slidebean separates writing from design. You enter text for each slide in an outline-style editor, choose a slide type such as problem, solution, market, or team, and the software lays it out, selecting fonts, spacing, and graphics so the result looks professional. That means founders can focus on the message and avoid spending hours on layout. AI features help draft slide content and improve wording.

Because it was built for startups, Slidebean adds extras beyond slides. It offers pitch deck templates based on successful funding decks, tools to share decks and see who viewed them, and a financial model builder for projections. The company has also offered pitch deck design and fundraising services for founders who want expert help.

Slidebean has a free option with limits and paid plans for more decks, branding, and analytics. The automatic design is efficient but gives less flexibility than a manual tool, and templates can feel similar. Gamma, Pitch, and Beautiful.ai are popular alternatives with different design styles, Canva offers broad flexibility, and Google Slides and PowerPoint remain the defaults. Slidebean is a fit for early-stage founders who need a good-looking pitch deck quickly.
