SoftSmile makes VISION, orthodontic planning software that brings scan segmentation, diagnostics, tooth setup, and case management into one place. The AI proposes a setup and the orthodontist adjusts it.

The software finds collisions, optimizes the plan, and prepares 3D models for production, which helps practices that make their own aligners or send cases to a lab.

SoftSmile is sold to practices and labs. Time-saving figures come from the company, so test it on your own cases. Alternatives include uLab Systems, 3Shape Ortho System, and Invisalign ClinCheck. SoftSmile suits orthodontists who want AI-assisted setups with control over each step.
