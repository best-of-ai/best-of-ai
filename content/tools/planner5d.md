Planner 5D lets anyone design interiors. A user draws walls and rooms, adds furniture from a large catalog, and views the result in 3D from any angle or as a realistic render. It is aimed at homeowners and hobbyists planning remodels, with simple controls.

AI features generate design suggestions from a room photo, restyle spaces, and build floor plans from sketches. A community gallery provides projects to copy and adapt. Mobile and web apps share projects.

Planner 5D has a free version with limited items and paid plans with more content and higher quality renders. It is not a substitute for professional drawings that meet building codes. Alternatives include SketchUp, Roomstyler, HomeByMe, Collov AI, and Floorplanner. Planner 5D suits homeowners planning room layouts.
