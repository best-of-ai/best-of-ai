Fliz focuses on one-click video creation. A user pastes a link or text, picks a style and voice, and Fliz writes a script if needed, assembles scenes, adds captions and music, and renders a video. The goal is a finished piece that can go straight to social media or a website.

Typical uses are turning blog posts into short videos, creating product videos for e-commerce, and producing news-style clips. Editing tools allow changes to visuals, text, and voices, and the platform supports many languages. Automation features generate videos in bulk from feeds.

Fliz has a free tier with limits and paid plans. One-click output is a first draft, and brand-specific polish needs manual work. Alternatives include Pictory, Fliki, InVideo, Lumen5, and HeyGen. Fliz suits marketers who need a steady stream of short videos.
