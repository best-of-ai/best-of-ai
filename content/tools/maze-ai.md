Maze helps product teams learn from users. Designers import a prototype from Figma, set tasks for participants, and share a link to run a test that records clicks, paths, and success rates. Surveys, card sorts, and interview studies are supported as well, and a panel service can recruit participants.

AI speeds up analysis. It transcribes sessions, pulls out themes and quotes, and writes summaries of what the data shows, so teams spend less time sorting through recordings. Reports can be shared with stakeholders.

Maze has a free plan with limits and paid plans for teams. Test results depend on good task design and participant quality. Alternatives include UserTesting, Lyssna, Optimal Workshop, Dovetail, and Useberry. Maze suits product designers and researchers who test often.
