Appen is one of the long-standing companies behind AI training data. Machine learning models need large amounts of labeled examples, such as transcribed speech, tagged images, ranked answers, and annotated documents, and Appen supplies them through a global crowd of contributors plus managed teams. Its customers have included major technology companies, and its work has fed search, voice assistants, and recommendation systems.

The services now lean toward generative AI. Appen provides data for aligning large language models, such as human preference rankings, expert-written examples, and red-teaming to find unsafe behavior. It also offers multimodal and speech datasets, coding data, and model evaluation services that test how a system performs in many languages and regions. A platform manages the workflows, quality checks, and contributor payments.

Appen works on a project basis with custom pricing, selling to enterprises and AI labs rather than individuals. The data labeling industry faces scrutiny over pay and working conditions for contributors, and the business has been affected as large customers moved some work in-house or to competitors. Scale AI, Surge AI, Labelbox, and Toloka compete in the same space. Buyers typically weigh data quality, language coverage, and security requirements when choosing a vendor.
