Kindroid lets users create a companion from scratch. You write the character's background and personality, choose how they look using generated images, and give them a voice, and then talk by text or voice calls. The app emphasizes memory, so the character can recall details from earlier conversations, and users can adjust how the AI responds by editing the character's profile and diary.

Other features include group chats with several characters, image generation of the character in various scenes, and selfie sharing. Users value the high level of control and the more natural conversation compared with simpler chatbots. Some use it for roleplay and storytelling, others for companionship or practicing conversation.

Kindroid has a free tier with limits and paid plans that unlock more messages, voice, and advanced models. As with other companion apps, there are concerns about emotional dependence, privacy of intimate conversations, and content safety, and it is not a substitute for human relationships or professional mental health care. Alternatives include Replika, Nomi, Character.AI, and Chai. Kindroid suits adults who want a highly customizable AI companion.
