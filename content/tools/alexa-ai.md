Alexa popularized the smart speaker. You speak to an Echo device to play music, set timers, control lights, check weather, shop on Amazon, and ask questions. Over the years, it grew into a platform with thousands of skills from third parties and support for a huge range of smart home devices. It's built into many speakers, TVs, cars, and appliances.

Alexa+ is the next generation. Built on large language models, including Amazon's own Nova and models from other providers, it holds more natural conversations, understands context across a dialogue, and can handle multi-step requests such as planning an evening, booking a reservation, or coordinating a family calendar. It can act across services, remember preferences, and work with smart home devices more flexibly, and a web and app experience complements the voice interface. Some promised features arrived later than first shown.

Standard Alexa is free on compatible devices. Alexa+ is included for Prime members and available for a monthly fee otherwise, with regional availability still expanding. Privacy is a consideration, since voice recordings may be stored and reviewed, with controls to manage them. Competitors include Google Assistant and Gemini on Android and Nest devices, Apple's Siri, and ChatGPT's voice mode. Alexa suits people invested in Amazon's devices and smart home ecosystem.
