ServiceNow began as an IT service management tool and became a general workflow platform for large organizations. Employees and customers submit requests, such as a laptop problem, a payroll question, or a customer complaint, and the platform routes, tracks, and resolves them using defined workflows. Companies use it as a system of record and action across IT operations, HR, legal, security, and customer service.

AI is central to its current strategy. Now Assist adds generative AI to summarize cases, draft replies, and help agents find answers, and AI agents can take on tasks such as triaging incidents and fulfilling requests with minimal human intervention. The company acquired Moveworks, whose employee assistant is intended to become the front door to the platform, and offers tools to govern and monitor agents across the enterprise.

ServiceNow is sold on enterprise subscriptions with significant implementation work, and pricing is high and based on modules and users. It's powerful but complex, and benefits depend on process maturity. Competitors include Atlassian Jira Service Management, Salesforce Service Cloud and Agentforce, Microsoft Dynamics and Copilot Studio, BMC, and Freshservice. ServiceNow suits large organizations standardizing workflows on one platform.
