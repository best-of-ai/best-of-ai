MarketMan is back-of-house software for food service. Operators record what they buy and use, build recipes with ingredient costs, and see the cost of each dish. When prices change or usage varies, the system flags it, helping managers protect margins.

Ordering and invoicing are handled in one place. Staff place orders with suppliers, receive deliveries, and match invoices to what arrived, which catches errors and short shipments. Forecasting tools predict how much to order based on sales history, and integrations connect with point of sale and accounting systems.

MarketMan is paid, with plans by location. Accurate counts take staff discipline. Alternatives include Restaurant365, BlueCart, Toast inventory, Apicbase, and xtraCHEF (Toast). MarketMan suits restaurants and groups managing food costs.
