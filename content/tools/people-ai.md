People.ai solves the data entry problem in sales. Instead of relying on reps to log everything, it connects to email, calendars, and phone systems and records contacts, meetings, and conversations, then matches them to the right accounts and opportunities in the CRM. That keeps records current and gives managers a more complete picture of how engaged each deal is.

On top of the data, it provides analytics and AI. Dashboards show activity, engagement with key contacts, and whether deals are at risk, and forecasting tools estimate which will close. AI agents can summarize accounts, answer questions about the pipeline in natural language, and prepare reps for meetings. The aim is to give sales leaders a reliable view without chasing updates.

People.ai is sold to enterprises on custom contracts, and integrates mainly with Salesforce. It requires access to employees' communications, which raises privacy and change-management issues, and results depend on data quality. Competitors include Clari, Gong, Salesforce's own Einstein tools, Outreach, and Aviso. Companies often compare how well each product handles forecasting, rep adoption, and integration with their CRM. It suits mid-sized and large sales organizations on Salesforce.
