Latitude makes interactive fiction with AI. Its best-known product, AI Dungeon, lets players describe any action in natural language, and an AI game master responds with the next part of the story. The result is open-ended storytelling that can go in any direction, from fantasy quests to comedy and mystery.

The studio has experimented with other AI-driven games and with platforms for creators who want to build their own AI-powered worlds. It uses a mix of language models, including models it trains and fine-tunes, and offers different tiers of models to subscribers.

AI Dungeon has a free tier with limits and paid plans for stronger models and longer memory. Generated stories can wander or contradict themselves, and the company has had to handle content moderation and privacy questions along the way. Alternatives include NovelAI, Character.AI, Hidden Door, and Sudowrite for writing. Latitude suits fans of interactive AI storytelling.
