Sembly joins your online meetings, records them, and produces a transcript with a structured summary. The summary highlights the main points, tasks, decisions, and risks mentioned, so teams can see what needs follow-up without rewatching. It also supports in-person meetings through its mobile app and uploads of audio files, and works in many languages.

Beyond single meetings, Sembly helps organize knowledge. Workspaces and meeting libraries let teams search past conversations and ask questions across them, and templates adjust the notes for different meeting types such as sales calls or interviews. Integrations push summaries and tasks into Slack, Notion, Asana, Jira, HubSpot, and other tools. Analytics track patterns like meeting time and topics.

Sembly has a free plan with limited meetings and paid plans for more history, features, and team administration. Its summaries depend on audio quality and, like all AI notes, should be checked for accuracy. People in meetings should know they are recorded. Competitors include Fireflies, Otter, Fathom, MeetGeek, and Avoma. Sembly suits small teams that want automated notes and task extraction.
