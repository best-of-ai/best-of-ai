Draw.io is a free tool that handles a surprising range of diagrams. It offers shape libraries for flowcharts, UML, entity relationships, network and cloud architecture, floor plans, and more, with smart connectors and alignment guides that keep drawings tidy. Because there is no account requirement, you can open it, draw, and save without signing up. Many software teams use it for documentation diagrams.

A defining feature is where your files live. You can save diagrams to your device, to Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, GitHub, or GitLab, instead of a vendor's server, which suits organizations with strict data rules. Diagrams export to PNG, SVG, PDF, and other formats, and can be embedded in Confluence, Jira, Notion, and other tools through plugins. You can also create diagrams from text by inserting Mermaid or PlantUML code.

The software is open source, free to use, and also available as a desktop application for Windows, macOS, and Linux. It has no built-in team commenting and real-time collaboration comparable to Miro or Lucidchart, unless you pair it with a service that offers it. Its interface looks dated, and advanced layouts take manual work. Lucidchart, Creately, and Miro offer more collaboration and AI diagram generation for a fee, and Mermaid and PlantUML serve people who prefer diagrams as code.
