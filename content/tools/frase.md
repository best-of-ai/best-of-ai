Frase helps content teams plan and write articles that can rank. Enter a keyword, and Frase analyzes the top search results to show the topics, questions, and headings they cover and the terms they use, then creates a brief that guides a writer. As you draft, a score shows how well your article covers the topic compared with competitors, and suggestions help fill gaps.

AI features support writing and optimization. Frase can generate outlines and drafts, answer questions drawn from search data, and update existing articles to improve their performance. Newer tools track visibility in AI-generated answers from assistants and search engines, reflecting the shift in how people find information, and an agent can handle parts of the content workflow. Integrations with Google Search Console and publishing tools connect data to action.

Frase is paid, with plans based on the number of articles or documents, and a trial. Like other optimization tools, following a content score does not guarantee rankings, and drafts need human editing for accuracy and originality. Alternatives include Surfer, Clearscope, MarketMuse, Semrush's content tools, and general assistants. Frase suits SEO teams and content marketers who produce briefs and optimized articles regularly.
