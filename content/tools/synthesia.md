Synthesia lets companies produce videos without cameras, studios, or actors. A user writes or pastes a script, selects an AI avatar from a library of presenters or creates a custom one, and the platform generates a video with natural speech and lip movement. Scenes, slides, screen recordings, and media can be combined, and editing the script and regenerating takes the place of a reshoot.

The platform is built for business use. It supports a large number of languages, so one video can be localized quickly, and offers templates, brand kits, and collaboration features. Interactive and agent-style avatars can respond to viewers, and an API lets teams automate video creation. Enterprise features cover security, compliance, and consent controls, since custom avatars require permission from the person depicted. Companies commonly use it for learning and development, onboarding, product explainers, and sales content.

Synthesia has a free trial, paid plans by video minutes and features, and enterprise contracts. Avatars have become far more realistic, but they can still feel slightly artificial, so audiences may respond better to real people for emotional content. Disclose AI-generated presenters where trust matters. HeyGen is the main competitor, Colossyan serves learning teams, D-ID and Hour One offer other avatar options, and Runway and Sora-style models generate different kinds of video. Synthesia is a leader in corporate avatar video.
