Synthesia is the enterprise-grade end of AI video production. The avatar quality is notably high — lip sync is accurate, expressions are varied, and the presenter gestures look natural rather than mechanical. You write a script, select an avatar and voice, add slides or a background, and Synthesia produces a polished MP4. No camera, studio, lighting, or video editing skills required. For L&D teams producing dozens of training modules or comms departments that need regular internal updates, the time saving is substantial.

The 160+ avatar library covers different ethnicities, ages, and presenter styles — from formal business presenters to casual instructors. Personal AI avatar allows you to record a short video of yourself and create a digital avatar that looks and sounds exactly like you, which is used by executives and founders for scale communication. The 120+ language support means the same script can be delivered in French, Mandarin, Spanish, or Portuguese without re-recording or hiring voice talent.

Synthesia's Starter plan starts at $22/month for 10 minutes of video per month — enough for evaluation but limited for production use. Creator at $67/month gives 30 minutes; Enterprise plans are negotiated for higher volume. The API allows integration into content management systems or learning platforms for automated video generation. Synthesia is most commonly adopted by L&D teams in large organisations, HR for onboarding content, marketing for product explainers, and customer success for training libraries — anywhere that video has high production value and recurring demand.
