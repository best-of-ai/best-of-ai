Sight Machine tackles the data mess inside factories. Machines, sensors, and systems produce data in many formats, and plants often cannot easily combine it. Sight Machine's platform pulls in data from equipment, quality systems, and enterprise software, standardizes it into a common model of the production process, and creates a digital twin that shows how products move through the plant.

With that foundation, teams can analyze performance. Dashboards track throughput, yield, scrap, and downtime across lines and sites, machine learning finds the factors that drive defects or slowdowns, and alerts flag issues in near real time. An AI assistant lets operators and engineers ask questions about production data in plain language, and the platform supports sustainability tracking such as energy use.

Sight Machine is sold to large manufacturers on enterprise subscriptions, with implementations that require work to connect and model plant data. Results depend on data quality and the willingness to change processes. Competitors include AVEVA, Siemens, Rockwell Automation, Tulip, Augury for maintenance, and cloud platforms from AWS, Microsoft, and Google. Sight Machine suits manufacturers looking to analyze performance across plants.
