Tencent Cloud ADP gives companies tools to create agents. Users connect knowledge bases for retrieval, design workflows with a visual editor, and combine agents that work together. Models include Tencent's Hunyuan family and others.

Typical applications are customer service, internal assistants, and process automation. The platform includes tools for testing and monitoring and integrates with Tencent's ecosystem, including WeChat and enterprise messaging.

It is sold on usage-based pricing within Tencent Cloud. Outside China, availability and support are more limited, and organizations must consider data residency. Alternatives include Alibaba Cloud's Bailian, Baidu Qianfan, ByteDance's Volcano Engine, Dify, and AWS Bedrock Agents. Tencent Cloud ADP suits companies building agents on Tencent Cloud.
