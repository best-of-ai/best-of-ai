Born to Found guides first-time founders through the early journey. It divides company building into stages, such as validating an idea, building a first product, and finding customers, and supplies playbooks and checklists for each, so a founder knows what to focus on at their point.

An AI coach answers questions and reviews work, drawing on the founder's stage and project details. This offers a low-cost alternative to mentors for people who lack access to experienced advisors, and a structure to keep going when the path is unclear.

Advice from an AI coach is generic by nature, and startup outcomes depend on specifics that a tool cannot see. Treat it as a starting framework and check ideas with real customers and mentors. Born to Found has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Y Combinator's Startup School, Techstars resources, Lenny's Newsletter, and general assistants like ChatGPT and Claude. Born to Found suits early-stage founders who want structure.
