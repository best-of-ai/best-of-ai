Beamery treats talent like a relationship, not a one-off hire. Recruiters build pools of potential candidates, track interactions, run campaigns to stay in touch, and see who might fit upcoming roles. The same system can surface current employees for internal moves, which helps companies fill jobs from within.

Its AI builds around skills. It infers skills from resumes and profiles, maps them to a company's roles, and recommends matches for jobs and development. Workforce planning tools help leaders see skill gaps and plan hiring or training. Integrations connect with applicant tracking systems such as Workday and SuccessFactors.

Beamery is sold to large enterprises on custom contracts. It requires data quality and change management to deliver value, and AI in hiring raises concerns about bias that regulations increasingly address. Alternatives include Eightfold, Phenom, Gem, SmartRecruiters, and HiredScore. Beamery suits large employers building talent pipelines.
