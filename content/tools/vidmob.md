Vidmob focuses on the creative side of advertising. Instead of generating ads, it analyzes them. Its models score creative before it runs and break down which elements, such as visuals, messaging, and formats, are linked to better results after launch.

Features include Creative Scoring, Creative Analytics, and a Creative Data API for exporting results into other systems. A separate Influencer Intelligence product applies similar analysis to creator content. The platform uses a large set of proprietary models, and it serves brands, agencies, and in-house creative and media teams.

Vidmob does not publish pricing and sells through demos. It suits teams with enough ad volume to learn from patterns across campaigns. AdCreative.ai and Smartly are neighbors in the ad tooling space, with a stronger focus on generating and managing ads.
