Jobscan helps people tailor resumes to individual jobs. A user pastes in a resume and a job posting, and Jobscan reports a match score, lists keywords and skills from the posting that are missing or underused, and points out formatting problems that could trip up automated parsers.

Additional tools include a LinkedIn optimization feature, cover letter checks, and AI that suggests wording and rewrites sections. A job tracker helps organize applications. The service is popular with career coaches and people applying to large companies that use applicant tracking systems.

Jobscan has a limited free tier and paid plans. A high score does not guarantee an interview, and stuffing keywords unnaturally can backfire with human readers. Alternatives include Resume Worded, Enhancv, Teal, Rezi, and Kickresume. Jobscan suits job seekers tailoring resumes to specific postings.
