Hippocratic AI focuses on non-diagnostic work in healthcare. Its agents call patients to check on recovery after discharge, remind them to take medication, prepare them for procedures, and monitor chronic conditions. The calls are long, patient conversations that clinical staff rarely have time to make.

Safety is the stated priority. The company tests its models with clinicians and designs the agents to stay within defined roles, escalating to a nurse or doctor when a patient reports symptoms that need attention. Health systems deploy them to extend staff capacity and reach patients between visits.

Hippocratic AI is sold to health systems and payers on custom contracts. Patients should know when they are speaking with an AI, and clinical oversight remains necessary. Alternatives include Assort Health, Qventus, Notable, Infinitus, and Care.ai. Hippocratic AI suits healthcare organizations scaling patient outreach.
