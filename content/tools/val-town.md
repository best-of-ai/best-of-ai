Val Town makes small bits of server code easy. A developer writes a val, such as an HTTP endpoint, a cron job, or an email handler, and it runs immediately without setup. Vals can be forked, shared, and combined, giving the platform a social feel.

It has become a place for quick scripts, bots, and glue code, and for experiments with AI. An assistant helps write and edit vals, and a library of examples helps newcomers.

Val Town has a free tier and paid plans. It suits small tasks more than large applications. Alternatives include Cloudflare Workers, Deno Deploy, Replit, Glitch (now closed), and Pipedream. Val Town suits developers who want quick hosted scripts.
