Docusign made electronic signatures routine. Senders upload a document, specify signers and fields, and recipients sign on any device, with an audit trail and legal validity in many countries. It's used for sales contracts, HR paperwork, real estate, and any agreement that once needed a pen, and it integrates with Salesforce, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and many business systems.

The company has expanded to manage the whole agreement lifecycle. Its Intelligent Agreement Management platform includes tools to prepare and negotiate agreements, a repository that stores them, and AI that reads contracts to extract terms such as renewal dates, obligations, and parties. Its AI can summarize documents, answer questions about a contract portfolio, and flag risks, and workflow tools automate steps such as approvals and routing.

Docusign is sold by subscription in tiers, and add-ons for advanced features cost extra. AI-extracted terms and summaries should be reviewed by people, especially for legal and financial matters. Alternatives include Adobe Acrobat Sign, Dropbox Sign, PandaDoc, Ironclad for contract management, and simple signing tools built into other platforms. Docusign suits organizations that handle a large volume of agreements.
