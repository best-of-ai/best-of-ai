Climate FieldView helps farmers use their data. With a device plugged into farm equipment, FieldView Drive collects information about planting, applications, and harvest, and the data syncs to the cloud, where farmers view maps of their fields, compare hybrids and practices, and analyze yield. Satellite imagery tracks crop health during the season, and weather data and field notes add context.

Advanced features support planning. Variable-rate prescriptions tell equipment to apply seed and fertilizer at different rates across a field based on zones, which can improve efficiency, and scripts or models estimate the profitability of decisions. Integrations with equipment and other agriculture software reduce manual data transfers. Machine learning supports yield predictions and recommendations.

FieldView offers a free tier with basic features and paid plans that add advanced analytics and prescriptions. It belongs to Bayer, so some farmers consider data ownership and privacy when deciding whether to use it. Alternatives include John Deere Operations Center, Granular from Corteva, Trimble Ag, Farmers Edge, and Cropwise. Climate FieldView suits row crop farmers who want to analyze performance and plan inputs.
