DISCO helps legal teams handle the huge volumes of documents produced in lawsuits and investigations. Teams upload email, files, and chats, search and tag them, and decide what is relevant and privileged. The platform runs in the cloud, so reviewers can work together without managing servers.

AI features reduce manual review. Models rank documents by likely relevance, so reviewers see the important ones first, and generative tools summarize documents, answer questions about a set, and help build timelines. Law firms and corporate legal departments use it to cut review costs and speed up cases.

DISCO is a public company selling by project or subscription. Lawyers remain responsible for the quality of review, and courts expect defensible processes when using AI. Alternatives include Relativity, Everlaw, Reveal, Logikcull (now part of Reveal), and Nextpoint. DISCO suits law firms and legal departments running e-discovery.
