MySpec starts with the plan instead of the code. A user describes a product or feature, and the platform produces a specification covering requirements, architecture, and tasks. That document guides the build, whether a human or an AI coding agent does the work.

The idea responds to a common problem: coding agents given vague prompts produce inconsistent results. A clear spec gives the agent a target and gives the team something to review before time is spent. It fits the approach seen in tools like Kiro and GitHub's Spec Kit.

Independent information about MySpec is limited, and specs need to be reviewed by people who understand the product. It has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Kiro, GitHub Spec Kit, Traycer, Linear with AI, and writing specs in Notion with an assistant. MySpec suits teams adopting spec-first workflows with AI agents.
