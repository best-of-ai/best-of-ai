Prefect lets developers turn Python functions into reliable workflows. By adding decorators, a script becomes a flow with tasks that retry on failure, run on a schedule, and report state. Teams view runs, logs, and results in a dashboard.

It supports dynamic workflows, concurrency, and deployment to different infrastructure, from a laptop to Kubernetes. A hosted cloud service handles scheduling and observability, and the open-source server can be self-hosted. It is used for data pipelines and machine learning jobs.

Prefect has a free tier and paid plans. It competes in a busy field, and teams choose it for its Python-first approach. Alternatives include Airflow, Dagster, Temporal, Metaflow, and Flyte. Prefect suits data teams that want modern, Pythonic orchestration.
