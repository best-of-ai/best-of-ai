Muse Image brings Meta's own image model to its apps. Users describe an image in the Meta AI app, or create pictures in Instagram Stories and WhatsApp, and the model generates it. Meta says the model reasons about a prompt before drawing, rather than mapping text directly to pixels, which helps with complex requests.

Meta plans to extend it to more surfaces, including Facebook, Messenger, and tools for advertisers. Because it is built into apps with billions of users, it makes image creation available to people who would never open a dedicated generator.

Muse Image is free to use within Meta's apps, subject to limits. Generated images carry labels or markers under Meta's policies for AI content, and rules restrict some content. Alternatives include GPT Image, Google's Imagen and Nano Banana, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, and Flux. Muse Image suits casual creators within Meta's apps.
