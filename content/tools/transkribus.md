Transkribus is built for historical documents. Archivists and researchers upload scans of handwritten or old printed material, and the platform transcribes them with models that can be trained on a specific hand or period. The result is searchable text that can be corrected and exported.

It grew from European research projects and is now run by a cooperative, READ-COOP. Thousands of archives, libraries, and genealogists use it, and public models cover many languages and eras. AI search finds words across collections.

Transkribus uses a credit system with free credits and paid plans. Accuracy depends on handwriting and training data, and proofreading is needed for scholarly work. Alternatives include eScriptorium, Google Document AI, Azure Document Intelligence, and Ancestry's tools. Transkribus suits archivists, historians, and genealogists.
