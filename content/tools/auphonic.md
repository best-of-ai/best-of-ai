Auphonic automates the finishing steps of audio production. You upload a recording, such as a podcast episode or interview, and it applies adaptive leveling to even out volume differences between speakers, normalizes loudness to the standards used by streaming and broadcast, reduces background noise and hum, and applies filtering to improve clarity. The result is consistent, professional sounding audio with little manual work.

It supports multitrack recordings, where each person is recorded separately, and balances them automatically. Additional features include speech recognition to create transcripts, chapter marks, and exports to podcast hosts and cloud storage, and an API allows batch processing. Presets and a web interface make it approachable for non-engineers, and it's used by independent podcasters, radio stations, and educators.

Auphonic offers a free monthly allowance of processing time and paid plans or credits for more. It automates mastering tasks, but it doesn't edit content or remove mistakes, so it complements an editor like Audacity or Descript. Alternatives include Adobe Podcast Enhance Speech, Cleanvoice, Descript's Studio Sound, Podcastle, and Riverside's audio tools. Auphonic suits podcasters who want consistent loudness and noise cleanup without becoming audio engineers.
