Headspace is one of the best-known meditation apps, with a friendly style and a structured approach for beginners. It offers guided meditations of different lengths, sleep content, movement and breathing exercises, focus music, and courses on topics such as stress, anxiety, and relationships. Short daily sessions are designed to build a habit, and progress features encourage consistency.

The company has expanded beyond meditation into broader mental healthcare. Depending on the market and plan, users can access coaching and therapy services, and employers offer Headspace as a benefit. An AI companion, Ebb, provides conversational support for reflecting on feelings and finding relevant exercises. It is meant to support wellbeing, not to diagnose or treat conditions, and Headspace points users in crisis toward professional help.

Headspace offers a free trial and then requires a subscription, with individual, family, and student plans. Evidence suggests that meditation apps can help with stress and sleep for many people, with modest effects, and that they are not a replacement for treatment of depression, anxiety disorders, or trauma. Calm is the closest competitor with more focus on sleep and relaxation, Insight Timer offers a huge free library, and Ten Percent Happier has a teacher-led approach. Headspace suits people new to meditation.
