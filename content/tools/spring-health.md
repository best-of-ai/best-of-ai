Spring Health provides employer-sponsored mental health care. Employees take an assessment, and the system recommends a path, from self-guided tools to coaching, therapy, or psychiatric care, with a care navigator to help. Providers are drawn from its network.

AI and measurement-based care play a role in matching and tracking progress. The company states that it collects outcomes data to adjust care. It serves employers, health plans, and other organizations.

Anyone in crisis should reach emergency services or a crisis line. Spring Health is sold to organizations, not directly to individuals. Alternatives include Lyra Health, Headspace Health, Talkspace, Modern Health, and Teladoc. Spring Health suits employees whose employers offer it.
