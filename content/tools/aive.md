AIVE was described as a video enhancement service that used deep-learning super-resolution to sharpen detail, reduce noise, and raise the resolution of existing footage. That kind of tool takes low-resolution or damaged video, such as old family tapes, archival film, or compressed web clips, and reconstructs a clearer version. The idea is sound, and a number of products do it well.

The domain listed for AIVE no longer hosts a video enhancement tool. It now shows an unrelated media site, and searches turn up no active product of this name with the features described. A separate company called Aive makes a platform for automating video post-production for brands, which is a different kind of product. Because the original service cannot be confirmed, this entry should not be used as a recommendation.

For video upscaling and restoration, active products include Topaz Video AI, which is widely used for sharpening and frame interpolation, and AVCLabs Video Enhancer. DaVinci Resolve Studio includes a super scale feature, and Adobe's Premiere Pro offers enhancement tools for speech and some footage fixes. Results depend heavily on the source quality: AI can add believable detail but cannot recover information that was never recorded.
