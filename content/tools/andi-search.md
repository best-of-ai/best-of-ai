Andi is a search engine for people who find link lists cluttered. You ask a question, and it returns a short answer with cited sources, plus links you can open. The idea is to combine the answering ability of a language model with the freshness of the web.

Privacy and design are part of the pitch. Andi has said it does not rely on advertising or sell user data, and the interface is clean, with options to refine a query and explore related questions. It is one of several startups, along with Perplexity, You.com, and Phind, that took on the conventional search model.

Andi is free to use. As a small company, it competes with far larger rivals, and generated answers can still be wrong, so checking the cited pages is wise. Features and availability may change. Alternatives include Perplexity, Kagi, You.com, Brave Search with AI answers, and DuckDuckGo. Andi suits users who want a simple, ad-free answer engine.
