EnergyCAP collects utility bills and meter data and puts them into one system. Facility managers, energy consultants, and sustainability teams use it to track electricity, gas, water, and other utilities across a portfolio, pay bills, and report on costs and emissions.

Analytics find savings. The software flags bills with errors, such as duplicate charges or wrong rates, and highlights usage that departs from normal patterns, which may point to equipment problems. Benchmarking compares buildings, and reports support sustainability disclosures and incentive programs.

EnergyCAP is sold by subscription to schools, governments, commercial property owners, and consultants. Value depends on accurate data and follow-through on findings. Alternatives include Urjanet for data, UtilityAPI, Schneider Electric Resource Advisor, Arcadia, and Measurabl. EnergyCAP suits organizations managing utility costs at scale.
