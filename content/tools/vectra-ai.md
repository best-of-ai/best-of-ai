Vectra focuses on attacker behavior inside a network. Its platform watches traffic, identities, and cloud logs, uses machine learning to spot patterns such as lateral movement or privilege abuse, and prioritizes the few detections that matter among thousands of alerts.

Security teams use it for threat detection and investigation across on-premises, cloud, and software-as-a-service environments, with integrations into SIEM and response tools. Managed services provide round-the-clock monitoring.

Vectra is sold to enterprises on subscription. Alert quality depends on tuning and coverage. Alternatives include Darktrace, CrowdStrike, Microsoft Defender XDR, ExtraHop, and Palo Alto Cortex. Vectra AI suits security operations teams.
