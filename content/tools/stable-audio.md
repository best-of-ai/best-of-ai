Stable Audio generates sound from descriptions. A user types a prompt such as an upbeat electronic track with a driving bass line, and the model produces a piece of the requested length. It can also create sound effects and ambient textures.

The models were trained on licensed audio, which Stability emphasizes to address rights concerns. Producers and sound designers use it for sketching ideas, backing tracks, and game audio. Smaller open models can run locally.

Stable Audio has a free tier with limits and paid plans. Output quality and structure are less predictable than in services built mainly for full songs with vocals. Alternatives include Suno, Udio, ElevenLabs music, Soundful, and Mubert. Stable Audio suits creators needing instrumentals and effects.
