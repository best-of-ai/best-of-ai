Stable Audio generates music that sounds produced — not loops or simple MIDI-like compositions, but tracks with arrangement structure, instrument variation, and dynamic development over time. The prompt interface accepts detailed descriptions: "dark cinematic orchestral music with low strings and building tension, 130 BPM, for a thriller film" produces something meaningfully different from "upbeat lo-fi hip hop with mellow keys and background rain." The model handles genre diversity well, covering electronic, classical, jazz, ambient, folk, and contemporary pop styles.

The use case that makes Stable Audio particularly practical for content creators is royalty-free background music. Video producers, podcast creators, and online educators who need background music for their content regularly spend time licensing tracks or searching royalty-free libraries with limited selection. Generating custom audio that fits a specific mood and duration solves this without negotiating licences or paying per-use fees. Sound effect generation covers atmospheric backgrounds, foley-style sounds, and transition audio that would otherwise come from sound libraries.

Stable Audio is available through the web at stableaudio.com. The free tier provides a limited number of generations per month at 45-second maximum duration. Pro at $11.99/month increases the limit to 3-minute tracks and provides more monthly generations with commercial usage rights. The model is also available through Stability AI's API for developers building audio generation into products. Musicians using it as a composition aid — generating a starting point they develop further in a DAW — represent one segment of users alongside content creators who use the output directly.
