Quizgecko speeds up quiz writing. A user pastes text, uploads a file, or links to a page, chooses a question type and difficulty, and the AI generates a set of questions with answers. Quizzes can be edited, shared with a link, embedded, or exported to learning platforms.

Teachers use it for classroom quizzes and practice tests, trainers for compliance checks, and students for self-testing. Flashcards and other study formats are available, and analytics show how learners performed.

Quizgecko has a free tier and paid plans. Generated questions need review for accuracy and fair wording, since AI can produce ambiguous items or wrong answers. Alternatives include Quizlet, Kahoot, Conker, ClassMarker, and ChatGPT with prompts. Quizgecko suits educators who want fast quiz creation.
