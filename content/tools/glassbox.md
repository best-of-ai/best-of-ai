Glassbox captures user sessions on digital properties. Teams can replay a visit to see what a customer clicked, where they hesitated, and what errors they hit. Heatmaps summarize click and scroll behavior across many sessions, pointing to confusing pages or broken flows.

The platform adds analytics and detection. Journey analysis shows where users leave a funnel, and automatic detection flags frustration signals such as repeated clicks. It has features for regulated industries like banking, including data masking so that sensitive information is not stored. The company has expanded through acquisitions into areas like anomaly detection.

Glassbox is sold on enterprise subscriptions, and is privately owned. Session recording raises privacy concerns, so organizations must mask sensitive data and follow consent rules. Alternatives include Contentsquare, Quantum Metric, FullStory, Hotjar, and Heap. Glassbox suits enterprises, especially in financial services, that analyze digital experience.
