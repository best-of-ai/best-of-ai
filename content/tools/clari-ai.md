Clari began as a tool to make sales forecasting more reliable. It reads CRM data and signals from email, calendar, and calls to show how deals are progressing, then rolls those into a forecast that managers can inspect by team, region, and stage. The goal is to replace spreadsheet-based forecast calls with data that people trust.

AI helps spot trouble. It scores deals, flags those with stalled activity or missing stakeholders, and suggests actions. Conversation intelligence summarizes calls and highlights risks, and pipeline inspection tools show where coverage is thin. Revenue leaders use it to prepare for board and forecast meetings.

Clari merged with Salesloft, a sales engagement company, to offer forecasting and outreach in one platform, and packaging is still evolving as the products come together. It is sold to mid-sized and large sales organizations on custom pricing. Alternatives include Gong, Aviso, BoostUp, Salesforce Revenue Intelligence, and HubSpot forecasting. Clari suits revenue teams who need dependable forecasts.
