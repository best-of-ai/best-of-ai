MarketMuse approaches content from the perspective of topical authority. It scans your website and compares the topics you cover with what search engines expect from a site in your area, then shows gaps where new or improved content could help. Instead of focusing on single keywords, it helps plan clusters of related articles that together establish expertise.

Its tools turn that strategy into work. Content briefs list the subtopics, questions, and terms a piece should address, an optimization tool scores drafts against those expectations, and AI helps with drafting and updating existing pages. Metrics such as personalized difficulty estimate how hard a topic will be for your site specifically. Teams use the platform to prioritize where to invest effort.

MarketMuse is paid, with plans scaled by usage, and a limited free tier. It is more strategic and often costlier than tools like Surfer or Frase. Its recommendations are only as good as human judgment on what serves readers, and ranking depends on many factors outside the content. Alternatives include Clearscope, Surfer, Frase, Semrush, and Ahrefs. MarketMuse suits content teams managing large sites and long-term strategies.
