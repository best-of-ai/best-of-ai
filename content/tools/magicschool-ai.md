MagicSchool was built for educators. Teachers choose a tool, such as a lesson plan generator, rubric builder, quiz maker, IEP helper, or email writer, fill in a short form, and receive a draft they can edit. More than eighty tools cover planning, assessment, communication, and support for students with different needs, which saves time on routine paperwork.

Student-facing features let teachers create safe AI chat rooms for classes, where they set the topic, rules, and level of help, and see the conversations. The platform emphasizes privacy and compliance for schools, with settings designed to protect students, and districts can adopt it with administrative controls. Integrations connect with Google Classroom and other learning tools.

MagicSchool has a generous free plan for individual teachers and paid plans for schools and districts. AI output can contain errors or bias, so teachers need to review materials, and policies on student use vary. Alternatives include Khanmigo, Brisk Teaching, Diffit, Eduaide, and general assistants such as ChatGPT and Gemini for Education. MagicSchool is among the most widely used AI tools for teachers.
