Narrato helps teams manage the whole content process. A content calendar plans what to publish, assignments route work to writers and editors, and approval workflows track progress. The AI writer generates drafts, outlines, social posts, and ad copy in a team's style, and SEO tools provide keyword suggestions and optimization scores.

Additional tools address agencies and publishers. Brief generation creates instructions for writers, a plagiarism checker and AI content detector help check originality, and a marketplace lets teams hire freelance writers for content that needs a human touch. Integrations publish directly to WordPress and other platforms, and an AI assistant helps with ideas and repurposing.

Narrato has a free plan with limited use and paid plans scaled by users and words. Features overlap with a wide range of tools, and AI drafts need editing for quality and facts. Alternatives include Jasper, Writesonic, Copy.ai, CoSchedule for planning, Frase for SEO, and project tools like Notion or Asana combined with a general assistant. Narrato suits small content teams and agencies who want planning and AI writing in one place.
