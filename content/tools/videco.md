Upload a demo, a webinar or an interview and Videco's AI finds the strongest moments and scores them. From the transcript it generates short clips with captions and your brand kit, a blog draft, and LinkedIn posts with hooks.

It is also a video host. The player embeds on any page, watch pages carry structured data for search engines, and you can build landing pages with lead capture and Calendly embeds. Client review links collect feedback, and a video API and webhooks let developers connect it to other systems.

A seven-day free trial comes with limits, and after that you pick a paid plan, Pro or Business, billed monthly. AI credits power the Content Studio. Business includes a monthly allotment, and you can buy credit packs that don't expire. It suits marketing and product teams that publish a lot of video.
