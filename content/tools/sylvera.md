Sylvera brings independent analysis to the carbon market. Analysts and models assess projects, such as forest protection or renewable energy, against criteria like additionality and permanence, and issue ratings. Satellite data and machine learning help measure forest carbon and detect deforestation.

Buyers use the ratings to choose credits, and sellers use them to demonstrate quality. The platform tracks market prices and supply, and supports due diligence.

Sylvera is sold on subscription to enterprises and financial institutions. Carbon markets remain contested, and ratings reflect methodology choices. Alternatives include BeZero Carbon, Pachama, MSCI Carbon Markets, Calyx Global, and Renoster. Sylvera suits buyers evaluating carbon credits.
