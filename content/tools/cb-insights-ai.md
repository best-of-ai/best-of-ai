CB Insights tracks the innovation economy. Its database covers private companies, funding rounds, investors, acquisitions, patents, and news, and analysts and algorithms turn that into research on market trends. Corporate strategy teams, investors, and consultants use it to understand which sectors are growing and who the major players are.

Features include company profiles, market maps, forecasts of company outcomes, and an AI assistant that answers questions from the platform's data and research. Alerts and newsletters keep subscribers up to date, and tools support building lists of companies matching specific criteria.

CB Insights is a paid subscription, aimed at enterprise users, and priced accordingly. Data on private companies is inherently incomplete, and predictions are probabilistic. Alternatives include PitchBook, Crunchbase, Dealroom, Tracxn, and Gartner for research. CB Insights suits strategy and investment teams tracking emerging companies.
