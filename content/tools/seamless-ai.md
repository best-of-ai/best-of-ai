Seamless.AI helps salespeople find who to contact. Users search by title, industry, and location, or use a browser extension to look up contacts on websites, and the platform returns emails and phone numbers pulled from public sources and its database. Lists export to CRMs and engagement tools.

Real-time search and enrichment aim to keep data fresh, and intent signals show which accounts may be in a buying cycle. AI features help write outreach and prioritize accounts.

Seamless.AI has a limited free tier and paid plans, and data accuracy is a common point of complaint across the category. Outreach must follow privacy and anti-spam laws. Alternatives include ZoomInfo, Apollo, Lusha, Cognism, and Kaspr. Seamless.AI suits sales teams building prospect lists.
