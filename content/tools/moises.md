Moises helps musicians work with recordings. A user uploads a song, and the app splits it into individual tracks, so a guitarist can mute the guitar and play along, or a singer can remove the lead vocal for practice. Tempo and key changes help with learning difficult parts.

Extra tools include chord detection, a metronome, and a feature to detect beats. Producers use stem separation for remixes and sampling, and teachers use it to demonstrate parts. Mobile and web apps make it easy to practice anywhere.

Moises has a free tier and paid plans. Separated stems are imperfect and may contain artifacts, and using separated material from commercial music raises copyright issues for publishing. Alternatives include LALAL.AI, iZotope RX, Demucs, Spleeter, and Logic Pro stem splitter. Moises suits musicians who practice with recordings.
