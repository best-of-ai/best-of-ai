Fireflies joins your calendar-connected calls automatically without needing to be manually added each time. It appears as a participant named "Fireflies notetaker," records audio, and produces a timestamped transcript with speaker identification within minutes of the call ending. The transcript is indexed and searchable across all your meetings — which means if you're trying to recall a commitment made three months ago in a call, you can search for it rather than trying to remember which recording it was in.

The intelligence layer goes beyond transcription. Fireflies identifies questions asked during a call, tracks topic frequency, and can flag sentiment — useful for sales teams analysing how prospects respond to different parts of a pitch. The meeting summary includes action items with the name of whoever was assigned each item, which helps with follow-up accountability. All of this syncs automatically to Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, Slack, and other connected tools within minutes of the meeting ending.

Fireflies' free plan provides 800 minutes of storage and limited AI summary features. Pro at $10/seat/month gives unlimited storage and full AI summaries. Business at $19/seat/month adds conversation analytics, smart search, and team collaboration features. Enterprise adds SSO, custom data retention, and compliance controls. It's particularly popular with sales teams, client services teams, and remote-first companies who conduct a high volume of calls and need to capture and share outcomes efficiently without manual note-writing.
