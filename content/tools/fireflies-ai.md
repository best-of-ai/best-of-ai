Fireflies sends a bot into your meetings, usually named Fred, that records the call and creates a transcript with speaker labels. After the meeting, it produces a summary with action items and key topics, and posts them where your team works. Every meeting becomes searchable text, so you can find what a customer said last month or when a decision was made. It supports dozens of languages and works with the main video call platforms and uploaded recordings.

It does more than take notes. Talk-time and sentiment analytics show who spoke and how conversations went, which sales and customer teams use for coaching. Integrations connect with Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, Gmail, and many other tools, and AI Skills can automate follow-up work such as updating a CRM or scoring candidates. You can ask questions across all your meetings in a chat, and an MCP connection lets assistants like Claude read the transcripts. Newer voice agents can run simple screening or discovery calls.

A free plan covers transcription, summaries, and search with limits on storage and AI use, and paid tiers add more features, with enterprise controls for security and admin. A visible bot joining a call can make some participants uncomfortable, so it is polite to tell people it will be there. Otter, Fathom, and Read AI are close competitors, and Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet now offer their own built-in note-taking.
