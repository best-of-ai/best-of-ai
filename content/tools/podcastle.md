Podcastle is a browser-based studio for audio. Users can record remote interviews with separate tracks, edit by working with the transcript, and clean up audio with one-click enhancement that removes noise and levels voices. A text-to-speech and voice cloning feature lets creators fix a mistake by typing.

The platform includes tools for video podcasts, hosting and distribution, and AI features such as summaries and show notes. It aims to give new podcasters an all-in-one workflow without learning a traditional audio editor.

Podcastle has a free plan with limits and paid tiers. Heavy post-production may still be better in a dedicated tool. Voice cloning should be used only with consent. Alternatives include Descript, Riverside, Adobe Podcast, Zencastr, and Audacity. Podcastle suits podcasters who want an easy all-in-one studio.
