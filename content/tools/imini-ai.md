iMini is a super AI agent capable of autonomously completing and delivering results based on human instructions. It simulates the human thought process — from receiving instructions, planning, retrieving, analyzing, and reasoning, to final delivery.

With its powerful model orchestration capabilities, iMini can autonomously handle a wide range of tasks such as in-depth professional reports, create multiple slides, business plans, as well as video and image generation — all in a one-stop solution.

More importantly, iMini’s “autonomous decision-making” mechanism simulates human work logic. Users only need to input a single command — for example: “Generate a 2024 North American EV market competitive analysis report” — and the system will automatically carry out a series of operations and deliver a high-quality result. Tasks that usually take humans two days can be completed by iMini AI in just 3–5 minutes.
