Talkpal is built for learners who struggle to find someone to practice speaking with. You have spoken conversations with an AI partner on topics you choose, and it corrects pronunciation and grammar as you go. The app covers a very wide range of languages, including many that larger apps skip.

Conversations can be immersive role-plays, and the app adapts to your level through personalized learning paths. Feedback arrives in real time, so you can fix a mistake while the conversation is still fresh.

The free plan has limited features, and Premium removes the limits and includes a trial period. Talkpal is a practice tool and does not offer the structured curriculum of an app like Pimsleur or Duolingo, so beginners may want a course alongside it. It works well once you know the basics and need speaking time.
