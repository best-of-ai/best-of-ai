GrowthBar tries to simplify SEO for people who are not specialists. You enter a keyword, and the tool shows search volume and difficulty, related keywords, and what the top-ranking pages cover, including their headings and word counts. A Chrome extension displays similar data directly in search results, which helps when researching competitors.

The AI content features turn that research into drafts. GrowthBar can generate outlines, blog posts, and meta descriptions, and tools for rewriting and expanding text help refine them. It also includes rank tracking and backlink data, and an AI assistant to help brainstorm topics. The target users are small business owners, freelancers, and agencies that publish content regularly.

GrowthBar is paid, priced lower than enterprise SEO suites, and offers a trial. Its data is less extensive than Semrush or Ahrefs, and AI drafts need editing for accuracy and voice. Alternatives include Frase, Surfer, Semrush, Ahrefs, and Mangools for budget keyword research. GrowthBar suits small publishers who want keyword research and drafting in one inexpensive tool.
