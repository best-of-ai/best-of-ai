Circle gives communities a home of their own. A creator sets up spaces for topics, posts content, hosts live events, runs courses, and lets members message each other, all under a custom domain and brand. It aims to feel more focused than a social network and less cluttered than a forum.

Monetization is built in. Communities can charge for memberships, offer paid tiers, and sell courses and events, with workflows to onboard new members. AI features draft welcome messages, summarize conversations, and help members find information, while analytics show engagement and which members are most active. Mobile apps and integrations with tools like Zapier extend reach.

Circle has paid plans by features and community size, and plans with branded mobile apps cost more. Its customization is deep compared with simpler products, though the setup takes time. Alternatives include Mighty Networks, Skool, Discord, Kajabi, and Slack communities. Circle suits creators and companies building a branded community.
