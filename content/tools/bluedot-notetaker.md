Bluedot records meetings through a Chrome extension or a desktop app, so no bot joins the call. It works with Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack and Webex, and it records in person from iOS, Android and Apple Watch. A phone calls feature lets you place or record calls from Bluedot using your own phone number.

Transcripts and summaries come in more than 100 languages, and you can chat with the AI across your meetings. It syncs with CRM and ATS tools and offers MCP, webhooks and a Claude connector. Its site says it is backed by Google.

A free plan covers five meetings in total. Basic is audio only, Pro adds video, and Business adds CRM and ATS sync, SSO and SCIM. Pricing is per user who records, not per seat. Founders, sales teams, recruiters and managers are the audience. It is not the same company as BlueDot, the outbreak surveillance service, which has its own listing here.
