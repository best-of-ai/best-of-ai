Cognism helps salespeople reach the right people. Its database covers companies and contacts across regions, with a reputation for strong coverage in Europe and for verified mobile numbers, which are valuable for cold calling. Users filter by role, industry, size, and technology, then export or sync contacts to their CRM.

Intent data adds timing. Signals show which accounts are researching topics related to a product, so teams can prioritize outreach. Compliance is a selling point: the company screens contacts against do-not-call lists and says it follows privacy rules like GDPR, which matters for outreach in Europe.

Cognism is sold on custom pricing, usually at a higher level than self-serve tools. Contact data decays quickly, and compliance remains the user's responsibility. Alternatives include ZoomInfo, Apollo, Lusha, Seamless.AI, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator. Cognism suits B2B sales teams, especially those selling in Europe.
