Fivetran handles the extract and load steps of data pipelines. A team connects sources such as SaaS applications, databases, and files, picks a destination warehouse, and Fivetran keeps the data synced, adapting automatically when source schemas change. This saves engineers from building and maintaining custom connectors.

The company merged with dbt Labs, the maker of the popular transformation tool, bringing loading and transformation together under one roof. Related products include a metadata and governance layer, and tools that prepare data for AI agents. Features for monitoring, anomaly detection, and lineage help teams trust their data.

Fivetran is sold on usage-based pricing, which can become costly at high volume. dbt Core remains open source. Alternatives include Airbyte, Matillion, Stitch, Hevo, and cloud-native tools like AWS Glue and Azure Data Factory. Fivetran suits data teams that want managed pipelines.
