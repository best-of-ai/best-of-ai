uLab Systems builds software for planning clear aligner treatment. Its AI proposes tooth movements from a scan, and the clinician edits the plan before approving it.

The company also supports in-office aligner production, so a practice can design and make aligners itself rather than sending every case to an outside lab.

uLab is a paid platform. The in-house approach requires printing and thermoforming equipment and some learning time. Alternatives include SoftSmile, 3Shape Ortho System, and Invisalign ClinCheck. uLab suits practices that want to control aligner design and manufacturing.
