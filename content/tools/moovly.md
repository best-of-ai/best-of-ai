Moovly serves companies that need many versions of the same video. Using a template, teams can generate hundreds or thousands of variations that change names, products, languages, or data, such as personalized sales videos or product videos for each item in a catalog. A browser editor handles design, and an API automates production from spreadsheets or other systems.

Localization is a major theme. Moovly can add subtitles, translate scripts, and generate voiceovers in multiple languages using AI voices, helping companies serve international audiences, and AI tools assist with text-to-video and editing. It's used in marketing, e-commerce, training, and communications, and offers brand controls and collaboration for large teams.

Moovly is sold to businesses on subscription plans and custom enterprise terms, with a free trial. Automated video is best for structured, repeatable content, and the style can look templated. Competitors and alternatives include Synthesia, HeyGen, Canva, Vimeo Create, Creatomate, and Shotstack for programmatic video. Moovly suits organizations that need to produce personalized or multilingual video at scale.
