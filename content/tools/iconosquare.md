Iconosquare started as an Instagram analytics tool and expanded to other networks. Social teams connect accounts and see how posts perform, when audiences are active, and how growth compares with competitors. Reports can be exported for clients, which makes it popular with agencies.

Scheduling and planning tools let teams build a content calendar and publish across networks. AI features suggest captions and hashtags, and a listening module monitors mentions and comments. The focus is on analysis that guides what to post, more than on broad community management.

Iconosquare is paid, with plans by profiles and features, and a trial. Alternatives include Sprout Social, Hootsuite, Buffer, Later, and Metricool. Iconosquare suits social media managers and agencies that want strong analytics.
