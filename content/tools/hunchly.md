Hunchly is a browser extension for investigations. As a researcher browses, it saves each page as it appeared, with screenshots, timestamps, and hash values that help show the content has not been altered. Pages are organized into cases, so evidence from a long investigation stays together.

The tool adds notes, tags, and a way to search across captured content. Reports can be generated for sharing or for legal use. It is popular with open-source intelligence investigators, journalists, and fraud and law enforcement analysts who need to preserve content that may disappear.

Hunchly is a paid tool, licensed per user. It captures what you view, so privacy and legal rules on collecting data still apply. Alternatives include Webrecorder, Archive.today, Page Vault, and Maltego for link analysis. Hunchly suits investigators who need reliable documentation of web research.
