lemlist helps sales teams and agencies reach prospects at scale. Users build sequences that combine email, LinkedIn steps, and phone tasks, and personalize messages with variables such as name and company. The brand made its name with personalized images and videos that change for each recipient, a way to stand out in crowded inboxes.

Deliverability is a major part of the product. Inbox warm-up gradually builds sender reputation, and tools check domains and monitor placement so emails land in primary inboxes, not spam. A lead database lets users find contacts and verify emails, and AI helps write openers and suggest follow-ups.

lemlist is paid, with plans per user, and extra costs for lead credits and some features. Cold outreach is regulated by privacy and anti-spam laws that vary by country, and poorly targeted campaigns hurt reputation. Alternatives include Instantly, Apollo, Outreach, Salesloft, Smartlead, and Woodpecker. lemlist suits small sales teams and agencies running outbound campaigns.
