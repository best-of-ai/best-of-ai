Sales Boomerang helps brokers stay in touch at the right time. It connects to a broker's client list, watches for signals such as changes in credit, equity, or market rates, and alerts the broker when a former client might benefit from refinancing or a new purchase.

That turns a dormant database into leads. Automated outreach can follow, with messages personalized to the situation. Brokers use it to generate repeat and referral business without constant manual review.

Sales Boomerang is sold by subscription to mortgage professionals. Use of credit data is regulated, and outreach must follow consumer protection and privacy rules. Alternatives include Surefire CRM, Total Expert, Jungo, and general CRM tools. Sales Boomerang suits mortgage brokers working their past clients.
