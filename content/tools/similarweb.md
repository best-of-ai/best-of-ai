Similarweb lets you look at the performance of sites you do not own. Enter a domain, and it shows estimated monthly visits, traffic sources such as search, social, and direct, top keywords, referring sites, audience demographics, and engagement metrics like time on site. Data comes from a mix of panels, partnerships, and public sources, modeled to produce estimates. This helps in benchmarking competitors and finding growth opportunities.

The platform covers more than websites. It tracks app usage and downloads, advertising, and market segments, and ranks sites within categories. Recent additions include tracking how brands and sites are cited and visited through AI chatbots, reflecting the way people increasingly use AI assistants instead of search. Investors use the data to spot trends in company performance before financial reports, and sales teams use it to find leads.

Similarweb has a free version with limited data and paid plans, with enterprise pricing that can be high. Estimates can differ from a site's own analytics, particularly for small sites with little traffic, so treat them as directional. Alternatives include Semrush and Ahrefs, which are stronger for SEO data, Sensor Tower and data.ai for apps, and Comscore. Similarweb is widely used for competitive intelligence.
