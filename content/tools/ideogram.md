Text rendering inside AI-generated images has been broken for years — garbled letters, missing characters, words that look right from a distance but dissolve into nonsense up close. Ideogram solved this problem ahead of every other major image generator. Ask it to create a poster with a specific headline, a product label with a tagline, or a book cover with legible title text, and the words come out spelled correctly and styled appropriately for the context. This single capability opens up a category of creative work that other generators can't reliably handle.

Beyond typography, Ideogram's image quality is competitive with the best generators available. Its prompt adherence is strong — it produces what you describe rather than an interpretation of it — and it handles a wide range of styles from photorealistic product shots to illustrated graphics and typographic posters. The canvas feature lets you arrange and edit multiple generated elements, and a Magic prompt button can expand a simple description into a detailed generation prompt automatically.

Ideogram offers a free tier with 10 slow-queue generations per day and 25 priority credits on first sign-up. Basic plan at $7/month provides 400 priority generations monthly; Plus at $16/month offers 1,000. All plans include commercial usage rights for generated images. Designers, brand teams, social media creators, and marketers who produce visual content with text regularly — particularly advertising graphics, product labels, social cards, and promotional posters — find Ideogram solves a problem that previously required taking generated images into Photoshop for manual text work.
