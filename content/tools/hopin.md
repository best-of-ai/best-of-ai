Hopin offered an all-in-one environment for online events. Organizers sold tickets, ran live sessions on a main stage, hosted breakout rooms and video networking, and set up virtual expo booths, and attendees moved between these areas as if walking around a venue. It grew explosively as conferences moved online, with large funding rounds and acquisitions of other event and streaming companies.

As in-person events returned, demand for virtual events dropped sharply, and Hopin shrank and restructured. It sold its events platform to RingCentral, which continues to operate it as part of its own offering, and the company's other products, such as streaming and community tools, were separated or spun out. The brand is no longer the same independent business.

Event organizers now look at a range of tools. Cvent, Bizzabo, and Swapcard offer hybrid event platforms, RingCentral Events carries on the original product, Zoom Events and Webinars covers virtual sessions, and Eventbrite handles ticketing. This entry stays for reference because many event technology comparisons from the pandemic period mention Hopin.
