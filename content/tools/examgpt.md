ExamGPT, developed by Excelas, is an AI-powered marking system for GCSE STEM subjects. Accessible through our school portal, the system offers educators a robust solution with over 90% marking similarity to UK schools. By integrating its core capabilities with advanced language models like ChatGPT, the system generates comprehensive marking data tailored to established educational formats. It is designed to be fully customizable to specific school requirements and can process scanned exam papers directly.

Key Features
- Straightforward process: Simply scan and upload GCSE papers for rapid, automated grading and receive comprehensive student feedback in less than an hour, freeing educators up to focus on teaching.
- Read Any Handwriting: Our advanced OCR technology can decipher even the most challenging handwriting, ensuring no student work goes unread. From complex mathematical equations to detailed scientific explanations, our AI understands it all with exceptional accuracy.
- Trained on The Exam Specification: Our AI exam marker goes beyond keywords to deliver consistent grading that is fully compliant with official exam specifications. It intelligently applies complex rules, like follow-through marks (ft), just like a chief examiner.

Benefits
- Massive Time Savings: Our AI marking platform is designed to save 80% of marking time and reduce overall teacher workload. Get fast, automated grading for entire class sets of mock exams in under an hour.
- Consistent and Unbiased Marking: Achieve fair, unbiased marking which is 90-95% similar to your marking on every paper. Our AI exam marker provides perfectly consistent grading by applying the official mark scheme without human error. 
- Actionable Data & Student Feedback: Go beyond a simple score with actionable insights and detailed student feedback. Use our data analysis to quickly identify knowledge gaps and inform targeted teaching interventions. Our approach is backed by findings from the Education Endowment Foundation (EEF), which highlights high-quality feedback as a top strategy for improving student attainment.
