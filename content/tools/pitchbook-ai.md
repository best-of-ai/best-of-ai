PitchBook tracks private markets. Its database covers companies, funding rounds, investors, funds, acquisitions, and valuations, and analysts use it to find targets, benchmark deals, and research sectors. Reports and data feeds support investment decisions and strategy work.

AI tools help users query the database in natural language, summarize companies and markets, and speed up research tasks. Integrations with Excel and CRM systems bring data into daily workflows. The company also publishes market research.

PitchBook is sold on expensive professional subscriptions. Private company data is never complete, and some figures are estimates. Alternatives include CB Insights, Crunchbase, Dealroom, Preqin, and S&P Capital IQ. PitchBook suits investment professionals and corporate development teams.
