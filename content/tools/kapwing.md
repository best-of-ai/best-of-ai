Kapwing is an online editor aimed at creators and teams who need fast, polished content. You upload video or start from a template, trim, add captions, music, and effects, and export in formats suited to platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. Because it runs in the browser, there is nothing to install, and collaborators can work in the same project and leave comments.

AI features are at the center of the workflow. Auto subtitles transcribe speech and add stylized captions in many languages, background removal works without a green screen, and tools can remove silences and filler words, translate and dub videos, and clean up audio. A repurposing tool cuts long videos into short clips for social media, and text-to-video and script features help generate drafts. Brand kits keep fonts and colors consistent.

Kapwing has a free tier with watermarks and limited export length, and paid plans that remove them and add more storage, longer videos, and team tools. It is easier than traditional editors but less powerful for complex edits. CapCut offers many similar AI features for free, Descript is stronger for transcript-based editing, Veed and Canva are close competitors, and Opus Clip specializes in turning long videos into shorts. Kapwing suits social media teams and educators.
