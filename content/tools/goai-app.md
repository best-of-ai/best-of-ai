GoAI aims to give individual investors some of the research help professionals have. A user searches a company or asks a question, and the tool pulls financial data, summarizes earnings calls and filings, and highlights notable insider trades. Answers are written in plain language to be readable for non-experts.

Such tools save time reading long documents, but they do not predict the market. AI summaries can contain errors, so important figures should be checked against filings. Past results do not guarantee future performance, and nothing a tool generates should be treated as a recommendation.

GoAI has a free tier and paid plans. Independent information is limited, so check its data sources and update frequency. Alternatives include Seeking Alpha, Koyfin, Finchat, Perplexity Finance, and TipRanks. GoAI suits retail investors who want help with research.
