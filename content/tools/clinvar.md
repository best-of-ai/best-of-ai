Users search by gene or variant and see classifications such as pathogenic or benign, plus the evidence and submitter. Entries show review status, so counselors can judge confidence.

Classifications can conflict between labs and change over time.

gnomAD and OMIM are related resources. ClinVar suits genetic counselors.
