Seequent software helps geologists understand what lies underground. Its flagship Leapfrog creates 3D models from drill holes, geophysics, and maps, so teams can see ore bodies and structures, plan drilling, and estimate resources. Other products handle geotechnical data, groundwater, and geophysical modeling.

Mining companies, geotechnical engineers, and environmental consultants use these models for decisions. Cloud tools allow collaboration and centralized data management, and newer features apply machine learning to modeling tasks.

Seequent is sold by subscription and license. Models rely on sparse data and geologist interpretation, so they carry uncertainty. Alternatives include Datamine, Maptek, Hexagon, Micromine, and Petrel for oil and gas. Seequent suits geoscientists and mining engineers.
