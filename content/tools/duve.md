Duve is a guest experience platform. Before arrival, guests receive a digital check-in flow, can upload documents, and see offers for upgrades and extras. During the stay, a messaging assistant answers questions about the property and the area, and requests are routed to staff when a person is needed.

The platform includes a digital guidebook, upsell tools that present add-ons at the right moment, and integration with property management systems. For hoteliers and vacation rental managers, this reduces repetitive messages and brings extra revenue per booking.

Duve is sold on subscription to hotels and rental operators. Results depend on integration with the property's systems, and guests still value a human option. Alternatives include Canary Technologies, Mews, Guesty, Hostaway, and Enso Connect. Duve suits hospitality operators who want to automate guest communication.
