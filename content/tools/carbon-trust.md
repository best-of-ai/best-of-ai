The Carbon Trust began as a UK organization set up to help businesses and the public sector cut emissions, and it now works internationally. It advises companies on how to measure their greenhouse gas emissions across operations and supply chains, set reduction targets, and plan the changes needed to meet them. Its work spans corporate carbon footprinting, product footprints, net zero strategy, and support with climate disclosure requirements.

A well-known part of its business is certification and standards. The Carbon Trust Standard recognizes organizations that measure and reduce their footprint, and other programs verify claims about products and supply chains. Software and data tools support these services, and AI is used to process large amounts of supplier and energy data, estimate emissions where measured data is missing, and identify the best reduction opportunities.

The Carbon Trust sells consulting and certification services, typically on project-based quotes, rather than a self-serve app. That makes it a different kind of entry from software tools like Persefoni, Watershed, or Sweep, which focus on carbon accounting platforms. It suits larger organizations that need expert guidance and credibility with regulators, customers, and investors. Results depend on data quality and on the willingness to act on the recommendations, not just to report them.
