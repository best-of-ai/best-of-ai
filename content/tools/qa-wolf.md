QA Wolf offers testing as a service. Instead of a company writing and maintaining its own automated tests, QA Wolf's team creates Playwright tests for the critical flows, runs them in parallel on every release, and fixes tests when the app changes. They report real bugs, and filter flaky failures.

AI generates and repairs tests, while engineers verify results. The aim is high coverage without the burden of test maintenance, which often overwhelms small teams. Tests are written in open frameworks, so customers own the code.

QA Wolf is sold on custom pricing, usually at a higher level than self-serve tools. Alternatives include Momentic, mabl, Testim, Katalon, and BrowserStack. QA Wolf suits teams that want outsourced end-to-end testing.
