Observe.AI is built for contact centers that handle huge numbers of customer interactions. Quality teams traditionally review a small sample of calls by hand. Observe.AI transcribes and scores all interactions automatically against a company's quality and compliance criteria, flags issues such as missed disclosures or poor handling of angry customers, and surfaces trends across thousands of conversations.

It also supports agents during and after the call. Real-time guidance suggests what to say or reminds an agent of a required step, and post-call summaries cut the time spent on notes. Coaching tools turn scores into specific feedback for each agent, and dashboards help supervisors see which skills to work on. The platform has added AI agents that speak with customers directly for routine requests, handing off to humans when needed.

Observe.AI is sold to enterprises on custom contracts, and implementation involves connecting telephony systems and defining scorecards. Automated scoring is only as good as the criteria and the transcription accuracy. Recording customers requires consent in many regions. Competitors include NICE, Verint, Calabrio, CallMiner, and Balto, and platforms such as Genesys and Five9 have built in similar analytics. It suits mid-sized and large support operations that want full coverage instead of sampling.
