Encore AI builds on conversation data. It analyzes recordings of calls and chats to learn which behaviors lead to good outcomes, such as how top reps handle objections or open a conversation. Those patterns become the basis for AI agents that talk with customers.

The agents can take calls or messages themselves or support human reps with live guidance. The idea is to scale the best performers' approach across a team instead of relying on training alone. Companies with large volumes of calls and recorded history are the target.

Independent information is limited, so ask for evidence from pilots in similar industries. Call recording and AI agents involve consent and disclosure rules. Alternatives include Cresta, Balto, Gong, Observe.AI, and Sierra. Encore AI suits contact centers and sales teams with substantial call data.
