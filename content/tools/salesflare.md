Salesflare fills itself in. It connects to email, calendar, and phone, then builds contact and company profiles with conversation history, social data, and notes, so sales reps do not need to type everything. A timeline shows every interaction with a contact.

Features include pipelines, workflows, email tracking, and sequences, plus an intelligent assistant that nudges reps when a lead has gone quiet. It works with Gmail, Outlook, and a mobile app.

Salesflare is paid, with plans per user. It is designed for small teams with complex B2B sales. Alternatives include Pipedrive, HubSpot, Copper, Close, and Attio. Salesflare suits small B2B sales teams.
