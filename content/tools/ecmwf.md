Meteorologists use ECMWF forecast charts and open data to compare with other models. The centre also publishes the ERA5 reanalysis, a long record of past weather used in climate research. Its AI forecasting system adds machine learning models alongside traditional physics.

Some data is open and some is available to member states or by license.

NOAA GFS is another global model. ECMWF suits meteorologists who compare model output.
