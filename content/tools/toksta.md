Toksta is the SaaS intelligence platform built for founders, growth marketers, and B2B companies. It combines a verified database of 1,500+ creator stacks with AI-driven research tools to show which software products top influencers actually use and trust. Toksta’s AI research agent compares pricing, features, and sentiment in seconds, saving hours of manual research. Companies can track which creators mention or adopt their tools, identify partnership opportunities, and access market-validated playbooks showing how experts use SaaS products to grow. By revealing real adoption trends and creator influence, Toksta empowers teams to choose the right tools, run smarter influencer campaigns, and stay ahead of competitors.
