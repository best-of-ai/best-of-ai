Seeking Alpha combines crowd-sourced investment writing with a quantitative rating system. Contributors publish stock analysis, the site covers news and earnings, and its Quant Ratings score stocks on factors such as valuation, growth, and momentum.

The free tier gives limited access to articles and data. Premium adds a stock screener, fuller ratings, and portfolio tools, and some AI-generated stock analysis that draws on the ratings and analyst coverage sits in paid tiers. A separate stock-picking service, Alpha Picks, is sold on its own. Sources disagree on exactly how much of the ratings the free tier includes, so check the plan page.

Contributors are mostly independent investors and not licensed advisers, and they often hold positions in the stocks they write about. That is useful context when reading. The platform works best for self-directed investors who want a mix of opinions and a systematic rating to compare against.
