Notion AI sits inside the workspace you already use rather than requiring a separate tool to open. Highlight any block of text and ask the AI to improve it, shorten it, translate it, or continue it. Open a blank page and ask it to draft a project brief, a meeting agenda, or a product spec from a one-line prompt. The AI is context-aware — it knows you're working in a team wiki, not just a general text editor — and generates output that fits Notion's block-based structure naturally.

The Q&A feature is what makes Notion AI more than a writing assistant. It can answer questions about your entire workspace — "what did we decide about the pricing model?" or "which team is responsible for the mobile launch?" — by searching across all your pages and synthesising an answer with links to the relevant sources. For teams that live in Notion, this effectively gives every employee a way to query institutional knowledge without interrupting colleagues.

Notion AI is available as an add-on to any Notion plan at $8/member/month (billed monthly) or $10 with annual billing. It works across the free, Plus, Business, and Enterprise tiers. The integration means there's no friction between using the AI and acting on what it produces — a generated action item can immediately become a task in a linked database, a drafted spec can live alongside the project it describes, and summaries don't need to be copied anywhere because they're already in the right place.
