Rodin creates 3D assets from simple inputs. A user types a description or uploads an image, and the system produces a textured 3D model that can be exported to common formats. It also offers options for controlling geometry and materials.

The output can serve as a starting point for games, animation, product visualization, and augmented reality. Quality has improved quickly in this field, but generated meshes often need cleanup of topology and textures before production use.

Rodin has a free tier and paid plans with credits. Alternatives include Meshy, Tripo, CSM, Luma AI, and Kaedim. Rodin suits creators prototyping 3D assets.
