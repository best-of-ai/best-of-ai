Dovetail gives research teams a home for customer feedback. Researchers upload interviews and other data, and the platform transcribes recordings, lets them highlight key quotes, and organizes everything so insights can be found later. Teams build a searchable repository instead of losing findings in slide decks.

AI shortens the analysis step. It tags content automatically, groups related feedback into themes, summarizes sessions, and answers questions across a body of research. Reports and shareable highlights help researchers communicate with product teams and executives, and integrations pull in data from support and survey tools.

Dovetail has a free plan for small use and paid tiers by seats and features. AI-generated themes should be checked against the raw data, because nuance can be lost. Alternatives include Condens, Aurelius, Notion, Airtable, and Marvin. Dovetail suits user researchers and product teams.
