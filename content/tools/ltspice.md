Users draw a schematic, run transient, AC, or DC simulations, and plot waveforms. Libraries include models of many Analog Devices parts. It runs on Windows and macOS.

The interface is plain, and some skill with SPICE is needed.

KiCad ngspice and PSpice are alternatives. LTspice suits electrical engineers who design circuits.
