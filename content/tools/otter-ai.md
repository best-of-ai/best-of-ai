Otter joins your Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams calls automatically as a participant, records and transcribes in real time, and separates speakers by name if you connect your calendar. While the call is happening, you can see the live transcript in the Otter app, highlight important moments, and add comments — without taking notes or breaking focus on the conversation. After the call, a summary with action items is ready within minutes.

The searchable transcript archive is what makes Otter valuable beyond individual meetings. Every recorded conversation is stored and fully text-searchable, so you can search across months of meetings for a specific decision, commitment, or topic that came up. Sales teams use this to verify what was promised during a call, research teams use it to retrieve quotes and insights from user interviews, and managers use it to audit their own call quality and follow-up on stated action items.

The free tier provides 300 minutes of transcription per month and a limit of three recordings in a 30-day period. Pro at $10/month per user increases limits to 1,200 minutes monthly and adds features like AI chat (ask Otter questions about a transcript), recording import, and shared workspaces. Business plans at $20/user/month add admin controls, custom vocabulary, and priority support. Otter's integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, and Notion mean that meeting outcomes can flow automatically into wherever your team already tracks work.
