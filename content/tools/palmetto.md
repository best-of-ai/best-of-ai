Palmetto connects homeowners with solar and energy services. A homeowner enters an address and gets an assessment of how much a system could save, compares financing options, and is matched with installers. The company also offers energy management that coordinates devices like batteries and EV chargers.

On the business side, Palmetto provides software and data used by installers, utilities, and fintechs, including tools for sizing systems, tracking incentives, and managing projects. AI helps optimize when to charge or use stored energy based on rates and weather.

Estimates depend on roof, local rates, and incentive rules, which change, so get several quotes and read financing terms carefully. Palmetto is free to explore, with costs tied to services purchased. Alternatives include EnergySage, Sunrun, Tesla Energy, Aurora Solar, and OpenSolar. Palmetto suits homeowners researching solar and energy savings.
