Patronus AI helps teams find problems in AI applications. Developers run test sets through their systems, and Patronus scores the outputs with evaluators that check for hallucinations, unsafe content, leaks of sensitive data, and policy violations. Red-teaming tools probe for weaknesses with adversarial inputs.

The company has published benchmarks, including tests for financial question answering, and trained evaluation models such as a judge model for hallucination detection. Monitoring features watch production traffic and alert teams to drops in quality.

Patronus has a free tier and paid plans. Automated evaluation is itself imperfect, so combine it with human review. Alternatives include Braintrust, Arize Phoenix, Galileo, Langfuse, and Promptfoo. Patronus AI suits teams that need systematic LLM testing.
