Pilots plan missions on a map with waypoints, altitudes, and camera actions. Orbit and follow-me modes help with filming. Missions can be saved and repeated.

It is a one-time paid app for supported DJI models.

DroneDeploy and Pix4D capture offer survey-focused planning. Litchi suits drone operators who want custom flight paths.
