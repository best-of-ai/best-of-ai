Stampli keeps the conversation about an invoice attached to the invoice. When a bill arrives, teams discuss it in context, approvers sign off, and the payment goes out, with no hunting through email threads. It connects to accounting systems and does not require replacing them.

Billy the Bot reads invoices, suggests coding and approvals based on past behavior, and flags anomalies. Payment tools handle payments and cards, and the platform provides audit trails and reporting.

Stampli is sold on subscription by invoice volume. Alternatives include BILL, Tipalti, Coupa, Ramp, and Basware. Stampli suits finance teams processing many invoices.
