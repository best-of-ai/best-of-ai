LeanTaaS builds software for healthcare capacity. Hospitals struggle with uneven demand: operating rooms sit idle while patients wait, infusion chairs are overbooked on some days, and beds are not available when needed. LeanTaaS forecasts demand and recommends schedules that balance resources and reduce wait times.

Its iQueue products cover operating rooms, infusion centers, and inpatient beds. Algorithms assess historical patterns and help staff adjust block time, appointment slots, and staffing. Improvements can increase the number of patients served without adding space.

LeanTaaS is sold to hospitals and health systems on enterprise contracts. Gains depend on clinicians and managers adopting the recommendations. Alternatives include Qventus, Hospital IQ (Press Ganey), Epic's scheduling tools, and GE HealthCare Command Center. LeanTaaS suits health systems optimizing capacity.
