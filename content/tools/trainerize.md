Trainerize is a coaching platform for fitness professionals. Trainers create programs from an exercise library, assign them to clients, and monitor workouts logged in a mobile app. Messaging, habit tracking, and progress photos keep clients engaged.

Additional features include nutrition coaching, integrations with wearables, payment processing, and a branded app. AI helps draft programs and suggest changes based on adherence and goals.

Trainerize has a free tier for a small number of clients and paid plans. It is owned by ABC Fitness. Alternatives include TrueCoach, Everfit, PT Distinction, Mindbody, and Hevy Coach. Trainerize suits independent trainers and gyms.
