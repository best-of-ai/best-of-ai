Pilot offers accounting as a service. A company connects its bank accounts and tools, and Pilot's team and software keep the books, close each month, prepare financial statements, and handle tax filings. Clients get a dedicated bookkeeper and a dashboard.

Automation takes on the repetitive work, such as categorizing transactions, while humans check results and answer questions. Add-on services include CFO advice, R&D tax credits, and sales tax support.

Pilot is priced by monthly fee based on complexity, and aimed at startups and small to mid-sized businesses. It is a service, not self-serve software. Alternatives include Bench (which shut down and was acquired by Employer.com), Puzzle, Digits, Xero with an accountant, and QuickBooks Live. Pilot suits startups that want outsourced accounting.
