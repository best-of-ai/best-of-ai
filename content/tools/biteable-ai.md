Biteable makes video creation approachable for non-editors. Users pick a template for something like a company announcement, training clip, or social promo, and replace text, images, and footage scene by scene. Brand settings keep colors, fonts, and logos consistent across a team.

AI features accelerate the first draft. A script or prompt can generate a sequence of scenes with suggested visuals, text can be turned into narration, and videos can be resized for formats such as vertical social posts and widescreen. Collaboration features let teammates review and edit, and analytics show how videos perform.

Biteable has offered a limited free option and paid plans for teams. It is aimed at simple, template-driven videos, and heavy customization is limited compared with professional editors. Alternatives include Canva, Animoto, InVideo, Powtoon, and Clipchamp. Biteable suits business teams that need regular, polished explainer and internal videos.
