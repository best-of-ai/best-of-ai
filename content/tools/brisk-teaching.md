Teachers can generate lesson materials, adjust reading levels, and draft feedback on student writing. A student-facing feature lets pupils chat with an AI tutor under teacher guidance. It also supports quizzes.

A free plan covers basic features, and paid plans add more. Teachers should check output before using it with students.

MagicSchool and Diffit are alternatives. Brisk Teaching suits educators who work in Google Workspace.
