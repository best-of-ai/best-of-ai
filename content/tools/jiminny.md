Jiminny captures conversations between sales teams and customers. It records calls and meetings, creates transcripts, and uses AI to summarize them, extract action items, and detect topics, objections, and talk ratios. Managers use this to coach reps with specific examples.

Deal insights help forecast. The platform flags risks such as lack of next steps or missing stakeholders, and keeps the CRM updated with notes and fields without manual entry. Search across calls lets teams find how customers talk about competitors or pricing.

Jiminny is sold on subscription to sales teams, and recording requires consent. Alternatives include Gong, Chorus (ZoomInfo), Clari, Avoma, and Salesloft. Jiminny suits mid-sized sales teams wanting conversation intelligence.
