01.AI started with the goal of building leading large language models. Its Yi series, released with open weights, performed strongly on benchmarks for its size and supported long context and Chinese and English. Developers around the world downloaded and fine-tuned the models, and the company also offered an API and consumer products. Kai-Fu Lee, a well-known AI executive and investor, led the company.

The company's strategy changed as the cost of competing at the frontier rose and powerful open models from other Chinese labs became available. It reduced its investment in pretraining its own large models and moved toward selling AI solutions to businesses, using other models where appropriate. Reports describe a focus on enterprise data infrastructure and a path toward a public listing.

The original Yi models remain available as open weights under their licenses, but the company is no longer the active developer of new frontier models, so for new projects, models like Qwen, DeepSeek, Llama, and Mistral have more active development. 01.AI may be relevant for companies in China looking for enterprise AI services. Check the company's current offerings before depending on it, as its focus has changed.
