Igly focuses on product images. A seller uploads photos of items, and the tool removes or replaces backgrounds, places products in lifestyle scenes, adjusts lighting, and creates variations for marketplaces. An infinite canvas lets users arrange and edit many images in one project.

Agents handle multi-step edits from a plain description, such as producing a set of listing images in a consistent style. Presets match the dimensions and rules of common platforms. Sellers use it to reduce the cost of photography and design.

Igly has a free tier and paid plans. AI-generated product images must still show the real product accurately, since misleading images cause returns and violate marketplace rules. Alternatives include Photoroom, Pebblely, Flair AI, Canva, and Adobe Firefly. Igly suits online sellers producing listing photos.
