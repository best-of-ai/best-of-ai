project44 is a supply chain visibility platform. It gathers location and status data from trucks, rail, ocean, and air carriers, merges it into one view, and shows shippers where each shipment is. Predictive ETAs account for traffic, weather, and port conditions.

Alerts flag delays early, so teams can reroute or notify customers. The platform also offers tools for appointment scheduling, drayage, and analytics on carrier performance, and AI features automate routine tracking tasks and communication.

project44 is sold to shippers, retailers, and manufacturers on enterprise contracts, and its value grows with the carrier network. Competitors include FourKites, Descartes, Transporeon, E2open, and Shippeo. project44 suits logistics teams managing many shipments.
