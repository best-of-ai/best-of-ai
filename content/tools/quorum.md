Quorum is a public affairs platform. Users track legislation and regulations, follow lawmakers, and keep a record of their outreach, all in one system. Alerts notify them when a bill moves or a hearing is scheduled, and a database of officials supports advocacy campaigns.

AI summarizes long bills, flags relevant provisions, and helps draft messages and reports. Constituent engagement tools let organizations mobilize supporters to contact officials. Teams use the data to plan strategy and measure impact.

Quorum is sold on subscription to corporations, trade groups, and nonprofits. Forecasting legislative outcomes is uncertain. Alternatives include FiscalNote, Bloomberg Government, Plural Policy, Muck Rack for media, and vLex Fastcase. Quorum suits government affairs and advocacy teams.
