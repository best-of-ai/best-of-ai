BlueDot is an epidemic intelligence company. Its system scans huge volumes of text and data, including news in many languages, animal disease reports, and travel patterns, looking for signs of unusual outbreaks. Epidemiologists review the signals and issue alerts with analysis.

The company drew attention when it flagged the cluster of pneumonia cases that became COVID-19 before many official announcements. Flight data helps estimate where infected travelers might go, which supports early warnings to governments, airlines, and health organizations.

BlueDot is sold to public health bodies and companies that need to assess risk to staff and operations. Early warnings can be false alarms, and prediction of disease spread is uncertain. Alternatives and peers include HealthMap, ProMED, Airfinity, Metabiota, and the WHO's Epidemic Intelligence from Open Sources. BlueDot suits organizations tracking infectious disease risk.
