Mathway helps students check and understand math. A user enters a problem or snaps a photo, and the app returns the answer and, with a subscription, the steps used to reach it. It covers algebra, trigonometry, calculus, statistics, and more.

Used well, step-by-step solutions help a student learn a method. Used carelessly, they let students copy answers without understanding, and teachers often restrict such tools. Automated solvers can also make errors, especially with word problems and unusual notation.

Mathway has a free tier showing answers and a paid plan for steps. Alternatives include Photomath, Symbolab, Wolfram Alpha, Khan Academy, and Desmos. Mathway suits students who want to verify work and learn methods.
