Enlive is a prompt layer. Instead of writing a long, careful prompt, a user types a brief request, and Enlive expands it with relevant context, structure, and instructions before sending it to a model. The idea is to get better answers from people who are not prompt engineers.

It works across multiple models and tracks preferences, such as tone and format, so later requests fit the user's habits. This addresses the common experience that vague prompts return vague answers. For power users it can save the time spent rewriting prompts.

Newer models often infer intent well from short requests, so the benefit shrinks as models improve. Enlive is free, and independent information is limited. Alternatives include PromptPerfect, built-in prompt improvers in ChatGPT and Claude, and custom instructions and projects. Enlive suits people who want better results without learning prompting techniques.
