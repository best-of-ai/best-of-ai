Noty.ai is a lightweight tool for people who want meeting notes without much setup. It records and transcribes online meetings, then creates a summary that highlights the main discussion points, decisions, and tasks. The goal is to let participants focus on the conversation instead of typing, and to give everyone a shared record afterward.

Its integrations make the notes easy to reuse. Summaries can be pushed to Notion and other workspace tools, and the transcript is searchable, so you can find what was said about a topic in a past meeting. A browser extension handles recording in Google Meet and Zoom, and a free plan lets individuals try the product.

Noty.ai is a small product in a crowded field, and its features are simpler than larger platforms. It is a fit for individuals and small teams with basic needs, while organizations that need CRM sync, analytics, or enterprise security usually look at Fireflies, Fathom, Otter, or Avoma. As with any recorder, you should tell participants when a meeting is being recorded and follow privacy rules. Check the current plans and supported platforms before relying on it for important meetings.
