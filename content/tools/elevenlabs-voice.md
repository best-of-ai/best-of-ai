ElevenLabs is known for natural-sounding synthetic speech. Users type text and choose a voice, or clone a voice from a sample, and get audio that carries intonation and emotion. The platform supports dozens of languages and accents and offers a library of voices.

Its products now span several uses. Dubbing translates video into other languages while keeping the speaker's voice, audiobook and narration tools produce long-form audio, and a conversational agent platform builds voice assistants that talk with customers in real time. Music and sound effect generation, plus speech to text, round out the suite. An API lets developers embed these features.

ElevenLabs has a free tier and paid plans by usage. Voice cloning raises consent and misuse concerns, and the company applies safeguards such as verification. Alternatives include OpenAI voice models, Cartesia, PlayHT (now shut down), Murf, WellSaid Labs, and Google Cloud Text-to-Speech. ElevenLabs suits creators, developers, and companies that need high-quality voices.
