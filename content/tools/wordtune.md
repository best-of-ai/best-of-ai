Wordtune's approach is different from grammar checkers and paraphrasers that rewrite text from scratch. It presents several alternative ways to phrase what you're already saying, so you're choosing the best version of your own idea rather than accepting the AI's interpretation. Highlight a sentence and Wordtune shows you four or five alternatives — some shorter, some more formal, some punchier — that you can switch between with a click. The phrasing stays close to your original voice.

The Spices feature addresses the blank-page problem for writers who know what they want to say but need material to support it. Spices adds supporting evidence — relevant statistics, examples, historical context, counterpoints — to any selected sentence. This is particularly useful for business writers who need credibility in their claims but don't want to spend 20 minutes searching for a supporting data point. Wordtune draws from its training data to surface relevant supporting content inline.

Wordtune's free plan allows 10 rewrites per day, which is enough to evaluate the quality but too limited for daily use. Plus at $6.99/month (billed annually) gives unlimited rewrites and Spices access. Advanced at $9.99/month adds a summariser, AI-powered reading assistance, and increased limits. The Chrome and Edge extensions integrate with Gmail, Google Docs, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and other web apps. Business and academic writers use it most — anyone who writes professionally and cares about how their writing sounds rather than just whether it's grammatically correct.
