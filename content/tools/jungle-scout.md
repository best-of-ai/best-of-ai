Jungle Scout is built around Amazon data. Sellers and brands use it to size a category, compare prices, see how competitors advertise, and decide what to launch or stock. It started with tools for finding product ideas and has grown into a set of products for different sizes of business.

The lineup is split by audience. Cobalt gives brands category benchmarking, pricing, and ad-spend insights. Cloud supplies raw data to teams that run their own analytics, Consult offers expert-written reports, and Catalyst is aimed at smaller sellers. Jungle Scout MCP connects approved AI tools to the data, so you can ask questions in plain English and get answers grounded in Amazon figures.

The main page lists a demo and a pricing page and does not mention a free plan, so check current plans before signing up. Helium 10 is the closest alternative and also covers listings and advertising tools. Jungle Scout leans toward research and market intelligence, which suits sellers deciding where to compete.
