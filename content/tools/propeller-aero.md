Propeller serves construction, mining, and quarry sites. A drone flies a site, often with ground control targets, and the images are processed into accurate maps and 3D models. Users measure stockpiles, cut and fill volumes, and compare the site with design plans.

Dashboards share progress with project teams, and tools track haul roads, compare surveys over time, and generate reports for clients. Its PPK-enabled hardware and workflow aim to improve accuracy and reduce the need for ground control points.

Propeller is sold on subscription to contractors, surveyors, and owners. Results depend on flight practice. Alternatives include DroneDeploy, Pix4D, Trimble, Bentley, and Agisoft Metashape. Propeller Aero suits earthworks and site monitoring.
