Grain records meetings on Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and other platforms and turns them into transcripts, summaries, and short clips. Its clip feature is a distinguishing idea: instead of sharing a whole recording, you highlight a moment, such as a customer's feedback or a question about pricing, and share just that clip with colleagues. This makes customer insights easier to spread across product, marketing, and leadership.

AI features produce structured notes from each meeting using templates, such as discovery calls or customer interviews, and answer questions across a library of recordings. Integrations send notes and fields to Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, and Slack, which cuts manual updates. A desktop capture option can record meetings without a visible bot joining the call, which some teams prefer for external meetings.

Grain has a free plan with a limited number of recordings and paid plans for more history, AI features, and team tools. As with other recorders, you should disclose recording and handle customer data carefully. Fathom offers free unlimited recording, Fireflies and Otter are general-purpose note takers, and Gong and Avoma provide more analytics for sales teams. Grain is a good fit for teams that want to share customer moments and keep CRM records tidy.
