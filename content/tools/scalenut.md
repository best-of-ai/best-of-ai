Scalenut combines SEO research with AI writing. A user enters a keyword, and the platform analyzes search results to suggest topics, questions, and structure, then produces an outline and draft article, optimized for search. A one-click mode called Cruise creates a full article by following the steps of research, outline, and writing automatically.

Other features support content marketing. A keyword planner clusters terms into topics, an optimization score guides edits, and tools help with rewriting, product descriptions, and social content. The platform includes brand voice settings, a plagiarism checker, and a feature for tracking content performance. Teams can collaborate and manage projects in the app.

Scalenut has a free trial and paid plans based on the number of articles and features. Output needs human editing for accuracy, insight, and originality, and optimization scores do not guarantee rankings. Alternatives include Frase, Surfer, Koala, Writesonic, Jasper, and MarketMuse. Scalenut suits small marketing teams and agencies who want research and writing in one tool.
