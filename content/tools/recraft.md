Recraft is aimed at designers. Besides raster images, it can produce true vector graphics, such as logos, icons, and illustrations, that remain sharp at any size and can be edited in design software. Style controls keep outputs consistent across a project.

Brand features let a team upload colors, fonts, and example images so generations follow a style. The model handles text in images well, which helps with posters and mockups. Editing tools allow changing parts of an image, removing backgrounds, and upscaling.

Recraft has a free tier with daily credits and paid plans. Commercial rights depend on the plan. Alternatives include Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, Ideogram, GPT Image, and Canva. Recraft suits designers who need vectors and consistent style.
