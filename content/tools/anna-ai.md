Anna presents itself as an adaptive AI companion. Instead of treating every chat as new, it tries to remember what you have told it and adjusts its tone and suggestions as it learns your preferences and goals. That continuity is the main selling point of companion-style apps.

People use apps like this for conversation, motivation, planning, and reflection. They differ from task-focused assistants in that they emphasize an ongoing relationship. Mainstream assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude have also added memory, so the line between categories is blurring.

Little independent information about Anna is available, so read the privacy policy and check what data is stored before sharing personal details. AI companions are not a replacement for professional mental health care or for human connection. Alternatives include Replika, Pi, Nomi, Character.AI, and the memory features of general assistants. Anna suits adults curious about a personalized AI companion.
