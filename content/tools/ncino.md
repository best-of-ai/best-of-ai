nCino is cloud banking software. A bank uses it to manage the full lending process, from application to documents, analysis, approval, and closing, replacing paper and email chains. It covers commercial, consumer, and mortgage lending and account opening.

AI and automation reduce manual work. Models extract financial data from documents, assess credit risk, and flag items for review, and agents help with tasks like drafting credit memos. Dashboards give management visibility across the portfolio.

nCino is sold to banks and credit unions on enterprise contracts and is built on the Salesforce platform. Implementations take time. Competitors include Finastra, Blend, Temenos, Q2, and Abrigo. nCino suits financial institutions modernizing lending.
