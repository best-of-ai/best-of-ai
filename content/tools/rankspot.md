RankSpot gives an agent such as Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex or n8n one MCP link to SEO and AI-search tools. It tracks rankings against competitors and checks whether your brand is cited in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google.

It researches, writes and publishes articles to WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Ghost and others, and requests indexing. Backlink outreach emails go out after you approve them, and replies for Reddit, Quora and LinkedIn are drafted for you to post yourself. Scheduled automations, per-client memory and an agency mode are included. Site fixes are marked as coming soon.

It is free to start and pay as you go, with no subscription. Each AI visibility check, article, found editor email and sent outreach email has its own per-use rate, and there is a small minimum top-up. The site positions it as a replacement for a stack of Semrush, Ahrefs and Surfer plus outreach tools. Marketers, business owners and agencies are the audience.
