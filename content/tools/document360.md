Document360 helps companies maintain help centers and internal documentation. Writers use an editor with categories, versioning, and review workflows, then publish to a branded site that customers or employees can search. Analytics show what people look for and which articles fall short.

AI makes content easier to find and keep up to date. Semantic search finds answers by meaning, a chatbot answers questions from the documentation, and suggestions point out gaps and outdated articles. Writers can also use AI to draft and improve content. Integrations connect with helpdesk and chat tools.

Document360 has a free plan for small use and paid tiers by project and features. It is aimed at software and support teams, and creative content may need other tools. Alternatives include Zendesk Guide, Helpjuice, GitBook, Confluence, and ReadMe. Document360 suits product and support teams running help centers.
