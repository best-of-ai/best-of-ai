PDF.ai lets people interact with documents. A user uploads a PDF, and an AI answers questions about it, with references to the pages where the answers appear. It can summarize chapters, pull out key points, and extract data.

It is used for papers, contracts, reports, and manuals, where reading everything is time consuming. Page citations let users verify answers, which matters because language models can misread or invent details.

PDF.ai has a free plan with limits and paid tiers. Sensitive documents deserve a check of the privacy policy. Alternatives include ChatPDF, NotebookLM, Humata, Claude and ChatGPT with file uploads, and AnythingLLM for local use. PDF.ai suits people who work with long PDFs.
