Talkdesk provides the software contact centers use to handle calls and messages. Customers reach an agent by phone, chat, or messaging, and the platform routes them, records interactions, and reports on performance. Cloud delivery makes setup quicker than legacy systems.

AI is part of each layer. Virtual agents resolve common requests, agent assist suggests replies and surfaces knowledge during calls, quality tools score conversations automatically, and forecasting helps schedule staff. Industry versions serve healthcare, financial services, and retail.

Talkdesk is sold on subscription per seat with modules. Alternatives include Genesys, NICE, Five9, Amazon Connect, and Twilio Flex. Talkdesk suits mid-sized and large customer service operations.
