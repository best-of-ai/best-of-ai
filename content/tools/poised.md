Poised is a communication coach for online meetings. During a call, the app runs privately on the user's device, tracks how they speak, and shows real-time prompts such as slowing down or reducing filler words. After the call, it provides a summary and trends over time.

Practice features let users rehearse presentations or interviews and get analysis. It is designed for professionals, students, and non-native English speakers who want to improve how they come across.

Poised has a free tier and paid plans. Feedback focuses on delivery rather than content, and real-time prompts can be distracting for some. Alternatives include Yoodli, Orai, Speeko, Otter's analytics, and a human coach. Poised suits people working on speaking skills.
