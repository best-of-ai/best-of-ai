What file formats does Text2Resume support?
Text2Resume supports virtually any format you have your resume in. Copy-paste unstructured text, upload PDFs, or even take screenshots of your resume - our AI system will automatically extract and structure your information.

Which job boards does Text2Resume support?
Text2Resume supports LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Lever, Indeed, ZipRecruiter, and Workday job boards. Simply paste any job URL and our system will automatically extract all relevant information to optimize your resume for that specific position.

How do AI suggestions work?
Our AI analyzes both your resume and the job description to suggest targeted improvements using Claude 3.5 Sonnet. This includes strategic skill additions and removals, bullet point enhancements, content reorganization, and category optimization. Each suggestion is designed to be conflict-free so they can be applied in any order. All suggestions include clear reasoning and can be previewed and applied with one click.

What layout customization options are available?
Text2Resume offers pixel-perfect control over your resume layout. You can adjust spacing between elements, fine-tune margins, customize alignment, and control the width of columns. Our system provides interactive controls for these adjustments with real-time preview, ensuring your resume looks exactly the way you want while remaining ATS-friendly.

What resume templates are available?
Text2Resume offers a variety of professional templates including modern, minimal, traditional, and YellowPlate designs. Each template is optimized for specific industries like tech, law, education, and government positions. All templates are ATS-friendly while looking visually appealing to human recruiters.

How do you handle my data?
Your data is encrypted and securely stored. We only use your information to provide and improve our service. We never share your personal data with third parties. You can delete your data at any time from your account settings.
