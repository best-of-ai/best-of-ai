VEED is an online video editor that makes common edits simple. You upload clips, trim and arrange them on a timeline, add text, music, and effects, and export in formats for different platforms. It includes a screen and webcam recorder for tutorials and messages, templates for social videos, and stock media. Everything runs in the browser, which allows collaboration without installing software.

AI features are a big part of the draw. Auto subtitles transcribe and style captions in many languages, background removal and noise cleanup polish footage, eye contact correction makes you appear to look at the camera, and AI avatars and voices generate presenter videos from scripts. Tools for translation and dubbing help reach other markets, and an assistant can clean up speech by removing filler words and silences.

VEED has a free plan with watermarks and limits and paid plans with higher resolution, longer exports, and more AI. It's easier than desktop editors but less powerful for complex projects. CapCut offers many comparable features for less, Descript is better for transcript-based editing, Kapwing and Canva are close alternatives, and Adobe Premiere Pro serves professional work. VEED suits marketers and creators who edit often and want a simple online tool.
