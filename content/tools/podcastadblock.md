PodcastAdBlock is the simplest way to enjoy your favourite shows without all the noise. Using advanced AI, it automatically detects and removes both auto-inserted and host-read ads from any podcast feed. That means no more mattress promos, VPN shoutouts, or endless sponsorship reads breaking the flow of your listening.

Key Features:
- Ad-Free Listening: Strips out host-read ads and dynamically inserted spots.
- Universal Compatibility: Works with Apple Podcasts, Overcast, Pocket Casts, and any app that accepts a custom RSS feed.
 -Time Saver: Cut down listening time by removing up to 15 minutes of ads per hour.
- Easy Setup: Just search for your show inside PodcastAdBlock, grab the new feed URL, and drop it into your podcast player.

Benefits:
You’ll reclaim your time, keep your focus on the stories you love, and enjoy podcasts the way they were meant to be heard, without interruptions.

How It Works:
Our AI scans each episode, intelligently detects advertising segments, and generates a clean feed URL that plays seamlessly in your favourite podcast app. Nothing to install, nothing to configure, just copy, paste, and listen.
