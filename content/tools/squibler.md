Squibler is a writing platform for longer projects. Authors plan a book with outlines and a corkboard, write in a distraction-free editor, and track characters, settings, and plot threads. An AI assistant generates outlines, suggests plot developments, and drafts text on request.

It targets novelists, nonfiction authors, and scriptwriters, with templates for different forms and export to formats ready for self-publishing. Collaboration features let editors comment.

Squibler has a free trial and paid plans. AI-drafted text should be edited heavily, and publishing platforms ask authors to disclose AI-generated content. Alternatives include Scrivener, Sudowrite, Atticus, Novelcrafter, and Google Docs. Squibler suits authors organizing long manuscripts.
