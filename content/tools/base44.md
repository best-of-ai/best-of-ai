Base44 lets someone describe an app in a chat and get a working version. The platform generates the interface, data model, logic, and authentication, hosts it, and lets the user keep refining by asking for changes. Because the backend is included, there is no need to wire up separate services.

It is popular for internal tools, simple customer portals, and prototypes. Users can add integrations, such as email and payments, and publish with a custom domain. The platform is part of Wix, which gives it a route to a large base of small business customers.

Base44 has a free tier and paid plans based on credits and features. AI-generated apps can have bugs, and security and data handling need review before real customer data is involved. Complex products may outgrow a prompt-driven builder. Alternatives include Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Bubble, and v0. Base44 suits non-developers who want to turn ideas into apps quickly.
