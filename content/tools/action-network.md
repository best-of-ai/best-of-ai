Organizers build action pages, collect signatures, and send email to supporters. The platform tracks who took which action, which helps with follow-up. Nonprofits can use it at reduced cost.

It is meant for progressive causes and has simple tools compared with a full campaign CRM.

NationBuilder and Mailchimp overlap in places. Action Network suits campaign managers and community organizers.
