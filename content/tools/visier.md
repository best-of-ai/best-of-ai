Visier gathers data from HR systems into a model built for workforce questions. HR teams and managers explore metrics such as turnover, hiring speed, diversity, and compensation, and see how they vary by team and over time. Prebuilt analyses save the effort of building reports from scratch.

AI predicts who may leave, models the effect of decisions, and answers questions in plain language through a virtual assistant. Planning tools support headcount and cost scenarios, and integrations pull data from systems such as Workday and SAP.

Visier is sold on enterprise subscriptions. Predictions about individuals raise privacy and fairness concerns that organizations must handle. Alternatives include Workday Prism, One Model, Orgvue, Peakon, and Lattice analytics. Visier suits HR teams in large organizations.
