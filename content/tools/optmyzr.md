Optmyzr is software for people who run paid search and paid social accounts. It connects to Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, Amazon, Meta, and other platforms and gives you tools for automation, monitoring, audits, and reporting, so routine optimization work does not eat the week.

Sidekick is its natural-language copilot, where you can ask questions about an account and get answers. Language-model features also help optimize ad text. Agencies, freelancers, in-house teams, and enterprises use it, and it is built so a PPC specialist stays in control of changes.

A 14-day free trial is available without a credit card, with paid plans after that. Adalysis is the closest competitor for PPC management, and Madgicx leans toward Meta. Optmyzr is best for specialists who manage several accounts and want to standardize their checks.
