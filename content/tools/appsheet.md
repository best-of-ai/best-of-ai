AppSheet lets people create business apps without programming. You point it at data in Google Sheets, Excel, a database, or another source, and AppSheet generates an app with views, forms, and actions. Typical uses include field service, inspections, inventory tracking, approvals, and data collection, where workers need to capture information on a phone, with photos, barcodes, GPS, and signatures.

AI helps create apps. Gemini can build an app from a plain-language description, and an assistant can suggest improvements and write expressions. Automation features, called bots, trigger actions such as sending emails, updating records, or creating documents when data changes, and integrations connect with Google Workspace, Apps Script, and other services. Governance features cover access control and deployment for organizations.

AppSheet has a free option for prototyping and paid plans per user, with enterprise options through Google Cloud. Apps are tied to AppSheet's platform, and complex needs can exceed what no-code can do. Microsoft Power Apps is the closest competitor, Glide and Softr build apps from spreadsheets, Airtable has its own interface builder, and FlutterFlow offers more custom design. AppSheet suits organizations already using Google Workspace.
