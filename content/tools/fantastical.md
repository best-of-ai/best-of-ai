Fantastical is known for natural language entry. A user types a phrase such as lunch with Sam next Tuesday at noon, and the app creates the event with the right time, place, and attendees. It shows calendars from many accounts in one view, including Google, iCloud, and Exchange, along with tasks and reminders.

Scheduling features help with coordination. Users can share availability and let others book time, propose several slots to a group, and attach video conference details automatically. Newer AI features help with preparation for meetings and smart suggestions, and the app integrates with other tools through its calendar sets and openings.

Fantastical has a free tier with core features and paid plans for advanced scheduling, team tools, and integrations. It is built for Apple platforms, with a web and Windows presence that has grown more slowly. Alternatives include Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Notion Calendar, Calendly, and Morgen. Fantastical suits Apple users who want a polished calendar.
