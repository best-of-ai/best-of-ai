Talkspace lets people get mental health care remotely. Users complete an assessment, are matched with a licensed therapist, and can communicate through messaging, live video, or audio sessions, which makes treatment convenient and sometimes more affordable than in-person visits. Psychiatry services provide evaluations and medication management where appropriate.

A major difference from some competitors is insurance coverage: many users can pay with commercial insurance or Medicare plans through Talkspace, and employers and health plans offer it as a benefit. The company has added AI tools for matching, documentation, and safety monitoring, and is exploring ways AI can support providers while keeping therapy human. It publishes research on outcomes.

Talkspace pricing depends on whether you use insurance or pay out of pocket, and plans differ in messaging and session frequency. Text-based therapy works for some but not everyone, and online care is not suited for emergencies; people in crisis should contact local emergency services or a crisis line. Alternatives include BetterHelp, which is not insurance-based, Headway and Alma for finding in-network therapists, Teladoc, and local providers. Talkspace suits people wanting insured, flexible therapy.
