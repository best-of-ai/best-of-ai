Duet is built on the idea that an assistant should not wait for you to ask. Each morning it sends a brief on what matters across your connected apps, and it checks those accounts every few hours for new items that need attention. You can hand it tasks, and it works on its own virtual machine with a browser, so your laptop or phone does not need to stay on. It keeps working while you are away and tells you when something is done or needs a decision.

Control is part of the design. Duet asks for your approval before it sends messages, makes payments, signs documents, or deletes anything, so the highest-risk actions stay in your hands. It draws on a large catalog of connectors and plugins to reach email, calendars, documents, and business tools. It runs as its own app on iPhone and Android and on the web, separate from the standard Claude chat.

Duet is a research preview with access through a waitlist, starting with paid Claude subscribers in the United States. Its usage counts against your normal Claude limits. As a proactive agent with access to your accounts, it raises privacy and security questions that you should weigh before connecting sensitive systems. ChatGPT Work and the agent features in Microsoft Copilot and Google's Gemini are comparable products, and many people may find chat-based Claude enough for less continuous help.
