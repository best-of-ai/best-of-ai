Wisdom Panel offers genetic testing for dogs and cats. An owner swabs the pet's cheek, mails the sample, and receives a report on breed ancestry, family tree, and traits. The report also screens for genetic health conditions the pet may carry or be at risk for, which can inform decisions with a veterinarian.

The company draws on a large database of breed samples and research, and sells tests through retailers, directly to owners, and through veterinary clinics. Health screening results are meant to start conversations with a vet, and some conditions come with guidance on diet or monitoring. The brand belongs to Mars Petcare, which also owns other pet care businesses.

Tests are paid, with different tiers that add health panels. Breed results for mixed-breed dogs are estimates, and genetic risk does not mean a disease will develop. Not every condition is covered, and quality varies between companies. Alternatives include Embark, Basepaws for cats, and DNA My Dog. Wisdom Panel suits owners curious about their pet's ancestry and health.
