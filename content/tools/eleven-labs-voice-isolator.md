ElevenLabs Sound Effects creates audio from a sentence. You describe the sound you need, such as a door creaking in an empty house or a crowd cheering in a stadium, and the model generates several options in seconds, which you can adjust for length and download. This helps video editors, game developers, podcasters, and filmmakers who otherwise search libraries for the right clip or record effects themselves.

The company also offers a Voice Isolator that separates speech from background noise and music, helping clean up interviews and field recordings. These tools fit alongside ElevenLabs' text-to-speech, dubbing, and music features, so a creator can produce a full soundtrack in one place. An API allows developers to generate effects inside apps and games.

Sound effects generation is included in ElevenLabs plans with credit limits, and paid plans cover commercial use. Results can be hit and miss for very specific or complex sounds, and layering several effects often gives the best result. Alternatives include Epidemic Sound and Artlist for libraries, Freesound for free clips, Stability's audio tools, and traditional foley recording. The Voice Isolator competes with Adobe Podcast Enhance Speech and Krisp. This suits creators who need quick custom sounds.
