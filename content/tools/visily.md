Visily is built for people who need to mock up interfaces quickly without learning a full design tool. You can upload a screenshot of an app, a hand-drawn sketch, or a description of a screen, and the AI creates an editable wireframe made of real components. A library of templates, UI kits, and smart components helps assemble screens for web and mobile products.

Collaboration is part of the appeal. Teams can edit together, leave comments, and share clickable prototypes with stakeholders, and designs can be exported or moved into Figma for refinement. Product managers, business analysts, and founders use it to communicate ideas visually and gather feedback early, before engineering work starts.

Visily has a free plan with limited projects and paid plans for teams. The output is intended for wireframes and early concepts, not final visual design, and it's less capable than full design platforms. Alternatives include Figma, Uizard, Balsamiq for low-fidelity wireframes, Google Stitch, Claude Design, and Whimsical. Visily suits non-designers drafting product ideas.
