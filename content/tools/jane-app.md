Jane is practice management software for health and wellness clinics. Patients book online, receive reminders, and pay through the system, while practitioners chart visits, manage insurance billing, and run reports. A telehealth feature supports virtual visits.

The AI assistant helps with documentation. A clinician dictates or types notes, and Jane structures them into a SOAP note, saving time at the end of the day. Multi-practitioner clinics can manage schedules, rooms, and staff in one place.

Jane is sold by subscription per practitioner. It is built for allied health and not for hospitals. Alternatives include Cliniko, SimplePractice, Kareo, Practice Better, and WebPT for physical therapy. Jane suits small and mid-sized clinics.
