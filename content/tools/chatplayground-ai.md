ChatPlayground addresses a simple question: which model is best for this task? Instead of opening several chatbots in separate tabs, a user types a prompt once and sees answers from multiple models side by side, including offerings from OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, DeepSeek, and open models.

That helps with writing, coding, and research tasks where quality varies by model. Users can save conversations, keep prompts organized, and switch between models while working. For people who pay for several subscriptions, a single place can be convenient.

The service has a free tier and paid plans. The list of models changes as providers release new versions, and accessing models through an intermediary may differ from using the official apps. Alternatives include Poe, OpenRouter's chat, LMArena for blind comparisons, TypingMind, and Chatbot UIs like Open WebUI. ChatPlayground AI suits people who compare models regularly.
