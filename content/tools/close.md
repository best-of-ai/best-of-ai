Close takes the position that sales reps should do their work from one screen. Calls, emails, and texts happen inside the CRM and are logged automatically, so there is less manual data entry and a complete history on each lead. Power dialers let reps work through lists quickly.

Sequences automate multi-step outreach that mixes email, calls, and texts, and workflows keep pipelines moving. AI features transcribe and summarize calls, suggest follow-ups, and score leads, which helps managers coach and helps reps decide where to spend time. Reporting shows activity and results by rep.

Close is paid and priced per user, with a trial. It is designed for small and mid-sized teams selling by phone and email, and is less suited to complex enterprise selling. Alternatives include HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, Copper, and Attio. Close suits inside sales teams that live on the phone.
