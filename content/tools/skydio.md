Skydio drones fly themselves safely. Cameras and onboard computing let the drone see its surroundings and avoid obstacles in real time, even in complex places such as under bridges or around structures, which reduces crashes and operator skill demands.

Software supports missions: automated inspection routes, 3D scans of structures, and live video for first responders. Docks let drones launch and land on their own for repeated tasks. Customers include police and fire departments, utilities, infrastructure inspectors, and defense agencies.

Skydio is sold as hardware plus software subscriptions, to enterprise and government buyers. Drone use is subject to aviation and privacy rules. Alternatives include DJI, Parrot, Teal Drones, Flock Safety, and Autel. Skydio suits organizations flying drones for inspection and public safety.
