Agents view a client, quote and bind policies, track renewals, and manage documents in one system. Accounting and carrier downloads reduce data entry. Reporting shows book of business and producer results.

It is used by mid-size and large agencies, with custom pricing.

HawkSoft and EZLynx are alternatives. Applied Epic suits insurance agents at larger agencies.
