ThoughtSpot built its product around search. Instead of building reports by dragging fields, business users type a question such as sales by region last quarter, and ThoughtSpot generates the right chart from the connected data warehouse, with the ability to drill down and refine. This lowers the barrier for people who are not analysts and reduces the queue of requests for data teams.

Generative AI has expanded the approach. Spotter acts as an AI analyst that understands questions in conversation, runs analyses, explains results, and proposes follow-up questions, while agents can answer questions inside other tools. Liveboards combine charts into shareable dashboards that update from live data, and an embedded analytics offering lets companies put these capabilities in their own products.

ThoughtSpot connects directly to cloud data platforms like Snowflake, BigQuery, and Databricks, querying data in place. It's sold by subscription to mid-sized and large organizations with custom pricing. Quality depends on a well-modeled dataset with clear definitions, and AI answers should be validated. Competitors include Power BI with Copilot, Tableau Agent, Looker, Sigma, and Domo. ThoughtSpot suits organizations that want self-service, conversational analytics.
