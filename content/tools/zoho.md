Zoho builds a wide range of business applications on its own platform. Companies can use Zoho CRM for sales, Books for accounting, Desk for support, Projects for work tracking, Mail for email, People for HR, and dozens of other apps, many of which integrate with one another. Zoho One bundles the whole suite for a single per-user price.

Zia is the AI layer. It predicts which leads are likely to convert, detects anomalies in data, drafts replies, and answers questions in natural language, and Zoho has added a generative assistant and agents that can act across apps. The company also supports the option of running models on its own infrastructure for customers who want data kept in their region.

Zoho is privately held and positions itself on privacy, with no ad-based business. Many apps have free or low-cost plans, which suits small businesses. Depth varies between products, and some are less polished than category leaders. Alternatives include Microsoft 365 and Dynamics, Google Workspace, Salesforce, HubSpot, and Odoo. Zoho suits small and mid-sized businesses wanting an integrated, affordable suite.
