Together AI hosts open models such as Llama, Qwen, and DeepSeek, and offers a developer-friendly API compatible with common formats. Its inference stack is tuned for speed, and options range from pay-per-token serverless access to dedicated endpoints for steady workloads.

Customization is a second focus. Teams can fine-tune models on their data, train at scale on GPU clusters, and run batch jobs at lower cost. The company's research contributions in efficient attention and inference feed into its products.

Together AI is pay as you go with enterprise agreements. Its catalog follows the open model ecosystem and lacks closed models.
