Later started as a way to plan Instagram feeds visually. Users drag photos and videos onto a calendar, see how the grid will look, write captions, and schedule posts to go out automatically. It has since expanded to other networks, including TikTok, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Facebook, and X, with tools to manage a content library, collaborate with teammates, and track results.

Other features support social growth. A link-in-bio page lets creators send followers to multiple destinations, analytics show which posts perform best and when audiences are most active, and AI helps write captions, suggest hashtags, and repurpose content. The company also owns an influencer marketing platform, which helps brands find creators and manage campaigns.

Later has a limited free plan and paid plans that scale by social sets and users. It is less feature-rich in community management and listening than enterprise tools like Sprout Social, and visual planning is its signature. Alternatives include Buffer, Hootsuite, Planoly, Metricool, and Flick. Later suits creators and small brands whose strategy centers on visual platforms.
