Tempus builds a bridge between lab results and clinical decisions. It sequences tumors and other samples, combines the results with clinical data, and provides physicians with reports to help guide treatment choices, such as matching patients to targeted therapies or clinical trials. Over time, the company has assembled a large library of linked clinical and molecular data.

That data supports a second business: working with pharmaceutical companies. Tempus licenses de-identified data and builds models to help discover and develop drugs, find suitable patients for trials, and analyze outcomes. It has expanded through acquisitions into digital pathology with Paige and into radiology with the former Arterys platform, aiming to combine images, genomics, and clinical records into foundation models.

Tempus is a public company that earns revenue from testing and data licensing. Outcomes rely on the quality of data and on clinical validation, and uses of patient data attract privacy scrutiny. Competitors and peers include Foundation Medicine, Caris Life Sciences, Guardant Health, Flatiron Health, and Recursion in drug discovery. Tempus suits oncologists and pharmaceutical developers using data-driven precision medicine.
