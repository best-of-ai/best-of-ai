DocScrib transforms clinical documentation into a fast, accurate, and intelligent process using AI. It listens to patient-provider conversations during consults and instantly generates specialty-formatted notes using real-time speech recognition and medical NLP. Clinicians can edit, approve, and submit documentation in minutes, not hours—saving up to 90 minutes per day. With seamless EHR integration, DocScrib ensures no duplication of work and maintains full compliance with data security protocols. Its personalization engine adapts to your style, specialty, and workflows, improving over time. It also helps with coding, billing, and audit readiness by suggesting appropriate ICD-10 and CPT codes. Whether you're in primary care or neurology, DocScrib delivers faster, smarter, and safer documentation.
