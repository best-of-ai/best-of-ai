Runway's Gen-3 Alpha model produces video clips with the kind of motion quality and photorealism that makes it genuinely useful for pre-visualisation and concept work, not just experimentation. You describe a scene — or provide a reference image — and Runway generates a short clip with coherent movement, consistent lighting, and cinematic framing. The output isn't always perfect, but it's good enough that production teams use it to pitch ideas before committing to expensive shoots.

What distinguishes Runway from standalone video generators is the full creative suite around the generation model. Inpainting lets you remove an object from existing footage. Green Screen works without an actual green screen, cutting subjects from video by AI matting. Motion Tracking attaches text or graphics to moving objects. Audio enhancement, caption generation, and image upscaling are all built into the same interface. For post-production workflows that involve multiple AI tasks, having them integrated saves significant time versus using separate tools.

The free tier gives 125 one-time generation credits. Standard plans start at $12/month for 625 credits per month, with Pro at $28/month for higher credit volumes and longer clips. Credits are consumed per second of video generated. Teams working at volume — agencies, studios, content departments — typically need the Unlimited plan to avoid credit anxiety. Runway is available entirely in the browser with no local GPU required.
