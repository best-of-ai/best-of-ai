Grammarly sits in your browser, your email client, your Google Docs, and your desktop apps — it follows your writing everywhere rather than waiting in a separate tab. The free tier catches spelling, punctuation, and basic grammar in real time. What makes the Premium tier worth using is the deeper feedback: clarity scores, tone analysis that tells you whether your email reads as confident or passive, and full-sentence rewrites that fix the structure of an idea rather than just its surface errors.

Grammarly's generative AI layer, added in 2023, lets you do more than fix existing text. Highlight a section and ask it to make it more formal, shorter, more persuasive, or restructured as a bulleted list. Open the AI assistant to draft from scratch — a response to a customer complaint, an introductory paragraph for a report, or a meeting follow-up. The tone detector helps non-native English speakers in particular, flagging when business communication sounds unintentionally abrupt or overly casual.

Business plans add a shared style guide, brand tone settings, and analytics showing which team members are flagging the most writing issues — useful for onboarding or identifying communication patterns across a team. Grammarly Business supports SAML SSO and admin dashboards for enterprise rollouts. The free tier is genuinely useful for basic writing checks; Premium at $12/month is where the AI writing and tone features become available. Individual writers, academics, customer support teams, and sales professionals are the heaviest users.
