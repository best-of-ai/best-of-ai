Motion tries to take planning off your hands. You add tasks with deadlines, durations, and priorities, connect your calendar, and Motion places each task into open time on your calendar. If a meeting gets added or a task runs long, it rebuilds the schedule automatically so you always see a realistic plan for the day. The goal is to protect focus time and make sure deadlines are met without constant manual rearranging.

The product has expanded into a wider work platform. It includes project management with timelines and dependencies, a meeting scheduler with booking links, and note-taking features for meetings. AI assistants and agents can help create tasks from emails or messages, answer questions about your workload, and handle routine coordination. Teams use it to plan shared work and see capacity.

Motion is a paid product with a free trial, priced per user, and more expensive than basic task apps. Automatic scheduling works well for people with a stable set of tasks, though some find it rigid or dislike giving up control over their day. Alternatives include Reclaim.ai, Akiflow, Sunsama, Clockwise, and Todoist with calendar integration. Motion suits busy professionals who want an automated planner.
