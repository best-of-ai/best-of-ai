Users load data, pick analyses such as t-tests or regression from menus, and get results that update live. The R syntax is available for reproducibility. Modules extend it.

It suits teaching and standard analyses, but advanced work usually moves to R.

JASP and SPSS are alternatives. jamovi suits statisticians and students who want a free tool.
