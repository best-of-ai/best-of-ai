Betterworks supports how companies manage performance throughout the year. Employees set goals or OKRs that link to company priorities, managers hold regular check-ins, and feedback and recognition are captured along the way. Review cycles then draw on this record, so evaluations rely less on recent memory.

AI features help with the writing and analysis. An assistant can improve goal statements, summarize a year of feedback into review drafts, and suggest coaching points, while analytics show goal progress and engagement across teams. Integrations connect with HR systems like Workday and collaboration tools such as Slack and Microsoft Teams.

Betterworks is sold to mid-sized and large organizations on subscription. Tools like this only work if managers use them consistently, and AI-written reviews should be edited by the person responsible. Alternatives include Lattice, 15Five, Culture Amp, Workday Performance, and Leapsome. Betterworks suits organizations that run goal-based performance programs.
