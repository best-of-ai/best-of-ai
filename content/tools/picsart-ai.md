Picsart began as a mobile photo editor and grew into a broad creative suite. Users edit photos with filters, collages, stickers, and text, make videos, and design social posts from templates. A community lets people share and remix content, which helped it spread among younger creators and small businesses.

AI is now central. Tools generate images from prompts, remove backgrounds and objects, expand pictures, replace elements, enhance and upscale photos, and create stylized avatars. Text-to-video and animation tools turn ideas into clips, and an AI writer helps with captions. Picsart also provides APIs and plugins so other platforms can use its image tools, and teams can use brand kits and shared workspaces.

Picsart has a free tier with ads and limited features and paid Gold and team plans that unlock premium content, higher resolution, and more AI credits. Its depth of effects and stickers is a plus for social content, and its design capabilities are less advanced than Canva's for layouts. Competitors include Canva, Adobe Express, Fotor, PicMonkey, and Pixlr. Check commercial licensing terms for stock content and AI outputs.
