Apollo is one of the most used tools for outbound sales. At its core is a searchable database of business contacts and companies, which you can filter by job title, industry, company size, location, technology used, and more. You export the matches or push them into sequences, and Apollo supplies emails and phone numbers. Startups and small sales teams like that it bundles data and outreach in one affordable product.

The engagement tools let reps run multi-step sequences across email, calls, and LinkedIn tasks, schedule meetings, and track replies. A built-in dialer and call recording support phone prospecting, and a lightweight CRM and integrations with Salesforce and HubSpot keep records in sync. AI helps write personalized emails, summarize accounts, score leads, and suggest who to contact. A Chrome extension pulls contact details while you browse LinkedIn or company sites.

Apollo has a free plan with limited credits, and paid plans that add credits, sequences, and features per user. Data accuracy varies, and bounce rates can be high without verification, so list hygiene matters. Cold outreach carries deliverability and legal responsibilities, and bad practices can get a domain blocked. ZoomInfo has larger enterprise data at a higher price, Amplemarket and Clay offer more AI-driven workflows, and Lusha and Seamless.AI focus on contact data.
