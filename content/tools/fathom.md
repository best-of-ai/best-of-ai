Fathom is known for making meeting notes free. It joins your Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams calls, records them, and creates a transcript in real time. You can highlight important moments during the call with a click, and within seconds of the meeting ending, it produces a summary with decisions, action items, and key points that you can share. The free plan includes unlimited recordings and transcripts, which set it apart from competitors that cap free use.

Paid plans add deeper AI features. These include custom summary templates, more detailed answers to questions about your meetings, and integrations that push notes into CRMs like Salesforce and HubSpot and into tools like Slack and Notion. Sales and customer success teams use it to capture call details and update records automatically, and managers can review calls and share clips without sitting through the whole recording.

Fathom's free plan limits some AI summary features and storage options, and teams that want advanced analytics and admin controls pay for higher tiers. As with any recorder, tell participants that the call is being recorded and follow privacy rules. Fireflies, Otter, and Avoma are competitors, and Gong targets enterprise revenue teams. Fathom is a good starting point for individuals and small teams who want reliable notes at low cost.
