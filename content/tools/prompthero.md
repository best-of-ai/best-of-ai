PromptHero started as a search engine for AI image prompts. You can browse a huge gallery of generated images, each shown with the prompt and settings that created it, and search for styles or subjects. That makes it a learning tool: you can see how others phrase things to get a certain look, and adapt their prompts to your own ideas.

The site has added features around the community. Users upload their creations, follow artists, and join challenges, and a generator lets you create images directly on the site using several models. Courses and guides teach prompt writing and workflow tips for different tools, and a paid tier offers more generations and features.

PromptHero is free to browse, with paid plans for generation and extras. Prompts that work with one model version may not work well with others, and the gallery contains a mix of quality and content styles. Check usage rights for images and prompts. Alternatives include Lexica, PromptBase, Civitai for Stable Diffusion models and examples, OpenArt, and the community galleries of Midjourney and Leonardo.ai. PromptHero suits beginners learning prompt writing for image generators.
