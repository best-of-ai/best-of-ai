AIDir.wiki collects information about AI products in a wiki format. Each entry describes a tool or model, notes what it is for, lists key features and pricing information, and links to the official site. Because it is community-maintained, contributors can add new tools and update existing entries as products change, which is meant to keep information fresher than a static list.

The wiki structure encourages cross-linking, so related tools, alternatives, and categories connect. Visitors can browse by category or search for a specific product, and use it as a reference when comparing options. It joins a broad field of AI directories, from curated lists to large databases, and positions itself as an open, collaborative resource.

The site is free. As with any community-edited source, completeness and accuracy depend on contributors, so verify details on the official sites before making decisions. Larger directories such as There's an AI For That, Futurepedia, and Product Hunt offer greater volume, and Best of AI offers curated listings with descriptions and alternatives. AIDir.wiki suits people who like community-driven reference sites.
