Sider began as a browser sidebar for chatting with AI about web pages. Omni Sidebar extends the idea to the whole Mac: a panel that sees the content in the active app and lets users ask questions, summarize, draft replies, or take actions without copying and pasting.

The assistant supports several underlying models and can work with files and screenshots. For people who live in email, documents, and spreadsheets, an assistant that sees the same screen saves steps.

Reading the screen raises privacy considerations, so review permissions and what is sent to the cloud. Sider has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Raycast AI, ChatGPT desktop app, Claude desktop, Apple Intelligence, and Rewind-style tools. Sider Omni Sidebar suits Mac users who want a context-aware assistant.
