Gainsight was one of the first platforms for the customer success discipline. It pulls usage, support, survey, and billing data to produce a health score for every customer, so teams can see who needs attention before a renewal is at risk. Playbooks guide the steps a manager should take for each situation.

AI features predict churn, suggest next actions, summarize account history, and draft communications. Surveys, in-app engagement, and a community product complete the suite, and the company has expanded into customer education. Reports help leaders show the effect of customer success on retention.

Gainsight is sold on custom pricing to mid-sized and large subscription companies, and implementation takes planning. Alternatives include ChurnZero, Totango, Vitally, Planhat, and the customer success features in HubSpot and Salesforce. Gainsight suits established customer success organizations.
