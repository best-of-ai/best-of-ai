Smartsheet is built around the grid. Teams manage projects, requests, and processes in sheets that look like spreadsheets but add features such as attachments, comments, dependencies, and approvals. The same data can be viewed as a Gantt chart, calendar, board, or form, and reports and dashboards summarize progress for leaders. It's used for project plans, marketing calendars, intake processes, and portfolio tracking in many large organizations.

Automation and AI reduce manual work. Rules can send alerts, request approvals, or update fields when conditions are met, and AI helps by generating formulas from plain language, summarizing sheets, creating content, and analyzing data to find trends. Newer features allow building workflows and agents with natural language. Integrations connect with Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Slack, Salesforce, and more.

Smartsheet is paid, with plans by user and feature tier, and enterprise options with governance. It was taken private by investment firms. It's familiar to spreadsheet users but can become unwieldy for very complex projects, and some find it expensive compared with newer tools. Asana, Monday.com, ClickUp, and Airtable are the main competitors, and Microsoft Project serves traditional project management. Smartsheet suits organizations with spreadsheet-based processes.
