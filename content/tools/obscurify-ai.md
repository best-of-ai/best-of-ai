Obscurify.ai sits between you and the big AI providers. Your prompt goes to Obscurify first, which strips identifying details and forwards it to the model under its own credentials. The pitch is that OpenAI, Anthropic or Google never see who is asking. The catalog is listed at 200+ models, and some reviews put it closer to 300, including a few uncensored ones.

You do not need an email address. Guests can chat for free, and a paid account can be just a 16-digit number. You top up a balance with vouchers, and the site accepts Monero, Bitcoin, Lightning or a card. Pricing is usage-based, so you pay for what you send. There is also an API that works with OpenAI-style clients, a Tor hidden service, and an Android app. The web client and the app are open source under GPL-3.0.

Most of the privacy claims come from the operator. The founder says chats are not stored and that payments use blind-signed vouchers, and an independent review could not verify either. That review also found the service keeps IP addresses for 30 days. The company says it launched in November 2023 as CryptoTalks and took the Obscurify name in July 2025, which no other source confirms. If you need strong anonymity, read the privacy policy first and treat the claims as promises, not proof.
