Adside bundles several agents into one workspace. They research competitors with ad boards and an ad library, watch social channels such as X, Reddit and G2, write campaign copy from a brief, and make static and video ads. You can import creatives in bulk and have them resized for each placement.

The agents keep an eye on your accounts and rotate creatives as performance changes, and reports summarize the results. It connects to Claude Code and ChatGPT, and it offers an MCP server and a Slack agent.

There is no free tier. The AI Agency plan is priced per brand per month, with unlimited users and creative credits included. The Managed Service adds a human expert and is billed as a percentage of ad spend with a monthly minimum. Startups, brands and agencies running several brands are the intended users.
