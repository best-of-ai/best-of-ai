Tripo focuses on speed and consistency. Enter a description or upload an image, and it generates a 3D model in seconds to a minute, with options for style and level of detail. The models are meant to be usable in games, animation, 3D printing, and e-commerce, and the platform provides downloads in common formats like GLB, FBX, and OBJ.

Features go beyond the initial generation. Tools can create cleaner topology for animation, generate or replace textures from a prompt, add skeletons so characters can be posed, and apply animations. A segmentation option separates a model into parts for editing. An API and integrations with Blender, Unity, Unreal, and other applications fit it into creative pipelines, and a community library offers starting points.

Tripo has a free tier with a limited number of generations and paid plans with more credits and commercial rights. The market for AI 3D generation moves quickly, so quality and capabilities change. Generated models still often need cleanup for production use, especially complex shapes. Competitors include Meshy, Rodin, Luma's tools, Kaedim, and Hunyuan3D, with Blender and Maya for manual modeling. Tripo suits artists and developers who need fast starting points.
