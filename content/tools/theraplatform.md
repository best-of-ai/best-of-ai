Speech, occupational, and mental health therapists use templates for notes and track treatment plans. Clients book online and pay. A built-in video room supports telehealth sessions.

It is a subscription product priced per clinician.

SimplePractice and TherapyNotes are alternatives. TheraPlatform suits speech therapists in private practice.
