AI-Writer takes a different approach from many article generators. You provide a headline and optional keywords, and it creates a full-length article that pulls in information from sources it finds and cites them, so the draft includes references you can check. This responds to a common complaint about AI writing, which is that it makes claims without support.

Additional features support content marketing. It offers SEO analysis to guide the article toward relevant terms, a built-in indicator that shows how much of the text is verifiable against its sources, and tools for creating outlines, rewriting, and summarizing. The tool is aimed at bloggers, affiliate marketers, and agencies that publish many articles.

AI-Writer is a paid product, with plans based on the number of articles. Drafts still require human review: sources can be weak or misread, the writing can be generic, and publishing large volumes of unedited content risks poor performance in search and reader trust. Alternatives include Jasper, Writesonic, Copy.ai, and general assistants like ChatGPT and Claude, which can also cite sources when browsing. AI-Writer suits content teams that want citations built into first drafts.
