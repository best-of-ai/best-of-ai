AirDNA is a data provider for the short-term rental industry. It collects listing and booking signals from Airbnb, Vrbo, and other platforms and turns them into market metrics such as occupancy, average daily rate, revenue per available rental, seasonality, and supply trends for a chosen area.

Investors use its Investment Explorer to model returns on a property before buying, and hosts use it to benchmark their results against competitors. There is also API access for companies that want the data inside their own products, along with tools for monitoring competitors and checking regulations and demand in a market.

Because the data is estimated from public listing signals, accuracy varies by location and property type, so treat the numbers as guidance. Pricing is on the AirDNA site and depends on the plan. AirDNA answers where to operate and what to expect, while PriceLabs handles the nightly rates once you are running.
