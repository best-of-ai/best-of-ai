Perfect Corp makes AI and augmented reality tools for beauty and fashion. Its skin analysis reads a selfie, scores concerns, and recommends products, while virtual try-on shows makeup, hair color, and accessories on the user's face in real time.

Brands embed the technology in their websites, apps, and in-store screens to help shoppers choose and reduce returns. Clinics use simulations to show likely effects of treatments. The company also owns the YouCam consumer apps.

Perfect Corp sells to brands and retailers on enterprise terms. Skin analysis from a photo is not a medical diagnosis, and face data raises privacy questions. Alternatives include ModiFace (L'Oréal), Banuba, Revieve, Haut.AI, and Glamlab. Perfect Corp suits beauty retailers adding virtual try-on.
