NotebookLM is built around the idea of grounding AI in your own sources. You create a notebook and add materials such as PDFs, Google Docs, web pages, YouTube videos, and pasted text. The AI answers questions using only those sources and shows citations that link to the exact passages, which reduces the risk of invented facts and makes answers easy to check. It is popular with students, researchers, writers, and professionals who work with a lot of reading.

It also turns sources into other formats. Audio Overviews generate a conversational podcast-style discussion of your material, video overviews create narrated slides, and other tools produce study guides, briefing documents, timelines, flashcards, quizzes, and mind maps. A research mode can search the web for additional sources to add. Notes you write or save sit alongside the sources.

NotebookLM is free with usage limits, and higher limits and features come with paid Google AI plans and the Plus tier. There is also a version for businesses and schools. Output depends on the sources you provide, and generated audio can sound confident while missing nuance, so verify important details. Alternatives include ChatGPT Projects, Claude Projects, Perplexity Spaces, Microsoft Copilot Notebooks, and Notion AI. NotebookLM is among the best tools for studying a fixed set of documents.
