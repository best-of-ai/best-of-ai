SEOJuice focuses on one SEO chore. Internal links help search engines understand a site and pass authority between pages, but adding them by hand across hundreds of articles is tedious. SEOJuice scans a site's content, finds natural places to link related pages, and inserts the links automatically or for approval.

It installs through a script or plugin and keeps links updated as content grows. Additional tools include basic audits and content suggestions. It appeals to publishers and small businesses that lack time for manual optimization.

SEOJuice has a free trial and paid plans. Automated linking should be monitored for relevance, since poor anchor text can hurt readers. Alternatives include Link Whisper, Yoast, Rank Math, Ahrefs audits, and Semrush. SEOJuice suits sites with large content libraries.
