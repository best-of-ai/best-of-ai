Poe gives access to many AI models without separate accounts. A single app and website lets you switch between leading chatbots, image generators, and video tools, compare answers, and keep your conversations in one place. This is helpful for people who want to try different models and use whichever works best for each task.

Creators can build bots on top of models with custom prompts and knowledge, and publish them for others, with ways to earn revenue based on usage. The platform also offers an app builder and an API so developers can use Poe's models in their projects. Group chats let multiple bots and people interact in one conversation.

Poe has a free tier with daily limits and a subscription that provides a pool of compute points to spend on different models, with more expensive models using more points. The model lineup changes, and some experiences are not as feature-rich as the official apps from each provider, such as file handling or newest tools. Alternatives include ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini individually, Perplexity for model choice, OpenRouter for developers, and TypingMind. Poe suits people who want one place to try many models.
