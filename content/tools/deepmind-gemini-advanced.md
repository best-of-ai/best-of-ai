Gemini Advanced was Google's answer to ChatGPT Plus. When Google rebranded Bard as Gemini, it introduced Advanced as a subscription, sold through Google One, that gave access to its strongest model, longer conversations, and features like file uploads and integration with Gmail and Docs. It also bundled extra Google One storage, which made it attractive to people already paying for cloud storage.

Google later reorganized the plans. Gemini Advanced's features moved into Google AI Pro, and a higher tier called Google AI Ultra provides the most advanced models, agent features, and generous limits for heavy users. The plans include perks such as video generation, deeper research tools, and integrations with Workspace apps, and the exact lineup changes with each product update.

Anyone looking for Gemini Advanced today should look at Google AI Pro or Ultra on Google's subscription page. Prices and features differ by region and change often. Free Gemini is still available with lower limits. Competing paid tiers include ChatGPT Plus and Pro, Claude Pro and Max, and Microsoft Copilot Pro. This entry stays for reference because many older articles and comparisons still use the Gemini Advanced name.
