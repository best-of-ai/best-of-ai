Milan Voice AI generates speech for creators and developers. Users type text and choose or create a voice, with options to clone a voice from a short sample or describe a new one. Multi-speaker mode takes a script with several characters and produces a dialogue, useful for podcasts, audiobooks, and videos.

Typical uses include narration, character voices, and prototypes of voice features. An API supports integration into apps. Voice cloning requires permission from the person whose voice is copied, and synthetic audio should be disclosed where appropriate.

Milan Voice AI has a free tier and paid plans. Independent information is limited, so test voice quality and check terms. Alternatives include ElevenLabs, Fish Audio, Murf, Cartesia, and Kokoro for local use. Milan Voice AI suits creators needing multi-voice audio.
