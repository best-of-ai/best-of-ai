Prospero.ai aims to give individual investors the kind of signals institutions pay for. Its AI scans market data to produce stock picks, recognize price trends, and gauge sentiment, and it surfaces what large institutional traders are doing so you can see where money is moving.

The app is free to use, and the company describes it as built for retail investors. It works as a research starting point, not an adviser. AI signals can be wrong, so check the underlying filings and your own risk tolerance before acting on any suggestion.

Prospero.ai does not describe paid tiers on its main page. Compare it with Seeking Alpha for analyst-written research and TrendSpider for charting. It suits people who want a free way to generate ideas and learn what to look at.
