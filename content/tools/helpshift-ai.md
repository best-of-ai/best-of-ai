Helpshift places support inside the app. Users reach help from a screen in a game or mobile product, without leaving to email, and conversations carry context about the device, account, and recent activity. Agents see this information and respond in the same thread.

AI reduces the load. Intent detection reads messages, classifies the issue, and routes it to the right team, while chatbots answer common questions and collect details before a human steps in. A knowledge base and automation tools support self-service, and analytics track satisfaction and resolution times.

Helpshift is sold to game studios and app companies on custom pricing. It is a specialized product for mobile, with less relevance for web-only businesses. Alternatives include Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, Gladly, and Kustomer. Helpshift suits mobile publishers with large player or user bases.
