Nano Banana is the name Google gave to its Gemini-based image model, and the Pro version targets higher quality. Users describe an image or edit a photo by instruction, such as changing the background or combining subjects, and the model produces a result that keeps people and objects consistent.

It is known for rendering legible text inside images, producing high-resolution output, and following detailed prompts, which makes it useful for posters, infographics, and product mockups. It is available in the Gemini app, through Google AI Studio and the API, and in other Google products.

Access depends on plan and region, with limits on free use, and images carry invisible watermarks to mark them as AI generated. Alternatives include GPT Image, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux, and Meta's Muse Image. Nano Banana Pro suits people who want quick, high-quality image generation and editing.
