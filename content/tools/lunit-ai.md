Lunit applies deep learning to medical images. Insight CXR reads chest X-rays and flags abnormalities such as nodules and signs of tuberculosis, while Insight MMG reviews mammograms to help radiologists detect breast cancer. Radiologists see marked regions and probability scores to support their reading.

The company also works in oncology pathology. Its SCOPE tools analyze tissue slides to characterize the tumor environment, which helps predict how patients may respond to treatments such as immunotherapy. This feeds into drug development and personalized care.

Lunit sells to hospitals, screening programs, and pharmaceutical partners, with regulatory clearances in many markets. AI findings are decision support, and clinicians make the diagnosis. Alternatives include Qure.ai, Aidoc, Annalise.ai, Hologic's AI tools, and iCAD. Lunit suits radiology and cancer screening programs.
