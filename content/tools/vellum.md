Vellum supports teams building AI features for production. Developers and product managers write and compare prompts across models, build workflows that chain steps, and run evaluations against test sets before releasing changes.

Deployment and monitoring tools track versions, costs, and quality, and let teams update prompts without redeploying code. A visual builder and SDK suit both technical and non-technical members.

Vellum has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Langfuse, LangSmith, Braintrust, PromptLayer, and Humanloop, which has shut down. Vellum suits product teams shipping LLM features.
