RoomGPT lets people visualize a space in new styles. A user uploads a photo of a living room or bedroom, chooses a theme such as modern, minimalist, or tropical, and the AI generates redesigned images that keep the room's structure while changing furniture and decor.

Homeowners use it for inspiration before shopping or renovating, and some real estate agents use it for staging ideas. Results show style direction but do not match real products or exact dimensions.

RoomGPT has offered free trials and paid credits. Disclose AI staging in real estate listings where required. Alternatives include Collov AI, Planner 5D, Interior AI, REimagineHome, and Decorilla. RoomGPT suits people exploring room redesign ideas.
