Uplight helps utilities communicate with customers and manage demand. It combines data on how households and businesses use energy with tools for personalized messages, home energy reports, smart thermostat programs, and marketplace offers for efficient appliances. The goal is to reduce peak demand and help customers cut bills, which lowers the need for expensive new capacity.

By acquiring AutoGrid, Uplight added software for managing distributed energy resources such as batteries, solar, EV chargers, and flexible loads. Together, the platform can enroll customers, control devices, and optimize their use as a virtual power plant, supported by machine learning for forecasting and targeting. It serves electric and gas utilities in many regions.

Uplight sells to utilities on enterprise contracts, so consumers meet it through their utility's programs. Results depend on customer participation and regulatory structures. Competitors include Opower, which is now part of Oracle, Enel X, Voltus, Generac Grid Services, and Itron. Uplight suits utilities building customer-centric flexibility programs.
