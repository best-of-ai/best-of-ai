Zors is a franchise intelligence platform purpose-built by industry experts to streamline territory mapping, sales, and management. Its advanced mapping and analysis tools empower franchisors to define, evaluate, and offer territories using key economic indicators and demographic insights. Zors transforms complex territory data into clear, actionable visuals through intuitive, pre-built charts, graphs, and tables.

Built on a powerful territory-first architecture, Zors seamlessly integrates advanced analytics and CRM capabilities to connect geographic areas with the people, companies, and vendors that support them—driving smarter, data-informed growth. With built-in e-signature functionality, franchise approval tracking, and milestone tracking, Zors helps franchise systems stay organized, audit-ready, and operate with reduced risk.
