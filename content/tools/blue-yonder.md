Blue Yonder helps companies plan and run their supply chains. Demand forecasting predicts what customers will buy, inventory planning decides how much stock to hold and where, and transportation and warehouse systems manage how goods move. Retailers, manufacturers, and logistics providers use it to balance supply and demand, reduce stockouts and excess inventory, and respond to disruptions. The company grew from JDA Software and acquired an AI startup that gave it its name.

Machine learning is at the center of its forecasting and optimization. The Luminate platform supplies cloud applications, data, and AI services, and newer agentic features help planners analyze problems, recommend actions, and automate routine decisions. Panasonic acquired Blue Yonder and sees it as a way to connect manufacturing and logistics.

Blue Yonder is sold to large enterprises through subscriptions and long implementation projects, which makes it costly and complex. The company has also been affected by a cyberattack, a reminder of the risks of cloud supply chain systems. Competitors include SAP Integrated Business Planning, Oracle SCM, Kinaxis, o9 Solutions, and Manhattan Associates for warehouse management. Blue Yonder suits large retailers and manufacturers with complex supply chains.
