Seismic organizes marketing and sales content so reps can find and use it. Instead of searching shared drives, a seller gets recommended decks, case studies, and playbooks based on the deal stage and customer, and can assemble personalized presentations quickly.

Analytics show which content gets used and which correlates with wins, and engagement data tells sellers how buyers interact with shared materials. Coaching and training modules, and AI that writes tailored messages and summaries, add to the suite.

Seismic is sold on enterprise subscriptions. Its value depends on keeping content curated. Alternatives include Highspot, Showpad, Mindtickle, Salesloft, and HubSpot. Seismic suits large sales organizations.
