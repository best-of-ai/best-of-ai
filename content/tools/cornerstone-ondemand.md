Cornerstone OnDemand is one of the established vendors of learning management systems. Large employers use it to host training, assign compliance courses, track completion, and manage development plans. The platform covers learning, performance reviews, succession planning, and a content library from outside providers.

Its newer direction centers on skills. AI infers the skills employees have from their roles and activity, compares them to what jobs require, and recommends courses and career paths. Generative AI helps create content, summarize learning, and give managers guidance. The aim is to move from course catalogs to skill growth.

Cornerstone is sold on enterprise contracts, and implementations take time and configuration. Employees often find enterprise learning systems less engaging than consumer apps, so adoption depends on content and design. Alternatives include Workday Learning, SAP SuccessFactors, Docebo, 360Learning, and Degreed. Cornerstone suits large organizations that need compliance and skills management in one system.
