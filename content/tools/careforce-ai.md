Careforce provides AI workers for healthcare administration. Agents place outbound calls and texts to patients, such as to schedule overdue screenings, confirm appointments, and collect information before a visit. They handle follow-up and rescheduling, which often falls through in busy offices.

The company says its agents support many languages and can work across scheduling systems and payer portals, which lets clinics reach patients who might otherwise be missed. Scripts are configurable for different campaigns, such as preventive care outreach and post-operative check-ins.

Most performance claims, including booking rates, come from the vendor and should be tested in a pilot. Healthcare calls involve privacy law and patient consent, and clinics should confirm compliance details. Alternatives include Hippocratic AI, Assort Health, Notable, Luma Health, and Qventus. Careforce AI suits clinics and health systems trying to fill schedules and close care gaps.
