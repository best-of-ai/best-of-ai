Lyra provides employer-sponsored mental health care. Employees take an assessment and are matched with a provider from Lyra's network of clinicians, with options for therapy, coaching, medication management, and digital programs. Employers pay for the benefit, so employees may have little or no out-of-pocket cost for a set number of sessions.

The company says it focuses on quality and outcomes by vetting providers and measuring progress. AI supports the matching process, helps clinicians with documentation, and powers a conversational tool offering support between sessions, while keeping clinicians involved in care.

Lyra is sold to employers and health plans, and individuals access it through their workplace. Anyone in crisis should contact emergency services or a crisis line. Alternatives include Spring Health, Headspace Health, Talkspace, Modern Health, and Teladoc. Lyra Health suits employees whose employers offer it.
