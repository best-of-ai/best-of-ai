Copper integrates with the tools its users already work in. Because it lives inside Gmail and connects with Google Calendar, Drive, and Docs, contacts and conversations are logged automatically, so reps do not have to copy information into a separate system.

The CRM covers the usual sales functions: contacts, companies, deals, pipelines, tasks, and reporting. Project management features track work after a deal closes, which suits agencies and service firms. AI features help summarize communication and suggest follow-ups, and workflow automation moves deals through stages.

Copper is paid and priced per user. It is tied to Google Workspace, so it is less appealing for teams on Microsoft. Alternatives include HubSpot, Pipedrive, Close, Zoho CRM, and Salesforce. Copper suits small and mid-sized teams that work in Google tools.
