Conduyt takes an approach to CRM pricing that departs from the per-seat model. A team pays a single price no matter how many people use the product, which suits small sales teams and agencies that would otherwise pay for every added user.

The basics are familiar: a contact database, deal pipelines, and activity tracking, with AI features that draft follow-up messages, summarize interactions, and reduce routine data entry. Positioned as AI-native, it aims to have the software do the admin work that salespeople dislike.

Because independent coverage of Conduyt is limited, evaluate it with a trial and check integrations with the email and calendar tools you use, plus export options. Alternatives include HubSpot, Pipedrive, Close, Attio, and Folk. Conduyt suits small teams looking for a simple CRM with predictable cost.
