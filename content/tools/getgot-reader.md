Getgot Reader is an AI-powered web article reader browser extension featuring its unique cleanRead engine that removes ads and clutter for distraction-free reading, offers instant bilingual/translated switching, generates mind-maps in one click, and provides eye-care themes. 

It can send original or translated content to Kindle, Notion and Readwise, and export Markdown or high-resolution screenshots—making research, learning and content creation effortless.

- **CleanRead**: AI smart content cleaning removes ads and clutter for focused reading.
- **Smart Translation**: real-time bilingual or translated view with instant switch.
- **Mind-Map Generator**: turn any article into a mind-map for quick comprehension.
- **Export & Share**: export Markdown, PNG screenshots or copy full text.
- **Send To**: Kindle, Notion, Readwise with one click.
- **Multi-theme & Shortcuts**: light/dark/eye-care themes
