Scan a QR code with a phone or an iPad and it joins the session in the browser, with no app to install. Shot.video records the screen, the laptop camera, the microphone and every phone angle in sync. Then AI makes a first edit with camera cuts, screen zooms and B-roll suggestions, and you can change any of them.

Screen styles include a clean view, a browser frame and a 3D showcase. Recordings run up to 10 minutes, exports come in 16:9 or 9:16 without a watermark, and OBS Virtual Camera is supported.

Pricing is credit based and billed monthly. The free allowance of a couple of AI edits and some B-roll credits is given once, and the Creator, Pro and Studio plans add more. It is aimed at creators, product demos, courses, tutorials and team updates.
