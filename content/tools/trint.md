Trint speeds up working with recordings. Users upload interviews, press conferences, or footage and get transcripts that link to the audio, so editing the text is easy to verify. Teams can search across an archive, highlight quotes, and build stories from selections.

The platform supports many languages, translation, and subtitles, and offers collaboration for newsrooms with permissions and shared workspaces. AI features create summaries and help extract key moments.

Trint is paid, with plans by features and seats. Accuracy depends on audio quality. Alternatives include Otter.ai, Descript, Transkriptor, Verbit, and Rev. Trint suits media teams that work with large volumes of recorded content.
