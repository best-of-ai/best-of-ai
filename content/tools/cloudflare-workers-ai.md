Workers AI brings model inference close to users. A developer writes a Cloudflare Worker, calls a model through a binding or REST API, and Cloudflare runs it on GPUs in its data centers around the world. There are no servers to manage, and the platform scales automatically.

The catalog includes open models for chat and text generation, embeddings, image generation, speech recognition, translation, and classification. Developers combine it with other Cloudflare products, such as Vectorize for vector search, D1 for databases, R2 for storage, and AI Gateway for monitoring, to build full applications at the edge.

Workers AI has a free allowance and usage-based pricing, billed in units tied to compute. The model list is mostly open source, so the most powerful proprietary models are not included. Alternatives include Together AI, Fireworks AI, Replicate (now part of Cloudflare), AWS Bedrock, and Hugging Face Inference. Workers AI suits developers who build on Cloudflare.
