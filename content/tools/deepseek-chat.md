DeepSeek Chat offers consumer access to DeepSeek's language models. Users ask questions, draft text, write code, and work through math and logic, and a mode shows the model's step-by-step reasoning before it gives an answer. It supports uploading files and searching the web.

DeepSeek drew wide attention when its models matched strong Western systems at much lower reported training cost, and it released the model weights openly, which let others run and study them. The chat app was one of the most downloaded in many countries soon after it launched.

The service is free. Data sent to the hosted app is processed by a company in China, which leads some governments and organizations to restrict its use and some users to prefer running the open models locally or through a US or European host. Responses on politically sensitive topics may be filtered. Alternatives include ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Qwen, and Kimi. DeepSeek Chat suits users who want a capable free assistant and accept its data practices.
