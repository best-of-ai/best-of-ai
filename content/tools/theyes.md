The Yes used a swipe-based onboarding to learn a shopper's taste in fashion and then curated a feed of products from many brands. Its algorithm tried to act like a personal stylist, adjusting to what a user liked and avoiding what they skipped.

Founded by a former Stitch Fix executive, the company was acquired by Pinterest, which folded the team and its taste-based personalization ideas into Pinterest's shopping features. The standalone app and site were closed.

Shoppers looking for similar experiences can use Pinterest shopping, Stitch Fix, Google Shopping with AI, and retailer apps with personalization. This entry is kept for reference, and The Yes is no longer available.
