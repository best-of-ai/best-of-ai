Tailor Brands serves new businesses. A user enters a company name and preferences, and the AI proposes logos and a brand kit with colors and fonts, then applies it to business cards, social graphics, and a website. The goal is a complete starter identity in minutes.

The platform has added services around the brand: website building, domain, and incorporation help. Subscriptions bundle logos and tools.

Tailor Brands has a free tier to design and paid plans to download. AI logos can resemble others, and trademark checks are the user's job. Alternatives include Looka, Canva, LogoAI, Wix Logo Maker, and hiring a designer. Tailor Brands suits small business owners starting a brand.
