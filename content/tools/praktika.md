Praktika turns language practice into a conversation with a character. The app has several AI tutors with different personalities, and you talk with them out loud. They correct pronunciation, grammar, and word choice as you speak, and you can steer the conversation toward topics you care about.

A personalized study plan adjusts to your level and goals, so sessions build on one another instead of repeating the same small talk. The app runs on iOS and Android, and the focus is on speaking confidence at your own pace.

The app is subscription based, and prices appear in the app store. Check current plans, since offers change. Praktika overlaps with Speak and Talkpal, and which one you like comes down to the tutor style and the languages you need.
