Buzzsprout focuses on being the easiest way to get a podcast online. You upload an audio file, add show notes, and publish, and it distributes the episode to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and other directories through an RSS feed it manages. It provides an embeddable player for your website, detailed download statistics, and tools for promoting episodes on social media.

Additional features help creators improve and grow. Magic Mastering automatically cleans up and balances audio loudness, an AI transcription service creates transcripts, and tools can generate chapters, episode titles, and descriptions. Dynamic content features and a way to import an existing podcast make switching easy. Monetization options include support from listeners and ads in some plans.

Buzzsprout has a free plan with a small amount of upload time per month and episodes that expire after a period, and paid plans priced by hours of audio per month. It is simple and doesn't offer the extensive features of larger platforms. Competitors include Libsyn, Podbean, Transistor, Captivate, Spotify for Creators, and Riverside for recording. Buzzsprout suits new and independent podcasters who value ease of use.
