Convai makes game characters that respond to what players say. A developer defines a character's personality, backstory, and knowledge, and Convai handles listening to speech, generating replies with a language model, speaking them with a synthetic voice, and driving lip sync and gestures. Plugins integrate with Unity, Unreal Engine, and other platforms.

Characters can also take actions in the game world, such as navigating or interacting with objects, based on conversation. Use cases include immersive games, training simulations, virtual tours, and educational experiences where open-ended dialogue adds value over scripted lines.

Convai has a free tier for experimenting and paid plans by usage. Designers must guard against characters saying inappropriate or story-breaking things, and cloud-based AI adds latency and cost. Alternatives include Inworld AI, Charisma.ai, NVIDIA ACE, and Replica Studios (now closed). Convai suits game developers and creators building interactive characters.
