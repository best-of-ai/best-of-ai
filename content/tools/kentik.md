Kentik gives network teams insight into how traffic moves. It collects flow records, routing information, and metrics from routers, switches, and cloud networks, stores them at large scale, and lets engineers query who is talking to whom and how much. That helps with capacity planning, troubleshooting, and cost control.

Security and reliability uses include detecting DDoS attacks and unusual traffic patterns, analyzing BGP routing changes, and finding the reason for a slowdown across multiple networks. An AI assistant lets users ask questions in plain language and speeds up root cause analysis. Cloud and data center networks are covered.

Kentik is sold to enterprises and service providers on subscription. Value depends on collecting data from the right devices. Alternatives include ThousandEyes (Cisco), Datadog Network Monitoring, SolarWinds, Arbor (NETSCOUT), and Akamai tools. Kentik suits network operations teams.
