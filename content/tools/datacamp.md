DataCamp is built around learning by doing. Each lesson combines a few minutes of video with exercises you complete in an in-browser editor, so you write real code with immediate feedback and no setup. Courses cover Python, R, SQL, Excel, Power BI, statistics, machine learning, and more recently AI and large language models, taught by instructors from industry and academia.

Structured paths help learners progress. Career tracks, such as data analyst or data scientist, string together courses and projects, skill assessments show strengths and gaps, and certifications validate skills for employers. An AI assistant gives hints and explains errors, and a workspace allows practice in notebooks. Teams can buy it for corporate training with reporting.

DataCamp offers a limited free tier and a paid subscription for the full library. Its short, guided exercises are great for building basics but can feel scripted, and they may not prepare you for messy real-world projects, so combining them with independent work helps. Alternatives include Coursera and edX for university-style courses, Codecademy, Dataquest, Kaggle Learn, which is free, and Udemy. DataCamp suits beginners and professionals picking up data skills.
