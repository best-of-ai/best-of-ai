Culture Amp helps companies listen to employees. HR teams run engagement, onboarding, and exit surveys, analyze the results by team and demographic group, and compare against benchmarks from other organizations. Dashboards point out areas where scores are low and what drives them.

The platform also covers performance and development, with review cycles, goal tracking, and feedback. AI helps by summarizing open-ended comments into themes, drafting review text, and recommending actions to managers, which saves analysts days of reading. An assistant answers questions about results.

Culture Amp is sold on subscription to mid-sized and large companies. Surveys only help when leaders act on them, and anonymity needs careful handling in small teams. Alternatives include Lattice, Qualtrics EmployeeXM, Glint (Microsoft Viva), 15Five, and Peakon (Workday). Culture Amp suits HR teams that want to measure and improve engagement.
