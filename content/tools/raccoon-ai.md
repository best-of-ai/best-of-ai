Raccoon AI presents itself as an agent that does the work, not a chatbot that explains how. A user describes a task, such as researching vendors and emailing a summary, and the agent plans the steps, uses a browser and connected apps, and reports back.

Such agents promise to save time on repetitive chores, but reliability varies with task complexity, and actions that send messages or spend money need human review. Permissions to accounts should be limited to what the task requires.

Independent information is limited, so evaluate it on low-stakes tasks first. It has a free tier and paid plans. Alternatives include Manus, Genspark, ChatGPT agent mode, Lindy, and Claude with computer use. Raccoon AI suits people exploring general-purpose agents.
