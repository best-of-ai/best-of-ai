GLM-Image is a state-of-the-art open-source image generation model developed by Z.AI (also known as Zhipu AI), released on January 14, 2026. Featuring a groundbreaking hybrid architecture combining a 9B-parameter autoregressive transformer with a 7B-parameter diffusion decoder, GLM-Image achieves industry-leading performance in text rendering (0.9116 CVTG-2K accuracy) and knowledge-intensive content generation.
