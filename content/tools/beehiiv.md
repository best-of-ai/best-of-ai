Beehiiv is built for people who run newsletters as a business. Writers compose and send posts, manage subscriber lists, and publish a website for the archive. Growth tools include referral programs, recommendations between newsletters, and landing pages, which help build an audience without outside ads.

Monetization is a central feature. Creators can sell paid subscriptions, run ads through the Beehiiv ad network, add sponsors, and offer digital products. Analytics show opens, clicks, and which posts convert readers. An AI assistant helps with drafts, subject lines, and ideas, and automation handles welcome sequences.

Beehiiv has a free plan for small lists and paid tiers as the audience grows. Switching platforms later means exporting subscribers, which is possible but takes effort. Alternatives include Substack, Ghost, Kit (formerly ConvertKit), Mailchimp, and Medium. Beehiiv suits creators building a newsletter business.
