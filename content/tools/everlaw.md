Everlaw helps legal teams work through evidence. Documents are uploaded, processed, and made searchable, and reviewers tag them, share notes, and build case narratives. Visual tools show relationships and timelines, and cloud delivery means teams can collaborate without installing software.

AI speeds up the work. Predictive coding ranks documents by likely relevance, clustering groups similar content, and generative features summarize documents, answer questions across a set, and draft deposition outlines. Everlaw has tools used by public interest lawyers and human rights investigators, and the company supports access programs.

Everlaw is sold per project or by subscription, with pricing by agreement. Lawyers must verify AI outputs, and courts expect defensible processes. Alternatives include Relativity, DISCO, Reveal, Casepoint, and Nextpoint. Everlaw suits litigation teams handling large document collections.
