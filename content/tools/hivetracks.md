Users keep a history for each hive, with guided inspection forms matched to their experience level. The app suggests when to inspect next and offers a bee weather forecast for choosing inspection days. It works on phones with offline use and syncs later.

The core app is free with paid upgrades. Reviews note that mite counts and reports are basic, so serious operations may want extra tools.

Spreadsheets and BeeKeeper-style apps are alternatives. HiveTracks suits hobby and sideline beekeepers.
