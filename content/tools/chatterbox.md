ChatterBox is an all-in-one API that makes it easy to integrate your software with leading video conferencing platforms like Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Whether you're building a meeting bot, automating call insights, or enabling real-time collaboration features, ChatterBox gives you seamless access to real-time transcripts, speaker identification, audio streams, call metadata, and more.

Built for developers, ChatterBox is designed to be simple to integrate, self-serve, and fully managed—so you can launch powerful meeting experiences without the hassle of maintaining infrastructure or dealing with platform-specific quirks.

Key Features:
- Real-Time Transcription with Speaker Tags: Live transcripts labeled by speaker for clarity and analysis.
- Post-Call Audio + Transcripts: Get full recordings and searchable transcripts immediately after each meeting.
- Call Metadata: Access rich context like participant info, timestamps, meeting links, and platform identifiers.
- Multi-Platform Support: Works with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams through a unified API.
- Webhook Events: Get notified of live meeting events like participants joining, leaving, or the bot being kicked.

Benefits:
- Fast Time to Value: Start building in minutes with simple, well-documented APIs.
- Lower Cost: Pay-as-you-go pricing with no upfront commitments or hidden fees.
- Full Control: Manage your integration through a developer-friendly admin portal.
- Zero Ops: Fully managed infrastructure, scaling, and maintenance handled for you.
- Flexible Use Cases: Enable everything from meeting bots and note-taking assistants to compliance monitoring and productivity tools.

How It Works:
- Create a Bot: Use the ChatterBox API or dashboard to configure your meeting bot.
- Join a Meeting: Programmatically send your bot into live Zoom, Meet, or Teams calls.
- Capture Data: As the meeting runs, ChatterBox delivers real-time transcripts, audio, and metadata via webhooks or polling endpoints.
- Use the Insights: Feed the data into your app, CRM, analytics platform, or internal tools to drive automation and insights.

Whether you're enhancing internal operations or building customer-facing features, ChatterBox gives you the power and flexibility to bring rich meeting data into your product—without complexity.
