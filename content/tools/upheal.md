Upheal helps therapists reduce paperwork. With client consent, the tool records or transcribes a session and drafts a note in the clinician's preferred format, which they review and sign. It can summarize progress over time and suggest treatment plan updates.

The product emphasizes privacy and compliance with health data rules, and offers a free tier for solo practitioners. Session insights, such as talk time and themes, give feedback to the clinician.

Clients must be informed about recording, and therapists remain responsible for note accuracy. Alternatives include Mentalyc, SimplePractice AI, Blueprint, Freed, and Heidi Health. Upheal suits therapists and counselors.
