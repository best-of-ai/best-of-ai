Users pick a location and date to see stars, planets, and satellites, and can control telescopes through plug-ins. It includes sky cultures and deep sky catalogs.

A web version runs in the browser, and desktop apps exist for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Cartes du Ciel is another open-source option. Stellarium suits astronomers and amateur observers.
