Creatopy is built for ad production. Designers or marketers create a layout in an editor, and the tool adapts it to dozens of banner sizes and social formats, saving the manual resizing that normally eats hours. Animation tools turn static ads into HTML5 banners, and brand kits keep colors, fonts, and logos consistent.

Teams can scale campaigns with a feature that fills templates from a spreadsheet or product feed, producing many variations of an ad automatically. Approval workflows let clients comment and sign off, and integrations push ads directly to ad networks, so there is less downloading and uploading. AI helps with copy, images, and layout suggestions.

Creatopy has a free trial and paid plans by team size and features, with enterprise options. It is more specialized than Canva, and general design work may be easier elsewhere. Alternatives include Bannerflow, Canva, Celtra, AdCreative.ai, and Figma with plugins. Creatopy suits agencies and marketing teams producing many ad variations.
