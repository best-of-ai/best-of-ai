Factorial gathers HR tasks in one system. Companies store employee records, track attendance and time off, manage documents and contracts, run performance reviews, and handle recruiting and onboarding. Payroll is offered in several countries, and integrations connect with accounting and communication tools.

AI helps with administrative work. It can draft job descriptions and offer letters, answer employee questions about policies, and speed up payroll preparation. Reports give managers a view of headcount and absence.

Factorial is paid, with pricing per employee and modules. Originally European, it is strongest in Spain and neighboring markets, so check local payroll support. Alternatives include BambooHR, Personio, HiBob, CharlieHR, and Rippling. Factorial suits small and mid-sized employers wanting an all-in-one HR tool.
