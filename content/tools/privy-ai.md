Privy gives small stores a way to capture and convert visitors. Pop-ups and banners collect emails and texts with offers, automated emails recover abandoned carts, and campaigns promote products to subscribers. Everything runs from one dashboard connected to the store.

AI assists with writing and targeting, such as drafting emails and choosing segments. Templates and recipes simplify setup for merchants without a marketing team, and analytics show revenue from each campaign.

Privy has a free plan and paid tiers by contacts. Text messaging must follow consent rules. Alternatives include Klaviyo, Omnisend, Mailchimp, Justuno, and Postscript. Privy suits small online stores starting with email and SMS.
