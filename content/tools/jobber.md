Jobber is built for small service companies such as lawn care, cleaning, plumbing, and locksmith businesses. Owners send quotes, schedule jobs on a calendar, dispatch crews, and invoice customers, with a mobile app for the crew to see their route, take notes, and collect payment on site.

Client-facing features help win and keep work. Customers can request services online, approve quotes, pay invoices, and receive reminders and arrival notices, and a client hub keeps their history together. Automations send follow-ups and review requests. An AI assistant drafts messages and helps convert rough notes into a clear quote.

Jobber has paid plans by number of users and features, plus payment processing fees. It targets small and mid-sized home service businesses, and very large operations may need heavier tools. Alternatives include Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Workiz, FieldEdge, and Service Fusion. Jobber suits small trade businesses.
