Meta AI is the assistant that Meta put where its users already are. In WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, and Facebook, you can mention it in a chat or open it from the search bar to ask a question, get recommendations, or generate an image. There is also a standalone app and website with fuller features, including natural voice conversations, image creation and editing, and a feed where people share what they have made.

The assistant runs on Meta's Llama family of models, and Meta has also released several Llama versions with open weights for developers. In the app, it can search the web for current information, remember preferences you share, and use information from your connected Meta accounts to personalize answers, depending on settings and region. Smart glasses from Meta, such as the Ray-Ban line, use it as a voice assistant. Vibes is a feed for AI-generated short videos tied to the same product.

Meta AI is free to use. Features differ by country and by app, and some are still rolling out. Because it ties into social accounts, privacy settings deserve attention, and Meta has faced criticism over how conversations and shared content are used. The assistant is convenient for quick questions inside a chat but is not a replacement for specialist tools when you need deep reasoning or long documents. ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude are stronger for sustained work.
