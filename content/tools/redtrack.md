RedTrack is a tracker for people who buy traffic and promote affiliate offers. It links traffic sources, affiliate networks, and offers into one view so you can see which campaigns make money. Conversion data goes back to ad platforms so their optimization works from accurate results.

Automation rules can pause or scale campaigns based on performance. Through MCP access, AI agents can read data and take actions using the same controls you set, which allows an assistant to monitor campaigns for you within limits.

The page offers a way to get started for free, but does not spell out trial length or prices, so check the pricing page. Voluum is the other well-known tracker in this space. RedTrack suits affiliates and media buyers who run enough spend that tracking mistakes get expensive.
