Figma Make lets designers and product teams bring ideas to working form. A user describes an app, or starts from a Figma design, and the AI builds an interactive version using code. They can then edit it by chatting, adjusting on the canvas, or changing the code directly.

Because it connects to Figma, designs, components, and styles can serve as the starting point, which keeps results consistent with a design system. Teams use it to test ideas with realistic prototypes before involving engineering, and to share working demos with stakeholders. Backend features such as data storage let prototypes behave more like real apps.

Figma Make is included in Figma plans with usage limits tied to AI credits. Prototypes are good for exploration, but production applications need engineering review. Alternatives include v0, Lovable, Bolt, Claude Design, and Framer. Figma Make suits designers and product teams who want to turn designs into working prototypes.
