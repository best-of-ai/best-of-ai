Researchers drag icons into a canvas and edit them to build pathways, experimental workflows, and graphical abstracts. Templates speed up common layouts, and teams share a library. Paid plans allow publication use and export options.

Free accounts have limits on exports and icons, so check the license before publishing.

Illustrator and Inkscape give more freedom but take more time. BioRender suits biologists and biochemists.
