Jan lets you use language models without sending data anywhere. After installing the app, you browse a model hub, download models such as Llama, Mistral, or Qwen in a size your hardware can handle, and chat with them offline. Everything runs on your machine, which is attractive to people concerned about privacy or working without internet access, and it avoids per-use fees.

It is more than a chat window. Jan can connect to remote services like OpenAI and Anthropic with your own API keys, so you can switch between local and cloud models in one interface. A local server exposes an OpenAI-compatible API, so other tools can use your models, and support for the Model Context Protocol lets assistants use tools. The project also develops its own small models tuned for tasks.

Jan is free and open source under the Apache 2.0 license. The quality of local models depends on your hardware, with smaller models less capable than frontier cloud models, and larger ones need a strong GPU or lots of memory. Alternatives include LM Studio, Ollama, GPT4All, Open WebUI, and llama.cpp directly. Jan suits people who want a friendly, privacy-focused way to run local AI.
