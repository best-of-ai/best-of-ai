Mermaid is a popular open-source way to create diagrams by writing text. You describe a flowchart or sequence diagram in a few lines of code, and it renders as a graphic, which makes diagrams easy to version in Git, embed in documentation, and update alongside code. GitHub, GitLab, and many documentation tools render Mermaid natively, and developers like that diagrams stay in plain text.

Mermaid Chart is the company and product founded by the creator of Mermaid. It provides a web editor with live preview, a visual drag-and-drop editor for people who prefer not to write code, templates, and collaboration features such as sharing and comments. AI can generate diagrams from a description, fix syntax errors, and suggest changes, which lowers the barrier for beginners. Plugins integrate with VS Code, Confluence, Jira, and other tools.

Mermaid itself is free and open source, and Mermaid Chart has a free plan with limits and paid plans for teams and advanced features. Text-based diagrams are great for technical content but less suited to free-form visual design. Alternatives include draw.io, Lucidchart, Excalidraw, PlantUML for text-based UML, and Whimsical. Mermaid Chart suits developers and technical writers who want diagrams as code.
