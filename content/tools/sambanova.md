SambaNova designs custom chips, called reconfigurable dataflow units, along with the software and systems around them. The goal is high-speed inference and training for large language models at lower power than general-purpose GPU setups.

Its cloud service offers fast access to open models through an API, and enterprises can deploy systems on premises for data control. Government and large organizations are among its customers.

SambaNova is sold on usage-based cloud pricing and system contracts. The company has faced a competitive field of chip makers and has been the subject of reports about funding and acquisition talks, so check its status. Alternatives include Groq, Cerebras, NVIDIA, Together AI, and Fireworks AI. SambaNova suits organizations needing fast inference.
