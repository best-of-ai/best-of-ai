Paperspace rents GPU computing by the hour. Users launch virtual machines with GPUs for training models, running experiments, or heavy graphics, and connect remotely. Gradient adds managed Jupyter notebooks and workflows that make it easy to start without setting up servers.

DigitalOcean acquired Paperspace and has been integrating it with its cloud offerings, aiming to give developers and small teams access to GPUs with simple pricing. Students and independent researchers have long used it for coursework and experiments.

Pricing is pay as you go, with some free-tier notebook options that depend on availability. GPU supply varies. Alternatives include Lambda, RunPod, Vast.ai, Google Colab, and the major clouds. Paperspace suits individuals and small teams needing flexible GPUs.
