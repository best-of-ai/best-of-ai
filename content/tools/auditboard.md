Audit teams plan engagements, test controls, and track findings through remediation. SOX teams map controls to risks and gather evidence once for several frameworks. Newer AI features draft summaries and help search policies and past work.

Large and mid-size companies use it as a system of record for assurance work. Implementation takes planning, since control libraries must be set up.

Workiva and Diligent are alternatives. AuditBoard suits internal auditors and compliance teams.
