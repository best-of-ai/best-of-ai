Personetics supplies the intelligence behind money management features in banking apps. Banks connect transaction data, and the platform generates timely, personalized messages and suggestions, like warning a customer that a bill will overdraw their account or noticing a good time to save.

The aim is a more engaging and useful banking relationship. Personetics works with large banks around the world and presents itself as a way to turn data into proactive guidance. AI generates the insights and chooses when and how to show them.

Personetics is sold to financial institutions on enterprise contracts, and consumers see it only as part of their bank's app. Alternatives include Kasisto, MX, Backbase, Mambu's tools, and in-house analytics. Personetics suits banks adding proactive money guidance.
