Madgicx is built for performance marketers and e-commerce brands running paid social. It connects to ad accounts and analyzes campaigns, then recommends or applies changes such as shifting budget toward better-performing ad sets, pausing losers, or testing new audiences. Automation rules run these changes continuously.

Creative tools help with the content side. The platform analyzes which creatives perform best and helps generate variations, and reporting dashboards combine data from ads and online stores into one view. Audience tools find lookalike segments and retargeting groups.

Madgicx is paid, with plans scaled by ad spend, and a trial. Automation can make decisions that a marketer wants to check, and results depend on product, offer, and creative quality. Alternatives include Revealbot (Bïrch), Smartly.io, AdCreative.ai, Triple Whale, and Meta's own Advantage tools. Madgicx suits e-commerce advertisers managing Meta campaigns.
