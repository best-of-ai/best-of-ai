Vivino started as a wine label scanner. A user photographs a bottle, and the app recognizes it, showing the average rating, tasting notes, price ranges, and what other drinkers say. This helps shoppers choose in stores and restaurants.

The app has become a marketplace as well, where users can buy wines from partner merchants. A taste profile learns from your ratings and recommends bottles, and features include food pairing suggestions and a cellar to track wines.

Vivino is free to use, with revenue from sales and advertising. Ratings reflect crowd taste, which may not match expert opinion or your own. Alternatives include Wine-Searcher, Delectable, CellarTracker, Untappd for beer, and Hello Vino. Vivino suits wine drinkers who want quick information.
