Spark runs in the background and handles longer jobs, such as pulling together information from your inbox and documents or following up on a task over several days. Google says it works with Gmail and Docs first, and that outside tools will connect through MCP later.

At launch it was limited to US subscribers on the Google AI Ultra plan. Android is expected to show an agent's progress at the top of the screen later in the year.
