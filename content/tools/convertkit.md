ConvertKit was designed for independent creators rather than traditional businesses. It makes it easy to build a list with forms and landing pages, tag subscribers by interest, and send newsletters and automated sequences. The company later renamed the product Kit, and the old name remains widely used.

Creator commerce is a major part. Users can sell digital products, paid newsletters, and subscriptions directly, with payments handled in the platform. A recommendation network lets creators promote each other's newsletters, and automations route subscribers based on what they click. AI tools help draft emails and subject lines.

Kit has a free plan for small lists and paid plans as the audience grows. It is simpler than enterprise email platforms, with fewer design tools. Alternatives include Beehiiv, Substack, Mailchimp, MailerLite, and Flodesk. ConvertKit suits creators who want straightforward email marketing and commerce.
