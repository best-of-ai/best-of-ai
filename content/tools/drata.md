Drata addresses the burden of proving security compliance. Companies selling to enterprises are often asked for a SOC 2 report or other certifications, and preparing for an audit traditionally takes months of gathering screenshots and documents. Drata connects to systems such as AWS, Google Workspace, GitHub, Okta, and HR tools, automatically checks whether controls are in place, and collects evidence continuously, so the company remains audit-ready.

The platform covers more than evidence. It manages policies and employee training, tracks vendor risk, supports risk assessments, and offers a trust center that lets customers see a company's security posture. Auditors can work directly in the platform. AI features help fill out security questionnaires, summarize risks, and map controls across multiple frameworks, saving time for small security teams.

Drata is paid, priced by frameworks and company size, with a demo-based sales process. Automation does not replace the underlying work of running secure practices, and the audit still requires an independent auditor. Vanta is the closest competitor, with Secureframe, Sprinto, and Scrut also serving this market. Drata suits startups and mid-sized companies pursuing their first certifications.
