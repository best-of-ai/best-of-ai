QuillBot's paraphraser is the tool's defining feature, and it goes well beyond simple synonym substitution. You paste in a paragraph, select one of seven rewrite modes — Standard, Fluency, Formal, Academic, Simple, Creative, Shorten — and get a rewritten version that changes sentence structure, vocabulary, and tone to match the selected mode while preserving the original meaning. The Fluency mode produces naturally flowing text; Academic mode strengthens the formal register of essays and research writing; Shorten compresses long explanations without losing the point.

The summariser condenses long articles, research papers, or documents into key points, and supports both bullet-point and paragraph output formats. The grammar checker goes sentence by sentence, explaining why each correction was made rather than just marking errors in red — which makes it useful for learning as well as fixing. The citation generator accepts a URL or title, finds the source, and formats it in APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, or other academic citation styles correctly.

QuillBot's free tier provides 125 words per paraphrase attempt, which is limiting for serious use. Premium at $4.17/month (billed annually) removes word limits, unlocks all paraphrase modes, adds plagiarism checking, and enables advanced grammar suggestions. It integrates directly with Google Docs and Microsoft Word as a browser extension. Students are the largest user group by far — QuillBot is one of the most widely used academic writing tools globally — but ESL writers, content marketers, and non-native English speakers also use it heavily for polishing professional communication.
