Meshy makes 3D asset creation fast. You type a description or upload an image, and it generates a 3D mesh with textures in a minute or two. The results can be rotated, refined, and downloaded in common formats. Indie game developers, 3D printing hobbyists, product designers, and virtual world builders use it to produce props, characters, and environment pieces without modeling each from scratch.

The platform includes tools for the steps after generation. It can retexture a model from a prompt, remesh to adjust polygon counts and topology, and add skeletons and animations to characters. Plugins and an API connect it with Blender, Unity, Unreal Engine, Maya, and other tools, so assets can flow into existing pipelines. A library of community models provides starting points.

Meshy has a free tier with limited credits and paid plans with more generations and commercial rights. Generated meshes are best for background assets, prototypes, and inspiration, since topology and fine detail often need cleanup before production use. Competitors include Tripo, Rodin, Luma's Genie, Kaedim, and Hunyuan3D, and traditional modeling in Blender or Maya gives full control. Meshy is among the most widely used text-to-3D tools, valued for speed and its range of follow-up tools.
