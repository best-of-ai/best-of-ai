Floot belongs to the family of prompt-to-app builders. A user describes what they want, such as a booking tool or a simple marketplace, and the platform generates a working application with interface, logic, and data storage, hosts it, and lets the user keep refining by chat.

The target is non-technical founders who want to test an idea without hiring developers. Quick prototypes help validate demand and communicate a concept to partners or investors. Generated apps need testing and security review before handling real customers or payments.

Independent information is limited, so try a small project and check export options and hosting terms. Alternatives include Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Base44, and v0. Floot suits first-time founders building early versions of web products.
