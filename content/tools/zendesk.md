Zendesk is one of the most widely used help desk systems. Customer messages from email, web chat, phone, WhatsApp, and social media become tickets in a shared workspace, where they can be routed, prioritized, and tracked against service targets. A help center and community forum let customers find answers themselves, and reports show response times and satisfaction. It scales from small support teams to large contact centers.

The current product is built around AI. AI agents take on requests from start to finish, answering from help articles and past conversations, and taking actions in connected systems when allowed. A copilot assists human agents by suggesting replies, summarizing long threads, and flagging urgent or unhappy customers. The platform has added contact center, workforce management, and quality assurance tools, and an employee service product that applies the same ideas to internal IT and HR requests.

Zendesk sells tiered plans per agent, with a free trial, and AI resolutions are typically priced by usage on top. Costs climb with add-ons, and setup can be involved for large organizations. It is more powerful and more expensive than Freshdesk or Help Scout, and Intercom is the main rival for AI-first support. Salesforce Service Cloud suits companies already deep in Salesforce. Zendesk is a safe default for mid-sized and large support operations.
