Zendesk's ticket management system is the foundation of enterprise support operations — all customer contacts from email, chat, voice, Twitter, and WhatsApp land in a single queue, routed to the right team based on skills, priority, and capacity. For support organisations managing thousands of tickets per day, this unified view and intelligent routing prevents contacts from falling through the cracks between channels or teams. The reporting suite shows resolution time, customer satisfaction, and agent performance across the operation.

Zendesk AI, powered by their acquisition of machine learning company and expanded with LLMs, has changed how tickets get handled. It classifies incoming tickets by intent and urgency automatically, suggests the most relevant macros (canned responses) to agents, generates first-draft replies agents can review and send in one click, and resolves common questions autonomously without any agent involvement. The AI learns from historical ticket data specific to your organisation, improving as it processes more of your actual support interactions.

Suite Team starts at $55/agent/month; Suite Professional at $115/agent/month adds AI features, expanded analytics, and SLA management. Enterprise pricing is negotiated. Zendesk integrates with Salesforce, Jira, Slack, and hundreds of other tools through its app marketplace. For enterprise support teams dealing with high volume and complex multi-channel contact management, Zendesk is the standard — the question is usually which tier of features the team needs rather than whether to use it.
