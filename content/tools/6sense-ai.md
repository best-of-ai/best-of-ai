6sense helps B2B teams find buyers who are not yet raising their hands. It collects signals from many sources, such as web visits, content consumption, and third-party research activity, identifies the companies behind the anonymous traffic, and uses machine learning to predict which accounts are in the market and at what stage of the buying journey. Teams see lists of target accounts ranked by readiness to buy.

The platform acts on those insights. Marketing can run advertising aimed at accounts showing interest, sales gets alerts when key accounts spike in activity, and workflows enroll contacts in email sequences. AI agents help with outreach and qualification, and integrations with CRMs and marketing automation tools keep data in sync. Predictions rely on data from many sources, so they are probabilistic.

6sense is sold to mid-sized and large B2B companies on annual contracts and has a reputation for being costly. It takes time and clean CRM data to get results, and the accuracy of intent signals is debated. Competitors include Demandbase, ZoomInfo, Bombora for intent data, and HubSpot's and Salesforce's tools. Privacy laws also affect how companies use account identification. 6sense suits organizations with account-based strategies.
