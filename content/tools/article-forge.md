Article Forge is one of the older AI article generators. You enter a keyword and optionally subtopics, and it produces a long article with headings, sections, and optionally images and videos, in a few minutes. It was designed for people who publish a lot of content, such as affiliate sites, small business blogs, and agencies that manage many clients.

Further tools support publishing. A built-in scheduler can post articles to WordPress automatically, an uniqueness check helps avoid duplicate content, and options control article length, language, and tone. A rewriting function can adjust text, and API access lets developers integrate generation into other systems.

Article Forge is paid, with monthly or annual plans and a trial. As with other tools that produce articles from a keyword, quality varies, and unedited, bulk-produced content can be repetitive or inaccurate and may perform poorly in search or hurt a site's reputation, since search engines target low-value content. A human editor should review facts and style. Alternatives include Jasper, Writesonic, Copy.ai, Frase, and general assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude. Article Forge suits people who want a quick first draft.
