FourKites is a supply chain visibility platform. It collects location and status data from carriers, telematics devices, and ocean and air sources, and shows shippers where each load is. Predictive estimates of arrival time account for traffic, weather, and dwell time at facilities, which are more accurate than static schedules.

Alerts warn teams of delays so they can reroute or notify customers. Yard and dock scheduling tools coordinate appointments, and analytics help identify problem carriers or lanes. AI agents handle tasks like checking on late shipments and communicating with carriers.

FourKites is sold to shippers, retailers, and manufacturers on enterprise contracts. Its value grows with the number of carriers connected to the network. Competitors include project44, Descartes, Transporeon, E2open, and Shippeo. FourKites suits logistics teams managing many shipments.
