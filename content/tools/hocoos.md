Hocoos aims at people who want a website without a design process. The user answers questions about their business and style, and the AI generates pages, text, and images, and sets up features such as contact forms, an online store, or appointment booking. An editor allows changes afterward.

The platform includes hosting, a domain option, and SEO basics, plus marketing helpers such as email tools and social media content. It is positioned for small and local businesses and solo entrepreneurs who need an online presence quickly.

Hocoos has a free plan with limits and paid plans for custom domains and e-commerce. Generated content needs review for accuracy and a personal touch, and design flexibility is less than with advanced builders. Alternatives include Wix, Squarespace, Durable, Hostinger Website Builder, and Framer. Hocoos suits small business owners launching a simple site.
