Crimson Hexagon was a consumer insights company that analyzed social media, news, forums, and review data at large scale. It was known for its ForSight platform and for machine learning that classified posts by sentiment and topic with accuracy that researchers could tune. Market researchers, agencies, and brands used it to track what people said about products and campaigns, and its archive of historical social data was a draw for studying trends over time.

The company merged with Brandwatch, another social listening provider, and the two combined their strengths into Brandwatch Consumer Research. That product joined Brandwatch's interface with Crimson Hexagon's classification technology and historical data. The Crimson Hexagon name was phased out, and while former customers were supported for a time, new customers are directed to Brandwatch.

For anyone searching for Crimson Hexagon today, Brandwatch Consumer Research is the successor. Other social listening and consumer intelligence platforms include Talkwalker, Sprinklr, Meltwater, and Sprout Social's listening tools. Access to social data has changed a lot as networks restricted their APIs, which affects what any of these tools can collect. This entry stays for reference, since older research and articles still cite Crimson Hexagon.
