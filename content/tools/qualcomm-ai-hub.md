Qualcomm AI Hub targets on-device AI for phones, PCs, and other products built with Qualcomm chips. A developer uploads a model, and the service converts and optimizes it for the target hardware, then runs it on real devices in the cloud to measure speed and accuracy.

A library of ready-to-run models, such as vision, speech, and language models, saves time. Profiling shows where a model spends time and memory, guiding further tuning. The goal is to make it easy to run AI locally, with benefits for privacy, cost, and latency.

Access is free for developers, with models optimized for Qualcomm hardware only. Alternatives include Apple Core ML, Google's LiteRT, ONNX Runtime, Intel OpenVINO, and Edge Impulse (Qualcomm). Qualcomm AI Hub suits developers deploying models on Snapdragon devices.
