Lindy AI is the product behind agents called Lindies. A user assembles an agent from triggers, actions, and instructions, linking it to services such as Gmail, Slack, Google Calendar, and CRMs. The agent can then run tasks like replying to routine messages, scheduling meetings, and logging notes.

Typical deployments include an executive assistant that manages an inbox and calendar, a sales assistant that researches leads and updates records, and a support helper that answers common questions. Agents can ask for approval before sensitive actions, which keeps a person in the loop.

Pricing is credit based with a free tier. As with any agent that acts in real accounts, start with limited permissions and review its work. Alternatives include Zapier Agents, Relay.app, Gumloop, Cassidy, and ChatGPT agent mode. Lindy AI suits professionals who want a personal automation layer.
