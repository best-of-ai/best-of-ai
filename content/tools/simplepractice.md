SimplePractice is widely used by solo and small group private practices. Clients book appointments online, complete intake forms in a portal, and join video sessions, while clinicians keep notes, treatment plans, and billing in the same system. Insurance claims can be submitted electronically.

AI features help clinicians write progress notes from session details and speed up documentation, which is a chief burden for therapists. The platform offers reminders, reporting, and a client app for messaging and payments.

SimplePractice is paid, priced per clinician by plan. AI notes must be reviewed, and clients should be informed about how data is used. Alternatives include TheraNest, Jane, TherapyNotes, Mentalyc, and Upheal. SimplePractice suits private practice clinicians.
