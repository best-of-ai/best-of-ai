Relativity is a leading e-discovery platform. Legal teams load documents collected in a case, and the system processes them, makes them searchable, and lets reviewers tag and code them for relevance, privilege, and confidentiality. RelativityOne runs in the cloud.

AI helps lawyers cut review volume. Active learning ranks documents by likely relevance, clustering groups similar items, and generative features summarize documents and find key evidence. Analytics show patterns in communications, and audit trails support defensible processes.

Relativity is sold to law firms, corporations, and service providers through subscriptions or per-gigabyte pricing. Lawyers remain responsible for quality. Alternatives include Everlaw, DISCO, Reveal, Casepoint, and Nuix. Relativity suits large litigation and investigation teams.
