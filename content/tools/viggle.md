Viggle made character animation easy to try. A user uploads a picture of a character, a person, or a meme figure, picks a motion from a template or uploads a reference video, and the tool produces a video of the character performing it. The physics-based approach gives believable movement.

It went viral for dance and meme videos, and creators use it for short animations, parody, and mockups. Additional features include text prompts for scenes and a mix of motion templates.

Viggle has a free tier and paid plans. Animating real people raises consent concerns, and quality varies with the source image. Alternatives include Runway, Pika, Kling, Hedra, and Move AI. Viggle suits creators making quick character animations.
