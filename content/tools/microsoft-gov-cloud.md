Azure Government is a separate cloud region set up for US federal, state, and local agencies and their partners. It runs in data centers staffed by screened US personnel and is isolated from commercial Azure, which allows it to meet compliance regimes for sensitive government data.

Agencies use it for the usual range of cloud work, including hosting applications, analytics, and AI. Services such as Azure OpenAI and Copilot have been made available in versions that meet government requirements, which lets agencies use generative AI with data protections.

Pricing follows Azure's pay-as-you-go model with government-specific offerings. Availability of new services in government regions often lags commercial ones. Alternatives include AWS GovCloud, Google Public Sector, Oracle Government Cloud, and IBM's government offerings. Microsoft Azure Government suits agencies and contractors needing compliant cloud.
