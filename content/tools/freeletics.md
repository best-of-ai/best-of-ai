Freeletics grew out of bodyweight training. The app creates workout plans based on your goals, fitness level, available time, and equipment, with programs that range from short high-intensity sessions to longer strength and running plans. Videos demonstrate exercises, and you log workouts, so the plan can adjust to how you perform and how you feel.

An AI coach is at the center of the experience. It tailors weekly schedules, changes difficulty, and gives feedback, aiming to feel like a personal trainer at a fraction of the cost. The app also provides nutrition recommendations and recipes, mindfulness sessions, and community features. It works with smartwatches and other health apps.

Freeletics has a limited free tier and a subscription for full programs. Automated plans cannot correct form in real time or account for injuries like a human trainer, so beginners should start carefully. Alternatives include Nike Training Club, which is free, Fitbod for strength, Peloton, Apple Fitness+, and Future for human coaching. Freeletics suits people who want structured workouts they can do anywhere with little or no equipment.
