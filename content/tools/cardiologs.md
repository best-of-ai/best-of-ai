Cardiologs applies AI to heart rhythm recordings. Technicians and cardiologists traditionally spend hours reviewing long ECG tracings, such as 24-hour or multi-day Holter recordings. The software analyzes the signal, classifies heartbeats and rhythms, and highlights segments that need attention so reviewers can concentrate on them.

Reports summarize findings, including atrial fibrillation burden, pauses, and other events, in a form the physician can verify and edit. The tool works with data from different monitoring devices and integrates into clinical workflows. Philips acquired the company to add ECG analysis to its cardiac monitoring portfolio.

Cardiologs is sold to health systems, clinics, and monitoring providers, often through Philips. AI interpretation is decision support, and clinicians confirm results. Alternatives include iRhythm, Eko, AliveCor, Biotricity, and AccurKardia. Cardiologs suits cardiology teams that review large volumes of ECG data.
