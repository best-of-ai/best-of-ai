Jasper was one of the first purpose-built AI writing tools to gain serious traction with marketing teams, and its enterprise focus has deepened over time. The Brand Voice feature is the core differentiator: you feed Jasper your brand guidelines, example content, and tone descriptors, and it produces output that sounds like it came from your organisation rather than a generic AI. For teams producing high volumes of content across multiple writers, this consistency is worth paying for.

The template library spans the full marketing content stack: long-form blog posts with SEO optimisation, Google and Facebook ad copy, email subject lines, LinkedIn posts, product descriptions, video scripts, and press releases. The workflow engine lets teams automate production sequences — a topic goes in, and Jasper produces an outline, then drafts, then a meta description, all in sequence without manual intervention at each step. This is where Jasper earns its enterprise price for teams doing serious content at scale.

Jasper is priced as an enterprise product — Creator starts at $39/month and Teams at $99/month for three seats, with Business plans negotiated based on size and usage. It integrates with Surfer SEO and Google Search Console for keyword-informed content, and with Zapier for workflow automation. Compared to using ChatGPT directly, Jasper adds the brand training layer, team collaboration features, and content workflow tools that make it a managed content operations system rather than just a chat interface.
