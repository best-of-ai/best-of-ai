New Relic helps engineering teams understand how their software behaves in production. You add agents or OpenTelemetry instrumentation to your applications and infrastructure, and it collects performance data, errors, logs, and traces of requests as they move through services. Dashboards and alerts show slow endpoints, failing deployments, and resource pressure, and distributed tracing helps pinpoint which service in a chain caused a delay.

The platform covers application performance monitoring, infrastructure monitoring, log management, browser and mobile monitoring, and synthetic checks that test your site from outside. Applied intelligence groups related alerts and reduces noise, and an AI assistant lets people ask questions about their systems in plain language, summarize incidents, and get suggested next steps. It also includes tools for monitoring AI applications, tracking model calls, latency, cost, and quality.

New Relic charges based on data ingested and the number of users with full access, and it includes a free tier with a monthly data allowance. That model can be simpler than per-host pricing but needs watching, since high log volume increases costs. Datadog is the main competitor and is often seen as broader, while Grafana, Dynatrace, and Splunk cover other parts of the market. New Relic suits teams wanting one platform with a generous free start.
