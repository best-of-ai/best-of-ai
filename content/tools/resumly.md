What is Resumly?
Resumly is an all-in-one AI platform designed to completely automate your job search, from discovering relevant opportunities to submitting tailored applications. It tackles the time-consuming and often frustrating aspects of job hunting, enabling you to secure more interviews faster and with significantly less effort.

Key Features
🎯 AI-Powered Job Matching: Discover over a million new jobs monthly, intelligently matched to your profile and preferences. Resumly's AI actively searches and filters fresh, relevant roles daily, ensuring you never miss a suitable opportunity.

✍️ AI-Tailored Resumes & Cover Letters: Generate perfectly optimized resumes and cover letters for each specific job posting in seconds. This ensures your application is highly relevant, beats Applicant Tracking System (ATS) filters, and genuinely stands out to recruiters.

🚀 Automated Application Submission: Apply to hundreds of jobs effortlessly across major platforms using Resumly’s Chrome extension. Your AI-tailored documents are automatically sent, transforming hours of manual effort into minutes.

🗣️ AI-Powered Interview Practice: Prepare confidently for upcoming interviews with AI-driven mock interview sessions. Gain real-time feedback and refine your responses to enhance your performance and increase your chances of success.

📊 Comprehensive Application Tracking: Monitor your entire job hunt from a single, intuitive dashboard. Track applications, responses, and progress, giving you clear insights and control over your search.
