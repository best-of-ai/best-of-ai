Brandwatch is used by marketing, communications, and research teams to understand what people say online. It collects posts and mentions from social networks, news sites, forums, blogs, and reviews, and lets analysts build searches to track a brand, a competitor, a campaign, or a topic. Dashboards show volume over time, sentiment, demographics, top influencers, and emerging themes, which helps with campaign measurement, crisis monitoring, and product feedback.

AI plays a role in making sense of the data. Language models classify sentiment and topics, group conversations into themes, summarize large sets of posts, and answer questions about the findings in natural language. Image recognition finds logos in pictures posted without any text. Alerts warn teams when mentions spike, and the social management side supports publishing and responding to messages from one place.

Brandwatch is part of Cision and is sold on custom enterprise contracts, so it is expensive and aimed at large brands and agencies. Data access depends on the policies of the social platforms, which has changed access to some networks over time. Competitors include Sprinklr, Talkwalker, Meltwater, and Sprout Social's listening tools. For small teams, simpler tools like Mention or Google Alerts may be sufficient, while Brandwatch is best for deep analysis at scale.
