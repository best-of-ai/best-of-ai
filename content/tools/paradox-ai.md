Paradox focuses on hiring for roles with many applicants, such as retail, hospitality, and logistics. Olivia chats with candidates by text or on a career site, asks qualifying questions, answers common questions about the job and company, and books interviews directly on managers' calendars.

That removes much of the scheduling and screening back and forth that slows recruiting. Candidates get fast responses, and recruiters spend time on interviews instead of coordination. The platform includes an applicant tracking tool and onboarding support.

Workday acquired Paradox to add conversational recruiting to its HR suite. AI in hiring is subject to rules on bias and transparency in some places, and candidates should know when they are talking to an AI. Alternatives include Phenom, HireVue, Eightfold, iCIMS, and Sense. Paradox suits employers hiring at volume.
