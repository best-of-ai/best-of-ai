Aleph Alpha, based in Heidelberg, built models for European governments and enterprises that want AI hosted outside US and Chinese providers. Its Luminous and Pharia models were aimed at that audience.

In April 2026 it agreed to merge with Cohere, with Cohere's shareholders holding about 90 percent of the combined company and Aleph Alpha's holders 10 percent. Schwarz Group, an Aleph Alpha investor, said it would put $600 million into Cohere's Series E. The deal needs antitrust approval and we could not confirm that it has closed. One source puts the combined valuation near $20 billion. Cohere describes the pairing as a transatlantic sovereign AI company.
