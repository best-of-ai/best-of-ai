Cartesia makes low-latency voice models built on state space architectures, which the founders helped develop. Sonic-3, announced in October 2025, supports 42 languages with model latency near 90 milliseconds, though one post cites 190 milliseconds end to end. A $100 million round was reported with it, from NVIDIA and others, but funding totals differ by source, from $64 million for an earlier Series A to $191 million overall.

2026 items on aggregator pages include Mamba-3, an inference-first architecture in March, and Ink-2, a speech-to-text model that reportedly topped Artificial Analysis's new STT leaderboard in May. We found no confirmed 2026 funding round and no primary sources for those two.
