Deepgram builds speech-to-text and voice agent infrastructure. In January 2026 it raised $130 million in a Series C led by AVP at a $1.3 billion valuation and bought OfOne, a Y Combinator company doing voice AI for restaurant drive-thrus. Total funding estimates range from $215 million to $245 million. It also announced a partnership with IBM in February to bring voice AI to watsonx.

On the product side, Flux is its speech recognition model that handles interruptions in conversation, and Flux Multilingual reached general availability on April 29, 2026 in 10 languages. One source mentions a Flux TTS for real-time agents and annual recurring revenue above $100 million, without a date. Nova-3 is the main transcription model.
