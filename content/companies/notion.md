Notion's AI went from a writing helper to agents that run on their own. Custom Agents arrived in Notion 3.3 on February 24, 2026. They work in the background on a schedule or when something happens, such as a new meeting note, and every run is logged so changes can be reviewed and reversed. They were free to try until May 3, 2026, and after that run on Notion credits sold as an add-on for Business and Enterprise plans.

Later releases kept adding to this. Notion 3.4 in April reportedly made agents 35 to 50 percent cheaper to run, 3.5 in May opened a Developer Platform, and 3.6 in July added External Agents. Admins can set which AI models the agents use to keep costs predictable. One report says customers built over a million custom agents in the first three months.

The older Notion AI features for drafting, summarizing and answering questions across a workspace still sit alongside the agents. Most of the dates here come from Notion's release notes and secondary blogs.
