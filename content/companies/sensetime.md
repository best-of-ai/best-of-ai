SenseTime started in computer vision and now sells large multimodal models. In the first half of 2026 it reported revenue of 2.91 billion yuan, up 23.4 percent, with recurring revenue of 1.14 billion yuan, and said it recorded its first profit in a half-year period. Full-year 2025 revenue passed 5 billion yuan, up 33 percent, with second-half EBITDA turning positive for the first time since its IPO.

Its model line is SenseNova. Releases listed in the H1 report include the NEO-Unify architecture, SenseNova U1 and U1.5, a Flash-Lite agent model for office work, a content model called U1-Pro and a spatial model, SI-8B. Sources disagree on whether U1-Pro had shipped and on whether the Flash-Lite version is 6.7 or 6.8. Seko, a video agent, produces about 10,000 minutes of video a day, and one report says it serves more than 800,000 creators and 1,300 enterprise clients.

SenseTime is under US sanctions and says cheaper models can still win. These results are the company's own figures.
