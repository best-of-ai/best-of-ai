AMD's AI business centers on the Instinct accelerators and the ROCm software stack. In October 2025 OpenAI agreed to deploy 6 gigawatts of AMD GPUs across several generations, starting with 1 gigawatt in 2026, and received a warrant for up to 160 million AMD shares that vest as deployment and share-price milestones are met. Oracle plans 50,000 MI450 GPUs in a supercluster built on AMD's Helios rack. We found no confirmation that OpenAI has finished a full MI450 deployment.

On software, AMD announced ROCm 7.2 support for Ryzen AI 400 processors at CES 2026 and ROCm.ai at Advancing AI on July 23, 2026, including a command-line tool for installing, serving and managing AI workloads. IDC describes ROCm as AMD's common layer across client, edge and data center hardware.

Our hardware list covers the Instinct line from MI100 through MI455X, plus Radeon and Radeon Pro cards used for local inference. One report claimed Meta signed a matching 6 GW deal in February 2026, but we found no second source.
