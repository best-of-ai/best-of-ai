Cohere sells language models to enterprises, with an emphasis on private deployment for banks, governments and other regulated customers. Command A is its flagship generation model, Embed and Rerank power search and retrieval, and North is its agent platform for employees, with a version aimed at pharmaceutical work.

In April 2026 Cohere announced it would merge with Germany's Aleph Alpha, with Cohere's shareholders holding about 90 percent of the combined company. Schwarz Group, an Aleph Alpha backer, said it would put $600 million into Cohere's Series E. Later reports put the round at $2 billion to $3 billion, including money from the Canadian government, and one source gives a combined valuation near $20 billion. The deal needs antitrust approval, and we could not confirm it has closed.

Cohere also bought Reliant AI in May 2026 to add a biotech data platform to North. Its pitch is sovereignty: models and agents that run where the customer's data already lives.
