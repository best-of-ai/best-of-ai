xAI makes Grok, the chatbot that lives inside X and also runs as a standalone app and API. The company was founded by Elon Musk in 2023, and SpaceX acquired it in February 2026. The two were fully merged by May, and the work now sits under a SpaceXAI division.

Grok 4.5, released on July 8, 2026 alongside a partnership with Cursor, is the newest model we could confirm. It targets coding and agent use. Other sources mention a Grok 4.6, but we found only one report of it. Grok 5 is reported to be in training on Colossus 2, a cluster SpaceX puts at roughly one gigawatt of combined draw with the original Colossus.

xAI also sells image and voice models (Grok Imagine and Grok Voice) through the same API. In May 2026 it agreed to give Anthropic access to Colossus 1 capacity, an unusual deal between two direct competitors.

SpaceX also reported an agreement in June 2026 to acquire Cursor for $60 billion in stock, which would put a widely used coding editor under the same parent as Grok. We have not confirmed that the deal has closed.
