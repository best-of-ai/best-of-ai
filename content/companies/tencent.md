Tencent's models carry the Hunyuan name, and its consumer assistant is Yuanbao. The current flagship is Hy3, launched on July 6, 2026 after an open-sourced preview in April. It is a mixture-of-experts model with 295 billion total parameters, about 21 billion active, under Apache 2.0, with three reasoning modes. One source lists a 256K token context window.

Tencent rebuilt the Hunyuan line in 2026 under chief AI scientist Yao Shunyu, a former OpenAI researcher, and Hy3 was the first major result. It was put straight into Yuanbao and into Tencent's coding and work tools, including CodeBuddy and WorkBuddy, and developer pricing was cut at the same time. WeChat, Tencent's messaging app, is the obvious distribution channel for whatever comes next.

Earlier Hunyuan releases cover other media, including Hunyuan-Video for video, Hunyuan3D for 3D assets and Hunyuan-DiT for images.
