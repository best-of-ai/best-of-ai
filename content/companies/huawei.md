Huawei's AI role is mainly hardware. Its roadmap from September 2025 has the Ascend 950PR for prefill and recommendation in the first quarter of 2026, the 950DT for decode and training in the fourth quarter, and an Atlas 950 SuperPoD of up to 8,192 chips offering 8 exaflops at FP8. The Ascend 960 is set for 2027 and the 970 for 2028. A Digitimes report in March 2026 said the 950PR has nearly three times the performance of NVIDIA's China-market H20, but we saw only the headline.

The chips matter for Chinese labs. DeepSeek says V4 is fully adapted to run on Ascend SuperNodes and that Huawei chips handled part of training. One analyst source puts a single Ascend 950 roughly on par with NVIDIA's Hopper generation, which is commentary rather than a Huawei claim.

On models, Huawei's Pangu line includes the Pangu Pro MoE 72B from 2025, designed to balance load across Ascend chips. We found no confirmed new Pangu releases for 2026.
