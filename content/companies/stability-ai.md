Stability AI built its name on Stable Diffusion, the open image model that started the open generative image wave in 2022. The company has since shifted toward audio and music. In August 2026 it closed a $76 million Series B with Electronic Arts, Sony Music, Universal Music, Warner Music, AMD Ventures and others. The three music companies also licensed their catalogs for training.

That focus matches Stable Audio 3.0, which Stability says is trained only on licensed data. Reports disagree on whether it launched in May or June of 2026. Warner Music announced a collaboration with Stability earlier on tools for music creation that give artists control and pay them.

Image models remain in the lineup, including Stable Diffusion 3.5 and 4, plus video through Stable Video Diffusion. Legal risk has not gone away: a class action from illustrators over Stable Diffusion's training data was scheduled for trial on September 8, 2026, and we could not confirm the outcome.
