Google's AI is spread across the products people already use. Gemini is the assistant and the model family behind it, and Gemini 3.5 Flash became the default model after I/O in May 2026. Gemini Spark, a background agent that works inside Gmail and Docs, started with US Google AI Ultra subscribers. Google says AI Mode in Search passed 1 billion monthly users, and AI Overviews reach about 2.5 billion.

For developers, Google AI Studio and Vertex AI give access to the models through an API, and Antigravity 2.0 is a desktop app for running several coding agents at once. NotebookLM turns uploaded documents into a research assistant that can also produce audio summaries. Workspace has Gemini built into Docs, Sheets, and Gmail.

The research itself comes from Google DeepMind, which has its own page on this site. Google also runs the hardware side: its TPUs train Gemini and are rented out through Google Cloud, and Anthropic signed a deal in October 2025 for up to one million of them.
