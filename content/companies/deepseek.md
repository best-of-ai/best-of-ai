DeepSeek is the Chinese lab that showed strong reasoning models could be trained and released openly for much less than Western labs spend. DeepSeek V4 previewed on April 24, 2026 in two versions. V4-Pro reportedly has about 1.6 trillion parameters with 49 billion active, and V4-Flash about 284 billion with 13 billion active. Both handle up to one million tokens of context. It is still labeled a preview, and DeepSeek has not given a date for the full release.

Hardware is a notable part of the story. Huawei says its Ascend SuperNode systems are fully adapted for V4 inference and that its chips were used for part of the training, while DeepSeek has not said whether NVIDIA chips were also involved. DeepSeek said Pro could cost up to 12 times more than Flash because of limits on high-end compute, and expects Pro prices to fall once Ascend 950 supernodes ship in volume in the second half of the year. Fortune put Pro's output price near $3.48 per million tokens.

Independent testing of V4 is still limited, so the benchmark claims are mostly the company's own.
