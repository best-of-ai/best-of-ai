Workday's AI is built around agents for HR and finance. In September 2025 it agreed to buy Sana, a Swedish AI company, for about $1.1 billion, adding Sana Agents and an agent builder with search. Workday called the aim becoming the "front door for work." It had already bought Paradox for recruiting and Flowise, a low-code agent builder, in 2025.

The company also set up an Agent System of Record, where customers can register and manage agents, including those built in Microsoft's Azure AI Foundry and Copilot Studio. Illuminate is the brand for its AI features in recruiting, payroll and planning.

We found no 2026-specific announcements or confirmation that the Sana deal closed, though Workday said it expected to complete it by the end of January 2026.
