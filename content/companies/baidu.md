Baidu's AI work revolves around Ernie. ERNIE 5.0 was unveiled in November 2025 as a natively multimodal model and reached final release in early 2026. ERNIE 5.1 followed on May 9, 2026 as a closed-weight sparse mixture-of-experts model with a 128K token context window. Baidu has not published a parameter count. Earlier versions, Ernie 4.0 and 4.5, are in our model list.

Baidu designs its own chips under the Kunlun name. The roadmap has the M100 for inference in 2026 and the M300 for training very large multimodal models in 2027, and it reportedly plans to spin off the chip unit, Kunlunxin, and list it in Hong Kong. Baidu says a Kunlunxin cluster helped train part of the ERNIE 5.1 series.

Apollo Go, its robotaxi service, is the other major AI business. Qianfan is the cloud platform that sells the models to developers.
