Microsoft's AI business has two sides. Copilot is the assistant inside Windows, Microsoft 365, Edge and GitHub, and Azure AI is the cloud service that hosts models from OpenAI, Anthropic and others. Microsoft has been OpenAI's largest outside backer since 2019.

In June 2026, around its Build conference, Microsoft AI under Mustafa Suleyman announced seven in-house MAI models. The flagship is MAI-Thinking-1, a reasoning model Microsoft says was trained without distilling from other companies' models. The set also covers coding, image generation, speech and transcription. Suleyman described the effort as aimed at long-term self-sufficiency, which reads as hedging against dependence on OpenAI.

Smaller research models continue under the Phi name, which Microsoft positions for running on laptops and phones, and the company builds its own Maia accelerators for Azure. Build also brought a standalone GitHub Copilot desktop app and Copilot Autopilots, agents that handle multistep tasks.
