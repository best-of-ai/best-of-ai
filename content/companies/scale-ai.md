Scale AI sells training data, labeling and evaluation to AI labs and governments. Its biggest event came in June 2025, when Meta took a 49 percent stake for $14.3 billion and hired founder Alexandr Wang to lead its Superintelligence effort. In July 2025 Scale cut about 14 percent of its staff, around 200 roles, and ended contracts with 500 contractors. Interim CEO Jason Droege said the company had grown too fast.

Several large customers reportedly left after the Meta deal, and rivals such as Mercor, Surge, Turing and Labelbox picked up business from labs wary of Meta's stake. Scale reorganized its generative AI group from 16 pods into five teams and said it would keep hiring in enterprise and government work.
