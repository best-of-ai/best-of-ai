Synthesia makes AI avatar video for training and corporate communication. In January 2026 it closed a $200 million Series E at a $4 billion valuation, led by Google Ventures, with NVentures, Accel and Air Street Capital participating. Staff were able to sell shares in a secondary sale alongside it. Earlier rounds valued it at $2.1 billion in early 2025 and $1 billion in 2023.

Express-2 avatars have full-body gestures and are available on paid plans. Sources disagree on whether Express-2 launched in September 2025 or September 2026. Synthesia 3.0 added Video Agents, interactive videos that answer viewer questions in real time, per one summary. We found no further 2026 funding.
