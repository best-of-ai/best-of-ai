Cerebras builds wafer-scale processors and sells their speed for inference. It went public on Nasdaq in mid-May 2026 under CBRS, the largest US tech IPO since Snowflake. Reports disagree on the numbers: some say $4.8 billion raised at a $48.7 billion valuation, others $5.55 billion at around $100 billion after the first day.

OpenAI is the anchor customer. It agreed in January to 750 megawatts of low-latency capacity, later widened to a deal worth about $20 billion through 2028, in three 250 MW tranches through Cerebras cloud. OpenAI also holds warrants worth up to 10 percent of Cerebras and loaned it $1 billion, per the S-1. A separate deal with AWS in March eased worries about customer concentration, though Cerebras has not said how much revenue comes from OpenAI.

Cerebras claims large speed and power advantages over GPUs for inference. The sources we found did not discuss the WSE-3 by name, and the speed figures are the company's own.
