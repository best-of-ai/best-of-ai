Baichuan changed direction toward medicine. On January 13, 2026 it open-sourced Baichuan-M3, a medical model that CEO Wang Xiaochuan presented as able to run a consultation like a doctor. The company says it beat OpenAI's GPT-5.2 on HealthBench and HealthBench Hard, with a score of 65.1 on the former, and that about 80 percent of its compute goes to reinforcement learning. Nine days later it released M3 Plus, which cut the reported hallucination rate from 3.5 to 2.6 percent and API prices by 70 percent. It also offers the model free by API to Chinese healthcare institutions.

Wang said Baichuan had about 3 billion yuan in cash and an IPO planned for 2027. The benchmark and hallucination figures are the company's own and have not been independently checked, and our searches found nothing dated after early March 2026. Weights for Baichuan-M3-235B are on Hugging Face.
