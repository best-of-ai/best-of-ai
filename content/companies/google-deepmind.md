Google DeepMind is where Google's frontier models get built. It formed in 2023 when DeepMind and Google Brain merged, and its main output is the Gemini family. Gemini 3.5 Flash shipped in May 2026 with a Pro model in testing at the time, and later releases include Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 4 Argon. Gemini Omni, announced at I/O, combines language reasoning with video generation and takes image, audio, video, and text as input.

The lab also keeps up open and research work. Gemma is the open-weight family, with Gemma 4 released under Apache 2.0 in April 2026. Veo generates video, Imagen and the Nano Banana models handle images, and Genie builds interactive worlds from prompts. Genie 4 opened to trusted testers on October 27, 2026. Gemini Robotics extends the same models to physical robots.

AlphaFold is the project most people outside AI know it for. AlphaFold 3 predicts how proteins interact with DNA, RNA, and small molecules, and the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry went partly to DeepMind's Demis Hassabis and John Jumper for the protein work. One aggregator reported in mid-2026 that the dedicated AlphaFold team had been reassigned, which we could not confirm from a primary source.
