Shanghai AI Laboratory is a state-backed research institute that publishes the InternLM language models and the InternVL multimodal models. Its newest confirmed release is Intern-S1-Pro, open-sourced on February 4, 2026. It is a 1-trillion-parameter scientific multimodal model built on the SAGE architecture, with a technical report on arXiv. The first Intern-S1 launched at the World AI Conference in July 2025.

One benchmark site claims a release in September 2026 but lists only one model with a date that conflicts with others, so we ignore it. The InternLM Hugging Face collection and GitHub organization are the places to check for anything newer.
