StepFun builds multimodal models for text, voice and video. Step 3.5 Flash came out in February 2026 under Apache 2.0, Step 3.7 Flash in May, and Step 5 Preview on September 18 with closed weights, a departure from the open releases before it.

The company has raised heavily and is preparing a Hong Kong IPO. It announced a Series B+ of over 5 billion yuan in January, and in May reportedly closed a $2.5 billion round while dismantling its offshore structure. One source says over 30 billion yuan, which conflicts. In June a report said it might file at a $12 billion valuation. We found no confirmation that the listing has happened.
