Sakana AI is a Tokyo lab known for nature-inspired methods such as model merging and evolutionary search. Its AI Scientist, first shown in August 2024, automates research from idea to paper at about $15 per paper by the company's estimate. Sakana and collaborators at the University of British Columbia, the Vector Institute and Oxford published the system in Nature on March 26, 2026.

It raised about $135 million (20 billion yen) in November 2025 at a post-money valuation near $2.65 billion, with MUFG and Khosla Ventures among the backers, which made it Japan's most valuable startup. Google invested in January 2026, and a further strategic investment in March was undisclosed. Total funding figures differ by source, from $228 million to $379 million. Fugu is its commercial model line.
