Tenstorrent, led by chip architect Jim Keller, builds AI processors on the open RISC-V standard. Its Blackhole chip sits inside Galaxy servers with 32 chips each. Sales opened in April 2026 and volume shipping was announced in May. In a June interview, Keller said it was producing 1,000 Galaxy servers with about half already sold, though that is his claim relayed by a Taiwanese outlet. An April analysis said the software stack was still early for Blackhole.

Reports on funding disagree. Dealroom has a $1.5 billion Series E at a $6.2 billion valuation announced October 19, 2026, while another tracker cites about $1.29 billion in August from an SEC filing. The same interview says Tenstorrent is pursuing an IPO. We found nothing on a chip called Quasar.
