Salesforce has bet its product line on Agentforce, its platform for AI agents that work inside CRM, Slack and Data Cloud. Dreamforce 2026 in September focused on getting agents into production. Salesforce announced seven job-specific agents, including Piper for inbound pipeline, which is generally available, and Hunter for outbound sales, due in November 2026. Agentforce Coworker reached 100,000 users in its first 35 days, according to the company.

A partnership with Anthropic moved to open beta at the event, bringing Claude into Salesforce and letting Salesforce data be used from inside Claude. Salesforce also showed its first in-house reasoning model during the keynote. Earlier research from Salesforce AI Research, including BLIP, CodeT5 and XGen, is still used in open-source work.

Reception has been mixed. Some analysts said TDX 2026 held few surprises, and customers still ask how much of the agent demo carries over into everyday use.
