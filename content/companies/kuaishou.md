Kuaishou, the short-video platform, runs Kling, one of the strongest video generation models to come out of China. Kling 3.0 launched in February 2026 with video up to 15 seconds, up from 10, and inputs and outputs across text, images, audio and video. Turbo and native 4K output followed.

Kling is now a real business. Kuaishou reported Kling revenue over 650 million yuan in the first quarter of 2026, up more than 300 percent year on year, and over 850 million yuan in the second quarter, up more than 200 percent. It put annualized revenue at about $500 million in March. These figures are from Kuaishou's earnings releases, which we read through news reproductions.

The group's profit fell 27 percent in the first quarter even as AI grew, which shows how much the company is spending on it. Kolors handles images.
