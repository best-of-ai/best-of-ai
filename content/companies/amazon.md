Amazon's AI work mostly runs through AWS. Bedrock lets customers call models from Anthropic, Meta, Mistral and others through one API, and Amazon's own Nova family sits alongside them. Nova 2 is the current generation, with Pro handling text, images, video and speech, and Sonic covering voice. Bedrock AgentCore is the platform AWS sells for building and running production agents, with newer features for memory and policy controls.

Consumer AI centers on Alexa+, the rebuilt assistant that uses Nova and Anthropic models and is now available to US customers. For developers, Kiro is Amazon's agentic coding environment, and Amazon Q covers coding help and business questions.

Amazon builds its own chips too. Trainium is the training and inference accelerator, and AWS says PyTorch workloads can move over with a configuration change. Amazon is also Anthropic's main cloud partner, and in February 2026 it announced a large investment in OpenAI, with the first $15 billion tied to initial terms and a further $35 billion conditional.
