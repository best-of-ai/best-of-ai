Together AI is a cloud for running open models. Developers use it to call models such as Llama, Qwen and DeepSeek through an API, to fine-tune them, or to rent GPU clusters. It also published RedPajama, an early open training dataset and model set.

The company raised an $800 million Series C led by Aramco Ventures, reported on July 1, 2026, at an $8.3 billion valuation. It says it plans to scale capacity roughly fifty times over five years. Partnerships include a multi-year deal to run inference on IBM Cloud with NVIDIA B300 systems, a strategic deal with Humain in Saudi Arabia and an Inference Exchange with Equinix and NVIDIA, expected in early 2027. A job posting put the IBM deal at $240 million, which we could not confirm.

In March 2026 it launched voice agents that run speech-to-text, the language model and text-to-speech on one cloud, with end-to-end latency under 500 milliseconds. Revenue figures online vary widely, so we leave them out.
