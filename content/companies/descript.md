Descript is an editor where you cut audio and video by editing the transcript. Its AI side is Underlord, an assistant that takes instructions such as removing filler words, finding highlights or building a short clip for social media. Underlord can remember your editing preferences across projects, and custom instructions let you set a house style once.

Descript has leaned on outside models for Underlord. Third-party coverage says it added Claude Sonnet 5, Fable 5 and Opus 5 during 2026, with Fable bringing video-aware editing, though we could not confirm those dates on Descript's own pages. A public API entered open beta on May 14, 2026, so other tools can drive edits.

Studio Sound, which cleans up poor recordings, and the Overdub voice clone remain part of the toolset.
