SAP's AI is Joule, an assistant built into its business applications, and at Sapphire 2026 in May it moved toward agents. The SAP Business AI Platform merges the Business Technology Platform, Business Data Cloud and Business AI into one governed environment, with a Knowledge Graph that gives agents access to a company's business data and processes. Joule Studio 2.0 lets customers and partners build their own agents, with first availability from June 2026, and SAP set up a roughly 100 million euro fund for partners to build on it.

Counts of prebuilt agents vary: coverage says over 50 assistants and 200 agents, while an SAP product leader cited 224 agents and 51 assistants. Some capabilities are being extended to ECC and on-premises customers who have not moved to the cloud.

A headline about a managed Joule Studio with Cursor and Claude Code support also appeared, but we could not check the detail.
