Ant Group, the company behind Alipay, runs the inclusionAI lab, which publishes the Ling and Ring open-weight models. Ling is the general model line and Ring the reasoning line. In February 2026 it released Ling-2.5-1T and Ring-2.5-1T, the latter described as the first hybrid linear-architecture thinking model. Ling-2.6-1T, built for agents and tool use, followed on April 30, and Ring-2.6-1T, a trillion-parameter MoE reasoning model under the MIT license, around May 8 to 14.

Aggregators list later Ling 3.0 and 3.1 flash models, including Ling-3.0-flash with 124 billion total parameters, which we could not verify from a primary source. Confirm the current lineup on inclusionAI's Hugging Face page. Ant also released Ling models tuned for finance.
