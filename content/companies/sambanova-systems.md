SambaNova makes its own AI chips and sells inference systems built around them. The SN50 chip arrived in February 2026 alongside a $350 million round led by Vista Equity Partners and Cambium Capital, and SambaNova says it beats NVIDIA's B200 systems. General availability of SambaRack SN50 systems is expected in the second half of 2026, and SoftBank already deploys SN50.

Intel considered buying SambaNova for about $1.6 billion, but Bloomberg reported in January that talks collapsed. Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan is also SambaNova's chairman and recused himself. The companies signed a multiyear partnership in February, under which SambaNova adopts Intel server chips and graphics cards, and sources disagree on how much Intel put into the round.

One source reports a $1 billion raise at an $11 billion valuation in July 2026, with JPMorgan as an inference partner. We found only one source, so treat it as unconfirmed.
