Perplexity runs an answer engine that cites its sources. It grew from search into a broader set of agent products. Comet is its AI browser, available free and using a mix of models including Perplexity's own. Perplexity Computer, launched in February 2026, is a cloud agent that works on longer tasks, and Labs and Research modes produce reports and small apps.

For developers, the Sonar API is being replaced by a new Agent API, and Perplexity says it will support Sonar until September 27, 2026. New projects are told to start on the Agent API, which can route to models from other providers alongside Perplexity's own.

The valuation numbers are murky. A round in early 2026 is reported at roughly $21 to $23 billion, with annualized revenue around $500 million, and a later round at higher marks was rumored without confirmation.
