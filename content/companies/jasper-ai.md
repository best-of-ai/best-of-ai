Jasper sells AI for marketing teams and has repositioned from a writing tool to agents. Jasper Agents and Canvas launched in June 2025, and the company now says it offers over 100 specialized agents for jobs such as social planning, competitor research, content repurposing and email optimization. Jasper Grid, an orchestration layer for large content workflows, followed in early 2026.

On January 28, 2026 it published a State of AI in Marketing report from a survey of 1,400 marketers with Benchmarkit. It says 91 percent of teams now use AI, up from 63 percent in 2025, though only 41 percent can show ROI easily. A review described the 91 percent as daily use, which differs from the press release wording.

A single blog post mentions a translation agent in June 2026 and a new chief marketing officer in August, neither confirmed. Most coverage is vendor-written or review content rather than independent reporting.
