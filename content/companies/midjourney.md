Midjourney builds its own image models and has stayed independent of outside venture funding. V8 went to alpha testers on March 17, 2026. V8.1 became the default model on April 30, and one source says V8.2 followed as the default on July 24. V9 is in progress, and the public roadmap also mentions Niji 8 and a second video model.

Video is still on V1, launched in June 2025, which animates still images through the Animate button in the web editor. Sources disagree on video length and frame rate for the V8 era, with figures from 5 to 10 seconds, and some aggregator pages seem to label endpoints differently from Midjourney's own names, so we do not give a spec here.

Most people use Midjourney through its web app, which has replaced the original Discord-only workflow.
