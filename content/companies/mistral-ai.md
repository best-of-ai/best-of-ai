Mistral is the largest European model lab. It started with small open-weight models like Mistral 7B and Mixtral, and has since moved into commercial releases. Mistral Large 3, released in December 2025, is its most advanced language model, and the lineup also includes Codestral and Devstral for code, Pixtral for images and Voxtral for speech.

Le Chat is the consumer assistant, and Vibe, launched in May 2026, is a work and coding agent. Mistral sells to enterprises that want models running in their own data centers, which is where its open weights give it an edge over closed rivals.

Compute is now a big part of the story. Mistral raised $830 million in debt in March 2026 to build a data center in Bruyères-le-Châtel, near Paris, which will hold 13,800 NVIDIA GB300 GPUs. It wants 200 megawatts of capacity across Europe by 2027. Reports in September 2026 put a new equity round near 3 billion euros at a valuation above 21 billion, though the exact figures vary between outlets.
