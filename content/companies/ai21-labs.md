AI21 Labs, based in Tel Aviv, makes the Jamba models, which combine Mamba state-space layers with transformer layers to handle long inputs more cheaply. It also sells Maestro, an agent platform, and earlier built the Jurassic models. NVIDIA and Google backed a $300 million round in May 2025, and AI21 has raised about $626 million across nine rounds.

In late December 2025, Calcalist reported that NVIDIA was in advanced talks to buy AI21 for $2 billion to $3 billion, possibly folding Maestro into NVIDIA AI Enterprise, with some coverage calling it mainly an acquihire of about 200 staff. Neither company confirmed a deal. One secondary post says it did not go ahead and that Nebius then looked at buying AI21, but that claim has a single undated source.

We found no 2026 report of a signed acquisition, so the company's current status needs checking.
