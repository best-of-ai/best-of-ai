Codeium made a free coding assistant and the Windsurf editor. Its path changed in 2025. Reports say Cognition closed a $250 million acquisition of Windsurf in December 2025, while one source says Google paid about $2.4 billion to license technology and hire its founders, and we could not reconcile the two. On June 2, 2026 Windsurf became Devin Desktop, the next generation of the editor, and windsurf.com now redirects to devin.ai/desktop.

Cognition describes SWE-1.6, its own coding model, in an early preview from an ongoing training run, and the free tier is said to include it. Two secondary sources report a SWE-2 on September 10, 2026 that we could not confirm. Cognition raised over $1 billion in a May 2026 Series D at about $26 billion post-money. These details come mostly from blogs and aggregators.
