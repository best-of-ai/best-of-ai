Databricks sells a data platform and has turned it into an AI platform. Agent Bricks builds and evaluates agents on a company's own data, Genie answers questions in plain language, and Lakebase, generally available since February 2026, is a serverless Postgres database meant for agent workloads. Databricks says Lakebase passed $100 million in run-rate revenue within months.

It also trains models, though less than before. MosaicML, acquired in 2023, produced DBRX and the MPT models, and Dolly was an early open instruction-tuned model. Today most customers use Databricks to run models from Anthropic, OpenAI and others against their own data.

The company closed $5 billion in funding in August 2026 at a $190 billion valuation, led by Coatue, and reports a revenue run-rate above $7 billion growing more than 80 percent year over year.
