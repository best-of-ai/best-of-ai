Palantir's AI product is AIP, the Artificial Intelligence Platform, which puts language models on top of a customer's operational data in Foundry or Gotham and lets them take actions inside business systems. Customers range from hospitals and banks to defense agencies. For example, Customers Bank said on October 15, 2026 it would use Foundry and AIP for deposit onboarding.

The financial results show how much demand there is. Palantir's Q3 2026 release raised full-year revenue guidance to about $8.53 billion, and its Q2 release put U.S. commercial revenue growth at 134 percent or more. We could not confirm the actual Q3 2026 figures from the sources we found.

Palantir does not build frontier models. It sells the software that connects models from other labs to private data, and it uses boot camps to get customers running quickly. Its defense work, including large Army contracts, is a major part of the business.
