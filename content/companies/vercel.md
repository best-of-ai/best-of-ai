Vercel is a platform for web developers that has pushed hard into AI tooling. The AI SDK, an open-source TypeScript library, reached version 7 around late June 2026, and Vercel says it has a harness layer for running agents. AI Gateway gives a single endpoint for many models with fallback and spend tracking, and new models appear there quickly. In June 2026 its changelog noted Claude Fable 5 access was suspended there while other Anthropic models stayed available.

v0 turns prompts into working interfaces and apps and reportedly has about 4 million users. Vercel Agent, in public beta in June, investigates production alerts and proposes fixes as pull requests. Usage figures for Gateway come from Vercel-linked sources, so we do not repeat them.

Vercel does not train its own models. Its pitch is the layer between the model providers and the apps developers ship.
