Character.AI lets people chat with custom characters, and about 20 million people use it each month. Its largest recent changes are about safety. It ended open-ended chat for users under 18 on November 25, 2025 and steers them toward Stories, a guided fiction format. Age checks can involve a selfie or ID. Reports say minors are about 10 percent of users.

In January 2026 Character.AI and Google agreed to settle several lawsuits over teen suicides and self-harm, including the case of Sewell Setzer, filed in Florida, Colorado, New York and Texas. Terms were not disclosed and neither company admitted liability. Kentucky's attorney general filed the first state consumer action against the company on January 8, 2026. We did not confirm final court approval.

Google licensed the company's technology and hired its founders in 2024, so Character.AI's models now sit in a closer relationship with Google than a normal startup.
