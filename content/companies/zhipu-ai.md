Zhipu AI, which trades its products as Z.ai, became the first foundation model company listed on the Hong Kong exchange, on January 8, 2026 (ticker 2513.HK), raising about HK$4.35 billion at HK$116.20 a share. It builds the GLM models. GLM-5, reported at 744 billion parameters, was released as open source in mid-February 2026, aimed at agents and complex engineering tasks. GLM-5.2 followed on June 17 and one source ranks it second on Code Arena's front-end benchmark, behind Claude Fable 5.

The company has kept raising capital since going public. Reports describe a roughly $4 billion Hong Kong placement around July 2026 and, per one source, about $5 billion more through shares and convertible bonds by September. A $2.2 billion follow-on listing on Shanghai's STAR Market was also reported, with no confirmation of its status.
