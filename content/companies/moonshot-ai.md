Moonshot AI makes Kimi, which first drew attention for long-context chat. Kimi K3, released in mid-July 2026, is an open-weight model with about 2.8 trillion parameters and a 1 million token context window. Moonshot published the weights and a technical report on Hugging Face on July 27.

Money followed. On July 29, 2026 it closed a $3.5 billion round at a $35 billion valuation, well above its original $1 to $2 billion target, with China's National AI Industry Investment Fund among the leaders. One French report says annualized revenue passed $1 billion in August, up from $300 million two months earlier. Another source says Moonshot is weighing a Hong Kong listing and seeking NVIDIA Blackwell chips to train Kimi K4.

One outlet reported a far larger $200 billion valuation round, which conflicts with Bloomberg and Yicai and looks unreliable. Kimi Code, a coding agent, and earlier models like K2 and Kimi-VL remain available.
