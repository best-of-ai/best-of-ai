Cloudflare's AI business has two sides: running AI and controlling who can scrape the web for it. On the first, Workers AI runs inference on Cloudflare's network, reportedly including large open models like Kimi K2.5. In April 2026 the company announced Agent Cloud, with tools such as Dynamic Workers for sandboxing code that an AI writes. Agents Week 2026 brought around 30 announcements.

On the second, Cloudflare started blocking AI crawlers by default in 2025 and launched Pay Per Crawl so publishers could charge them. Reports say it is moving to a pay-per-use or pay-per-citation model, with You.com and Ceramic.ai among launch partners, though accounts differ on the details. One source says mixed-use AI crawlers are blocked by default from September 15, 2026 on pages with ads, and another headline says agents can now carry wallets to pay for access.

We did not find Cloudflare's own press releases for these, so the payment mechanics are provisional.
