HubSpot's AI products come under the Breeze name. The company has sold Breeze agents for content, social, prospecting and customer support. In April 2026 it moved to outcome-based pricing for its agents, so customers pay when an agent completes the task. Agent actions are billed in HubSpot Credits, separate from seat licenses.

On July 23, 2026, according to one blog, HubSpot announced Agent Hub as a home for its agents, renamed Breeze Studio to Agent Builder and retired five beta agents. Reports differ on which model underlies the agents: one cites a developer changelog saying the default moved to GPT-5, another says GPT-4.x.

Spring 2026 releases also covered Answer Engine Optimization, which tracks how a brand appears in AI answers. Most of these details come from third-party blogs, so check HubSpot's changelog before relying on dates.
