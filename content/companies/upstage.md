Upstage, in Seoul, makes the Solar models and document AI. Solar Pro 3, released in March 2026, is a 102-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model focused on agents, tool calling and coding. It is one of the national champions in South Korea's sovereign AI foundation model program, whose first output was Solar Open 100B in January 2026. A plagiarism dispute followed that release.

AMD and Upstage expanded their collaboration on March 18, 2026, and on May 3 a government-backed fund approved a $400 million direct equity investment, though one analysis's breakdown could not be verified. Sources disagree on Solar Pro 4. One calls it the new flagship reasoning model, another says it was released in August 2026, and a third says it was planned for late July, so the timing is unconfirmed.
