Otter.ai transcribes meetings and is shifting toward agents. On October 20, 2026 it announced $320 million in non-dilutive growth financing from General Catalyst, with sources split on whether it came from the Customer Value Fund or the growth fund. It plans to use the money for enterprise customers, international expansion and agentic workflows. Recent products include Live Assist, a coaching agent for live calls, released in July 2026, and a new CMO joined in August.

One news site claims 40 million users and adoption by 86 percent of Fortune 500 companies, which we could not verify. Earlier funding totals range from about $50 million to more than $100 million depending on the source.
