The Technology Innovation Institute in Abu Dhabi makes the open Falcon models. On January 5, 2026 it released Falcon H1R 7B, a reasoning model built on a hybrid Transformer and Mamba design. TII says it matches or beats much larger reasoning models on math, code and general benchmarks. A fully FP8-quantized version followed on February 16 with the same benchmark results and lower memory use, according to the Falcon team's own write-up.

TII also launched Falcon-H1 Arabic in January 2026. The earlier Falcon 40B and 180B put the institute on open leaderboards in 2023, and Falcon 3 and Falcon Mamba extended the line. Benchmarks here come from TII, and the models are on Hugging Face.
