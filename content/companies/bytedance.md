ByteDance's AI efforts sit under Seed, and the consumer face is Doubao, which Quest Mobile counts at about 155 million weekly active users in China. Doubao-Seed-2.0 launched on February 14, 2026, with a Pro model positioned against GPT-5.2 and Gemini 3 Pro for long reasoning and agent tasks.

Seedance 2.0, a video generation model, debuted on February 12 and moved quickly into the market: it is available in Doubao, Jimeng and Volcano Engine, ByteDance's cloud, and the API opened to the public in April. It reached overseas users through CapCut with copyright safeguards. Seedream handles images.

Later releases, including a 4K Seedance update, Seedream 5.0, Seed-Audio 1.0 and SeedRealtime, appear on one consultancy page we could not match to ByteDance sources, and one site hinted at a Seedance 2.5 without detail. We leave them out. TikTok's recommendation systems are the older, separate AI business.
