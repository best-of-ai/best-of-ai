Twilio's AI work is about voice and messaging agents. ConversationRelay, generally available since May 2025, lets developers connect any language model to a phone call with streaming speech. Its insights feature, which reports latency, interruptions and handling time, reached general availability on May 6, 2026. Twilio's changelog also lists Deepgram's Flux speech-to-text model for turn detection and PCI-compliant voice workflows.

At SIGNAL 2026 Twilio presented a "Conversation Layer" over voice, SMS, WhatsApp and RCS that adds context, memory and orchestration for agents. One blog says the HIPAA and PCI support could open regulated industries, though we found only one source for the full product list.

Twilio does not train frontier models. It supplies the telephony and channel layer that language models talk through.
