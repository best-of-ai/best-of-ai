Writer sells a full-stack AI platform to large companies and trains its own Palmyra models. Palmyra X5, released in April 2025, is an adaptive reasoning model with a 1 million token context window, available on Writer's platform and Amazon Bedrock. Writer says it was trained mostly on synthetic data for about $1 million in GPU costs, a claim from the company. AI HQ is the hub where enterprises build, run and supervise agents on those models, and early use cases include financial reporting, RFP responses and support documentation.

A VentureBeat article from the X5 launch put Writer's valuation at $1.9 billion. We found no coverage of a 2026 funding round or a newer flagship model, so this page reflects 2025 releases.
