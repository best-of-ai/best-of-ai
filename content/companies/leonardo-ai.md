Leonardo.Ai makes image generation tools for game art, product design and other creative work, built around its Phoenix model. Canva bought it in 2024, reportedly for about $320 million with roughly 120 staff, and Phoenix now runs Canva's Dream Lab and Magic Media. A newsletter from August 2026 says Leonardo keeps operating independently and that Canva users' content goes to Leonardo only if they opt in.

A September 2026 review lists Free, Essential at $12 a month, Premium at $30 and Ultimate at $60, with 150 fast tokens a day on the free tier. Most of the 2026 coverage is from review sites, not announcements, so confirm prices on the site.
