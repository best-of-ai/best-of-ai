Qualcomm's AI business started in phones. Its Snapdragon chips include Hexagon NPUs that run assistants, camera features and translation on-device for most non-Apple flagship phones. AI Hub gives developers pre-optimized models for those chips.

In October 2025 Qualcomm announced a move into data centers with the AI200, due in 2026, and the AI250, due in 2027. The AI200 is a rack-scale inference system with 768 GB of LPDDR memory per card and liquid cooling. For the AI250 Qualcomm claims more than 10 times the effective memory bandwidth, using near-memory compute, but it has not published independent performance figures. Saudi Arabia's Humain is the launch customer, with a planned 200 megawatt deployment.

Qualcomm also agreed to buy Alphawave, a data center connectivity company, for about $2.4 billion. We did not confirm whether the AI200 has shipped.
