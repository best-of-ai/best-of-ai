Alibaba's AI efforts run through Qwen, one of the most widely used open model families in the world. Qwen3.8 is the current generation. The company announced it in July 2026, and Qwen3.8-Max became generally available on August 3 as a mixture-of-experts model with 2.4 trillion total parameters, about 95 billion active per query and a 1 million token context window. Open weights followed in August, along with a smaller dense 27B vision-language model under Apache 2.0.

Alibaba says Qwen3.8 ranks just behind Anthropic's Claude Fable 5, but it has not published benchmark scores to back the claim and no outside group has confirmed it. On Arena it was the top-ranked Chinese model for text at launch, still trailing several Anthropic models.

The family reaches beyond text. Wan handles video, Qwen-Image handles images and CosyVoice handles speech. Qwen Studio is the chat interface, QwenWork is an agent platform for office tasks, and the models are sold through Alibaba Cloud's Model Studio. Alibaba also designs its own AI chips under the Zhenwu name.
