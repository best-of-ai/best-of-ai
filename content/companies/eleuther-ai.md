EleutherAI is a nonprofit research collective that started the open language model movement with GPT-Neo, GPT-J and GPT-NeoX. Its Pythia suite, released in April 2023, has 16 models from 70 million to 12 billion parameters, built for studying how training shapes behavior. In June 2025 it released Common Pile v0.1, an 8 terabyte dataset of public domain and openly licensed text from 30 sources, with two 7B models, Comma v0.1-1T and 2T, trained on it to show that openly licensed data is enough for competitive models.
