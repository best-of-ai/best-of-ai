Anthropic builds Claude, a model family sold in four tiers: Fable, Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku. The 5.5 generation is current, with Haiku 5.5 arriving on October 7, 2026, after Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 in September. Fable 5.1 is the top general-access model. Mythos is a separate, restricted class offered only to approved organizations, mostly in cybersecurity and life sciences.

The products around the models have grown quickly. Claude Code, the command-line coding agent, reached general availability in May 2025. Cowork brings the same file and task automation to people who do not write code, and Claude Design, launched on April 17, 2026, produces visuals and prototypes. Claude Code Security, introduced in February 2026, scans codebases for vulnerabilities.

Safety research is a large part of the company's identity. Constitutional AI trains Claude against a written set of principles, and its interpretability team has published work identifying millions of internal features in the models. In September 2026 Anthropic let outside evaluators work with employee-level access to its models, with Accenture first. A public IPO filing followed on October 26, 2026.
