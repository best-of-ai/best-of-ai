Replit lets people build apps from a prompt in the browser. Agent 4, launched in March 2026, is billed as ten times faster than Agent 3 and moves from a chat window to a canvas where you can also make spreadsheets, charts and games. It reached iPhone in May 2026 after a four-month dispute with Apple's App Store review.

The company raised $400 million in a Series D led by Georgian on March 11, 2026, at a $9 billion valuation, three times its value six months earlier. Replit said it had more than 50 million registered users and 150,000 paying customers, and aims for $1 billion in annual revenue by the end of 2026. Revenue trackers differ on 2025 and 2026 figures, so treat those as estimates.

Replit's early code models, such as replit-code, are now minor compared with the agent products. It uses models from outside labs for most of the work.
