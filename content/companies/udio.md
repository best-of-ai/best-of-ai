Udio was founded by former DeepMind researchers to turn prompts into songs. In October 2025 it settled its lawsuit with Universal Music Group and agreed to build a licensed platform, trained only on authorized music on an opt-in basis, as a subscription where users can customize, stream and share songs inside a protected space. Artists are to be paid both for training use and when fans generate music from their work. The platform was set for 2026, with no firm date, and we found no report that it has launched. The existing product stays up in a restricted form during the transition, with fingerprinting and filtering in place.
