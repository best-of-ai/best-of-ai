Xiaomi has built a serious open model family, MiMo, to run across its phones, cars and smart home products. MiMo-V2-Pro and Omni, aimed at agents and multimodal use, launched in March 2026. MiMo-V2.5 and V2.5-Pro followed on April 28 under the MIT license. MiMo-V2.6-Pro, Flash and a 9B model arrived on September 21, 2026 with open weights, training code and reinforcement learning environments. Flash is a mixture-of-experts model with 309 billion total parameters and 15 billion active, and Pro scored 46 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index, the highest for an open-weight model there at launch.

Sources differ by a day on the V2.6 date. Xiaomi also open-sourced MiMo Code V0.1, a terminal coding assistant, in June 2026.
