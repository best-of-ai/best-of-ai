Cursor is an AI code editor, and the company behind it, Anysphere, has become one of the most valuable AI startups. Its own model, Composer, launched in November 2025 and Composer 2.5 followed in May 2026. The editor lets developers run several agents at once and choose models from different labs.

Ownership changed in 2026. In April, xAI reportedly paid for exclusive collaboration rights with an option to buy Cursor for $60 billion, ahead of a planned $2 billion raise at about $50 billion. On June 16, SpaceX exercised the option in an all-stock deal valued at $60 billion, expected to close in the third quarter. A model built with xAI is expected to ship in both Cursor and Grok Build.

Those details come from news blogs and aggregators rather than filings, and we did not confirm whether the deal has closed. Earlier, Anysphere had been valued at $29.3 billion in November 2025.
