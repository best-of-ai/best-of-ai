Adobe builds AI into the apps its customers already use. Firefly is its family of image, video, vector and audio models, trained on licensed and Adobe Stock content, which Adobe pitches as safer for commercial work. Firefly runs on a credit system, and usage inside Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere and Express draws from the same pool.

In April 2026 Adobe launched the Firefly AI Assistant, which can operate across Creative Cloud apps from a single prompt. Asking it to prepare a social campaign, for example, can involve Photoshop, Premiere and Express in sequence. By June it was available in Photoshop, Premiere, Illustrator, InDesign and Frame.io. Adobe has said it is exploring working with third-party models, and some outlets reported Claude support, though Adobe's own wording was more cautious.

Acrobat has its own AI Assistant for summarizing and answering questions about documents, and Express uses Firefly for quick design generation.
