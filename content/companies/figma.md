Figma's AI work centers on putting agents and generation tools inside the design canvas. At Config 2026 it announced Weave tools, more than 20 prebuilt AI workflows for jobs like background replacement and style transfer, free of AI credits during the open beta on Professional plans and above. Deeper integration between Figma and Weave is planned for later in the year.

The Figma Agent gained Skills, Connectors and Attachments so it can follow a team's processes and pull context from other tools. Figma Motion, for animation, moved to open beta, and Code Layers lets designers work with real code on the canvas. Figma Make, which builds working prototypes from prompts, remains the main generative feature, though our sources said little about it.

Availability varies by plan and rollout stage, and coverage is mostly blogs, so check Figma's forum post for current terms.
