LangChain sells the tools around agent building. The stack is layered: LangChain as the base framework, LangGraph for stateful agents, Deep Agents, an open-source agent harness launched in March 2026, and LangSmith for observability and evaluation, with Fleet running on LangSmith. In July 2026 it announced a collaboration with NVIDIA on a NemoClaw blueprint for Deep Agents. A package snapshot from October 4 listed langchain 1.4.3 and langgraph 1.2.12, and warned that AgentExecutor is deprecated, with migration needed before December 2026.

The last priced round we could confirm is a $125 million Series B led by IVP in October 2025 at about $1.25 billion. One aggregator lists a Series C valuation of $3.01 billion for September 2026 from filings, which we could not corroborate, while a secondary market estimate gives a lower value. Treat valuation figures with care.
