Black Forest Labs was started by researchers who built the original Stable Diffusion, and its FLUX models are some of the most widely used image generators. The lineup includes FLUX.1, FLUX.1 Kontext for editing and FLUX.2, which arrived days before a $300 million Series B in December 2025 at a $3.25 billion valuation. AMP and Salesforce Ventures led, with NVIDIA, a16z and General Catalyst reported among participants.

The Financial Times has reported talks on a new round that could value the company at up to $4 billion, but we found no confirmation that it closed. Total funding figures from trackers range from about $450 million to $576 million.

Our model list includes FLUX.3 entries. A single investor summary mentions a FLUX 3 introduced in July 2026, and we found no announcement or press coverage that confirms it, so check Black Forest Labs directly. Many FLUX variants ship with open weights for non-commercial use, and the company sells API access for commercial work.
