Samsung's AI shows up in three places: Galaxy phones, its own models and its chips. Galaxy AI covers features like live translation, photo editing and Circle to Search across phones, tablets and wearables. With the Galaxy S26 in February 2026, Samsung added Perplexity as a built-in system assistant with its own wake word, "Hey Plex", next to Google Gemini and Bixby. Bixby uses Perplexity for more complex web questions while still handling actions on the phone.

Gauss is Samsung's in-house model family, and pre-launch reports said users might choose among Gemini, Perplexity and Gauss. We could not confirm how Gauss ended up in shipping phones. A headline also said Samsung is working with Google on an "AI OS", but we only saw the headline.

On the hardware side, Samsung's memory division makes HBM for AI accelerators, and the Mach-1 accelerator is in our hardware list. The search results covered the phone side much better than the chip side, so we say little here about HBM4.
