Pika builds consumer-friendly AI video tools. Pika 2.5, released in early 2026, added Scene Extension and Motion Control, which let you lengthen a clip or steer how things move. In April 2026 the company launched the beta of PikaStream 1.0, a real-time video model that powers a video chat skill for AI agents, so an agent can show up as a talking face.

AI Selves, announced in February 2026, is a different direction: digital twins with persistent memory that can post and chat on your behalf. It shows Pika moving away from competing only on clip quality against Runway, Google's Veo and OpenAI's Sora.

Much of the available coverage is thin or low quality, and we found no official announcement of a 2026 flagship beyond Pika 2.5 and PikaStream, so treat details here as provisional.
