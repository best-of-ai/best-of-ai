Zapier connects apps, and its AI work is mostly about letting agents use those connections. Zapier MCP, built on the Model Context Protocol and adopted in September 2025, lets assistants such as Claude call actions across thousands of apps with a couple of clicks. Third-party coverage cites anywhere from 8,000 to over 9,000 apps depending on date.

Zapier Agents moved from chat-style helpers toward automation-first agents that run and orchestrate Zaps, and are now generally available with enterprise MCP support. An SDK in open beta since April 2026 reportedly lets agents make arbitrary API calls to about 10,000 apps. Enterprise customers can now apply one policy layer to no-code workflows, agents and MCP-connected assistants.

Our sources here are mostly blogs and review sites, so check Zapier's changelog for exact app counts and plan details.
