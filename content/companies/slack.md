Slack's AI work is Slackbot, which Salesforce rebuilt in 2026 from a simple helper into an agent. A new version launched in January, reportedly powered by Anthropic's Claude, and on March 31 Salesforce announced more than 30 additional capabilities. Slackbot can answer questions, find information across channels, draft content, schedule meetings and act inside Slack, and it can send tasks to Agentforce agents.

It is included in Business+ and Enterprise+ plans without extra cost, a limited version arrived for free and Pro plans from April 2026, and Slack is to be bundled with every new Salesforce customer account from the summer. Slack's model setup is reported to sit on a FedRAMP-certified model for government customers.

Salesforce describes a future "super agent" that coordinates third-party tools over MCP. Release dates in the coverage differ by a few days, so check Salesforce's newsroom for specifics.
