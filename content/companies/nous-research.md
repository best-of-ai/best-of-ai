Nous Research is known for the Hermes open models and, more recently, Hermes Agent. On October 7, 2026 it confirmed a $90 million Series B at a reported $1.5 billion valuation, bringing total funding to about $158 million. Reported investors include NVIDIA, Union Square Ventures, Menlo Ventures, Samsung and 1789 Capital. Nous has not itself confirmed the valuation. The round came with news of Hermes for Businesses, an enterprise product built on Hermes Agent, with no launch date or pricing.

In September it released Hermes 4.3, post-trained on Psyche, its decentralized training network, with context up to 512K tokens. The 36B size comes from a secondary source. Nous later said it trained the model both on Psyche and the usual centralized way. We found no sign of a Hermes 5.
