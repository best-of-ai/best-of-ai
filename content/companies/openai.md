OpenAI's AI work centers on the GPT line and the products built on it. ChatGPT is the consumer face, and the API sells the same models to developers. The current generation is GPT-6, which ships in tiers named Luna, Sol, and Terra, with GPT-6.1 Sol and Terra following in the autumn of 2026. Reasoning models started with o1 and o3, and open-weight releases such as gpt-oss-20b and gpt-oss-120b let people run OpenAI models on their own hardware.

Beyond text, OpenAI makes the Sora video models, the gpt-image line (Images 2.6 landed in early November 2026), and Whisper for speech. Codex is now OpenAI's coding agent rather than the old completion model, and it sits alongside ChatGPT Atlas, a browser with an agent built in, and Operator for web tasks.

DevDay 2026 in late September was mostly about agents. OpenAI announced Dots, always-on agents running on GPT-6 Astra, and a higher-priced Pro 500 tier. CNBC reported that the company held back a GPT-6.1 Astra launch because the model did not meet its safety bar. OpenAI also gave 100,000 academic researchers free access to its strongest models.
