Poolside trains models for software engineering. Its Laguna family includes Laguna XS.2, a 33B model with 3B active released in April 2026, and Laguna M.1, a 225B model with 23B active, both reported as Apache 2.0 on Hugging Face. Its agent product and Malibu model name appear in aggregator pages, with Malibu as the flagship for complex engineering and Point for low-latency use.

The company's finances had a setback in 2026. A planned $2 billion Series C at about $14 billion, with up to $1 billion from NVIDIA, reportedly collapsed in April after CoreWeave walked from a 15-year anchor lease for Project Horizon, its 2-gigawatt Texas data center plan. Another source gives $12 billion. Bloomberg reported on April 24 that NVIDIA would not lead a rescue, and Google talks over a 400 megawatt slice went quiet. One timeline gave cash runway through the third quarter of 2026. These come from aggregators citing Bloomberg, so check the originals.
