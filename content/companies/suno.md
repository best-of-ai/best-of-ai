Suno generates full songs, with vocals, from a text prompt. The company settled with Warner Music Group in November 2025 and agreed to move to licensed models, which will replace its current ones in 2026. Warner's artists decide whether their voices, names and compositions can be used, and Suno is buying Songkick from Warner.

A new model family, reported as v6, launched on September 9, 2026 with Warner, BMG and Believe. The version name is from a single source and we could not confirm it elsewhere. Suno reportedly raised a $400 million Series D in June 2026 at a $5.4 billion valuation, with about 2 million paid subscribers and $300 million in annual recurring revenue as of February, though these are secondary numbers.

Legal problems continue. Copyright groups including Denmark's Koda and Germany's GEMA have claims against it, and some reports say Sony and Universal litigation is still open.
