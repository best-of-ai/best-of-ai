01.AI, founded by Kai-Fu Lee, built the open Yi models, which were strong on release in 2023 and 2024 but have not kept pace with newer Chinese labs. The company has shifted toward enterprise work. Its Number One Officer project has Lee acting as a client's chief AI strategy officer, and Wanzhi, launched in March 2025, is a platform for deploying AI apps inside companies.

Lee told LatePost the company is dismantling its offshore structure to prepare for an IPO, with quarterly profit targeted by 2027. We could not confirm when he said it. A 2026 Fudan listing calls Lee CEO of Lingyi Wanwu, which looks like the company's Chinese-facing name, though sources do not state that outright.
