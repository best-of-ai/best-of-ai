IBM sells AI to large organizations, and the company says the point is governance and integration rather than the biggest model. Watsonx is the umbrella brand. Granite is the open model family, and Granite 4.0 introduced a hybrid Mamba and transformer design that IBM says cuts memory needs for long inputs by more than 70 percent. Granite models are released under Apache 2.0.

At Think 2026 in May, IBM positioned watsonx Orchestrate, now in private preview in its next-generation form, as a control plane for agents from any source, with shared policies. IBM Bob, an agentic development assistant, became generally available, and IBM Sovereign Core targets regulated customers that need to keep data in region. Reports also mention a Granite 4.2 release, which we could not confirm.

IBM's pitch to customers is that AI should fit into existing mainframes, hybrid clouds and compliance rules, an area where newer labs have less experience.
