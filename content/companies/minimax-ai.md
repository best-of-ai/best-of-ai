MiniMax listed in Hong Kong in January 2026 and has been planning a second listing on Shanghai's STAR Market, signing a tutoring agreement with CITIC Securities on May 29. Reports on its share performance conflict, ranging from a doubling on debut to a surge of over 1,600 percent, so we give no figure.

M3, launched on June 1, 2026, is an open-weight model for coding, agent work, long context and native multimodality, with a context window up to one million tokens, five times that of M2.7. MiniMax has not disclosed its size or training setup. A July analyst note expected upgrades in the second half focused on coding and agent post-training and on larger scale.

Hailuo, its video model, was due a third version: a July note said Hailuo 3 had finished training and was weeks from launch, and we did not confirm that it shipped. MiniMax also completed a $2 billion financing, according to a report.
