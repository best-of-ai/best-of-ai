Zoom's AI is AI Companion, now at version 3.0. It summarizes meetings, drafts replies and, since March 2026, builds custom agents and AI-first Docs, Sheets and Slides. Zoom calls its approach federated: tasks go to its own small and large models or to third-party models from OpenAI and Anthropic, and users can reach open models such as NVIDIA's Nemotron. Zoom claims this combination beat OpenAI and Google on one hard benchmark, which we did not verify.

AI Companion is included in paid plans at no extra cost, with a standalone subscription at $10 a month and a low-code Custom AI Companion at $12 per user per month. Zoom said monthly active users of AI Companion more than tripled year over year in its fourth quarter of fiscal 2026. It also launched Zoom AI Services, a set of APIs for speech, language, reasoning and image tasks.

Analysts point out the catch of the federated design: customers either accept that queries may go to outside providers or stay on Zoom-hosted models, which limits what they can use.
