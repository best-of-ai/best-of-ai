Ai2 is a nonprofit that releases fully open models, meaning weights, data and training code. Olmo 3, released in November 2025 in 7B and 32B sizes, was its first generation to include a reasoning model, with Base, Think, Instruct and RL Zero versions, and Olmo 3.1 extended the reinforcement learning. Molmo 2, from December 2025, is an open vision-language model with video understanding, pointing and tracking.

Ai2's news page lists Olmo Hybrid in March 2026, a 7B model that combines transformer attention with linear RNN layers, and MolmoPoint, a pointing architecture. One aggregator dates an Olmo-core 3 training stack for mixture-of-experts models to October 2026, which we could not confirm. Ai2 also takes part in the NSF-backed OMAI project. Some hosted endpoints for older Olmo and Molmo models were retired in March 2026.
