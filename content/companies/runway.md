Runway makes AI video generation and editing tools for filmmakers and studios. Its Gen models run from Gen-1 through Gen-4, and Gen-4.5 is the newest release we could confirm. One report says it topped the Artificial Analysis text-to-video leaderboard at launch, ahead of Google and OpenAI models. We found no announcement of a Gen-5 in the sources we checked.

The company's attention has turned to world models, which simulate environments rather than just render clips. Runway shipped its first in December 2025 and has said a second will follow later in 2026. In February 2026 it raised $315 million in a Series E at a $5.3 billion valuation, backed by NVIDIA and AMD Ventures, with part of the money going to compute through CoreWeave.

Day to day, most users meet Runway through its editor, where Act-One transfers a performer's expressions onto an animated character, and through its API.
