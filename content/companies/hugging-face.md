Hugging Face is where most open models get shared. Its Hub hosts well over a million models and several hundred thousand datasets, and the Transformers and Diffusers libraries are standard tooling for working with them. Most open-weight releases from other labs, including Qwen, Mistral and NVIDIA's Nemotron, ship there first.

The company also trains its own models, mostly small ones. SmolLM and its successors target phones and laptops, StarCoder came out of the BigCode project, and smolagents is a lightweight library for building agents.

Robotics is the newer side. Hugging Face bought Pollen Robotics in 2024 and sells Reachy Mini, a desktop robot priced around $299 to $449. In May 2026 it opened an app store for the robot with more than 200 apps, and around 10,000 units have reportedly sold. LeRobot, an open library of robotics models and datasets, supports the same effort. We did not find confirmed new open model releases from Hugging Face itself in 2026.
