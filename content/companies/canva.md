Canva introduced Canva AI 2.0 in research preview in May 2026, which it calls its biggest product change since launching in 2013. You can design by conversation, have the AI edit across a project, and keep a persistent memory of your brand and preferences. Canva describes it as a "creative OS" tying together six connected workflows. Magic Studio, the earlier set of image, writing and video tools, remains underneath.

The company has also been buying creative software. Affinity, the design suite Canva acquired in 2024, was relaunched as a free app in late 2025 and reportedly gained a Claude integration in 2026. Canva also acquired the motion-design tool Cavalry and made it free, plus several smaller companies including MangoAI and Simtheory. In August 2026 designs could be created inside Google Gemini and AI Search.

Dates for some of this vary between sources, since most coverage is from blogs rather than Canva's own newsroom.
