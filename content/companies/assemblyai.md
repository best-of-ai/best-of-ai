AssemblyAI sells speech recognition and audio intelligence by API. Universal-3 Pro, launched around February 4, 2026, lets developers add a text prompt alongside audio to shape the transcript. A streaming version followed in early March for voice agents. The company says it has a lower missed-entity rate than rivals in hard audio, which is a vendor benchmark. Its site now promotes Universal-3.5 Pro, listed on OpenRouter on September 22, 2026, and we found no detailed coverage of how it differs.
