Luma AI began with 3D capture and now builds video and world models. Ray3, released in September 2025, is a reasoning video model that interprets a prompt before generating video, images and audio. Ray3.14, announced in January 2026, targeted animation, video-to-video and cinematic work. Dream Machine is the product people use.

In November 2025 Luma raised $900 million in a Series C led by Saudi Arabia's Humain, with AMD Ventures, a16z, Amplify and Matrix, at a reported valuation near $4 billion. The money ties to Project Halo, a planned 2 gigawatt AI supercluster in Saudi Arabia where Luma will be an anchor customer. Luma says it wants to expand into simulation, design and robotics through world models.

Its CEO told The Wrap that the first film significantly aided by AI could arrive in 2026. Funding totals reported by trackers vary from about $968 million to $1.07 billion.
