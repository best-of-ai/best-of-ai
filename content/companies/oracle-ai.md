Oracle's AI business is mostly infrastructure. It is OpenAI's partner for Stargate, with a July 2025 agreement for 4.5 gigawatts of data center capacity in the US. Active sites in Texas, New Mexico, Wisconsin and Michigan are under construction, and the Abilene, Texas flagship is designed to scale to 5 gigawatts. One source puts the OpenAI contract at $300 billion over five years, though figures vary, and reports in February 2026 said talks on a broader expansion had stalled while the core agreement held.

OCI Zettascale10 is the cluster fabric behind the Abilene site, and in March 2026 Oracle announced a next-generation supercluster built on NVIDIA's Vera Rubin platform. Oracle AI Database 26ai reached on-premises Linux release in January 2026, with features like agent memory and deep data security. Oracle plans to raise up to $50 billion in 2026 to fund the buildout, and one analysis put its contracted backlog at $638 billion.

Many of these numbers come from financial commentary, not Oracle filings, so treat them as approximate.
