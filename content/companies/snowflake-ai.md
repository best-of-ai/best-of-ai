Snowflake adds AI to the data warehouse through Cortex, a set of services that let customers run models and agents against data that never leaves their account. Cortex offers models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and Mistral, plus Snowflake's own Arctic family.

Two partnerships define 2026. Snowflake and OpenAI announced a multi-year $200 million deal in February, putting OpenAI's models natively inside Cortex across AWS, Azure and Google Cloud. A similar Anthropic deal, also reported at about $200 million, came first, and at Snowflake Summit in June 2026 the two said Claude was powering Cortex Code, a coding assistant. Snowflake is also a launch partner in Anthropic's Claude Marketplace.

Arctic Embed 2.0 is the company's open embedding model, used for search and retrieval. Most of what is public about Snowflake's AI is about partnerships and platform features rather than frontier models of its own.
