Stripe's AI efforts focus on how software agents pay for things. In September 2025 it launched the Agentic Commerce Protocol with OpenAI, and it later worked with Google, whose Universal Commerce Protocol Meta also joined. At Sessions in 2026 Stripe added the Machine Payments Protocol for billing agents over HTTP, along with a Link agent wallet, virtual cards for agents and x402 settlement in USDC stablecoin.

Stablecoins are central to the pitch. Stripe's 2025 annual letter said stablecoin payment volume doubled to $40 billion, and co-founder John Collison predicts a torrent of agent-driven commerce. Observers note that actual agent spending is still small and the field is speculative.

Stripe also uses machine learning in Radar for fraud detection. We found no details on a Stripe-built foundation model.
