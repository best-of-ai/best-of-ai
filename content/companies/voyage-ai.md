Voyage AI makes embedding and reranking models for retrieval, and MongoDB acquired it in 2025 to put vector search inside its database. On January 15, 2026 MongoDB announced the Voyage 4 family: voyage-4, the flagship voyage-4-large, voyage-4-lite for cost and latency, and an open-weights voyage-4-nano for local use. MongoDB says the series beats Gemini and Cohere on the public RTEB leaderboard, and that voyage-4-large improves retrieval F1 by 3.7 percent over the nearest competitor. Those are vendor claims.

The same announcement made voyage-multimodal-3.5 generally available with support for text, images and video, and put Automated Embedding in public preview, which creates and stores embeddings as data is written. The models are available through MongoDB Atlas and also as a standalone platform. An OpenRouter listing dates voyage-4 to July 2026, which looks like an error.
