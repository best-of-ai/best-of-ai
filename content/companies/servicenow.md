ServiceNow now sells AI under a few names. Now Assist is the generative layer inside its workflows. The Moveworks acquisition, announced at $2.85 billion and closed in December 2025, added conversational AI and enterprise search, and EmployeeWorks combines them with ServiceNow's portal in early 2026. Autonomous Workforce uses teams of AI specialists with defined roles, such as a level-one service desk agent. ServiceNow says early internal results show it handling more than 90 percent of employee IT requests, a vendor-reported figure.

At Knowledge 2026 in May, ServiceNow introduced Otto, which merges Now Assist, Moveworks and agents into one interface, plus Action Fabric, Autonomous CRM and wider AI Control Tower governance. One lower-reliability source reported a related OpenAI partnership, which we could not confirm.

The company's argument is governance: agents need audit trails and permissions, and its workflow platform already supplies them.
