This repository bundles the following skills:

- **Madman**: Skill for brainstorming and generating raw material before any organizing.
- **Whirlybird**: Skill for nonlinear outlines and mindmap diagrams of article structure.
- **Architect**: Skill for turning raw material into an outline and a throughline.
- **Carpenter**: Skill for writing draft prose section by section from an approved outline.
- **Judge**: Skill for editing drafts, checking for AI voice patterns, and readability analysis.
- **Quality Rubric**: Skill for scoring finished content and naming the phase that needs rework.
- **SEO Writer**: Skill for keyword targeting, heading structure, and on-page SEO checks.
- **Content Strategist**: Skill for planning pillar and cluster articles from a research corpus.
- **Research Intake**: Skill for building a knowledge map from notes and finding research gaps.
- **Knowledge Harvester**: Skill for saving sources and citations back to a knowledge base.
