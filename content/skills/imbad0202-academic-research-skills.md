This repository bundles the following skills:

- **Deep Research**: A 13-agent research skill with modes including full, quick, review, and fact-check.
- **Academic Paper**: A 12-agent paper-writing skill with modes including plan, outline-only, and revision.
- **Academic Paper Reviewer**: A 7-agent multi-perspective review skill with full and re-review modes.
- **Academic Pipeline**: A 10-stage orchestrator skill managing the full workflow with integrity verification.
