This repository bundles the following skills:

- **baoyu-xhs-images**: Generates Xiaohongshu image card series in 12 art styles and 6 layouts.
- **baoyu-infographic**: Builds infographics from 21 layouts and 17 visual styles.
- **baoyu-diagram**: Produces SVG diagrams including flowcharts and sequence diagrams.
- **baoyu-cover-image**: Creates article cover images using a 5-dimension style system.
- **baoyu-slide-deck**: Turns written content into presentation slides with 16+ style presets.
- **baoyu-comic**: Generates educational comics in configurable art styles and tones.
- **baoyu-image-gen**: Generates images across multiple providers including OpenAI and Google.
- **baoyu-youtube-transcript**: Downloads YouTube transcripts and subtitles with chapter support.
- **baoyu-url-to-markdown**: Converts web pages to markdown using Chrome CDP.
- **baoyu-translate**: Translates text with quick, normal, and refined modes.
