This repository bundles the following skills:

- **seo-audit**: Runs a full-site audit and returns a scored, prioritized report.
- **seo-content**: Evaluates page content for usefulness, E-E-A-T, readability and thin pages.
- **seo-content-brief**: Writes competitive content briefs with section word counts and keyword placement.
- **seo-backlinks**: Analyzes a backlink profile, anchor text, toxic links and disavow candidates.
- **seo-cluster**: Clusters keywords by SERP overlap and plans hub-and-spoke content.
- **seo-competitor-pages**: Generates 'X vs Y' and 'alternatives to X' comparison pages.
- **seo-dataforseo**: Pulls live SERP and keyword data through the DataForSEO MCP server.
- **seo-agentic**: Checks how ready a site is for AI agents, including robots rules and the accessibility tree.
