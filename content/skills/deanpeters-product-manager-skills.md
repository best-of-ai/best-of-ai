This repository bundles the following skills:

- **problem-framing-canvas**: MITRE's Look Inward/Outward/Reframe sequence.
- **positioning-statement**: Geoffrey Moore template for market differentiation.
- **stakeholder-mapping**: Power x Interest and Impact x Power grid analysis.
- **discovery-interview-prep**: Mom Test-style interview planning.
- **prioritization-advisor**: Context-driven framework selection, including RICE, ICE, and Kano.
- **prd-development**: Structured PRD from problem through metrics.
- **user-story**: Mike Cohn format with Gherkin acceptance criteria.
- **tam-sam-som-calculator**: Market sizing in three modes.
- **business-health-diagnostic**: SaaS health analysis across growth and retention.
- **battle-card-builder**: Sales enablement content from research findings.
