This repository bundles the following skills:

- **bug-bounty**: Runs the full bounty workflow from recon and scope review to reporting.
- **bb-methodology**: Helps decide what to do at the start of a hunt or when switching targets.
- **apk-redteam-pipeline**: Acquires and tests Android APKs end to end.
- **cloud-iam-deep**: Covers external attack paths against AWS, Azure and GCP IAM.
- **enterprise-vpn-attack**: Attack matrix for external SSL VPN appliances such as Cisco ASA and Fortinet.
- **evidence-hygiene**: Covers evidence capture and redacting cookies and secrets from proof-of-concept material.
- **bugcrowd-reporting**: Bugcrowd-specific reporting advice, including category selection.
