This repository bundles the following skills:

- **chekhov-dramaturgy**: Writes plays and screenplays in the Chekhovian mode.
- **ozu-screenplay-style**: Writes screenplays in the style of Ozu Yasujiro and Noda Kogo.
- **succession-series-writing**: Writes ensemble series in the manner of Succession.
- **sw-character-conflict**: Covers character creation, protagonist and antagonist design, and dramatic conflict.
- **sw-american-case-studies**: Case studies of American screenwriting.
- **sw-chinese-opera-banqiang**: Playwriting for banqiang-system Chinese opera.
