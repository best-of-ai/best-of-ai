This repository bundles the following skills:

- **design-critique**: Runs a structured team critique with feedback rules and clear outcomes.
- **design-debt-audit**: Inventories and ranks accumulated design inconsistencies.
- **design-qa-checklist**: Builds a checklist for verifying a build against the design.
- **design-review-process**: Sets up review gates, criteria and approval flow.
- **design-sprint-plan**: Plans a design sprint from framing the challenge to prototype testing.
- **handoff-spec**: Writes the implementation handoff with measurements, states and edge cases.
- **design-impact-reporting**: Explains design's contribution to business outcomes for stakeholders.
