This repository bundles the following skills:

- **Opportunity Solution Tree**: Structures desired outcomes into testable hypotheses.
- **Interview Script**: Builds jobs-to-be-done customer research interview templates.
- **Product Strategy Canvas**: A nine-section strategic planning template.
- **Value Proposition**: A six-part jobs-to-be-done positioning framework.
- **Create PRD**: Generates an eight-section product requirements document.
- **OKRs / Roadmaps**: Aligns team objectives and roadmaps to company strategy.
- **Pre-Mortem**: Identifies risks ahead of time using a Tiger/Elephant classification.
- **GTM Strategy**: Plans channel, messaging, and launch activities.
- **Growth Loops**: Designs self-sustaining growth flywheels.
- **North Star Metric**: Selects a core metric and its supporting input metrics.
- **Cohort Analysis**: Analyzes retention and engagement trends by cohort.
