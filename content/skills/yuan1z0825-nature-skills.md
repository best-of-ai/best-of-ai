This repository bundles the following skills:

- **nature-reader**: Generates a bilingual Chinese-English Markdown reader of a paper with figure-text alignment.
- **nature-polishing**: Revises academic text into Nature-style English with consistent terminology.
- **nature-paper2ppt**: Converts a research paper into a presentation deck.
- **nature-reviewer**: Simulates a blinded, three-reviewer peer review.
- **nature-response**: Generates a point-by-point rebuttal to reviewer comments.
- **nature-figure**: Produces publication-grade, multi-panel scientific figures.
- **nature-academic-search**: Performs multi-source literature search with citation verification.
