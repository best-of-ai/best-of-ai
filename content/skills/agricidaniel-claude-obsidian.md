This repository bundles the following skills:

- **wiki**: Core skill for building and maintaining wiki-style notes.
- **save**: Captures and preserves source material before synthesis.
- **wiki-ingest**: Ingests new source content into the vault.
- **wiki-query**: Retrieves grounded answers from existing vault content.
- **wiki-lint**: Checks vault notes for consistency and health issues.
- **autoresearch**: Automates research workflows across multiple sources.
- **canvas**: Creates Obsidian Canvas views of connected notes.
- **obsidian-markdown**: Reference skill covering Obsidian-flavored Markdown syntax.
