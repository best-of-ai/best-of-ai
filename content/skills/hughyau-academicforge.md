This repository bundles the following skills:

- **scientific-visualization**: Makes publication figures with matplotlib, seaborn and plotly.
- **alphafold2**: Predicts protein structures with AlphaFold2 through ColabFold.
- **boltz**: Predicts structures of protein, nucleic-acid and small-molecule complexes with Boltz-2.
- **chai1**: Predicts complex structures with the Chai-1 foundation model.
- **borzoi**: Predicts genome-wide functional tracks from DNA sequence.
- **compute-env-setup**: Sets up a remote compute environment for running jobs.
- **algorithmic-art**: Creates generative art in p5.js with seeded randomness.
