This repository bundles the following skills:

- **coupling-analysis**: Analyzes coupling between modules by strength, distance and volatility.
- **domain-analysis**: Maps business domains and suggests service boundaries using DDD strategic design.
- **component-identification-sizing**: Maps architectural components and measures their size.
- **component-common-domain-detection**: Finds duplicate business logic spread across components.
- **component-flattening-analysis**: Finds misplaced classes and fixes component hierarchy problems.
- **decomposition-planning-roadmap**: Plans step-by-step migrations for breaking up a monolith.
- **evolutionary-modular-architecture**: Guides design of modular monoliths with DDD.
