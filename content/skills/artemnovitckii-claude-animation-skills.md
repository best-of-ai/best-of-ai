This repository bundles eleven skills, including:

- **zach-d-films**: Makes a 3D cartoon "what if" explainer with slow-motion impacts and captions timed to a voiceover.
- **stick-figure**: Makes a stick-figure explainer reel with whip transitions, short captions and sound made in code.
- **minimalistic-graphics**: Makes black-and-white line art animations with hairline steps and a hopping white dot.
- **map-route**: Animates a route on a map from a short text request.
