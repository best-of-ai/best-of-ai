This repository bundles the following skills:

- **citation-management**: Searches OpenAlex, PubMed and Google Scholar and manages citations.
- **clinical-reports**: Drafts clinical case and diagnostic report structures and runs local checks.
- **clinical-decision-support**: Prepares research-only clinical decision-support evaluations.
- **generate-image**: Generates or edits images through the OpenRouter Image API.
- **pdf**: Reads, extracts from and creates PDF files.
- **docx**: Creates and edits Word documents.
- **xlsx**: Creates and edits spreadsheets.
