Alibaba released Qwen-Audio-3.1-Realtime on September 28, 2026 as the flagship of a five-model Qwen-Audio-3.1 stack that also covers speech recognition and text-to-speech. It's built for full-duplex voice agents, meaning it can listen and respond at the same time and decide mid-conversation when to speak versus when to call an external tool.
