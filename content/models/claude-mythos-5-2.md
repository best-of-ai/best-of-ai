Claude Mythos 5.2 is the version of Fable 5.2 without the cyber and biology safeguards, and Anthropic calls it its most capable model for cybersecurity engineering. Access is by application only, through the Cyber Verification Program for security work and the Life Sciences Verification Program for biology. Claude Enterprise customers also get it inside Claude Security. Pricing matches Fable 5.2 at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens.

On Anthropic's CVE Rediscovery benchmark Mythos 5.2 scores 89.6 percent, up from 78.3 percent for Mythos 5.1, and it found 10.2 percent more vulnerabilities than Opus 5.5. It also scores 76.3 percent on CyScenarioBench. A fast mode exists, but only for organizations in the verification programs.
