OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra on November 17, 2026 as its model for the hardest projects. It writes and revises software, uses a computer and works with documents. The API gives it a 1.05 million token context window with up to 128,000 output tokens. Price is $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, 20 percent below GPT-6 Astra.

OpenAI says it beats GPT-6 Astra by 5.2 points on Agents' Last Exam at under a third of the cost, and by 6.2 points on its internal GDPval v2 while using 22 percent fewer output tokens. Astra is the first model OpenAI has released externally that meets its High threshold for AI self-improvement. It is available to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and through the API as gpt-6.2-astra.
