Ideogram 3.0 is Ideogram's third-generation image model, released on March 26, 2025 with sharper photorealism and a text-rendering accuracy that independent testing put around 90 percent, well ahead of the roughly 30 percent seen on Midjourney for similar short phrases. It introduced Style References, which let users upload a reference image and carry its visual style into new generations, plus Magic Fill and Extend, inpainting and outpainting tools built into Ideogram's Canvas editor. Batch generation at scale is gated to paid plans, reflecting how Ideogram has increasingly split free and pro tiers by production-scale features rather than just image quality. API pricing lands around six cents per image, keeping it in a similar range to other commercial text-to-image APIs. The release keeps typography as Ideogram's signature strength while catching up on editing tools that competitors like Midjourney and Adobe Firefly had already built out.
