Meta released Muse Ember on November 9, 2026 as the smallest model in the Muse line, aimed at tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction and long document parsing. It was the first model trained with Muse Wonder Full. The context window is 1 million tokens with up to 512,000 output tokens. Pricing is $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens, rising to $0.30 and $1.50 for prompts over 200,000 tokens.

Meta compares it with Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna. It scores 40.9 percent on Terminal-Bench 4.0 against 39.2 percent for Haiku 5.5, and 36 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, one point under Muse Spark 1.3. Meta says it matches Haiku's best score at about a third of the cost and suggests pairing it with Muse Wonder for the hardest steps. The model ID is muse-ember-1.0.
