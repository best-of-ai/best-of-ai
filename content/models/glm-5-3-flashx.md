Z.ai (Zhipu AI) released GLM-5.3-FlashX on September 18, 2026, carrying over GLM-5.3-Flash's 320-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts design with 18 billion active parameters per token, native multimodality, and hybrid sparse-plus-linear attention. The FlashX variant is tuned for serving speed, targeting throughput up to 200 tokens per second, while keeping the same 1 million token context window.
