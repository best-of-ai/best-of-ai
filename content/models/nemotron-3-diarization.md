NVIDIA released Nemotron 3 Diarization on September 23, 2026, a 100-million-parameter open-weight model that tracks up to eight overlapping speakers in real-time streaming audio. It targets call-center and meeting-transcription pipelines that need to know who said what as the audio comes in, rather than after the fact.
