Gemini 4 Hydrogen is the Gemini 4 model Google calls its daily driver for agentic workflows, released November 12, 2026. It costs $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens, flat across the full 1 million token context window, and the API has a free tier. Thinking levels run from low to xhigh, with high as the default.

Against Gemini 3.8 Flash, Google says Hydrogen is 2.5 times faster at its default setting, makes over five times fewer fabricated claims, and scores 57 percent on Terminal-Bench 4.1 versus 18.2 percent. Free Gemini app users get it through automatic model selection, and it also runs in Search, Sheets and Android Studio. In Antigravity, the orchestrator switches between Carbon and Hydrogen inside one session.
