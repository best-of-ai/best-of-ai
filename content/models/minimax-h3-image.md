MiniMax released H3-Image on November 11, 2026 as an open-weight image model built on its H3 base. It does text-to-image, generation from reference images and image editing. MiniMax says it makes a 4096 by 4096 image in under 10 seconds and accepts up to 16 reference images in one request.

The weights are on Hugging Face as MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3-Image, and the model is also on the MiniMax API Platform, MiniMax Code and Hailuo AI. MiniMax prices it at 50 to 70 percent of comparable offerings, with 50 percent off 4K requests during launch week. It claims the best prompt adherence among open-weight models but gives no numbers for that.
