Google released Gemini 4 Neon on November 18, 2026. It sits between Carbon, built for the hardest long-horizon projects, and Hydrogen, built for fast everyday execution. The context window is 1 million tokens, and API pricing is $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.15. Google says it costs 32 percent less per task than Carbon at the high setting.

Google reports 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at xhigh, third overall, and 65.2 percent on Terminal-Bench 4.1. Neon is in the Gemini API, Gemini for business, Antigravity and the Gemini app for Pro and Ultra subscribers. A cyber variant goes to trusted defenders through the Fairwind Program.
