Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber is Google's dedicated security model, released alongside the general-purpose 3.8 Flash on September 2, 2026, and gated behind the new Fairwind Program rather than sold as a standard API product. Google says it clears 70 percent-plus on internal vulnerability-discovery tests across 20 languages, scores 47.2 percent pass@1 on CWE-Bench, and delivers 2.6 times more correct patches to Chrome vulnerabilities than leading commercial models in the company's own testing.

Fairwind access goes case by case to organizations where a security failure carries outsized stakes (governments and national cyber authorities are the first priority tier), and it pairs the model with CodeMender, Google's harness for finding, verifying, and fixing vulnerabilities at agentic scale.
