Ant Group's InclusionAI team released Ling-3.0-Flash-Fin on August 28, 2026, continuing the training of the base Ling-3.0-Flash model on financial data developed with CICC and other institutions. It keeps the same 124 billion parameter, 5.1 billion active mixture-of-experts design and 256K context window, but adds source-grounded financial search, multi-document reasoning across regulatory filings and earnings reports, and spreadsheet-native valuation work.

Ant published BF16 weights on Hugging Face under an MIT license and opened a one-month free API trial, aiming the model at tasks like reading annual reports, building DCF models, and cross-referencing filings: the kind of grunt work that eats analyst hours at banks and research desks.
