Cognition released SWE-2 Flash on November 4, 2026 for Devin Desktop and Devin CLI. The company describes it as a fully American coding model, made by mid-training Inkling-Small (276B total, 12B active) and then running large-scale reinforcement learning. Cognition says it costs one fifth as much as SWE-2 and runs three times faster, at about 200 tokens per second. It is free in Devin through November 30.

Cognition reports 89.2 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 60.1 on DeepSWE 1.1, and says it beats every American open-weight model, including ones three times its size. Cognition is not publishing the weights yet and plans a program to share them with trusted partners and research labs.
