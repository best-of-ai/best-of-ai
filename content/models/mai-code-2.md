Microsoft AI released MAI Code 2 on November 17, 2026 at Ignite. It targets everyday enterprise coding: production code, debugging, multi-file implementation, repo-wide reasoning and UI work. Microsoft says it is not yet tuned for fundamental science research or very long-horizon agentic tasks. It is available in GitHub Copilot, and the page gives costs only as percentages, with a 50 percent promotional price shown.

Averaged over DeepSWE 1.1, Terminal Bench 2.1, SWE-Atlas and FrontierCode v1.1, Microsoft says MAI Code 2 reaches 98 percent of GPT-6.1 Sol's score at 40 percent of the cost, and 96 percent of Claude Opus 5.5's score at 5 percent of the cost. In a blind frontend test, 258 annotators preferred it to GPT-6 Sol 64 percent of the time. These are Microsoft's own runs.
