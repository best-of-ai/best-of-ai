Hy4 Flash is a 224B mixture-of-experts model with 12B active parameters, distilled from Hy4 and released in mid-November 2026. Trackers date it to November 16 or 17. It handles text and image input with context up to 1 million tokens.

API pricing is $0.07 per million input tokens and $0.281 per million output tokens, at half price until November 30. Tencent has said weights will follow later but has given no date, so this page lists it as not open-weight for now.
