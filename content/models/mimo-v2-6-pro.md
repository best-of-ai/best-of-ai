Xiaomi open-sourced the MiMo-V2.6 line on September 22, 2026, made up of MiMo-V2.6-Flash, the flagship MiMo-V2.6-Pro, and a faster inference variant called MiMo-V2.6-Pro-UltraSpeed. The release leans on reinforcement-learning gains from Xiaomi's research team, and the company cites inference throughput above 150 tokens per second as one of the family's selling points. MiMo-V2.6-Pro ships under the MIT license.
