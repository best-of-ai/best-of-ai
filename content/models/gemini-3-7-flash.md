Google shipped Gemini 3.7 Flash on August 13, 2026, just three weeks after 3.6 Flash, keeping the same 1,048,576 token input window and 65,536 token output ceiling while moving the knowledge cutoff to March 2026. Google credits the gains to developer feedback and algorithmic changes rather than a bigger model, and the benchmark jumps are large: DeepSWE v1.1 climbs from 49.0 to 65.3 percent, FrontierCode 1.1 Main goes from 34.4 to 43.6 percent, and Zapier's AutomationBench nearly doubles from 17.0 to 30.4 percent. Input pricing is cut in half to $0.75 per million tokens as introductory pricing through the end of 2026.

The model is rolling out to the Gemini app's Spark tier for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, alongside availability in Google Antigravity, AI Studio, Android Studio, and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform.
