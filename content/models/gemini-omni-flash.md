Gemini Omni Flash is the first model in Google's Omni series, announced at I/O 2026. It accepts image, audio, video, and text as input and generates video, bringing the ideas behind Veo and Genie into one reasoning model. It rolled out to AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app, Google Flow, and YouTube Shorts.
