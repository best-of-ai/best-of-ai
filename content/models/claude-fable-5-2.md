Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 on November 9, 2026, alongside Mythos 5.2. Both are the same underlying model: Fable keeps the cybersecurity and biology safeguards and is open to everyone, while Mythos drops some of them for vetted organizations. API pricing is $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, below Fable 5.1, and output is up to 30 percent faster. A fast mode at $16 and $80 generates tokens up to 2.5 times quicker.

Anthropic reports 49.1 percent on FrontierCode 2.0 at xhigh effort (Opus 5.5 scored 45.5 percent) and 74.6 percent on Terminal-Bench-Science. It says Fable 5.2 blocks about 55 percent fewer requests than 5.1. Blocked cyber queries fall back to Opus 4.8 and biology ones to Opus 5. For most coding and knowledge work, Anthropic still points people to Opus 5.5 as the daily driver and keeps Fable for open-ended technical projects.
