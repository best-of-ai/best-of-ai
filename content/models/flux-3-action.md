Black Forest Labs published FLUX 3 Action on September 23, 2026, a 7-billion-parameter open-weight model that predicts robot actions from video. It scored 42.2% on NVIDIA's RoboLab-120 benchmark compared with 36.8% for NVIDIA's own Cosmos 3 Nano, using less than half the parameters. Weights are released on Hugging Face under a new FLUX Kommunity License.
