ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, released on November 11, 2026, is the first post-trained model built on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base. Baidu calls it an intermediate checkpoint from post-training. It has 280B total parameters with 14B active and a 1 million token context window. Baidu says it is strong at coding, 3D modeling, knowledge work and content creation, and admits some capabilities may still fall short.

It is available through the Qianfan API and as a free listing on OpenRouter. Baidu says the weights will be open-sourced under Apache 2.0 soon, so this page lists it as not yet open-weight.
