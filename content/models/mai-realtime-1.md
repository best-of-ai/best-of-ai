MAI Realtime 1 is Microsoft AI's first speech-to-speech model, announced on November 17, 2026. It handles interruptions and acknowledgments, stays steady in noisy audio, connects to enterprise tools and takes personas through prompting. It is compatible with the OpenAI Realtime API, so existing voice agents can point at it with few changes. Harder requests are routed to a separate reasoning engine.

Base speech-to-speech costs $0.01 per audio minute, and reasoning is billed separately at $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens. Microsoft says it beats GPT Realtime 2.1 on quality, speed and price, but publishes no scores in the text. Access is through MAI Labs or a limited-access preview in Microsoft Foundry, and customers in the preview are not billed until public preview starts.
