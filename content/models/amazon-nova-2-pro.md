Amazon Nova 2 Pro is the first refresh of the Nova Pro tier since the original family launched at re:Invent 2024, and it's exclusive to AWS Bedrock like the rest of the line. It moves the context window up to 500,000 tokens and adds video understanding alongside text and image, priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $6.00 per million output tokens, a jump from the original Nova Pro that reflects the added capability.

Amazon has kept the Nova family's pitch consistent across generations: tight integration with Bedrock's fine-tuning, distillation, and guardrails tooling, aimed at enterprises already committed to AWS rather than developers shopping across providers. Nova 2 Pro doesn't top independent leaderboards, but Amazon's sales motion has always been that customers running compliance-heavy workloads on Bedrock get more value from the surrounding tooling than from chasing a benchmark score.
