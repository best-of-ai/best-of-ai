Groq 3 LPX is the first chip to ship from Nvidia's $20 billion acquisition of Groq, announced in production at Hot Chips 2026. Each LP30 die carries around 500MB of on-chip SRAM with 150 TB/s of bandwidth and handles only token generation, not training or prefill. Nvidia pairs the chips into liquid-cooled racks of 256 LPUs as a companion to its Vera Rubin platform, aimed at the low-latency demands of agentic AI workloads.
