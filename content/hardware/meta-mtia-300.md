Meta detailed the MTIA 300 on August 24, 2026, its first MTIA chip aimed at large training workloads rather than just inference. It pairs a 12x6 grid of processing elements with 216GB of HBM3E and two network chiplets carrying twelve custom 800 Gbps RDMA NICs, giving each chip 1.2 TB/s of I/O bandwidth so training jobs can scale across racks without relying on separate networking hardware.
