Meta detailed the MTIA 400 at Hot Chips 2026, built on a 3nm process with 288GB of HBM3E and a 1200W TDP. Unlike earlier MTIA chips split into separate training and inference lines, the 400 is built to run both jobs on the same silicon, reaching about 12 petaflops of MXFP4 compute and 6 petaflops at FP8. Meta says it plans to ship a new MTIA generation roughly every six months going forward.
