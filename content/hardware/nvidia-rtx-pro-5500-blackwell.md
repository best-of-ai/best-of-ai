NVIDIA announced the RTX PRO 5500 Blackwell on September 9, 2026, filling a gap between the RTX PRO 5000 and the 96GB RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell in its workstation lineup. It carries 84GB of ECC GDDR7 with 1,398GB/s of bandwidth, fifth-generation Tensor Cores, fourth-generation RT Cores, and a 600W power limit on a PCIe Gen 5 x16 card. NVIDIA has not announced a shipping date or price, and the product page currently invites customers to sign up for availability updates.
