What is the difference between free and freemium AI tools?

Free tools cost nothing. Freemium tools have a free tier with limited usage or features; paid plans unlock more.

Are these AI tools actually free to use?

Yes. Every tool listed here is either fully free or has a free tier you can use without a credit card.

Which free AI tools are best for writing?

ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini all have solid free tiers for writing. Browse the Writing Assistants category for the full list.

Are there free AI tools for image generation?

Yes. Several tools including Adobe Firefly offer free image generation. See the Image Generation category.
